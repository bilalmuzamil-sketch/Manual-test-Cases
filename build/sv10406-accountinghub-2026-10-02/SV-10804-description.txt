Found while testing [SV-10406|https://shopview.atlassian.net/browse/SV-10406]

h3. Summary

When a returned part's vendor credit is posted, AccountingHub takes the whole credit *including the tax* out of *1300 Parts Inventory* and posts no tax line. When that part was received, the tax went into *6110 Sales Tax Expense*, so the credit should take the tax back out of Sales Tax Expense.

h3. Steps to reproduce

Open staging: https://app.staging.shopview.com/ (admin login, location *Staging Heavy Duty - 9919*).

# Open a work order with an approved line (for example *S2-34864*).
# Under the line's *Parts*, click *+ Add Part*. Fill in *Part number*, *Description*, *Qty* 2, *Cost* 85.50 and *Sell price* 130, click *More options*, choose a *Vendor* (Source is already *Vendor*) and click *Save Part*.
# Click *Order* on the new part's row.
# Click *Receive* on the part's row. In *Receive parts*, enter a *Vendor Invoice Number* and tick the part. *Tax* fills in by itself. Click *Receive Parts (1)*.
# Click the *⋮* next to the part → *Return*. Enter a *Return Reason* and *Quantity* 2 → *Save & Close*.
# Go to *Parts* → *Returns*, tick the part's row and click *Receive Credit*. Enter a *Credit Memo Number* and click *Post Credit*. The credit shows the parts, the tax and the total:

!01-shopview-credit-360-plus-18-tax.png|width=900,height=185!

# Click the module switcher at the top left (*ShopHub*) → *AccountingHub* → *Purchases* → *Vendor credits*. Open the new credit and click its *Journal entry*.

*Fastest way to see it:* open credit C-13808 (https://app.staging.shopview.com/accounting/purchases/credits/01a0fe48-d839-73b0-b50b-5ea9ed220c0e) and click *Journal entry #15501*. Compare it with the entry for when that part was received, *#15500* (https://app.staging.shopview.com/accounting/ledger/journal-entries/01a0fe48-290d-728d-864d-250b944cb0ae).

h3. Expected behaviour

The vendor credit takes the tax back out of *6110 Sales Tax Expense*, the account the receipt put it in, and only the part's value comes off the parts account. Inventory is never credited with tax.

*Where this comes from:*
* [SV-9537|https://shopview.atlassian.net/browse/SV-9537] (Done), acceptance criteria: _"Posting a vendor bill with tax debits Sales Tax Expense, not Sales Tax Payable."_ That is why the tax is in Sales Tax Expense, and the credit has to undo that.
* [SV-10370|https://shopview.atlassian.net/browse/SV-10370], S3-R10, on how vendor credits post: _"separately attributable tax remains separate."_

h3. Current behaviour

The vendor credit credits *1300 Parts Inventory* with the full amount including tax, and there is no tax line. Sales Tax Expense keeps the tax:

!02-receipt-vs-return-journal-entries.png|width=900,height=497!
