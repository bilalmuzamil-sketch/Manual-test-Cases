Found while testing [SV-10406|https://shopview.atlassian.net/browse/SV-10406]

h3. Summary

When a part is sent back to the vendor and the vendor's credit is posted, AccountingHub puts the *tax* back in the wrong place.
When the part came in, the tax was recorded as a tax cost. When the part goes back, that tax cost is not reversed. Instead, the tax is taken off the value of the parts in stock.

h3. Steps to reproduce

Open staging: https://app.staging.shopview.com/ (admin login, location *Staging Heavy Duty - 9919*).

# Open a work order with an approved line (for example *S2-34864*).
# Under the line's *Parts*, click *+ Add Part*. Fill in *Part number*, *Description*, *Qty* 2, *Cost* 85.50 and *Sell price* 130. Click *More options*, choose a *Vendor* and click *Save Part*.
# Click *Order* on the new part's row.
# Click *Receive* on the part's row. Enter a *Vendor Invoice Number* and tick the part. The *Tax* fills in by itself. Click *Receive Parts (1)*.
# Click the *⋮* next to the part → *Return*. Enter a *Return Reason* and *Quantity* 2, then click *Save & Close*.
# Go to *Parts* → *Returns*, tick the part's row and click *Receive Credit*. Enter a *Credit Memo Number* and click *Post Credit*. The credit includes tax:

!01-shopview-credit-hd.png|width=560,height=453!

# Click *ShopHub* at the top left → *AccountingHub* → *Vendor credits*. Open the new credit and click its *Journal entry*. Then compare it with the journal entry for when the part was received.

*Fastest way to see it:* open the credit that is already there, https://app.staging.shopview.com/accounting/purchases/credits/01a0fe48-d839-73b0-b50b-5ea9ed220c0e, and click *Journal entry #15501*.

h3. Expected behaviour

The tax on the credit is taken back out of the tax cost (*Sales Tax Expense*), where it was recorded when the part came in. The value of parts in stock is not reduced by the tax.

*Where this comes from:*
* [SV-9537|https://shopview.atlassian.net/browse/SV-9537]: tax paid to a vendor is recorded as a tax cost — _"Posting a vendor bill with tax debits Sales Tax Expense"_. Returning the part should undo that.
* [SV-10370|https://shopview.atlassian.net/browse/SV-10370] (how vendor credits should be recorded): _"separately attributable tax remains separate."_

h3. Current behaviour

The whole credit, tax included, is taken off the value of the parts in stock (*Parts Inventory*). The tax cost is never reversed:

!02-received-vs-returned-hd.png|width=1000,height=564!
