*How to see it yourself*
# Open Staging: https://app.staging.shopview.com and click *Work Orders* in the top menu.
# Above the list, on the left, click the *All* tab. On the right of the same bar, click the first of the three view buttons (the one with horizontal lines) so the *List* is shown.
# Click *Search* (the magnifying glass above the list) and type *S2-363*.
# Look at the coloured pills in the *Status* column (second column): Ready For Review, Paid, Invoiced, Estimate and Approved pills are all the same width.

*Separate issue found (also on Production):* the pills all share one width per screen, but that width changes with what is on the screen. For example, on the *Estimates* tab every pill is 67 px, while on the *All* tab it is 114 px. Production works the same way today, so it is not caused by this fix. It has been reported separately as a bug: [SV-11464|https://shopview.atlassian.net/browse/SV-11464]
