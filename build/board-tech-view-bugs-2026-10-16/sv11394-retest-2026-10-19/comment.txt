*Where the expected result comes from*
The ticket's own sources: the spec (version 64) covers what hover reveals (S3-R13) but not how the hover looks, so the look comes from the design named in story SV-10046's UI/UX section:
{quote}Hover is a colour shift plus a lift (elev-1 → elev-2), never a scale.{quote}
Chris confirmed it on 16 October in this ticket: "please remove that override on Board View cards so hovering a card shows the design's soft shadow, with the darker border as today."

*How to see it yourself*
# Open Staging: https://app.staging.shopview.com and sign in as Admin.
# Click *Work Orders* in the top menu, then the *All* tab (top left).
# In the *Search* box above the list type {{S2-33692}}.
# Click the *Board View* button (the right of the three view buttons, right side of the bar above the list).
# Move the mouse pointer onto the card (don't click): its border turns darker and a soft shadow appears under it. Move the pointer away: the shadow goes.
