*How to see it yourself*
# Open Staging: https://app.staging.shopview.com/workorders
# Click your initials at the top right, then the location name, then *ZZAUTOTEST F2 Empty Shop*.
# Click the *Estimates* tab (top left). This location has no estimates.
# On the right of the bar above the list, click the *Tech View* button (the middle of the three view buttons, a person icon): you see "No work orders yet" and a *Create Work Order* button. Click the *Board View* button (the right one) to see the same.
# Click the *Create Work Order* button in the middle of the page: the New Work Order dialog opens. Close it with the x at its top right; if asked "Are you sure you want to close this dialog?", click *Close*.
# Click *Assigned to me* in the bar above the list: the message changes to "No work orders match these filters". Click *Clear all filters* to get the first view back.
# Switch back to your own location when done.
