*Where the expected result comes from*
Spec "Work Orders — Board View & Tech View Display Options", version 64:
{quote}S1-R9a: The switcher uses the design's icons at 20px: List is Lucide list, Tech View is user-round, Board View is square-kanban. In Tech View and Board View these icons are the design's: density (row-height in Tech View, card-height in Board View, custom icons in the design system's icon set), collapse chevron (chevron-down), pin and unpin (pin / pin-off), Invoiced or Paid lock (lock), Unassigned avatar (user-round) and the empty state when there are no work orders at all (clipboard-list). … These are the only changes to List. Every other List icon is unchanged.{quote}
Chris asked for this to be tested against version 51. The spec has changed since then: version 51 said all "empty states" use the clipboard icon, while the current version (64, 16 October 2026) keeps the clipboard only for the "no work orders at all" page and gives a search with no results the List's magnifier page. I tested against the latest version, and check 6 covers that change.

*How to see it yourself*
# Open Staging: https://app.staging.shopview.com/workorders
# On the right of the bar above the list there are three view buttons. Click the second one (the person icon) to open *Tech View*. Hover each of the three to see their names: List, Tech View, Board View.
# In Tech View, look at the left end of each technician's grey header row: the six-dot grip, the small arrow, the round avatar, the name, then the pin and the count.
# Click the third view button to open *Board View*. Look at a card with a green *Paid* label: a small lock sits right after the work order number.
# To see the empty page: click your initials (top right) > the location name > *ZZAUTOTEST F2 Empty Shop*, then click the *Estimates* tab. Tech View and Board View show "No work orders yet" with a clipboard icon. Switch back to your own location when done.
