I tested this on the QA branch *sv10628* (build *v26.40.17-ec15c08*) on *13 October 2026*, logged in as a non-Admin user, which is the situation the customer was in.

A Parts Manager can now change the Sell Price of a part on a *Complete* part sale. There is no error, the new price is kept, the part sale stays Complete and the totals follow. The same works on a Complete service work order. Users who are not allowed to edit still can't.

h3. How to see it
# Open the QA branch: [https://sv10628.qa.shopview.com/|https://sv10628.qa.shopview.com/]. On the login page click the *Tech* button. On this branch the Tech user has the *Parts Manager* role.
# At the top of the page, click the search box ({{Search customers, work orders, parts...}}), type {{209}}, then click the {{Part sales}} tab in the search window.
# Click the result *Una Truck Center* (status Complete). It opens part sale *P2-209*. (Another way in: top menu {{Parts}} -> left menu {{Part Sales}} -> click the blue {{Status: Estimate, +1}} filter -> {{Clear selection}} -> tick {{Completed}} -> click *P2-209*.)
# You are on the {{Parts (3)}} tab. In the first row (Part Number {{95749}}), click the {{Sell Price}} box, type a new price and press Enter.
# No error appears. Reload the page: the new price is still there, the part sale still says *Complete* at the top left, and {{Financial Info}} on the left shows the new Parts and Total.

*Fastest way to see it:* open [P2-209 directly|https://sv10628.qa.shopview.com/parts/part-sale/e71fd04b-aa1c-4b9a-b895-857803117877/part-requests] after clicking *Tech* on the login page, then do step 4.
