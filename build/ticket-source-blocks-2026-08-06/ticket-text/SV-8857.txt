# SV-8857 — The Schedule sidebar filters have no 'Clear all' and the Filters button does not show how many are on
# parent: SV-8687

Description
The work order filters on the left of the Schedule can be switched on one at a time but not switched off in one go - there is no 'Clear all'. There is also nothing on the Filters button to show that filters are currently applied or how many, so a planner can easily be looking at a filtered list and not realise it, and then has to hunt through three groups of tick boxes to undo it.

Steps to reproduce
Open the Schedule.

Click 'Filters' in the left-hand panel.

Tick 'Unassigned' under Assignment and 'Approved' under Status.

Close the popover and note the work order list is now narrowed.

Look at the Filters button for any badge or count showing that filters are on.

Open the popover again and look for a 'Clear all' control.

Expected behaviour
The Filters button carries a small count of how many filters are applied, and the popover has a 'Clear all' that resets every filter in one click.
Current behaviour
There is no count on the button and no 'Clear all' anywhere in the popover. Each tick box has to be unticked individually.
