# SV-8848 — Every time on the Schedule is shown six hours later than the time it was scheduled for
# parent: None

Description
When you schedule a technician for the morning, the Schedule shows the job in the afternoon. A one-hour job put on a technician's calendar for 9:00 in the morning appears on the board, in the pop-up summary and in the shift window as 3:00 in the afternoon. Every time on the page is shifted by six hours - the block position, the hover summary, the start and end times in the shift window, and the little 'now' marker in Day view. A twelve-hour day shift that should read 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM instead reads 1:00 PM to 1:00 AM and spills over into the next day on the Day view. This makes the Schedule unusable as a planning board: nobody can tell a technician when to start, and a morning job looks like an evening job.

Steps to reproduce
Open the Schedule and pick the Week view.

Find the work order S-15855 Vuchester Retail, unit 10123073, line ' Service - Mobile service call made by George Donald 403-369-7067' in the left-hand list (type S-15855 into 'Search work orders').

Drag that card onto the row for technician Ayesha Khan, on the Tuesday column.

Note that Ayesha Khan's working hours in Edit Staff Member are Monday 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM and Tuesday to Friday 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM, so the job should start at 7:00 in the morning.

Hover the new block and read the time range in the pop-up summary.

Click the block and read the two time boxes in the shift window.

Switch to the Day view for that date and look at where the block sits against the hour labels, and at the time printed on the 'now' marker.

Expected behaviour
The Schedule shows shop-local times. A job scheduled to start at 7:00 in the morning reads 7:00 AM everywhere - on the block, in the hover summary, in the shift window and in the Day view position. The 'now' marker matches the clock on the wall at the shop.
Current behaviour
Every time is six hours later than it should be. The 7:00 AM job reads 1:00 PM; a 9:00 AM job reads 3:00 PM; the 'now' marker read 4:11 PM while the shop clock read 10:11 AM. The date can roll over too: a 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM shift reads 1:00 PM to 1:00 AM and is drawn partly on the following day.

Technical details for developers
The write path converts shop-local to UTC correctly and the read path never converts back, so UTC is rendered as wall-clock. Evidence: shift cea79194-02d3-46d1-958b-a76f17ee1cec is stored startsAt 2026-08-02T16:01:00Z (= 10:01 local, America/Edmonton is UTC-6) and the tooltip reads 'Sun, Aug 2, 2026 - 4:01 PM - 5:13 PM'. Creating a shift with startTime '09:00' via POST /api/schedule/shifts stored 15:00Z and the modal's native input[type=time] value attribute is literally '15:00'. This is NOT a client-locale artefact: forcing the browser timezone to America/Edmonton (the workplace's own timezone from GET /api/staff/my-workplaces) produced the identical wrong value. Affected surfaces: schedule_shift_block text_schedule_block_time, the hover card, input_shift_detail_start_time / input_shift_detail_end_time, text_schedule_now_time, and Day-view horizontal positioning.
