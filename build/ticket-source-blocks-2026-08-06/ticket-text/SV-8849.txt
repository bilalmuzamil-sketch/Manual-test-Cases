# SV-8849 — A shift that is part of a multi-day series cannot be opened from the Week view
# parent: SV-8692

Description
When a big job is spread across several days, the Week view draws it as one labelled block on the first day with small 'continues' markers on the following days. None of those blocks can be clicked open. Nothing happens at all - no shift window, no menu. That means from the Week view a planner cannot see the detail of a spread job, cannot change its colour, cannot add a note and, most importantly, cannot delete it. The same shift opens perfectly well from the Day view and the Month view, so the information exists - it is only the Week view that will not open it.

Steps to reproduce
Open the Schedule and pick the Week view.

Type S-14527 into 'Search work orders' to find the Qispring Rentals work order (5 lines, 40.3h estimate).

Drag the card onto technician Brittany Anderson's row on the Monday column.

Choose 'Schedule whole work order' and then 'Create 4 shifts'.

Back on the Week view, click the labelled Monday block that reads 'Qispring Rentals / 5 Lines / Part of a series'.

Click each of the small 'continues' markers on Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday.

Now switch to the Day view for Monday and click the same block.

Expected behaviour
Clicking any part of a spread job in the Week view opens the shift window, exactly as clicking a single-day shift does, so the planner can see the detail, recolour it, add a note or delete it.
Current behaviour
In the Week view neither the labelled block nor any 'continues' marker responds to a click - no window opens. Right-clicking and double-clicking do nothing either. The very same shift opens normally from the Day view and the Month view.
