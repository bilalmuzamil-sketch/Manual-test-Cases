# SV-8693 [Story] Overlap & Lane Stacking
# parent: SV-8685

Summary
When multiple shifts overlap for the same technician, the grid splits them into stacked lanes so nothing is visually hidden — capped at 3 visible lanes with a "+N more" overflow for extreme cases.
Context
Overlap on the same technician is a conflict (flagged by the Conflict Detection story). This story handles the visual rendering of overlapping shifts so they're all visible without colliding.
Requirements
Shifts whose time ranges do not intersect share a single lane (row at normal height). — (PRD: §4.7)

Shifts whose time ranges intersect split into stacked lanes; row grows to fit. — (PRD: §4.7)

Visible lanes capped at 3. Additional shifts collapse into "+N more" affordance that opens a popover listing hidden shifts. Applies in day, week, and month views. — (PRD: §4.7)

Week and month views reach the overflow sooner due to narrower cells. — (PRD: §4.7)

Acceptance Criteria
Given a technician has 2 non-overlapping shifts (8am–12pm and 1pm–5pm), when rendered in day view, then both share a single lane at normal height.Given a technician has 2 overlapping shifts (8am–12pm and 10am–2pm), when rendered, then 2 stacked lanes appear and the row grows.Given a technician has 5 overlapping shifts, when rendered, then 3 lanes are visible and a "+2 more" affordance appears.Given the user clicks "+2 more", when the popover opens, then the 2 hidden shifts are listed with their details.UI/UX
