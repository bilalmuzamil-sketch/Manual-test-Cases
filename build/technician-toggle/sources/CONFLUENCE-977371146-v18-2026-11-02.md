## 1. Business Case

Today ShopView treats everyone with Time Clock on as a technician, unless their role is Office User or Time Clock User. A Parts Manager who clocks in to be paid by the hour therefore gets a column on Board View, a group on Tech View, and a place in every dropdown used to assign work, next to the people who actually turn wrenches (SV-11719, Thompson Diesel: a non-billable Parts Manager showing in Tech View). Shops cannot fix it without giving up time tracking for that person, and the request is recurring in customer feedback: PowerTools insight 204853 ("Tech View Mode Shows Non-Billable Users (Parts Manager/Admin) in Technician List"), 72596 ("Role management to add and remove staff from technician dropdown via staff settings") and 117140 ("Customer Requires Distinction Between Staff Roles (Technician vs Non-Technician Login/Time Tracking)"). This PRD adds a Technician toggle to each staff member, in the staff window next to Sales Representative, that decides who appears on Board View, Tech View and the technician dropdowns; on release day it is turned on for everyone who did technician work recently, so no shop loses a technician, while Time Clock, Billable, time sheets and reports stay exactly as they are.

### Workarounds

- **Living with the extra names.** Service writers and shop owners scroll past non-technicians in the technician dropdowns on every assignment, and on Board View and Tech View they work around the extra empty columns and groups. It costs attention on every assignment and makes the board harder to read; the extra names never go away.

- **Turning Time Clock off for the person.** The only way to take someone off the technician lists today. It stops ShopView tracking that person's hours, so shops that pay them hourly cannot use it.


## 2. Feature Overview

**Core: Technician toggle on each staff member**

- The New Staff Member and Edit Staff Member windows gain a "Technician" toggle, to the right of the Sales Representative toggle.

- A staff member is a technician when their Technician toggle is on and their Time Clock is on. Only technicians are offered on Board View, Tech View and the dropdowns used to assign work.

- Technician needs Time Clock: while Time Clock is off the toggle is off and grayed out with the reason, and turning Time Clock off turns Technician off. Staff on the built-in Office User and Time Clock User roles can never be technicians; for them the toggle is grayed out with the reason.

- New staff start with Technician set from their role: on for the built-in Technician and Foreman roles, off for every other role. The user can switch it either way before saving.

- Turning Technician off for someone who still holds open work shows a warning first, then saves. Nothing is unassigned: they keep the work they already have.

- Anyone already on a work order (as lead or on a labor line) who is not a technician any more keeps showing there, grayed out in the dropdown, so no existing assignment reads as blank.

- The staff list gains a read-only Technician column (Yes/No), and a notice at the top explains the change until one week after release.

- The Public API's staff records gain a read-only technician value.

- On release day Technician is turned on for every staff member who did technician work recently. Every existing technician stays a technician.


**Out of Scope**

- **Billable and Sales Representative.** No new rules: anyone can still be billable or a sales representative, technician or not, and Technician never changes either of them. Billable keeps doing only what it does today (stamping new time as billable or not, and the Technician Utilization report filter).

- **Clocking in on a job.** Anyone with Time Clock on can still press Start on an approved line and record time, as today. Time sheets, the time sheet dialogs and Punch Clock Activities keep listing everyone with Time Clock on except the Office User and Time Clock User roles, as today.

- **Reports.** Technician Efficiency, Technician Utilization and every other report list the same people as today.

- **Schedule.** Schedule lanes keep following departments; the Technician toggle does not change who appears on the schedule. No follow-up is planned.

- **Who can see or edit work.** Technician grants no access to anything. It only decides who is offered as a technician.

- **Bulk editing or filtering staff by Technician.** The staff list column is sortable only.


## 3. Jobs to be Done / Goals

**When** I assign a work order or a line, **I want to** pick from the people who actually do technician work, **so I can** assign quickly without scrolling past office and parts staff.

**When** I run the shop from Board View or Tech View, **I want to** see a column or group for each technician and nobody else, **so I can** read the board at a glance.

**When** I add or edit a staff member, **I want to** say whether they are a technician, **so I can** keep the lists right without changing their time tracking.

**Goals**

- A shop can take a Parts Manager (or anyone who is not a technician) off Board View, Tech View and every technician dropdown while that person keeps clocking in.

- On release day no shop loses a technician: everyone who appeared as a technician the day before and did technician work in the last 90 days still appears.

- Existing shops need no setup: release day sets the toggle from recent work. New staff on the built-in Technician or Foreman role start on; everyone else starts off, so a parts or office hire never lands on the board by accident.

- Nobody is logged out by the release or by a later change to Technician.


## 4. Key Decisions

- **Technician is a toggle on each staff member, in the staff window next to Sales Representative.** Rationale: whether someone does technician work is a fact about the person, and shops already mark people this way in the staff window (Sales Representative); a shop sets it where it adds and edits staff. Decided by the owning PM, 2026-10-29.

- **Who is a technician is defined by two things only: the person's Technician toggle and their Time Clock.** Technician needs Time Clock: it cannot be on while Time Clock is off. Rationale: both are visible, owned settings a shop can check and change, and only staff who clock in can be technicians today. Rejected: deciding from behavior (who has clocked onto jobs), because any Time Clock user can press Start, time sheet editors can create job time for anyone, and the schedule can assign anyone. Rejected: independent toggles, which would allow a Technician toggle that is on but does nothing. Decided by the owning PM, 2026-10-23, confirmed 2026-10-29.

- **Billable is not used to decide who is a technician, and Technician never changes Billable.** Billable is only one piece of release-day evidence that can turn Technician on, never off. Rationale: billable is an accounting flag, and a non-billable person can still be a technician (Foothills Group's non-billable technician must stay). Rejected: using Billable as the technician marker, and graying out or turning off Billable for non-technicians, which would change existing staff records. Decided by the owning PM, 2026-10-29. *Basis:* `d-reversal-billable-flag-tech-indicator`.

- **Release day keeps every shop as it is: Technician is turned on per person, from evidence.** Rationale: existing customers must wake up with the same technicians; shops then turn off the people who are not. Rejected: turning it on for everyone who appears as a technician today, which keeps every shop's problem until someone fixes each record by hand. *Basis:* `d-new-default-existing-customers`.

- **New staff start with Technician set from their role: on for the built-in Technician and Foreman roles, off for every other role.** Rationale: new hires on those two roles are almost always technicians, so the common case needs no extra click, while a parts or office hire never lands on the board by accident. The toggle stays the only rule and the user can change it before saving. Rejected: always off for new staff (the 2026-10-29 rule), which made every technician hire a second step; on whenever Time Clock is on, which recreates SV-11719 for every new hire who clocks in. Decided by the owning PM, 2026-11-02.

- **Turning Technician off warns and then allows; it never unassigns.** Rationale: the editor learns what the person still holds, and nothing they lead or work on disappears. Rejected: blocking the change until the work is reassigned, and silently allowing it. *Basis:* `d-never-force-keep-escape-hatch`.

- **Only the lists used to assign work, and Board View and Tech View, change.** Time sheets, reports and the schedule keep everyone they list today. Rationale: a non-technician still needs to record time, and real job time must stay in the reports. Rejected: narrowing every list of Time Clock users. Technician also decides only who is offered, not who can be assigned or record time: assignment and clocking are checked as today. Rejected: refusing on the server any assignment or job time for a non-technician. Decided by the owning PM, 2026-10-27; SV-11750 stays separate.

- **Changing Technician never logs anyone out.** Rationale: it grants no access, so there is nothing for a session to pick up. Rejected: logging the person out as a Time Clock change does, which would sign out most users of most shops on release day. Decided by the owning PM, 2026-10-26.

- **No feature flag.** The release-day step keeps every shop's lists as they are, so there is nothing to hide. *Basis:* `d-ship-without-feature-flag`.

- **Permissions.** Changing a staff member's Technician toggle, and seeing the staff list with its Technician column and notice, are governed by 'Settings → App Settings', the existing permission for Staff. No new permission is introduced. Technician grants no access.


## 5. Terminology

- **Technician** → in this PRD, a staff member whose Technician toggle is on and whose Time Clock is on. It is not the role named "Technician": a person on any role except the built-in Office User and Time Clock User roles can be one.

- **Time Clock, Billable and Technician** → three different settings on the staff member. Time Clock (a toggle): the person clocks in and out. Billable (a Yes/No dropdown): their time is stamped as billable to customers. Technician (a toggle): the person is offered as a technician.

- **Technician lists** → Board View columns, Tech View groups, the Technician filter on Work Orders, the Assign / Reassign lead technician window, the Lead Technician dropdown on a work order, Add Technician on a line, and the Technician dropdown in the Edit Labor window. Nothing else.

- **Open work** → in the turn-off warning, a service work order in Estimate, Approved, In Progress or Ready for Review that the person leads, or a line of such a work order that is not declined and that they are on (with labor on it, added with Add Technician or through a schedule shift, or as the line's own technician). On release day, only Approved, In Progress and Ready for Review count (Story 6).

- **Tech View** → the Work Orders display with one group per technician. The role setting "View mode: Tech view" in Roles & Permissions, a simplified interface, is a different thing and does not change.

- **Vocabulary.** This PRD uses the frontend vocabulary, *location*, which maps to *workplace* in the backend.


## 7. Requirements

### Story 1: Technician toggle on a staff member

**As a** shop owner, **I want** to say on each staff member whether they are a technician **so that** the technician lists show the right people.

**Design:** [https://claude.ai/artifact/1fuPz8Y4xTg7Syq1mfmJ9R](https://claude.ai/artifact/1fuPz8Y4xTg7Syq1mfmJ9R), pages "2 Turn Technician on for a staff member" and "3 When Technician can't be turned on"
**Jira:** [SV-11879](https://shopview.atlassian.net/browse/SV-11879)

**Form factor:** Desktop only by rule - the staff window is a settings page, so mobile is not applicable.

**Prerequisites:**

- User must have 'Settings → App Settings' setting enabled.


**Requirements:**

- **S1-R1:** The New Staff Member and Edit Staff Member windows (Settings, Staff) show a toggle labeled "Technician" directly to the right of the Sales Representative toggle, as the last toggle in that row (Time Clock, Sales Representative, Technician). In both windows the three toggles share one row of their own, below the Billable field, so New and Edit look the same, in the same style as the window's other toggles.

- **S1-R2:** A "New" pill sits at the top right of the "Technician" label, touching the end of the word. It looks the same as the pill on the Work Orders display switcher. It shows until 07:00 UTC on the seventh day after the release that ships this feature, then disappears for everyone, including in a window that is already open. The moment is fixed in UTC and read from the viewer's own clock: the pill shows while that clock is before it and is gone from that moment on. The exact date is set when the release is scheduled.

- **S1-R3:** In the New Staff Member window, Technician is pre-filled from the role shown: on when the role is the built-in Technician or the built-in Foreman role and Time Clock is on, otherwise off. Every other role pre-fills off, including Parts Technician and every custom role, even one made from Technician or Foreman. The pre-fill runs when the window opens and again each time the role or Time Clock is changed, until the user switches Technician by hand. From then on the user's choice wins: changing the role or Time Clock no longer pre-fills it, though S1-R4 and S1-N1 still turn it off, and only the user turns it back on. The toggle as shown is what is saved. Save & Add Next clears the form as it does today (no role, Time Clock off, Technician off) and forgets any earlier switch by hand, so the next person is pre-filled from their own role. Edit Staff Member never pre-fills (S1-E1).

- **S1-R4:** Technician can only be on while Time Clock is on. While Time Clock is off, the Technician toggle is off and grayed out, and hovering it shows "Turn on Time Clock first. Only staff who clock in can be technicians." Turning Time Clock off, in the window or any other way (for example through the Public API), turns Technician off.

- **S1-R5:** In Edit Staff Member, turning Time Clock back on leaves Technician off; the user turns it on. In New Staff Member, turning Time Clock on pre-fills Technician from the role (S1-R3), unless the user has already switched Technician by hand.

- **S1-R6:** Switching Technician on or off changes nothing until the user saves the staff member with the window's existing Save. Once saved, the change applies to every technician list (Story 3) the next time each list loads (S1-E3).

- **S1-R7:** A save that turns Technician on never shows a warning. A save that turns it off may show the Turn Off Technician warning first (Story 2).

- **S1-R8:** Saving a change to Technician never logs the staff member out. A save that also changes Time Clock or the role logs them out, as a Time Clock or role change does today.

- **S1-R9:** Billable and Sales Representative work exactly as today. Technician does not change, gray out or require either of them, and neither of them changes Technician.


**Negative Cases:**

- **S1-N1:** When the role picked in the window is the built-in Office User or Time Clock User role, the Technician toggle is off and grayed out, and hovering it shows "This role can't be a technician." Picking one of those roles turns Technician off. In Edit Staff Member, saving a staff member who was saved with Technician on and now has one of those roles shows the Turn Off Technician warning first (Story 2). Any other role, including a custom role made from either built-in role, never grays it out.

- **S1-N2:** If the user does not have the 'Settings → App Settings' setting, they cannot open the staff list or the staff window, as today.

- **S1-N3:** This feature adds no history of who changed Technician or when.


**Edge Cases:**

- **S1-E1:** In Edit Staff Member, changing the staff member's role to any role other than the built-in Office User or Time Clock User role leaves Technician as it is. Picking the built-in Technician or Foreman role does not turn it on; the pre-fill (S1-R3) is for new staff only.

- **S1-E2:** A deactivated staff member keeps their Technician toggle as it was, but is not a technician while deactivated (Story 3). Reactivating them brings it back as it was.

- **S1-E3:** A screen that is already open when a staff member's Technician toggle is saved keeps its lists until it loads them again; nothing is pushed to open screens. A work order's technician dropdowns show the change once the work order is opened again or the page is reloaded; reopening a dropdown on the same page does not. Board View and Tech View show it when opened from the menu, a link or the display switcher, when the filter or location changes, or on reload; coming back with the browser's Back within 30 seconds shows the board as it was. The Assign / Reassign lead technician window shows it every time it opens.

- **S1-E4:** Deactivate Account shows no Turn Off Technician warning. The person keeps their Technician toggle as it was (S1-E2), is not offered as a technician while deactivated, and keeps every work order and line they hold.


**Error Handling:**

- The window never sends Technician on without Time Clock, or for a staff member on the built-in Office User or Time Clock User role. A request from elsewhere (for example a second tab, or a staff window opened before another user changed the record) that would switch Technician from off to on in either case is refused with "A technician must have Time Clock on." or "This role can't be a technician." respectively, shown through the existing request error behavior, and nothing is saved. The Public API cannot set Technician, so it never gets these messages (S5-N1). When both apply (Time Clock off and one of those roles), the message is "This role can't be a technician.", both on hover in the window and in the refusal. A request for a staff member whose Technician is already on that turns Time Clock off, or moves them to one of those roles, is not refused: it saves, and Technician turns off (S1-R4, S1-N1).


### Story 2: Warning before turning Technician off

**As a** shop owner, **I want** to know what a staff member still holds before I turn Technician off **so that** I am not surprised by who stays on existing work.

**Design:** [https://claude.ai/artifact/1fuPz8Y4xTg7Syq1mfmJ9R](https://claude.ai/artifact/1fuPz8Y4xTg7Syq1mfmJ9R), page "4 Turn Technician off with open work"
**Jira:** [SV-11880](https://shopview.atlassian.net/browse/SV-11880)

**Form factor:** Desktop only by rule - the staff window is a settings page, so mobile is not applicable.

**Prerequisites:**

- User must have 'Settings → App Settings' setting enabled.

- The staff member was saved with Technician on, and in Edit Staff Member the user has switched Technician off, switched Time Clock off (which turns Technician off, S1-R4), or picked the built-in Office User or Time Clock User role (which turns Technician off, S1-N1).


**Requirements:**

- **S2-R1:** When the user saves, before anything else, the system counts the staff member's open work across every location of the organization: the work orders they lead and the lines they are on, whether they have labor on the line, were added to it with Add Technician, or are the line's own technician, where the work order is a service work order in Estimate, Approved, In Progress or Ready for Review and the line is not declined. Each work order and each line is counted once. A line on a work order the person also leads still counts, so that job shows as one work order they lead and one line they are on. A person added to a line through a schedule shift is on the line, the same as one added with Add Technician.

- **S2-R2:** When at least one work order or line is counted, a warning window titled "Turn Off Technician" shows. The text names the staff member as last saved (a new name typed in the same save is not used) and what they hold, for example: "Jordan Lee leads 8 open work orders and is on 50 labor lines. They'll stay on them, but won't be offered as a technician on anything new."

- **S2-R3:** The text adapts to the counts. A count of zero leaves its part out, and "it" is used only when the work orders and lines add up to 1. Exact text for each case ([Name] is the staff member's name):

  - 1 work order, no lines: "[Name] leads 1 open work order. They'll stay on it, but won't be offered as a technician on anything new."

  - No work orders, 1 line: "[Name] is on 1 labor line. They'll stay on it, but won't be offered as a technician on anything new."

  - 1 work order, 1 line: "[Name] leads 1 open work order and is on 1 labor line. They'll stay on them, but won't be offered as a technician on anything new."

  - No work orders, 5 lines: "[Name] is on 5 labor lines. They'll stay on them, but won't be offered as a technician on anything new."

  - 3 work orders, no lines: "[Name] leads 3 open work orders. They'll stay on them, but won't be offered as a technician on anything new."


- **S2-R4:** The window has Cancel and Save. Save continues the staff save as in S1-R6. Cancel, the X or Escape close the warning and leave the staff member unsaved, with the staff window still open and the toggle as the user left it; clicking outside the warning does nothing. While the counts load after Save, the Save button shows its loading state and cannot be clicked again. Closing the staff window while the counts load cancels the save: nothing is saved and no warning shows. Save in the warning saves the staff member exactly as the window showed when the warning opened.

- **S2-R5:** Saving with Technician off unassigns nothing. Every lead and labor line stays as it was (Story 3, S3-R7 and S3-R8).


**Negative Cases:**

- **S2-N1:** If no open work order or line is counted, no warning shows and the save goes straight on.

- **S2-N2:** If the save does not turn Technician off (it was already off, or stays on), no warning shows.


**Edge Cases:**

- **S2-E1:** Declined, Complete, Invoiced and Paid work orders, part sales, and declined lines on any work order are never counted.

- **S2-E2:** A save that turns Technician off in more than one way at once (for example Technician and Time Clock both switched off, or Time Clock off and the Office User role picked) shows the warning once.

- **S2-E3:** Turning off Sales Representative still shows its existing removal warning at the moment that toggle is switched off, as today; the Turn Off Technician warning shows only on Save. Cancelling the Turn Off Technician warning cancels the whole save, including a Sales Representative removal already confirmed, and the window stays open as the user left it.


**Error Handling:**

- If the counts cannot be loaded, the save goes ahead without the warning; any error message follows the existing request error behavior. The warning is information, not a guard.


### Story 3: Who appears on the technician lists

**As a** service writer, **I want** the technician lists to offer only technicians **so that** I can assign work and read the board without the office and parts staff in the way.

**Design:** [https://claude.ai/artifact/1fuPz8Y4xTg7Syq1mfmJ9R](https://claude.ai/artifact/1fuPz8Y4xTg7Syq1mfmJ9R), pages "5 Board View and Tech View" and "6 Technician dropdowns on a work order"
**Jira:** [SV-11881](https://shopview.atlassian.net/browse/SV-11881)

**Form factor:** Mobile-optimized - no new controls; the existing screens keep their current mobile behavior and only the names they list change.

**Prerequisites:**

- The user can see and use each screen as today; no permission changes.


**Requirements:**

- **S3-R1:** A person is offered on a technician list at a location when all of these hold: their Technician toggle is on, their Time Clock is on, their user is active, they are enrolled at that location, and their role is not the built-in Office User or Time Clock User role.

- **S3-R2:** Board View gives a column, and Tech View a group, to each person offered by S3-R1, plus everyone who leads work shown on the board (S3-E1, S3-E2).

- **S3-R3:** The Technician filter on Work Orders offers the same people the board gives a column or group to.

- **S3-R4:** The Assign lead technician and Reassign lead technician window (from a card's more-actions menu on Board View or Tech View) offers only people per S3-R1. Searching for anyone else shows the existing No technicians match “[search]” message. A current lead who is not offered per S3-R1 is listed last, cannot be picked, and is labeled "Current · no longer assignable"; searching for their name finds that entry.

- **S3-R5:** The Lead Technician dropdown on a work order offers only people per S3-R1.

- **S3-R6:** Add Technician on a line (in the New Line and Edit Line windows) and the Technician dropdown in the Edit Labor window offer only people per S3-R1.

- **S3-R6a:** Part sales show no technician dropdown, so nothing on screen changes for them.

- **S3-R7:** A work order's current lead who is not offered per S3-R1 still shows as the lead on the work order and in Board View and Tech View. In the Lead Technician dropdown they are listed last, grayed out, with the check mark, and cannot be picked. Picking someone else saves at once, as today, and the former lead then leaves the dropdown.

- **S3-R7a:** If a location has no technicians and the work order has no lead, the Lead Technician field shows as read-only text, the label "Lead technician" with "Unassigned" under it, as it does today whenever that list is empty. If the work order's lead is not a technician, the dropdown still shows (S3-R7), listing "Unassigned" and that lead, grayed out, even when the location has no technicians.

- **S3-R8:** Someone already on a line who is not offered per S3-R1 stays on the line. In the Edit Labor technician list they show grayed out, the same as a deactivated technician today; that list is in name order, so they appear among the others alphabetically, and they cannot be unticked there. In Edit Line they show as an ordinary chip that can be removed with its X, unless they have logged time on the line, in which case the chip shows the existing lock and "This technician has logged time on the line and cannot be removed.", as today. Saving a line's technicians in Edit Line keeps a person who is no longer a technician on the line unless the user removed them. As today, saving also drops a person from the line who has a labor entry on the work order but is not in the location's Time Clock list (deactivated, Time Clock off, on the built-in Office User or Time Clock User role, or not enrolled at the location) and has no time logged on that line; such a person who is on the line only through Add Technician, with no labor entry, stays.

- **S3-R9:** Lists that are not technician lists keep everyone with Time Clock on except the Office User and Time Clock User roles, as today: time sheets and the time sheet dialogs, Punch Clock Activities, the Technician Efficiency and Technician Utilization reports, and the schedule (which follows departments).

- **S3-R10:** When Board View or Tech View has no technician column or group at all (no technician at the location, no column kept for a non-technician lead under S3-E1 and no pinned column under S3-E2), and neither Assigned to me nor a Technician filter is on, it shows the title "No technicians yet" with the text "Turn Technician on for your technicians in Staff to assign work orders. Until then, every work order stays in Unassigned." A Technician filter can be on at such a location only through its Unassigned or Deleted technician choices: people picked at another location are dropped from the filter when the location changes (S3-N1). When the open tab has no work orders either, the "Nothing here yet" state keeps its title, and its text reads "Turn Technician on for your technicians and create your first work order to track work by tech." The button the Board View and Tech View PRD (S1-N1d, SV-11555) calls Add Technician reads "Set Up Technicians" and opens the staff list (Settings, Staff), for the same users who see it today. This replaces S1-N1d's text and button target once this feature ships.


**Negative Cases:**

- **S3-N1:** A person with Technician off, or with Time Clock off, gets no Board View column, no Tech View group, and is not offered in the Technician filter or any technician dropdown, unless S3-E1 or S3-E2 applies. A person already picked in the Technician filter who is no longer offered (Technician turned off, or a person from another location) is dropped from the filter the next time the board loads, as if never picked. Their work orders do not move.

- **S3-N2:** A person on the built-in Office User or Time Clock User role is never offered, as today.

- **S3-N3:** Technician narrows only what is offered. A non-technician who is made lead or added to a labor line another way (an outside tool through the Public API, or a screen opened before the change) is accepted as today, and so is job time they record (S3-E3).


**Edge Cases:**

- **S3-E1:** Someone who is not a technician but still leads a work order shown on the board keeps a column (Board View) or group (Tech View) for that work until it is reassigned or closed.

- **S3-E2:** Someone who is not a technician but whose column the user pinned on Board View keeps the pinned column at the locations they are enrolled at, until the user unpins it. Dropping a work order on that column is refused with "This technician can't be assigned work orders."

- **S3-E3:** A person who is not a technician can still press Start on an approved line and record time, as today; doing so adds them to the line's labor as today.


**Error Handling:**

- No new failure paths: each list loads and fails as it does today. If only the list of technicians fails to load on a work order, the existing request error behavior applies, the technician pickers keep what they had (empty on first load) and anyone already assigned shows grayed out; the rest of the work order still loads.

**Form factor:** Desktop only by rule - the staff list is a settings page, so mobile is not applicable.

**Prerequisites:**

- User must have 'Settings → App Settings' setting enabled.


**Requirements:**

- **S4-R1:** The staff list (Settings, Staff) gains a read-only "Technician" column directly left of Billable, on the Active and Deactivated tabs. Each row shows "Yes" (green) when the person is active, their Technician toggle is on and their Time Clock is on, otherwise "No" (red), in the same style as the Billable column. A deactivated person always shows "No", whatever their toggles.

- **S4-R1a:** The column does not depend on location: a person can show "Yes" without being enrolled at the location the user is viewing.

- **S4-R2:** The Technician column sorts like the other columns.

- **S4-R3:** A "New" pill sits at the top right of the "Technician" column header, touching the end of the word and inside the column, clear of the Billable header. It follows the same end date as S1-R2.

- **S4-R4:** A notice shows on the staff list, below the page toolbar and above the Active and Deactivated tabs, including when the list fails to load (the existing "Something went wrong loading the staff list. Please try again." line then sits between the toolbar and the notice), with this text: "New: Decide who appears on Board View, Tech View and the technician dropdowns. Turn Technician on or off for each person in their staff settings, next to Sales Representative. To start, it is on for everyone who worked on a work order in the last 90 days, is on open work, or is billable."

- **S4-R5:** The notice cannot be closed. It disappears for everyone at the same moment as the "New" pill (S1-R2), including in a page that is already open.


**Negative Cases:**

- **S4-N2:** After the end date, neither the notice nor the pills show anywhere.

**Error Handling:**

### Story 5: Public API - technician value on staff

**As a** shop using the Public API, **I want** staff records to say whether each person is a technician **so that** my integration can list the same technicians as ShopView.

**Design:** no new screens
**Jira:** [SV-11883](https://shopview.atlassian.net/browse/SV-11883)

**Trigger:** When an integration reads staff through the Public API, after a staff member's Technician toggle (Story 1, S1-R6) or Time Clock has changed.

**Prerequisites:**

- The integration has Public API access to staff, as today.


**Requirements:**

- **S5-R1:** Each staff record the Public API returns includes a read-only technician value: true when the person is active, their Technician toggle is on and their Time Clock is on (the same rule as the staff list column, S4-R1), otherwise false. A deactivated person is always false.

- **S5-R2:** The Public API changelog announces the new value.


**Negative Cases:**

- **S5-N1:** The technician value cannot be set through the Public API. A staff update that includes it succeeds as today and ignores that value; the record returned carries the value worked out by S5-R1.


**Edge Cases:**

- **S5-E1:** The value is the result of S5-R1, not the toggle alone: a deactivated person whose toggle is on reads false.


**Error Handling:**

- No new failure paths.


### Story 6: Release day

**As a** shop owner, **I want** ShopView to keep my current technicians on release day **so that** nobody disappears from the board or the dropdowns before I have looked at the new toggle.

**Design:** [https://claude.ai/artifact/1fuPz8Y4xTg7Syq1mfmJ9R](https://claude.ai/artifact/1fuPz8Y4xTg7Syq1mfmJ9R), page "1 Staff list" (the notice)
**Jira:** [SV-11884](https://shopview.atlassian.net/browse/SV-11884)

**Trigger:** The release that ships this feature, once.

**Prerequisites:**

- None: it runs for every organization.


**Requirements:**

- **S6-R1:** Technician is turned on for each staff member whose user is active, whose Time Clock is on and who has a role other than the built-in Office User or Time Clock User role, when they meet any of these, at any location of their organization:

  - (a) has a time entry on a work order, part sales included, whose start is no more than 90 days (to the second) before the moment the release step runs, or later, including manual entries made by someone else (time on a department does not count);

  - (b) leads, has labor on, or is on a line that is not declined (with labor on it, added with Add Technician or through a schedule shift, or as the line's own technician) of, a service work order that is Approved, In Progress or Ready for Review;

  - (c) has Billable on.


- **S6-R2:** Every other staff member starts with Technician off.

- **S6-R3:** Nothing else on any staff record changes: Time Clock, Billable, Sales Representative and roles stay as they are. A staff member turned on by the step is marked as updated at that moment, so software connected through the Public API that asks which staff changed picks up the new technician value on its next check (S5-R1). Staff left off are not marked.

- **S6-R4:** Nobody is logged out by the release step (S1-R8).

- **S6-R5:** Open work is untouched: every lead and labor line stays as it was, so S3-R7 and S3-R8 cover anyone who is no longer offered.


**Negative Cases:**

- **S6-N1:** A staff member who meets none of S6-R1 (for example a Parts Manager who never works on work orders and is not billable) starts with Technician off and leaves the technician lists.

- **S6-N2:** A staff member with no role is never turned on, whatever they meet, matching who the board offers today. In practice this only affects Public API sandbox staff.


**Edge Cases:**

- **S6-E1:** A person who appeared as a technician before release but starts with Technician off keeps every work order and line they hold (S3-E1, S3-R7, S3-R8).

- **S6-E2:** Estimates, declined work orders, declined lines and part sales do not count toward S6-R1 (b).

- **S6-E3:** A staff member who is deactivated, or has Time Clock off, at the moment the step runs starts with Technician off. Turning Time Clock on later does not turn Technician on (S1-R5). A seasonal technician who is deactivated on release day and reactivated later is not offered on any technician list until the shop turns Technician on for them.

- **S6-E4:** The step runs once. A shop that turns Technician off for someone after release keeps it off.


**Error Handling:**

- No user-facing failure path.
