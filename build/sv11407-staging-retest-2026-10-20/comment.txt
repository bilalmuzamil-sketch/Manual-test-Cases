*How to see it yourself*
# Open Staging: https://app.staging.shopview.com and sign in with the *Tech* user (the "Tech" button on the staging login page).
# In the top menu click {{Work Orders}}, then the {{All}} tab (top left of the page).
# On the right of the bar above the list there are three view buttons. Click the right one, {{Board View}}.
# Scroll the {{Unassigned}} column (the first column) until its top card is half hidden under the column's top edge.
# Click that card's work order number (for example {{S2-32218}}). The work order opens.
# Press the browser's *Back* button: the card is back at the top of the Unassigned column, fully shown.
# Click the middle view button, {{Tech View}}, scroll until the top row is half hidden under the grey {{Unassigned}} header, open a work order from a row and press *Back*: the row is back at the top, fully shown, below the header.
