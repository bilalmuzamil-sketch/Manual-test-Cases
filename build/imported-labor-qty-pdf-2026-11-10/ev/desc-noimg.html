Found while testing SV-12435

Summary

On an imported invoice, the Quantity of a labor or sublet line is right on screen but wrong in the downloaded PDF. The PDF shows the hours multiplied by 60 (the minutes): 2.5 hours prints as 150.00, 3 hours as 180.00. Part quantities are fine. A customer who receives the PDF sees 150.00 × $40.00 = $100.00, which does not add up.

Where it happens: Reproducible on production, and also on the QA environment it was found on: sv12435.qa.shopview.com. It was not caused by the SV-12435 fix (that fix only changed colours on the screen).

Steps to reproduce (production)

  1. Log in to app.shopview.com. Check that the location at the top right says Trucks Hill 2 (change it there if not).
  2. In the top menu click Work Orders and stay on the All tab.
  3. On the right of the filter bar, click the list view button (three lines with dots, box 1 in Picture 1).
  4. Click Status, tick only Imported, then click anywhere outside the menu. The button now reads Status: Imported (box 2).
  5. Click the row ZZQTYPDF-P (customer aqeel transport 56, box 3). The imported invoice opens.
  6. Look at the Quantity column on screen: Tow in 3.00, Brake inspection 2.50, Brake pad 4. These match the import file.
  7. At the top right of the invoice click the Download button (arrow pointing down, box A in Picture 2) and open the PDF.
  8. In the PDF the Quantity column shows Tow in 180.00, Brake inspection 150.00, Brake pad 4.00. The Rate and Amount columns are the same as on screen.

Fastest way to see it: open this imported invoice on production, then click the Download button at the top right and compare the Quantity column with the screen.

Expected behaviour

The PDF shows the same quantity as the screen and the import file (2.50, not 150.00). Source: History Import - Overview: “All values import exactly as they appear in the file.” and History Import - Internal Training §9.1 Labor Rows: “Store Line Title, Line Description, Tech Story, Qty, Rate, Total, Tax.” (§9.5 Sublet: “Behaves like Labor rows.”)

@Milos Vasic @Chris Ward please confirm: should the PDF be fixed to show the hours, or should it stay as it is?
