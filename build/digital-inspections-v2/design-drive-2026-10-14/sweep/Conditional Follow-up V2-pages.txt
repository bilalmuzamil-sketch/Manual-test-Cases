SV-8181 · DIGITAL INSPECTIONS V2 · CONDITIONAL FOLLOW-UP
A follow-up attached to one response

The reference file on a checkbox field is unconditional: the technician sees it whatever they answer. A follow-up is the same content, text plus one optional file, tied to a single response and shown only when that response is given. The technician reads it on the card as an instruction; the builder calls it a follow-up, because that is what the author is adding. Acknowledgement is offered on Monitor and Not OK only, on by default, and the author can turn it off. Follow-ups on OK and N/A are read-only and never need acknowledging. Where it is on, the inspection cannot be submitted until the technician has confirmed they read it. It carries no colour of its own. The blue already used for the reference file marks it as something to read, and the acknowledgement pill takes the colour of the response that triggered it, so red and amber keep meaning what they mean. Per axle fields do not carry follow-ups: a verdict there belongs to a position, and a follow-up would have no single position to sit on.

INSTRUCTION
Required

Before you touch the chamber, chock the wheels, cage the spring brake on that wheel end and drain the wet tank until the gauge reads zero. Do not crack the supply line while the system is charged.

Tag the unit out of service before it leaves the bay and tell the foreman in person, not over the radio. If the foreman is not on site, call the service advisor and write the time of the call in the note.

Record the chamber part number and the date code from the canister tag. Photograph the clamp band from both sides, the push-rod at rest and the push-rod at full stroke, and put all three on this field.

If the leak is at the push-rod seal rather than the clamp band, check the slack adjuster travel on the same wheel end and measure the lining at the same time, because a seal that has been weeping will usually have taken the shoe with it.

Replace the chamber as an assembly. Do not reuse the clamp band, the mounting nuts or the push-rod yoke pin, and torque the mounting nuts to the value on the axle decal, not the generic value in the manual.

Do not release the unit for road test until the replacement chamber is fitted, the system has held 90 psi for five minutes with the engine off, and the brake has been stroked ten times with no drop beyond the allowance in the attached procedure.

If any of the above cannot be completed on this visit, answer Not OK, say in the note exactly what is outstanding, and leave the unit tagged.
