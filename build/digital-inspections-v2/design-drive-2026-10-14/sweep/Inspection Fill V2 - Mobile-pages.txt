SV-8181 · DIGITAL INSPECTIONS V2 · MOBILE ARTBOARDS
Fill screen: phone, 402 px

The footer is section navigation and nothing else, Back: Section 1 and Next: Section 2, naming where each goes. Axle switching happens only in the sub-header chips, which swap the page to that axle. What is outstanding rides on the action as a count badge, and tapping it lists the items, each one going straight to the field, not to a summary. Verdict dots are gone: the field is already coloured. A tire takes the worst verdict entered for it so far and stays grey until one arrives. Marking OK in bulk exists at the same three levels as on desktop: the app-bar menu for the whole inspection, a 44 px row at the foot of the section header for the section, and the axle-set card header for one field. OK only, verdicts only, and it never overwrites a verdict already picked.
