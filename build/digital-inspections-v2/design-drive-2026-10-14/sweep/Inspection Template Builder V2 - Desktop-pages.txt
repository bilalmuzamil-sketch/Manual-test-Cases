SV-8181 · DIGITAL INSPECTIONS V2 · TEMPLATE BUILDER, FULL SCREENS
Template builder: whole screens

Whole screens, so each state can be read in place. Three changes: a measurement row is called what it is; the unit left the name and became a light Defaults line between the name and its options, costing one line of height; every field has a general reference file that is always available, with its accepted types stated before the upload, and Monitor and Not OK responses can each carry a follow-up, an instruction with text, a file or both; and one rule arrives on for new templates: a note when Monitor or Not OK; the photo rules are opt-in. Renaming happens on the field, with a check to keep it and an X to drop it. TB7 is Preview mode: the template rendered as the technician gets it, in the canvas, without publishing it first.
