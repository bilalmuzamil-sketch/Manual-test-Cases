SV-8181 · DIGITAL INSPECTIONS V2 · INSPECTION → WORK ORDER
The hand-off

Building work order lines from an inspection is a ShopCoach capability, so the same completed inspection is two different screens. Without ShopCoach the inspection reports what is wrong and a human builds the work order; no build action anywhere, not disabled and not explained. With ShopCoach the action is purple and carries the AI badge, so nothing else needs saying. Pressing it asks nothing: the technician lands on the work order with the lines already proposed and pre-selected, reviewing rather than instructing. The same action and the same panel appear wherever a finished inspection is met, on the inspection itself, on the work order’s Notes tab, in the unit’s Inspections list, and started from the work order with no inspection at all.
