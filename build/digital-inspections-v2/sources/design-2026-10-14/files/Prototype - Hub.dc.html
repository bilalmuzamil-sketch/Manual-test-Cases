Digital Inspections V2

Three clickable flows, in the order a template is used. Each has a Desktop and Phone switch at the top. Data is fixed per flow: builder edits do not carry into the fill screen.

1 Template builder Add checkbox and per axle fields, rename responses, turn on follow-ups and acknowledgement, set validation, then switch to Preview. 2 Filling an inspection Verdict per tire position across axles, single and dual, Mark OK. Checkbox follow-ups with acknowledgement, the outstanding list, and submit. 3 Inspection → work order The completed inspection, with ShopCoach on or off. Build lines, review the drafts, add them. Work order Notes and the asset's Inspections tab.
