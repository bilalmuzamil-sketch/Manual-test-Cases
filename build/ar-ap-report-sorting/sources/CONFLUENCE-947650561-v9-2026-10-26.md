## 1. Business Case

Shops reconcile ShopView's six A/R and A/P aging reports against QuickBooks, but today the A/R reports come out in no set order, no column can be sorted, and the A/R print reproduces that same unsorted list, so neither the screen nor the paper copy can be checked line by line against QuickBooks. There is no way to get the reports into a spreadsheet at all, the A/P reports cannot be printed, and multi-location shops cannot see one location's aging without adding it up by hand. Fast Truck and Trailer raised this twice (SV-10798, SV-7192) and pressed Cody for it as a large client; BRITE Truck Maintenance (SV-6366), Skyline Diesel (SV-6037) and Squires Industrial (SV-6849) asked for parts of the same thing. This PRD makes all six reports open alphabetically by customer or vendor, sortable on every column, exportable to PDF and CSV in the order on screen, narrowable by location, and easier to read; who can see these reports, and the organization-wide numbers they show, do not change.

### Workarounds

- **Copying report rows into a spreadsheet by hand**: office and accounting staff copy rows off the screen to sort, filter or share them; BRITE Truck Maintenance reported copying from work order lists to get A/P data out (SV-6366), and PowerTools insight \#189565 records the same copy-paste workaround. It costs time at every month-end and invites transcription errors.

- **Re-sorting the printed A/R list against QuickBooks**: Fast Truck and Trailer's staff could not match the printed A/R to QuickBooks because the print came out in database order (SV-7192), so the paper copy has to be matched to QuickBooks by hand.

- **Adding up each location's aging separately**: multi-location shops such as Squires Industrial (SV-6849) and the shops behind insights \#166344 (Fort Lauderdale and Tampa Bay) and \#98003 cannot isolate one branch, so managers split the combined totals outside ShopView.

## 2. Feature Overview

**Core: the six aging reports** (A/R Summary, A/R Detail, A/R Collection; A/P Summary, A/P Detail, A/P Unpaid Invoices, under Reports \> Finance)

- Every report opens in customer or vendor name order, A to Z, the way QuickBooks orders names.

- Every column header sorts; the first click gives the useful order for that kind of data, the second reverses it.

- Every report has an Export menu with PDF and CSV; both follow the exact order and filters on screen. The A/P reports gain a PDF for the first time.

- The grouped reports gain Collapse all / Expand all, and the group row in view stays pinned at the top while scrolling.

- A Location filter narrows any report to one or more locations; it opens with every location ticked (All locations), so an untouched report is identical to today.

- Every report shows a Location column when more than one location is in view.

- Every report gains a Customer filter (A/R) or Vendor filter (A/P), where several can be ticked, and a Past due filter by aging range; the Totals row always adds up exactly the rows the filters leave.

- Invoice and bill numbers link only when the user can actually open them; an invoice from another location the user works at asks to switch location first. Vendor credits link to the vendor return they came from.

- A/R Collection shows each customer's phone and, for users who manage A/R and A/P data, their credit term, so it works as a call list.

- Each report title has an info icon that explains what the report includes.

**What changes for existing users**

- A/R reports no longer come out in an unpredictable order; A/R Summary and A/R Collection no longer repeat or skip customers between pages.

- A/P Detail opens with rows in vendor name order inside each bucket (today: most overdue first).

- A/R's Print button becomes the Export menu; its PDF prints the on-screen order and the as-of date the user picked (today it can print the next day's date).

- The Totals row on A/R Collection and A/P Unpaid Invoices now follows the customer or vendor filter; today it ignores it, on production too.

- The A/R Summary Totals row now adds up exactly the customer rows above it. Today it also counts overpaid invoices (an invoice paid for more than its amount), which the rows don't, so for those customers the Totals don't match the rows. Production has 16 such invoices across 13 customer accounts; their A/R Summary Totals will differ slightly from today. The rows themselves don't change.

- Clicking a row no longer opens it. The document number, or the customer or vendor name on the Summaries, is the link, as on the newer reports (Story 7).

- The date filter is labelled "As of" (was "Date"). On A/R Collection and A/P Unpaid Invoices the Customer or Vendor chip now comes after it.

- Re-sorting A/R Collection or A/P Unpaid Invoices no longer collapses the open groups.

- The A/R column "Location full name" and the A/P column "Location Name" are renamed "Location".

- The A/R column headings change to Title Case, as on the A/P reports and the other reports: "Transaction Type", "Customer Full Name", "Due Date", "Past Due", "Open Balance".

- Which rows and amounts each user can see is unchanged: an untouched report shows exactly today's organization-wide numbers (apart from the Totals rows noted above), and the reports show money exactly as they do today.

**Out of Scope**

- **Background exports for very large reports** ("we'll email you when it's ready"), as Sage Intacct does. In this release, over the export limits the user is told to choose an earlier as-of date. Background exports come as a later phase of this epic (SV-11445): over the limit, the export is prepared in the background and the user gets an in-app notification with a download link. That phase is designed and specified before it is planned.

- **Restricting these reports by the user's location enrollment.** The reports stay organization-wide for everyone who can open them today; the Location filter only narrows. Any enrollment-based restriction would be its own announced permissions change.

- **Renaming columns other than Location.** Only the capitals change (S2-R4a): A/R keeps "ID" and "Customer Full Name", A/P keeps "No." and "Vendor Name".

- **Other improvements found in research,** each a separate ticket on SV-11315: a credit dated on the as-of date landing in 1-30 instead of Current (SV-11441, checked against QuickBooks first); configurable aging periods (SV-11442); send a statement from the report (SV-11443); salesperson and Parts vs Service filters (SV-11444); last payment date and credit limit (SV-11446); a collections notes log (SV-11447).

- **Email and a primary contact on the A/R Collection call list.** A customer has no primary contact in ShopView today; the call list shows the phone and credit term only.

- **Links for A/R credit memos and deposits.** They have no page of their own to open, so they stay plain text.

- **A/P print from a separate print button.** A/P printing is delivered through the Export menu's PDF (SV-6163).

- **The location switcher in the top right of ShopView.** It is not changed. Switching location while on one of these reports reloads it, as on every other report: the filters and the rows stay, because the reports are organization-wide; the sort returns to the opening order, groups collapse and the page returns to the top; which documents link, prompt or show as plain text updates (Story 7).

- **A phone or tablet layout.** Narrower than 1024 pixels, the reports show the native mobile filter row and the table scrolls sideways; nothing else is designed for small screens in this release.

## 3. Jobs to be Done / Goals

> **When** I reconcile ShopView's A/R or A/P against QuickBooks at month-end, **I want to** see and print customers and vendors in the same order QuickBooks uses, **so I can** check them line by line without searching.

> **When** I chase overdue accounts or plan vendor payments, **I want to** sort by any column (most overdue, largest balance), **so I can** work the list in priority order.

> **When** I need the aging outside ShopView, **I want to** download it as a spreadsheet or PDF in the order I'm looking at, **so I can** filter, share or file it without retyping.

> **When** I manage one branch of a multi-location shop, **I want to** narrow the aging to my location, **so I can** follow up on my own customers and bills.

Goals:

- A user can open any of the six reports and find a customer or vendor by scanning A to Z, with the same order on screen, in the PDF and in the CSV.

- A user can sort every column in one click and reverse it with a second.

- A user can export each report to PDF and CSV; A/P can be printed for the first time.

- A user can narrow each report to chosen locations, and an untouched report shows exactly today's organization-wide numbers.

- Nobody gains or loses access to any data, and no setup is needed.

## 4. Key Decisions

- **Names sort the way QuickBooks sorts them: plain character order, capitals ignored, accented letters with their base letter.** "10 Trucking" comes before "2 Trucking"; "Émile's" sorts with the E names. **Rationale:** shops reconcile against QuickBooks, and Chris asked to "match quickbooks"; Intuit's own advice for numbered names is zero-padding, which confirms QuickBooks does not sort numbers by value. Accented letters fold to their base letter because users type them as plain letters on a North American keyboard. **Rejected:** number-aware name order (the 2026-10-09 Default Sorting rule), which would make ShopView disagree with QuickBooks. *Basis:* `d-follow-incumbent-convention`.

- **Document numbers (ID / No.) sort by number value.** S1-99 comes before S1-100. **Rationale:** people count document numbers, they don't spell them. **Rejected:** plain character order for numbers, which scrambles them.

- **The third click on a header repeats the first; there is no unsorted state.** **Rationale:** matches the app-wide header rule decided 2026-10-13. **Rejected:** a third click that returns to the opening order.

- **Groups never move; header clicks sort the rows inside each group.** Aging buckets stay in their fixed order and customer or vendor groups stay A to Z. **Rationale:** shops read aging by bucket and by account; moving groups breaks that, and it keeps paging stable. **Rejected:** letting the name or total header reorder the groups.

- **The sort is held while the user stays on the report and is not remembered between visits.** **Rationale:** every visit opens on the QuickBooks order shops reconcile against, with no "why is my report in a weird order" support calls. **Rejected:** remembering the sort per user.

- **Exports follow the screen, with a PDF limit of 2,000 rows and a CSV limit of 50,000 rows.** **Rationale:** industry research: QuickBooks Online caps PDF exports at 50,000 cells and sends large reports to Excel (about 44,000 rows of a 9-column aging report); NetSuite caps emailed CSV at 10,000 rows; Sage Intacct runs large reports in the background. Timing on real data: a 1,000-row A/P Detail PDF takes 13 seconds, 3,000 rows 44 seconds, and 5,000 rows did not finish, so the PDF limit protects the server; CSV is cheap, so its limit sits near QuickBooks' Excel ceiling. **Rejected:** the app-wide 10,000-row CSV cap (a shop with 13,540 open A/P lines could not export A/P Detail at all); background exports in this release (they delay Fast Truck's fix).

- **The Location filter only narrows; it never restricts.** It lists every location in the report's own data and opens with every location ticked. **Rationale:** these reports are organization-wide today for everyone who can open them; adopting the standard filter's enrollment scoping would silently take data away from users who see it now, and A/R totals would stop matching QuickBooks. **Rejected:** opening on the user's current location, and limiting the list to enrolled locations, as the other new reports do.

- **Past due narrows documents on the transaction reports and accounts on the Summaries.** On A/R Detail, A/R Collection, A/P Detail and A/P Unpaid Invoices the ticked ranges keep only the invoices and bills in them and always keep credit memos, deposits and vendor credits. On A/R Summary and A/P Summary they list only the customers or vendors with some invoice or bill amount in a ticked range, at their full balances. **Rationale:** credits still reduce what a customer owes, so hiding them would overstate the balance a collector calls about; a Summary row showing only part of a balance would no longer match QuickBooks. **Rejected:** hiding credits; showing only the overdue part of each balance on the Summaries.

- **The Totals row adds up exactly the rows the filters leave.** **Rationale:** a total that ignores a filter contradicts the rows above it; on A/R Collection and A/P Unpaid Invoices it ignored the customer or vendor filter, on production too, and this release fixes it. **Rejected:** leaving that Totals row as it is for a follow-up.

- **The reports stay organization-wide, by decision.** Everyone with access sees every location's data, as today; the Location filter only narrows. **Rationale:** no permission changes in this release (decided 2026-10-15). **Rejected:** restricting the reports to the locations a user is enrolled in, which would be its own announced permissions change later.

- **A row belongs to the location of its own open document** (the invoice, credit memo or deposit; the bill or vendor credit), the same location the Location column shows. **Rationale:** the aging reports list open documents, not payments, so a partly paid invoice keeps its own location and the filter never contradicts the column. **Rejected:** splitting a balance by where each payment was taken.

- **Documents link only when the user can open them, in the right location.** **Rationale:** today every document the user has the permission for links, at any location, and other-location documents dead-end on an error (SV-5706). **Rejected:** keeping every link; making every other-location document plain text even where the user works.

- **The reuse rule.** Every control on these reports reuses an existing ShopView pattern: the native filter chips, the native totals row and pinned headline column of the newer reports, the existing location-switch prompt, the design system's chevron icon. Nothing new is drawn. *Basis:* `d-reuse-existing-pattern`.

- **Drill-down keeps the report's scope.** Clicking a customer or vendor on a Summary opens Collection or Unpaid Invoices with the same as-of date and the same chosen locations. *Basis:* `d-drill-in-inherits-scope`.

- **Permissions.** Viewing all six reports, and exporting them, is governed by 'Reports → Page Access', unchanged. No new permission is introduced and no existing permission changes. Opening a document from a report uses the permission that already opens that document (see Story 7). The credit term on the A/R Collection call list follows the customer card's rule: only users with the 'View and Manage AP/AR Data' switch see it, on screen, in the PDF and in the CSV (Story 9). Amounts show to everyone who can open the reports, as today and as on Sales By Customer and Inventory Value; 'See Financial Data' does not hide them.

## 5. Terminology

- **Vocabulary.** This PRD uses *location* and *customer* / *vendor*. A/R is about customers, A/P about vendors.

- **Open document.** A row the aging reports age: an unpaid or partly paid invoice, an unused credit memo or deposit (A/R); an unpaid bill or unused vendor credit (A/P).

- **Headline column.** The last money column of a report, the one it totals: Open Balance on A/R Detail, A/R Collection, A/P Detail and A/P Unpaid Invoices; Total on A/R Summary and A/P Summary.

- **Grouped reports.** A/R Detail and A/P Detail (grouped by aging bucket), A/R Collection (grouped by customer), A/P Unpaid Invoices (grouped by vendor).

## 6. Assumptions

- A shop's A/P Detail can carry well over 10,000 open lines (the QA organization has 13,540 on one as-of date and 437 vendors); A/R Summary in the same organization has 46 customers.

- Multi-location shops commonly have the same customer or vendor with open documents at two or more locations.

## 7. Requirements

### Story 1: Reports open in QuickBooks name order

As an office or accounting user, I want each report to open in customer or vendor name order so that I can reconcile it against QuickBooks line by line.

**Design:** [A/R and A/P Report Sorting design canvas](https://claude.ai/artifact/BRYCdjKwGRSgGPC9xxkgF4), page 1 (Opening order and header sorting)

**Jira:** [SV-11531](https://shopview.atlassian.net/browse/SV-11531)

**Form factor:** Desktop only by rule: these are detailed reports.

**Prerequisites:**

- User must have 'Reports → Page Access' setting enabled.

**Requirements:**

- **S1-R1:** A/R Summary opens with customers in name order, A to Z; A/P Summary opens with vendors in name order, A to Z.

- **S1-R2:** Name order compares names ignoring capitals, in plain character order: digits and punctuation compare as characters, so "10 Trucking" comes before "2 Trucking" and "A-1 Towing" before "a1 towing". Names that start with a punctuation mark follow the same order: "#1 Diesel", "-1 Diesel", "10 Trucking", "@Home Towing".

- **S1-R3:** Accented letters sort with their base letter: "Émile's" sorts among the E names (é è ê ë sort as e, á à â ä ã å as a, ç as c, ñ as n, ö ó ò ô õ ø as o, ü ú ù û as u, í ì î ï as i, ý ÿ as y, ß as ss, æ as ae, œ as oe, in both cases).

- **S1-R4:** Two names that compare equal are ordered by the plain lower-cased name, then by the customer or vendor itself, so the same two customers always come out in the same order on screen, in the PDF and in the CSV. For example, "Emile's Towing" comes before "Émile's Towing": the accent folds for the name order (S1-R3), so the two tie, and in the plain lower-cased name the unaccented letter comes first.

- **S1-R5:** A/R Detail and A/P Detail keep their aging buckets in this fixed order: "91 or more days past due", "61-90 days past due", "31-60 days past due", "1-30 days past due", "Current". Inside each bucket, rows open in customer or vendor name order (S1-R2 to S1-R4), ties by document number (S2-R4).

- **S1-R6:** A/R Collection groups by customer and A/P Unpaid Invoices by vendor, groups in name order (S1-R2 to S1-R4); inside each group, rows open oldest date first; rows on the same date, whatever the time of day they were created, go in document-number order (S2-R4).

- **S1-R7:** The column the report opened on is marked as sorted: the name column on the Summaries and Detail reports, the Date column on Collection and Unpaid Invoices.

- **S1-R8:** A/R Summary, A/P Summary, A/R Collection and A/P Unpaid Invoices show 30 customers or vendors at a time, and the next 30 load as the user scrolls down, as today. There are no page buttons. A/R Detail and A/P Detail show each bucket's first 100 lines, then load more as the user scrolls (S4-R4). Paging never repeats or skips a customer, vendor or row, in any sort.

**Negative Cases:**

- **S1-N1:** If the user lacks 'Reports → Page Access', the reports are not available, as today.

**Edge Cases:**

- **S1-E1:** A customer with no open documents on the as-of date does not appear, as today.

### Story 2: Sort by clicking any column header

As a user working the aging, I want to sort by any column in one click so that I can work the list in priority order.

**Design:** [A/R and A/P Report Sorting design canvas](https://claude.ai/artifact/BRYCdjKwGRSgGPC9xxkgF4), page 1 (Opening order and header sorting)

**Jira:** [SV-11532](https://shopview.atlassian.net/browse/SV-11532)

**Form factor:** Desktop only by rule: detailed reports.

**Prerequisites:**

- User must have 'Reports → Page Access' setting enabled.

**Requirements:**

- **S2-R1:** Every column on all six reports sorts when its header is clicked, except A/P Unpaid Invoices' Vendor Name column (S2-N1). The columns are:

  - A/R Summary and A/P Summary: the name column, Location (when shown), Current, 1-30 Days, 31-60 Days, 61-90 Days, 91 And Over, Total.

  - A/R Detail: Date, Location (when shown), Transaction Type, ID, Customer Full Name, Due Date, Amount, Open Balance.

  - A/R Collection: Date, Location (when shown), Transaction Type, ID, Due Date, Past Due, Amount, Open Balance.

  - A/P Detail and A/P Unpaid Invoices: Date, Location (when shown), Transaction Type, No., Vendor Name, Due Date, Past Due, Amount, Open Balance.

- **S2-R2:** The first click gives the useful order for the kind of data: text (names, Location, Transaction Type) A to Z; money columns largest first; dates oldest first; Past Due largest first; ID and No. smallest number first (S2-R4). The second click reverses it. The third click repeats the first; there is no unsorted state.

- **S2-R3:** A header that is already the sorted column reverses on its first click.

- **S2-R4:** ID and No. sort by number value: S1-99 before S1-100 before S1-101. The first click puts the smallest number first and the second click the largest first. The same number-aware order breaks ties in every sort, always smallest document number first: reversing a sort reverses the sorted column only, so tied rows keep that order in both directions. A date column compares the day only, never the time of day, so two rows on the same date are a tie.

- **S2-R4a:** Column headings are in Title Case on all six reports, on screen, in the PDF and in the CSV, as on the other reports (Sales By Customer, Inventory Value, Parts Velocity): the A/R reports read "Transaction Type", "Customer Full Name", "Due Date", "Past Due" and "Open Balance", the same as the A/P reports.

- **S2-R5:** On the grouped reports, a header click sorts the rows inside each group; the groups themselves never move (S1-R5, S1-R6).

- **S2-R6:** A blank value (for example a vendor credit on A/P Unpaid Invoices, which has no due date and no Past Due) sorts to the bottom of its group in both directions. On A/P Detail a vendor credit shows its own date as its due date, as today, so it is not blank there.

- **S2-R7:** The sort is kept while the user stays on the report: changing the as-of date, loading more rows by scrolling, and changing the customer, vendor, Past due or Location filter keep it.

- **S2-R8:** The sort is not remembered between visits: opening the report again starts from Story 1's order.

- **S2-R9:** Re-sorting never changes which groups are expanded or collapsed (Story 4).

**Negative Cases:**

- **S2-N1:** On A/P Unpaid Invoices, the Vendor Name header is not clickable, because every group holds a single vendor and sorting by it could never change the order.

**Edge Cases:**

- **S2-E1:** A column where every row has the same value (for example Location when each customer has one location) shows no visible change; the tie-break (S2-R4) still applies.

### Story 3: Export to PDF and CSV

As a user, I want to download any of the six reports as a PDF or a CSV in the order on screen so that I can file, print, share or analyze it outside ShopView.

**Design:** [A/R and A/P Report Sorting design canvas](https://claude.ai/artifact/BRYCdjKwGRSgGPC9xxkgF4), page 2 (Export)

**Jira:** [SV-11533](https://shopview.atlassian.net/browse/SV-11533)

**Form factor:** Desktop only by rule: detailed reports.

**Prerequisites:**

- User must have 'Reports → Page Access' setting enabled.

**Requirements:**

- **S3-R1:** Each of the six reports has the native report Export menu (the ⋯ button at the right of the title row, labelled "Export report") with "Download (PDF)" and "Download (CSV)". A/R's former Print button is replaced by this menu. The A/P reports gain a PDF.

- **S3-R2:** The PDF downloads as a file.

- **S3-R3:** The PDF and the CSV contain exactly the rows on screen for the chosen as-of date, customer or vendor and locations, in the on-screen sort, including a sort changed after an earlier export, without refreshing the page.

- **S3-R4:** Each export states the as-of date the user picked: the PDF "As of Oct 15, 2026", the CSV "As of: Oct 15, 2026" (format: three-letter month, day, four-digit year).

- **S3-R5:** Each export states the locations under the as-of line: "Locations: All locations" when every location is ticked, otherwise "Locations: " followed by the ticked locations' names in the Location menu's order (A to Z), separated by a comma and a space, for example "Locations: Main Shop, North Bay". Under it, only when set: "Past due: " and the ticked ranges in menu order (for example "Past due: 31-60 days, 91+ days"), then, for one ticked customer or vendor, "Customer: " or "Vendor: " and the name, and for several, "Customers: " or "Vendors: " and the names in the order they were ticked.

- **S3-R6:** The Location column appears in the export under the same rule as on screen (Story 6).

- **S3-R7:** The A/R and A/P Summary PDF and CSV head the last bucket column "91 And Over", exactly as the screen shows it; in the PDF the heading stays on one line.

- **S3-R8:** The CSV starts with the lines of S3-R5, each in its own row, in this order: As of, Locations, then Past due and the customer or vendor line only when set. The column heading row follows directly, then one flat table: one row per customer or vendor (Summaries) or per document (Detail, Collection, Unpaid Invoices), with the aging bucket, customer or vendor in its own column; no group header or subtotal rows; one Totals row at the bottom. Money is a plain number with two decimals, and an empty bucket is a blank cell; dates are YYYY-MM-DD.

- **S3-R8b:** The CSV column headings are the on-screen headings, letter for letter (S2-R4a): "Customer Full Name" on A/R Detail, "Customer" on A/R Collection, "Vendor Name" on the A/P reports. The bucket column on A/R Detail and A/P Detail is headed "Aging Bucket", and its values are the bucket names shown on screen.

- **S3-R8a:** On A/R Collection, every CSV row also carries the customer's "Phone" and, for users with 'View and Manage AP/AR Data', "Credit term", in columns right after "Customer"; the PDF prints them on each customer's line (Story 9).

- **S3-R9:** The row count for the export limits is the number of customer or vendor rows (Summaries) or document rows (the other four), not counting headers, subtotals or the Totals row.

- **S3-R10:** A successful export shows the native "Success" message, "Data exported successfully." An export with no rows downloads nothing and shows the native warning "Empty export", "Export didn't yield any results".

**Negative Cases:**

- **S3-N1:** Over 2,000 rows, the PDF is refused before anything is generated: no download, and the message "This report is too large for PDF. Export it as CSV, or narrow the filters, then try again." Exactly 2,000 rows is allowed.

- **S3-N2:** Over 50,000 rows, the CSV is refused: no download, and the message "This report is too large to export. Choose an earlier as-of date, then try again." Exactly 50,000 rows is allowed.

- **S3-N3:** A refused export shows exactly one message (S3-N1 or S3-N2), never a second generic error.

**Error Handling:**

- If an export fails for any other reason (a server error or a lost connection), nothing downloads and one error message shows: "We couldn't export this report. Please try again." This message is on these six reports only; the other reports keep theirs.

### Story 4: Collapse all, Expand all and pinned group rows

As a user reading a grouped report, I want to open or close every group at once and always see which group I'm scrolling through so that I don't lose my place.

**Design:** [A/R and A/P Report Sorting design canvas](https://claude.ai/artifact/BRYCdjKwGRSgGPC9xxkgF4), page 3 (Collapse all and pinned group rows)

**Jira:** [SV-11534](https://shopview.atlassian.net/browse/SV-11534)

**Form factor:** Desktop only by rule: detailed reports.

**Prerequisites:**

- The report is a grouped report: A/R Detail, A/R Collection, A/P Detail or A/P Unpaid Invoices.

**Requirements:**

- **S4-R1:** The table's top-left header cell holds one chevron button. While any group is expanded it points down, its tooltip (above the button) reads "Collapse all", and a click collapses every group. When every group is collapsed it points right, its tooltip reads "Expand all", and a click expands every group.

- **S4-R2:** After Expand all, groups that arrive later as the user scrolls also arrive expanded.

- **S4-R3:** Clicking the button never sorts the column under it.

- **S4-R4:** On A/R Detail and A/P Detail, expanding a bucket (by hand or with Expand all) shows its first 100 lines, then loads more as the user scrolls. This is as today on A/P Detail and new on A/R Detail, which today shows a whole bucket at once.

- **S4-R5:** Each group's own expand toggle uses the same chevron: pointing down when open, right when closed. Behavior is unchanged.

- **S4-R6:** While the user scrolls through an expanded group, that group's row (the bucket label, customer or vendor) stays pinned just under the column headings. Only one group row is held at a time: when the next group's row reaches it, the next row pushes it up and off and takes its place. Scrolling back reverses this.

- **S4-R7:** When the table scrolls sideways, each group row's label stays visible at the left.

- **S4-R8:** Re-sorting and paging keep every group's expanded or collapsed state.

- **S4-R9:** Expanded state is not remembered between visits.

**Negative Cases:**

- **S4-N1:** Changing the as-of date, the customer or vendor filter, the Past due filter or the Location filter collapses every group, as changing the date or filter does today.

- **S4-N2:** A/R Summary and A/P Summary have no groups, so they have no Collapse all button.

### Story 5: Filters: customer or vendor, past due and location

As a manager of a multi-location shop, I want to narrow the aging to chosen locations so that I can follow up on my own branch.

**Design:** [A/R and A/P Report Sorting design canvas](https://claude.ai/artifact/BRYCdjKwGRSgGPC9xxkgF4), page 4 (Location filter and Location column) and page 7 (Filters and call list)

**Jira:** [SV-11535](https://shopview.atlassian.net/browse/SV-11535)

**Form factor:** Desktop only by rule: detailed reports.

**Prerequisites:**

- User must have 'Reports → Page Access' setting enabled.

**Requirements:**

- **S5-R1:** Each report's filter row is the native report filter row of the newer reports (Inventory Value, Sales By Customer), in this order on all six reports: the date chip ("As of: 10/15/2026"), the customer chip on the A/R reports or the vendor chip on the A/P reports, the Past due chip, then the Location chip.

- **S5-R2:** The Location chip and its open menu are the native ones, exactly as on Sales By Customer, Inventory Value and Work In Progress: label "Location" with the location pin icon; the menu shows a "Select all" row, a "{N} selected" count, the locations with the ticked ones first, a search box with the placeholder "Search locations" once there are more than 8 locations, and "Clear selection".

- **S5-R3:** The list offers every location at which the organization has open documents in this report on the as-of date, whatever location the user is signed into and whatever locations they are enrolled in. It is not narrowed by the Location filter, the customer or vendor filter, or the Past due filter; it changes only with the as-of date.

- **S5-R4:** Each location is named exactly as the location switcher in the top right of ShopView names it, and the Location column uses the same name.

- **S5-R5:** The filter opens with every location in the list ticked (All locations), so an untouched report shows exactly today's organization-wide rows and totals. "Select all" ticks every location again.

- **S5-R6:** The chip reads "Location: All locations" when every location is ticked, the location's name when one is ticked (shortened with "..." when long), "Location: N locations" when N are ticked, and "Location: No locations" when none is.

- **S5-R6a:** With no location ticked ("Clear selection" or unticking every row), the report shows no rows: the native empty state (S8-R5) with "Clear all filters", no totals and no Location column. An export from that state downloads nothing and shows "Empty export", "Export didn't yield any results", as on Sales By Customer.

- **S5-R7:** Choosing locations narrows every row, group, subtotal, total, page count, sort and export to the open documents at those locations. A row belongs to the location of its own document. On A/R: the invoice, credit memo or deposit. On A/P: a bill belongs to the location of the delivery it was received on; a vendor credit from a vendor return belongs to that return's location; any other vendor credit keeps its vendor's location, as today. That is the location the Location column shows.

- **S5-R8:** On the Summaries, a customer's or vendor's row adds up only their open documents at the chosen locations; a customer or vendor with nothing open there is not listed.

- **S5-R9:** The chip is shown whenever the report's list (S5-R3) has two or more locations, and always while fewer than all are ticked, so the choice can always be undone.

- **S5-R10:** The ticked locations are kept in the page address while fewer than all are ticked, so browser Back, a reload and a shared link restore them; a link listing several locations separated by commas is read as those locations. "No locations" is not kept in the address: a reload or shared link opens on All locations, as on Sales By Customer. A link naming every location reads as All locations.

- **S5-R10a:** Filter choices are not remembered between visits: opening a report from the Reports menu starts on today's as-of date, no customer or vendor, Past due Any, and All locations.

- **S5-R11:** Clicking a customer or vendor on A/R Summary or A/P Summary opens A/R Collection or A/P Unpaid Invoices with just that customer or vendor ticked, the same as-of date, the same ticked Past due ranges and the same ticked locations.

- **S5-R12:** The filter does not change who can see what: everyone who can open these reports sees every location's data, as today.

- **S5-R13:** The customer chip (A/R) and vendor chip (A/P) and their open menus are the native ones: Customer exactly as Sales By Customer's Customer filter (person icon), Vendor exactly as Inventory Value's Vendor filter (truck icon, "Search vendors"; as there, the search box shows only when there are more than 8 vendors). The menu has a search box, an "All customers" or "All vendors" row, the native line "All customers are included. Pick one or more to narrow." (or vendors) while none is ticked, and the list; several can be ticked. The chip reads "Customer: All customers" or "Vendor: All vendors" in the resting (grey) style when none is ticked, the name when one is (shortened with "..." when long), and "Customer: N customers" or "Vendor: N vendors" when N are. The ticked customers or vendors narrow every row, group, total, page, sort and export; the Location list does not change (S5-R3).

- **S5-R14:** A customer or vendor ticked through a link, a reload or a Summary drill-down is named and ticked on the chip, even when it is not among the first results of the chip's search.

- **S5-R15:** The Past due chip and its open menu are the same native checkbox dropdown as Customer and Location: label "Past due", an hourglass icon, an "Any" row, the native line "All ranges are included. Pick one or more to narrow." while none is ticked, then "1-30 days", "31-60 days", "61-90 days", "91+ days", in that order; several can be ticked. The chip reads "Past due: Any" in the resting (grey) style when none is ticked, the range when one is (for example "Past due: 91+ days"), and "Past due: N ranges" when N are. Ticking all four reads "Past due: 4 ranges", not Any, because Any also keeps invoices and bills that are not due yet. The ranges are the reports' own aging buckets: 1 to 30, 31 to 60, 61 to 90 and more than 90 days past the due date on the as-of date.

- **S5-R16:** With ranges ticked, A/R Detail, A/R Collection, A/P Detail and A/P Unpaid Invoices keep only the invoices and bills whose days past due on the as-of date fall in a ticked range, and always keep credit memos, deposits and vendor credits.

- **S5-R17:** With ranges ticked, A/R Summary and A/P Summary list only the customers or vendors with some open invoice or bill amount in a ticked range; their rows show their full balances in every bucket.

- **S5-R18:** The Totals row always adds up exactly the rows the filters leave (customer or vendor, Past due, Location), on screen on every page, in the PDF and in the CSV. On A/R Collection and A/P Unpaid Invoices this fixes today's Totals row, which ignores the customer or vendor filter.

- **S5-R18a:** For the same as-of date, customer or vendor and Location, with Past due on Any, A/R Summary's Totals equal A/R Detail's Open Balance Totals bucket by bucket, and A/P Summary's equal A/P Detail's. The Detail reports' Amount column holds original amounts and is not compared. With a Past due filter they can differ: a Summary keeps the whole row of a customer or vendor that qualifies (Current and credits included), while a Detail report lists only the invoices or bills that qualify, plus every credit.

- **S5-R18b:** The figures are read fresh on every load: reloading the report shows a payment recorded a moment before. Going away and back inside the app within 30 seconds, without reloading, can show the figures from the earlier visit, as on every screen.

- **S5-R19:** The ticked customers or vendors and the ticked Past due ranges are kept in the page address like Location (S5-R10).

**Negative Cases:**

- **S5-N1:** If the organization's open documents sit at only one location and it is ticked, the Location chip is not shown.

- **S5-N2:** A location in a link that is not in the report's Location list (S5-R3), or is not a valid location at all, is ignored: the report loads, the chip reads as if it were not there, and the Location column follows the remaining choice.

- **S5-N3:** A Past due value in a link that the chip does not offer (for example 5, or "any") is read as Any.

**Edge Cases:**

- **S5-E1:** If a location is chosen and the user then picks a customer or vendor with nothing open there, the report is empty and the Location chip stays visible so the selection can be cleared.

- **S5-E2:** Switching location in the top right of ShopView while on a report reloads it, as on every other report: the user stays on the report with the same filters and the same rows; the sort returns to the opening order (Story 1), every group collapses and the page returns to the top; which documents link, prompt or show as plain text updates (Story 7).
