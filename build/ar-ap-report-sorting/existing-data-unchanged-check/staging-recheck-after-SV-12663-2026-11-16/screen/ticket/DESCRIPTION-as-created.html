Description

What the PRD requires:

The PRD does not ask for this change. It does not say which calendar day a document's date is shown on, and its list of what changes for existing users has no date change. The developer's note on SV-12663 (13 Nov) says the reports would keep "the time zone the reports used before this release".

What happens instead:

On staging, the reports now show each document's Date and Due Date on the calendar day of the location the user is at. Production shows them on the UTC (Greenwich) calendar day. So a document saved late in the day, or saved at midnight UTC, shows a different day than on production, and its Past Due changes by one. This happens in North American time zones too, not only far-away ones.

For example, as of 11/12/2026:

  • A/P Aging Detail (screen on Mountain time): bill X101364617:01 reads "Aug 16, 2026", Due Date "Oct 15, 2026", Past Due "28". Before the release it read "Aug 17, 2026", "Oct 16, 2026", "27".
  • A/R Aging Collection (screen on Mountain time): credit memo CM2-4357 reads Due Date "Sep 6, 2026", Past Due "67". Before the release: "Sep 7, 2026", 66.
