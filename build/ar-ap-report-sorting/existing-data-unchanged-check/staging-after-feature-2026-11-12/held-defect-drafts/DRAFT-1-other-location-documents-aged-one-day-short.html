Description

What the PRD requires:

A document is Current when its due date is on or after the as-of date. Its Past Due, where the report shows one, is the number of days from the due date to the as-of date: negative while the document is not yet due (a bill due 9 days after the as-of date reads -9, as today) and 0 on its due date.

What happens instead:

On staging, documents at a location in a different time zone from the signed-in location are aged one day short. With the active location Staging Heavy Duty - 9919 (Pakistan time), documents at Staging Lethbridge - 4310 (Mountain time) whose due date is shown as yesterday are listed in "Current", and a bill due 61 days ago shows Past Due "60".

For example,

  • A/R Aging Detail (As of: 11/12/2026, Customer: E2E Credits Customer): invoice S3-39986, Due Date "Nov 11, 2026", is under "Current (25)".
  • A/R Aging Collection (As of: 11/12/2026, Customer: Tucson Truck Center): invoices S3-17147, S3-17148, S3-17149 and S3-17238, Due Date "Sep 12, 2026", show Past Due "60" (Sep 12 to Nov 12 is 61 days).

What this shows: the age is counted in the active location's calendar, not in the calendar of the date the report itself prints. In all, 16 documents at Staging Lethbridge - 4310 changed bucket this way (12 from 1-30 to Current, 4 from 61-90 to 31-60); the 2 Heavy Duty documents that changed bucket are correct.

Steps to Reproduce

Example 1 - A/R Aging Detail

  1. Sign in to staging as an Admin at Staging Heavy Duty - 9919.
  2. Open Reports → A/R Aging Detail. As of: 11/12/2026, Customer: E2E Credits Customer.
  3. Click the chevron in the Date heading (Expand all).
    • S3-39986, Due Date "Nov 11, 2026", is under "Current (25)".

Example 2 - A/R Aging Collection

  1. Open Reports → A/R Aging Collection. As of: 11/12/2026, Customer: Tucson Truck Center. Expand the group.
    • S3-17147, Due Date "Sep 12, 2026", Past Due "60".

Actual Result:

  • A Lethbridge document due yesterday is Current; a Lethbridge bill due 61 days ago shows Past Due 60.

Expected Result:

  • "A document is Current when its due date is on or after the as-of date. Its Past Due … is the number of days from the due date to the as-of date" (PRD v11, S1-R5a): S3-39986 in 1-30 days past due with Past Due 1; S3-17147 in 61-90 with Past Due 61.

Environment

  • Staging: https://app.staging.shopview.com (build v26.45.4-d07623f), active location Staging Heavy Duty - 9919 (Asia/Karachi), Admin. Seen on 12 Nov 2026 at 14:03:39 UTC and again at 14:06:09 UTC. The same values come from the report's own data call (no browser): daysPastDue 0 and 60.
  • Staging before the feature: build v26.45.3-6197d19, 12 Nov 2026 06:46 UTC: S3-39986 in 1-30, S3-17147 in 61-90 (snapshot folder staging-before-feature-captured-2026-11-12).
  • Checked: A/R Detail, A/R Collection, every A/R and A/P document compared before/after. Not checked: the signed-in location set to Staging Lethbridge - 4310 (the QA lead's session was restored to Heavy Duty; not repeated).
