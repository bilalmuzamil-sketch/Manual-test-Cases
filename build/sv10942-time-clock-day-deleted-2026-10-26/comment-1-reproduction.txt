h3. The one way I found that removes a day's punches
Creating, editing or clocking out a timesheet entry that covers other entries removes those other entries. This is the overlap rule from SV-10696. Office admins have timesheet edit permission, so they can trigger it on their own day.
!SV-10942_4-overlap-rule-deletes-punches.png|width=700,height=698!
_Picture 4: production now lists what will be deleted and asks first. I pressed Cancel and both punches were kept._

How to see it:
# Open [Reports > Timesheet Activities|https://app.shopview.com/reports/punch-clock-activities] on production as an Admin.
# Click {{New}} at the top right.
# Pick a staff member who already has punches today, pick a department, and set {{Start}} and {{End}} so they cover those punches (for example 5:00 PM to 6:00 PM).
# Click {{Create}}. The {{Confirm Overwrite}} window says how many entries will be permanently deleted.

Since build v26.43.0 (22 October, SV-10696) this window warns first. Before that, the entries were removed with no message at all. Both of the customer's cases happened before 22 October, so this may be what happened: for example, a user who thought a punch was missing adds or edits an entry that covers the rest of the day. I can't confirm it, because I can't see the customer's own data. Related and still open: SV-11590 ("Clock Out from a work order's Timesheets fills Start with the wrong time", Ready to Fix), because a wrong Start can make an entry cover others.
