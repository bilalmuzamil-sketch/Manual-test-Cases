## Description

**Not related to Roles/Permissions: But related to the Custom Roles QA Branch**  
  
When a contact is granted **Customer Portal Access**, the activation email is successfully sent. However, clicking the activation link from the email redirects the user to an error page instead of allowing the account to be activated.

---

### **Steps to Reproduce (STRs):**

1. Navigate to **Customers**.
2. Open any customer.
3. Click **Contacts**.
4. Click **New Contact**.
5. Enter contact details and a valid email address.
6. Enable **Customer Portal Access**.
7. Click **Save**.
8. Open the activation email received by the contact.
9. Click the **Activate Account** CTA/link.

---

### **Actual Result:**

* The activation email is received successfully.
* Clicking the activation link redirects the user to an error page (as shown in the attached PDF/screenshot).
* The customer portal account is not activated.
