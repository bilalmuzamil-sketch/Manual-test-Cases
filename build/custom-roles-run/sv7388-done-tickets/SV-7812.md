# SV-7812 — Service Advisor:  Invoice Reversal, Payment Deletion,..... Depend on AP/AR Permission

## Description

**Affected Role:**  
**Service Advisor** (and any custom role with **Invoicing & Payments → Delete/Reverse = ON** and **Manage Accounts Payable & Receivable = OFF**)

---

### **Description:**

In QA/Staging, users cannot **reverse invoices**, **delete payments**, or **void transactions** unless **Manage Accounts Payable & Receivable (AP/AR)** is enabled.

According to the specification, these actions should be controlled exclusively by **Invoicing & Payments → Delete/Reverse** permission. The **Manage Accounts Payable & Receivable** permission controls visibility of AP/AR-related pages and data, but does not govern invoice reversal, payment deletion, or transaction voiding.

Enabling the AP/AR permission immediately allows these actions to function, confirming an unintended permission dependency.

---

### **Specification Reference**

| Permission | Spec Definition |
| --- | --- |
| **Invoicing & Payments → Delete/Reverse** | Reverse invoices, delete payments (including customer payments), void transactions |
| **Manage Accounts Payable & Receivable (seeApArData)** | Access to Unpaid Invoices, Payments, Credits, AP/AR aging reports, bulk payments, and sensitive customer financial data |

Per the specification, **reverse/delete/void actions are not listed under AP/AR permissions**.

---

### **Steps to Reproduce (STRs):**

1. Create or use a role with:

    * **Invoicing & Payments → Delete/Reverse = ON**
    * **Manage Accounts Payable & Receivable = OFF**
    
2. Log in as a user assigned to that role.
3. Attempt to:

    * Reverse an invoice
    * Delete a payment
    

---

### **Actual Result:**

* Reverse Invoice action is blocked.
* Delete Payment action is blocked.
* Enabling **Manage Accounts Payable & Receivable** causes all actions to work.

---

### **Environment:**

* QA
* Staging (SV4235)
