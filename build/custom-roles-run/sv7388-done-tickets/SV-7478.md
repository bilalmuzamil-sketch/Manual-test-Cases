## Summary

Many area View bundles fan out to a long list of fine-grained atoms (e.g. `WorkOrdersView` → `ROLE_WORK_ORDER::VIEW`, `ROLE_WORK_ORDER_LINE::VIEW`, `ROLE_WORK_ORDER_HISTORY::VIEW`, `ROLE_WORK_ORDER_PART_REQUEST::VIEW`, `ROLE_VEHICLE::VIEW`, `ROLE_CUSTOMER::VIEW`). Most of these guard endpoints that should simply be checked against **one coarse area atom** (e.g. `ROLE_WORK_ORDER::VIEW`). This ticket is the discovery + simplification pass to find such families and collapse them, reducing the \~300-atom surface.

## Approach

1. **Discovery (can start immediately):** inventory atoms grouped by area; find clusters of fine-grained atoms guarding endpoints that belong to a single area-level concept. Produce a proposed collapse map (atom → canonical area atom).
2. **Apply:** repoint `#[IsGranted]` annotations to the canonical atom; remove the redundant atoms from `PermissionEnum.php` and the bundle→atom map.
3. Guard against over-grant: a coarse atom must not unintentionally widen access beyond the PRD matrix — verify with the atom-diff CLI (CRP-BE-08).

## Dependencies

Discovery is unblocked (can begin now). Applying coordinates with **CRP-BE-02** (same map) — relates-to, sequence the apply just after BE-02. Verified by **CRP-BE-08** atom-diff CLI.
