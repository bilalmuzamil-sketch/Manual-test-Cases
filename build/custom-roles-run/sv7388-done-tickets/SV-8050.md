# SV-8050 — Customers: View-only role sees Part Sales and Work Orders tabs (with payment status, PO numbers, WO records) on the customer profile despite Parts Department, Part Sales, and Work Orders all being OFF — no permission for either area

- **Status:** Done
- **Resolution:** Done

## Description

**Issue Type:** Bug  
  
**Epic:** SV-7388 (Custom Roles and Permissions)

**Environment:** Staging

**Affected Area:** Customer Profile — sub-tab visibility (Part Sales, Work Orders)

**Preconditions:**

* Custom role with **Customers: View ON** only
* **Work Orders OFF**, **Parts Department OFF**, **Part Sales OFF**, **See Financial Data OFF**, **Manage AP/AR OFF**
* User is assigned to that custom role

**Steps to Reproduce:**

1. Log in as the Customers: View-only user
2. Open a customer profile
3. Open the **Part Sales** tab and observe the records (Status, PO numbers, Parts, Returns)
4. Open the **Work Orders** tab and observe the WO records

**Expected Result:**  
Customer-profile sub-tabs should respect area permissions. Part Sales is gated by Parts Department ON + Part Sales: View (§1e, §2d), and Work Orders records by Work Orders: View (§1a) — none of which this role has. The Part Sales and Work Orders tabs and their records should not be accessible to a Customers: View-only role.

**Actual Result:**  
The Customers: View-only role can open both sub-tabs on the customer profile:

* **Part Sales tab:** part sale records visible, including **payment status** (e.g., Estimate, Paid) and **PO numbers**.
* **Work Orders tab:** work order records visible.  
  Both appear despite the role having no Work Orders, Parts Department, or Part Sales permission.

**What is correctly hidden (working as expected):**

* Credit Terms and Credit Limit are **not** visible (Manage AP/AR gate holding — §6b).
* Part **cost** / dollar amounts are **not** visible (See Financial Data gate holding — §6a).

**Scope / Notes:**

* This is a **record/tab visibility** issue, not a financial-figure leak — the financial gates (§6a, §6b) are holding. The gap is that customer-profile sub-tabs (Part Sales, Work Orders) display records from areas the role has no permission for.
* Affects at least two tabs (Part Sales, Work Orders), suggesting the customer-profile sub-tabs are gated only by Customer Management: View rather than by each area's own permission. Worth checking the remaining tabs (Contacts, Assets, Notes) for the same pattern — those may be legitimately part of the customer record.
* Exposed data is limited to non-cost records (status, PO numbers, WO records), which lowers severity relative to a dollar-amount exposure, but is still unauthorized visibility.

**Open Question for PM (VIU):**  
The spec gates Part Sales (Parts Department + Part Sales: View) and Work Orders (Work Orders: View) as their own areas, but does not explicitly state which permission governs the corresponding **sub-tabs on the customer profile**. Please confirm whether these tabs should be gated by each area's permission (Part Sales: View, Work Orders: View) or are intended to be part of Customer Management: View. Currently both tabs and their records are visible with only Customers: View.

**Spec references:**

| # | Section | Verbatim quote |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | §1a Work Orders, View row | "See work orders in the work order list and open work order detail pages. If OFF, the Work Orders top level nav item is hidden." |
| 2 | §1e Part Sales, Financial gate | "Requires See Financial Data to be ON (part sales inherently involve pricing)." |
| 3 | §2d Parts Department | "When Parts Department is OFF, the three child CRUD areas are hidden in the role editor." |
| 4 | §6a See Financial Data, OFF state | "All financial data is hidden across every page. Dollar amounts, rates, costs, margins, and totals are not displayed." |
