# SV-7999 — Custom role editor wipes Settings sub-options when the Settings group is toggled off and back on

- **Type:** Bug
- **Status:** Done
- **Resolution:** Done
- **Created:** 2026-06-29  **Updated:** 2026-07-07T16:08
- **Link:** https://shopview.atlassian.net/browse/SV-7999

## Description

**Area:** Settings → Roles & Permissions → Create / Edit Role

**Severity:** Medium (silent loss of configuration; can lead to a role saved with the wrong permissions)

## What happens

When you build a custom role, the **Settings** permission group has six sub-options: App Settings, Service, Parts, Finance, Data Import, and View/Manage Wages.

If you turn the **Settings** group **off and then back on**, every sub-option you had selected is silently reset to off. You have to re-pick them all from scratch.

This is inconsistent with the **Parts** permission group, which _remembers_ your sub-selections when you toggle it off and on. So the same action behaves differently depending on which group you touch, and there is no warning that your choices were discarded.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Log in as an Owner or Admin.
2. Go to **Settings → Roles & Permissions** and start creating a new role (skip the template picker).
3. Turn **on** the **Settings** permission group.
4. Inside Settings, switch on a couple of sub-options — e.g. **App Settings** and **Finance**.
5. Turn the **Settings** group **off**.
6. Turn the **Settings** group **back on**.

## Expected result

Your previously selected sub-options (App Settings, Finance) come back on — the way the **Parts** group restores its sub-selections.

## Actual result

All Settings sub-options are off again. Your earlier selections are gone, with no notice.

## Why it matters to customers

It is easy to lose configuration without realizing it. If someone flips the Settings group off to glance at something and turns it back on, they can save a role that is **missing permissions they thought they had set** — producing a role that is either under- or over-scoped. Because nothing warns them, the mistake surfaces later as "this role cannot do X" or "this role can see too much."

## Notes

* Found during E2E automation of the Custom Roles suite (TestRail case C26444, SV-5319).
* TestRail C26444's title claims the editor _"preserves sub-toggle selections,"_ which is the opposite of current behavior — so this is either a real bug to fix (make Settings behave like Parts) **or** the case is mis-specified and should be rewritten to expect the reset. Product decision needed.
