# SV-8177 — AP/AR aging reports still gated by "Manage AP/AR" instead of the Reports permission 

- **Type:** Bug
- **Status:** Done
- **Resolution:** Done
- **Created:** 2026-07-06  **Updated:** 2026-07-08T10:45
- **Link:** https://shopview.atlassian.net/browse/SV-8177

## Description

_Per the updated Custom Roles spec:_  
 "Manage AP/AR no longer gates the AR/AP aging reports — they now follow the **Reports** permission (all-or-nothing)."   
  
A user with **Reports ON** should see all 6 AP/AR aging reports regardless of whether **Manage Accounts Payable & Receivable** is ON or OFF.

**Steps to reproduce (staging):**

1. As Admin, create/edit a custom role with **Reports = ON** and **Manage Accounts Payable and Receivable = OFF**.
2. Assign it to a user and log in as that user.
3. Go to **Reports**.
4. Look for the 6 AP/AR aging reports: AR Aging Summary, AR Aging Detail, AR Aging Collection, AP Aging Summary, AP Aging Detail, AP Unpaid Invoices.

**Expected (per spec):** All 6 aging reports are listed and open normally — the **Reports** permission governs them; Manage AP/AR does not.

**Actual:** The 6 aging reports are **hidden** (only non-AP/AR reports like Sales / Sales Tax show). They appear **only** when Manage AP/AR is turned ON — i.e., aging is still gated by Manage AP/AR (old behavior).
