h3. How to see it yourself
# Open [https://sv10360.qa.shopview.com|https://sv10360.qa.shopview.com] and sign in as Admin.
# At the top left, next to the logo, click *ShopHub* (with the small arrow) and choose *AccountingHub*.
# In the left menu under *BANKING*, click *Transactions*. In the row of bank cards near the top, click the card *ZZAUTOTEST SV-10903 bank*.
# Open the same page in a second browser tab (copy the address) and click the same bank card there too.

*Reload keeps what you typed (row ZZ11026-11 supplies, -$211.00)*
# In the first tab, on the row *ZZ11026-11 supplies*, click the three dots (⋮) at the right end of the row and choose *Edit*.
# In the second tab, on the same row, open the *ACCOUNT* dropdown and pick another account, for example *6100 Insurance*.
# Back in the first tab, type something in *Memo (optional)* and *Payee (optional)*, then click *Save*. The red message "This transaction changed. Reload it before saving." appears with *Reload*.
# Click *Reload*. What you typed is still in Memo and Payee, and *Account* now shows 6100 Insurance. Click *Save*: "Saved." appears and the row shows all three.

*A matched row shows its message inside the window (row ZZ11026-12 supplies, -$212.00)*
# In the first tab, on the row *ZZ11026-12 supplies*, click ⋮ and choose *Edit*.
# In the second tab, on the same row, click ⋮ and choose *Match transaction*. In the window that opens, click *Select* on *JE #13762* and then click *Match entry*.
# Back in the first tab, type something in *Memo (optional)* and click *Save*. The message "Only pending transactions can be edited; this one is matched." appears inside the Edit window with *Dismiss*, and *Save* is greyed out. Click *Dismiss* to close the window.

Checks 7, 8 and 9 need a failure or a slow save made on purpose, so they can't easily be repeated by hand.
