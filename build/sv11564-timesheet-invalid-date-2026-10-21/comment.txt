h3. Separate issue raised

While testing Clock Out from a work order's *Timesheets* section, I found that the window fills *Start* six hours late at an Edmonton-time location. Clicking *Clock Out* saves that wrong start. Production does the same, so it is not caused by this ticket. I raised it as [SV-11590|https://shopview.atlassian.net/browse/SV-11590] "Clock Out from a work order's Timesheets fills Start with the wrong time".
