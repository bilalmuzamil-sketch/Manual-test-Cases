Found while testing SV-12112

Summary

When a clock-in is added by hand (Reports > Timesheet Activities > New, with no End time), the person's name shows in the Clocked In column of the Work Orders list. Sorting by Clocked In, though, treats that work order as if nobody is clocked in, so it lands below work orders that show nobody. It only moves into its right place after someone clocks in or out on that work order. Production does the same.

Steps to reproduce

Open the QA branch to reproduce the issue: https://sv12112.qa.shopview.com (sign in as admin).

  1. In the top menu, click Reports. In the left menu under LABOR, click Timesheet Activities.
  2. At the top right of the page, click the blue New button. The New Timesheet window opens.
  3. Choose any staff member in Select Staff, choose work order S12112-17584 in Work Order, and its line 1 Service - Electric Brake Trailer CVIP in Line.
  4. Set a Start time earlier today. Leave End empty. Click Create. The new row shows a green Active label, which means that person is clocked in.

Picture 1: the New Timesheet window. 1 New, 2 Work Order, 3 Line, 4 Start, 5 End left empty, 6 Create.

  1. In the top menu, click Work Orders, then the All tab at the top left of the list. If you see cards instead of a table, click the list icon (three lines) at the right of the same row, just left of Create Work Order. Make sure Assigned to me on that row is not switched on.
  2. On the same row, click the magnifying glass (just left of Status) and type S12112.
  3. Click the Clocked In column heading once. The small arrow next to it points down, so the most people clocked in come first.
  4. Look at row S12112-17584. Its Clocked In column shows the person you added, but the row is at the bottom, below S12112-17586 and S12112-17580, which show nobody.

Picture 2: the list sorted by Clocked In, most first. S12112-17584 (red) shows Admin ShopView but sorts last, below the two rows that show nobody (orange).

Fastest way to reproduce: open https://sv12112.qa.shopview.com/workorders, click the All tab, search S12112 and click the Clocked In heading once. S12112-17584 already has a hand-added clock-in for Admin ShopView, so it shows at the bottom straight away.

Expected behaviour

A work order with a hand-added clock-in sorts with the people the Clocked In column shows. S12112-17584 shows one person, so it should sort next to S12112-17582 (also one person), above the rows that show nobody.

From SV-12112, "Done when":

sorting by Clocked In orders rows by the same people the column shows.

SV-12112 also names this case:

The counter also isn't refreshed when an open time record is created by hand.

Current behaviour

The row shows the person but is sorted as if nobody is clocked in. It moves into place only after someone clocks in or out on that work order.
