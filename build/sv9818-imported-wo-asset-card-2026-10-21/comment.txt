h3. How to see it

# Open the QA branch: [https://sv9818.qa.shopview.com/] and sign in as Admin.
# In the top menu, click *Work Orders*, then the *All* tab.
# Click the *Status* filter above the list and tick *Imported*.
# Click the *ZZ9818-A* row (customer Colesville Diesel Repair).
# On the left, the asset card *2011 Hyundai Santa Fe* shows *Mileage 45* and *Engine Hours 9,987* under *VIN/Serial #*.

To make your own: *Settings* → *Invoices* (under Imports) → *Download Template*, fill in one row with Mileage and Hours, then use the upload box and *Import Invoices*.
