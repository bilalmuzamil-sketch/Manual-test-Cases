I tested this on the QA branch [sv11499|https://sv11499.qa.shopview.com/workorders] (build v26.40.20-00f1fd6) on 21 October 2026, as Admin at Staging Heavy Duty - 9919, and compared it with staging (build v26.40.20-c627fc7), where the problem was reported.

* A column turned off in List stays off after logging out and signing in again, and in a new browser.
* The cause was a second Work Orders window: changing the view, density or Tech View columns there brought back its old List columns. That no longer happens.
* Tech View keeps its own column choice, separate from List.

h3. How to see it

# Open the QA branch: [https://sv11499.qa.shopview.com/] and sign in as Admin.
# In the top menu, click {{Work Orders}}, click the {{All}} tab, then the first of the three view icons ({{List}}).
# Click {{Column Selection}} (the columns icon next to the view icons) and turn {{Progress}} off. Close the menu.
# Click your initials at the top right, click {{Logout}}, and sign in again.
# Open {{Work Orders}}, {{All}} and {{List}}. Progress is still off.
# To check the two-window case: open Work Orders in a second browser tab first, turn Progress off in the first tab, then click the middle view icon ({{Tech View}}) in the second tab. Open Work Orders in a third tab: Progress is still off.
