h3. How to see it

# Open [https://sv11588.qa.shopview.com/workorders] and sign in as Admin.
# Above the list, on the right, click the last of the three view icons ({{Board View}}).
# Move the mouse over the grey bar at the top of any technician's column (where their name is). The pointer is a white open hand with a black outline.
# Press on any work order card and drag it a little. The pointer is a white closed hand with a black outline for as long as you hold the button. Let go where it started.
# Click the middle view icon ({{Tech View}}). Move the mouse over the six dots at the very left of any row: the open hand. Press and drag: the closed hand.
