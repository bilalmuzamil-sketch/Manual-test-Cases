### 4.1 DESIGN-NOTES.md §5.1 "Retraction (honesty)" is itself WRONG — retracted again
DESIGN-NOTES §5.1 currently says, verbatim:

> "| 1 `11985:9686` | Plain WO list + the five-filter bar. **No sort control at all.** |"

> wrong** — there is no sort-named or sort-shaped layer anywhere on step 1 or step 2. The
> sort entry point (the Switch-vertical icon) first appears on **step 3**."

and finding 2:

> "**Direction is never shown as anything but 'Ascending.'** There is no Descending state,
> no asc/desc toggle icon, and **no sorted-column indicator on any column heading on any
> of the four boards.**"

**The render disproves all three claims.** Step 1 `11985:9686` shows, at pixel level:
an **up/down double-arrow sort icon in the toolbar** between the filter icon and the
column/layout icon (cropped and re-read at 3× to be certain), **and** a **`↓` arrow
immediately after the word `Status` in the column heading row** (also cropped and
re-read). Both are present on steps 1, 2 and 3.

So the **ORIGINAL** reading was right and the 2026-07-30 retraction was a false
correction — the node-tree pass evidently missed icon layers whose names do not contain
"sort" (the icon lives inside a Button instance). Only the direction claim survives:
**`Descending` genuinely never appears anywhere.**

**Lesson worth keeping:** a layer tree is not a substitute for a render when the question
is "is this control present?" — an icon can be present under a name that no keyword
search will find. Rule 35's insistence on 100% of frames is doing real work here.

### 4.2 The mobile chip-row scroll arrow — checked before changing anything
FLT-MOB-01 (C29621) asserts *"An arrow at the right-hand edge shows that the row can be
scrolled."* My newly-rendered `12867:12201` shows **no** such arrow, which looked like a
case error. Before touching the case I rendered-and-cropped the board the case actually
cites, **`11884:20807`** (already in `frames/`): it **does** show a circular `❯`
right-arrow button at the right edge of the chip row. **The case is correct and was left
alone.** Recorded here because it is exactly the kind of change that would have
introduced an error.

Sorting boards, while the filter **chip** on the same board correctly reads
**`Lead Technician`**. The mobile sheet boards also use `Lead Tehnician`. Our cases use
