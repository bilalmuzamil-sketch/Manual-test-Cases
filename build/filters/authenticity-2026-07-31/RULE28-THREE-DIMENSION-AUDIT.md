## 4. IS THE CRITIC RIGHT? — the honest answer on BOTH halves of the claim

Stefan Mitrovic's claim (2026-07-27) has two halves. Answering both for **this** suite:

### Half 1 — "more than 70 % useless / a waste"

**No, and by a wide margin — but say the number, not a slogan.**

- Cases that would be **deleted outright as worthless: 0 of 110 (0 %).** Not one case in
  this suite is spec-parroting, untestable, or a framework test.
- Cases that are **redundant with a better sibling and could be folded away: 27 of 110
  (24.5 %)** — the 26 merge members + 1 duplicate — and even those are *consolidation*,
  not waste: each still tests real behaviour on a real screen and each would be reduced
  to a step inside a survivor, not thrown away.
- **Floor if every open recommendation were executed: 110 → 84 cases (−24 %).** So the
  most hostile honest reading of this suite is **~25 % consolidatable, 0 % worthless** —
  against a claim of **>70 % useless.**
- And the suite has already **paid** that debt once this month: **146 authored → 110
  active**, a **25 % reduction we performed on ourselves** (36 retirements: 27
  presence-matrix merge members, 9 mis-scoped palette cases). The trend is the answer to
  the claim.

### Half 2 — "some tests just do not make sense"

**On this suite today: 0 of 110 do not make sense** — after a full cold read of every
body, not a sample. But he was **right about the pattern**, and the record proves it
rather than hides it:

- The previous audit found **1 genuine NONSENSE** (FLT-SRCH-09 — a QA/PO *scope
  decision* dressed up as a test case). It is **retired**, along with its 8 palette
  siblings.
- The previous audit found **12 FIX-WORDING** defects; all 12 are repaired.
- **This** pass found **2 more** by cold-reading (a case whose first five steps were
  verb-less lists; a case leaking design jargon into a tester-facing line) — **both
  repaired in-pass.**
- **This** pass also found **80 of 110 cases carrying a STALE traceability pointer**
  (76 citing a spec ingest eight Confluence versions out of date, 2 citing a resolved
  v1.3 conflict, 1 leaking an internal case id, 1 with no anchor statement). Not one of
  those was visible to a tester — but every one weakened the claim that the case is
  *genuine*. **All 80 are repaired.**

**So: the critic is wrong on the headline (0 % worthless, not >70 %) and right that a
suite drifts if nobody audits it.** The counter-evidence is not an assertion, it is this
folder: 110 cases scored on three dimensions with a per-case CSV, 1,959 machine
assertions passing, a cold read of 83,699 characters, and every defect found this month
either repaired or carried as a named, reasoned, authorization-pending recommendation.

1. **No live-build verification.** Filters has no QA branch, so all 110 are
   `VIU-Pending`. Roughly 18 on-screen labels are design-sourced and explicitly hedged
   in the case text; the shared-dropdown-component question that gates 19 of the 26
   merges cannot be answered from a desk.
2. **No Jira traceability.** No epic, no stories — an upstream gap (see
   `TRACEABILITY-AUDIT.md` §1), not an authoring one.
