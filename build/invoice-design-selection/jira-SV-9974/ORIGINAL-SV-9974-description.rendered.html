Description
The invoice email sent from the customer portal attributes the invoice to "Bravo Mechanical Services" in four places. The organisation is Staging Heavy Duty - 9919. A customer receiving this sees a company that has nothing to do with their invoice.

Environment
staging.portal.shopview.com · org Staging Heavy Duty - 9919 · invoice S-32981

Steps

  1. Portal → Invoices → open S-32981
  2. Click the email icon and send to yourself
  3. Open the email

Actual

  • Subject: Invoice #S-32981 - Bravo Mechanical Services
  • Body: "Bravo Mechanical Services has shared an invoice with you"
  • "please don't hesitate to contact Bravo Mechanical Services directly"
  • Footer: "Sent by ShopView on behalf of Bravo Mechanical Services"

The attached PDF itself is correct — it shows Staging Heavy Duty - 9919.

Expected
The email names the invoice's own organisation.

Note for dev
Worth confirming whether that string is hardcoded or read from another organisation's record. If the latter, it's a cross-tenant data issue.
