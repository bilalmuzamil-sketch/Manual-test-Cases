How to see it on the branch

  1. On a touchscreen laptop (or in Chrome developer tools with touch turned on and the desktop browser kept), open part sale P2-162 on sv11904 as Admin, window about 1024 wide.

  2. In the parts table, swipe sideways to the right until you see the Fees & Discounts column, just left of Actions.

  3. Tap Such A Lo... on the first row (Ignition Switch).

  4. The Fees & Discounts viewer opens and lists "Such a Long name testing" and "Disc" in full. No grey tooltip is left on top of it. Wait a few seconds: nothing appears.

  5. With a mouse instead: point at Such A Lo... without clicking. A dark tooltip shows "Such a Long name testing".

Not checked on a real phone or a real touchscreen laptop: these were Chrome device presets and Chrome with touch turned on.
