h3. Notes
# *Customer with no contacts:* New Work Order is greyed out for such a customer ("You need to create at least one contact"), and a contact that still has assets can't be deleted, so items 4 and 7 couldn't be tried with a no-contact customer. Saving a New Part Sale for a customer with no contacts fails with "Customer not found" on both production and the branch. That's already covered by [SV-11256|https://shopview.atlassian.net/browse/SV-11256].
# *ShopCoach:* to get a line to fail its review I blocked that one request in my own browser's developer tools. This is comparison only. It is not a ShopView feature and users can't do it.
