h3. How to reproduce the missing note

# Open Production: [https://app.shopview.com] and sign in.
# In the top menu, click {{Work Orders}} and open an invoiced work order whose customer contact has an email address you can read. I used *S2-983* (customer *ZZAUTOTEST SV6915 Email Note*, contact *Inbox SV6915*).
# Click the {{Finance}} tab (the last tab, after {{Stats}}).
# In the row of icons above the invoice, click the envelope icon. The {{Sending invoice}} window opens.
# Leave {{Include 'For Customer' Attachments in email}} unticked, leave {{Optional Emails (Comma Separated)}} empty and leave {{Include your email to BCC}} off.
# Click in the {{Email Content}} box and type: {{<Note from me: please call the customer before starting work.}}
# Click {{Send Email}}. A green message says {{Successfully sent invoice to email.}}
# Open the email in the contact's inbox. Between {{Please find your attached invoice for work order: S2-983.}} and {{Best regards,}} there is no note.
# Send again with {{Tires replaced. <see attached photo> Brakes still need a second look.}} The email shows {{Tires replaced. Brakes still need a second look.}} The part in angle brackets is missing.

!02-note-starting-with-angle-bracket-missing.png|width=775,height=1143!
_Pictures 3 and 4: the note starts with "<" (try 8), and the email that arrived has no note._

*Current behaviour:* any part of the note written inside angle brackets is missing from the email. When the note starts with {{<}}, the whole note is missing.

*Expected behaviour:* as the ticket says, "The sent work order email should include the full note body/message exactly as entered, regardless of whether attachments, BCC recipients, or additional recipients are included."

This looks like the same kind of problem as [SV-11227|https://shopview.atlassian.net/browse/SV-11227] (mention emails showing note text as formatting), which is Done. The invoice and estimate emails still behave this way.

h3. What I could not show separately

The BCC copy goes to the signed-in user's own address, which is the same inbox as the contact's address, so the inbox shows one copy per email. In tries 3, 5, 6, 7 and 9 that one copy had the same note as the contact's copy.
