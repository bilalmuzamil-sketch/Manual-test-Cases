ShopView QA Team Dashboard

Scope: the live epics the QA team is working on — picked automatically at every refresh: an epic is in scope when it is not closed and at least one QA member is the QA Assignee on an open ticket under it. When an epic is closed it drops off on the next refresh. This refresh: . Outside the project-wide panels (marked as such), nothing outside these epics is counted. Every story, defect, bug and task under them is included, whether or not a QA member is on it. Filter by QA member, epic, and time period — every number, chart, and table below recomputes for that selection. Pick a member to focus the whole page on one person. Who-did-what comes from the QAComplete_<name> labels first, then the QA Assignee field; InProgress_<name> labels feed the in-progress tracker. All dates are Pakistan time.

🏆 Bug hunting leaderboard — who finds and reports the bugs

Hunting down and reporting a bug is the hardest QA work, so it leads the page. Ranked by Bugs + Story Defects created in the selected date range (the bar). The Today / Wk / Mo / Until today columns are fixed reference points ("Until today" = the whole 2-month data window). The member & epic filters apply.

🎫 Tickets created by each QA member

How many tickets each QA member created (they are the reporter), counted by the day the ticket was created. By week shows one week at a time — a column per dated day, Monday to Sunday, with the week's total on the right. By month compares this month with the previous month. This table is project-wide — it counts every ticket the person created anywhere in ShopView, so it is not limited to the epics in scope and ignores the epic filter (the rest of the page stays scoped to those epics). The member filter still applies. Dates are Pakistan time.

Label hygiene — fix these to make the tracking reliable
    🙋 Assigned to a QA member & not yet Done — QA likely needs to respond

    Tickets whose Assignee is a QA member and status is less than Done — usually a question or change waiting on QA. Also includes Tasks raised by a QA member that are still open (shown with whom they're assigned to). Respects the epic/member filters.

    🧾 In a QA member's queue (QA Assignee) & not yet Done

    Tickets where the QA Assignee field is a QA member and status is less than Done — the QA member's open testing workload. Respects the epic/member filters.
