Label hygiene — fix these to make the tracking reliable
    🙋 Assigned to a QA member & not yet Done — QA likely needs to respond

    Tickets whose Assignee is a QA member and status is less than Done — usually a question or change waiting on QA. Also includes Tasks raised by a QA member that are still open (shown with whom they're assigned to). Respects the epic/member filters.

    🧾 In a QA member's queue (QA Assignee) & not yet Done

    Tickets where the QA Assignee field is a QA member and status is less than Done — the QA member's open testing workload. Respects the epic/member filters.
