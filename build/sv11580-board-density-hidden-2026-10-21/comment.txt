h3. How to see it

# Open staging: [https://app.staging.shopview.com/] and sign in as Admin. At the top right, choose the location {{Staging Heavy Duty - 9919}}.
# In the top menu, click {{Work Orders}}. Above the list, on the right, click the last of the three view icons ({{Board View}}).
# Between the two thin dividers to the left of the view icons there is only one icon, the columns icon. Hover it: the tooltip reads {{Column Selection}}. Click it to open the field list.
# Click the middle view icon ({{Tech View}}). Two icons sit between the dividers there: {{Density}} and {{Column Selection}}.
