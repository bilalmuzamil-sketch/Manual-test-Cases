- **cod-switch-back**: Whether switching from COD back to Net 30 restores the old limit: S5-R5 "Switching from COD or Credit Hold to another term leaves the limit at $0.00 until the user enters one." Answered by S5-R5.
- **new-organization**: How a tester gets an organization created after release (S11-R12) is a test-environment question for the QA lead, not a product decision; it is in REPORT.md OUTSTANDING, and the case is held with that reason.
- **accountinghub-visibility**: What AccountingHub and IBS receive cannot be seen in ShopView; that is a test-access question for the QA lead, not a product decision (REPORT.md OUTSTANDING).
- **every-status**: Whether archived or inactive customers count: Terminology "Customers and vendors count and are listed whatever their status." Answered.
- **duplicate-inactive**: Whether a new name may match a deactivated term of the shop: S3-R9 "Name matches another of the shop's terms, active or inactive". Answered.
