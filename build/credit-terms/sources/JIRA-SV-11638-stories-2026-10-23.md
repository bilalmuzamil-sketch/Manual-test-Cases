# SV-11670 — Credit terms - Story 1 - Credit Terms page and list

Status: Open · created 2026-10-22T11:45:40.196-0500 · updated 2026-10-22T12:26:03.965-0500 · comments 0

## Summary

As a shop owner or office manager, I want one page that lists every credit term my shop has, how each one falls due and who is on it, so that I can see and manage my shop's terms in one place.

## Context

Today a shop has no place to see or manage its credit terms; the list is fixed and nobody can tell who is on which term. This story adds the Credit Terms page under Settings, in the Finance group, listing every term, how it falls due and how many customers and vendors hold it.

Only users with 'Settings → Finance' can open the page. The row menus (Story 2), the New Credit Term window (Story 3) and the audit log (Story 12) sit on this page but are built in their own stories.

## Requirements

* S1-R1: Under Settings, in the Finance group, a "Credit Terms" item appears directly below "Payment Methods". Selecting it opens the Credit Terms page.
* S1-R2: The page header shows the title "Credit Terms" and, top right, a primary "New Credit Term" button (Story 3: Create or edit a credit term) with a "⋯" page menu beside it (Story 12: Credit Terms audit log).
* S1-R3: Below the header are two tabs, "Active" and "Inactive", each followed by a count of the terms in it. The page opens on Active.
* S1-R4: Each tab shows one table with the columns Name, Due, Customers, Vendors, and an unlabeled actions column holding the row's "⋯" menu (Story 2: Row menu actions). The columns keep the same widths on both tabs.
* S1-R5: Rows are listed in this order on both tabs: COD; Due on Receipt; every Net term (standard and the shop's own) by number of days, lowest first; every "day of the following month" term by day, lowest first; any other term A to Z; Credit Hold last. Ties sort A to Z by name. The same order is used in every customer and vendor Credit Terms dropdown.
* S1-R6: Clicking a column header sorts the table by that column, following the app-wide sorting rule: the first click sorts Name A to Z, Customers and Vendors lowest first, and Due in the S1-R5 order; each further click reverses the direction. The sort is not saved: leaving and returning to the page shows the S1-R5 order.
* S1-R7: The Due column describes each term in words:

    * COD: "Paid when the invoice is created"
    * Due on Receipt: "Due on the invoice date"
    * Credit Hold: "Invoicing is blocked"
    * A Net term: "{X} days after the invoice date" ("1 day after the invoice date" when X is 1), for example "30 days after the invoice date"
    * A "day of the following month" term: "{ordinal day} of the following month", for example "10th of the following month", "1st of the following month", "22nd of the following month"
    
* S1-R8: COD, Due on Receipt and Credit Hold each show, after the name, an info icon and a lock icon. Hovering the info icon shows:

    * COD: "Cash on delivery. COD customers can't charge to account, so payment is taken when the invoice is created."
    * Due on Receipt: "Payment is due on the invoice date. You can charge the invoice to the customer's account instead of taking payment."
    * Credit Hold: "No invoices can be created for a customer on Credit Hold until their credit term is changed."
    
* S1-R9: Hovering the lock icon shows "Always available." with the line "COD, Due on Receipt and Credit Hold can't be deactivated." below it.
* S1-R10: No other term (standard Net terms and every term the shop created) shows an info icon or a lock icon.
* S1-R11: The shop's default term shows a "Default" tag after its name (after the info and lock icons for COD and Due on Receipt). Exactly one row carries the tag.
* S1-R12: The Customers column shows how many customers hold the term, right aligned, showing 0 when none. The Counter Sale customer is never counted. A customer with a blank term is counted under COD. Names that differ only in capitals and spaces count toward the same term ("NET 30" counts as Net 30; see §6).
* S1-R13: The Vendors column shows how many vendors hold the term, right aligned, 0 when none, by the same rules as S1-R12 (a blank vendor term counts under COD).
* S1-R14: The Active tab lists every active term; the Inactive tab lists every deactivated term, standard and the shop's own, in the same columns and order.
* S1-R15: Term names are never cut off in the table; a long name wraps onto a second line, with its icons and "Default" tag after it.
* S1-R16: The page is organization-wide. Switching location while on the page reloads it with the same terms, counts and default.
* S1-N1: If the user does not have the 'Settings → Finance' setting, they do not see "Credit Terms" under Settings, and opening the page's address directly does not show the page (same behavior as Payment Methods).
* S1-N2: If the Inactive tab has no terms, it shows the line "No inactive terms" in place of the table, and its count reads 0.
* S1-N3: Every row has a "⋯" menu, because "Move Customers" appears on every row for every user who can open the page (S2-R1), greyed when it cannot be used. Credit Hold and the default COD row therefore always have a menu.
* S1-E1: If every term that can be deactivated has been deactivated, the Active tab lists COD, Due on Receipt, Credit Hold and, when the default is another term, the default (it can't be deactivated, S2-N4). The Inactive tab lists everything else.
* S1-E2: A term held only by invoices (no customer or vendor on it today) shows 0 in both columns but still counts as in use for Story 2: Row menu actions's locks.
* S1-E3: While the list is loading, neither tab shows the "No inactive terms" line.
* Error handling: If the list cannot be loaded, the page shows the line "Something went wrong loading credit terms. Please try again." above the tabs. It stays until the page is reloaded successfully.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Given a user with 'Settings → Finance', when they open Settings, then "Credit Terms" appears in the Finance group directly below "Payment Methods" and opens the Credit Terms page on the Active tab (S1-R1, S1-R3).
- [ ] Given the page is open, then the header shows "Credit Terms", a "New Credit Term" button and a "⋯" page menu, and the table shows Name, Due, Customers, Vendors and an actions column with the same widths on both tabs (S1-R2, S1-R4).
- [ ] Given the page opens, then rows run COD, Due on Receipt, Net terms by days, "day of the following month" terms by day, any other term A to Z, Credit Hold last (S1-R5).
- [ ] Given a column header is clicked, then the table sorts by it per S1-R6, a further click reverses it, and leaving and returning shows the S1-R5 order again.
- [ ] Given each kind of term, then the Due column reads exactly as S1-R7 (for example "30 days after the invoice date", "1 day after the invoice date", "10th of the following month").
- [ ] Given COD, Due on Receipt and Credit Hold, then each shows an info icon and a lock icon with the exact S1-R8 and S1-R9 hover texts, and no other term shows either icon (S1-R10).
- [ ] Given the shop default, then exactly one row shows the "Default" tag (S1-R11).
- [ ] Given customers stored as "NET 30", customers with a blank term and the Counter Sale customer, then "NET 30" counts under Net 30, blank counts under COD and Counter Sale is never counted; vendors follow the same rules (S1-R12, S1-R13).
- [ ] Given a long term name, then it wraps onto a second line and is never cut off (S1-R15).
- [ ] Given the user switches location on the page, then it reloads with the same terms, counts and default (S1-R16).
- [ ] Given a user without 'Settings → Finance', when they look under Settings or open the page address directly, then they do not see the page (S1-N1).
- [ ] Given no inactive terms, when the Inactive tab is opened, then it shows "No inactive terms" and its count reads 0; while loading, that line does not show (S1-N2, S1-E3).
- [ ] Given the list fails to load, then "Something went wrong loading credit terms. Please try again." shows above the tabs until a successful reload.

# SV-11671 — Credit terms - Story 2 - Row menu actions (Edit, Make Default, Deactivate, Reactivate, Delete)

Status: Open · created 2026-10-22T11:45:54.939-0500 · updated 2026-10-22T12:26:03.342-0500 · comments 0

## Summary

As a shop owner or office manager, I want each term's actions in its own row menu, with impossible actions left out and blocked ones explained, so that I can manage a term without guessing what is allowed.

## Context

Each term on the Credit Terms page has its own "⋯" row menu holding Edit, Make Default for New Customers, Move Customers, Deactivate, Reactivate and Delete Credit Term. Items that never apply to a term are left out; items that apply but are blocked show greyed with the reason on hover.

Users need 'Settings → Finance' to reach the page (Story 1). Move Customers itself is built in Story 4 and the Edit window in Story 3; this story owns the menu, the blocking rules, Make Default, Deactivate, Reactivate and Delete.

## Requirements

* S2-R1: Each row's "⋯" menu shows only the items that apply to that term, in this order. An item that applies but is blocked shows greyed out and does nothing when clicked; hovering it shows the reason.

    * "Edit" (pencil); Shown on: Active terms the shop created; Blocked when (hover text): The term is in use: "Used by {holders}" with "Deactivate it instead to take it out of the pickers." below
    * "Make Default for New Customers" (circle check); Shown on: Active terms that are not the default and not Credit Hold; Blocked when (hover text): Never
    * "Move Customers" (person); Shown on: Every term, active or inactive; Blocked when (hover text): The user lacks 'Customers → Create & Edit' or 'View and Manage AP/AR Data': "You need Customers → Create & Edit and View and Manage AP/AR Data to move customers." Otherwise no customer holds the term: "No customers are on this term"
    * "Deactivate" (archive); Shown on: Active terms except COD, Due on Receipt and Credit Hold; Blocked when (hover text): The term is the default: "This is your default for new customers" with "Pick another default first." below
    * "Reactivate" (rotate); Shown on: Inactive terms; on the Inactive tab it comes before "Move Customers"; Blocked when (hover text): Never
    * "Delete Credit Term" (trash, red, last, below a divider); Shown on: Terms the shop created, active or inactive; Blocked when (hover text): The term is the default: "This is your default for new customers" / "Pick another default first."; otherwise the term is in use: "Used by {holders}" / "Deactivate it instead to take it out of the pickers."
    
* S2-R2: "{holders}" names only the holder types the term has, each with its count, singular or plural: for example "Used by 1 customer and 2 vendors", "Used by 12 customers, 3 vendors and 40 invoices", "Used by 5 invoices".
* S2-R3: Hovering "Deactivate" (when not blocked) shows "Hide from customer and vendor pickers". Hovering "Reactivate" shows "Show in pickers again".
* S2-R4: "Edit" opens the "Edit Credit Term" window (Story 3: Create or edit a credit term) filled with the term's name, kind and days.
* S2-R5: "Make Default for New Customers" opens a confirmation titled "Make {name} the default?" with the message "New customers start on {name}. Customers you already have keep their current term." and the buttons "Cancel" and "Make Default".
* S2-R6: On "Make Default", the term becomes the default, the "Default" tag moves to its row, and a success toast shows "{name} is now the default" with the line "New customers start on {name}." No customer, vendor or invoice changes.
* S2-R7: "Deactivate" acts at once, without a confirmation: the term moves to the Inactive tab, both tab counts update, and a success toast shows "{name} deactivated". Every customer and vendor on the term keeps it (Story 5: New Customer and Edit Customer, Story 6: Vendors).
* S2-R8: "Reactivate" acts at once: the term moves back to the Active tab, the counts update, it appears in the customer and vendor pickers again, and a success toast shows "{name} reactivated".
* S2-R9: "Delete Credit Term" opens a confirmation titled "Confirmation" with the message "Are you sure you want to delete this credit term?" and the buttons "Cancel" and a red "Delete Credit Term". On confirm the term is removed from the page and a success toast shows "{name} deleted".
* S2-R10: Make Default is not available on inactive terms (it is absent from their menu).
* S2-R11: The confirm button in the Make Default and Delete confirmations is disabled while the change is saving, so a double click saves once.
* S2-R12: Every success toast that shows a term name ({name} or {term}) on the Credit Terms page, in Story 3: Create or edit a credit term and in Story 4: Move Customers shows the full name; a long name wraps and is never cut off.
* S2-N1: Standard terms never show "Edit" or "Delete Credit Term".
* S2-N2: COD, Due on Receipt and Credit Hold never show "Deactivate" (S1-R9 explains why on the lock icon).
* S2-N3: Credit Hold never shows "Make Default for New Customers".
* S2-N4: The current default never shows "Make Default for New Customers"; its "Deactivate" and, for a term the shop created, "Delete Credit Term" show blocked with the default reason (S2-R1).
* S2-N5: A term in use shows "Edit" and "Delete Credit Term" blocked with the "Used by ..." reason; "Deactivate" stays available.
* S2-N6: Clicking or pressing Enter on a blocked item does nothing and keeps the menu open.
* S2-E1: Editing the name of an unused term that is the current default keeps it the default under its new name.
* S2-E2: If a term becomes in use, becomes the default, or is changed by another user after the page loaded, the save is refused with the reason in S2 Error Handling, and the page keeps its state.
* S2-E3: Deactivating a standard term in use (for example Net 30 with 290 customers) is allowed; those customers keep Net 30, shown "Net 30 (Inactive)" on their card and in Edit Customer.
* Error handling: If a save is refused, the app's standard error alert shows the reason, using these exact texts: "This is your default for new customers. Pick another default first." (deactivating or deleting the default); "Used by {holders}. Deactivate it instead to take it out of the pickers." (editing or deleting a term in use); "Always available. COD, Due on Receipt and Credit Hold can't be deactivated."; "Pick an active term other than Credit Hold." (making an inactive term or Credit Hold the default); "Credit term not found." (the term was deleted meanwhile). The delete confirmation stays open when the delete is refused.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Given a term the shop created that is active and unused, when its "⋯" menu opens, then it shows Edit, Make Default for New Customers, Move Customers, Deactivate and a red Delete Credit Term below a divider, in the S2-R1 order.
- [ ] Given a term in use by 12 customers, 3 vendors and 40 invoices, then Edit and Delete Credit Term show greyed with "Used by 12 customers, 3 vendors and 40 invoices" and "Deactivate it instead to take it out of the pickers." and Deactivate stays available (S2-R2, S2-N5).
- [ ] Given Make Default for New Customers is chosen and confirmed, then the "Default" tag moves to that row, the toast "{name} is now the default" shows with "New customers start on {name}.", and no customer, vendor or invoice changes (S2-R5, S2-R6).
- [ ] Given Deactivate is chosen, then the term moves to the Inactive tab at once with no confirmation, both counts update, the toast "{name} deactivated" shows, and its customers and vendors keep it (S2-R7).
- [ ] Given Reactivate is chosen on the Inactive tab, then the term moves back to Active, appears in the pickers again and the toast "{name} reactivated" shows (S2-R8).
- [ ] Given Delete Credit Term is confirmed on an unused term the shop created, then the row is removed and the toast "{name} deleted" shows (S2-R9).
- [ ] Given the confirm button in Make Default or Delete is double clicked, then the change saves once (S2-R11).
- [ ] Given a standard term, then Edit and Delete Credit Term never show; given COD, Due on Receipt or Credit Hold, then Deactivate never shows; given Credit Hold or an inactive term, then Make Default never shows (S2-N1, S2-N2, S2-N3, S2-R10).
- [ ] Given the current default, then Make Default is absent and Deactivate and Delete Credit Term show greyed with "This is your default for new customers" / "Pick another default first." (S2-N4).
- [ ] Given a blocked item, when it is clicked or Enter is pressed on it, then nothing happens and the menu stays open (S2-N6).
- [ ] Given another user made the term in use or the default after the page loaded, when the save is attempted, then it is refused with the exact S2 Error Handling text and the delete confirmation stays open (S2-E2).

# SV-11672 — Credit terms - Story 3 - Create or edit a credit term

Status: Open · created 2026-10-22T11:45:56.259-0500 · updated 2026-10-22T12:26:02.702-0500 · comments 0

## Summary

As a shop owner or office manager, I want to add the terms my shop actually trades on, as "Net X days" or a day of the following month, so that I can put customers and vendors on them.

## Context

Shops trade on terms the fixed list does not have, such as Net 14 or the 10th of the following month. This story adds the New Credit Term and Edit Credit Term window, with a Net X days option and a day of the following month option, a live explanation line and exact validation messages.

Users need 'Settings → Finance'. Edit only opens for an active, unused term the shop created (Story 2). A new term is usable in every customer and vendor dropdown at once.

## Requirements

* S3-R1: "New Credit Term" opens a window titled "New Credit Term"; Edit opens the same window titled "Edit Credit Term". The buttons are "Cancel" and "Create Credit Term" (New) or "Save Changes" (Edit).
* S3-R2: The window holds:

    * Name (required), focused on open, accepting at most 31 characters, with the hint "Up to 31 characters. Prints on invoices and goes to QuickBooks."
    * Two options, one chosen at a time: "Due Net X days" with a Days box followed by "days after the invoice date", and "Due day of the following month" with a Day box followed by "of the following month".
    
* S3-R3: A new term opens on "Due Net X days" with both boxes empty and the Day box disabled. Only the chosen option's box is active; the other is disabled and empty.
* S3-R4: Clicking the disabled box chooses its option and puts the cursor in it. Switching options empties the box being left, so only the chosen option's number is checked and saved.
* S3-R5: While the chosen box holds a valid number, a blue information line below the options explains the term, using today's date as the example invoice date (S3-R16):

    * Net: "{Name} means payment is due {X} days after the invoice date. An invoice dated {today} is due {due}." For example: "Net 14 Days means payment is due 14 days after the invoice date. An invoice dated Oct 21 is due Nov 4."
    * Day of the following month: "{Name} means payment is due on the {ordinal X} of the following month. An invoice dated {today} is due {due}." For example: "10th of next month means payment is due on the 10th of the following month. An invoice dated Oct 21 is due Nov 10."
    * With the Name empty, "{Name}" reads "This term". Dates read as a short month and day ("Oct 21"). The line updates as the user types and disappears while the number is invalid.
    
* S3-R6: "Create Credit Term" / "Save Changes" stays disabled until the Name and the chosen box are both valid.
* S3-R7: On Create, the window closes, a success toast shows "{name} created", and the term appears on the Active tab in its S1-R5 position, with 0 customers and 0 vendors, available at once in every customer and vendor Credit Terms dropdown.
* S3-R8: On Save Changes, the window closes, a success toast shows "{name} saved", and the row shows the new name and Due text.
* S3-R9: Validation rules and their exact messages, shown under the field:

    * Name empty: "Enter a name."
    * The Name field stops accepting characters after the 31st. A longer name reaching the server is refused with "Use 31 characters or fewer."
    * Name matches a standard term ignoring capitals and spaces (for example "net 30" or "Net30"): "{Standard name} is already a standard term. Pick another name." (for example "Net 30 is already a standard term. Pick another name.")
    * Name matches another of the shop's terms, active or inactive, ignoring capitals and spaces: "A term called {existing name} already exists."
    * Days not a whole number from 1 to 365: "Enter 1 to 365 days."
    * Day not a whole number from 1 to 31: "Enter a day from 1 to 31."
    
* S3-R10: The Name is checked only once the user has typed in it (then each time they leave the field) or presses Create / Save. Opening the window, or clicking an option or a box before typing a name, never shows a Name error.
* S3-R11: The chosen box re-checks on every change: correcting an invalid number (for example 0 to 25 in the Day box) clears its error immediately, without leaving the box.
* S3-R12: Leading and trailing spaces in the Name are removed, and runs of spaces become one space, before the name is saved.
* S3-R13: Editing a term keeps its own name valid (saving "Net 10" unchanged on the term "Net 10" is not a duplicate).
* S3-R14: "Create Credit Term" and "Save Changes" are disabled while saving, so a double click saves once.
* S3-R15: Pressing Escape or clicking outside the window does not close it, and keeps everything typed.
* S3-R16: The example date in S3-R5 is today on the calendar of the location the user is working in; after a location switch it follows the new location.
* S3-N1: If the user does not have the 'Settings → Finance' setting, they cannot reach the page and do not see "New Credit Term".
* S3-N2: If the term is in use, standard, or inactive, Edit is not offered or is blocked (Story 2: Row menu actions), so the window cannot open in Edit mode for it.
* S3-N3: "Cancel" or the close icon closes the window without saving and clears its contents; reopening "New Credit Term" starts empty.
* S3-E1: A name that reads like a standard term but is not one (for example "Net 14", "Net 10", "Net 6") is allowed. The name and the rule are independent: a term named "Net 6" may be due on the 25th of the following month. The Due column always shows the rule.
* S3-E2: If another user creates a term with the same name while the window is open, Create is refused with "A term called {name} already exists." and the window stays open.
* Error handling: If Create or Save is refused by the server, the app's standard error alert shows the reason (the S3-R9 texts, or "Pick Due Net X days or Due day of the following month." when no option is sent, or "Used by {holders}. Deactivate it instead to take it out of the pickers." when the term became in use meanwhile). The window stays open with the user's input.
* Error handling: On Create, a success toast shows "{name} created". On Save Changes, a success toast shows "{name} saved". Both are the same green toast as the page's other actions.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Given "New Credit Term" is clicked, then a window titled "New Credit Term" opens with Name focused, "Due Net X days" chosen, both boxes empty and the Day box disabled (S3-R1, S3-R2, S3-R3).
- [ ] Given the disabled Day box is clicked, then "Due day of the following month" is chosen, the cursor is in Day, and the Days box empties (S3-R4).
- [ ] Given Name "Net 14 Days" and Days 14 on Oct 21, then the blue line reads "Net 14 Days means payment is due 14 days after the invoice date. An invoice dated Oct 21 is due Nov 4." and it disappears while the number is invalid (S3-R5).
- [ ] Given a valid name and number, when "Create Credit Term" is pressed, then the window closes, the toast "{name} created" shows and the term appears on Active in its S1-R5 position with 0 customers and 0 vendors (S3-R7).
- [ ] Given an unused term is edited and saved, then the toast "{name} saved" shows and the row shows the new name and Due text (S3-R8).
- [ ] Given each invalid input in S3-R9, then the exact message shows under the field (for example "Net 30 is already a standard term. Pick another name.", "Enter 1 to 365 days.", "Enter a day from 1 to 31.").
- [ ] Given the window has just opened, when the user clicks an option or a box before typing a name, then no Name error shows (S3-R10).
- [ ] Given Day holds 0, when it is changed to 25, then the error clears immediately without leaving the box (S3-R11).
- [ ] Given the name "  Net   14 " is saved, then it saves as "Net 14" (S3-R12); given "Net 10" is saved unchanged on the term "Net 10", then it is not a duplicate (S3-R13).
- [ ] Given Escape or a click outside, then the window stays open with everything typed; given Cancel or the close icon, then it closes without saving and reopens empty (S3-R15, S3-N3).
- [ ] Given a double click on Create or Save, then the term saves once (S3-R14).
- [ ] Given another user created the same name meanwhile, when Create is pressed, then "A term called {name} already exists." shows and the window stays open (S3-E2).

# SV-11673 — Credit terms - Story 4 - Move Customers

Status: Open · created 2026-10-22T11:45:57.242-0500 · updated 2026-10-22T12:26:02.216-0500 · comments 0

## Summary

As a shop owner or office manager, I want to move some or all customers on one term to another term in one step, so that I do not have to open and edit each customer.

## Context

Changing many customers to another term today means opening and editing each one. Move Customers moves some or all customers on one term to another active term in one step, from the term's row menu on the Credit Terms page.

The user needs 'Settings → Finance', 'Customers → Create & Edit' and 'View and Manage AP/AR Data', and at least one customer other than the Counter Sale customer must hold the term. Moving to COD or Credit Hold sets each moved customer's credit limit to $0 after a confirmation.

## Requirements

* S4-R1: "Move Customers" in a term's row menu (Story 2: Row menu actions) opens a window titled "Move Customers from {term}".
* S4-R2: The window shows, top to bottom: a "Search customers" box; "Select All" and "Select None" with "{n} selected" on the right; a scrolling list of every customer on the term, A to Z, each with a checkbox; a "Move To" dropdown; and the buttons "Cancel" and "Move {n} Customers" ("Move 1 Customer" when one is selected).
* S4-R3: The list holds every customer whose term matches this term ignoring capitals and spaces. On COD it also holds customers with a blank term. The Counter Sale customer is never listed.
* S4-R4: Every listed customer starts ticked.
* S4-R5: Typing in the search box narrows the list to customers whose name contains the text, ignoring capitals. Pressing Enter in the search box does not move anyone. When nothing matches, the list area shows "No customers match your search."
* S4-R6: "Select All" ticks, and "Select None" unticks, every customer the search currently matches, including those not scrolled into view. Customers hidden by the search keep their ticks.
* S4-R7: "Move To" offers every active term except the one being moved from, in the S1-R5 order. It is prefilled with the shop's default; while the default is selected, the hint "Prefilled with your default for new customers." shows below it. When the term being moved from is the default, "Move To" starts empty.
* S4-R8: "Move {n} Customers" is disabled while no customer is ticked or no term is chosen in "Move To".
* S4-R9: On confirm, each ticked customer's credit term changes to the chosen term, the window closes, the page's Customers counts update, and a success toast shows "{n} customers moved to {term}." ("1 customer moved to {term}." for one).
* S4-R10: When "Move To" is COD or Credit Hold, the window shows the line "Their credit limit will be set to $0." below "Move To".

# SV-11676 — Credit terms - Story 7 - Due dates

Status: Open · created 2026-10-22T11:46:00.118-0500 · updated 2026-10-22T12:26:00.324-0500 · comments 0

## Summary

As a shop owner, I want every invoice and vendor invoice to fall due exactly as its term says, on the calendar of the location the invoice belongs to, so that due dates, aging and statements are right.

## Context

Every invoice and vendor invoice must fall due exactly as its term says, including the shop's own Net terms and the new "day of the following month" terms. Due dates are counted on the calendar of the location the record belongs to, so a daylight saving change never moves them.

No permission is involved; this applies to every shop on every invoice and vendor invoice. Due dates already stored are never rewritten.

## Requirements

* S7-R1: A Net term (standard or the shop's own) is due that many days after the invoice date: an invoice dated Oct 5 on Net 30 is due Nov 4; on a shop's "Net 10" it is due Oct 15.
* S7-R2: A "day of the following month" term is always due in the month after the invoice date, whatever the day of the invoice: with day 10, an invoice dated Oct 5 and one dated Oct 28 are both due Nov 10.
* S7-R3: When the following month is shorter than the term's day, the due date is that month's last day: day 31 invoiced in October is due Nov 30; day 30 or 31 invoiced in January is due Feb 28 (Feb 29 in a leap year); day 31 invoiced in March is due Apr 30.
* S7-R4: An invoice dated in December on a "day of the following month" term is due in January of the next year (day 10, invoice Dec 15 2026, due Jan 10 2027).
* S7-R5: COD, Due on Receipt and Credit Hold are due on the invoice date, as today.
* S7-R6: Due dates are counted on the calendar of the location the invoice or vendor invoice belongs to, never the location of the user, API connection or import that creates or views it, so a daylight saving change inside the span never moves the date: an Edmonton invoice dated Oct 5 on "10th of the following month" is due Nov 10 (not Nov 9).
* S7-R6a: The same invoice shows the same due date to every user, whichever location they are working in. A vendor invoice created by receiving a delivery uses the delivery's location, even when the receiving user is working in another location.
* S7-R6b: When the record's location has no time zone set, the due date is counted on UTC.
* S7-R7: An invoice keeps the term and due date it was created with. Changing the customer's term later, deactivating the term or moving the customer never changes an existing invoice.
* S7-R8: When a user changes the invoice date on an invoice (existing behavior), the due date is recalculated from the customer's term by the rules above, including the shop's own terms.
* S7-R9: A vendor invoice stores no term. Its due date is calculated by the same rules from the vendor's term when the vendor invoice is created, recalculated when its invoice date or its vendor changes, and stored.
* S7-R10: A Net 30 invoice or vendor invoice whose span crosses the March or November clock change is due on the 30th calendar day after its date.
* S7-R11: The Vendor Bill Export shows each vendor invoice's stored due date (S7-R9), never one recalculated from the vendor's current term; it is blank when no due date is stored.
* S7-N1: A term whose text ShopView does not recognize (from an old import or the Public API) is dated exactly as today.
* S7-E1: A deactivated term still dates invoices and vendor invoices for the customers and vendors that keep it.
* S7-E2: Due dates already stored on existing invoices and vendor invoices are never rewritten. A screen that recalculates a due date from the customer's term shows the date on the calendar of the transaction's own location (S7-R6, S11-R14).
* S7-E3: An invoice created after a reversal takes the customer's term at that moment, as today: a deactivated term the customer still holds, or the new term after a move or edit. The reversed invoice's old due date does not come back.
* S7-E4: If the customer on an open work order or part sale is changed, the invoice takes the new customer's term at invoicing, by the same rule. Nothing about the term is copied onto the work order or part sale before invoicing.
* Error handling: None. Due-date calculation has no user-facing failure.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Given an invoice dated Oct 5, then on Net 30 it is due Nov 4 and on a shop's "Net 10" it is due Oct 15 (S7-R1).
- [ ] Given a day 10 "following month" term, then invoices dated Oct 5 and Oct 28 are both due Nov 10 (S7-R2).
- [ ] Given day 31 invoiced in October, then the due date is Nov 30; day 30 or 31 invoiced in January is due Feb 28 (Feb 29 in a leap year) (S7-R3).
- [ ] Given day 10 and an invoice dated Dec 15 2026, then it is due Jan 10 2027 (S7-R4).
- [ ] Given COD, Due on Receipt or Credit Hold, then the invoice is due on its invoice date (S7-R5).
- [ ] Given an Edmonton invoice dated Oct 5 on "10th of the following month", then it is due Nov 10, and every user sees the same date from any location (S7-R6, S7-R6a).
- [ ] Given a location with no time zone set, then the due date is counted on UTC (S7-R6b).
- [ ] Given an existing invoice, when the customer's term changes, the term is deactivated or the customer is moved, then the invoice keeps its term and due date (S7-R7).
- [ ] Given the invoice date is changed, then the due date is recalculated from the customer's term by these rules (S7-R8).
- [ ] Given a vendor invoice, then its due date is calculated from the vendor's term at creation, recalculated when its invoice date or vendor changes, and stored; the Vendor Bill Export shows that stored date (S7-R9, S7-R11).
- [ ] Given a Net 30 span crossing the March or November clock change, then the due date is the 30th calendar day after the invoice date (S7-R10).
- [ ] Given a term text ShopView does not recognize, then the invoice is dated exactly as today (S7-N1).

# SV-11677 — Credit terms - Story 8 - QuickBooks sync - credit terms

Status: Open · created 2026-10-22T11:46:00.998-0500 · updated 2026-10-22T12:25:59.612-0500 · comments 0

## Summary

As a shop owner using QuickBooks Online, I want my shop's own terms to appear in QuickBooks with the same due dates, so that the books match ShopView without setting terms up twice.

## Context

Shops using QuickBooks Online need their own terms to exist in QuickBooks with the same due dates. A term is created in QuickBooks only when a record that uses it is sent, the same way the standard terms work today.

Applies only when the QuickBooks Online integration is connected. ShopView never changes or removes a term already in QuickBooks, and AccountingHub and IBS are unchanged.

## Requirements

* S8-R1: When a record is sent to QuickBooks with a term QuickBooks does not have, ShopView first creates the term in QuickBooks under the same name, then sends the record. This is how the standard terms already work today.
* S8-R2: A "Due Net X days" term is created in QuickBooks as a standard term due in X days.
* S8-R3: A "Due day of the following month" term is created in QuickBooks as a date-driven term due on day X of the following month, so QuickBooks dates an invoice dated Oct 5 or Oct 28 with day 10 as due Nov 10, matching ShopView.
* S8-R4: A term already in QuickBooks under the same name is used as it is; ShopView never changes a term in QuickBooks.
* S8-R5: Each invoice carries its own term and due date to QuickBooks, as today. A vendor invoice is sent to QuickBooks as a bill with its stored due date (S7-R9) and the vendor's term at the moment it is sent.
* S8-R6: Changing a customer's term in ShopView, by Edit Customer or by Move Customers (Story 4: Move Customers), does not change the customer record in QuickBooks, exactly as an Edit Customer save does not today. The QuickBooks customer keeps the term it was created with; new invoices still carry the right term.
* S8-R7: Deactivating, reactivating, editing or deleting a term in ShopView sends nothing to QuickBooks. A term already created in QuickBooks stays there.
* S8-R8: AccountingHub and IBS are unchanged: both receive each invoice's or vendor invoice's due date only, never the term name, so a shop's own term names never reach them.
* S8-N1: If the QuickBooks integration is not connected, nothing is sent and Story 3: Create or edit a credit term, Story 4: Move Customers, Story 5: New Customer and Edit Customer and Story 7: Due dates behave exactly as they do without the integration.
* S8-N2: A term the shop created but nobody has used on a synced record is never sent to QuickBooks.
* S8-E1: If QuickBooks already has a term with the same name but a different rule, QuickBooks keeps its own rule for that term (S8-R4). Each invoice still carries its own due date, so invoices stay correct; only the QuickBooks customer's term may differ.

# SV-11679 — Credit terms - Story 10 - Imports and Public API

Status: Open · created 2026-10-22T11:46:02.888-0500 · updated 2026-10-22T12:25:58.613-0500 · comments 0

## Summary

As a shop owner importing customers or connecting another system, I want new customers without a term to get my shop's default, so that every customer starts on the right term whichever way it was created.

## Context

Customers created by the contacts import or the Public API must start on the right term too. A customer created without a term gets the shop default, and term text matching one of the shop's terms is saved in that term's spelling.

Contacts import uses the existing import permission and the Public API a valid API key, both unchanged. Imports and the API never refuse a row or request because of its credit term.

## Requirements

* S10-R1: A customer created by the contacts import with a blank credit term gets the shop's default.
* S10-R2: A customer created through the Public API without a credit term, or with a blank one, gets the shop's default.
* S10-R3: A credit term that matches one of the shop's terms (standard or its own) ignoring capitals and spaces is saved in that term's spelling: "net 10", "NET10" and "Net 10" all save as the shop's "Net 10". This applies to customers and vendors created or updated by an import, and created or updated through the Public API.
* S10-R4: Any other text is saved exactly as sent and dates invoices as it does today (lenient, as today).
* S10-R5: Updating a customer's credit term through the Public API follows S10-R3 and S10-R4.
* S10-R6: A new customer created by the contacts import on COD or Credit Hold gets a $0 credit limit, whatever limit the file holds.
* S10-R7: The Public API reads, creates and updates a customer's credit term. It has no way to list the shop's terms or its default; an integrator sends a term name and reads back the saved spelling (S10-R3). The vendor API is unchanged.
* S10-R8: A customer created or updated through the Public API, or updated by the contacts import, onto COD or Credit Hold is saved with a $0 credit limit, whatever limit was sent; the request or row is not refused (S10-N1). A Public API update that sends neither a credit term nor a credit limit leaves the stored limit as it is.
* S10-N1: Imports and the Public API never refuse a row or a request because of its credit term.
* S10-N2: Vendors imported with a blank term start on COD, not the default (S6-E1).
* S10-E1: A name that matches a deactivated term is saved as that term; the customer then shows it as "{name} (Inactive)" (S5-R8).
* S10-E2: Credit limits sent with an import or API customer are handled as today, except as S10-R6 and S10-R8 state. Both apply only to customers created or updated after release; an existing COD or Credit Hold customer's stored limit is never changed until that customer is next created or updated by one of those paths (S11-R11).
* S10-E3: When an import updates an existing customer or vendor, a blank credit term cell keeps the stored term.
* Error handling: None new. Import and API errors are unchanged.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Given the contacts import creates a customer with a blank credit term, then it gets the shop default (S10-R1).
- [ ] Given the Public API creates a customer with no or a blank credit term, then it gets the shop default (S10-R2).
- [ ] Given "net 10", "NET10" or "Net 10" is sent for a customer or vendor by import or Public API, then it saves as the shop's "Net 10" (S10-R3, S10-R5).
- [ ] Given term text matching no term, then it is saved exactly as sent and dates invoices as today (S10-R4).
- [ ] Given a new imported customer on COD or Credit Hold with a $500 limit in the file, then it is saved with a $0 limit (S10-R6).
- [ ] Given a Public API create or update, or an import update, onto COD or Credit Hold with a limit sent, then the limit saves as $0 and the request or row is not refused (S10-R8, S10-N1).

- [ ] Given a name matching a deactivated term, then the customer is saved on it and shows "{name} (Inactive)" (S10-E1).

# SV-11681 — Credit terms - Story 12 - Credit Terms audit log

Status: Open · created 2026-10-22T11:46:04.685-0500 · updated 2026-10-22T12:25:57.669-0500 · comments 0

## Summary

As a shop owner or office manager, I want a log of every change made on the Credit Terms page, so that I can see who changed a term, the default or a group of customers, and when.

## Context

Shop owners need to see who changed a term, the default or a group of customers, and when. This story adds the Credit Terms Log, opened from the "⋯" page menu on the Credit Terms page, with the same layout as the Work Order Log and the Part Sale Log.

Users need 'Settings → Finance'. The log is organization-wide, starts empty on release day, cannot be edited or deleted, and records only changes saved on the Credit Terms page.

## Requirements

* S12-R1: The Credit Terms page header has a "⋯" page menu top right, beside "New Credit Term", with one item: "Audit Log".
* S12-R2: "Audit Log" opens a window titled "Credit Terms Log" with the same layout as the Work Order Log and the Part Sale Log: a search box, then a table with the columns Event, User, Details, Date and Time, newest first.
* S12-R3: Each change saved after release adds one row. There is no Line column. "{rule}" is the term's Due column text (S1-R7). The Event and Details columns read:

    * A term is created (Story 3: Create or edit a credit term); Event: "Credit term created"; Details: "{name}: {rule}", for example "Net 14: 14 days after the invoice date"
    * An unused term is edited (Story 3: Create or edit a credit term); Event: "Credit term edited"; Details: Only what changed: "Name: {old} → {new}" and/or "Due: {old rule} → {new rule}"
    * A term is deactivated (Story 2: Row menu actions); Event: "Credit term deactivated"; Details: "{name}: {rule}"
    * A term is reactivated (Story 2: Row menu actions); Event: "Credit term reactivated"; Details: "{name}: {rule}"
    * A term is deleted (Story 2: Row menu actions); Event: "Credit term deleted"; Details: "{name}: {rule}"
    * The default is changed (Story 2: Row menu actions); Event: "Default changed"; Details: "{old} → {new}", for example "COD → Net 14"
    * Customers are moved (Story 4: Move Customers); Event: "Customers moved"; Details: "{n} customers from {from} to {to}" ("1 customer from {from} to {to}" for one), followed by "Credit limit set to $0 (previous limits are in each customer's note)" when the target is COD or Credit Hold
    
* S12-R4: The User column shows the name of the user who made the change; Date and Time show when, on the calendar of the location the user is working in.
* S12-R5: Typing in the search box narrows the table to rows whose Event, User or Details text contains the search text, ignoring capitals.
* S12-R6: The log is organization-wide (one log for all locations), kept indefinitely, and cannot be edited or deleted by anyone.
* S12-R7: A row is written only when a change is saved. A refused or canceled action (the error handling of Stories 2, 3 and 4, or Cancel) writes nothing, and neither does an action that changes nothing (for example making the current default the default again).
* S12-R8: Event, User and Details text is never cut off; long names wrap.
* S12-N1: If the user does not have the 'Settings → Finance' setting, they cannot reach the page or its "⋯" menu, and a request for the log is refused by the server.
* S12-N2: Changes made before release are not in the log; it starts empty on release day.
* S12-N3: Changes made outside the Credit Terms page (a customer's or vendor's term or credit limit changed in New Customer, Edit Customer, Edit Vendor, by import or by the Public API) are not in this log.
* S12-E1: When the log has no rows, the table shows the log's standard empty state, "No history here. Yet."
* S12-E2: A deleted term's earlier rows (created, edited, deactivated) stay in the log under its name.
* S12-E3: A move where some ticked customers had already left the term (S4-E1) logs the number actually moved.
* Error handling: If the log cannot be loaded, the app's standard error alert shows "Something went wrong loading the Credit Terms Log. Please try again.", the table shows no rows and the same sentence in place of the "No history here. Yet." line, and the Credit Terms page is unaffected.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Given the Credit Terms page, when the "⋯" page menu beside "New Credit Term" is opened, then it holds one item, "Audit Log" (S12-R1).
- [ ] Given "Audit Log" is chosen, then a window titled "Credit Terms Log" shows a search box and a table with Event, User, Details, Date and Time, newest first, and no Line column (S12-R2, S12-R3).
- [ ] Given a term "Net 14" due in 14 days is created, then a row reads Event "Credit term created", Details "Net 14: 14 days after the invoice date" (S12-R3).
- [ ] Given an unused term is renamed only, then Details shows only "Name: {old} → {new}" (S12-R3).
- [ ] Given the default changes from COD to Net 14, then a row reads "Default changed" / "COD → Net 14" (S12-R3).
- [ ] Given 3 customers are moved from Net 30 to COD, then a row reads "Customers moved" / "3 customers from Net 30 to COD" followed by "Credit limit set to $0 (previous limits are in each customer's note)" (S12-R3).
- [ ] Given a row, then User shows the user who made the change and Date and Time use the calendar of the user's working location (S12-R4).
- [ ] Given text is typed in the search box, then rows narrow to those whose Event, User or Details contain it, ignoring capitals (S12-R5).
- [ ] Given a refused or canceled action, or one that changes nothing, then no row is written (S12-R7).
- [ ] Given a user without 'Settings → Finance', then they cannot reach the menu and a request for the log is refused by the server (S12-N1).
- [ ] Given a customer's term changed in Edit Customer, by import or by the Public API, then nothing is written to this log (S12-N3).
- [ ] Given an empty log, then the table shows "No history here. Yet."; given the log fails to load, then "Something went wrong loading the Credit Terms Log. Please try again." shows and the page is unaffected (S12-E1).
