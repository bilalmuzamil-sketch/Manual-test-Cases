# SV-11638 — Credit terms (epic), read 3 Nov 2026 (updated 2026-10-27T09:12:50-05:00)

### What

Let each shop choose the credit term new customers start on, and create its own credit terms ("Net X days" or "day of the following month"), on a new Settings page: Finance, Credit Terms. Existing customers keep their terms until someone changes them; a Move Customers action moves customers from one term to another in bulk.

### Why

Every new customer starts on COD today, with no way to change it, and shops that bill on Net 10 or the 10th of the month have no term to pick. Shops fix customers one by one (around a dozen tickets and many customer insights).

### Scope

* Settings, Finance, Credit Terms: one list of standard and shop terms, Active / Inactive, a default for new customers, create, edit, deactivate, delete, Move Customers.
* New Customer: Credit Terms and Credit Limit (same permission as Edit Customer).
* Customer and vendor pickers, due dates on invoices and vendor bills, QuickBooks terms, the Over Credit warning.
* Ships after SV-11414.
