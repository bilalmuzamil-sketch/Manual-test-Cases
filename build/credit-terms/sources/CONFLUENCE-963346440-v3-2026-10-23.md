## 1. Business Case

Today every new customer in ShopView starts on COD, a term the system picks rather than the shop, and the twelve credit terms a shop can choose from are fixed. COD hides Charge Account and forces payment the moment the invoice is created, so a shop that bills walk-ins Due on Receipt or gives fleets Net 30 has to leave New Customer, open the customer and change the term by hand on every new customer, and a shop that sells on "Net 10" or "10th of next month" cannot record its real terms at all. Customer demand is broad and repeated: two unbuilt backlog tickets ask for exactly this (SV-3752 "Add Setting for Default Credit Terms on Customer Creation", SV-1637 "Customized credit terms"), backed by about 75 customer insights, including at least 43 asking for a shop-chosen default term, 17 reporting that the COD default blocks invoicing on account, 32 asking for terms the fixed list lacks (11 of them Net 10) and 18 asking to change terms on many existing customers at once. This PRD adds a Credit Terms page under Settings where a shop picks the term new customers start on, adds its own "Net X days" and "day of the following month" terms, and moves existing customers between terms; New Customer gains Credit Terms and Credit Limit fields; and the Over Credit warning stops firing for customers who have no credit to exceed, while the twelve standard terms keep their names and behavior, and no existing customer, vendor or invoice changes unless a user changes it.

### Workarounds

- **Fixing the term by hand after creating each customer.** Whoever creates customers (the set-up team, service writers, office staff) saves New Customer, opens the customer, opens Edit Customer and changes the term, on every new customer whose term is not COD. The cost is extra screens on every customer and missed changes: SV-10604 (Set-ups Team): "I have to get out of it and then go to the Customer tab ... to then add your credit terms ... Is it possible ... to have it all under one ... One click." SV-3352: "Many customers don't populate their customer or vendor credit terms and it used to default to COD..." A missed change leaves the customer on COD, which hides Charge Account at invoicing (about 17 insights, SV-3439).

- **Changing terms on existing customers one at a time.** Shops moving many customers to a new term open each customer and edit it individually; about 18 insights ask for a bulk change. No shop has a faster route today.

- **Going without their real terms.** Shops whose terms are not among the twelve have no workaround inside ShopView: Lennard Ag, Morken (net 10th of the month) and Antares NZ (end-of-month terms) are named in SV-1637 ("Lennard Ag is going to need this... they have a lot of terms we don't have"). Their invoices carry a term that is not the one they trade on.

- **Clicking through a false Over Credit warning.** Shops with COD and Due on Receipt customers dismiss the over-credit-limit confirmation every time they open a work order for those customers, because a \$0 limit reads as exceeded (Southern Truck and Trailer, SV-6712 / SV-6713; also SV-10842, SV-9493).

## 2. Feature Overview

**Core: Credit Terms**

- A new Credit Terms page under Settings, in the Finance group directly below Payment Methods, lists every credit term the shop has: the twelve standard terms and the shop's own terms, in one table with Active and Inactive tabs.

- The shop picks which term new customers start on ("Make Default for New Customers"), from any active term except Credit Hold. The default is tagged "Default" on the page and in the customer Credit Terms dropdown.

- The shop adds its own terms of two kinds: "Due Net X days" (1 to 365 days) and "Due day of the following month" (day 1 to 31). Both behave exactly like the standard Net terms: Charge Account is allowed and invoicing is never blocked.

- A term nobody uses can be edited or deleted. A term in use by any customer, vendor or invoice keeps its name and days for good; it can be deactivated, which takes it out of the pickers while everyone on it keeps it.

- Any standard term except COD, Due on Receipt and Credit Hold can be deactivated to shorten the pickers. Standard terms are never edited or deleted.

- "Move Customers" on any term's row menu moves some or all of that term's customers to another active term (moving them to COD or Credit Hold also sets their credit limit to \$0, after a confirmation, with each customer's previous limit kept in its note), and writes a note on each moved customer naming who moved them.

- New Customer gets Credit Terms (prefilled with the default) and Credit Limit fields beside each other, with the same rules and permission as Edit Customer. COD and Credit Hold customers have a \$0 credit limit that cannot be changed.

- Customers and vendors share one term list. The shop's default applies to new customers only; new vendors keep starting on COD.

- "Day of the following month" terms are due on that day of the month after the invoice date, with short months using their last day. All due dates are counted on the calendar of the invoice's or vendor invoice's own location, so every user sees the same due date.

- Imports and the Public API give a new customer the shop's default when the term is blank.

- A Credit Terms Log, opened from the page's "⋯" menu, records every change made on the Credit Terms page: terms created, edited, deactivated, reactivated and deleted, default changes and customer moves, with who and when.

- The Over Credit warning and the Over limit badge show only when a credit limit above \$0 is set and exceeded, and never for COD, Due on Receipt or Credit Hold customers.

- What existing shops see on release day is its own story (Story 11: Release day).

**QuickBooks Integration**

- When an invoice carrying one of the shop's own terms, or a vendor invoice for a vendor on one, is sent to QuickBooks and QuickBooks does not have the term yet, ShopView creates it in QuickBooks by name: a "Net X days" term as a standard term due in X days, a "day of the following month" term as a date-driven term.

- If QuickBooks refuses the term, the record lands on the QB Unexported report with QuickBooks' reason, as any failed export does today.

- Changing a customer's term in ShopView (by Edit Customer or Move Customers) does not update the customer record in QuickBooks, exactly as an Edit Customer save does not today. Each new invoice carries its own term to QuickBooks.

**Design coverage:** States with no board on the design canvas (toasts, error alerts, the list load error, Move Customers search with no match, an unknown-text term, phone width, and long term names on printed invoices) follow the exact text in this PRD and the app's existing pattern; no board is needed.

**Out of Scope**

- **A separate default term for new vendors.** New vendors keep starting on COD. Whether vendors get their own default is a follow-up decision (open question on the review page).

- **Merging with AccountingHub's own Payment Terms list.** AccountingHub keeps its separate Payment Terms list (Accounting Settings). A merge waits until AccountingHub's term rules are confirmed (follow-up, open question on the review page).

- **Early-payment discount terms** (for example "2% 10 Net 30"). 5 insights, mostly vendor-side; not a "Net X" or "day of the following month" term. Not planned.

- **Per-location or per-work-type default terms.** 2 insights. One default per organization.

- **Updating the customer's term inside QuickBooks** after it was created there. No such sync exists today; building one is new scope.

- **Renaming or changing the days of a term in use.** QuickBooks cannot rename a term and invoices hold the name. To rename, deactivate the term and create a new one.

- **Editing, renaming or deleting the twelve standard terms.** Their names are already on every shop's invoices and in every connected QuickBooks file, and QuickBooks cannot rename a term; shops that want different wording create their own term.

- **A finer-grained permission to stop users editing a customer's sales rep.** Raised in the 2026-10-21 founder meeting; a separate follow-up.

- **Vendor credit limits.** Vendor credit limits behave exactly as today on every term, including COD and Credit Hold. A vendor's limit plays no part in invoicing customers or in the Over Credit warning, so the customer \$0 rule has no problem to solve there (owning PM, 2026-10-20).

- **Statements, the customer portal and A/R aging** gain no new screens and show no credit term name on their own screens (an invoice document opened from the portal prints the term as any printed invoice does); see S11-R14 for the due dates they recalculate. They show stored due dates, except where one is recalculated today: the A/R dashboard for records with no stored due date, and credit memo due dates on statements and the unpaid transactions list. Those recalculated dates are counted on the calendar of the record's own location (S7-R6).

- **A bulk term change from the Customers list.** Move Customers lives on the Credit Terms page only, where the term's full customer list and the default already are. A Customers-list bulk change is a possible follow-up.

- **A change history for single customers and vendors.** A term or credit limit changed one record at a time (New or Edit Customer, Edit Vendor, imports, the Public API) leaves no visible record, exactly as today; only Move Customers writes a note on each moved customer (S4-R11) and a Credit Terms Log row. A customer and vendor audit log is a later Founder Mode item.

- **Release notes.** No release note is part of this PRD; release notes are written when the owning PM asks for them.

- **AccountingHub and IBS** are unchanged: both receive each invoice's or vendor invoice's due date only, never the term name, so a shop's own term names never reach them (S8-R8).

## 3. Jobs to be Done / Goals

> **When** I set up a new customer, **I want to** have them start on the term my shop actually uses, **so I can** invoice them on account without going back to fix the term afterwards.

> **When** my shop trades on terms the standard list does not have, such as Net 10 or the 10th of the following month, **I want to** add those terms once, **so I can** put customers and vendors on them and have invoices show the right due date.

> **When** I change how my shop bills a group of customers, **I want to** move them from one term to another in one step, **so I can** avoid opening and editing each customer.

> **When** I open a work order for a COD or Due on Receipt customer, **I want to** not be warned about a credit limit they do not have, **so I can** keep moving without dismissing a false alarm.

**Goals**

- A user with 'Settings → Finance' can set the default term for new customers to any active term except Credit Hold, and every customer created afterwards in ShopView, by contacts import or by the Public API without a term starts on it.

- A shop can create a "Net X days" or "day of the following month" term and select it on any customer or vendor; invoices and vendor invoices dated afterwards show the due date the term defines.

- Creating a customer on a non-default term takes zero extra screens: the term is set on New Customer itself.

- Moving any number of customers from one term to another takes one window and one confirm, plus one confirmation when the move sets credit limits to \$0.

- The Over Credit warning never appears for a customer on COD, Due on Receipt or Credit Hold, or with a \$0 or blank credit limit.

- With no setup at all, existing shops behave exactly as before release (default COD, the same twelve terms), and new shops start on Due on Receipt.

## 4. Key Decisions

- **Existing organizations keep COD as their default at release; organizations created after release start on Due on Receipt. No existing customer changes term.** Rationale: we may not silently change how an existing shop collects; a shop that wants a different default picks it on the page. Due on Receipt is the term new shops most often want (about 10 to 1 over other defaults in the insights) because COD hides Charge Account. **Rejected:** switching every organization to Due on Receipt at release, which would change collection behavior for every existing shop overnight. *Basis:* `d-new-default-existing-customers` (decided 2026-09-01).

- **A changed default applies to new customers only; moving existing customers is a separate, explicit action (Move Customers).** Rationale: a shop flipping its default is choosing how future customers start, and terms already on customers were often set on purpose. Move Customers lets the shop apply a change to exactly the customers it picks. **Rejected:** a one-off "apply the default to every customer on COD" action (built in round 1, replaced on 2026-10-21 by Move Customers on every row, which covers any term and partial selections). *Basis:* `d-new-default-existing-customers` (decided 2026-09-01).

- **The default can be any active term except Credit Hold.** Rationale: shops ask for Due on Receipt, Net 15 and Net 30 defaults alike; Credit Hold blocks invoicing, so it cannot be a starting term. **Rejected:** offering only COD or Due on Receipt (SV-3752), which leaves the Net-default shops out.

- **One default and one term list per organization, shared by customers and vendors.** Rationale: the customer's term is already organization-wide, and one list keeps the pickers identical everywhere. **Rejected:** per-location defaults (1 insight, and would need a rule for which location's default a customer gets); a second list for vendors. *Basis:* `d-settings-last-resort` (decided 2026-09-01).

- **The shop's own terms behave like Net terms: Charge Account allowed, invoicing never blocked.** Rationale: COD already covers pay-now; a per-term "collect at invoicing" switch adds a setting and a test matrix for no stated need. **Rejected:** a per-term behavior flag. *Basis:* `d-settings-last-resort` (decided 2026-09-01).

- **Once a term is in use (by any customer, vendor or invoice), its name and days are locked; it can be deactivated but not deleted. An unused term can be edited or deleted.** Rationale: invoices keep the term and due date they were created with, QuickBooks matches terms by name and cannot rename one, and a rename would rewrite history (a term change rewriting invoiced work orders was ruled a bug in SV-5633). **Rejected:** fully editable terms with a warning (SV-1637's original acceptance criteria).

- **Deactivating a term hides it from the customer and vendor pickers only.** Everyone on it keeps it, shown as "{name} (Inactive)" on their card and in Edit, until someone picks another term; invoices print the plain name; it can be reactivated at any time. Rationale: deactivation is reversible and must not change anyone's term. **Rejected:** permanent deactivation; moving holders to the default automatically.

- **The current default cannot be deactivated or deleted; the action is shown disabled with the reason.** Rationale: a default must always point at an active term, and a silent fallback to COD would change new-customer behavior without anyone choosing it. **Rejected:** falling back to COD when the default is removed.

- **Any other standard term (Net 7 to Net 120) can be deactivated to shorten the pickers.** Rationale: shops that never use most of the standard Net terms want shorter customer and vendor dropdowns; the owning PM explained this in the 2026-10-21 founder meeting. **Rejected:** limiting deactivation to the shop's own terms.

- **Shops name their terms freely; the Due column always shows the rule.** A term named "Net 6" may be due on the 25th of the following month (S3-E1). Rationale: shops already have names for their terms and the page never hides the rule behind the name (owning PM, 2026-10-21). **Rejected:** refusing or warning on a "Net" plus number name whose rule differs.

- **Net terms run from 1 to 365 days.** The 365-day ceiling is the owning PM's choice; no QuickBooks limit is known. It is reopened if a shop asks for a longer term. The 31-character name limit is QuickBooks' term-name limit, and day 1 to 31 is the calendar.

- **The page is one table with Active and Inactive tabs, the default picked from each row's menu.** The design authority accepted this layout on 2026-10-21 (canvas linked in the header). **Rejected:** two sections, "Standard terms" and "Your terms" (the grill's working position, built in rounds 3 and 4).

- **COD, Due on Receipt and Credit Hold can never be deactivated.** Rationale: the app's payment, invoicing and Over Credit behavior is built on these three; each shows a lock icon with the reason. **Rejected:** allowing any standard term to be deactivated.

- **The page explains every term inline in a Due column; only COD, Due on Receipt and Credit Hold carry an info tooltip.** Rationale: a settings page touched rarely is read, not learned, so the explanation is visible without hovering; the three special terms need more than the Due column can say (what happens at invoicing). **Rejected:** an info tooltip on every term (built in round 5, removed in round 6 as noise). *Basis:* `d-inline-descriptions-not-tooltips` (decided 2026-09-01).

- **Deleting a term asks for confirmation; deactivating, reactivating and making a term the default are confirmed only where the result is not obvious.** Delete is permanent and asks first. Deactivate and Reactivate are reversible and act at once with a toast. Make Default confirms, because its effect on existing customers (none) needs saying. *Basis:* `d-confirmation-scales-with-destruction` (decided 2026-09-01).

- **Every change on the Credit Terms page is recorded in an organization-wide Credit Terms Log (Story 12: Credit Terms audit log), and every customer moved by Move Customers also gets a note naming the old term, the new term and the user.** Rationale: a default change or a bulk move changes how a shop collects, so it must be traceable both at the page level (what changed, who, when) and per customer record, which keeps no other history. **Rejected:** no record of page changes; per-customer notes only, which cannot show a default change, a deactivation or a deleted term. *Basis:* `d-audit-logging-explicit` (decided 2026-09-01).

- **Imports and the Public API stay lenient.** A blank term gets the default; a name matching any of the shop's terms (standard or its own, active or deactivated, ignoring capitals and spaces) is saved as that term in its correct spelling, and a deactivated one then shows as "{name} (Inactive)"; any other text is saved as sent and dated as today. Rationale: rejecting unknown text would break existing integrations and spreadsheets, and a deactivated term is still a real term the shop trades on. **Rejected:** refusing unknown terms; treating a deactivated term's name as unknown text (the grill's first position, reversed by the owning PM on 2026-10-21).

- **No feature flag.** The feature ships to everyone; release-day behavior (Story 11: Release day) leaves existing shops exactly as they were until they act. *Basis:* `d-ship-without-feature-flag` (decided 2026-10-08).

- **COD and Credit Hold customers have a \$0 credit limit that cannot be changed, in New Customer and Edit Customer, and imports and the Public API save them with \$0 (S10-R6, S10-R8).** Rationale: neither can charge to account, so a limit has no meaning and was the source of false Over Credit warnings. The rule applies only to customers created or edited after release: an existing COD or Credit Hold customer with a limit stored from before keeps it until the customer is next edited in Edit Customer, updated by the contacts import, or updated through the Public API with a credit term or credit limit or moved to COD or Credit Hold by Move Customers; each of those saves \$0. Release itself never changes an existing customer's limit (S11-R11). **Rejected:** clearing every existing COD customer's limit at release (changes customer data without anyone choosing it).

- **Moving customers to COD or Credit Hold sets their credit limits to \$0 only after a confirmation, and keeps each previous limit in that customer's note.** Rationale: one move can clear thousands of limits at once and cannot be undone automatically, so it gets its own confirmation in red, and the old value stays on each record so a shop can restore it by hand; the Credit Terms Log records the move. Moves to any other term change no limit and need no extra confirmation. **Rejected:** clearing limits with only the in-window line as warning; keeping limits on COD customers. *Basis:* `d-confirmation-scales-with-destruction` (decided 2026-09-01).

- **Departure: Move Customers keeps a gate from another area on a Settings page.** It needs 'Customers → Create & Edit' and 'View and Manage AP/AR Data' as well as 'Settings → Finance'. Rationale: it rewrites customer records in bulk, thousands at once, so it keeps the permissions that govern editing a customer's term and limit (owning PM, 2026-10-21). **Rejected:** following the precedent as written, which would let any 'Settings → Finance' user change customer terms and limits they cannot change in Edit Customer. *Basis:* `d-permissions-page-scoped-simplicity` (decided 2026-09-01); the departure is routed to the design authority on the review page.

- **Departure: the New Credit Term and Move Customers windows do not close on Escape or a click outside.** Rationale: typed names and a large customer selection are not lost by accident; Cancel or the close icon discards (owning PM, 2026-10-21; as built). **Rejected:** following the precedent's dismissal rules as written. *Basis:* `d-modal-behaviour-contract` (decided 2026-09-01); the departure is routed to the design authority on the review page.

- **Permissions.** Viewing and using the Credit Terms page (the list, New Credit Term, Edit, Make Default, Deactivate, Reactivate, Delete) is governed by 'Settings → Finance', the same permission as Payment Methods. Move Customers additionally needs 'Customers → Create & Edit' and 'View and Manage AP/AR Data', because it changes customer records; a user with 'Settings → Finance' who lacks either sees "Move Customers" greyed with the reason, and the server refuses the move. The Credit Terms and Credit Limit fields on New Customer and Edit Customer, and the Credit Terms field on New Vendor and Edit Vendor, are governed by 'View and Manage AP/AR Data', as the existing Edit fields are. A user without 'View and Manage AP/AR Data' who creates or edits a customer has any term or limit they send ignored by the server (S5-N4). The organization's credit term list itself (the terms that fill those dropdowns, and which one is the default) loads for every user in the shop, as today, because invoice due dates and the "(Inactive)" label rely on it; seeing and setting the Credit Terms and Credit Limit fields needs 'View and Manage AP/AR Data'; only the Credit Terms page, its actions and the Credit Terms Log (Story 12: Credit Terms audit log) need 'Settings → Finance', and the log is refused by the server without it. No new permission is introduced. On the Roles & Permissions screen these appear as "Finance" (a sub-row under "Settings" in the page-access toggles), the "Customers" row's "Create & Edit" column, and the "View and Manage AP/AR Data" cross toggle. By default 'Settings → Finance' is on for the Admin profile (required, cannot be turned off), Parts Manager and Office User, and off for Service Manager, Senior Service Advisor, Service Advisor, Foreman, Technician, Parts Technician, Sales Representative and Time Clock User. Out of the box, Move Customers is therefore available to Admin, Parts Manager and Office User; Service Manager, Senior Service Advisor and Sales Representative hold 'Customers → Create & Edit' and 'View and Manage AP/AR Data' but not 'Settings → Finance', so they cannot open the Credit Terms page. *Basis:* `d-inherit-nearest-permission` and `d-permissions-page-scoped-simplicity` (both decided 2026-09-01).

## 5. Terminology

- **Credit term** → The term on a customer or vendor that sets when their invoices or vendor invoices are due. This is the app's existing word (customer card "Credit term", Edit Customer "Credit Terms"). It is not AccountingHub's "Payment Terms", a separate list this PRD does not touch.

- **Standard term** → One of the twelve terms every organization has: COD, Due on Receipt, Net 7, Net 15, Net 20, Net 30, Net 45, Net 50, Net 60, Net 90, Net 120, Credit Hold.

- **The shop's own term** → A term the shop created on the Credit Terms page, of one of two kinds: **Due Net X days** (due X days after the invoice date) or **Due day of the following month** (due on day X of the month after the invoice date).

- **Default** (default credit term) → The term a new customer starts on. It never changes an existing customer.

- **In use** → A term is in use when at least one customer, vendor or invoice (work order or part sale invoice) holds it. Vendor invoices, deliveries and vendor credits store no term, so they never count. The Counter Sale customer is never counted. A customer or vendor with a blank term counts as COD. Every invoice ever created counts, including reversed and paid ones; estimates never count. Customers and vendors count and are listed whatever their status.

- **Inactive** → A deactivated term: absent from the customer and vendor pickers, still held by whoever had it, and shown as "{name} (Inactive)" on their card and in Edit.

- **Counter Sale customer** → The organization's built-in walk-in customer that over-the-counter sales are invoiced to; there is exactly one per organization, created with the organization. It always stays on COD and is never counted, listed or moved on the Credit Terms page.

- **Make Default vs Move Customers** → Make Default changes only which term new customers start on. Move Customers changes the term of existing customers the user picks. Neither does the other's job.

- **Vendor invoice** → The app's word for a bill received from a vendor. QuickBooks calls the same record a bill; this PRD says "bill" only when describing what appears inside QuickBooks.

- **Vocabulary.** This PRD uses the frontend vocabulary: *customer*, *vendor* and *location*, which map to *company*, *part vendor* and *workplace* in the backend.

## 6. Assumptions

- A single term can hold more than 1,500 customers (the test organization holds 1,528 on Due on Receipt and 768 vendors on COD); the Credit Terms page and the Move Customers window are sized for that.

- Stored customer and vendor terms carry inconsistent spellings from old imports and migrations (for example "NET 30" and "Due On Receipt"); every count, match and move treats names as equal when they differ only in capitals and spaces.

- The person using the Credit Terms page is an office or admin user at a desktop, not a technician on the floor.

## 7. Requirements

### Story 1: Credit Terms page and list

**As a** shop owner or office manager, **I want** one page that lists every credit term my shop has, how each one falls due and who is on it, **so that** I can see and manage my shop's terms in one place.

**Applies to:** Users with 'Settings → Finance'.

**Design:** <https://claude.ai/artifact/2erYxWz2ffDNcoXzEfu6wf>, pages "1 List and row menus", "3 New credit term and Inactive tab" and "4 Everything deactivated and an empty tab"  
**Jira:** SV-11670

**Form factor:** Desktop only by rule. This surface is a settings page, so mobile is not applicable.

**Prerequisites:**

- User must have 'Settings → Finance' setting enabled.

**Requirements:**

- **S1-R1:** Under Settings, in the Finance group, a "Credit Terms" item appears directly below "Payment Methods". Selecting it opens the Credit Terms page.

Ties sort A to Z by name. The same order is used in every customer and vendor Credit Terms dropdown.

The sort is not saved: leaving and returning to the page shows the S1-R5 order.

  - Due on Receipt: "Due on the invoice date"

  - Credit Hold: "Invoicing is blocked"

  - A "day of the following month" term: "{ordinal day} of the following month", for example "10th of the following month", "1st of the following month", "22nd of the following month"

- **S1-R8:** COD, Due on Receipt and Credit Hold each show, after the name, an info icon and a lock icon. Hovering the info icon shows:

  - COD: "Cash on delivery. COD customers can't charge to account, so payment is taken when the invoice is created."

  - Due on Receipt: "Payment is due on the invoice date. You can charge the invoice to the customer's account instead of taking payment."

  - Credit Hold: "No invoices can be created for a customer on Credit Hold until their credit term is changed."

- **S1-R9:** Hovering the lock icon shows "Always available." with the line "COD, Due on Receipt and Credit Hold can't be deactivated." below it.

- **S1-R10:** No other term (standard Net terms and every term the shop created) shows an info icon or a lock icon.

- **S1-R11:** The shop's default term shows a "Default" tag after its name (after the info and lock icons for COD and Due on Receipt). Exactly one row carries the tag.

- **S1-R12:** The Customers column shows how many customers hold the term, right aligned, showing 0 when none. The Counter Sale customer is never counted. A customer with a blank term is counted under COD. Names that differ only in capitals and spaces count toward the same term ("NET 30" counts as Net 30; see §6).

- **S1-R13:** The Vendors column shows how many vendors hold the term, right aligned, 0 when none, by the same rules as S1-R12 (a blank vendor term counts under COD).

- **S1-R14:** The Active tab lists every active term; the Inactive tab lists every deactivated term, standard and the shop's own, in the same columns and order.

- **S1-R15:** Term names are never cut off in the table; a long name wraps onto a second line, with its icons and "Default" tag after it.

- **S1-R16:** The page is organization-wide. Switching location while on the page reloads it with the same terms, counts and default.

**Negative Cases:**

### Story 7: Due dates

**As a** shop owner, **I want** every invoice and vendor invoice to fall due exactly as its term says, on the calendar of the location the invoice belongs to, **so that** due dates, aging and statements are right.

**Applies to:** Every shop, on every invoice and vendor invoice.

**Design:** <https://claude.ai/artifact/2erYxWz2ffDNcoXzEfu6wf>, page "9 Invoice due date"  
**Jira:** SV-11676

**Form factor:** Mobile-optimized: no new screen; due dates appear wherever they appear today, in each surface's existing mobile variant.

**Prerequisites:**

- The customer (for an invoice) or vendor (for a vendor invoice) has a credit term.

**Requirements:**

- **S7-R1:** A Net term (standard or the shop's own) is due that many days after the invoice date: an invoice dated Oct 5 on Net 30 is due Nov 4; on a shop's "Net 10" it is due Oct 15.

- **S7-R2:** A "day of the following month" term is always due in the month after the invoice date, whatever the day of the invoice: with day 10, an invoice dated Oct 5 and one dated Oct 28 are both due Nov 10.

- **S7-R3:** When the following month is shorter than the term's day, the due date is that month's last day: day 31 invoiced in October is due Nov 30; day 30 or 31 invoiced in January is due Feb 28 (Feb 29 in a leap year); day 31 invoiced in March is due Apr 30.

- **S7-R4:** An invoice dated in December on a "day of the following month" term is due in January of the next year (day 10, invoice Dec 15 2026, due Jan 10 2027).

- **S7-R5:** COD, Due on Receipt and Credit Hold are due on the invoice date, as today.

- **S7-R6:** Due dates are counted on the calendar of the location the invoice or vendor invoice belongs to, never the location of the user, API connection or import that creates or views it, so a daylight saving change inside the span never moves the date: an Edmonton invoice dated Oct 5 on "10th of the following month" is due Nov 10 (not Nov 9).

- **S7-R6a:** The same invoice shows the same due date to every user, whichever location they are working in. A vendor invoice created by receiving a delivery uses the delivery's location, even when the receiving user is working in another location.

- **S7-R6b:** When the record's location has no time zone set, the due date is counted on UTC.

- **S7-R7:** An invoice keeps the term and due date it was created with. Changing the customer's term later, deactivating the term or moving the customer never changes an existing invoice.

- **S7-R8:** When a user changes the invoice date on an invoice (existing behavior), the due date is recalculated from the customer's term by the rules above, including the shop's own terms.

- **S7-R9:** A vendor invoice stores no term. Its due date is calculated by the same rules from the vendor's term when the vendor invoice is created, recalculated when its invoice date or its vendor changes, and stored.

- **S7-R10:** A Net 30 invoice or vendor invoice whose span crosses the March or November clock change is due on the 30th calendar day after its date.

- **S7-R11:** The Vendor Bill Export shows each vendor invoice's stored due date (S7-R9), never one recalculated from the vendor's current term; it is blank when no due date is stored.

**Negative Cases:**

- **S7-N1:** A term whose text ShopView does not recognize (from an old import or the Public API) is dated exactly as today.

**Edge Cases:**

- **S7-E1:** A deactivated term still dates invoices and vendor invoices for the customers and vendors that keep it.

- **S7-E2:** Due dates already stored on existing invoices and vendor invoices are never rewritten. A screen that recalculates a due date from the customer's term shows the date on the calendar of the transaction's own location (S7-R6, S11-R14).

- **S7-E3:** An invoice created after a reversal takes the customer's term at that moment, as today: a deactivated term the customer still holds, or the new term after a move or edit. The reversed invoice's old due date does not come back.

- **S7-E4:** If the customer on an open work order or part sale is changed, the invoice takes the new customer's term at invoicing, by the same rule. Nothing about the term is copied onto the work order or part sale before invoicing.

**Error Handling:**

- None. Due-date calculation has no user-facing failure.

### Story 8: QuickBooks sync - credit terms

**As a** shop owner using QuickBooks Online, **I want** my shop's own terms to appear in QuickBooks with the same due dates, **so that** the books match ShopView without setting terms up twice.

**Applies to:** Shops using QuickBooks Online.

**Trigger:** When an invoice (Story 7: Due dates, S7-R1 to S7-R4) carrying a term QuickBooks does not have yet, or a vendor invoice (S7-R9) for a vendor whose current term QuickBooks does not have yet, is sent to QuickBooks, or a customer on such a term is first created in QuickBooks; the term itself is created in Story 3: Create or edit a credit term (S3-R7).

**Requirements:**

- **S8-R3:** A "Due day of the following month" term is created in QuickBooks as a date-driven term due on day X of the following month, so QuickBooks dates an invoice dated Oct 5 or Oct 28 with day 10 as due Nov 10, matching ShopView.

- **S8-R4:** A term already in QuickBooks under the same name is used as it is; ShopView never changes a term in QuickBooks.

**Negative Cases:**

**Error Handling:**
