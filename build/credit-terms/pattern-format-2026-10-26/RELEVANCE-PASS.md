## 2. The gates (authoring/build.py, permanent)
- **Relevance gate.** Every record a precondition names (term A/B, customer A/B/C, vendor A/B, work order 1/2, invoice A, location A/B, user A, role A, number A, date A, your user name, "the second …") must appear in the steps or the expected results, or the bullet that names it must say why it is needed with a "so …" clause (for example "…, so the term is still in use"). Otherwise a PROBLEM line.
- **Independence gate.** (a) No case key or C-id in anything the tester reads (title, preconditions, steps, results, note). (b) In a case whose steps change something (move, deactivate, delete, save, create, invoice, reverse, import, receive, send), every record of a changeable kind the steps or results name (term, customer, vendor, work order, invoice, vendor invoice, credit memo, part sale, estimate, asset) is described in the preconditions as new or made for this test (or as data from before the release, the built-in Counter Sale customer, or a name the steps use to create it). Otherwise a PROBLEM line.
- Also added: a hand-written precondition form in `pattern.py` that no longer matches its case is a PROBLEM (stale).
