  markdown rows). Line diff of the two decodes = **12 changed lines + 1 added

  "⋯ (more) menu" glyph and the S3-R12 / S5-R2 / S12-R3 "↳" arrow are
  doubly-mojibake'd differently in the two exports. Verified at the raw

  surrounding requirement text is identical.
