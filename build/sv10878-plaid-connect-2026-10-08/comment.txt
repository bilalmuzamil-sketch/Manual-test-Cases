h3. How to see it yourself
# Open [https://sv10360.qa.shopview.com|https://sv10360.qa.shopview.com] and sign in as Admin.
# At the top left, next to the logo, click {{ShopHub}} (with the small arrow) and choose {{AccountingHub}} from the list that opens.
# In the left menu under {{SETUP}}, click {{Chart of accounts}} and check there is at least one active Bank account nobody uses yet. If not, click {{New account}} at the top right and save one with {{Type}} = {{Asset}}, {{Sub-type}} = {{Bank}}, any unused {{Account number}} and a {{Name}}.
# In the left menu under {{BANKING}}, click {{Transactions}}, then the {{Connect a bank}} button at the top right of the page.
# In the Plaid window: click {{Continue without phone number}}, type {{Platypus}} in the search box, click {{First Platypus Bank}} (the last one with exactly that name), enter user name {{user_good}} and password {{pass_good}}, click {{Submit}}, then {{Continue}}, then {{Finish without saving}}.
# Back on the {{Connect a bank}} page, leave {{Plaid Checking}} and {{Plaid Saving}} ticked and untick the rest.
# On {{Plaid Checking}}, set {{Chart account}} to {{Use an existing chart account}} and pick your account under {{Existing account}}.
# On {{Plaid Saving}}, set {{Chart account}} to {{Use an existing chart account}} and open {{Existing account}}: the account picked on Plaid Checking is not in the list (picture 1).
