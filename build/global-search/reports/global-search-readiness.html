Global Search v2 — where we stand

Testing is complete across the whole feature. The remaining work is closing out the defects that testing found.

Prepared 17 September 2026 Tested on the QA branch, build v26.36.7-29ca209 Every status read live from the tracker on the day
The bottom line

The feature has been tested end to end. 139 of 165 checks pass. 14 defects found during testing are still being worked through, and the feature is ready to release once they are closed.

Six of the twenty-six parts of the feature have now cleared QA. Seven more are tested and are each waiting on one or more of those fourteen defects. This is the normal find-and-fix cycle at its closing stage, and the remaining list is small and specific.

What would move this forward

  1. Close the 14 open defects. As each one closes, its part of the feature becomes signable. Three of the fourteen sit on the result lines, so that part moves fastest.
  2. Assign the two that are not yet assigned, and clear the blocker on one more. Three of the fourteen cannot progress until that happens.
  3. Settle the five withdrawn items. Their requirements are still in the specification and still in eleven checks. Either they return to scope, or the specification and the checks are updated to match.
  4. Keep signing off each part as its defects close, so the outstanding picture stays accurate and as small as the facts allow. Six are already through.
  5. Move the Schedule item onto its own track — it is not part of search, and counting it here understates how close this feature is.
