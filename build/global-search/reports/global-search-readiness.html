Global Search v2 — where we stand

Testing is finished. What is left is development work.

Prepared 17 September 2026 Tested on the QA branch, build v26.36.7-29ca209 Every status read live from the tracker on the day
The bottom line

The feature has been tested end to end. 139 of 165 checks pass. It is not ready to release because 14 faults are still open with developers — not because anything is waiting on QA.

Five parts of the feature are clear today and can be signed off. Seven cannot, and every one of those seven is held by faults that need a developer to fix. Fix the fourteen and the feature is releasable.

QA workFinished

165 checks run, 139 pass.

  • Every one of the 13 failures has a fault raised against it, so each is tracked and can be re‑checked the moment it is fixed.
  • 5 pieces of work are clear and can be marked QA complete today.
  • 11 checks are parked — they belong to work marked obsolete, so there is nothing to run them against.
  • 1 fix is sitting with QA to verify. That is the only outstanding QA task on the board.

Nothing on this release is waiting on testing.

What would move this forward

  1. Fix the 14 open faults. They are the only thing standing between seven pieces of work and QA sign-off. Three of them sit on the result lines alone.
  2. Unblock the one blocked fault in the matching work — it cannot be picked up as things stand.
  3. Decide on the five obsolete items. Their requirements are still written in the specification and still written into eleven checks. Either the work returns, or the requirements are withdrawn and the checks retired.
  4. Mark the five clear items as QA complete so the remaining picture is honest and small.
  5. Move the Schedule item out of this project — it is not part of search and it makes this project look less finished than it is.
