## 4 · A separate finding, new today — the close-match marker is missing on contact matches

Requirement, verbatim, from the live page read 22 September (page 576978945, version 17, §7,
Highlighting):

> When a match is fuzzy rather than exact, the matched token in the row is still highlighted, and a
> subtle ≈ or italicized treatment indicates the soft match.

**A fuzzy match found on a CONTACT's name carries neither.** The row reads `Contact match ·` with no
`≈` and the contact's name is not shown at all, so the reader is told the contact matched when in
fact the contact is called something else.

Measured on three queries, each with its own positive control — in the same list, a fuzzy match on a
**company** name always carries the marker, which is what rules out the reader as the cause:

| Query | Fuzzy on a company name — carry the marker | Fuzzy on a contact name — carry the marker |
|---|---|---|
| `Marlene` | 5 of 5 | **0 of 4** |
| `Darlene` | 1 of 1 | **0 of 3** |
| `Petersn` (the specification's own worked example) | 2 of 2 | **0 of 14** |

Picture: `contact-nomarker.png`. Raw data: `INDICATOR-CONFIRM.json`, `MATCH-REASONS.json`.

**C44848 *A soft (fuzzy) match is visually indicated* passed on 21 September** against a fuzzy match
on a name. That pass did not cover this class of row. Re-judged **Failed** today under Rule 101 —
the check is against §7, and §7 is not met for every fuzzy row.
