      What this is and is not. It reproduces what the old search did, and nothing else — whether the new version should do the same is a decision for the product owner, taken after the tests have run. Every match comes from the old version's own two rules, rebuilt line for line from FetchDataQueryHandler.php and useGlobalSearch.ts at commit 55767168, and all 42 keyword expectations were checked against that rebuild before this page was published. Lines marked on the new version today were measured by hand on the QA branch on 15 September 2026, or say reported as where they come from someone else's testing rather than a measurement — and they are dated so a stale one is obvious.
