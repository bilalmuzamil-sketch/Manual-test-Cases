### Proposed new Expected for C44900 (documents only, Rule 57 — v1.5 §4/§5.3/§9)
Keeps the already-disclosed tri-state-badge divergence (engineering/designer 2026-08-17); only the
type caveat changes.
1. Vendor Invoices appear as their own searchable entity, grouped under a 'Vendor Invoices' heading
   with a count and a scope tab (subject to permission).
2. A vendor-invoice row carries the file-text icon; the primary line shows the invoice number +
   vendor, with the total and the invoice date on the second line.
3. The payment status badge shows the real status using the app's design-system status colours —
   Paid, Partially paid, or Unpaid (all states, not just Paid/Unpaid).
4. **No invoice 'type' is shown on the row, and no type value can be searched — invoice type is out
   of scope in v1, neither indexed nor displayed. ShopView has no 'Sublet' concept.**
5. A Vendor Invoice row has no quick action.
6. Vendor Invoice results are gated by 'Vendor & Order Management' access.

1. **CONFLICT (v1.5-relevant) — Vendor Invoice type chip.** `global-search.jsx` renders the
   vendor-invoice sample rows with an invoice-type value in the row meta:
   `["$4,180.20", "·", "May 12, 2026", "·", "Invoice"]` and
   `["$862.40", "·", "Apr 30, 2026", "·", "Sublet"]`. The design still displays the invoice type

   the design lag is the finding to raise, not a reason to keep the type.**

   spec §5.4 says **"View part history"**. Wording only. (Design correctly shows no "Add to work
   order" on parts — agrees with v1.4/v1.5 dropping it.)
3. **CARRIED conflict (not new) — empty-state helper text.** Design shows "Type to start searching
   for work orders, parts, customers and more"; spec §5.2 says **"Search for something"**. This is the
   already-open **PO-GS-EMPTY-1**; still present, unchanged. Cases follow the spec.
