## 3 · C55736 — why it is Blocked, and what it DID prove

The check needs the price visible first, then hidden. **No price is shown on a part's row at all** —
not even for a user who may see financial data. The row reads the same both ways.

**But the rule underneath is working**, measured in the data the row is built from: with financial
access the part's price (`137.45`) is included; without it the price is **not sent at all**.

**This is not a fault against the product.** §4 lists what a part row displays — description, part
number, quantity with its stock badge. A price is not among them. Prices are specified for part-sale
rows, and purchase-order rows do show one on screen today (`$58.50` observed).

**Ask:** permission to correct the check so it watches a record whose row is meant to show a price.

## 4 · A trap this pass nearly fell into, recorded for the next session

My "is the target present?" helper matched on **text anywhere in the result**. For C55735 the part is
called *ZZTOGVEN Supply Brake Shoe Kit*, so it contains the supplier's name and the helper reported
the supplier as still present after the permission was removed — when the **Vendors group was gone
entirely**. The supplier row was absent; only a part carrying its name in the description remained.

**Judge by the GROUP the row sits in and the record's identity, never by a substring** (this is
learning L0154 again, in a new costume).
