🔴 **A trap worth recording: `POST /api/customers/create` silently ignores `address` and `phone`.**
The fields must be written as `address_1` and `telephone` through `POST /api/customers/change` with
the whole record. The first measurement round read "the address field is not searchable" — which was
my own seeding, not the product. Read the record back before believing any negative.

🔴 **And every record I create is +0.10 to ME.** The requirement counts a create as a view, so a
freshly seeded record carries the recency bonus for the account that made it. Every expected figure
below therefore includes it; without allowing for it the whole matrix reads 0.10 too high.

## What is NOT correct — raised as [SV-10238](https://shopview.atlassian.net/browse/SV-10238)

**Words that live in different fields of the same record never combine.** Adding a second word that
sits on the same record in another field removes the record from the results.

| Typed | What it is | Result |
|---|---|---|
| `2019` | a vehicle's year | 20 vehicles |
| `Freightliner` | that vehicle's make | 20 vehicles |
| **`2019 Freightliner`** | **both, and exactly what its row prints** | **0 vehicles** |
| `Freightliner Cascadia` | make + model, one field | 20 vehicles ✅ |
| `S9160-17671` | a work order number | that work order |
| **`S9160-17671 Fibridge`** | **number + its customer, as the row prints it** | **nothing at all** |
| `ZZT-FIB-1001` | a part number | that part |
| **`Brake Shoe ZZT-FIB-1001`** | **description + number, as the row prints it** | **nothing at all** |
| `ZZMATRIX zzzznonsense` | one real word + one nonsense word | nothing (correct) |

The boundary is the **field**, not the number of words — two words of one field work. The plainest
statement of it: **the product cannot find a record by the text it prints for that record.**
Pictures: `PIC1-vehicle.png`, `PIC2-workorder.png`, `PIC3-part.png` (each is the same search twice —
one word, then two — annotated, 760 px wide in the ticket).

ticket and correcting the case** — its fixture compares two records that differ in three ways at once.

## Checked and found conformant, so deliberately NOT in the ticket

* **Fuzzy noise on a correctly spelled name** (SV-10025) — the 0.70 similarity bar produces exactly
  those words; a product decision, not a break. Re-measured today, unchanged.
* **A mid-word fragment finding some records and not others** (C55660) — the bar is length-scaled, so
  `ernva` against `Fernvale` falls to 0.625 and is correctly rejected while `idgepor` against
  `Bridgeport` reaches 0.70 and is correctly accepted.
* **A catalogue part that has never been stocked is unfindable** (SV-10001) — §4 names the searchable
  entity as **Parts (Inventory)**, so the build follows the requirement; the Product Owner's ruling
  that it should work is a scope decision, and the ticket stays on that footing.
* Scores **clamp at 1.0**, which makes strong candidates tie. The requirement neither promises nor
  forbids a cap, and the order within a tie was stable across six reads, so this is recorded here
  rather than raised.
