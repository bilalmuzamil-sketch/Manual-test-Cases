*Split from SV-10161.* The fix on that ticket works and should stay. This ticket is about what stopped working because of it.

----

h3. Description

*In one sentence:* search used to put the most useful result at the top — the part you actually have in stock, the company whose name really is the one you typed — and since the change on SV-10161 it no longer does.

Search gives every result a score and shows the highest first. The score cannot go above *full marks*. Since the change, a result whose *own name* contains the word you typed gets *full marks straight away*, before anything else about it is looked at. Nothing can then lift it and nothing can hold it back — so everything else the specification counts (in stock, open work orders, used recently, how closely the name matches) stops making any difference.

When several results all sit on full marks, the one shown first is simply whichever was changed most recently.

For example,
* a part that is *in stock* and a part that is *out of stock* score the same
* a company whose name *starts with* the word and one where the word is in the middle score the same
* typing two everyday words returns *every* list with nothing separating the rows

h3. Steps to Reproduce

*Example 1 — an out-of-stock part sits level with one that is in stock*

# Press {{Ctrl}}+{{K}}
# Type {{shoe}}
# Open the *Parts* tab

*Actual Result:* 20 parts come back, 7 of them showing *0 Available*, and all 20 score the same.
*Expected Result:* the parts that are in stock score higher, so they appear above the ones showing *0 Available*.

*PRD 6.1, exactly as written:*
{quote}Parts. In stock (>0) → +0.20; bin location present → +0.05; sold/used in last 30 days (frequency, log-scaled) → up to +0.15; viewed recently → +0.10.{quote}

# Press {{Ctrl}}+{{K}}

*Actual Result:* all 20 rows score the same in *Work orders*, *Customers*, *Vendors*, *Part sales*, *Purchase orders* and *Vendor invoices*, and all 20 in *Assets* score the same as each other. The order you see is whichever record was changed most recently.

*PRD 6.1, exactly as written:*
{quote}Each candidate gets a numeric score combining match quality and entity-specific recency/importance signals.{quote}
{quote}The score is clamped to a sane range; ties are broken by recency (most recently updated wins).{quote}

h3. Why This Happens

Full marks is 1.00. The word being found in a record's own name is now worth 0.50 on its own, on top of 0.50 for finding the word at all — so a name match arrives at 1.00 with nothing else counted. Everything PRD 6.1 adds after that has nowhere to go.

Full marks really is a ceiling and not just the best row of the day: across 230 results nothing went above it, and plenty of searches finish well below it — the customers returned for {{brake}} stop at 0.32.

*The list can still look correctly ordered*, because results on the same score fall back to whichever was changed most recently. It is that fallback holding the order, not the specification.

h3. Suggested Fix

# Give a record's own name enough of a lift to beat a match on somebody's contact details, but *not* enough to reach full marks — so there is still room above it for in stock, open work orders and recent use to do their job.
# Update PRD 6.1, which still says this lift is *+0.10* while the product is applying at least *+0.50*.
