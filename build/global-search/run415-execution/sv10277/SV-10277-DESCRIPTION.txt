*Split from SV-10161.* The fix on that ticket works and should stay. This ticket is about what stopped working because of it.

----

h3. Description

h4. How search worked before the fix

Search gives every result a score and shows the highest score first. Two things make up that score:
* *how well the result matches* what you typed
* *how recent and how important the record is* — a part that is in stock, a customer with open work orders, a record viewed recently

Because the second part could be worth almost as much as the first, a company that matched only on a contact's name or email address could rank above a company whose *own name* matched what you typed. That is the problem reported on SV-10161.

h4. What the fix corrected

The bonus given to a match on a record's *own name* was raised. A company whose own name matches now appears above a company that matches only on a contact's details. Checked on 20 different person-name searches across 230 customer rows — correct every time. *This part is right and should stay.*

h4. What the fix broke

A score cannot go above the maximum. The bonus was raised so far that a result whose own name contains the word you typed now reaches *the maximum score on the match alone*, before anything else about it is counted. No signal can raise that score and none can lower it — so being in stock, having open work orders, being viewed recently, and how closely the name matches no longer change the order.

When several results reach the maximum score, the tie is broken by recency, so the most recently updated record is shown first.

For example,
* a part that is *in stock* and a part that is *out of stock* score the same
* a company whose name *starts with* the word and one where the word is in the middle score the same
* typing two common words returns every list with all its rows scoring the same

{quote}Each candidate gets a numeric score combining match quality and entity-specific recency/importance signals.{quote}

h3. Why This Happens

The maximum score is 1.00. The bonus for finding the word in a record's own name is now 0.50 by itself, on top of the 0.50 for a whole-word match — so a name match reaches 1.00 before any other signal is added. Every signal PRD 6.1 adds after that is clamped away.

1.00 really is the maximum, and not just the highest score returned that day: across 230 results nothing went above it, and plenty of searches finish well below it — the customers returned for {{brake}} stop at 0.32.

*The list can still appear correctly ordered*, because results on the same score are separated by the recency tie-break. It is that tie-break deciding the order, not the scoring rules in PRD 6.1.

h3. Suggested Fix

# Set the bonus for a record's own name high enough to rank above a contact-field match, but low enough that the score stays below the maximum — so the in-stock, open-work-order and recency signals can still change the order.
# Update PRD 6.1, which still gives this bonus as *+0.10* while the product applies at least *+0.50*.
