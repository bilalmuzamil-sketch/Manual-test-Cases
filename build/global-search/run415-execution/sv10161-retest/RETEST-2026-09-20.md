## 1 · What the requirements say

§6.1: exact identifier +1.00 · prefix on primary name +0.70 · whole word anywhere +0.50 ·
fuzzy ≤ +0.40 scaled · **primary-display-name bonus +0.10** (a contact-field match is secondary
and earns no bonus). Customer signals: ≥1 open work order +0.20 · log-scaled open count up to
+0.15 · viewed in the last 7 days +0.10.

## 2 · The bonus as built — measured, not inferred

Clean pair created as the `tech` user (so my own viewing recency could not contaminate it):

| Record | Where the word matched | Score |
|---|---|---|
| `Cedarline ZZBONUS Freight` | company name (whole word) | **1.00** |
| `Harbourgate Cartage Ltd` (address `4 ZZBONUS Road`) | address (whole word) | **0.50** |

Whole word = 0.50 in both cases, so the difference **is** the bonus: **0.50 as built vs 0.10 as
written.** The build and the requirements page disagree (Rule 57 — the document is the standard).

The bonus is name-only and is also applied to a *fuzzy* name match
(`Delano Diesel Services LLC`, fuzzy on name, scored 0.58 = 0.08 + 0.50).

## 4 · Does it hold for other keywords? (the question actually asked)

20 keywords, 230 customer rows scanned (`/tmp/gs/sweep161.mjs`). In **every** head-to-head where a
company's own name matched **exactly, as a whole word, or as a prefix**, that company ranked above

Per §6.1 a prefix (0.70) legitimately outranks a fuzzy match (≤0.40 scaled) even after the bonus.
**These are not regressions of SV-10161.**

## 5 · Residual risks — all three observed live

**(a) The score ceiling is 1.00, and the fixed case sits exactly on it.**
Max score seen anywhere across 230 rows = **1**. A name whole-word match is now 0.50 + 0.50 = 1.00,
i.e. pinned at the ceiling: it cannot rise further, while a challenger can climb to 0.95.

**(b) A contact-field match can also reach 1.00.** Proved with a seeded pair:
`zzcap161@shopview.com` → `Northgate Cartage Partners` **1.00 exact / contact_emails**.
So the bonus no longer guarantees separation — it guarantees a tie at the ceiling.

**(c) Two different companies at exactly 1.00 was observed in live data**: query `Northgate Cartage`
returned `Northgate Cartage Company` **1.00** and `Northgate Cartage Partners` **1.00`. Order is then
decided by a tie-break, not by the ranking rule.

**(d) Collateral flattening.** Of the 9 ranking comparisons maintained for this suite, **7 now tie at
1.00** (`/tmp/gs/pairs.mjs`): ZZPREFIX (prefix vs whole word), ZZCUSTOPEN, ZZVENDORPO, ZZTIEBREAK,
ZZSTOCKPART (in stock vs out of stock), ZZPARTBUSY, ZZOPENCOUNT. Only ZZASSETLIFT (0.95 / 0.75) and
ZZNAMEBONUS (1.00 / 0.50) are still separated by score. Ordering was **correct and stable across 8
consecutive reads**, and `verify_ranking.py` passes **20/20** — but those orders are now produced by a
tie-break rather than by a score difference, so they are not protected by the rule.

## 6 · Saturation check

Adding a 12th open work order to `ZZAUTOTEST Fibridge Commercial` moved its score **not at all**
(0.95 before and after) — its customer signals were already saturated. A single page view, by
contrast, lifted it **0.85 → 0.95** (+0.10 recency). So the 0.05 margin in §3 is what one ordinary
signal is worth.

## 7 · Verdict

- The reported failure is **fixed**, and fixed for its class, not just for `deshawn`.
- The **build now deviates from the written requirement** (0.50 vs +0.10); the requirements page has
  not been updated. Rule 57: the document is the standard, so this is a deviation to resolve one way
  or the other.
- The fix works by pushing the winner onto the score ceiling, which is why the margin is 0.05 and why
  seven other comparisons have flattened into ties.

C45139 recorded **Passed with caveat** in run 415; the caveat names the 0.10-vs-0.50 gap and the
0.05 margin.

## 9 · End-user impact of the flattening (added 2026-09-20, after the QA lead asked)

**The 1.00 ceiling is absolute, not a per-group normalisation.** Proof: `brake` → customers group
tops out at **0.32**, `tire` → assets tops out at **0.30**. Groups are not renormalised to 1, so a row
reading 1.00 is genuinely clamped.

**A name match now reaches the ceiling with no entity signals at all.** `Cedarline ZZBONUS Freight`
(created as `tech`, zero open work orders, never viewed) = **1.00**. From that point the customer
signals (+0.20 open WO, +0.15 log count, +0.10 recency) can add nothing.

**Observed on realistic queries** (`/tmp/gs/impact161.out`):

| Query | Group | Rows on the identical top score |
|---|---|---|
| `brake` | parts | **10 of the first 10** at 1.00 — incl. `Brake Shoe Kit w/ Hardware 16.5x7` qty **176** level with `Brake Pad Set, Thick` qty **1** |
| `truck repair` | vendors | **10 of 10** at 1.00 |
| `truck repair` | part_sales | **10 of 10** at 1.00 |
| `truck repair` | purchase_orders | **10 of 10** at 1.00 |
| `truck repair` | vendor_invoices | **10 of 10** at 1.00 |

Contrast: `tire` → parts spreads normally (1.00 / 0.75 / 0.75 / 0.70 …) because the matches land on
`category` and `description` of records whose primary name does not carry the word.

**Secondary-field weighting is NOT a deviation — checked and cleared.** `Raghav` → prefix on
`contact_names` scores **0.50**, i.e. the whole-word tier, not the 0.70 primary-name prefix tier.
`ZZAUTOTEST Fibridge Commercial` at 0.95 = 0.50 + 0.20 + 0.15 + 0.10 exactly. The §6.1 wording
*"Prefix match on primary name field → +0.70"* is honoured. (An earlier working hypothesis that the
0.70 tier leaked onto contact fields is **withdrawn** — measured false.)

**Therefore the root imbalance is in the written weights themselves:** match quality tops out at
+0.50 for a whole word, while a customer's activity signals total +0.45. A busy weak match beats a
quiet strong one, and a +0.10 name bonus was never capable of closing that gap. Raising the bonus to
+0.50 closes it by saturation rather than by rebalancing — which is why the entity signals stop
separating anything whose primary name carries the query.

**Not measured:** the same queries on the pre-fix build. No before/after capture of `brake` or
`truck repair` exists, so the flattening is shown by the ceiling mechanism above, not by a
side-by-side (Rule 12 — stated, not inferred over).

**Recommendation to the QA lead:** raise it, leading with the flat result lists rather than with the
0.10-vs-0.50 documentation gap, and propose a bonus large enough to clear a busy stranger but small
enough to leave headroom for the entity signals.
