h2. The fix works

!PIC1-fixed.png|width=760,height=850!

*Steps to reproduce*
# Press {{Ctrl}}+{{K}}.
# Type {{deshawn}}.
# Open the *Customers* tab.

The company whose own name is Deshawn is now first, above the company that only has a contact of that name.

*This is not limited to the example in this ticket.* Checked across 20 different person-name words and 230 customer rows: wherever a company's own name matched exactly, as a whole word or as a prefix, it ranked above every contact match. Three searches returned a contact match on top, and in all three the company name was only a near-miss spelling, which PRD 6.1 says should lose.

*PRD 6.1, exactly as written:*
{quote}Bonus for match on the primary display name vs. a secondary indexed field → +0.10. A match on a contact field of a customer or vendor is a secondary-field match and does not receive this bonus (§4).{quote}

The measured credit is *at least +0.50*, not +0.10. *The behaviour above is the right one and should stay* — it is the PRD that needs updating to describe it.

h2. Follow-up raised

Raising that credit has stopped the other credits in PRD 6.1 from separating results, because a match on a record's own name now reaches the top of the scale on its own. That is written up separately, with examples and reproduction steps, in *SV-10277*.
