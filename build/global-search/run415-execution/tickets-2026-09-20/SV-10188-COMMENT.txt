h3. Re-checked on 20 September 2026 — this still happens

When you are looking at a work order and you search for a part, the parts *already on that work order* should drop to the bottom of the list. You are almost always looking for something you have not added yet. That is not happening.

h3. What is on the work order

!WOA1.png|width=760,height=168!

h3. What comes back when you search from that same work order

!WOA2.png|width=760,height=356!

*Actual Result:* the brake shoe kit that is already on this work order comes back in the middle of the list, above an air dryer cartridge that is not on the work order at all.

*Expected Result:* the brake shoe kit drops below both parts that are not on the work order.

h3. This is not a one-off

Searching {{Brake Shoe Kit}} from this work order page and from a Customers page returns the identical six parts in the identical order. Standing on the work order makes no difference to the list at all.

h3. Why it cannot work at the moment

Every result is given a score, and the score stops at a maximum. Since the change on SV-10161, a part whose own name carries the word you typed reaches that maximum on the name alone. The reduction for being already on the work order is taken off before the maximum is applied, so it never shows. This is the same underlying cause as SV-10277, and this cannot be treated as fixed until that is dealt with.
