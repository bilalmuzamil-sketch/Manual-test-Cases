## The v1.4 change-log entry (verbatim, newest)
> v1.4. `Show all N` now switches the modal to that entity's scope tab — the full-page search
> results screen and the handoff of the query to an entity's list page are both dropped, along with
> the results banner and its `Clear search` action. The scoped tab shows up to 20 rows, scrolled
> inside the modal, with no pagination and no further loading, and carries no `Show all` link of its
> own. All result counts are capped at 20 — tabs, group headers and the `Show all N` link never read
> higher, since 20 is what search returns per entity type (§2, §5.2, §5.6, §6.1, §8). The Part row's
> only quick action is `View part history` — `Add to work order` is dropped (§5.4).
