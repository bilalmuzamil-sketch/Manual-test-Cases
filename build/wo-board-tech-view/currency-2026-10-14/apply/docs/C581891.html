Setup: A line with only a Schedule shift technician follows the new lead technician

Test case: C581891, WO Board and Tech View. Part 1 is for a manual QA tester. Part 2 is for a Claude session that prepares and runs the case on its own. Both create the starting state listed in the test case's Preconditions.

How to read this document: a name in braces, such as {Part-A} or {Line-1}, stands for a record you create or pick. Every brace name is defined before it is used. Wherever a value is given "for example", you may use any other value: the example is not a record that already exists in the app. Write down the real value you used for each brace name, because the test steps refer to it.

What the setup creates (the test case's Preconditions)

• Your browser window is at least 1024 px wide, so the display switcher (List, Tech View, Board View) shows at the right of the Work Orders toolbar.

• Technician {Technician-A} (for example Esther Howard) is an active staff member at {Location-A} with Time Clock on, the Service department, and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User.

• Technician {Technician-B} (for example Ralph Edwards) is an active staff member at {Location-A} with Time Clock on, the Service department, and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User.

• Technician {Technician-C} (for example Dana Ortiz) is an active staff member at {Location-A} with Time Clock on, the Service department, and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User.

• Technician {Technician-D} (for example Jenny Wilson) is an active staff member at {Location-A} with Time Clock on, the Service department, and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User.

• Customer {Customer-A} (for example "ZZAUTOTEST NT3 Shift Line Follows") is used by this case only.

• Approved work order {Work-order-1} (for example S10043-17595) for {Customer-A} is led by {Technician-A} and has two approved lines, {Line-1} (for example "Service - Full grease service") and {Line-2} (for example "Service - Adjust clutch"). It has no recorded time and nobody is clocked in to it.

• {Line-1} has no technician set through Edit Line; its labor follows the lead {Technician-A}.

• {Line-2} has {Technician-C} as its technician, set through Edit Line.

• {Technician-B} has a shift tomorrow 9:00–11:00 on {Line-1} only, made on the Schedule.

• {Technician-A} has no shift on {Work-order-1}.

Part 1. Setup for manual QA tester

1. Sign in as an Owner or Admin user. On a QA site click the Admin quick-login button on the sign-in page; you appear as "Admin ShopView". To check the permissions: your initials (top right) > Settings > Roles & Permissions > Admin > Work orders: View and Create & Edit are on. Your role also needs Schedule access (Settings > Roles & Permissions > Admin > Schedule: View and Create & Edit).

2. Pick one location for the whole case; it is shown in the top bar next to the bell (to change it: your initials > Change Location). This is {Location-A} (for example Staging Heavy Duty - 9919). Create everything below at {Location-A}.

3. Use a desktop browser window at least 1024 px wide (narrower windows show only the List, with no display switcher).

4. Your initials > Settings > Staff. Reuse a staff member only if they are active, have Time Clock on, the Service department, {Location-A} in Location and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User. Otherwise click New Staff Member and enter First Name and Last Name (for example Esther Howard), an Email, Role Technician, Departments Service (only Service technicians get a row on the Schedule), Location {Location-A}, turn Time Clock on, then Save & Close. This is {Technician-A}.

5. Your initials > Settings > Staff. Reuse a staff member only if they are active, have Time Clock on, the Service department, {Location-A} in Location and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User. Otherwise click New Staff Member and enter First Name and Last Name (for example Ralph Edwards), an Email, Role Technician, Departments Service (only Service technicians get a row on the Schedule), Location {Location-A}, turn Time Clock on, then Save & Close. This is {Technician-B}.

6. Your initials > Settings > Staff. Reuse a staff member only if they are active, have Time Clock on, the Service department, {Location-A} in Location and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User. Otherwise click New Staff Member and enter First Name and Last Name (for example Dana Ortiz), an Email, Role Technician, Departments Service (only Service technicians get a row on the Schedule), Location {Location-A}, turn Time Clock on, then Save & Close. This is {Technician-C}.

7. Your initials > Settings > Staff. Reuse a staff member only if they are active, have Time Clock on, the Service department, {Location-A} in Location and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User. Otherwise click New Staff Member and enter First Name and Last Name (for example Jenny Wilson), an Email, Role Technician, Departments Service (only Service technicians get a row on the Schedule), Location {Location-A}, turn Time Clock on, then Save & Close. This is {Technician-D}.

8. Top menu Customers > New Customer > Name (for example "ZZAUTOTEST NT3 Shift Line Follows") > Save. This is {Customer-A}; use it for this case only. On the customer page click Contacts > New Contact > First Name and Last Name (for example ZZAUTOTEST Contact) > Save. Then Assets > New Asset > pick the contact, Year (for example 2022), Make (type "Freight" and pick Freightliner), Model (for example M2), Unit (for example TRK-118) > Save. Contact and Make are required.

9. Work Orders > Create Work Order > in the New Work Order window pick {Customer-A} and its asset > Save (if a Confirmation about the credit limit appears, click Create). The work order opens on its Lines tab with the New Line form. In What Are You Doing? type "grease" and pick the ready-made line "Service - Full grease service", turn Line Approved on and click Save & Add Line. In the next New Line form type "clutch", pick "Service - Adjust clutch", turn Line Approved on and click Save & Close. The status reads Approved. Write down the number shown at the top left (for example S10043-17595). This is {Work-order-1}. The first line is {Line-1} and the second is {Line-2}.

10. Click {Line-2}'s name to open Edit Line, choose Technicians {Technician-C} and save.

11. On the work order page pick the technician in the Lead Technician dropdown; it saves at once. Choose {Technician-A}. {Line-1}'s Labor row now shows {Technician-A} (it follows the lead); {Line-2} still shows {Technician-C}.

12. Schedule (top menu) > use the arrows next to Today until the date reads tomorrow > in Search work orders (left) type the number of {Work-order-1} > drag its card onto {Technician-B}'s row and drop it under 9 AM > in the window that opens choose Choose lines and tick {Line-1} only (leave the other line unticked) > use − / + until Hours reads 2h > click Create 1 shift.

13. Do not clock in to {Work-order-1} and do not log any time on it.

14. Check the setup worked: On the Lines tab {Line-1}'s Labor row shows {Technician-A} and {Line-2}'s shows {Technician-C}; neither shows recorded time. On the Schedule for tomorrow {Technician-B} has 9:00–11:00 on {Work-order-1} naming {Line-1} only, and {Technician-A} has nothing on {Work-order-1}. Work Orders > All > Board View with {Customer-A}'s name in Search shows {Work-order-1} in {Technician-A}'s column. If anything differs, create a fresh work order.

Part 2. Setup for Claude session

• Goal: one work order with an implicit {Line-1} that also carries a Schedule shift for {Technician-B}, and an Edit Line {Line-2} for {Technician-C}; then a reassign to {Technician-D}.

• GET /api/staff lists staff; each row has both id and staff_id, and the Schedule's staffId and the working-hours calls use staff_id.

• Create {Customer-A}, its contact and asset, and {Work-order-1} on screen as in Part 1. Record {Work-order-1 id}, {Line-1 id} and {Line-2 id} from the Lines tab requests.

• Faster route for shifts, UNVERIFIED on the Work Orders QA site (proven only on the Schedule QA site sv8685, build v3.5-7ec992f): POST /api/schedule/shifts with workOrderId, lineIds (every line id for an entire-work-order shift, the chosen line ids otherwise), staffId, startDate, startTime, spreadMode "single", totalMinutes, perDayMinutes and isAllDay false; 201 means created. If it is refused, use the Schedule screen as in Part 1 and record what the screen sent. For this case lineIds holds {Line-1 id} only.

• Read shifts back with GET /api/schedule/board?from=<ISO instant>&to=<ISO instant> (full instants such as 2026-10-15T00:00:00Z; a bare date or a range over 62 days is refused). Compare each shift's start and end with the location's time zone before deciding a shift is wrong (SV-11229, a shift saved six hours off, was closed Obsolete on 12 Oct 2026).

• Trap: on the Schedule a work order with only one line is booked at once as a whole, with no Entire work order / Choose lines window (recorded in C368178). That is why every work order in this case has two lines.

• Steps 3 and 4: open the card's More actions (…) on hover, click Reassign lead technician, pick {Technician-D} and click Reassign. Record whether a question opened (none is expected) and the message text.

• Steps 7 to 13: read the Labor rows and each line's Edit Line Technicians on screen; close Edit Line with Cancel or the close button, never Save. UNVERIFIED: where the build shows a Schedule-shift technician on a line that also has a labor technician (the Labor row, the Edit Line Technicians, or both). Record every place {Technician-B} appears for {Line-1}.

• Context: the developer reported on 12 Oct 2026 (SV-11128, build e84b3d1) that a line held only through a Schedule shift now follows the new lead. That is not evidence about the build you test; judge only what you see.

• Evidence: a screenshot after each step that has an expected result, the build marker, and the real value of every brace name.
