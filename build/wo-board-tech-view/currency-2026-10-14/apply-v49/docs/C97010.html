Setup: Nothing can be dropped on an inactive technician, and a failed drop goes back

Test case: C97010, WO Board and Tech View. Part 1 is for a manual QA tester. Part 2 is for a Claude session that prepares and runs the case on its own. Both create the starting state listed in the test case's Preconditions.

How to read this document: a name in braces, such as {Part-A} or {Line-1}, stands for a record you create or pick. Every brace name is defined before it is used. Wherever a value is given "for example", you may use any other value: the example is not a record that already exists in the app. Write down the real value you used for each brace name, because the test steps refer to it.

What the setup creates (the test case's Preconditions)

• You are signed in as an Owner or Admin user whose role has Work orders > View and Work orders > Create & Edit, at {Location-A} (for example Staging Heavy Duty - 9919).

• Your browser window is at least 1024 px wide, so the display switcher (List, Tech View, Board View) shows at the right of the Work Orders toolbar.

• Technician {Technician-A} (for example ZZAUTOTEST Ana Alpha) is an active staff member at {Location-A} with Time Clock on and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User.

• Technician {Technician-B} (for example ZZAUTOTEST Ben Bravo) is an active staff member at {Location-A} with Time Clock on and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User.

• Customer {Customer-A} (for example "ZZAUTOTEST C97010 Failed Drop") is used by this case only.

• Approved work order {Work-order-1} (for example S10043-17701) for {Customer-A} is led by {Technician-A}.

• Work order {Work-order-2} (for example S10043-17702) for {Customer-A} is led by {Technician-A}, is Complete (reviewed) and has not been invoiced, so Create Invoice is offered on its Finance tab.

• Approved work order {Work-order-3} (for example S10043-17703) for {Customer-A} is led by technician {Technician-C} (for example ZZAUTOTEST Dan Delta), made for this case only.

• After {Work-order-3} was given its lead, {Technician-C}'s staff account was deactivated.

• No technician has a shift on the Schedule for {Work-order-1}, {Work-order-2} or {Work-order-3}.

• On the Work Orders page nothing is ticked in Status, Asset on Site shows no choice (if its chip shows Yes or No, click the x on the chip), Assigned to me is off and Search is empty.

Part 1. Setup for manual QA tester

1. Sign in as an Owner or Admin user. On a QA site click the Admin quick-login button on the sign-in page; you appear as "Admin ShopView". To check the permissions: your initials (top right) > Settings > Roles & Permissions > Admin > Work orders: View and Create & Edit are on.

2. Pick one location for the whole case; it is shown in the top bar next to the bell (to change it: your initials > Change Location). This is {Location-A} (for example Staging Heavy Duty - 9919). Create everything below at {Location-A}.

3. Use a desktop browser window at least 1024 px wide (narrower windows show only the List, with no display switcher).

4. Your initials > Settings > Staff. Reuse a staff member only if they are active, have Time Clock on, {Location-A} in Location and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User. Otherwise click New Staff Member and enter First Name and Last Name (for example ZZAUTOTEST Ana Alpha), an Email, Role Technician, Departments Service, Location {Location-A}, turn Time Clock on, then Save & Close. This is {Technician-A}.

5. Your initials > Settings > Staff. Reuse a staff member only if they are active, have Time Clock on, {Location-A} in Location and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User. Otherwise click New Staff Member and enter First Name and Last Name (for example ZZAUTOTEST Ben Bravo), an Email, Role Technician, Departments Service, Location {Location-A}, turn Time Clock on, then Save & Close. This is {Technician-B}.

6. Your initials > Settings > Staff. Create a new staff member for this case only (do not reuse a shared one, because this case changes the record): click New Staff Member and enter First Name and Last Name (for example ZZAUTOTEST Dan Delta), an Email, Role Technician, Departments Service, Location {Location-A}, turn Time Clock on, then Save & Close. This is {Technician-C}.

7. Top menu Customers > New Customer > Name (for example "ZZAUTOTEST C97010 Failed Drop") > Save. This is {Customer-A}; use it for this case only. On the customer page click Contacts > New Contact > First Name and Last Name (for example ZZAUTOTEST Contact) > Save. Then Assets > New Asset > pick the contact, Year (for example 2022), Make (type "Freight" and pick Freightliner), Model (for example M2), Unit (for example TRK-118) > Save. Contact and Make are required.

8. Work Orders > Create Work Order > in the New Work Order window pick {Customer-A} and its asset > Save (if a Confirmation about the credit limit appears, click Create). The work order opens on its Lines tab with the New Line form. In What Are You Doing? type "grease" and pick the ready-made line "Service - Full grease service", turn Line Approved on and click Save & Close. The status reads Approved. Write down the number shown at the top left (for example S10043-17701). Repeat this twice more. Number them {Work-order-1}, {Work-order-2} and {Work-order-3} in the order you create them.

9. On the work order page pick the technician in the Lead Technician dropdown; it saves at once. Give {Work-order-1} and {Work-order-2} the lead {Technician-A}, and {Work-order-3} the lead {Technician-C}.

10. Complete {Work-order-2}: on its Lines tab click the line's Complete button, type a Tech Story (fill any missing detail it asks for, for example Mileage) and click Complete Line (the List shows "Ready For Review"). Then click Mark Reviewed, enter the VIN if asked and click Confirm Review. The status reads Complete. Do not click Create Invoice now.

11. Deactivate {Technician-C}: your initials > Settings > Staff > {Technician-C}'s edit icon > click Deactivate Account and confirm.

12. Do not book any Schedule shift on the three work orders.

13. Check the setup worked: Work Orders > All tab > Search with the name of {Customer-A} shows {Work-order-1} (Approved) and {Work-order-2} (Complete) led by {Technician-A}, and {Work-order-3} (Approved) led by {Technician-C}. {Work-order-2}'s Finance tab offers Create Invoice. Settings > Staff shows {Technician-C} deactivated. If anything differs, fix it before you start, then clear the search.

Part 2. Setup for Claude session

• Goal: a deactivated technician {Technician-C} still leading {Work-order-3}; a Complete, not yet invoiced {Work-order-2}; then a refused drop onto {Technician-C}, and a failed move of {Work-order-2} because it is invoiced in a second tab before the drop lands.

• GET /api/staff lists staff; each row has both id and staff_id, and the Schedule's staffId and the working-hours calls use staff_id.

• Create {Technician-C} new for this case (never deactivate a shared technician). Create {Customer-A}, its contact and asset, and {Work-order-1} to {Work-order-3} on screen as in Part 1. Record {Work-order-1 id} to {Work-order-3 id}.

• Use two pages in one Playwright browser context: page 1 holds Board View (steps 1 to 5 and 8 to 13), page 2 opens {Work-order-2} and clicks Create Invoice (steps 6 and 7). Never reload page 1 between step 7 and step 9.

• UNVERIFIED on the Work Orders QA site: whether Board View refreshes a card by itself when another tab changes its status, and whether Create Invoice needs anything more on this build. If page 1 already shows {Work-order-2} locked before step 9 (it is then offered only within {Technician-A}'s column), record that and mark steps 9 and 10 as not checked by hand; do not force the request outside the screen.

• Steps 9 and 10: record the network answer to the drop (the tech plan says the board move is refused only when the lead would change) and read the alert text exactly; compare it character for character with "Failed to update lead technician, please try again." Record the status explanation exactly as shown (the PRD does not give its wording).

• Steps 13 and 14: confirm with GET on {Work-order-2} that the lead is still {Technician-A} and the status is Invoiced.

• Context: this case follows PRD version 49 (14 Oct 2026). Nothing about how the build behaves today is assumed; judge only what you see.

• Evidence: a screenshot after each step that has an expected result, the build marker, and the real value of every brace name.
