Setup: An Entire work order shift stops counting once a new line is approved

Test case: C581893, WO Board and Tech View. Part 1 is for a manual QA tester. Part 2 is for a Claude session that prepares and runs the case on its own. Both create the starting state listed in the test case's Preconditions.

How to read this document: a name in braces, such as {Part-A} or {Line-1}, stands for a record you create or pick. Every brace name is defined before it is used. Wherever a value is given "for example", you may use any other value: the example is not a record that already exists in the app. Write down the real value you used for each brace name, because the test steps refer to it.

What the setup creates (the test case's Preconditions)

• You are signed in as an Owner or Admin user whose role has Work orders > View and Work orders > Create & Edit and Schedule access, at {Location-A} (for example Staging Heavy Duty - 9919).

• Your browser window is at least 1024 px wide, so the display switcher (List, Tech View, Board View) shows at the right of the Work Orders toolbar.

• Technician {Technician-A} (for example Esther Howard) is an active staff member at {Location-A} with Time Clock on, the Service department, and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User.

• Technician {Technician-B} (for example Ralph Edwards) is an active staff member at {Location-A} with Time Clock on, the Service department, and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User.

• Customer {Customer-A} (for example "ZZAUTOTEST S4R28 Line Added") is used by this case only.

• Work order {Work-order-1} (for example S10043-17651) for {Customer-A} is led by {Technician-A} and had two approved lines, {Line-1} (for example "Service - Full grease service") and {Line-2} (for example "Service - Adjust clutch"), when its shift was made.

• Work order {Work-order-2} (for example S10043-17652) for {Customer-A} is led by {Technician-A} and had two approved lines, {Line-3} (for example "Service - Full grease service") and {Line-4} (for example "Service - Adjust clutch"), when its shift was made.

• {Technician-A} has a shift tomorrow 8:00–12:00 on {Work-order-1} and a shift tomorrow 13:00–17:00 on {Work-order-2}, both made with Entire work order.

• After the shifts were made, a third line {Line-5} (for example "Replace - Brake pot") was added to {Work-order-1} with Line Approved on.

• After the shifts were made, a third line {Line-6} (for example "Replace - Brake pot") was added to {Work-order-2} with Line Approved off.

• No other shift is booked on {Work-order-1} or {Work-order-2}.

• On the Work Orders page nothing is ticked in Status, Asset on Site shows no choice (if its chip shows Yes or No, click the x on the chip), Assigned to me is off and Search is empty.

Part 1. Setup for manual QA tester

1. Sign in as an Owner or Admin user. On a QA site click the Admin quick-login button on the sign-in page; you appear as "Admin ShopView". To check the permissions: your initials (top right) > Settings > Roles & Permissions > Admin > Work orders: View and Create & Edit are on. Your role also needs Schedule access (Settings > Roles & Permissions > Admin > Schedule: View and Create & Edit).

2. Pick one location for the whole case; it is shown in the top bar next to the bell (to change it: your initials > Change Location). This is {Location-A} (for example Staging Heavy Duty - 9919). Create everything below at {Location-A}.

3. Use a desktop browser window at least 1024 px wide (narrower windows show only the List, with no display switcher).

4. Your initials > Settings > Staff. Reuse a staff member only if they are active, have Time Clock on, the Service department, {Location-A} in Location and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User. Otherwise click New Staff Member and enter First Name and Last Name (for example Esther Howard), an Email, Role Technician, Departments Service (only Service technicians get a row on the Schedule), Location {Location-A}, turn Time Clock on, then Save & Close. This is {Technician-A}.

5. Your initials > Settings > Staff. Reuse a staff member only if they are active, have Time Clock on, the Service department, {Location-A} in Location and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User. Otherwise click New Staff Member and enter First Name and Last Name (for example Ralph Edwards), an Email, Role Technician, Departments Service (only Service technicians get a row on the Schedule), Location {Location-A}, turn Time Clock on, then Save & Close. This is {Technician-B}.

6. Top menu Customers > New Customer > Name (for example "ZZAUTOTEST S4R28 Line Added") > Save. This is {Customer-A}; use it for this case only. On the customer page click Contacts > New Contact > First Name and Last Name (for example ZZAUTOTEST Contact) > Save. Then Assets > New Asset > pick the contact, Year (for example 2022), Make (type "Freight" and pick Freightliner), Model (for example M2), Unit (for example TRK-118) > Save. Contact and Make are required.

7. Work Orders > Create Work Order > in the New Work Order window pick {Customer-A} and its asset > Save (if a Confirmation about the credit limit appears, click Create). The work order opens on its Lines tab with the New Line form. In What Are You Doing? type "grease" and pick the ready-made line "Service - Full grease service", turn Line Approved on and click Save & Add Line. In the next New Line form type "clutch" and pick the ready-made line "Service - Adjust clutch", turn Line Approved on and click Save & Close. The status reads Approved. Write down the number shown at the top left (for example S10043-17651). Repeat this once more. Number them {Work-order-1} (lines {Line-1} and {Line-2}) and {Work-order-2} (lines {Line-3} and {Line-4}).

8. On the work order page pick the technician in the Lead Technician dropdown; it saves at once. Give both the lead {Technician-A}.

9. Schedule (top menu) > use the arrows next to Today until the date reads tomorrow > in Search work orders (left) type the number of {Work-order-1} > drag its card onto {Technician-A}'s row and drop it under 8 AM > in the window that opens keep Entire work order selected > use − / + until Hours reads 4h > click Create 1 shift.

10. Schedule (top menu) > use the arrows next to Today until the date reads tomorrow > in Search work orders (left) type the number of {Work-order-2} > drag its card onto {Technician-A}'s row and drop it under 1 PM > in the window that opens keep Entire work order selected > use − / + until Hours reads 4h > click Create 1 shift.

11. Only now, open {Work-order-1} > Lines tab > New Line > type "brake" and pick a ready-made line (for example "Replace - Brake pot"), turn Line Approved on and click Save & Close. This is {Line-5}.

12. Open {Work-order-2} > Lines tab > New Line > type "brake" and pick a ready-made line (for example "Replace - Brake pot"), leave Line Approved off and click Save & Close. This is {Line-6}.

13. Check the setup worked: on the Schedule for tomorrow {Technician-A}'s row shows 8:00–12:00 on {Work-order-1} and 13:00–17:00 on {Work-order-2}. {Work-order-1}'s Lines tab shows three approved lines; {Work-order-2}'s shows two approved lines and {Line-6} not approved. Work Orders > All > Board View with {Customer-A}'s name in Search shows both work orders in {Technician-A}'s column. Fix anything that differs before you start (if a line was added before its shift, create a fresh work order), then clear the search.

Part 2. Setup for Claude session

• Goal: two work orders led by {Technician-A}, each with an Entire work order shift tomorrow made while it had two approved lines; afterwards a third line is added, approved on {Work-order-1} and not approved on {Work-order-2}; then two drags in Board View.

• GET /api/staff lists staff; each row has both id and staff_id, and the Schedule's staffId and the working-hours calls use staff_id.

• Create {Customer-A}, its contact and asset, and {Work-order-1} and {Work-order-2} on screen as in Part 1. Record {Work-order-1 id}, {Work-order-2 id} and {Line-1 id} to {Line-4 id}.

• Faster route for shifts, UNVERIFIED on the Work Orders QA site (proven only on the Schedule QA site sv8685, build v3.5-7ec992f): POST /api/schedule/shifts with workOrderId, lineIds (every line id for an entire-work-order shift, the chosen line ids otherwise), staffId, startDate, startTime, spreadMode "single", totalMinutes, perDayMinutes and isAllDay false; 201 means created. If it is refused, use the Schedule screen as in Part 1 and record what the screen sent. For this case lineIds holds every line id the work order has at that moment ({Line-1 id} and {Line-2 id}; {Line-3 id} and {Line-4 id}).

• Read shifts back with GET /api/schedule/board?from=<ISO instant>&to=<ISO instant> (full instants such as 2026-10-15T00:00:00Z; a bare date or a range over 62 days is refused). Compare each shift's start and end with the location's time zone before deciding a shift is wrong (SV-11229, a shift saved six hours off, was closed Obsolete on 12 Oct 2026).

• Order matters: make both shifts first, then add {Line-5} and {Line-6}. UNVERIFIED on the Work Orders QA site: whether the Schedule extends an Entire work order shift to a line added later. Read the shift's lines back after adding the lines and record them; do not change them.

• Trap: on the Schedule a work order with only one line is booked at once as a whole, with no Entire work order / Choose lines window (recorded in C368178).

• Steps 3 to 7: Playwright drag-and-drop from each card to {Technician-B}'s column; record whether a question opened and its title exactly, and the message text.

• Context: this case follows PRD version 49 (14 Oct 2026). Nothing about how the build behaves today is assumed; judge only what you see.

• Evidence: a screenshot after each step that has an expected result, the build marker, and the real value of every brace name.
