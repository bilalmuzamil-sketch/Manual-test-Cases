Setup: The Clear shifts question ignores lines that are not approved or are declined

Test case: C581892, WO Board and Tech View. Part 1 is for a manual QA tester. Part 2 is for a Claude session that prepares and runs the case on its own. Both create the starting state listed in the test case's Preconditions.

How to read this document: a name in braces, such as {Part-A} or {Line-1}, stands for a record you create or pick. Every brace name is defined before it is used. Wherever a value is given "for example", you may use any other value: the example is not a record that already exists in the app. Write down the real value you used for each brace name, because the test steps refer to it.

Part 1. Setup for manual QA tester

1. Sign in as an Owner or Admin user. On a QA site click the Admin quick-login button on the sign-in page; you appear as "Admin ShopView". To check the permissions: your initials (top right) > Settings > Roles & Permissions > Admin > Work orders: View and Create & Edit are on. Your role also needs Schedule access (Settings > Roles & Permissions > Admin > Schedule: View and Create & Edit).

2. Pick one location for the whole case; it is shown in the top bar next to the bell (to change it: your initials > Change Location). This is {Location-A} (for example Staging Heavy Duty - 9919). Create everything below at {Location-A}.

3. Use a desktop browser window at least 1024 px wide (narrower windows show only the List, with no display switcher).

4. Your initials > Settings > Staff. Reuse a staff member only if they are active, have Time Clock on, the Service department, {Location-A} in Location and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User. Otherwise click New Staff Member and enter First Name and Last Name (for example Esther Howard), an Email, Role Technician, Departments Service (only Service technicians get a row on the Schedule), Location {Location-A}, turn Time Clock on, then Save & Close. This is {Technician-A}.

5. Your initials > Settings > Staff. Reuse a staff member only if they are active, have Time Clock on, the Service department, {Location-A} in Location and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User. Otherwise click New Staff Member and enter First Name and Last Name (for example Ralph Edwards), an Email, Role Technician, Departments Service (only Service technicians get a row on the Schedule), Location {Location-A}, turn Time Clock on, then Save & Close. This is {Technician-B}.

6. Top menu Customers > New Customer > Name (for example "ZZAUTOTEST S4R28 Approved Lines") > Save. This is {Customer-A}; use it for this case only. On the customer page click Contacts > New Contact > First Name and Last Name (for example ZZAUTOTEST Contact) > Save. Then Assets > New Asset > pick the contact, Year (for example 2022), Make (type "Freight" and pick Freightliner), Model (for example M2), Unit (for example TRK-118) > Save. Contact and Make are required.

7. Work Orders > Create Work Order > in the New Work Order window pick {Customer-A} and its asset > Save (if a Confirmation about the credit limit appears, click Create). The work order opens on its Lines tab with the New Line form. In What Are You Doing? type "grease" and pick the ready-made line "Service - Full grease service", turn Line Approved on and click Save & Add Line. In the next New Line form type "clutch" and pick the ready-made line "Service - Adjust clutch", leave Line Approved off and click Save & Close. Write down the number shown at the top left (for example S10043-17641). This is {Work-order-1}; its first line is {Line-1} (approved) and its second {Line-2} (not approved).

8. Work Orders > Create Work Order > in the New Work Order window pick {Customer-A} and its asset > Save (if a Confirmation about the credit limit appears, click Create). The work order opens on its Lines tab with the New Line form. In What Are You Doing? type "grease" and pick the ready-made line "Service - Full grease service", turn Line Approved on and click Save & Add Line. In the next New Line form type "clutch" and pick the ready-made line "Service - Adjust clutch", leave Line Approved off and click Save & Close, then click Decline on that second line so it reads Declined. Write down the number (for example S10043-17642). This is {Work-order-2}; its first line is {Line-3} (approved) and its second {Line-4} (declined).

9. Work Orders > Create Work Order > in the New Work Order window pick {Customer-A} and its asset > Save (if a Confirmation about the credit limit appears, click Create). The work order opens on its Lines tab with the New Line form. In What Are You Doing? type "grease" and pick the ready-made line "Service - Full grease service", turn Line Approved on and click Save & Add Line. In the next New Line form type "clutch" and pick the ready-made line "Service - Adjust clutch", turn Line Approved on and click Save & Close. Write down the number (for example S10043-17643). This is {Work-order-3}, with {Line-5} and {Line-6}.

10. On the work order page pick the technician in the Lead Technician dropdown; it saves at once. Give all three the lead {Technician-A}.

11. Schedule (top menu) > use the arrows next to Today until the date reads tomorrow > in Search work orders (left) type the number of {Work-order-1} > drag its card onto {Technician-A}'s row and drop it under 8 AM > in the window that opens choose Choose lines and tick {Line-1} only (leave every other line unticked) > use − / + until Hours reads 2h > click Create 1 shift. If the Schedule books it at once without that window, that is fine: it then covers the only approved line.

12. Schedule (top menu) > use the arrows next to Today until the date reads tomorrow > in Search work orders (left) type the number of {Work-order-2} > drag its card onto {Technician-A}'s row and drop it under 10 AM > in the window that opens choose Choose lines and tick {Line-3} only (leave every other line unticked) > use − / + until Hours reads 2h > click Create 1 shift. If the Schedule books it at once without that window, that is fine: it then covers the only approved line.

13. Schedule (top menu) > use the arrows next to Today until the date reads tomorrow > in Search work orders (left) type the number of {Work-order-3} > drag its card onto {Technician-A}'s row and drop it under 1 PM > in the window that opens choose Choose lines and tick both {Line-5} and {Line-6} (leave every other line unticked) > use − / + until Hours reads 2h > click Create 1 shift.

14. Check the setup worked: on the Schedule for tomorrow {Technician-A}'s row shows 8:00–10:00 on {Work-order-1}, 10:00–12:00 on {Work-order-2} and 13:00–15:00 on {Work-order-3}; none of the three shifts names {Line-2} or {Line-4}. Part 2. Setup for Claude session

• Goal: three work orders led by {Technician-A}, each with a shift tomorrow that covers every approved line (one with a line not approved, one with a declined line, one Choose lines shift ticking both approved lines); then three drags in Board View, each answered with Keep shifts.

• GET /api/staff lists staff; each row has both id and staff_id, and the Schedule's staffId and the working-hours calls use staff_id.

• Create {Customer-A}, its contact and asset, and {Work-order-1} to {Work-order-3} on screen as in Part 1. Record {Work-order-1 id} to {Work-order-3 id} and {Line-1 id}, {Line-3 id}, {Line-5 id} and {Line-6 id} from the Lines tab requests.

• Faster route for shifts, UNVERIFIED on the Work Orders QA site (proven only on the Schedule QA site sv8685, build v3.5-7ec992f): POST /api/schedule/shifts with workOrderId, lineIds (every line id for an entire-work-order shift, the chosen line ids otherwise), staffId, startDate, startTime, spreadMode "single", totalMinutes, perDayMinutes and isAllDay false; 201 means created. If it is refused, use the Schedule screen as in Part 1 and record what the screen sent. For this case lineIds holds {Line-1 id} only for {Work-order-1}, {Line-3 id} only for {Work-order-2}, and {Line-5 id} and {Line-6 id} for {Work-order-3} (made on screen with Choose lines if you want the exact screen route).

• Read shifts back with GET /api/schedule/board?from=<ISO instant>&to=<ISO instant> (full instants such as 2026-10-15T00:00:00Z; a bare date or a range over 62 days is refused). Compare each shift's start and end with the location's time zone before deciding a shift is wrong (SV-11229, a shift saved six hours off, was closed Obsolete on 12 Oct 2026).

• Trap: on the Schedule a work order with only one line is booked at once as a whole, with no Entire work order / Choose lines window (recorded in C368178). Record which window appeared.

• Step 14: read the Schedule on screen and confirm with the board read above that the three shifts are unchanged.
