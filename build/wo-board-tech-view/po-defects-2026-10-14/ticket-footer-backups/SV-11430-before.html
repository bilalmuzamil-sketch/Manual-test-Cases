Preconditions

  • Role: Admin.

  • The app is in dark theme.

Environment

  • Staging — app.staging.shopview.com, Chrome 154 / Windows 10.

Steps to reproduce

  1. Go to Work Orders and select Board View at a desktop width of 1440 px.

  2. Look at the right edge of the Unassigned column and of each technician column.

  3. Reload the page and look again.

Expected result

Per SV-10044 (PRD S1-R9c: Board View works in dark theme "using the design file's dark colors") and the design's dark-theme Board View, each column's scrollbar is a thin, dark track that blends into the board.

Actual result

Every column body shows a bright white scrollbar track against the dark panel (Unassigned and every technician column).
