Overview

While a work order is being dragged in Board View or Tech View, a small preview box (work order number + customer name) follows the pointer. When the customer name is long, the text runs outside that box. In Board View it runs across the next columns.

Steps to Reproduce

  1. Make sure there is a work order whose customer name is long (examples on Staging: S2-36633, customer "ZZBREAK <B>Bold</B> "Q" & 'X' Superlongunbrokencustomernamewithoutanyspaces…"; S2-35480, customer "ZZAUTOTEST F1 Invoiced Paid Locked 59417").

  2. Go to Work Orders → All → Board View.

  3. Start dragging that work order's card and hold it.

  4. Look at the preview box that follows the pointer.

  5. Switch to Tech View, start dragging the work order's row and look at the preview box again.

Observed Outcome

  • Board View (S2-36633): the customer name runs far past the preview's right edge, across the neighbouring columns and cards.

  • Tech View (S2-35480): the end of the customer name ("59417") sits outside the preview's right edge.

Expected Outcome

The customer name stays inside the drag preview, cut off or wrapped, so nothing is drawn outside the box.

The spec does not describe the drag preview or how long names are cut off there (checked: Story 9 S9-R1 to S9-E3, no rule on the preview). The exact treatment is for design or the PO to confirm.

Evidence

Source
