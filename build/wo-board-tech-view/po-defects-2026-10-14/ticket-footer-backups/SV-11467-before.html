Found while testing SV-11318

Summary

In Tech View at Compact density, the red and blue count bubbles on a work order's status pill cover most of the status name. On a Ready For Review pill they sit over the word "view", so the status is hard to read. At Regular and Comfortable, and in the List, the bubbles only touch the top of the letters and the status stays readable.

Steps to reproduce

Open Staging: https://app.staging.shopview.com (window about 1600 px wide, browser zoom 100%).

  1. In the top menu, click Work Orders, then the All tab.

  2. On the right of the bar above the list, click the second of the three view buttons (the person icon) to open Tech View.

  3. Click the density button (the icon just right of Asset on Site) and choose Compact.

  4. Click Search (the magnifying glass) and type S2-33351. If the Unassigned group is closed, click the small arrow to the left of Unassigned to open it.

  5. Look at the status pill in the first column of that row: the red 2 and blue 3 bubbles sit on top of the word "Review".

Fastest way to see it: open https://app.staging.shopview.com/workorders?search=S2-33351 (the search is filled in), switch to Tech View, set density to Compact and open the Unassigned group.

Expected behaviour

The count bubbles sit on the pill's corner and the status name stays readable at every density, the same as on the List's pill.

Where this comes from:

  • Spec "Work Orders — Board View & Tech View Display Options", version 64, S2-R19: Tech View's Status column is "the same status pill List shows in production, with the same red service-actions count and blue parts-actions count on it. Its size follows density (S6-R2a)."

  • Same spec, S6-R2a: "In Tech View the status pill is 16px tall at Compact and Regular and 22px tall at Comfortable … only its height follows density."

Current behaviour

At Compact the pill is 16 px tall and the 19 px bubbles sit lower on it than on the List's pill, so they cover most of the word.
