Description

When two people reorder the same technician's work orders at the same time, the later save should win and both should see that order after a refresh.

For example,

  • two people have the same Board View open

  • the first moves one card to the top of a technician's column

  • the second, without refreshing, moves a different card to the top of the same column

  • after a refresh both see a mix of the two moves, not the second person's order

Steps to Reproduce

  1. In two browsers open Work Orders > All > Board View and search the same customer; one technician leads three of its work orders, shown as Alpha Co, Bravo Co, Charlie Co.

  2. In browser 1, drag Charlie Co to the top of that technician's column.

    • Browser 1 shows Charlie Co, Alpha Co, Bravo Co.

  3. In browser 2, without refreshing, drag Bravo Co to the top of the same column.

    • Browser 2 shows Bravo Co, Alpha Co, Charlie Co. No warning in either browser.

  4. Refresh both browsers.

    • Both show Charlie Co, Bravo Co, Alpha Co.

Actual Result:

  • After both refresh, the column shows Charlie Co, Bravo Co, Alpha Co — a mix of the two moves.

Expected Result:

  • The later save wins: both browsers show browser 2's order, Bravo Co, Alpha Co, Charlie Co.

Environment

  • Build: v26.40.8-7a95011 on the QA branch sv10043

  • Location: Staging Heavy Duty - 9919 · Users: two people signed in separately (Admin and the technician quick-login)

  • Example (10 Oct 2026, seen again): customer search ZZAUTOTEST F3 Later Drop Wins, three work orders led by ZZAUTOTEST Ana Alpha: Alpha Co = S10043-18798, Bravo Co = S10043-18799, Charlie Co = S10043-18800 (the cards show the numbers)

Sources

  • PRD – Work Orders Board View & Tech View — S9-R11 (page version 44), read 10 Oct 2026:

    Save the manual order of work orders once per location. Everyone at the location sees the same order, in both Tech View and Board View. If two people reorder at the same time, the later save wins; the other person sees the saved order on their next refresh and is not warned.
