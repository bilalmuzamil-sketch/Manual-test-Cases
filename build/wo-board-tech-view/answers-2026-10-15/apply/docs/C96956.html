Setup: Reassign lead technician lists eligible technicians with Unassigned first

Test case: C96956, WO Board and Tech View. Part 1 is for a manual QA tester. Part 2 is for a Claude session that prepares and runs the case on its own. Both create the starting state listed in the test case's Preconditions.

How to read this document: a name in braces, such as {Part-A} or {Line-1}, stands for a record you create or pick. Every brace name is defined before it is used. Wherever a value is given "for example", you may use any other value: the example is not a record that already exists in the app. Write down the real value you used for each brace name, because the test steps refer to it.

What the setup creates (the test case's Preconditions)

• You are signed in as an Owner or Admin user whose role has Work orders > View and Work orders > Create & Edit, at {Location-A} (for example Staging Heavy Duty - 9919).

• Your browser window is at least 1024 px wide, so the display switcher (List, Tech View, Board View) shows at the right of the Work Orders toolbar.

• Technicians {Technician-A} (for example Esther Howard), {Technician-B} (for example Ralph Edwards) and {Technician-C} (for example Dana Ortiz) are active staff members at {Location-A} with Time Clock on and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User.

• Staff member {User-B} (for example Owen Office) is active at {Location-A} with the Office User role.

• Technician {Technician-D} (for example Ivan Inactive) has a Technician role at {Location-A} and is deactivated.

• Technician {Technician-E} (for example Lena Otherloc) is active with Time Clock on but works only at another location, {Location-B} (for example Staging Light Duty - 9920).

• Customer {Customer-A} (for example "ZZAUTOTEST F1 Reassign List") has one Approved work order, {Work-order-1} (for example S10043-17641), led by {Technician-A}.

• No technician has a shift on the Schedule for {Work-order-1}.

• On the Work Orders page nothing is ticked in Status, Asset on Site shows no choice (if its chip shows Yes or No, click the x on the chip), Assigned to me is off and Search is empty.

• Developer note kept from the automation engineer (nothing for the tester to do): Dev-layer test — fe-unit + be-functional. Existing coverage: ReassignLeadTechnicianDialog.spec.ts:279,463; BE ListLeadTechnicianCandidatesTest.php:59,65

Part 1. Setup for manual QA tester

1. Sign in as an Owner or Admin user. On a QA site click the Admin quick-login button on the sign-in page; you appear as "Admin ShopView". To check the permissions: your initials (top right) > Settings > Roles & Permissions > Admin > Work orders: View and Create & Edit are on.

2. Pick one location for the whole case; it is shown in the top bar next to the bell (to change it: your initials > Change Location). This is {Location-A} (for example Staging Heavy Duty - 9919). Create everything below at {Location-A}.

3. Use a desktop browser window at least 1024 px wide (narrower windows show only the List, with no display switcher).

4. Your initials > Settings > Staff. Reuse a staff member only if they are active, have Time Clock on, {Location-A} in Location and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User. Otherwise click New Staff Member and enter First Name and Last Name (for example Esther Howard), an Email, Role Technician, at least one Department (required), Location {Location-A}, turn Time Clock on, then Save & Close. This is {Technician-A}.

5. Your initials > Settings > Staff. Reuse a staff member only if they are active, have Time Clock on, {Location-A} in Location and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User. Otherwise click New Staff Member and enter First Name and Last Name (for example Ralph Edwards), an Email, Role Technician, at least one Department (required), Location {Location-A}, turn Time Clock on, then Save & Close. This is {Technician-B}.

6. Your initials > Settings > Staff. Reuse a staff member only if they are active, have Time Clock on, {Location-A} in Location and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User. Otherwise click New Staff Member and enter First Name and Last Name (for example Dana Ortiz), an Email, Role Technician, at least one Department (required), Location {Location-A}, turn Time Clock on, then Save & Close. This is {Technician-C}.

7. Your initials > Settings > Staff > New Staff Member: First Name and Last Name (for example Owen Office), an Email, Role Office User, at least one Department, Location {Location-A} > Save & Close. This is {User-B}.

8. Your initials > Settings > Staff. Reuse a staff member only if they are active, have Time Clock on, {Location-A} in Location and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User. Otherwise click New Staff Member and enter First Name and Last Name (for example Ivan Inactive), an Email, Role Technician, at least one Department (required), Location {Location-A}, turn Time Clock on, then Save & Close. This is {Technician-D}. Then open the staff member's edit icon > Edit Staff Member and deactivate the account.

9. Pick a second location you can reach (your initials > Change Location lists them). This is {Location-B} (for example Staging Light Duty - 9920). Your initials > Settings > Staff. Reuse a staff member only if they are active, have Time Clock on, {Location-B} in Location and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User. Otherwise click New Staff Member and enter First Name and Last Name (for example Lena Otherloc), an Email, Role Technician, at least one Department (required), Location {Location-B} only, turn Time Clock on, then Save & Close. This is {Technician-E}.

10. Top menu Customers > New Customer > Name (for example "ZZAUTOTEST F1 Reassign List") > Save. This is {Customer-A}; use it for this case only. On the customer page click Contacts > New Contact > First Name and Last Name (for example ZZAUTOTEST Contact) > Save. Then Assets > New Asset > pick the contact, Year (for example 2022), Make (type "Freight" and pick Freightliner), Model (for example M2), Unit (for example TRK-118) > Save. Contact and Make are required.

11. Work Orders > Create Work Order > in the New Work Order window pick {Customer-A} and its asset > Save (if a Confirmation about the credit limit appears, click Create). The work order opens on its Lines tab with the New Line form. In What Are You Doing? type "grease" and pick the ready-made line "Service - Full grease service", turn Line Approved on and click Save & Close. The status reads Approved. Write down the number shown at the top left (for example S10043-17641). This is {Work-order-1}. On {Work-order-1}'s page pick {Technician-A} in the Lead Technician dropdown; it saves at once (the dropdown shows once the work order is Approved).

12. Open the Schedule page and check that {Technician-A}, {Technician-B} and {Technician-C} have no shift on this case's work orders. If a window titled "Clear … scheduled shifts?" still appears during a move, click Keep shifts.

13. Work Orders > All: clear any Status selection, click the x on the Asset on Site chip if it shows Yes or No, turn Assigned to me off and empty Search.

14. Check the setup worked: Work Orders > All > Search the name of {Customer-A} shows {Work-order-1} led by {Technician-A}; Settings > Staff shows {User-B} with the Office User role, {Technician-D} as deactivated and {Technician-E} at {Location-B} only. Then empty Search.
