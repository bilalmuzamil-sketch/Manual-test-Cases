TITLE: Removing the lead asks the same clear-shifts question as changing it

=== PRECONDITIONS ===
Preconditions
- Needs: 1 user · 1 browser · Schedule access · about 20 minutes.
- Build: the Work Orders QA build (sv10043, v26.40.8-7a95011).
- [User-A] = you: an Owner/Admin user (on a QA branch the Admin quick-login user, shown as "Admin ShopView"). The Admin role holds Work orders > View and Work orders > Create & Edit.
- Desktop browser window at least 1024 px wide. Narrower windows show only the List, without the display switcher.
- One location for the whole case, shown in the top bar next to the bell (e.g. "Staging Heavy Duty - 9919"); everything below is created in this location.
- [Tech-A] "Esther Howard": one eligible technician at this location. A person only counts as a technician here when the staff member is active (not deactivated), Time Clock is on, the Role is neither Office User nor Time Clock User, and this location is picked in their Location field.
- Customer "ZZAUTOTEST F1 Remove Lead Prompt" (used by this case only) with two Approved work orders led by [Tech-A]: [WO-1] and [WO-2].
- A shift for [Tech-A] on each of them tomorrow 8:00–12:00 (entire work order).
- Esther Howard is named in the expected results (the prompt title) and stays as written; the numbers are examples.
Setup
- Sign in as an Owner/Admin user (on a QA branch: the Admin quick-login button on the sign-in page; you appear as "Admin ShopView"). The Admin role holds Work orders > View and Work orders > Create & Edit (to check: your initials at the top right > Settings > Roles & Permissions > Admin > Work orders).
- Use one location for the whole case: it is shown in the top bar next to the bell (e.g. "Staging Heavy Duty - 9919"); to change it click your initials at the top right > Change Location.
- Settings (your initials at the top right > Settings) > Staff: reuse a technician only after checking it is active, has Time Clock on, a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User and this location in Location; otherwise add one: New Staff Member > First Name and Last Name, an Email, Role "Technician", at least one Department (required), Location = this location, Time Clock on > Save & Close. Record them as [Tech-A] = "Esther Howard" (use exactly this name: the expected results name it).
- Create the customer used only by this case: top menu Customers > New Customer > Name "ZZAUTOTEST F1 Remove Lead Prompt" > Save. On the customer page click the Contacts tab > New Contact > First Name and Last Name > Save (a new asset needs a contact). Then click the Assets tab > New Asset > pick the Contact, then Year, Make, Model and Unit (e.g. 2022, Freightliner, M2, unit "TRK-118") > Save. Make is required; Unit can be left empty.
- Create these work orders for "ZZAUTOTEST F1 Remove Lead Prompt": Work Orders > Create Work Order > in the New Work Order window pick the Customer and its Asset > Save. The new work order opens with the New Line form (otherwise click New Line on the Lines tab): in "What Are You Doing?" pick a ready-made line (e.g. "Replace - Brake pot"; typed free text finds no results), turn on Line Approved so the work order status reads Approved, and click Save & Close. Write down the number the build gives the work order (on a QA branch they look like "S10043-17581"). Then pick the lead in the Lead Technician dropdown on the work order page; it saves at once. (While a work order is an Estimate its Lead technician is read-only on the work order page; give an Estimate its lead from Board View instead: the card's More actions (…) > Reassign lead technician > pick the technician > Reassign.) Leave Lead Technician empty on the ones that should sit in Unassigned.- [WO-1] (e.g. S1-702): Lead Technician = [Tech-A].
- [WO-2] (e.g. S1-698): Lead Technician = [Tech-A].

- On the Schedule, give [Tech-A] a shift on each of them tomorrow 8:00–12:00: Schedule > go to that day > drag the work order from the work order list onto [Tech-A]'s row at the start time > in the picker choose Entire work order > set the end time > save.
- Check the setup worked: on the Schedule for tomorrow, [Tech-A]'s row has an 8:00–12:00 shift on [WO-1] and one on [WO-2]; both work orders show Lead Technician [Tech-A]. If not, fix it before starting.

=== STEPS ===
- Open Work Orders, click the All tab and pick Board View in the display switcher (right side of the toolbar; tooltip "Board View").
- Type "ZZAUTOTEST F1 Remove Lead Prompt" in Search.
- Drag [WO-1] from [Tech-A]'s column into the Unassigned column.
- Read the prompt's title, text and buttons, then click Keep shifts.
- Hover [WO-2], click More actions > Reassign lead technician, pick Unassigned and confirm.
- Read the prompt, then click Keep shifts.

=== EXPECTED ===
Expected results
- Both removals open the same shift-clearing prompt as a change of lead: title "Clear Esther Howard's scheduled shifts?" with the buttons Keep shifts, Clear shifts and Cancel.
- After Keep shifts both work orders are in Unassigned and Esther Howard's shifts are still on the Schedule.
What you should see today (build v26.40.8-7a95011, 10/8/2026): changing the lead (by dragging the card or with Reassign lead technician) shows no question about scheduled shifts, even when the outgoing lead has a shift booked for the entire work order. The lead changes at once with the "Lead technician updated" (or "Lead technician removed") message, and the outgoing lead's shift stays on the Schedule. (1) If you see exactly that, mark the case Failed and raise nothing new. (2) If it fails in a different way, that is a new problem: report it. (3) If it behaves as the expected results above say, the change has shipped: mark it Passed and tell the QA lead.
