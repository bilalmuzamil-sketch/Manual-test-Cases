TITLE: The keyboard cannot get around permission or status limits

=== PRECONDITIONS ===
Setup (manual QA tester and Claude session)

Preconditions
- You are signed in as an Owner or Admin user whose role has Work orders > View and Work orders > Create & Edit, at {Location-A} (for example Staging Heavy Duty - 9919).
- Your browser window is at least 1024 px wide, so the display switcher (List, Tech View, Board View) shows at the right of the Work Orders toolbar.
- Technician {Technician-A} (for example ZZAUTOTEST Ana Alpha) is an active staff member at {Location-A} with Time Clock on and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User.
- Technician {Technician-B} (for example ZZAUTOTEST Ben Bravo) is an active staff member at {Location-A} with Time Clock on and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User.
- User {User-B} (for example ZZAUTOTEST Viewer) has a role (for example "ZZAUTOTEST WO View Only") with only View ticked under Work orders, at {Location-A}, and can sign in in a second browser or a private window.
- Customer {Customer-A} (for example "ZZAUTOTEST F3 Keyboard Limits") has two work orders led by {Technician-A}: {Work-order-1} (for example S10043-17711), Approved, and {Work-order-2} (for example S10043-17712), Invoiced.
- No technician has a shift on the Schedule for these work orders.
- On the Work Orders page nothing is ticked in Status, Asset on Site shows no choice (if its chip shows Yes or No, click the x on the chip), Assigned to me is off and Search is empty.

=== STEPS ===
- In the second browser, sign in as {User-B}, open Work Orders, click the All tab, type the name of {Customer-A} in Search and click Board View.
- Click once in the Search box, then use only the keyboard: press Tab until {Work-order-1}'s card has focus.
- Press Tab to reach anything on the card that opens a menu, press Enter on it, and look for Reassign lead technician.
- Press Space, the arrow keys and Shift with the arrow keys on {Work-order-1}'s card.
- As yourself (the Owner or Admin user), open Work Orders, click the All tab, type the name of {Customer-A} in Search and click Board View.
- Click once in the Search box, then use only the keyboard: press Tab until {Work-order-2}'s card has focus.
- Press Tab until its More actions (…) button has focus and press Enter.
- Move to Reassign lead technician and press Enter.
- Press Escape, then press Space, the arrow keys and Shift with the arrow keys on {Work-order-2}'s card.
- Refresh the page, open {Work-order-2} and read Lead Technician.

=== EXPECTED ===
Expected results
- Step 3: {User-B} cannot reach Reassign lead technician by keyboard: it is not offered, or it is disabled and Enter does nothing.
- Step 4: no key moves {Work-order-1}; it keeps its place and its lead, {Technician-A}.
- Steps 7 and 8: the menu opens, but Reassign lead technician is disabled for the Invoiced work order and Enter does nothing. Its note reads "The lead technician can’t be changed once a work order is Invoiced or Paid."
- Step 9: no key moves {Work-order-2} to another technician.
- Step 10: {Work-order-2}'s Lead Technician is still {Technician-A}.

Until 15 Oct 2026 Story 11 only listed keyboard behaviour the design still had to define, so this case asked the tester to try keys and write down what each did. Chris Ward answered on 15 Oct 2026 (answer 28): Tab reaches every control, Enter opens, Escape closes, focus returns to the control that opened a menu or dialog, reassigning by keyboard goes through the ⋯ menu, and there is no keyboard drag. PRD version 54 rewrites Story 11 into these requirements, and this case now checks them.
Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
- S11-N1: “Keyboard access must not bypass action permissions or work order status restrictions.”
- S11-R9: “Reassigning by keyboard goes through the more-actions menu: Reassign lead technician opens the Reassign dialog, and Unassign is a menu item. There is no keyboard drag in this release, so reordering work orders and technicians is by mouse or touch only.”
- S4-N1: “Require Work Orders create and edit permission to drag work orders or access Reassign lead technician.”
- S4-N6: “The disabled Reassign lead technician action on an Invoiced or Paid work order shows this tooltip: “The lead technician can’t be changed once a work order is Invoiced or Paid.””
- Tech plan Phase 11 tests: “a keyboard-initiated reassign is refused for a locked status and without permission (S11-N1).”
