TITLE: Dragging a work order onto the Schedule with Choose lines makes a line shift

=== PRECONDITIONS ===
Preconditions
- Needs: 1 user · 1 browser · about 10 minutes
- Build: the Work Orders QA build (sv10043, v26.40.8-7a95011), desktop browser, window at least 1024 px wide.
- Role: Owner/Admin. On the sign-in page click the Admin quick-login button (you appear as Admin ShopView). The Admin role has Work orders > View and Create & Edit (check: your initials (top right) > Settings > Roles & Permissions > Admin > edit).
- Location: Staging Heavy Duty - 9919 for the whole case (shown next to the bell; change it with your initials > Change Location).
- Schedule access: the Admin role has Schedule > View and Create & Edit (Settings > Roles & Permissions > Admin > edit).
- One technician [Tech-A] in the Service department at this location. Name is an example ("Esther Howard").
- Customer "ZZAUTOTEST Schedule Choose Lines" (used by this case only) with one contact and one asset.
- One Approved work order [WO-1] with two approved lines: Line 1 = "Service - Full grease service", Line 2 = "Service - Adjust clutch".
- Nothing is scheduled for [Tech-A] tomorrow between 1 PM and 4 PM.
Setup
- Your initials (top right) > Settings > Staff > New Staff Member: First Name, Last Name, Email > Role = Technician > Departments = Service (only technicians in the Service department show as rows on the Schedule) > Location = Staging Heavy Duty - 9919 > turn Time Clock on > Save & Close. Record the technician as [Tech-A] (the expected results call this technician "Esther Howard").
- Customers > New Customer > Name "ZZAUTOTEST Schedule Choose Lines" > Save (the customer page opens). Contacts tab > New Contact > First Name "ZZAUTOTEST", Last Name "Contact" > Save. Assets tab > New Asset > Contact = ZZAUTOTEST Contact > Year 2022 > Make: type "Freight" and pick Freightliner > Model: type "M2" and pick it > Unit "TRK-118" > Save (Contact and Make are required).
- Work Orders > Create Work Order > New Work Order: Customer = "ZZAUTOTEST Schedule Choose Lines" > Asset = its asset > Save. If a Confirmation says the customer is over their credit limit, click Create. The work order opens on its Lines tab with the New Line form open. Record the number shown at the top left as [WO-1] (the expected results call it "S1-742").
- In New Line > What Are You Doing? type "grease" and pick the ready-made line "Service - Full grease service" (it has no parts) > turn Line Approved on > Save & Add Line. In New Line > What Are You Doing? type "clutch" and pick the ready-made line "Service - Adjust clutch" (it has no parts) > turn Line Approved on > Save & Close.
- Check the setup worked: [WO-1] shows two Approved lines numbered 1 and 2.

=== STEPS ===
- Click Schedule in the top menu and click the > arrow next to Today once so the date reads tomorrow.
- In Search work orders (left) type [WO-1] and find its card.
- Drag the card onto [Tech-A]'s row and drop it under 1 PM.
- In the window that opens click Choose lines and tick only "Service - Full grease service" (Line 1).
- Use − / + until Hours reads 2h and click the Create button ("Create shift · 1 line · …").
- Reload the page, go to tomorrow again and click the new shift on [Tech-A]'s row to open its details.

=== EXPECTED ===
Expected results
- Step 5: the shift saves with no error message.
- Step 6: Esther Howard's row shows a 13:00–15:00 shift for "S1-742" covering Line 1 only (1 of 2 lines).
