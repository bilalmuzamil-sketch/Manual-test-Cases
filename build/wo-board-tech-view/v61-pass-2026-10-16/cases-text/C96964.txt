TITLE: Invoiced and Paid work orders show why the lead can't be changed

=== PRECONDITIONS ===
Preconditions
- Needs: 1 user · 1 browser · about 25 minutes.
- Build: the Work Orders QA build (sv10043, v26.40.8-7a95011).
- [User-A] = you: an Owner/Admin user (on a QA branch the Admin quick-login user, shown as "Admin ShopView"). The Admin role holds Work orders > View and Work orders > Create & Edit.
- Desktop browser window at least 1024 px wide. Narrower windows show only the List, without the display switcher.
- One location for the whole case, shown in the top bar next to the bell (e.g. "Staging Heavy Duty - 9919"); everything below is created in this location.
- [Tech-A]: one eligible technician at this location. A person only counts as a technician here when the staff member is active (not deactivated), Time Clock is on, the Role is neither Office User nor Time Clock User, and this location is picked in their Location field.
- Customer "ZZAUTOTEST F1 Locked Lead Tooltip" (used by this case only), with a contact, and two work orders with Lead Technician [Tech-A] set before invoicing: [WO-1] Invoiced and [WO-2] Paid.
- Names and numbers are examples; what must hold: one Invoiced and one Paid work order, both with a lead.
Setup
- Sign in as an Owner/Admin user (on a QA branch: the Admin quick-login button on the sign-in page; you appear as "Admin ShopView"). The Admin role holds Work orders > View and Work orders > Create & Edit (to check: your initials at the top right > Settings > Roles & Permissions > Admin > Work orders).
- Use one location for the whole case: it is shown in the top bar next to the bell (e.g. "Staging Heavy Duty - 9919"); to change it click your initials at the top right > Change Location.
- Settings (your initials at the top right > Settings) > Staff: reuse a technician only after checking it is active, has Time Clock on, a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User and this location in Location; otherwise add one: New Staff Member > First Name and Last Name, an Email, Role "Technician", at least one Department (required), Location = this location, Time Clock on > Save & Close. Record them as [Tech-A] (e.g. Esther Howard).
- Create the customer used only by this case: top menu Customers > New Customer > Name "ZZAUTOTEST F1 Locked Lead Tooltip" > Save. On the customer page click the Contacts tab > New Contact > First Name and Last Name > Save (a new asset needs a contact). Then click the Assets tab > New Asset > pick the Contact, then Year, Make, Model and Unit (e.g. 2022, Freightliner, M2, unit "TRK-118") > Save. Make is required; Unit can be left empty.
- How to reach each status (standard steps): Estimate = a new work order whose lines are not approved. Approved = approve its lines. In Progress = a technician clicks Start time clock on an approved line's Labor row. Review = set the work order to Ready for Review with its status control. Complete = enter a tech story on every line, complete each line, then complete the work order. Invoiced = Finance tab > Create Invoice (the work order needs a contact person). Paid = record a full payment on that invoice. Declined = decline the estimate.
- Create these work orders for "ZZAUTOTEST F1 Locked Lead Tooltip": Work Orders > Create Work Order > in the New Work Order window pick the Customer and its Asset > Save. The new work order opens with the New Line form (otherwise click New Line on the Lines tab): in "What Are You Doing?" pick a ready-made line (e.g. "Replace - Brake pot"; typed free text finds no results), turn on Line Approved so the work order status reads Approved, and click Save & Close. Write down the number the build gives the work order (on a QA branch they look like "S10043-17581"). Then pick the lead in the Lead Technician dropdown on the work order page; it saves at once. (While a work order is an Estimate its Lead technician is read-only on the work order page; give an Estimate its lead from Board View instead: the card's More actions (…) > Reassign lead technician > pick the technician > Reassign.) Leave Lead Technician empty on the ones that should sit in Unassigned.- [WO-1] (e.g. S1-511): Lead Technician = [Tech-A], then taken to Invoiced.
- [WO-2] (e.g. S1-366): Lead Technician = [Tech-A], then taken to Paid.

- Check the setup worked: Work Orders > All tab > Search "ZZAUTOTEST F1 Locked Lead Tooltip" shows [WO-1] (Invoiced) and [WO-2] (Paid), both led by [Tech-A]. If not, fix the status or lead before starting; then clear the search.

=== STEPS ===
- Open Work Orders, click the All tab and pick Board View in the display switcher (right side of the toolbar; tooltip "Board View").
- Type "ZZAUTOTEST F1 Locked Lead Tooltip" in Search.
- Look at the [WO-1] card and write down any sign that it can only be reordered within its technician (e.g. a lock icon) and any tooltip shown when you hover that sign.
- Hover the card, click More actions and hover the Reassign lead technician action.
- Read the tooltip shown next to the Reassign lead technician action.
- Repeat steps 3 to 5 for [WO-2].
- Repeat steps 3 to 6 in Tech View on the rows.

=== EXPECTED ===
Expected results
- Reassign lead technician is shown disabled, and its tooltip reads exactly: The lead technician can’t be changed once a work order is Invoiced or Paid. (A straight or curly apostrophe is not a difference.)
- The Invoiced and Paid cards and rows carry a visible sign that they can only be reordered within their current technician and not moved to another one. The PRD does not fix its look; write down what you see.
