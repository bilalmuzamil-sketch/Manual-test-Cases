TITLE: Invoiced and Paid work orders can only be reordered within their technician

=== PRECONDITIONS ===
Setup (manual QA tester and Claude session)

Preconditions
- You are signed in as an Owner or Admin user whose role has Work orders > View and Work orders > Create & Edit, at {Location-A} (for example Staging Heavy Duty - 9919).
- Your browser window is at least 1024 px wide, so the display switcher (List, Tech View, Board View) shows at the right of the Work Orders toolbar.
- Technician {Technician-A} (for example ZZAUTOTEST Ana Alpha) is an active staff member at {Location-A} with Time Clock on and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User.
- Technician {Technician-B} (for example ZZAUTOTEST Ben Bravo) is an active staff member at {Location-A} with Time Clock on and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User.
- Customer {Customer-A} (for example "ZZAUTOTEST F3 Invoiced Lock") is used by this case only and has six work orders (for example S10043-17811 to S10043-17816): {Work-order-1} Approved, {Work-order-2} Invoiced, {Work-order-3} Paid, {Work-order-4} Declined and {Work-order-5} Complete, all led by {Technician-A}, and {Work-order-6} Invoiced with no lead technician.
- No technician has a shift on the Schedule for these work orders.
- On the Work Orders page nothing is ticked in Status, Asset on Site shows no choice (if its chip shows Yes or No, click the x on the chip), Assigned to me is off and Search is empty.

=== STEPS ===
- Open Work Orders from the top menu and click the All tab.
- Type the name of {Customer-A} in Search.
- Click Board View in the display switcher.
- Look at the {Work-order-2} and {Work-order-3} cards and hover the lock or any marker on them.
- Drag {Work-order-2}'s card above {Work-order-1}'s card inside {Technician-A}'s column and release it.
- Drag {Work-order-2}'s card into {Technician-B}'s column and release it.
- Drag {Work-order-3}'s card into the Unassigned column and release it.
- Drag {Work-order-6}'s card from Unassigned into {Technician-A}'s column and release it.
- Drag {Work-order-4}'s card into {Technician-B}'s column and release it, then drag {Work-order-5}'s card into {Technician-B}'s column and release it.
- Click Tech View in the display switcher.
- Drag {Work-order-3}'s row into {Technician-B}'s group and release it.

=== EXPECTED ===
Expected results
- Step 4: {Work-order-2} and {Work-order-3} show that they can only be reordered within {Technician-A}'s work, not moved to another technician (the design shows a lock with the text "Invoiced · can't be reassigned"). Write down the words you see where they differ from these; a different label alone is not a fail.
- Step 5: {Work-order-2} moves above {Work-order-1}. Reordering within the same technician is allowed.
- Steps 6 and 7: {Work-order-2} cannot be dropped on {Technician-B} and {Work-order-3} cannot be dropped on Unassigned. Both stay in {Technician-A}'s column, no lead-change message appears and {Technician-A}'s count is unchanged.
- Step 8: {Work-order-6} cannot be moved out of Unassigned. It stays there.
- Step 9: {Work-order-4} (Declined) and {Work-order-5} (Complete) move to {Technician-B}'s column with "Lead technician updated".
- Step 11: the same rule holds in Tech View. {Work-order-3} stays in {Technician-A}'s group.

Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
- S9-N1: “Apply S4-N1 and S4-N2’s permission and status restrictions to every work order drag.”
- S4-N2: “Do not allow the lead technician to change in Invoiced or Paid status. An Invoiced or Paid work order can still be dragged to reorder it within its current technician (or within Unassigned), but it cannot be moved to another technician, to Unassigned, or out of Unassigned. Declined and Complete work orders can be moved freely, as today.”
- S4-N7: “Visually indicate that an Invoiced or Paid work order can only be reordered within its current technician, not moved to another one.”
- Tech plan 3.23 lead lock vs reorder: “In a locked status a work order can be dragged within its current technician (or within Unassigned if it has no lead); it cannot be dropped on another technician, and cannot move between a technician and Unassigned.”
