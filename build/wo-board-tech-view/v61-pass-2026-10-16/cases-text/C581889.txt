TITLE: Board View and Tech View show the filter message when no work order matches

=== PRECONDITIONS ===
Setup (manual QA tester and Claude session)


Preconditions
- You are signed in as an Owner or Admin user whose role has Work orders > View, at {Location-A} (for example Staging Heavy Duty - 9919).
- Your browser window is at least 1024 px wide, so the display switcher (List, Tech View, Board View) shows at the right of the Work Orders toolbar.
- Approved work order {Work-order-1} (for example S10043-17590) for customer {Customer-A} (for example "ZZAUTOTEST NT1 Filtered Empty") exists at {Location-A}, and it is {Customer-A}'s only work order.
- No work order at {Location-A} is in status {Status-A} (for example Paid). Pick a status other than Imported and Approved.
- On the Work Orders page nothing is ticked in Status, Asset on Site shows no choice (if its chip shows Yes or No, click the x on the chip), Assigned to me is off and Search is empty.

=== STEPS ===
- Open Work Orders and click the All tab.
- Click Board View in the display switcher.
- Click Status and tick {Status-A}.
- Click outside the Status list to close it.
- Read the board.
- Type the name of {Customer-A} in Search.
- Read the board.
- Click Tech View in the display switcher.
- Read the page.
- Click List in the display switcher.
- Read the page.
- Click Tech View in the display switcher.
- Click Clear all filters.
- Read the page.

=== EXPECTED ===
Expected results
- Steps 5 and 7: instead of work order cards, the board shows "No work orders match these filters", then "Try removing a filter to widen your results.", with a Clear all filters button, both without and with the search text.
- Step 9: Tech View shows the same two lines of text and the Clear all filters button.
- Step 11: the List shows the same empty state, with the same two lines of text and the same button.
- Steps 13 and 14: {Status-A} is no longer ticked in Status and {Work-order-1} shows again in Tech View.
Source — where this behaviour comes from
Epic SV-10043; story SV-10044 (Story 1, Switch between display options); PRD "Work Orders — Board View & Tech View Display Options", Confluence 845185030, version 48 (12 Oct 2026, Chris Ward), S1-N1 (reworded) and S1-N1a (added) in version 43 (8 Oct 2026, Chris Ward, after his Slack answer of 8 Oct that the earlier wording was out of date), and the User Feedback Summary row "No work orders match filters", read 14 Oct 2026. Replaces the removed C96918. Design export of 14 Oct differs (no button in Tech View and Board View; the search message when a filter and a search are both on); the PRD wins and the difference is with the PO and design.
