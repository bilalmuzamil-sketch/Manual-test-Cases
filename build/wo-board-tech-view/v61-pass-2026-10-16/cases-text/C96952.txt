TITLE: A deactivated technician's column keeps its work and pin and can be unpinned

=== PRECONDITIONS ===
Preconditions
- Needs: 1 user · 1 browser · about 15 minutes
- Build: the Work Orders QA build (sv10043, v26.40.8-7a95011). A desktop browser with the window at least 1024 pixels wide (full screen on an ordinary laptop or monitor). Below that width the page shows only List.
- [User-A]: an Owner/Admin user (on a QA branch: the Admin quick-login button on the sign-in page; you appear as "Admin ShopView"). The Admin role holds Work orders > View and Work orders > Create & Edit (to check: your initials at the top right > Settings > Roles & Permissions > Admin > Work orders).
- One location for the whole case, shown in the top bar next to the bell (e.g. "Staging Heavy Duty - 9919"); everything below is created in this location.
- Technician "Ralph Edwards" at this location, active (the expected results name him, so keep this name), and [Tech-B] (e.g. "Esther Howard"). A person only counts as a technician here when the staff member is active (not deactivated), Time Clock is on, the Role is neither Office User nor Time Clock User, and this location is picked in their Location field.
- Customer "ZZAUTOTEST F2 Inactive Pinned" (used by this case only), with one contact and one asset, so this case's work orders are kept apart from every other work order at the location.
- Two Approved work orders for that customer, [WO-1] and [WO-2], both led by Ralph Edwards.
- Ralph Edwards is pinned in Board View by [User-A]; no one else is pinned.
- What must hold: Ralph Edwards is active at the start; the case deactivates him in step 1 and he is set active again afterwards.
Setup
- Sign in with the Admin quick-login button on the sign-in page (you appear as "Admin ShopView"). Check the location in the top bar next to the bell; to change it click your initials at the top right > Change Location.
- Settings (your initials at the top right > Settings) > Staff: confirm each technician below is active at this location with Time Clock on, or add it: New Staff Member > First Name and Last Name, an Email, Role "Technician", at least one Department (required), Location = this location, Time Clock on > Save & Close. Record them as "Ralph Edwards" (keep this name, the expected results use it); [Tech-B] (e.g. Esther Howard).
- Top menu Customers > New Customer > Name "ZZAUTOTEST F2 Inactive Pinned" > Save (if it already exists, open it instead). On the customer page click the Contacts tab > New Contact > First Name and Last Name > Save (a new asset needs a contact). Then click the Assets tab > New Asset > pick the Contact, then Year, Make, Model and Unit (e.g. 2022, Freightliner, M2, TRK-118) > Save. Make is required; Unit can be left empty.
- Work Orders > Create Work Order > in the New Work Order window pick Customer "ZZAUTOTEST F2 Inactive Pinned" and its asset > Save. The work order opens on its Lines tab with the New Line form open: in "What Are You Doing?" pick a ready-made line (e.g. "Replace - Brake pot"; the build offers only ready-made lines), turn Line Approved on > Save & Close; the work order becomes Approved. Repeat for two work orders in all; record the numbers as [WO-1] (the expected results call it "S1-691") and [WO-2] (the expected results call it "S1-578") (numbers on this site look like "S10043-17581").
- Set the leads: on an Approved work order pick the technician in the Lead Technician dropdown on the work order page; it saves at once. (While a work order is an Estimate its Lead technician is read-only on the work order page; give an Estimate its lead from Board View instead: the card's More actions (…) > Reassign lead technician > pick the technician > Reassign.) Leave Lead Technician empty on the ones that should sit in Unassigned. Here: [WO-1] and [WO-2] = Ralph Edwards.
- Pin Ralph Edwards: Work Orders > Board View > pin control on his column header.
- Check the setup worked: Work Orders > All tab > Search ZZAUTOTEST F2 Inactive Pinned > Board View — Ralph Edwards' column is right after Unassigned with a filled pin and holds [WO-1] and [WO-2]. If not, fix the leads or pin before starting.

=== STEPS ===
- Deactivate Ralph Edwards: Settings > Staff > Ralph Edwards' edit icon > click Deactivate Account and confirm.
- Click Work Orders in the top menu.
- Click the All tab on the left of the toolbar, click Search and type ZZAUTOTEST F2 Inactive Pinned.
- In the display switcher at the right of the toolbar, click Board View (tooltip "Board View").
- Read Ralph Edwards' column.
- Click the pin control on his column to unpin him.
- Read the column order.

=== EXPECTED ===
Expected results
- Ralph Edwards' column is still shown, still pinned right after Unassigned, with S1-691 and S1-578 (count 2).
- The column shows an inactive indicator. Write down how it is shown.
- He can be unpinned: after unpinning, his column leaves the pinned area.
- Afterwards, set Ralph Edwards active again to restore the data.
