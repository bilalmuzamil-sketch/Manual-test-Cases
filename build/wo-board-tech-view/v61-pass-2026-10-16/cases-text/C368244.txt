TITLE: A new photo shows in Board View after a reload

=== PRECONDITIONS ===
Preconditions
- Needs: 2 users · 2 browsers · about 15 minutes
- Build: the Work Orders QA build (sv10043, v26.40.8-7a95011), desktop browser, window at least 1024 px wide.
- Role: Owner/Admin. On the sign-in page click the Admin quick-login button (you appear as Admin ShopView). The Admin role has Work orders > View and Create & Edit (check: your initials (top right) > Settings > Roles & Permissions > Admin > edit).
- Location: Staging Heavy Duty - 9919 for the whole case (shown next to the bell; change it with your initials > Change Location).
- Two clearly different photos on your computer (e.g. a red square and a blue square).
- One technician [Tech-A] at this location (Role Technician, Departments = Service, Time Clock on) who can sign in with her own email and password. Name is an example ("Esther Howard").
- [Tech-A] leads at least one work order at this location (for her Board View column).
Setup
- Your initials (top right) > Settings > Staff > New Staff Member: First Name, Last Name, Email > Role = Technician > Departments = Service (only technicians in the Service department show as rows on the Schedule) > Location = Staging Heavy Duty - 9919 > turn Time Clock on > Save & Close. Record the technician as [Tech-A] (the expected results call this technician "Esther Howard"). Give her an email you can open; she accepts the invitation and sets a password.
- Customers > New Customer > Name "ZZAUTOTEST Photo Board" > Save (the customer page opens). Contacts tab > New Contact > First Name "ZZAUTOTEST", Last Name "Contact" > Save. Assets tab > New Asset > Contact = ZZAUTOTEST Contact > Year 2022 > Make: type "Freight" and pick Freightliner > Model: type "M2" and pick it > Unit "TRK-118" > Save (Contact and Make are required).
- Work Orders > Create Work Order > New Work Order: Customer = "ZZAUTOTEST Photo Board" > Asset = its asset > Save. If a Confirmation says the customer is over their credit limit, click Create. The work order opens on its Lines tab with the New Line form open. Record the number shown at the top left as [WO-1].
- In New Line > What Are You Doing? type "grease" and pick the ready-made line "Service - Full grease service" (it has no parts) > turn Line Approved on > Save & Close.
- On [WO-1], on the status card at the left, click Lead Technician and pick [Tech-A]. (Lead Technician is a dropdown only once the work order is Approved; while it is an Estimate it reads "Unassigned" and cannot be changed.) The choice saves at once.
- As [Tech-A], upload the red photo (the person signs in, clicks their initials at the top right > Edit Profile, clicks "Click to upload a profile photo" and picks the file; the picture shows at once).
- Check the setup worked: as [Tech-A], the top bar shows the red photo.

=== STEPS ===
- Open Work Orders, pick Board View in the display switcher (tooltip "Board View") and look at the avatar in [Tech-A]'s column header (scroll right to her column if needed).
- Upload the blue photo as [Tech-A]'s photo (the person signs in, clicks their initials at the top right > Edit Profile, clicks "Click to upload a profile photo" and picks the file; the picture shows at once).
- Go back to the same place and reload the page.
- Look at the avatar again.

=== EXPECTED ===
Expected results
- Before the upload the avatar shows the red photo.
- After the reload it shows the blue photo, not the old red one.
