Chris Ward answered on 15 Oct 2026 (answer 27) that row and card text is 14 px at every density and Compact never shrinks it, while text the design sets at 12 px stays 12 px, and (answer 18) that no hours line shows at any density. PRD version 54 says so, and this case follows it.
