TITLE: Column headers stay at the top while scrolling down a long column

=== PRECONDITIONS ===
Preconditions
- Needs: 1 user · 1 browser · about 30 minutes
- Build: the Work Orders QA build (sv10043, v26.40.8-7a95011). A desktop browser with the window at least 1024 pixels wide (full screen on an ordinary laptop or monitor). Below that width the page shows only List.
- [User-A]: an Owner/Admin user (on a QA branch: the Admin quick-login button on the sign-in page; you appear as "Admin ShopView"). The Admin role holds Work orders > View and Work orders > Create & Edit (to check: your initials at the top right > Settings > Roles & Permissions > Admin > Work orders).
- One location for the whole case, shown in the top bar next to the bell (e.g. "Staging Heavy Duty - 9919"); everything below is created in this location.
- Two technicians at this location: "Esther Howard" (the expected results name her, so keep this name) and [Tech-B] (e.g. "Ralph Edwards"). A person only counts as a technician here when the staff member is active (not deactivated), Time Clock is on, the Role is neither Office User nor Time Clock User, and this location is picked in their Location field.
- Customer "ZZAUTOTEST F2 Sticky Column Header" (used by this case only), with one contact and one asset, so this case's work orders are kept apart from every other work order at the location.
- About 20 Approved work orders for that customer led by Esther Howard (more cards than fit on the screen) and one led by [Tech-B].
- What must hold: Esther Howard's column is taller than the screen.
Setup
- Sign in with the Admin quick-login button on the sign-in page (you appear as "Admin ShopView"). Check the location in the top bar next to the bell; to change it click your initials at the top right > Change Location.
- Settings (your initials at the top right > Settings) > Staff: confirm each technician below is active at this location with Time Clock on, or add it: New Staff Member > First Name and Last Name, an Email, Role "Technician", at least one Department (required), Location = this location, Time Clock on > Save & Close. Record them as "Esther Howard" (keep this name, the expected results use it); [Tech-B] (e.g. Ralph Edwards).
- Top menu Customers > New Customer > Name "ZZAUTOTEST F2 Sticky Column Header" > Save (if it already exists, open it instead). On the customer page click the Contacts tab > New Contact > First Name and Last Name > Save (a new asset needs a contact). Then click the Assets tab > New Asset > pick the Contact, then Year, Make, Model and Unit (e.g. 2022, Freightliner, M2, TRK-118) > Save. Make is required; Unit can be left empty.
- Work Orders > Create Work Order > in the New Work Order window pick Customer "ZZAUTOTEST F2 Sticky Column Header" and its asset > Save. The work order opens on its Lines tab with the New Line form open: in "What Are You Doing?" pick a ready-made line (e.g. "Replace - Brake pot"; the build offers only ready-made lines), turn Line Approved on > Save & Close; the work order becomes Approved. Repeat for about 21 work orders in all (numbers on this site look like "S10043-17581").
- Set the leads: on an Approved work order pick the technician in the Lead Technician dropdown on the work order page; it saves at once. (While a work order is an Estimate its Lead technician is read-only on the work order page; give an Estimate its lead from Board View instead: the card's More actions (…) > Reassign lead technician > pick the technician > Reassign.) Leave Lead Technician empty on the ones that should sit in Unassigned. Here: about 20 = Esther Howard; one = [Tech-B].
- Check the setup worked: Work Orders > All tab > Search ZZAUTOTEST F2 Sticky Column Header > Board View — Esther Howard's column does not fit on the screen. If it does, add more work orders led by her.

=== STEPS ===
- Open Work Orders.
- Click the All tab on the left of the toolbar.
- Click Search and type ZZAUTOTEST F2 Sticky Column Header so only this test's work orders show.
- In the display switcher at the right of the toolbar, click Board View (tooltip "Board View").
- Scroll down inside Esther Howard's column to her last card.
- Look at the top of her column and the other headers.

=== EXPECTED ===
Expected results
- Esther Howard's header (name and count) stays at the top of her column the whole time.
- The other column headers also stay in view: you can always see whose column you are in.
