TITLE: After a status change, Back shows the new status in the List

=== PRECONDITIONS ===
Preconditions
- Needs: 1 user · 1 browser · about 10 minutes
- Build: the Work Orders QA build (sv10043, v26.40.8-7a95011), desktop browser, window at least 1024 px wide.
- Role: Owner/Admin. On the sign-in page click the Admin quick-login button (you appear as Admin ShopView). The Admin role has Work orders > View and Create & Edit (check: your initials (top right) > Settings > Roles & Permissions > Admin > edit).
- Location: Staging Heavy Duty - 9919 for the whole case (shown next to the bell; change it with your initials > Change Location).
- View: on Work Orders the List is shown (click List in the display switcher at the right of the toolbar; its tooltip reads "List").
- Customer "ZZAUTOTEST Back Status" (used by this case only) with one contact, one asset and one Approved work order [WO-1] with one approved line.
Setup
- Customers > New Customer > Name "ZZAUTOTEST Back Status" > Save (the customer page opens). Contacts tab > New Contact > First Name "ZZAUTOTEST", Last Name "Contact" > Save. Assets tab > New Asset > Contact = ZZAUTOTEST Contact > Year 2022 > Make: type "Freight" and pick Freightliner > Model: type "M2" and pick it > Unit "TRK-118" > Save (Contact and Make are required).
- Work Orders > Create Work Order > New Work Order: Customer = "ZZAUTOTEST Back Status" > Asset = its asset > Save. If a Confirmation says the customer is over their credit limit, click Create. The work order opens on its Lines tab with the New Line form open. Record the number shown at the top left as [WO-1] (the expected results call it "S1-793").
- In New Line > What Are You Doing? type "grease" and pick the ready-made line "Service - Full grease service" (it has no parts) > turn Line Approved on > Save & Close.
- Check the setup worked: All tab > Search "ZZAUTOTEST Back Status" lists [WO-1] as Approved.

=== STEPS ===
- Open Work Orders, click the All tab, click Search and type "ZZAUTOTEST Back Status".
- Click the [WO-1] row to open it.
- Set it to Ready for Review: on the Lines tab click Complete on the line > type any Tech Story > if "Missing Details (1)" shows, click it and type a Mileage > Complete Line / Complete All Lines. The message "Work order sent to review." appears and the status reads Review.
- Press the browser Back button.
- Read the [WO-1] row's status.

=== EXPECTED ===
Expected results
- The row reads Review straight away, with no manual reload.
