Setup
- Sign in with the Admin quick-login button on the sign-in page (you appear as "Admin ShopView"). Check the location in the top bar next to the bell; to change it click your initials at the top right > Change Location.
- Settings (your initials at the top right > Settings) > Staff: confirm each technician below is active at this location with Time Clock on, or add it: New Staff Member > First Name and Last Name, an Email, Role "Technician", at least one Department (required), Location = this location, Time Clock on > Save & Close. Record them as [Tech-A] (e.g. Esther Howard).
- Top menu Customers > New Customer > Name "ZZAUTOTEST F2 Required Only" > Save (if it already exists, open it instead). On the customer page click the Contacts tab > New Contact > First Name and Last Name > Save (a new asset needs a contact). Then click the Assets tab > New Asset > pick the Contact, then Year, Make, Model and Unit 2022, Freightliner, M2, TRK-118 > Save. Make is required; Unit can be left empty. Add a second asset: Year 1999, Make Ford, Model Explorer, Unit left empty > Save.
- Work Orders > Create Work Order > in the New Work Order window pick Customer "ZZAUTOTEST F2 Required Only" and its asset > Save. The work order opens on its Lines tab with the New Line form open: in "What Are You Doing?" pick a ready-made line (e.g. "Replace - Brake pot"; the build offers only ready-made lines), turn Line Approved on > Save & Close; the work order becomes Approved. Repeat for two work orders in all: [WO-1] with the TRK-118 asset (the expected results call it "S1-702") and [WO-2] with the 1999 Ford Explorer (the expected results call it "S1-472") (numbers on this site look like "S10043-17581").
- Set the leads: on an Approved work order pick the technician in the Lead Technician dropdown on the work order page; it saves at once. (While a work order is an Estimate its Lead technician is read-only on the work order page; give an Estimate its lead from Board View instead: the card's More actions (…) > Reassign lead technician > pick the technician > Reassign.) Leave Lead Technician empty on the ones that should sit in Unassigned. Here: [WO-1] and [WO-2] = [Tech-A].
- Check the setup worked: Work Orders > All tab > Search ZZAUTOTEST F2 Required Only — [WO-1] (unit TRK-118) and [WO-2] (1999 Ford Explorer, no unit) show, both Approved and led by [Tech-A]. If not, fix them before starting.
