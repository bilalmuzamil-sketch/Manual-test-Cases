TITLE: A lead changed elsewhere moves the work order to its new group on refresh

=== PRECONDITIONS ===
Preconditions
- Needs: 1 user · 1 browser · 2 browser tabs · about 15 minutes
- Build: the Work Orders QA build (sv10043, v26.40.8-7a95011). A desktop browser with the window at least 1024 pixels wide (full screen on an ordinary laptop or monitor). Below that width the page shows only List.
- [User-A]: an Owner/Admin user (on a QA branch: the Admin quick-login button on the sign-in page; you appear as "Admin ShopView"). The Admin role holds Work orders > View and Work orders > Create & Edit (to check: your initials at the top right > Settings > Roles & Permissions > Admin > Work orders).
- One location for the whole case, shown in the top bar next to the bell (e.g. "Staging Heavy Duty - 9919"); everything below is created in this location.
- Technicians "Esther Howard" and "Ralph Edwards" at this location (the expected results name them, so keep these names). A person only counts as a technician here when the staff member is active (not deactivated), Time Clock is on, the Role is neither Office User nor Time Clock User, and this location is picked in their Location field.
- Customer "ZZAUTOTEST F2 Refresh Lead" (used by this case only), with one contact and one asset, so this case's work orders are kept apart from every other work order at the location.
- Five Approved work orders for that customer: [WO-1], [WO-2], [WO-3] led by Esther Howard (3) and [WO-4], [WO-5] led by Ralph Edwards (2).
- Numbers are examples.
Setup
- Sign in with the Admin quick-login button on the sign-in page (you appear as "Admin ShopView"). Check the location in the top bar next to the bell; to change it click your initials at the top right > Change Location.
- Settings (your initials at the top right > Settings) > Staff: confirm each technician below is active at this location with Time Clock on, or add it: New Staff Member > First Name and Last Name, an Email, Role "Technician", at least one Department (required), Location = this location, Time Clock on > Save & Close. Record them as "Esther Howard" and "Ralph Edwards" (keep these names).
- Top menu Customers > New Customer > Name "ZZAUTOTEST F2 Refresh Lead" > Save (if it already exists, open it instead). On the customer page click the Contacts tab > New Contact > First Name and Last Name > Save (a new asset needs a contact). Then click the Assets tab > New Asset > pick the Contact, then Year, Make, Model and Unit (e.g. 2022, Freightliner, M2, TRK-118) > Save. Make is required; Unit can be left empty.
- Work Orders > Create Work Order > in the New Work Order window pick Customer "ZZAUTOTEST F2 Refresh Lead" and its asset > Save. The work order opens on its Lines tab with the New Line form open: in "What Are You Doing?" pick a ready-made line (e.g. "Replace - Brake pot"; the build offers only ready-made lines), turn Line Approved on > Save & Close; the work order becomes Approved. Repeat for five work orders in all; record the numbers as [WO-1] (the expected results call it "S1-702"), [WO-2] (e.g. S1-644), [WO-3] (e.g. S1-455), [WO-4] (e.g. S1-691) and [WO-5] (e.g. S1-578) (numbers on this site look like "S10043-17581").
- Set the leads: on an Approved work order pick the technician in the Lead Technician dropdown on the work order page; it saves at once. (While a work order is an Estimate its Lead technician is read-only on the work order page; give an Estimate its lead from Board View instead: the card's More actions (…) > Reassign lead technician > pick the technician > Reassign.) Leave Lead Technician empty on the ones that should sit in Unassigned. Here: [WO-1], [WO-2], [WO-3] = Esther Howard; [WO-4], [WO-5] = Ralph Edwards.
- Check the setup worked: Work Orders > All tab > Search ZZAUTOTEST F2 Refresh Lead > Tech View — Esther Howard shows 3 and Ralph Edwards shows 2. If not, fix the leads before starting.

=== STEPS ===
- Open Work Orders in browser tab A.
- In tab A, click the All tab.
- In tab A, click Search and type ZZAUTOTEST F2 Refresh Lead so only this test's work orders show.
- In tab A, in the display switcher at the right of the toolbar, click Tech View (tooltip "Tech View").
- In browser tab B, open work order [WO-1] and change its Lead Technician to Ralph Edwards on the work order page. Save.
- Go back to tab A without refreshing and look at [WO-1].
- Refresh tab A.
- Look at [WO-1] and the two group counts.

=== EXPECTED ===
Expected results
- Before the refresh, tab A may still show S1-702 under Esther Howard.
- After the refresh, S1-702 is under Ralph Edwards. Esther Howard shows 2 (3 - 1) and Ralph Edwards shows 3 (2 + 1).
