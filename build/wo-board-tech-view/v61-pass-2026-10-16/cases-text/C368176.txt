TITLE: Clocking in on an unassigned line gives the line to that technician

=== PRECONDITIONS ===
Preconditions
- Needs: 2 users · 2 browsers · about 10 minutes
- Build: the Work Orders QA build (sv10043, v26.40.8-7a95011), desktop browser, window at least 1024 px wide.
- Role: Owner/Admin. On the sign-in page click the Admin quick-login button (you appear as Admin ShopView). The Admin role has Work orders > View and Create & Edit (check: your initials (top right) > Settings > Roles & Permissions > Admin > edit).
- Location: Staging Heavy Duty - 9919 for the whole case (shown next to the bell; change it with your initials > Change Location).
- 1 technicians eligible at this location: active, Role Technician, at least one Department, Location Staging Heavy Duty - 9919, Time Clock on. Names are examples ("Esther Howard"). [Tech-A] must be able to sign in with her own email and password.
- Customer "ZZAUTOTEST Clock In Line" (used by this case only) with one contact and one asset.
- One Approved work order [WO-1] with NO lead technician and one approved line with no technician (its Labor row reads "Unassigned").
- A second browser (or a private window) for [Tech-A].
- Names and numbers are examples.
Setup
- Your initials (top right) > Settings > Staff: if no suitable technician exists, click New Staff Member > First Name, Last Name, Email > Role = Technician > Departments = any one (at least one Department is required) > Location = Staging Heavy Duty - 9919 > turn Time Clock on > Save & Close. Record the technician as [Tech-A] (the expected results call this technician "Esther Howard"). Give her a real email you can open; she accepts the invitation from that email and sets a password (or use an existing technician who can sign in).
- Customers > New Customer > Name "ZZAUTOTEST Clock In Line" > Save (the customer page opens). Contacts tab > New Contact > First Name "ZZAUTOTEST", Last Name "Contact" > Save. Assets tab > New Asset > Contact = ZZAUTOTEST Contact > Year 2022 > Make: type "Freight" and pick Freightliner > Model: type "M2" and pick it > Unit "TRK-118" > Save (Contact and Make are required).
- Work Orders > Create Work Order > New Work Order: Customer = "ZZAUTOTEST Clock In Line" > Asset = its asset > Save. If a Confirmation says the customer is over their credit limit, click Create. The work order opens on its Lines tab with the New Line form open. Record the number shown at the top left as [WO-1] (the expected results call it "S1-730").
- In New Line > What Are You Doing? type "grease" and pick the ready-made line "Service - Full grease service" (it has no parts) > turn Line Approved on > Save & Close. Leave Lead Technician empty.
- Check the setup worked: reload [WO-1] > Lines tab. The status reads Approved, Lead Technician is empty and the line's Labor row reads "Unassigned" with a Start button.

=== STEPS ===
- In browser 1, open [WO-1], click the Lines tab and read the line's Labor row.
- In browser 2, sign in as [Tech-A].
- Open [WO-1] and click the Lines tab.
- Click Start on the line's Labor row (this starts [Tech-A]'s time clock on the line; the Labor row then shows a Stop button).
- Wait at least 1 minute, then click Stop on the Labor row. In the "Stop working on …" window type any text in What Have You Been Doing On This Line? and click Clock Out.
- In browser 1, reload the page and read the line's Labor row.

=== EXPECTED ===
Expected results
- Step 1: the Labor row reads "Unassigned".
- Step 6: the Labor row now shows "Esther Howard". The line took the technician who clocked in.
