TITLE: An asset's Work Orders tab count matches its rows

=== PRECONDITIONS ===
Preconditions
- Needs: 1 user · 1 browser · a production sign-in for the comparison · about 20 minutes.
- Sign in as an Admin (on a QA branch: the Admin quick-login button on the sign-in page; you appear as "Admin ShopView"). Desktop browser window at least 1024 px wide; narrower windows show only the List.
- Work in one location throughout, shown next to the bell in the top bar (e.g. "Staging Heavy Duty - 9919"); to change it click your initials (top right) > Change Location. Create everything in that location. (Asset tabs list only this location's work orders.)
- Customer "ZZAUTOTEST Asset Tab Count" (this case only) with two assets, units TRK-201 and TRK-202.
- On TRK-201: [WO-A] Estimate with one line; [WO-B] Approved with two lines, only line 1 completed; [WO-C] Complete. On TRK-202: [WO-D] Approved.
- For steps 4 to 6: a sign-in to production (app.shopview.com) that can open Customers. You only look there; nothing is changed.
- Names and numbers are examples, except names the expected results quote, which must be used exactly.
Setup
- Customer: Customers > New Customer > Name "ZZAUTOTEST Asset Tab Count" > Save. Contacts tab > New Contact > First and Last Name > Save. Assets tab > New Asset > pick the contact, Year, Make (required), Model, Unit "TRK-201" > Save. Add a second asset the same way with Unit "TRK-202".
- For each work order: Work Orders > Create Work Order > Customer "ZZAUTOTEST Asset Tab Count" > the asset named below > Save (if a credit-limit Confirmation appears, click Create). In New Line > "What Are You Doing?" type "grease" and pick "Service - Full grease service" (no parts). Write down the number (top left).- [WO-A] ("S1-750"), TRK-201: leave Line Approved off > Save & Close. Status: Estimate.
- [WO-B] ("S1-751"), TRK-201: Line Approved on > Save & Add Line; then type "clutch", pick "Service - Adjust clutch", Line Approved on > Save & Close. On line 1 click Complete > any Tech Story > Complete Line ("1 line completed."). Leave line 2 open. Status: Approved.
- [WO-C] ("S1-752"), TRK-201: Line Approved on > Save & Close. Bring to Complete: Lines tab > Complete on the line > type any Tech Story (if "Missing Details" shows, fill it, e.g. a Mileage) > Complete Line; the status reads Review. Click Mark Reviewed (if asked for a VIN, type any 17 characters, e.g. 1FVACWDT1BDAZ0001) > Confirm Review; the status reads Complete.
- [WO-D] ("S1-753"), TRK-202: Line Approved on > Save & Close.

- Check: Work Orders > All tab > Search "ZZAUTOTEST Asset Tab Count" lists exactly [WO-A] Estimate, [WO-B] Approved, [WO-C] Complete, [WO-D] Approved.

=== STEPS ===
- Click Customers in the top menu, open "ZZAUTOTEST Asset Tab Count", click its Assets tab, click the TRK-201 row (the asset page opens) and click its Work Orders tab.
- Read the count shown for the list, if there is one (on the tab label, e.g. "Work Orders (3)", or above or under the table, e.g. "1-3 of 3"). Write down the count, or "no count".
- Count the rows in the table.
- In another browser tab sign in to production (app.shopview.com) and click Customers in the top menu.
- Open any customer, click its Assets tab and open an asset that has work orders.
- Click the asset's Work Orders tab and write down whether a count shows (on the tab label, or above or under the table).

- Mark Failed if a count shows and differs from the number of rows, or if production shows a count and this build does not.
