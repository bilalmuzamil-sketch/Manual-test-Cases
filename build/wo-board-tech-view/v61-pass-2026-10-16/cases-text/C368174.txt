TITLE: Assigning a technician to lines from the Lines tab still works

- Build: the Work Orders QA build (sv10043, v26.40.8-7a95011), desktop browser, window at least 1024 px wide.
- Role: Owner/Admin. On the sign-in page click the Admin quick-login button (you appear as Admin ShopView). The Admin role has Work orders > View and Create & Edit (check: your initials (top right) > Settings > Roles & Permissions > Admin > edit).
- Location: Staging Heavy Duty - 9919 for the whole case (shown next to the bell; change it with your initials > Change Location).
- 1 technicians eligible at this location: active, Role Technician, at least one Department, Location Staging Heavy Duty - 9919, Time Clock on. Names are examples ("Ralph Edwards").
- Customer "ZZAUTOTEST Assign Lines Tech" (used by this case only) with one contact and one asset.
- One Approved work order [WO-1] with no lead technician and two approved lines with no technician (Labor rows read "Unassigned").
- Names and numbers are examples.
Setup
- Your initials (top right) > Settings > Staff: if no suitable technician exists, click New Staff Member > First Name, Last Name, Email > Role = Technician > Departments = any one (at least one Department is required) > Location = Staging Heavy Duty - 9919 > turn Time Clock on > Save & Close. Record the technician as [Tech-B] (the expected results call this technician "Ralph Edwards").
- Customers > New Customer > Name "ZZAUTOTEST Assign Lines Tech" > Save (the customer page opens). Contacts tab > New Contact > First Name "ZZAUTOTEST", Last Name "Contact" > Save. Assets tab > New Asset > Contact = ZZAUTOTEST Contact > Year 2022 > Make: type "Freight" and pick Freightliner > Model: type "M2" and pick it > Unit "TRK-118" > Save (Contact and Make are required).
- Work Orders > Create Work Order > New Work Order: Customer = "ZZAUTOTEST Assign Lines Tech" > Asset = its asset > Save. If a Confirmation says the customer is over their credit limit, click Create. The work order opens on its Lines tab with the New Line form open. Record the number shown at the top left as [WO-1] (the expected results call it "S1-724").
- In New Line > What Are You Doing? type "grease" and pick the ready-made line "Service - Full grease service" (it has no parts) > turn Line Approved on > Save & Add Line. In New Line > What Are You Doing? type "clutch" and pick the ready-made line "Service - Adjust clutch" (it has no parts) > turn Line Approved on > Save & Close. Leave Lead Technician empty.
- Check the setup worked: reload [WO-1] > Lines tab. Both lines' Labor rows read "Unassigned". Line 1 = the line numbered 1, Line 2 = the line numbered 2.

- Open [WO-1] and click the Lines tab.

- Reload the page and read both lines' technicians (their Labor rows).
