TITLE: The lead of an Invoiced or Paid work order cannot be changed in any way

=== PRECONDITIONS ===
Setup (manual QA tester and Claude session)

Preconditions
- You are signed in as an Owner or Admin user whose role has Work orders > View and Work orders > Create & Edit, at {Location-A} (for example Staging Heavy Duty - 9919).
- Your browser window is at least 1024 px wide, so the display switcher (List, Tech View, Board View) shows at the right of the Work Orders toolbar.
- Technician {Technician-A} (for example Esther Howard) is an active staff member at {Location-A} with Time Clock on and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User.
- Technician {Technician-B} (for example Ralph Edwards) is an active staff member at {Location-A} with Time Clock on and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User.
- Customer {Customer-A} (for example "ZZAUTOTEST F1 Invoiced Paid Locked") is used by this case only and has six work orders whose Lead Technician was set while they were still open (for example S10043-17801 to S10043-17806): {Work-order-1} Approved, led by {Technician-A}; {Work-order-2} Invoiced, led by {Technician-A}; {Work-order-3} Paid, led by {Technician-A}; {Work-order-4} Invoiced, with no lead technician; {Work-order-5} Declined, led by {Technician-A}; {Work-order-6} Complete, led by {Technician-A}.
- No technician has a shift on the Schedule for these work orders.
- On the Work Orders page nothing is ticked in Status, Asset on Site shows no choice (if its chip shows Yes or No, click the x on the chip), Assigned to me is off and Search is empty.

=== STEPS ===
- Open Work Orders, click the All tab and click Board View in the display switcher.
- Type the name of {Customer-A} in Search.
- Drag {Work-order-2}'s card from {Technician-A}'s column into {Technician-B}'s column.
- Drag {Work-order-2}'s card into the Unassigned column.
- Drag {Work-order-2}'s card within {Technician-A}'s column to above {Work-order-1}.
- Drag {Work-order-3}'s card into {Technician-B}'s column, then into the Unassigned column, then within {Technician-A}'s column to above {Work-order-1}.
- Drag {Work-order-4}'s card into {Technician-A}'s column, then drag it to a new place within the Unassigned column.
- Drag {Work-order-5}'s card into {Technician-B}'s column (click Keep shifts if asked).
- Drag {Work-order-6}'s card into {Technician-B}'s column (click Keep shifts if asked).
- Open {Work-order-2} and try to change Lead Technician on the work order page.
- Go back, click Tech View in the display switcher and do steps 3 to 7 again with the rows, then drag {Work-order-5} and {Work-order-6} from {Technician-B}'s group back into {Technician-A}'s group.

=== EXPECTED ===
Expected results
- Steps 3, 4 and 6: {Work-order-2} and {Work-order-3} cannot be moved to {Technician-B} or to Unassigned: the card goes back to {Technician-A}'s column and the lead stays {Technician-A}.
- Steps 5 and 6: {Work-order-2} and {Work-order-3} can be moved within {Technician-A}'s column and keep their new place.
- Step 7: {Work-order-4} cannot be moved out of Unassigned, but it can be reordered inside it.
- Steps 8 and 9: {Work-order-5} (Declined) and {Work-order-6} (Complete) move to {Technician-B} normally, with "Lead technician updated".
- Step 10: on the work order page the Lead Technician of {Work-order-2} cannot be changed either.
- Step 11: Tech View follows the same rules for every move. A change sent outside the screens is also refused by the server; this part cannot be checked by hand, so write "server refusal not checked by hand" in the result comment and pass or fail on what you can see. Imported work orders are checked in their own case.

Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
- S4-R10: “Enforce the prohibited-status rule in S4-N2 on every lead-change path, including requests that bypass interface controls.”
- S4-N2: “Do not allow the lead technician to change in Invoiced or Paid status. An Invoiced or Paid work order can still be dragged to reorder it within its current technician (or within Unassigned), but it cannot be moved to another technician, to Unassigned, or out of Unassigned. Declined and Complete work orders can be moved freely, as today.”
- Key decision: lead locked once Invoiced or Paid: “Lead technician cannot be changed once a work order is Invoiced or Paid. This is a product rule, not only a screen rule, so it must hold on every path that changes the lead technician — board drag, Reassign dialog, and the work order detail page.”
