TITLE: A lead change leaves status and recorded time alone

=== PRECONDITIONS ===
Preconditions
- Needs: 2 users · 2 browsers · Schedule access · about 30 minutes.
- Build: the Work Orders QA build (sv10043, v26.40.8-7a95011).
- [User-A] = you: an Owner/Admin user (on a QA branch the Admin quick-login user, shown as "Admin ShopView"). The Admin role holds Work orders > View and Work orders > Create & Edit.
- [Tech-A] can also sign in (a staff record with an email you can open and an accepted invite), in a second browser.
- Desktop browser window at least 1024 px wide. Narrower windows show only the List, without the display switcher.
- One location for the whole case, shown in the top bar next to the bell (e.g. "Staging Heavy Duty - 9919"); everything below is created in this location.
- Three eligible technicians at this location: [Tech-A] "Esther Howard", [Tech-B] (e.g. "Ralph Edwards"), [Tech-C] (e.g. "Dana Ortiz"). A person only counts as a technician here when the staff member is active (not deactivated), Time Clock is on, the Role is neither Office User nor Time Clock User, and this location is picked in their Location field.
- Customer "ZZAUTOTEST F1 Status And Time Kept" (used by this case only) with one work order [WO-1] led by [Tech-A], status In Progress: its line has logged labor by [Tech-A] and [Tech-A]'s time clock is running on it.
- A shift for [Tech-A] on [WO-1] tomorrow 8:00–12:00 (entire work order).
- Your written note of the line's Actual hours and the work order's Total Hours.
- If your shop locks time entries (timesheet approval), one of [Tech-A]'s entries on [WO-1] is locked; otherwise there is no locked time.
- Esther Howard is named in the expected results and stays as written; the other names, numbers and hours are examples.
Setup
- Sign in as an Owner/Admin user (on a QA branch: the Admin quick-login button on the sign-in page; you appear as "Admin ShopView"). The Admin role holds Work orders > View and Work orders > Create & Edit (to check: your initials at the top right > Settings > Roles & Permissions > Admin > Work orders).
- Use one location for the whole case: it is shown in the top bar next to the bell (e.g. "Staging Heavy Duty - 9919"); to change it click your initials at the top right > Change Location.
- Settings (your initials at the top right > Settings) > Staff: reuse a technician only after checking it is active, has Time Clock on, a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User and this location in Location; otherwise add one: New Staff Member > First Name and Last Name, an Email, Role "Technician", at least one Department (required), Location = this location, Time Clock on > Save & Close. Record them as [Tech-A] = "Esther Howard" (use exactly this name: the expected results name it), [Tech-B] (e.g. Ralph Edwards), [Tech-C] (e.g. Dana Ortiz). Give [Tech-A] an email you can open, accept the invite and set a password, so [Tech-A] can sign in.
- Create the customer used only by this case: top menu Customers > New Customer > Name "ZZAUTOTEST F1 Status And Time Kept" > Save. On the customer page click the Contacts tab > New Contact > First Name and Last Name > Save (a new asset needs a contact). Then click the Assets tab > New Asset > pick the Contact, then Year, Make, Model and Unit (e.g. 2022, Freightliner, M2, unit "TRK-118") > Save. Make is required; Unit can be left empty.
- Create these work orders for "ZZAUTOTEST F1 Status And Time Kept": Work Orders > Create Work Order > in the New Work Order window pick the Customer and its Asset > Save. The new work order opens with the New Line form (otherwise click New Line on the Lines tab): in "What Are You Doing?" pick a ready-made line (e.g. "Replace - Brake pot"; typed free text finds no results), turn on Line Approved so the work order status reads Approved, and click Save & Close. Write down the number the build gives the work order (on a QA branch they look like "S10043-17581"). Then pick the lead in the Lead Technician dropdown on the work order page; it saves at once. (While a work order is an Estimate its Lead technician is read-only on the work order page; give an Estimate its lead from Board View instead: the card's More actions (…) > Reassign lead technician > pick the technician > Reassign.) Leave Lead Technician empty on the ones that should sit in Unassigned.- [WO-1] (e.g. S1-702): Lead Technician = [Tech-A].

- As [Tech-A] (second browser) click Start time clock on the line's Labor row, wait at least 1 minute, click Stop time clock, then click Start time clock again and leave it running; the work order now reads In Progress.
- Write down the line's Actual hours (e.g. "0.02 / 2.00") and the work order's Total Hours.
- On the Schedule, give [Tech-A] a shift on the work order tomorrow 8:00–12:00: Schedule > go to that day > drag the work order from the work order list onto [Tech-A]'s row at the start time > in the picker choose Entire work order > set the end time > save.
- If your shop locks time entries (timesheet approval), lock one of [Tech-A]'s entries on this work order; otherwise write 'no locked time on this site' in the result comment.
- Check the setup worked: [WO-1] reads In Progress with Lead Technician [Tech-A] and [Tech-A]'s clock running; the Schedule shows [Tech-A]'s 8:00–12:00 shift on it tomorrow; you have written down Actual hours and Total Hours. If not, redo the missing step before starting.

=== STEPS ===
- On the Schedule, drag [Tech-A]'s shift on this work order to 13:00–17:00, then resize it to 13:00–16:00, then drag it onto [Tech-C]'s row.
- Open the work order and read Lead Technician and Status.
- Open Work Orders, click the All tab and pick Board View in the display switcher (right side of the toolbar; tooltip "Board View").
- Type "ZZAUTOTEST F1 Status And Time Kept" in Search.
- Drag the card from [Tech-A]'s column into [Tech-B]'s column and click Clear shifts if the prompt opens.
- Open the work order and read Status, the running clock, the line's Actual hours and Total Hours.

=== EXPECTED ===
Expected results
- Moving, resizing and reassigning the shift on the Schedule does not change the lead: Lead Technician still reads Esther Howard after step 2.
- After the lead change the Status still reads In Progress.
- Recorded time is unchanged (Actual hours and Total Hours as written down, plus only the minutes the running clock added), Esther Howard's clock is still running, and any locked entry is unchanged.
