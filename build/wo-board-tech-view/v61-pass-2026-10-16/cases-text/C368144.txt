TITLE: Removing the lead puts the work order in its normal place in Unassigned

=== PRECONDITIONS ===
Setup (manual QA tester and Claude session)

Preconditions
- You are signed in as an Owner or Admin user whose role has Work orders > View and Work orders > Create & Edit, at {Location-A} (for example Staging Heavy Duty - 9919).
- Your browser window is at least 1024 px wide, so the display switcher (List, Tech View, Board View) shows at the right of the Work Orders toolbar.
- Technician {Technician-A} (for example ZZAUTOTEST Ana Alpha) is an active staff member at {Location-A} with Time Clock on and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User.
- Customers {Customer-A} to {Customer-D} are used by this case only, their names start with the same words (for example "ZZAUTOTEST F3 Unassigned Place") and end, in name order, with Alpha Co, Bravo Co, Charlie Co and Delta Co.
- Approved work orders (for example S10043-17731 to S10043-17734): {Work-order-1} (for {Customer-A}) and {Work-order-3} (for {Customer-C}) are led by {Technician-A}; {Work-order-2} (for {Customer-B}) and {Work-order-4} (for {Customer-D}) have no lead technician.
- Nobody has reordered work in Unassigned at {Location-A}.
- No technician has a shift on the Schedule for these work orders.
- On the Work Orders page nothing is ticked in Status, Asset on Site shows no choice (if its chip shows Yes or No, click the x on the chip), Assigned to me is off and Search is empty.

=== STEPS ===
- Open Work Orders from the top menu and click the All tab.
- Type the shared start of the customer names (for example ZZAUTOTEST F3 Unassigned Place) in Search.
- Click Board View in the display switcher.
- Read the order of the Unassigned column.
- Hover {Work-order-3}'s card in {Technician-A}'s column, click its More actions (…) button and click Reassign lead technician.
- Pick Unassigned, and click the dialog's confirm button if it shows one.
- Read the order of the Unassigned column.
- Open {Work-order-1}, clear its Lead Technician on the work order page, then go back to Work Orders > All > Board View with the same search.
- Read the order of the Unassigned column.
- Click Tech View in the display switcher and read the Unassigned group.

=== EXPECTED ===
Expected results
- Step 4: Unassigned reads {Work-order-2}, {Work-order-4}.
- Step 6: the message "Lead technician removed" shows.
- Step 7: Unassigned reads {Work-order-2}, {Work-order-3}, {Work-order-4}. {Work-order-3} takes its normal customer-name place, not the bottom.
- Step 9: Unassigned reads {Work-order-1}, {Work-order-2}, {Work-order-3}, {Work-order-4}. {Work-order-1} is first by customer name, not at the bottom.
- Step 10: Tech View's Unassigned group shows the same order.
Source — where this behaviour comes from
Epic SV-10043; story SV-10052 (Story 9, Drag to reorder work and technician groups/columns); SV-10047 (Story 4, Reassign or unassign the lead technician); PRD "Work Orders — Board View & Tech View Display Options", Confluence 845185030, version 54 (15 Oct 2026, Chris Ward), S9-R14, S2-R6 and S4-R5, read 15 Oct 2026. In version 53 (15 Oct 2026) S2-R6 was reworded to spell out the List's tie-break; the expected results are unchanged. Starting order (customer name A to Z): Review Decisions page 853901313, DR-45 (29 Sep 2026). The PRD of 7 Oct changed the removal rule (DR-47 had put a removed work order at the bottom of Unassigned); this case follows the PRD. Source-verified 15 October 2026. Last checked against build v26.40.8-7a95011 on 10/8/2026.
Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
- S9-R14: “A work order that gets a new lead from the Reassign lead technician dialog or from its detail page goes to the bottom of the new technician’s work, for everyone at the location, in both views. A work order dragged to a technician goes where it is dropped. Removing the lead from the Reassign lead technician dialog or the detail page puts the work order in Unassigned in the initial sort, not at the bottom, so it is not lost behind every other unassigned work order. Going to the bottom applies only to work moved onto a technician. A work order created or split off with a lead keeps the initial sort (S9-R3).”
- S2-R6: “Before a manual order is saved, use the List’s default work order sort, ties included, exactly as the List sorts today: work orders for the same customer go by start date, newest first. The List itself does not change. A work order that gets a new lead after it was created goes to the bottom of that technician’s work instead (S9-R14).”
- S4-R5: “After successful reassignment, show “Lead technician updated”; after unassigning, show “Lead technician removed”.”
