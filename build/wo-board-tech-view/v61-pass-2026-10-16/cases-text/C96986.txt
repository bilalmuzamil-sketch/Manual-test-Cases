Setup
- Sign in with the Admin quick-login button on the sign-in page (you appear as "Admin ShopView"). Check the location in the top bar next to the bell; to change it click your initials at the top right > Change Location.
- Settings (your initials at the top right > Settings) > Staff > New Staff Member, enter First Name and Last Name (e.g. "Esther Howard") and an email. Pick a Role that is not Office User or Time Clock User (e.g. "Technician"), pick at least one Department (required), set Location to this location and turn Time Clock on, then click Save & Close. Record as [Tech-A].
- Settings > Roles & Permissions > Create Custom Role (e.g. "ZZAUTOTEST F2 Finance Toggle") > turn on Work Orders view and See Financial Data > Save.
- Settings (your initials at the top right > Settings) > Staff > New Staff Member for "Nate Nofinance" with an email you can open, Role = the new custom role, at least one Department (required), with this location picked in Location, Time Clock off > Save & Close, then accept the invitation and set a password. Keep him signed in in a second browser.
- Create a customer used only by this case so a search shows only its work orders: top menu Customers > New Customer, Name "ZZAUTOTEST F2 No Dollar Amounts" > Save. On the customer page click the Contacts tab > New Contact > First Name and Last Name > Save (a new asset needs a contact). Then click the Assets tab > New Asset > pick the Contact, then Year, Make, Model and Unit (e.g. 2022, Freightliner, M2, unit "TRK-118") > Save. Make is required; Unit can be left empty.
- Create the work order: Work Orders > Create Work Order > in the New Work Order window pick Customer "ZZAUTOTEST F2 No Dollar Amounts" and its Asset > Save, and record its number as [WO-1] (e.g. "S1-702"). The new work order opens with the New Line form (otherwise click New Line on the Lines tab): in "What Are You Doing?" pick a ready-made line (e.g. "Replace - Brake pot"; typed free text finds no results), turn on Line Approved so the work order status reads Approved, and click Save & Close.
- Set the leads: on an Approved work order pick the technician in the Lead Technician dropdown on the work order page; it saves at once. (While a work order is an Estimate its Lead technician is read-only on the work order page; give an Estimate its lead from Board View instead: the card's More actions (…) > Reassign lead technician > pick the technician > Reassign.) Leave Lead Technician empty on the ones that should sit in Unassigned. Here: [WO-1] = [Tech-A].
- As Nate Nofinance, turn Total price on in Tech View's Column Selection menu and in Board View's Fields to display.
- Check the setup worked: as Nate Nofinance, Work Orders > All tab > Search ZZAUTOTEST F2 No Dollar Amounts — Tech View shows a Total price column and the Board View card shows a dollar amount. If not, check his role has See Financial Data on.
