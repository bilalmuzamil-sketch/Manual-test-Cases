TITLE: Browser Back restores the scroll position in Tech View and Board View

=== PRECONDITIONS ===
Setup (manual QA tester and Claude session)

Preconditions
- You are signed in as an Owner or Admin user whose role has Work orders > View and Work orders > Create & Edit, at {Location-A} (for example Staging Heavy Duty - 9919).
- Your browser window is at least 1024 px wide, so the display switcher (List, Tech View, Board View) shows at the right of the Work Orders toolbar.
- Eight technicians, {Technician-A}, {Technician-B}, {Technician-C}, {Technician-D}, {Technician-E}, {Technician-F}, {Technician-G} and {Technician-H} (for example Esther Howard, Ralph Edwards, Jenny Wilson, Theresa Webb, Kristin Watson, Brenda Martinez, Cameron Williamson and Floyd Miles), are active staff members at {Location-A} with Time Clock on and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User.
- Customer {Customer-A} (for example "ZZAUTOTEST F1 Scroll Back") has about 20 Approved work orders led by {Technician-H} and 2 Approved work orders led by each of the other seven technicians.
- With a search on {Customer-A}, Board View is wider than the window (you must scroll right to reach {Technician-H}'s column) and {Technician-H}'s column and the Tech View table are taller than the window.
- On the Work Orders page nothing is ticked in Status, Asset on Site shows no choice (if its chip shows Yes or No, click the x on the chip), Assigned to me is off and Search is empty.

=== STEPS ===
- Open Work Orders and click the All tab.
- Type the name of {Customer-A} in Search.
- Click Board View in the display switcher.
- Scroll the board to the right until {Technician-H}'s column is fully in view.
- Scroll down inside {Technician-H}'s column until a card well below the first screen is at the top of the column.
- Write down the technician columns in view and the card at the top of {Technician-H}'s column.
- Click a card that is in view to open its work order.
- Press the browser Back button.
- Compare what is in view with what you wrote down at step 6.
- Click Tech View in the display switcher.
- Scroll down the table until a row well below the first screen is at the top, and scroll right if the table is wider than the window.
- Write down the row at the top and the columns in view.
- Click a row to open its work order.
- Press the browser Back button.
- Compare what is in view with what you wrote down at step 12.

=== EXPECTED ===
Expected results
- Step 9: the same technician columns are in view, at exactly the same sideways position as before. In {Technician-H}'s column the card that was at the top comes back at the top and is fully shown; it may sit up to one card away from the exact position you left.
- Step 15: the same columns are in view, at exactly the same sideways position as before. The row that was at the top comes back at the top and is fully shown; it may sit up to one row away from the exact position you left.
- Steps 9 and 15: List is not checked here; it keeps today's behaviour.

Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)

Vertically, the row or card that was at the top of the view comes back at the top, fully shown; it may sit up to one row or card from the exact pixel position. List behaves as it does today.”
