TITLE: Dropping next to a card someone just moved shows a refresh alert

=== PRECONDITIONS ===
Preconditions
- Needs: 2 users · 2 browsers · about 20 minutes.
- Sign in as an Admin (on a QA branch: the Admin quick-login button on the sign-in page; you appear as "Admin ShopView"). Desktop browser window at least 1024 px wide; narrower windows show only the List. This is [User-A], in browser 1.
- [User-B]: a second Admin user, signed in in browser 2.
- Work in one location throughout, shown next to the bell in the top bar (e.g. "Staging Heavy Duty - 9919"); to change it click your initials (top right) > Change Location. Create everything in that location.
- Technicians [Tech-A] (e.g. "Esther Howard") and [Tech-B] named exactly "Ralph Edwards" (the expected results quote this name).
- Customer "ZZAUTOTEST F1 Moved Neighbour" (this case only) with three Approved work orders, all led by [Tech-A].
- Names and numbers are examples, except names the expected results quote, which must be used exactly.
Setup
- Technicians: your initials (top right) > Settings > Staff. A person counts as a technician only if Active, Time Clock on, Role not Office User or Time Clock User, and this location in Location. To add one: New Staff Member > First Name, Last Name, Email, Role "Technician", one Department (required), Location = this location, Time Clock on > Save & Close. You need: [Tech-A] and "Ralph Edwards" as [Tech-B].
- [User-B]: Settings > Staff > New Staff Member with an email you can open, Role Admin, one Department, this location in Location > Save & Close. Accept the invitation, set a password, and sign in as [User-B] in browser 2.
- Customer: Customers > New Customer > Name "ZZAUTOTEST F1 Moved Neighbour" > Save. On its Contacts tab > New Contact > First and Last Name > Save. On its Assets tab > New Asset > pick the contact, Year, Make (required), Model, Unit "TRK-118" > Save.
- Work orders for "ZZAUTOTEST F1 Moved Neighbour": Work Orders > Create Work Order > pick the Customer and its Asset > Save (if a credit-limit Confirmation appears, click Create). In the New Line form that opens, type in "What Are You Doing?" and pick a ready-made line (e.g. "Replace - Brake pot"), turn Line Approved on > Save & Close: the status reads Approved. Write down the work order number (top left). Then pick the lead in Lead Technician on the card at the left of the work order page; it saves at once.- [WO-1] (e.g. S1-701): lead = [Tech-A].
- [WO-2] ("S1-702" in the expected results): lead = [Tech-A].
- [WO-3] ("S1-703"): lead = [Tech-A].

- Check, in both browsers: Work Orders > All tab > Search "ZZAUTOTEST F1 Moved Neighbour" shows the three work orders, each led by [Tech-A]. Clear the search.

=== STEPS ===
- In both browsers open Work Orders, click the All tab, pick Board View and search for "ZZAUTOTEST F1 Moved Neighbour".
- In browser 2 drag [WO-2] into [Tech-B]'s column (keep shifts if asked).
- In browser 1, without reloading, drag [WO-3] and drop it directly above [WO-2] (still shown in [Tech-A]'s column there).
- Read the alert in browser 1 and where [WO-3] ends up.
- Reload browser 1.

=== EXPECTED ===
Expected results
- Browser 1 shows an alert with this sentence: The card you dropped this next to has moved. Refresh the board and try again.
- Above that sentence the alert may also show the general headline "Couldn't save the new order. Please try again." That is accepted and is not a fault (QA lead's decision below). Mark the test Failed only if the sentence above is missing or worded differently.
- "S1-703" goes back to where it was, and after the reload the board matches browser 2 (S1-702 under Ralph Edwards).
