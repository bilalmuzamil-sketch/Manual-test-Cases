Setup (manual QA tester and Claude session)

- You are signed in as {User-A} (for example Admin ShopView), an Owner or Admin user whose role has Work orders > View, Work orders > Create & Edit and See financial data, at {Location-A} (for example Staging Heavy Duty - 9919).

=== STEPS ===
- Open Work Orders from the top menu and click the All tab.
- Click List in the display switcher at the right of the toolbar.
- Look at the three buttons of the display switcher.
- Look at the icon in the Search box and the icons on the Status, Assigned to me and Asset on Site filters, and compare them with the Tech View design picture.
- Click Tech View in the display switcher.
- Look at the display switcher, the density button and the Column Selection button, and compare them with the Tech View design picture.
- Look at the icon in the Search box and the icons on the filters.
- Click the density button.
- Look at the menu that opens.
- Press Escape.
- Click Board View in the display switcher.
- Look at the display switcher and the density button, and compare them with the Board View design picture.
- Look at the icon in the Search box and the icons on the filters.
- Click List in the display switcher.
- Look at the other icons in List's toolbar.

=== EXPECTED ===
Expected results
- Step 3: the switcher shows three outline icons side by side in a light-grey rounded track: a bulleted list (List), the head and shoulders of a person (Tech View) and a square board with three bars of different lengths (Board View), the same three drawings as in the design pictures and about the same size as in the pictures at 100% zoom (20 px in the PRD). They are thin-line outline icons of the same weight, not filled icons, emoji or text characters.
- Step 4: the Search box shows a magnifier, and the filters show the design's thin outline icons, as in the design picture: a circle of short strokes on Status, a clipboard with lines on Assigned to me and a truck on Asset on Site. They are drawn in the same thin outline style as the switcher icons, not filled icons, emoji or text characters.
- Step 6: the Tech View button sits on a white raised segment and the other two look flat. The density button shows three lines with an up-and-down arrow at their right (the design's "row-height" icon) and the column chooser button shows a rectangle split into three columns (the design's "columns-3" icon), drawn in the same thin outline style, as in the Tech View picture.
- Step 7: the Search box and the filters show the same icons as in List at step 4.
- Step 9: the density menu offers Compact, Regular and Comfortable, with Regular marked as the one in use.
- Step 12: the Board View button is now the white raised segment, and the density button shows a different drawing from Tech View's: a card with an up-and-down arrow (the design's "card-height" icon), as in the Board View picture.
- Step 13: the Search box and the filters show the same icons as in List at step 4.
- Step 15: apart from the Search and filter icons, the icons in List's toolbar look the same as in List in production today. The only other change to List is the display switcher.
