TITLE: A lead change in Edit Work Order adds one Lead tech changed entry

=== PRECONDITIONS ===
Preconditions
- Needs: 1 user · 1 browser · about 10 minutes.
- Sign in as an Admin (on a QA branch: the Admin quick-login button on the sign-in page; you appear as "Admin ShopView"). Desktop browser window at least 1024 px wide; narrower windows show only the List.
- Work in one location throughout, shown next to the bell in the top bar (e.g. "Staging Heavy Duty - 9919"); to change it click your initials (top right) > Change Location. Create everything in that location.
- Technicians [Tech-A] and [Tech-B] (the expected results call them "Esther Howard" and "Ralph Edwards").
- Customer "ZZAUTOTEST Edit WO Audit" (this case only) with a contact and an asset.
- One Approved work order [WO-1] with two approved lines that follow the lead (no technician of their own), led by [Tech-A].
- Names and numbers are examples, except names the expected results quote, which must be used exactly.
Setup
- Technicians: your initials (top right) > Settings > Staff. A person counts as a technician only if Active, Time Clock on, Role not Office User or Time Clock User, and this location in Location. To add one: New Staff Member > First Name, Last Name, Email, Role "Technician", one Department (required), Location = this location, Time Clock on > Save & Close. You need: two, [Tech-A] and [Tech-B].
- Customer: Customers > New Customer > Name "ZZAUTOTEST Edit WO Audit" > Save. On its Contacts tab > New Contact > First and Last Name > Save. On its Assets tab > New Asset > pick the contact, Year, Make (required), Model, Unit "TRK-118" > Save.
- Work order: Work Orders > Create Work Order > Customer "ZZAUTOTEST Edit WO Audit" > its asset > Save (if a credit-limit Confirmation appears, click Create). In New Line > "What Are You Doing?" type "grease" and pick "Service - Full grease service" (no parts) > Line Approved on > Save & Add Line; then type "clutch", pick "Service - Adjust clutch", Line Approved on > Save & Close. Write down the number (top left) as [WO-1] ("S1-716" in the expected results).
- On [WO-1], in the card at the left, pick [Tech-A] in Lead Technician (it saves at once; it can only be changed once the work order is Approved).
- Check: reload [WO-1]. Both lines' Labor rows show [Tech-A]. The history is at the three-dots button at the top right of the tabs > Audit Log (window "Work Order Log").

=== STEPS ===
- Open [WO-1] (Work Orders > All tab > Search "ZZAUTOTEST Edit WO Audit" > click the work order) and open the Audit Log (three-dots at the top right of the tabs > Audit Log). Count the "Lead tech changed" entries and close the window (X).
- Note the time.
- There is no separate Edit Work Order window; the lead is changed on the status card (the card at the left of the work order page that shows the status, Lead Technician, Mileage and Engine Hours).
- On the status card click Lead Technician and pick [Tech-B]. It saves at once.
- Open the Audit Log again.
- Read the newest entries.

=== EXPECTED ===
Expected results
- Exactly one new "Lead tech changed" entry appears (count at step 1 + 1).
- It names Esther Howard as the previous lead, Ralph Edwards as the new lead, your user, and the time you noted.
- No separate entries appear for the two lines that followed the lead.
