TITLE: Changing the lead on the board sends the same notifications as today

=== PRECONDITIONS ===
Preconditions
- Needs: 4 users · 2 browsers · about 25 minutes.
- Build: the Work Orders QA build (sv10043, v26.40.8-7a95011).
- [User-A] = you: an Owner/Admin user (on a QA branch the Admin quick-login user, shown as "Admin ShopView"). The Admin role holds Work orders > View and Work orders > Create & Edit.
- Desktop browser window at least 1024 px wide. Narrower windows show only the List, without the display switcher.
- One location for the whole case, shown in the top bar next to the bell (e.g. "Staging Heavy Duty - 9919"); everything below is created in this location.
- Three eligible technicians at this location: [Tech-A] (e.g. "Esther Howard"), [Tech-B] (e.g. "Ralph Edwards"), [Tech-C] (e.g. "Dana Ortiz"). Each of them can sign in (each staff record has an email and an accepted invite). A person only counts as a technician here when the staff member is active (not deactivated), Time Clock is on, the Role is neither Office User nor Time Clock User, and this location is picked in their Location field.
- Customer "ZZAUTOTEST F1 Lead Notifications" (used by this case only) with one Approved work order [WO-1] led by [Tech-A].
- A second browser (or a private window) for signing in as the technicians.
- Names and the number are examples; what must hold: all three technicians can sign in and see their notifications.
Setup
- Sign in as an Owner/Admin user (on a QA branch: the Admin quick-login button on the sign-in page; you appear as "Admin ShopView"). The Admin role holds Work orders > View and Work orders > Create & Edit (to check: your initials at the top right > Settings > Roles & Permissions > Admin > Work orders).
- Use one location for the whole case: it is shown in the top bar next to the bell (e.g. "Staging Heavy Duty - 9919"); to change it click your initials at the top right > Change Location.
- Settings (your initials at the top right > Settings) > Staff: reuse a technician only after checking it is active, has Time Clock on, a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User and this location in Location; otherwise add one: New Staff Member > First Name and Last Name, an Email, Role "Technician", at least one Department (required), Location = this location, Time Clock on > Save & Close. Record them as [Tech-A] (e.g. Esther Howard), [Tech-B] (e.g. Ralph Edwards), [Tech-C] (e.g. Dana Ortiz). Give each an email you can open, accept the invite and set a password, so [Tech-A], [Tech-B] and [Tech-C] can sign in.
- Create the customer used only by this case: top menu Customers > New Customer > Name "ZZAUTOTEST F1 Lead Notifications" > Save. On the customer page click the Contacts tab > New Contact > First Name and Last Name > Save (a new asset needs a contact). Then click the Assets tab > New Asset > pick the Contact, then Year, Make, Model and Unit (e.g. 2022, Freightliner, M2, unit "TRK-118") > Save. Make is required; Unit can be left empty.
- Create these work orders for "ZZAUTOTEST F1 Lead Notifications": Work Orders > Create Work Order > in the New Work Order window pick the Customer and its Asset > Save. The new work order opens with the New Line form (otherwise click New Line on the Lines tab): in "What Are You Doing?" pick a ready-made line (e.g. "Replace - Brake pot"; typed free text finds no results), turn on Line Approved so the work order status reads Approved, and click Save & Close. Write down the number the build gives the work order (on a QA branch they look like "S10043-17581"). Then pick the lead in the Lead Technician dropdown on the work order page; it saves at once. (While a work order is an Estimate its Lead technician is read-only on the work order page; give an Estimate its lead from Board View instead: the card's More actions (…) > Reassign lead technician > pick the technician > Reassign.) Leave Lead Technician empty on the ones that should sit in Unassigned.- [WO-1] (e.g. S1-702): Lead Technician = [Tech-A].

- Check the setup worked: Work Orders > All tab > Search "ZZAUTOTEST F1 Lead Notifications" shows [WO-1] with Lead Technician [Tech-A]; in the second browser you can sign in as each of the three technicians. If not, fix the lead or finish the missing invite before starting; then clear the search.

=== STEPS ===
- Open [WO-1]'s work order page and change Lead Technician from [Tech-A] to [Tech-B] there.
- In a second browser, sign in as [Tech-A] and then as [Tech-B], and write down any notification each of them received for this change (notification bell, email, or none).
- Open Work Orders, click the All tab and pick Board View in the display switcher (right side of the toolbar; tooltip "Board View").
- Type "ZZAUTOTEST F1 Lead Notifications" in Search.
- In the first browser, drag the card from [Tech-B]'s column into [Tech-C]'s column.
- If a "Clear …'s scheduled shifts?" prompt opens, click Keep shifts.
- In the second browser, sign in as [Tech-B] and then as [Tech-C] and write down any notification each received.
- Repeat steps 3 to 7 in Tech View with the row's More actions > Reassign lead technician, moving it back to [Tech-A].

=== EXPECTED ===
Expected results
- A lead change made in Board View or Tech View produces exactly the same notifications as a lead change made on the work order page (step 2 compared with steps 6 and 8). The product owner records that no notification is sent on a lead change today, so none is expected in either place.
