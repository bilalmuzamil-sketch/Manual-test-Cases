TITLE: Opening a tab or coming from another page starts at the top-left

=== PRECONDITIONS ===
Preconditions
- Needs: 1 user · 1 browser · Schedule access · about 45 minutes.
- Build: the Work Orders QA build (sv10043, v26.40.8-7a95011).
- [User-A] = you: an Owner/Admin user (on a QA branch the Admin quick-login user, shown as "Admin ShopView"). The Admin role holds Work orders > View and Work orders > Create & Edit.
- Desktop browser with the window at least 1024 pixels wide (full screen on an ordinary laptop or monitor). Below that width the page shows only List.
- One location for the whole case, shown in the top bar next to the bell (e.g. "Staging Heavy Duty - 9919"); everything below is created in this location.
- At least 8 eligible technicians at this location (as in the scroll test), [Tech-A] to [Tech-H]. A person only counts as a technician here when the staff member is active (not deactivated), Time Clock is on, the Role is neither Office User nor Time Clock User, and this location is picked in their Location field.
- Customer "ZZAUTOTEST F1 Top Left" (used by this case only) with about 20 Approved work orders led by one technician, [Tech-A], so the board and table scroll both ways.
- Names are examples; what must hold: the board and the Tech View table both scroll right and down.
Setup
- Sign in as an Owner/Admin user (on a QA branch: the Admin quick-login button on the sign-in page; you appear as "Admin ShopView"). The Admin role holds Work orders > View and Work orders > Create & Edit (to check: your initials at the top right > Settings > Roles & Permissions > Admin > Work orders).
- Check the location in the top bar next to the bell; to change it click your initials at the top right > Change Location. Create everything below in this location.
- Settings (your initials at the top right > Settings) > Staff: reuse a technician only after checking it is active, has Time Clock on, a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User and this location in Location; otherwise add one: New Staff Member > First Name and Last Name, an Email, Role "Technician", at least one Department (required), Location = this location, Time Clock on > Save & Close. Record them as [Tech-A] (e.g. Esther Howard), [Tech-B] (e.g. Ralph Edwards), [Tech-C] (e.g. Jenny Wilson), [Tech-D] (e.g. Theresa Webb), [Tech-E] (e.g. Kristin Watson), [Tech-F] (e.g. Brenda Martinez), [Tech-G] (e.g. Cameron Williamson), [Tech-H] (e.g. Floyd Miles).
- Create the customer used only by this case: top menu Customers > New Customer > Name "ZZAUTOTEST F1 Top Left" > Save. On the customer page click the Contacts tab > New Contact > First Name and Last Name > Save (a new asset needs a contact). Then click the Assets tab > New Asset > pick the Contact, then Year, Make, Model and Unit (e.g. 2022, Freightliner, M2, unit "TRK-118") > Save. Make is required; Unit can be left empty.
- Create these work orders for "ZZAUTOTEST F1 Top Left": Work Orders > Create Work Order > in the New Work Order window pick the Customer and its Asset > Save. The new work order opens with the New Line form (otherwise click New Line on the Lines tab): in "What Are You Doing?" pick a ready-made line (e.g. "Replace - Brake pot"; typed free text finds no results), turn on Line Approved so the work order status reads Approved, and click Save & Close. Write down the number the build gives the work order (on a QA branch they look like "S10043-17581"). Then pick the lead in the Lead Technician dropdown on the work order page; it saves at once. (While a work order is an Estimate its Lead technician is read-only on the work order page; give an Estimate its lead from Board View instead: the card's More actions (…) > Reassign lead technician > pick the technician > Reassign.) Leave Lead Technician empty on the ones that should sit in Unassigned.- About 20 work orders with Lead Technician = [Tech-A].

- Check the setup worked: Work Orders > All tab > Board View: you must scroll right to reach the last technician and [Tech-A]'s column is taller than the window; Tech View: the table is taller than the window. If not, add more technicians or work orders before starting.

=== STEPS ===
- Open Work Orders.
- Click the All tab on the left of the toolbar.
- In the display switcher at the right of the toolbar, click Board View (tooltip "Board View").
- Scroll the board right and scroll down inside a long column.
- Click the Estimates tab, then click the All tab.
- Note where the board is scrolled to.
- Scroll right and down again.
- Click Schedule in the top navigation, then click Work Orders in the top navigation.
- Note where the board is scrolled to.
- Scroll right and down again.
- While still on Board View, click Work Orders in the top navigation.
- Note where the board is scrolled to.
- In the display switcher at the right of the toolbar, click Tech View (tooltip "Tech View").
- Repeat steps 4 to 12 in Tech View (scroll down and right in the table).

=== EXPECTED ===
Expected results
- After clicking a tab the board starts at the top and all the way to the left: Unassigned and the first technicians are in view and every column starts at its first card.
- Coming back from Schedule through the Work Orders link it also starts at the top-left.
- Clicking Work Orders in the top navigation while already on Board View does not leave the page, so the board keeps exactly the scroll position you had.
- Tech View behaves the same way: top-left after a tab click or coming from another page, unchanged after clicking Work Orders while already on Tech View.
Source — where this behaviour comes from
Epic SV-10043; story SV-10044 (Story 1, Switch between display options); PRD Confluence 845185030, edited 7 Oct 2026 (status line "PRD v33"), S1-R12, read 8 Oct 2026. PO decision Chris Ward, PRD footer comment 6 Oct 2026, item 6 (header click keeps the place).
Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
- S1-R12: “In Tech View and Board View, opening a filter view any way other than browser Back (for example clicking its tab, a link, or a menu item) starts scrolled to the top and all the way to the left. List behaves as it does today. Clicking “Work Orders” in the header while already on Tech View or Board View does not leave the page, so it keeps the current scroll position, as the List does.”
