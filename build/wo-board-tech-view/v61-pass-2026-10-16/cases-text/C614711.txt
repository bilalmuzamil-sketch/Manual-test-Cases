Source — where this behaviour comes from
Epic SV-10043; story SV-10046 (Story 3, Board View); PRD "Work Orders — Board View & Tech View Display Options", Confluence 845185030, version 58 (15 Oct 2026, Chris Ward), S5-R11b and S3-R3, with S3-N2, S3-N4 and the Decisions entry of 2026-09-13 as the nearest rules, read 16 Oct 2026. Defect SV-11416 (open). Source-verified 16 October 2026.
The spec says what happens when a lead technician is deactivated (the work stays in their column, marked inactive, so it stays attributable) but not when the technician is deleted. SV-11416 asks the product owner to decide: show the technician's name with the inactive marker, as for a deactivated technician, or move the work order to Unassigned. Either keeps this case's results; once the product owner decides, this case is updated to that ruling.
Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
- S5-R11b: “Every technician name in Tech View and Board View, on cards, rows, group headers, column headers and the names shown on hover over any technician avatar (S2-R10, S7-R1), is the technician’s staff record name, the same name the List’s Lead Technician column shows.”
- S3-R3: “Column headers (technician names and counts) stay pinned at the top of the board while the user scrolls vertically, so long columns never hide whose column is being viewed.”
- S3-N2: “Keep matching work visible in its deactivated technician’s column.”
- S3-N4: “Show an inactive indicator on deactivated technician columns.”
- Decisions (2026-09-13): “Deactivating a technician does not change work orders they already lead. They remain the lead technician on those work orders so the work stays visible and attributable; they simply receive no new assignments (S2-N2, S3-N2, S3-E3).”
