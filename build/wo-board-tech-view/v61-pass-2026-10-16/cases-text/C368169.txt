TITLE: Edit Work Order saves a new lead and a new mileage together

=== PRECONDITIONS ===
Preconditions
- Needs: 1 user · 1 browser · about 10 minutes.
- Sign in as an Admin (on a QA branch: the Admin quick-login button on the sign-in page; you appear as "Admin ShopView"). Desktop browser window at least 1024 px wide; narrower windows show only the List.
- Work in one location throughout, shown next to the bell in the top bar (e.g. "Staging Heavy Duty - 9919"); to change it click your initials (top right) > Change Location. Create everything in that location.
- Technicians [Tech-A] and [Tech-B] (the expected results call them "Esther Howard" and "Ralph Edwards").
- Customer "ZZAUTOTEST Edit WO Lead Mileage" (this case only) with a contact and an asset.
- One Approved work order [WO-1] led by [Tech-A], with Mileage 120,000.
- Names and numbers are examples, except names the expected results quote, which must be used exactly.
Setup
- Technicians: your initials (top right) > Settings > Staff. A person counts as a technician only if Active, Time Clock on, Role not Office User or Time Clock User, and this location in Location. To add one: New Staff Member > First Name, Last Name, Email, Role "Technician", one Department (required), Location = this location, Time Clock on > Save & Close. You need: two, [Tech-A] and [Tech-B].
- Customer: Customers > New Customer > Name "ZZAUTOTEST Edit WO Lead Mileage" > Save. On its Contacts tab > New Contact > First and Last Name > Save. On its Assets tab > New Asset > pick the contact, Year, Make (required), Model, Unit "TRK-118" > Save.
- Work order: Work Orders > Create Work Order > Customer "ZZAUTOTEST Edit WO Lead Mileage" > its asset > Save (if a credit-limit Confirmation appears, click Create). In New Line > "What Are You Doing?" type "grease" and pick "Service - Full grease service" (no parts) > Line Approved on > Save & Close. Write down the number (top left) as [WO-1] ("S1-712" in the expected results).
- On [WO-1], in the card at the left, pick [Tech-A] in Lead Technician (it saves at once; it can only be changed once the work order is Approved).
- In the same card click Mileage, type 120000 and click outside the field (it saves when you leave it).
- Check: reload [WO-1]. Lead Technician reads [Tech-A] and Mileage reads 120,000.

=== STEPS ===
- Open [WO-1] (Work Orders > All tab > Search "ZZAUTOTEST Edit WO Lead Mileage" > click the work order).
- There is no separate Edit Work Order window; the edits are made on the status card (the card at the left of the work order page that shows the status, Lead Technician, Mileage and Engine Hours).
- On the status card click Lead Technician and pick [Tech-B]. It saves at once.
- On the status card click Mileage, change it to 120500 and click outside the field (it saves when you leave it).
- Watch for any error message.
- Reload the page and read Lead Technician and Mileage on the status card.

=== EXPECTED ===
Expected results
- Steps 3 to 5: both changes save with no error message.
- Step 6: Lead Technician reads "Ralph Edwards" and Mileage reads 120,500. Both changes were saved.
