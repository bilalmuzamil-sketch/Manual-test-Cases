TITLE: Exiting impersonation while a page loads shows your own data

=== PRECONDITIONS ===
Preconditions
- Needs: 1 user · 1 browser · about 15 minutes
- Build: the Work Orders QA build (sv10043, v26.40.8-7a95011), desktop browser, window at least 1024 px wide.
- Role: Owner/Admin. On the sign-in page click the Admin quick-login button (you appear as Admin ShopView). The Admin role has Work orders > View and Create & Edit (check: your initials (top right) > Settings > Roles & Permissions > Admin > edit).
- Location: Staging Heavy Duty - 9919 for the whole case (shown next to the bell; change it with your initials > Change Location).
- Your user can use the app as another staff member (account access). Name is an example ("Esther Howard").
- 1 technicians eligible at this location: active, Role Technician, at least one Department, Location Staging Heavy Duty - 9919, Time Clock on. Names are examples ("Esther Howard").
- Customer "ZZAUTOTEST Impersonate Exit" (used by this case only) with one contact and one asset, and one Approved work order [WO-1] led by [Tech-A]. You (Admin ShopView) lead no work orders at this location.
Setup
- Your initials (top right) > Settings > Staff: if no suitable technician exists, click New Staff Member > First Name, Last Name, Email > Role = Technician > Departments = any one (at least one Department is required) > Location = Staging Heavy Duty - 9919 > turn Time Clock on > Save & Close. Record the technician as [Tech-A] (the expected results call this technician "Esther Howard").
- Customers > New Customer > Name "ZZAUTOTEST Impersonate Exit" > Save (the customer page opens). Contacts tab > New Contact > First Name "ZZAUTOTEST", Last Name "Contact" > Save. Assets tab > New Asset > Contact = ZZAUTOTEST Contact > Year 2022 > Make: type "Freight" and pick Freightliner > Model: type "M2" and pick it > Unit "TRK-118" > Save (Contact and Make are required).
- Work Orders > Create Work Order > New Work Order: Customer = "ZZAUTOTEST Impersonate Exit" > Asset = its asset > Save. If a Confirmation says the customer is over their credit limit, click Create. The work order opens on its Lines tab with the New Line form open. Record the number shown at the top left as [WO-1] (the expected results call it "S1-797").
- In New Line > What Are You Doing? type "grease" and pick the ready-made line "Service - Full grease service" (it has no parts) > turn Line Approved on > Save & Close.
- On [WO-1], on the status card at the left, click Lead Technician and pick [Tech-A]. (Lead Technician is a dropdown only once the work order is Approved; while it is an Estimate it reads "Unassigned" and cannot be changed.) The choice saves at once.
- Check the setup worked: Work Orders > All > Assigned to me on lists no work orders for you; [WO-1] shows Lead Technician [Tech-A].

=== STEPS ===
- Start impersonating [Tech-A] (account access) and open Work Orders with Assigned to me on. [WO-1] is listed.
- Click Reports > Work In Progress and, while it is still loading, exit impersonation (Exit in the orange "Account Access Mode" bar at the top).
- Wait for the page to finish loading.
- Open Work Orders with Assigned to me on.

=== EXPECTED ===
Expected results
- Step 3: no red error message appears and the top bar shows your own user again.
- Step 4: "S1-797" is not listed (you lead no work orders). Nothing of Esther Howard's is left on screen.
