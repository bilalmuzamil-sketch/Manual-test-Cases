TITLE: Reassign and Unassign work from the keyboard through the More actions menu

=== PRECONDITIONS ===
Setup (manual QA tester and Claude session)

Preconditions
- You are signed in as an Owner or Admin user whose role has Work orders > View and Work orders > Create & Edit, at {Location-A} (for example Staging Heavy Duty - 9919).
- Your browser window is at least 1024 px wide, so the display switcher (List, Tech View, Board View) shows at the right of the Work Orders toolbar.
- Technician {Technician-A} (for example ZZAUTOTEST Ana Alpha) is an active staff member at {Location-A} with Time Clock on and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User.
- Technician {Technician-B} (for example ZZAUTOTEST Ben Bravo) is an active staff member at {Location-A} with Time Clock on and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User.
- Customer {Customer-A} (for example "ZZAUTOTEST F3 Keyboard Reassign") has four Approved work orders: {Work-order-1} (for example S10043-17701) and {Work-order-2} (for example S10043-17702) led by {Technician-A}, {Work-order-3} (for example S10043-17703) led by {Technician-B}, and {Work-order-4} (for example S10043-17704) with no lead technician.
- No technician has a shift on the Schedule for these work orders.
- On the Work Orders page nothing is ticked in Status, Asset on Site shows no choice (if its chip shows Yes or No, click the x on the chip), Assigned to me is off and Search is empty.

=== STEPS ===
- Open Work Orders from the top menu and click the All tab.
- Type the name of {Customer-A} in Search.
- Click Board View in the display switcher.
- Click once in the Search box, then put the mouse aside: from here on use only the keyboard.
- Press Tab until {Work-order-1}'s card has focus, then Tab to its More actions (…) button and press Enter.
- Move to Reassign lead technician with the arrow keys or Tab and press Enter.
- Type the first letters of {Technician-B}'s first name in the dialog's search box.
- Move to {Technician-B}'s row with the arrow keys or Tab and press Enter (if a "Clear … scheduled shifts?" question opens, move to Keep shifts and press Enter).
- Press Tab until {Work-order-3}'s card has focus, then Tab to its More actions (…) button and press Enter.
- Read the menu items.
- Move to Unassign and press Enter (if a "Clear … scheduled shifts?" question opens, move to Keep shifts and press Enter).
- Press Tab until {Work-order-2}'s card has focus, then press Space, the arrow keys and Shift with the arrow keys.

=== EXPECTED ===
Expected results
- Step 8: {Work-order-1} moves to {Technician-B}'s column and "Lead technician updated" shows.
- Step 10: the menu offers Reassign lead technician and Unassign.
- Step 11: {Work-order-3} moves to the Unassigned column and "Lead technician removed" shows.
- Step 12: no key moves {Work-order-2}; it stays in its place. Reordering is by mouse or touch only.

Until 15 Oct 2026 Story 11 only listed keyboard behaviour the design still had to define, so this case asked the tester to try keys and write down what each did. Chris Ward answered on 15 Oct 2026 (answer 28): Tab reaches every control, Enter opens, Escape closes, focus returns to the control that opened a menu or dialog, reassigning by keyboard goes through the ⋯ menu, and there is no keyboard drag. PRD version 54 rewrites Story 11 into these requirements, and this case now checks them.
Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
- S11-R9: “Reassigning by keyboard goes through the more-actions menu: Reassign lead technician opens the Reassign dialog, and Unassign is a menu item. There is no keyboard drag in this release, so reordering work orders and technicians is by mouse or touch only.”
- S4-R5: “After successful reassignment, show “Lead technician updated”; after unassigning, show “Lead technician removed”.”
- Out of Scope: “Reordering work orders or technicians by keyboard. Reordering is by mouse or touch drag in this release (S11-R9).”
- Tech plan Phase 11 dialog: “The dialog is the keyboard path for moving work between technicians; it runs the same permission and status checks as the pointer path (S11-N1)”
