TITLE: Fields to display offers the 13 optional fields with their usual values

=== PRECONDITIONS ===
Setup (manual QA tester and Claude session)

Preconditions
- You are signed in as an Owner or Admin user whose role has Work orders > View and Work orders > Create & Edit, at {Location-A} (for example Staging Heavy Duty - 9919).
- Your browser window is at least 1024 px wide, so the display switcher (List, Tech View, Board View) shows at the right of the Work Orders toolbar.
- Technicians {Technician-A} (for example Esther Howard) and {Technician-B} (for example Ralph Edwards) are active staff members at {Location-A} with Time Clock on, a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User, and can sign in.
- Staff member {Advisor-A} (for example James Smith) can be picked as a Service Advisor.
- Customer {Customer-A} (for example "ZZAUTOTEST F2 Card Field Values") has an asset with a VIN (for example 1FUJGLDR5CSBM1234).
- Approved work order {Work-order-1} (for example S10043-17661) for {Customer-A} is led by {Technician-A}, has Service Advisor {Advisor-A}, Asset on site turned on and three approved lines with labor hours, {Line-1}, {Line-2} and {Line-3} (for example 2.0, 3.0 and 3.0 hours, 8.0 in all).
- {Line-2}'s technician is {Technician-B}, set through Edit Line.
- {Technician-A} is clocked in on {Line-1}'s Labor row and {Technician-B} on {Line-2}'s, so two people are clocked in.
- List's Column Selection shows Lead Technician, Asset, VIN/Serial #, Progress, Service Advisor, Clocked In, Lines, Total price, On Site and Created on.
- On the Work Orders page nothing is ticked in Status, Asset on Site shows no choice (if its chip shows Yes or No, click the x on the chip), Assigned to me is off and Search is empty.

=== STEPS ===
- Open Work Orders, click the All tab and click List in the display switcher.
- Type the name of {Customer-A} in Search.
- Write down {Work-order-1}'s values: Lead Technician, Customer, Asset, VIN/Serial #, Progress, Service Advisor, Clocked In, Lines, Total price, On Site and Created on.
- Hover the Clocked In value and read the names.
- Click Board View in the display switcher and click Fields to display.
- Count the optional fields offered.
- Turn every optional field on and close the menu.
- Compare each field on {Work-order-1}'s card with the values you wrote down at step 3.
- Read the clocked-in field on the card and hover it.

=== EXPECTED ===
Expected results
- Step 4: List's Clocked In shows the first technician's full name, then +1 (for example "Esther Howard +1"), and hovering shows both names, {Technician-A} and {Technician-B}.
- Step 6: Fields to display offers 13 optional fields: lead technician name, customer, asset (year/make/model), VIN/serial, progress, service advisor, clocked in, line count, line technicians, estimated hours, total price, on-site indicator and created date.
- Step 8: each field on the card shows the same value as List for {Work-order-1} (for example 3 lines and the same total price), and the estimated hours show 8.0, the same as the work order page's Total Hours.
- Step 9: the card's clocked-in field reads the same as List's Clocked In at step 4: the first technician's full name, then +1, and hovering shows both names.

The PRD (version 52) said first name, then +N, while the List today shows the first person's full name. Chris Ward answered on 15 Oct 2026 (answer 31) that the card shows what the List shows: the first technician's full name, then +N, with every name on hover; PRD version 54 says so, and this case follows it.
Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
- S5-R11: “Offer the optional fields listed below using their existing production meaning.”
- S5-R11 field list: “*Optional fields:* lead technician name, customer, asset (year/make/model), VIN/serial, progress, service advisor, clocked in (the names of the technicians currently clocked in to the work order, shown the way List’s Clocked In column shows them today: the first technician’s full name, then +N, with every name on hover), line count, line technicians, estimated hours, total price, on-site indicator, and created date.”
- S5-R11a: “Estimated hours on a card is the total of the work order's lines with declined lines left out, the same figure as the work order page's Total Hours. It can differ from that page by up to 0.1 hours, because the page rounds each line to whole minutes. It can also differ from the List's Estimated Hours, which keeps its production figure (List is unchanged).”
