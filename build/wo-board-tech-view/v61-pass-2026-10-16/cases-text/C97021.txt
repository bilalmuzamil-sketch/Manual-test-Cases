TITLE: Tab reaches every card, row and header in on-screen order with a focus ring

=== PRECONDITIONS ===
Setup (manual QA tester and Claude session)

Preconditions
- You are signed in as an Owner or Admin user whose role has Work orders > View and Work orders > Create & Edit, at {Location-A} (for example Staging Heavy Duty - 9919).
- Your browser window is at least 1024 px wide, so the display switcher (List, Tech View, Board View) shows at the right of the Work Orders toolbar.
- Technician {Technician-A} (for example ZZAUTOTEST Ana Alpha) is an active staff member at {Location-A} with Time Clock on and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User.
- Technician {Technician-B} (for example ZZAUTOTEST Ben Bravo) is an active staff member at {Location-A} with Time Clock on and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User.
- Customer {Customer-A} (for example "ZZAUTOTEST F3 Keyboard Reach") has four Approved work orders: {Work-order-1} (for example S10043-17701) and {Work-order-2} (for example S10043-17702) led by {Technician-A}, {Work-order-3} (for example S10043-17703) led by {Technician-B}, and {Work-order-4} (for example S10043-17704) with no lead technician.
- No technician has a shift on the Schedule for these work orders.
- On the Work Orders page nothing is ticked in Status, Asset on Site shows no choice (if its chip shows Yes or No, click the x on the chip), Assigned to me is off and Search is empty.

=== STEPS ===
- Open Work Orders from the top menu and click the All tab.
- Type the name of {Customer-A} in Search.
- Click Board View in the display switcher.
- Click once in the Search box, then put the mouse aside: from here on use only the keyboard.
- Press Tab repeatedly until a card has focus, looking at the screen after each press.
- Keep pressing Tab and write down, in order, every card and column header that gets focus.
- Press Shift+Tab a few times and write down where focus goes.
- Click Tech View in the display switcher, click once in the Search box and put the mouse aside again.
- Press Tab repeatedly until a row has focus.
- Keep pressing Tab and write down, in order, every row and group header that gets focus.

=== EXPECTED ===
Expected results
- Step 5: Tab reaches the cards, and the focused card shows a visible focus ring.
- Step 6: Tab moves through the column headers and cards in on-screen order: column by column from left to right, and top to bottom within each column. Every card and every column header is reached. Tab also stops on controls inside a header or card (such as the pin or More actions button) on the way.
- Step 7: Shift+Tab moves back through the same order.
- Step 9: the focused row shows a visible focus ring.
- Step 10: Tab moves through the group headers and rows from top to bottom. Every row and every group header is reached.

Until 15 Oct 2026 Story 11 only listed keyboard behaviour the design still had to define, so this case asked the tester to try keys and write down what each did. Chris Ward answered on 15 Oct 2026 (answer 28): Tab reaches every control, Enter opens, Escape closes, focus returns to the control that opened a menu or dialog, reassigning by keyboard goes through the ⋯ menu, and there is no keyboard drag. PRD version 54 rewrites Story 11 into these requirements, and this case now checks them.
