TITLE: A line with only a Schedule shift technician follows the new lead technician

=== PRECONDITIONS ===
Setup (manual QA tester and Claude session)


Preconditions
- You are signed in as an Owner or Admin user whose role has Work orders > View, Work orders > Create & Edit and Schedule access, at {Location-A} (for example Staging Heavy Duty - 9919).
- Your browser window is at least 1024 px wide, so the display switcher (List, Tech View, Board View) shows at the right of the Work Orders toolbar.
- Technician {Technician-A} (for example Esther Howard) is an active staff member at {Location-A} with Time Clock on, the Service department, and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User.
- Technician {Technician-B} (for example Ralph Edwards) is an active staff member at {Location-A} with Time Clock on, the Service department, and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User.
- Technician {Technician-C} (for example Dana Ortiz) is an active staff member at {Location-A} with Time Clock on, the Service department, and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User.
- Technician {Technician-D} (for example Jenny Wilson) is an active staff member at {Location-A} with Time Clock on, the Service department, and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User.
- Customer {Customer-A} (for example "ZZAUTOTEST NT3 Shift Line Follows") is used by this case only.
- Approved work order {Work-order-1} (for example S10043-17595) for {Customer-A} is led by {Technician-A} and has two approved lines, {Line-1} (for example "Service - Full grease service") and {Line-2} (for example "Service - Adjust clutch"). It has no recorded time and nobody is clocked in to it.
- {Line-1} has no technician set through Edit Line; its labor follows the lead {Technician-A}.
- {Line-2} has {Technician-C} as its technician, set through Edit Line.
- {Technician-B} has a shift tomorrow 9:00–11:00 on {Line-1} only, made on the Schedule.
- {Technician-A} has no shift on {Work-order-1}.
- On the Work Orders page, Asset on Site shows no choice (if its chip shows Yes or No, click the x on the chip).

=== STEPS ===
- Open Work Orders, click the All tab and click Board View in the display switcher.
- Type the name of {Customer-A} in Search.
- On {Work-order-1}'s card in {Technician-A}'s column, open More actions (…) and click Reassign lead technician.
- Choose {Technician-D} and click Reassign.
- Click {Work-order-1}'s card to open the work order.
- Go to its Lines tab.
- Read the Labor row of {Line-1} and of {Line-2}.
- Click {Line-1}'s name to open Edit Line.
- Read the names in its Technicians field.
- Close Edit Line without saving.
- Click {Line-2}'s name to open Edit Line.
- Read the names in its Technicians field.
- Close Edit Line without saving.

=== EXPECTED ===
Expected results
- Step 4: the lead changes at once with the message "Lead technician updated" and no "Clear … scheduled shifts?" question ({Technician-A} has no shift on this work order); the card moves to {Technician-D}'s column.
- Steps 7 and 9: {Line-1} now follows {Technician-D}, and {Technician-B} is still listed among {Line-1}'s technicians.
- Steps 7 and 12: {Line-2} still has {Technician-C} as its technician; it did not move to {Technician-D}.

Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
- S4-R5: “After successful reassignment, show “Lead technician updated”; after unassigning, show “Lead technician removed”.”
- S4-R28: “The shift-clearing prompt (S4-R11, S4-R26) shows only when the outgoing lead has a work-order-level shift on this work order that has not ended. A work-order-level shift is one that covers every approved line of the work order, judged at the moment the lead changes; this is what the Schedule's Entire work order drop creates. Lines not yet approved, and declined lines, are ignored. On a work order with one line, a shift on that line counts, and so does a Choose lines shift that ticks every approved line. A shift on only some approved lines does not show the prompt, and that includes an Entire work order shift once a line has been added and approved after it was made.”
- S4-R8 context note (SV-11128): “"Explicitly assigned" means the line's own technician, set through Edit Line. A technician who is on a line only because of a Schedule shift, whether the shift was dropped on that line or on the entire work order, does not count: the line follows the incoming lead like any other line, and the shift technician stays on the line's technician list.”
- S4-R8 table: explicit: “Explicitly assigned to a different technician | No — the explicit assignment is preserved”
