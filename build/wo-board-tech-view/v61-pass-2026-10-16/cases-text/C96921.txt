TITLE: With the same user in two tabs, the last display change wins

=== PRECONDITIONS ===
Preconditions
- Needs: 1 user · 1 browser · about 10 minutes.
- Build: the Work Orders QA build (sv10043, v26.40.8-7a95011).
- [User-A] = you: an Owner/Admin user (on a QA branch the Admin quick-login user, shown as "Admin ShopView"). The Admin role holds Work orders > View and Work orders > Create & Edit.
- Desktop browser with the window at least 1024 pixels wide (full screen on an ordinary laptop or monitor). Below that width the page shows only List.
- One location for the whole case, shown in the top bar next to the bell (e.g. "Staging Heavy Duty - 9919"); everything below is created in this location.
- [Tech-A]: one eligible technician at this location. A person only counts as a technician here when the staff member is active (not deactivated), Time Clock is on, the Role is neither Office User nor Time Clock User, and this location is picked in their Location field.
- Customer "ZZAUTOTEST F1 Two Tabs" (used by this case only) with one Approved work order [WO-1] led by [Tech-A].
- Three tabs of one browser, all signed in as [User-A].
- Names are examples; what must hold: the same user in every tab.
Setup
- Sign in as an Owner/Admin user (on a QA branch: the Admin quick-login button on the sign-in page; you appear as "Admin ShopView"). The Admin role holds Work orders > View and Work orders > Create & Edit (to check: your initials at the top right > Settings > Roles & Permissions > Admin > Work orders).
- Check the location in the top bar next to the bell; to change it click your initials at the top right > Change Location. Create everything below in this location.
- Settings (your initials at the top right > Settings) > Staff: reuse a technician only after checking it is active, has Time Clock on, a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User and this location in Location; otherwise add one: New Staff Member > First Name and Last Name, an Email, Role "Technician", at least one Department (required), Location = this location, Time Clock on > Save & Close. Record them as [Tech-A] (e.g. Esther Howard).
- Create the customer used only by this case: top menu Customers > New Customer > Name "ZZAUTOTEST F1 Two Tabs" > Save. On the customer page click the Contacts tab > New Contact > First Name and Last Name > Save (a new asset needs a contact). Then click the Assets tab > New Asset > pick the Contact, then Year, Make, Model and Unit (e.g. 2022, Freightliner, M2, unit "TRK-118") > Save. Make is required; Unit can be left empty.
- Create these work orders for "ZZAUTOTEST F1 Two Tabs": Work Orders > Create Work Order > in the New Work Order window pick the Customer and its Asset > Save. The new work order opens with the New Line form (otherwise click New Line on the Lines tab): in "What Are You Doing?" pick a ready-made line (e.g. "Replace - Brake pot"; typed free text finds no results), turn on Line Approved so the work order status reads Approved, and click Save & Close. Write down the number the build gives the work order (on a QA branch they look like "S10043-17581"). Then pick the lead in the Lead Technician dropdown on the work order page; it saves at once. (While a work order is an Estimate its Lead technician is read-only on the work order page; give an Estimate its lead from Board View instead: the card's More actions (…) > Reassign lead technician > pick the technician > Reassign.) Leave Lead Technician empty on the ones that should sit in Unassigned.- [WO-1] (e.g. S1-702): Lead Technician = [Tech-A].

- Check the setup worked: Work Orders > All tab > Search "ZZAUTOTEST F1 Two Tabs" shows [WO-1] with lead [Tech-A]. If not, fix the lead before starting, then clear the search.

=== STEPS ===
- Open Work Orders in browser tab A.
- Open Work Orders in browser tab B (same user).
- In tab A, in the display switcher at the right of the toolbar, click Tech View (tooltip "Tech View").
- A few seconds later, in tab B, in the display switcher at the right of the toolbar, click Board View (tooltip "Board View").
- Refresh tab A.
- Refresh tab B.
- Open Work Orders in a new tab C.

=== EXPECTED ===
Expected results
- After refreshing, tab A, tab B and the new tab C all open in Board View, the last choice made.
- No warning or conflict message appears in either tab.
