TITLE: Tech View holds one group header at a time under the column headings

=== PRECONDITIONS ===
Setup (manual QA tester and Claude session)

Preconditions
- You are signed in as {User-A} (for example Admin ShopView), an Owner or Admin user whose role has Work orders > View, Work orders > Create & Edit and See financial data, at {Location-A} (for example Staging Heavy Duty - 9919).
- Your browser window is 1440 px wide, with the browser zoom at 100%.
- The design picture DC-22-tech-scrolled-light-1440.png is open beside the app at 100% zoom (actual size); it is attached to this case.
- In the design pictures the names, numbers, dates and amounts are sample data, and the rank numbers (1, 2, 3 …) beside rows and cards are left out of this release, so do not compare those.
- In the design picture only the column header row stays at the top and the group header scrolls away; the PRD keeps the group header on screen, so the case follows the PRD.
- Customer {Customer-A} (for example "ZZAUTOTEST DC22 Sticky Headers") is used by this case only.
- Estimate {Work-order-1} (for example S10043-17858) for {Customer-A} has no lead technician, so it sits in the Unassigned group.
- On the All tab the Unassigned group holds more work orders than fit on the screen (on a QA site it usually holds hundreds).
- At least one technician's group, with work orders in it, comes after Unassigned in Tech View.
- On the Work Orders page nothing is chosen in any filter (if the Asset on Site chip shows Yes or No, click the x on the chip), Assigned to me is off, Search is empty and density is Regular (the default).

=== STEPS ===
- Open Work Orders, click the All tab and click Tech View in the display switcher.
- Scroll down slowly through the Unassigned group until well past its first screen of rows.
- Look at the top of the table.
- Keep scrolling down until the next group's header reaches the Unassigned header.
- Keep scrolling a little further and look at the top of the table.

=== EXPECTED ===
Expected results
- Step 3: the column header row is still at the top of the table, and the Unassigned group header (with its arrow and count) stays visible just under it while the group's rows scroll past, so the group can be collapsed from there.
- Step 5: the next group's header pushes the Unassigned header up and off, and takes its place just under the column header row. Only one group header is held under the column headings at any time.

The design lets the group header scroll away. Chris Ward answered on 15 Oct 2026 (answer 20) that while you scroll through an expanded group its header stays just under the column headings, only one header is held at a time and the next group's header pushes it off, and that Unassigned works the same way. PRD version 54 says so, and this case follows it. The behaviour is also covered by C96935 and C368127.
Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
- S2-R18: “Unassigned opens expanded unless the user collapsed it (S2-R13). While the user scrolls through Unassigned, its header stays on screen as in S2-E1, so it can be collapsed from anywhere in the group. When it holds more work orders than fit on screen, which on the All tab can be thousands, it loads them in pages as the user scrolls, as List does, never all at once.”
- S2-E1: “While the user scrolls through an expanded group, that group’s header stays on screen just under the column headings. Only one group header is held at a time: when the next group’s header reaches it, the next header pushes it off and takes its place. Unassigned’s header works the same way (S2-R18).”
- Design — Work Orders.dc.html, Tech View column header row: “position: sticky; top: 0; z-index: 2; background: var(--sv-surface); border-bottom: 1px solid var(--sv-border-default);”
