TITLE: Tech View offers no pin control while Assigned to me is on

=== PRECONDITIONS ===
Preconditions
- Needs: 1 user · 1 browser · about 15 minutes
- Build: the Work Orders QA build (sv10043, v26.40.8-7a95011). A desktop browser with the window at least 1024 pixels wide (full screen on an ordinary laptop or monitor). Below that width the page shows only List.
- [User-A] ([Me]): an Owner/Admin user who is also a technician (Time Clock on, this location picked in Location) (on a QA branch: the Admin quick-login button on the sign-in page; you appear as "Admin ShopView"). The Admin role holds Work orders > View and Work orders > Create & Edit (to check: your initials at the top right > Settings > Roles & Permissions > Admin > Work orders).
- One location for the whole case, shown in the top bar next to the bell (e.g. "Staging Heavy Duty - 9919"); everything below is created in this location.
- Technicians at this location: [Tech-A] (e.g. "Esther Howard"), [Tech-B] (e.g. "Ralph Edwards") and yourself. A person only counts as a technician here when the staff member is active (not deactivated), Time Clock is on, the Role is neither Office User nor Time Clock User, and this location is picked in their Location field.
- Customer "ZZAUTOTEST F2 No Pin Assigned" (used by this case only), with one contact and one asset, so this case's work orders are kept apart from every other work order at the location.
- Two Approved work orders for that customer: [WO-1] led by you, [WO-2] led by [Tech-B].
- Assigned to me is off at the start. Names and numbers are examples.
Setup
- Sign in with the Admin quick-login button on the sign-in page (you appear as "Admin ShopView"). Check the location in the top bar next to the bell; to change it click your initials at the top right > Change Location.
- Make yourself a technician: Settings > Staff > your own row's edit icon > Time Clock on, with this location picked in Location > Save & Close. Record yourself ("Admin ShopView") as [Me].
- Settings (your initials at the top right > Settings) > Staff: confirm each technician below is active at this location with Time Clock on, or add it: New Staff Member > First Name and Last Name, an Email, Role "Technician", at least one Department (required), Location = this location, Time Clock on > Save & Close. Record them as [Tech-A] (e.g. Esther Howard); [Tech-B] (e.g. Ralph Edwards).
- Top menu Customers > New Customer > Name "ZZAUTOTEST F2 No Pin Assigned" > Save (if it already exists, open it instead). On the customer page click the Contacts tab > New Contact > First Name and Last Name > Save (a new asset needs a contact). Then click the Assets tab > New Asset > pick the Contact, then Year, Make, Model and Unit (e.g. 2022, Freightliner, M2, TRK-118) > Save. Make is required; Unit can be left empty.
- Work Orders > Create Work Order > in the New Work Order window pick Customer "ZZAUTOTEST F2 No Pin Assigned" and its asset > Save. The work order opens on its Lines tab with the New Line form open: in "What Are You Doing?" pick a ready-made line (e.g. "Replace - Brake pot"; the build offers only ready-made lines), turn Line Approved on > Save & Close; the work order becomes Approved. Repeat for two work orders in all; record the numbers as [WO-1] (e.g. S1-702) and [WO-2] (e.g. S1-691) (numbers on this site look like "S10043-17581").
- Set the leads: on an Approved work order pick the technician in the Lead Technician dropdown on the work order page; it saves at once. (While a work order is an Estimate its Lead technician is read-only on the work order page; give an Estimate its lead from Board View instead: the card's More actions (…) > Reassign lead technician > pick the technician > Reassign.) Leave Lead Technician empty on the ones that should sit in Unassigned. Here: [WO-1] = you; [WO-2] = [Tech-B].
- Check the setup worked: Work Orders > All tab > Search ZZAUTOTEST F2 No Pin Assigned — [WO-1] led by you and [WO-2] led by [Tech-B]. If not, set the leads before starting.

=== STEPS ===
- Open Work Orders.
- Click the All tab on the left of the toolbar.
- Click Search and type ZZAUTOTEST F2 No Pin Assigned so only this test's work orders show.
- In the display switcher at the right of the toolbar, click Tech View (tooltip "Tech View").
- Check the pin control shows on the group headers.
- Turn on Assigned to me.
- Look at the headers of every group still shown.
- In the display switcher at the right of the toolbar, click Board View (tooltip "Board View").
- Look at the column headers.
- Turn Assigned to me off.

=== EXPECTED ===
Expected results
- With Assigned to me off, group headers show a pin control.
- With Assigned to me on, no group header in Tech View offers a pin control, the same as Board View.
- Turning Assigned to me off brings the pin controls back.
