TITLE: A refused lead change in Edit Work Order saves none of the other edits

=== PRECONDITIONS ===
Preconditions
- Needs: 1 user · 1 browser · about 15 minutes.
- Sign in as an Admin (on a QA branch: the Admin quick-login button on the sign-in page; you appear as "Admin ShopView"). Desktop browser window at least 1024 px wide; narrower windows show only the List.
- Work in one location throughout, shown next to the bell in the top bar (e.g. "Staging Heavy Duty - 9919"); to change it click your initials (top right) > Change Location. Create everything in that location.
- Technicians [Tech-A] and [Tech-B] (the expected results call them "Esther Howard" and "Ralph Edwards").
- Customer "ZZAUTOTEST Edit WO Refused" (this case only) with a contact and an asset.
- One work order [WO-1] with status Complete (reviewed, not invoiced), led by [Tech-A], Mileage 120,000, Engine Hours 3,420, Customer PO "PO-100".
- Names and numbers are examples, except names the expected results quote, which must be used exactly.
Setup
- Technicians: your initials (top right) > Settings > Staff. A person counts as a technician only if Active, Time Clock on, Role not Office User or Time Clock User, and this location in Location. To add one: New Staff Member > First Name, Last Name, Email, Role "Technician", one Department (required), Location = this location, Time Clock on > Save & Close. You need: two, [Tech-A] and [Tech-B].
- Customer: Customers > New Customer > Name "ZZAUTOTEST Edit WO Refused" > Save. On its Contacts tab > New Contact > First and Last Name > Save. On its Assets tab > New Asset > pick the contact, Year, Make (required), Model, Unit "TRK-118" > Save.
- Work order: Work Orders > Create Work Order > Customer "ZZAUTOTEST Edit WO Refused" > its asset > Save (if a credit-limit Confirmation appears, click Create). In New Line > "What Are You Doing?" type "grease" and pick "Service - Full grease service" (no parts) > Line Approved on > Save & Close. Write down the number (top left) as [WO-1] ("S1-714" in the expected results).
- On [WO-1], in the card at the left, pick [Tech-A] in Lead Technician (it saves at once; it can only be changed once the work order is Approved).
- In the same card type Mileage 120000 and Engine Hours 3420 (each saves when you leave the field). On the Finance tab type "PO-100" in Customer PO.
- Bring [WO-1] to Complete: Lines tab > Complete on the line > type any Tech Story (if "Missing Details" shows, fill it, e.g. a Mileage) > Complete Line; the status reads Review. Click Mark Reviewed (if asked for a VIN, type any 17 characters, e.g. 1FVACWDT1BDAZ0001) > Confirm Review; the status reads Complete.
- Check: reload [WO-1]. Status Complete, Lead Technician [Tech-A], Mileage 120,000, Engine Hours 3,420, Customer PO "PO-100".

=== STEPS ===
- Open [WO-1] (Work Orders > All tab > Search "ZZAUTOTEST Edit WO Refused" > click the work order). Its status reads Complete.
- Click the Finance tab and click Create Invoice so the work order is Invoiced. Close the New Customer Payment window (X) without paying.
- Reload the page. The status reads Invoiced.
- There is no separate Edit Work Order window; the edits are tried on the status card (the card at the left of the work order page that shows the status, Lead Technician, Mileage and Engine Hours).
- On the status card try to change Lead Technician to [Tech-B].
- Try to change Mileage to 120500.
- Try to change Engine Hours to 3470.
- Reload the page and read Lead Technician, Mileage and Engine Hours on the status card.

=== EXPECTED ===
Expected results
- Steps 5 to 7: none of the three can be changed on an Invoiced work order. Lead Technician offers no list of technicians to pick from, and Mileage and Engine Hours cannot be edited (or, if a change is tried, it is refused with a message and not saved).
- Step 8: Lead Technician still reads "Esther Howard", Mileage 120,000 and Engine Hours 3,420. None of the edits was saved.
- Note: the Customer PO on the Finance tab has its own separate save and is not part of this check (QA lead's decision below).
Source — where this behaviour comes from
- Regression for epic SV-10043 (PR #3548); developer's regression list, received 8 Oct 2026, High 1; QA handoff test plan (generated 2026-10-08), §7; PRD Confluence 845185030 (edited 7 Oct 2026) where quoted (§4 Key Decisions). Source-verified 8 October 2026.
- QA lead decision, 9 Oct 2026: there is no separate Edit Work Order window for an existing work order; the lead technician, mileage and engine hours are changed on the card at the left of the work order page.
- QA lead decision, 12 Oct 2026: on an invoiced work order the lead, mileage and engine hours cannot be changed and stay as they were, which passes; the Customer PO is saved separately on the Finance tab and is not part of this check.
Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
- Dev reply High 1: “If the lead change is refused, the other edits in the same save (mileage, engine hours, PO) are not saved either.”
- QA handoff §7: “Edit Work Order on a Paid work order, with a different lead and a changed mileage, is refused”
- PRD §4 Key Decisions (lead lock): “Lead technician cannot be changed once a work order is Invoiced or Paid. This is a product rule, not only a screen rule, so it must hold on every path that changes the lead technician — board drag, Reassign dialog, and the work order detail page.”
