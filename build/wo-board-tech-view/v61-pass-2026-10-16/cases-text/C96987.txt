- Create the customer used only by this case: top menu Customers > New Customer > Name "ZZAUTOTEST F1 Density Choices" > Save. On the customer page click the Contacts tab > New Contact > First Name and Last Name > Save (a new asset needs a contact). Then click the Assets tab > New Asset > pick the Contact, then Year, Make, Model and Unit (e.g. 2022, Freightliner, M2, unit "TRK-118") > Save. Make is required; Unit can be left empty.
- Create these work orders for "ZZAUTOTEST F1 Density Choices": Work Orders > Create Work Order > in the New Work Order window pick the Customer and its Asset > Save.
