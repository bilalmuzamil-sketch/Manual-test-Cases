TITLE: Technician filter sits after Assigned to me in Tech View and Board View

=== PRECONDITIONS ===
Setup (manual QA tester and Claude session)

Preconditions
- You are signed in as {User-A} (for example Admin ShopView), an Owner or Admin user whose role has Work orders > View, Work orders > Create & Edit and See financial data, at {Location-A} (for example Staging Heavy Duty - 9919).
- Your browser window is 1440 px wide, with the browser zoom at 100%.
- The design picture DC-18-tech-technician-chip-open-light-1440.png is open beside the app at 100% zoom (actual size); it is attached to this case.
- In the design pictures the names, numbers, dates and amounts are sample data, and the rank numbers (1, 2, 3 …) beside rows and cards are left out of this release, so do not compare those.
- The design picture also shows a Department filter, which is left out of this release; do not compare it.

- Customer {Customer-A} (for example "ZZAUTOTEST DC18 Technician Filter") is used by this case only.

=== STEPS ===
- Open Work Orders, click the All tab and click Tech View in the display switcher.
- Look at the filter bar from left to right.
- Click the Technician filter.
- Look at the list that opens.
- Tick {Technician-A}.
- Press Escape.
- Click Board View in the display switcher and look at the filter bar.
- Click List in the display switcher and look at the filter bar.

=== EXPECTED ===
Expected results
- Step 2: the Technician filter sits directly after Assigned to me, showing the design's thin outline wrench icon, the word Technician and a down arrow, in the same style as the filters beside it, as in the design picture. There is no Department filter.
- Step 4: a list opens directly below the filter, starting with Unassigned, then the location's technicians by first name then last name, each with a tick box, as in the design picture (the picture has no Unassigned entry; the PRD adds it).
- Step 7: Board View shows the same Technician filter in the same place, still with {Technician-A} chosen.
- Step 8: List's filter bar has no Technician filter.

Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
- S1-R15: “In Tech View and Board View the filter bar also offers a Technician filter, after Assigned to me. It is a multi-select listing Unassigned first, then the location’s technicians (the same people who get a group or column, S9-R4), by first name then last name. Choosing one or more shows only those technicians’ groups or columns; every other group or column, pinned ones included, is hidden. It filters by lead technician only.”
- S1-R15b: “List does not offer the Technician filter and stays as in production. Switching to List while technicians are chosen shows List as if none were chosen; switching back to Tech View or Board View brings the choice back. The choice is remembered the same way as the other filters.”
- S1-R9a: “The switcher uses the design’s icons at 20px: List is Lucide `list`, Tech View is `user-round`, Board View is `square-kanban`. In Tech View and Board View these icons are the design’s: density (`row-height` in Tech View, `card-height` in Board View, custom icons in the design system’s icon set), collapse chevron (`chevron-down`), pin and unpin (`pin` / `pin-off`), Invoiced or Paid lock (`lock`), Unassigned avatar (`user-round`) and the empty state when there are no work orders at all (`clipboard-list`). The filtered no-results state is the List’s, magnifier included (S1-N1). The filter chips and Search use the design file’s icons in every display option, List included, each exactly as the design file draws it; this is the only change to List. Every other List icon is unchanged.”
- Out of Scope: “A Department filter. The design shows one, but a work order’s department is not shown or editable on any work order screen today, so users could not see why a work order matches. Revisit once the work order shows its department.”
