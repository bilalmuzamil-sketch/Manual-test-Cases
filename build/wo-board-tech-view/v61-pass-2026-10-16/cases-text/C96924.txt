TITLE: Tech View groups work orders under each lead with name, avatar and count

=== PRECONDITIONS ===
Preconditions
- Needs: 1 user · 1 browser · about 20 minutes
- Build: the Work Orders QA build (sv10043, v26.40.8-7a95011). A desktop browser with the window at least 1024 pixels wide (full screen on an ordinary laptop or monitor). Below that width the page shows only List.
- [User-A]: an Owner/Admin user (on a QA branch: the Admin quick-login button on the sign-in page; you appear as "Admin ShopView"). The Admin role holds Work orders > View and Work orders > Create & Edit (to check: your initials at the top right > Settings > Roles & Permissions > Admin > Work orders).
- One location for the whole case, shown in the top bar next to the bell (e.g. "Staging Heavy Duty - 9919"); everything below is created in this location.
- Two technicians at this location: [Tech-A] (the expected results call it "Esther Howard") and [Tech-B] (the expected results call it "Ralph Edwards"). A person only counts as a technician here when the staff member is active (not deactivated), Time Clock is on, the Role is neither Office User nor Time Clock User, and this location is picked in their Location field.
- Customer "ZZAUTOTEST F2 Tech Grouping" (used by this case only), with one contact and one asset, so this case's work orders are kept apart from every other work order at the location.
- Six work orders for that customer: [Tech-A] leads three ([WO-1] Approved, [WO-2] Approved, [WO-3] Estimate); [Tech-B] leads two ([WO-4], [WO-5]); [WO-6] has no lead.
- What must hold: [Tech-A] leads exactly two Approved and one Estimate work order for this customer. Names and numbers are examples.
Setup
- Sign in with the Admin quick-login button on the sign-in page (you appear as "Admin ShopView"). Check the location in the top bar next to the bell; to change it click your initials at the top right > Change Location.
- Settings (your initials at the top right > Settings) > Staff: confirm each technician below is active at this location with Time Clock on, or add it: New Staff Member > First Name and Last Name, an Email, Role "Technician", at least one Department (required), Location = this location, Time Clock on > Save & Close. Record them as [Tech-A] (e.g. Esther Howard); [Tech-B] (e.g. Ralph Edwards).
- Top menu Customers > New Customer > Name "ZZAUTOTEST F2 Tech Grouping" > Save (if it already exists, open it instead). On the customer page click the Contacts tab > New Contact > First Name and Last Name > Save (a new asset needs a contact). Then click the Assets tab > New Asset > pick the Contact, then Year, Make, Model and Unit (e.g. 2022, Freightliner, M2, TRK-118) > Save. Make is required; Unit can be left empty.
- Work Orders > Create Work Order > in the New Work Order window pick Customer "ZZAUTOTEST F2 Tech Grouping" and its asset > Save. The work order opens on its Lines tab with the New Line form open: in "What Are You Doing?" pick a ready-made line (e.g. "Replace - Brake pot"; the build offers only ready-made lines), then turn Line Approved on > Save & Close to make it Approved, or leave Line Approved off > Save & Close to keep it an Estimate. Repeat for six work orders in all: [WO-1] and [WO-2] Approved, [WO-3] an Estimate, [WO-4], [WO-5] and [WO-6] Approved. Record each number shown (the expected results call [WO-1] "S1-702", [WO-2] "S1-644" and [WO-3] "S1-455") (numbers on this site look like "S10043-17581").
- Set the leads: on an Approved work order pick the technician in the Lead Technician dropdown on the work order page; it saves at once. (While a work order is an Estimate its Lead technician is read-only on the work order page; give an Estimate its lead from Board View instead: the card's More actions (…) > Reassign lead technician > pick the technician > Reassign.) Leave Lead Technician empty on the ones that should sit in Unassigned. Here: [WO-1], [WO-2] and [WO-3] = [Tech-A]; [WO-4] and [WO-5] = [Tech-B]; [WO-6] no lead.
- Check the setup worked: Work Orders > All tab > Search ZZAUTOTEST F2 Tech Grouping — six work orders show; in List, Lead Technician reads [Tech-A] on [WO-1] to [WO-3], [Tech-B] on [WO-4] and [WO-5], and nothing on [WO-6]; [WO-3] has status Estimate. If not, fix the lead or status before starting.

=== EXPECTED ===
Expected results
- The table is split into groups, one per lead technician, with a visible separator line between groups.
- Each group header shows the technician's name, their avatar (photo or initials) and a number.
- The number equals the rows listed under it. Example: Esther Howard shows 3 with 3 rows (S1-702, S1-644, S1-455), Ralph Edwards shows 2 with 2 rows.
- After filtering to Approved, Esther Howard's number becomes 2 and 2 rows are listed (S1-702, S1-644): 3 - 1 Estimate = 2.
