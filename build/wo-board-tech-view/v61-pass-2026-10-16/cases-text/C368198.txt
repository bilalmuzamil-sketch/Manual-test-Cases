TITLE: With only Invoiced ticked on All, the List sorts newest invoice first

=== PRECONDITIONS ===
Preconditions
- Needs: 1 user · 1 browser · about 15 minutes
- Build: the Work Orders QA build (sv10043, v26.40.8-7a95011), desktop browser, window at least 1024 px wide.
- Role: Owner/Admin. On the sign-in page click the Admin quick-login button (you appear as Admin ShopView). The Admin role has Work orders > View and Create & Edit (check: your initials (top right) > Settings > Roles & Permissions > Admin > edit).
- Location: Staging Heavy Duty - 9919 for the whole case (shown next to the bell; change it with your initials > Change Location).
- View: on Work Orders the List is shown (click List in the display switcher at the right of the toolbar; its tooltip reads "List").
- Customer "ZZAUTOTEST Invoice Order" (used by this case only) with one contact, one asset and two Invoiced work orders: [WO-X] invoiced first, [WO-Y] invoiced at least one minute later.
- Names and numbers are examples.
Setup
- Customers > New Customer > Name "ZZAUTOTEST Invoice Order" > Save (the customer page opens). Contacts tab > New Contact > First Name "ZZAUTOTEST", Last Name "Contact" > Save. Assets tab > New Asset > Contact = ZZAUTOTEST Contact > Year 2022 > Make: type "Freight" and pick Freightliner > Model: type "M2" and pick it > Unit "TRK-118" > Save (Contact and Make are required).
- Work Orders > Create Work Order > New Work Order: Customer = "ZZAUTOTEST Invoice Order" > Asset = its asset > Save. If a Confirmation says the customer is over their credit limit, click Create. The work order opens on its Lines tab with the New Line form open. Record the number shown at the top left as [WO-X] (the expected results call it "S1-790").
- In New Line > What Are You Doing? type "grease" and pick the ready-made line "Service - Full grease service" (it has no parts) > turn Line Approved on > Save & Close.
- Bring it to Complete: on the Lines tab click Complete on the line > Tech Story: type any text > if a "Missing Details (1)" step shows, click it and type a Mileage > Complete Line (or Complete All Lines). The message "Work order sent to review." appears and the status reads Review. Click Mark Reviewed; if a Mark as reviewed window asks for VIN, type any 17 characters (e.g. 1FVACWDT1BDAZ0001) > Confirm Review. The status reads Complete.
- Click the Finance tab > Create Invoice. The status reads Invoiced and a New Customer Payment window opens; close it with the X (no payment).
- Wait one minute, then repeat for a second work order and record it as [WO-Y] (the expected results call it "S1-791").
- Check the setup worked: both work orders read Invoiced; [WO-Y] was invoiced after [WO-X].

=== STEPS ===
- Open Work Orders and click the All tab.
- Click Status and tick Invoiced only.
- Click Column Selection and turn on Invoiced Date.
- Click Search and type "ZZAUTOTEST Invoice Order".
- Read the order of the rows and their Invoiced Date.

=== EXPECTED ===
Expected results
- Y ("S1-791") is above X ("S1-790"): the rows are in Invoiced Date order, newest first.
