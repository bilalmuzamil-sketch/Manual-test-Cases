TITLE: A new line created with a technician keeps that technician

=== PRECONDITIONS ===
Preconditions
- Needs: 1 user · 1 browser · about 10 minutes
- Build: the Work Orders QA build (sv10043, v26.40.8-7a95011), desktop browser, window at least 1024 px wide.
- Role: Owner/Admin. On the sign-in page click the Admin quick-login button (you appear as Admin ShopView). The Admin role has Work orders > View and Create & Edit (check: your initials (top right) > Settings > Roles & Permissions > Admin > edit).
- Location: Staging Heavy Duty - 9919 for the whole case (shown next to the bell; change it with your initials > Change Location).
- 2 technicians eligible at this location: active, Role Technician, at least one Department, Location Staging Heavy Duty - 9919, Time Clock on. Names are examples ("Esther Howard", "Dana Ortiz").
- Customer "ZZAUTOTEST New Line Tech" (used by this case only) with one contact and one asset.
- One work order [WO-1] for that customer with one approved line and Lead Technician = [Tech-A].
- Names and numbers are examples.
Setup
- Your initials (top right) > Settings > Staff: if no suitable technician exists, click New Staff Member > First Name, Last Name, Email > Role = Technician > Departments = any one (at least one Department is required) > Location = Staging Heavy Duty - 9919 > turn Time Clock on > Save & Close. Record the technician as [Tech-A] (the expected results call this technician "Esther Howard").
- Your initials (top right) > Settings > Staff: if no suitable technician exists, click New Staff Member > First Name, Last Name, Email > Role = Technician > Departments = any one (at least one Department is required) > Location = Staging Heavy Duty - 9919 > turn Time Clock on > Save & Close. Record the technician as [Tech-D] (the expected results call this technician "Dana Ortiz"). Use a second, different technician.
- Customers > New Customer > Name "ZZAUTOTEST New Line Tech" > Save (the customer page opens). Contacts tab > New Contact > First Name "ZZAUTOTEST", Last Name "Contact" > Save. Assets tab > New Asset > Contact = ZZAUTOTEST Contact > Year 2022 > Make: type "Freight" and pick Freightliner > Model: type "M2" and pick it > Unit "TRK-118" > Save (Contact and Make are required).
- Work Orders > Create Work Order > New Work Order: Customer = "ZZAUTOTEST New Line Tech" > Asset = its asset > Save. If a Confirmation says the customer is over their credit limit, click Create. The work order opens on its Lines tab with the New Line form open. Record the number shown at the top left as [WO-1] (the expected results call it "S1-720").
- In New Line > What Are You Doing? type "grease" and pick the ready-made line "Service - Full grease service" (it has no parts) > turn Line Approved on > Save & Close. The status reads Approved.
- On [WO-1], on the status card at the left, click Lead Technician and pick [Tech-A]. (Lead Technician is a dropdown only once the work order is Approved; while it is an Estimate it reads "Unassigned" and cannot be changed.) The choice saves at once.
- Check the setup worked: reload [WO-1]. Lead Technician reads [Tech-A] and the Lines tab shows one line.

=== STEPS ===
- Open [WO-1] and click the Lines tab.
- Click New Line.
- In What Are You Doing? type "ZZAUTOTEST Oil change", wait until the list under it reads "No results", then press Enter (if a ready-made line is offered instead, change a word until it reads "No results"). In Estimated Time type 1.
- In Technicians click Add Technician and pick [Tech-D].
- Click Save & Close.
- Reload the page and read the new line's Labor row.

=== EXPECTED ===
Expected results
- Step 5: the line saves with no error message.
- Step 6: the new line shows "Dana Ortiz" as its technician.
