TITLE: The Clear shifts question ignores lines that are not approved or are declined

=== PRECONDITIONS ===
Setup (manual QA tester and Claude session)

Preconditions
- You are signed in as an Owner or Admin user whose role has Work orders > View and Work orders > Create & Edit and Schedule access, at {Location-A} (for example Staging Heavy Duty - 9919).
- Your browser window is at least 1024 px wide, so the display switcher (List, Tech View, Board View) shows at the right of the Work Orders toolbar.
- Technician {Technician-A} (for example Esther Howard) is an active staff member at {Location-A} with Time Clock on, the Service department, and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User.
- Technician {Technician-B} (for example Ralph Edwards) is an active staff member at {Location-A} with Time Clock on, the Service department, and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User.
- Customer {Customer-A} (for example "ZZAUTOTEST S4R28 Approved Lines") is used by this case only.
- Work order {Work-order-1} (for example S10043-17641) for {Customer-A} is led by {Technician-A} and has an approved line {Line-1} (for example "Service - Full grease service") and a line that is not approved, {Line-2} (for example "Service - Adjust clutch"), saved with Line Approved off.
- Work order {Work-order-2} (for example S10043-17642) for {Customer-A} is led by {Technician-A} and has an approved line {Line-3} (for example "Service - Full grease service") and a declined line {Line-4} (for example "Service - Adjust clutch").
- Work order {Work-order-3} (for example S10043-17643) for {Customer-A} is led by {Technician-A} and has two approved lines, {Line-5} (for example "Service - Full grease service") and {Line-6} (for example "Service - Adjust clutch").
- {Technician-A} has a shift tomorrow 8:00–10:00 on {Work-order-1} that covers {Line-1} and not {Line-2}.
- {Technician-A} has a shift tomorrow 10:00–12:00 on {Work-order-2} that covers {Line-3} and not {Line-4}.
- {Technician-A} has a shift tomorrow 13:00–15:00 on {Work-order-3} made with Choose lines, with both {Line-5} and {Line-6} ticked.
- No other shift is booked on {Work-order-1}, {Work-order-2} or {Work-order-3}.
- On the Work Orders page nothing is ticked in Status, Asset on Site shows no choice (if its chip shows Yes or No, click the x on the chip), Assigned to me is off and Search is empty.

=== STEPS ===
- Open Work Orders, click the All tab and click Board View in the display switcher.
- Type the name of {Customer-A} in Search.
- Drag {Work-order-1} from {Technician-A}'s column into {Technician-B}'s column.
- Read the title of the question that opens.
- Click Keep shifts.
- Drag {Work-order-2} from {Technician-A}'s column into {Technician-B}'s column.
- Read the title of the question that opens.
- Click Keep shifts.
- Drag {Work-order-3} from {Technician-A}'s column into {Technician-B}'s column.
- Read the title of the question that opens.
- Click Keep shifts.
- Click Schedule in the top menu.
- Use the arrow next to Today to go to tomorrow.
- Read {Technician-A}'s shifts on {Work-order-1}, {Work-order-2} and {Work-order-3}.

=== EXPECTED ===
Expected results
- Steps 4, 7 and 10: each drag opens the question "Clear {Technician-A}'s scheduled shifts?" (with the example name: "Clear Esther Howard's scheduled shifts?"). The line that is not approved ({Line-2}) and the declined line ({Line-4}) are ignored, and a Choose lines shift that ticks every approved line counts.
- Steps 5, 8 and 11: after Keep shifts each work order moves to {Technician-B}'s column with the message "Lead technician updated".
- Step 14: all three of {Technician-A}'s shifts are still on the Schedule tomorrow, at the same times.

Source-verified 14 October 2026. Not yet checked against a build.
Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
- S4-R28: “The shift-clearing prompt (S4-R11, S4-R26) shows only when the outgoing lead has a work-order-level shift on this work order that has not ended. A work-order-level shift is one that covers every approved line of the work order, judged at the moment the lead changes; this is what the Schedule's Entire work order drop creates. Lines not yet approved, and declined lines, are ignored. On a work order with one line, a shift on that line counts, and so does a Choose lines shift that ticks every approved line. A shift on only some approved lines does not show the prompt, and that includes an Entire work order shift once a line has been added and approved after it was made.”
- S4-R29: “The prompt reads: title “Clear {Name}'s scheduled shifts?” (with no name: “Clear the lead technician's scheduled shifts?”); text “{Name} has shifts scheduled for this whole work order. Clearing removes shifts that haven't started and ends one that's under way now. Shifts on individual lines and recorded time don't change.”; buttons “Keep shifts”, “Clear shifts” and “Cancel”.”
- S4-R23: “If the user chooses to keep shifts, change the lead technician and retain the existing shifts.”
- S4-R5: “After successful reassignment, show “Lead technician updated”; after unassigning, show “Lead technician removed”.”
