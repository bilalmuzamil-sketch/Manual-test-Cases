TITLE: Assigned to me shows only columns with my work orders and hides pinning

=== PRECONDITIONS ===
Preconditions
- Needs: 1 user · 1 browser · about 20 minutes
- Build: the Work Orders QA build (sv10043, v26.40.8-7a95011). A desktop browser with the window at least 1024 pixels wide (full screen on an ordinary laptop or monitor). Below that width the page shows only List.
- [User-A] (you): an Owner/Admin user who is also a technician (Time Clock on, this location picked in Location) (on a QA branch: the Admin quick-login button on the sign-in page; you appear as "Admin ShopView"). The Admin role holds Work orders > View and Work orders > Create & Edit (to check: your initials at the top right > Settings > Roles & Permissions > Admin > Work orders).
- One location for the whole case, shown in the top bar next to the bell (e.g. "Staging Heavy Duty - 9919"); everything below is created in this location.
- Technicians "Esther Howard", "Ralph Edwards" and "Jenny Wilson" at this location (the expected results name them, so keep these names), plus yourself. A person only counts as a technician here when the staff member is active (not deactivated), Time Clock is on, the Role is neither Office User nor Time Clock User, and this location is picked in their Location field.
- Customer "ZZAUTOTEST F2 Board Assigned" (used by this case only), with one contact and one asset, so this case's work orders are kept apart from every other work order at the location.
- Three Approved work orders for that customer: [WO-1] led by you, [WO-2] led by Ralph Edwards, [WO-3] with no lead but with you as its Service Advisor; nothing for Jenny Wilson.
- Jenny Wilson is pinned in Board View by you.
Setup
- Sign in with the Admin quick-login button on the sign-in page (you appear as "Admin ShopView"). Check the location in the top bar next to the bell; to change it click your initials at the top right > Change Location.
- Make yourself a technician: Settings > Staff > your own row's edit icon > Time Clock on, with this location picked in Location > Save & Close.
- Settings (your initials at the top right > Settings) > Staff: confirm each technician below is active at this location with Time Clock on, or add it: New Staff Member > First Name and Last Name, an Email, Role "Technician", at least one Department (required), Location = this location, Time Clock on > Save & Close. Record them as "Esther Howard", "Ralph Edwards" and "Jenny Wilson" (keep these names).
- Top menu Customers > New Customer > Name "ZZAUTOTEST F2 Board Assigned" > Save (if it already exists, open it instead). On the customer page click the Contacts tab > New Contact > First Name and Last Name > Save (a new asset needs a contact). Then click the Assets tab > New Asset > pick the Contact, then Year, Make, Model and Unit (e.g. 2022, Freightliner, M2, TRK-118) > Save. Make is required; Unit can be left empty.
- Work Orders > Create Work Order > in the New Work Order window pick Customer "ZZAUTOTEST F2 Board Assigned" and its asset > Save. The work order opens on its Lines tab with the New Line form open: in "What Are You Doing?" pick a ready-made line (e.g. "Replace - Brake pot"; the build offers only ready-made lines), turn Line Approved on > Save & Close; the work order becomes Approved. Repeat for three work orders in all; record the numbers as [WO-1] (the expected results call it "S1-702"), [WO-2] (e.g. S1-691) and [WO-3] (the expected results call it "S1-352") (numbers on this site look like "S10043-17581").
- Set the leads: on an Approved work order pick the technician in the Lead Technician dropdown on the work order page; it saves at once. (While a work order is an Estimate its Lead technician is read-only on the work order page; give an Estimate its lead from Board View instead: the card's More actions (…) > Reassign lead technician > pick the technician > Reassign.) Leave Lead Technician empty on the ones that should sit in Unassigned. Here: [WO-1] = you; [WO-2] = Ralph Edwards; [WO-3] no lead.
- On [WO-3] set the Service Advisor to yourself so an unassigned work order is "assigned to me" (if List with Assigned to me on does not show it, assign yourself as technician on one of its lines instead).
- Pin Jenny Wilson in Board View before you start.
- Check the setup worked: Work Orders > All tab > Search ZZAUTOTEST F2 Board Assigned — [WO-1] led by you, [WO-2] led by Ralph Edwards, [WO-3] with no lead; in Board View Jenny Wilson's column shows a filled pin. If not, fix it before starting.

=== STEPS ===
- Open Work Orders.
- Click the All tab on the left of the toolbar, click Search and type ZZAUTOTEST F2 Board Assigned.
- In the display switcher at the right of the toolbar, click Board View (tooltip "Board View").
- Turn on Assigned to me.
- Read every column shown.
- Look at the column headers for a pin control.
- Turn Assigned to me off.

=== EXPECTED ===
Expected results
- With Assigned to me on, Board View shows only the Unassigned column (holding S1-352) and your own column (holding S1-702).
- Ralph Edwards, Esther Howard and the pinned Jenny Wilson are hidden.
- No column header offers a pin control while Assigned to me is on.
- Turning Assigned to me off brings back every column and the pin controls, with Jenny Wilson still pinned.
