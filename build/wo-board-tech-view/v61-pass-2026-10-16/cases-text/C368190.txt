TITLE: Removing a line's only shift puts its Labor row back to Unassigned

=== PRECONDITIONS ===
Preconditions
- Needs: 1 user · 1 browser · about 10 minutes
- Build: the Work Orders QA build (sv10043, v26.40.8-7a95011), desktop browser, window at least 1024 px wide.
- Role: Owner/Admin. On the sign-in page click the Admin quick-login button (you appear as Admin ShopView). The Admin role has Work orders > View and Create & Edit (check: your initials (top right) > Settings > Roles & Permissions > Admin > edit).
- Location: Staging Heavy Duty - 9919 for the whole case (shown next to the bell; change it with your initials > Change Location).
- Schedule access: the Admin role has Schedule > View and Create & Edit (Settings > Roles & Permissions > Admin > edit).
- One technician [Tech-J] in the Service department at this location. Name is an example ("Jenny Wilson").
- Customer "ZZAUTOTEST Shift Removed" (used by this case only) with one contact and one asset.
- One Approved work order [WO-1] with NO lead technician and two approved lines with no labor and no technician; Line 1 = "Service - Full grease service".
- Tomorrow [Tech-J] has one shift on [WO-1] made with Choose lines and only Line 1 ticked (8 AM, 2h).
Setup
- Your initials (top right) > Settings > Staff > New Staff Member: First Name, Last Name, Email > Role = Technician > Departments = Service (only technicians in the Service department show as rows on the Schedule) > Location = Staging Heavy Duty - 9919 > turn Time Clock on > Save & Close. Record the technician as [Tech-J] (the expected results call this technician "Jenny Wilson").
- Customers > New Customer > Name "ZZAUTOTEST Shift Removed" > Save (the customer page opens). Contacts tab > New Contact > First Name "ZZAUTOTEST", Last Name "Contact" > Save. Assets tab > New Asset > Contact = ZZAUTOTEST Contact > Year 2022 > Make: type "Freight" and pick Freightliner > Model: type "M2" and pick it > Unit "TRK-118" > Save (Contact and Make are required).
- Work Orders > Create Work Order > New Work Order: Customer = "ZZAUTOTEST Shift Removed" > Asset = its asset > Save. If a Confirmation says the customer is over their credit limit, click Create. The work order opens on its Lines tab with the New Line form open. Record the number shown at the top left as [WO-1] (the expected results call it "S1-770").
- In New Line > What Are You Doing? type "grease" and pick the ready-made line "Service - Full grease service" (it has no parts) > turn Line Approved on > Save & Add Line. In New Line > What Are You Doing? type "clutch" and pick the ready-made line "Service - Adjust clutch" (it has no parts) > turn Line Approved on > Save & Close. Leave Lead Technician empty.
- Click Schedule in the top menu > click the > arrow next to Today once so the date reads tomorrow. In Search work orders (left) type [WO-1]. Drag its card onto [Tech-J]'s row (scroll the rows down to find it) and drop it under 8 AM. In the window that opens click Choose lines, tick only "Service - Full grease service", use − / + until Hours reads 2h and click the Create button (it reads "Create 1 shift" or "Create shift · 1 line · …").
- Check the setup worked: tomorrow [Tech-J]'s row shows a block named "Service - Full grease service"; click it: the details show [WO-1] and "1 Single line". Close the details (X).

=== STEPS ===
- Open [WO-1], click the Lines tab and read Line 1's Labor row.
- Click Schedule in the top menu, click the > arrow next to Today once (tomorrow), click [Tech-J]'s shift on [WO-1] and click the delete icon (Delete shift) at the top of its details. It is removed at once, with no question.
- Open [WO-1] again, click the Lines tab and read Line 1's Labor row.

=== EXPECTED ===
Expected results
- Step 1: Line 1's Labor row shows "Jenny Wilson".
- Step 3: Line 1's Labor row reads "Unassigned" again (no scheduled technician, no labor technician).
