• No Google Analytics access is needed. You read what the Work Orders page sends to Google Analytics in the browser's own developer tools (Chrome or Edge): press F12, click the Network tab, tick Preserve log, and type collect in the filter box. Each analytics message shows as a request named collect; click it and open its Payload tab. Each event in it lists: en (the event name), ep.element_location (the display: list, tech_view or board_view), ep.element_label (the field or the density), ep.setting_source (default = never changed, saved = chosen earlier) and uid (the user). One request can hold several events, one per line.
  • Turn off any ad blocker or privacy extension for this site, and use a new private window for each user, so the messages are not stopped or mixed up.
