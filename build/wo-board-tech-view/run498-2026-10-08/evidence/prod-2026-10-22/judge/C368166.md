## Preconditions
Preconditions
- Needs: 1 user · 1 browser · about 10 minutes
- Build: the Work Orders QA build (sv10043, v26.40.8-7a95011), desktop browser, window at least 1024 px wide.
- Role: Owner/Admin. On the sign-in page click the Admin quick-login button (you appear as Admin ShopView). The Admin role has Work orders > View and Create & Edit (check: your initials (top right) > Settings > Roles & Permissions > Admin > edit).

- 2 technicians eligible at this location: active, Role Technician, at least one Department, Location Staging Heavy Duty - 9919, Time Clock on. Names are examples ("Esther Howard", "Ralph Edwards").
- Customer "ZZAUTOTEST Lead Change Page" (used by this case only) with one contact and one asset.
- One Approved work order [WO-1] for that customer with one approved line and Lead Technician = [Tech-A].
- Names and numbers are examples.
