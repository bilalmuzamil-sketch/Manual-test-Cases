# C368212 — The List at one location shows no work orders from another location
Test in run 498: https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/tests/view/3792533

## Preconditions
Preconditions
- Needs: 1 user · 1 browser · about 15 minutes
- Build: the Work Orders QA build (sv10043, v26.40.8-7a95011), desktop browser, window at least 1024 px wide.
- Role: Owner/Admin. On the sign-in page click the Admin quick-login button (you appear as Admin ShopView). The Admin role has Work orders > View and Create & Edit (check: your initials (top right) > Settings > Roles & Permissions > Admin > edit).
- Locations: your usual location Staging Heavy Duty - 9919 and the second location Staging Lethbridge - 4310 (your initials > Change Location lists both).
- View: on Work Orders the List is shown (click List in the display switcher at the right of the toolbar; its tooltip reads "List").
- Customer "ZZAUTOTEST Loc2 Customer" with one contact and one asset, and one work order [WO-L2] for it created while Staging Lethbridge - 4310 was the chosen location.
- Numbers are examples.
Setup
- Customers > New Customer > Name "ZZAUTOTEST Loc2 Customer" > Save (the customer page opens). Contacts tab > New Contact > First Name "ZZAUTOTEST", Last Name "Contact" > Save. Assets tab > New Asset > Contact = ZZAUTOTEST Contact > Year 2022 > Make: type "Freight" and pick Freightliner > Model: type "M2" and pick it > Unit "TRK-118" > Save (Contact and Make are required).
- Your initials (top right) > Change Location > Staging Lethbridge - 4310 (the name next to the bell changes).
- Work Orders > Create Work Order > New Work Order: Customer = "ZZAUTOTEST Loc2 Customer" > Asset = its asset > Save. If a Confirmation says the customer is over their credit limit, click Create. The work order opens on its Lines tab with the New Line form open. Record the number shown at the top left as [WO-L2] (the expected results call it "S2-501").
- Your initials > Change Location > Staging Heavy Duty - 9919.
- Check the setup worked: the name next to the bell reads Staging Heavy Duty - 9919.

## Steps
- At your usual location (Staging Heavy Duty - 9919) open Work Orders and click the All tab.
- Click Search and type "Loc2".
- Clear the search and look through the list for [WO-L2] (or search its number).
- Your initials > Change Location > Staging Lethbridge - 4310, open Work Orders, click All and search "Loc2".

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- Steps 2 and 3: "S2-501" does not appear at your usual location.
- Step 4: "S2-501" does appear at the second location, so the record exists and is only hidden where it does not belong.
