# C598326 — Design: List status labels keep equal widths and Tech View uses the same widths
Test in run 498: https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/tests/view/4004068

## Preconditions
Setup (manual QA tester and Claude session)
Preconditions
- You are signed in as an Owner or Admin user whose role has Work orders > View and Work orders > Create & Edit, at {Location-A} (for example Staging Heavy Duty - 9919).
- Your browser window is 1440 px wide, with the browser zoom at 100%.
- Technician {Technician-A} (for example ZZAUTOTEST S6 Pia Pills) is an active staff member at {Location-A} with Time Clock on and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User.
- Customer {Customer-A} (for example "ZZAUTOTEST S6 Pill Widths") is used by this case only and has two work orders led by {Technician-A}: {Work-order-1} (for example S10043-17781), Approved, and {Work-order-2} (for example S10043-17782), Ready For Review.
- On the Work Orders page nothing is ticked in Status, Asset on Site shows no choice (if its chip shows Yes or No, click the x on the chip), Assigned to me is off and Search is empty.

## Steps
- Open Work Orders, click the All tab and click List in the display switcher.
- Type the name of {Customer-A} in Search.
- Look at the status pills of {Work-order-1} (Approved) and {Work-order-2} (Ready For Review), one above the other in the Status column, and compare their widths.
- Click Tech View in the display switcher.
- Look at the same two pills in {Technician-A}'s group and compare their widths with List's.
- Click the density button and choose Compact, and look at the two pills.
- Click the density button and choose Comfortable, and look at the two pills.
- Click the density button and choose Regular.
- Click List in the display switcher and look at the two pills again.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- Step 3: the two pills are the same width: the Approved pill is as wide as the Ready For Review pill, as the pills down the Status column are in production today. Neither pill is narrowed to fit its own word.
- Step 5: in Tech View the two pills have the same widths as in List at step 3.
- Steps 6 and 7: changing density changes only the pills' height (16 px at Compact, 22 px at Comfortable); their widths stay the same as in List.
- Step 9: List's pills look exactly as at step 3; Tech View's density did not change them.
Source — where this behaviour comes from
Epic SV-10043; stories SV-10049 (Story 6, Density), SV-10045 (Story 2, Tech View); PRD "Work Orders — Board View & Tech View Display Options", Confluence 845185030, version 54 (15 Oct 2026, Chris Ward), S6-R2a and S2-R19, read 15 Oct 2026. S6-R2a was reworded in version 53 on 15 Oct 2026 (Chris Ward ruling on Slavcho Mitrov's question on SV-11310, from production screenshots: List's status pills must match production's sizes exactly, including the matching widths down the column, and Tech View's pill takes the same widths). Related open ticket: SV-11310 (staging sizes each pill to its text). Last checked against build v26.40.19-72f4f3b (staging) on 10/16/2026. Product owner answers: Chris Ward, WO Board and Tech View question sheet (https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1gBGmNB4B1bRrD2ZJvBn1tuvbfxFPjcsKddz_I5H3CvU), answer 15, read 15 Oct 2026. Source-verified 15 October 2026.

Tech View’s pill takes the same widths as List’s; only its height follows density.”
