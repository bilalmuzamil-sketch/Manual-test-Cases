## Steps
- Open Work Orders, click the All tab and pick Board View in the display switcher (right side of the toolbar; tooltip "Board View").
- Type "ZZAUTOTEST F1 Drag Moves Open Lines" in Search.
- Drag [WO-1]'s card from [Tech-A]'s column into [Tech-B]'s column.
- If a "Clear …'s scheduled shifts?" prompt opens, click Keep shifts.
- Open the work order (click the card) and go to the Lines tab.
- Read the Labor technician of each of the six lines and whether Line 6's clock is still running.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- Step 6: Line 1 Unassigned now shows Ralph Edwards (a line with no technician moves to the new lead).
- Step 6: Line 2 Implicit now shows Ralph Edwards (a line following the old lead moves).
- Step 6: Line 3 Explicit still shows Dana Ortiz.
- Step 6: Line 4 Complete still shows Esther Howard.
- Step 6: Line 5 Logged still shows Esther Howard.
- Step 6: Line 6 Clocked still shows Esther Howard and her clock is still running.
- Steps 3 and 6: So exactly 2 of the 6 lines moved (Lines 1 and 2); each line was judged on its own, and wherever a line was Complete, explicit, had labor or a running clock it stayed, even though it also followed the old lead. The drag applied the same rules as the Reassign dialog.
