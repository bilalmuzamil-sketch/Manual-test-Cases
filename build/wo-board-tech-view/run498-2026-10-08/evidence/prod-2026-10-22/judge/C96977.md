# C96977 — Work order number, unit number and status cannot be turned off on cards
Test in run 498: https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/tests/view/3481921

## Preconditions
Preconditions
- Needs: 1 user · 1 browser · about 10 minutes.
- Sign in as an Admin (on a QA branch: the Admin quick-login button on the sign-in page; you appear as "Admin ShopView"). Desktop browser window at least 1024 px wide; narrower windows show only the List.
- Work in one location throughout, shown next to the bell in the top bar (e.g. "Staging Heavy Duty - 9919"); to change it click your initials (top right) > Change Location. Create everything in that location.
- Customer "ZZAUTOTEST F2 Card Always On" (this case only) with an asset whose Unit is "TRK-118", and one Approved work order [WO-1] on it led by a technician.
- Names and numbers are examples, except names the expected results quote, which must be used exactly.
Setup
- Technicians: your initials (top right) > Settings > Staff. A person counts as a technician only if Active, Time Clock on, Role not Office User or Time Clock User, and this location in Location. To add one: New Staff Member > First Name, Last Name, Email, Role "Technician", one Department (required), Location = this location, Time Clock on > Save & Close. You need: one, [Tech-A] (e.g. "Esther Howard").
- Customer: Customers > New Customer > Name "ZZAUTOTEST F2 Card Always On" > Save. On its Contacts tab > New Contact > First and Last Name > Save. On its Assets tab > New Asset > pick the contact, Year, Make (required), Model, Unit "TRK-118" > Save.
- Work orders for "ZZAUTOTEST F2 Card Always On": Work Orders > Create Work Order > pick the Customer and its Asset > Save (if a credit-limit Confirmation appears, click Create). In the New Line form that opens, type in "What Are You Doing?" and pick a ready-made line (e.g. "Replace - Brake pot"), turn Line Approved on > Save & Close: the status reads Approved. Write down the work order number (top left). Then pick the lead in Lead Technician on the card at the left of the work order page; it saves at once.- [WO-1] (e.g. "S1-702"): lead = [Tech-A].
- Check: Work Orders > All tab > Search "ZZAUTOTEST F2 Card Always On" shows [WO-1] with unit TRK-118, status Approved and lead [Tech-A].

## Steps
- Open Work Orders, click the All tab and pick Board View in the display switcher (right side of the toolbar; tooltip "Board View").
- Type ZZAUTOTEST F2 Card Always On in Search.
- Open the Fields to display menu in the toolbar and read the list of fields. Look for Work order number, Unit number and Status.
- Turn off every field in the list that can be turned off.
- Close the menu and read the [WO-1] card.
- Open Fields to display again, turn the fields back on as they were, and close it.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- There is no way to turn off Work order number, Unit number or Status. Either they are not in the Fields to display list at all, or they are listed as always on and cannot be unticked. Both are correct (QA lead's decision below).
- With every other field turned off, the card still shows the work order number (e.g. "S1-702"), the unit (e.g. "TRK-118") and the status (e.g. "Approved").
Source — where this behaviour comes from
- Epic SV-10043; story SV-10048 (Story 5, Fields to display (Board View) and columns (Tech View)); PRD Confluence 845185030, edited 7 Oct 2026 (status line "PRD v33"), S5-R3, read 8 Oct 2026. S3-R4 (cards include the mandatory fields). Source-verified 8 October 2026.
- QA lead decision, 12 Oct 2026: leaving Work order number, Unit number and Status out of Fields to display is accepted, because they can never be turned off.
Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
- S5-R3: “Work order number, unit number when present, and status are mandatory and cannot be deselected.”
