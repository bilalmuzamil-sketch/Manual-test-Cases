## Steps
- Click Dashboard in the top menu (on this build: click the ShopView logo at the top left; it opens /dashboard).
- Find each card that shows a table (e.g. At Risk Customers; also Efficiency by Tech, Billing Efficiency by Advisor, Effective Labor Rate (ELR) by Advisor, Utilization by Tech).
- Read its rows and, where offered, click a column header to sort.
