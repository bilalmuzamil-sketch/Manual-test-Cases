## Steps
- Open Work Orders.
- Click the All tab on the left of the toolbar.
- Click Search and type ZZAUTOTEST F2 Card Required Fields so only this test's work orders show.
- In the display switcher at the right of the toolbar, click Board View (tooltip "Board View").
- Open the Fields to display picker (same toolbar place as the Column Selection button in List and Tech View).
- Try to turn off work order number, unit number and status.
- Turn off every other field and close the picker.
- Read the [WO-1] and [WO-2] cards.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- Work order number, unit number and status cannot be turned off in the picker.
- With every other field off, the S1-702 card still shows S1-702, TRK-118 and its status.
- The S1-472 card shows S1-472 and its status, and no unit number because it has none.
- The design has no Fields to display picker on Board View: if the build has none, it is a fail.
Source — where this behaviour comes from
Epic SV-10043; story SV-10046 (Story 3, Board View — board by lead technician); PRD Confluence 845185030, edited 7 Oct 2026 (status line "PRD v33"), S3-R4, S5-R3, read 8 Oct 2026. Design: Claude Design export of 8 Oct 2026 (Work Orders.dc.html), Board toolbar (no Fields to display picker in the design); on-screen labels only.
Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
- S3-R4: “Board View cards include the mandatory fields specified in S5-R3.”
- S5-R3: “Work order number, unit number when present, and status are mandatory and cannot be deselected.”
