# C96970 — Drops are refused on an inactive technician and one removed from this location
Test in run 498: https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/tests/view/3481914

## Preconditions
Setup (manual QA tester and Claude session)
Preconditions
- You are signed in as an Owner or Admin user whose role has Work orders > View and Work orders > Create & Edit, at {Location-A} (for example Staging Heavy Duty - 9919).
- Your browser window is at least 1024 px wide, so the display switcher (List, Tech View, Board View) shows at the right of the Work Orders toolbar.
- Technician {Technician-B} (for example Ralph Edwards) is an active staff member at {Location-A} with Time Clock on and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User.
- Customer {Customer-A} (for example "ZZAUTOTEST C96970 Refused Drops") is used by this case only.
- Approved work order {Work-order-1} (for example S10043-17631) for {Customer-A} is led by technician {Technician-A} (for example ZZAUTOTEST Ina Inactive).
- Approved work order {Work-order-2} (for example S10043-17632) for {Customer-A} is led by {Technician-B}.
- Approved work order {Work-order-3} (for example S10043-17633) for {Customer-A} is led by technician {Technician-C} (for example ZZAUTOTEST Mona Moved).
- After the work orders were given their leads, {Technician-A}'s staff account was deactivated.
- You pinned {Technician-C} in Board View, and after that {Location-A} was removed from {Technician-C}'s Location field. {Technician-C} is still active.
- On the Work Orders page nothing is ticked in Status, Asset on Site shows no choice (if its chip shows Yes or No, click the x on the chip), Assigned to me is off and Search is empty.

## Steps
- Open Work Orders, click the All tab and click Board View in the display switcher.
- Type the name of {Customer-A} in Search.
- Read the column headers of {Technician-A} and {Technician-C}.
- Drag {Work-order-2} from {Technician-B}'s column onto {Technician-A}'s column and let go.
- Read which column {Work-order-2} is in.
- Drag {Work-order-2} from {Technician-B}'s column onto {Technician-C}'s column and let go.
- Read the message that appears and where {Work-order-2} is.
- Click Tech View in the display switcher.
- Read the group headers of {Technician-A} and {Technician-C}.
- Drag {Work-order-2}'s row from {Technician-B}'s group onto {Technician-A}'s group and let go.
- Drag {Work-order-2}'s row from {Technician-B}'s group onto {Technician-C}'s group (the group holding {Work-order-3}) and let go.
- Read which group {Work-order-2} is in.
- Click {Work-order-2} to open it and read its Lead Technician.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- Step 3: {Technician-A}'s column is still shown with {Work-order-1} and is marked inactive.
- Step 3: {Technician-C} is not among the pinned columns. {Technician-C}'s column appears further along as a normal column holding {Work-order-3}, and it has no inactive marker.
- Step 5: the drop is not accepted. {Work-order-2} goes back to {Technician-B}'s column.
- Step 7: the drop is refused with the message "This technician can't be assigned work orders." and {Work-order-2} stays in {Technician-B}'s column.
- Step 9: in Tech View {Technician-A}'s group is still shown with {Work-order-1} and is marked inactive.
- Steps 10 to 12: neither drop is accepted. {Work-order-2} stays in {Technician-B}'s group.
- Step 13: the Lead Technician of {Work-order-2} is still {Technician-B}.
Source — where this behaviour comes from
Epic SV-10043; stories SV-10047 (Story 4, Reassign or unassign the lead technician), SV-10046 (Story 3, Board View) and SV-10045 (Story 2, Tech View); PRD "Work Orders — Board View & Tech View Display Options", Confluence 845185030, version 49 (14 Oct 2026, Chris Ward), S4-N5 (widened in version 49) and S3-R20c (added in version 49), both from Chris Ward's answer 2 of 14 Oct 2026 to our question sheet; S3-N2, S3-N4, S3-R20b, S2-N2 and S2-N3; read 14 Oct 2026. Sasha Grosman's answer to engineering of 24 Sep 2026 treated a technician who is no longer at this location like an inactive one; PRD version 49 (14 Oct 2026) says that column shows no inactive marker, and we take the later PRD as prevailing. Source-verified 14 October 2026. This version of the case has not yet been checked against a build (the previous version was last checked against build v26.40.8-7a95011 on 10/8/2026).
Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
- S3-N2: “Keep matching work visible in its deactivated technician’s column.”
- S3-N4: “Show an inactive indicator on deactivated technician columns.”
- S3-R20b: “A pinned technician who is not enrolled at this location gets no column here. If they still lead work orders at this location, they appear as a normal, unpinned column holding that work.”
- S3-R20c: “The column of S3-R20b takes no drops. Dropping a work order on it is refused with "This technician can't be assigned work orders." and the work order stays where it was. The column shows no inactive marker, because the technician is not deactivated (S3-N4). Once it holds no work orders, the column is no longer shown.”
- S4-N5: “Nothing can be dropped onto the column/group of an inactive technician (S2-N2, S3-N2) or of a technician who is not enrolled at this location (S3-R20c).”
- S2-N2: “Keep matching work visible under its deactivated lead technician.”
- S2-N3: “Indicate deactivated technicians as inactive in group headers.”
Last checked against build v26.40.17-ddb9ba1 (staging) on 10/14/2026.
