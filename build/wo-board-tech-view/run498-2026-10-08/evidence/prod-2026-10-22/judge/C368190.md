## Steps
- Open [WO-1], click the Lines tab and read Line 1's Labor row.
- Click Schedule in the top menu, click the > arrow next to Today once (tomorrow), click [Tech-J]'s shift on [WO-1] and click the delete icon (Delete shift) at the top of its details. It is removed at once, with no question.
- Open [WO-1] again, click the Lines tab and read Line 1's Labor row.
