Preconditions
- You are signed in with the "Admin" role at {Location-A}, in a browser window at least 1024 px wide.
For example: {Location-A} is Staging Heavy Duty - 9919.
- Technicians {Technician-A} and {Technician-B} are active at {Location-A}, and "Line technicians" is on in Board View's "Fields to display".
For example: ZZAUTOTEST C682438 Ana and ZZAUTOTEST C682438 Ben.
- Customer {Customer-A} has an Approved work order {Work-order-1} led by {Technician-A}, with {Technician-B} on one of its lines.
For example: {Customer-A} is ZZAUTOTEST C682438 Avatar Keys.
- Starting screen: "Work Orders" > "All" tab, "Board View" shown, the name of {Customer-A} in "Search", nothing focused yet.

## Steps
- Click once in the "Search" box.
- Press Tab until a card shows the focus ring.
- Use the arrow keys to move the focus ring to the card for {Work-order-1}.
- Press Tab until one of the card's avatars has the focus.
- Press Tab again to move to the next avatar.

Expected results
- Step 1: the cursor is in "Search".
- Step 2: a card shows the focus ring.
- Step 3: the card for {Work-order-1} shows the focus ring.
- Step 4: the focused avatar shows its technician's name, the same as on hover.
- Step 5: the next avatar has the focus and shows its technician's name.
