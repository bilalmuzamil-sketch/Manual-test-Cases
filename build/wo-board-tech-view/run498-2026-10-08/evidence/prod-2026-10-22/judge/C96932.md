## Steps
- Open Work Orders, click the All tab, click Search and type ZZAUTOTEST F2 Row Click.
- In the display switcher at the right of the toolbar, click Tech View (tooltip "Tech View").
- Click on the customer name in the [WO-1] row.
- Press the browser Back button.
- Click on an empty part of the same row (e.g. between the Lines and Created on cells).
- Press the browser Back button.
- Press the mouse button on the row, move the pointer by one or two pixels, and release.
- Press the browser Back button.
- Hover the row and click its more-actions button (three dots) if it shows one.
- Press Escape.
- Press the mouse button on the row and move the pointer well away (a few centimetres) before releasing it back in place.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- Clicking anywhere on the row (customer name or empty space) opens work order S1-702.
- A press with a tiny movement still opens the work order and does not start a drag.
- Clicking the row's own more-actions button opens its menu and does not open the work order.
- Only a clear movement of the pointer starts a drag (the row lifts and follows the pointer).
Source — where this behaviour comes from
Epic SV-10043; story SV-10045 (Story 2, Tech View — table grouped by lead technician); PRD Confluence 845185030, edited 7 Oct 2026 (status line "PRD v33"), S2-R8, read 8 Oct 2026. Review Decisions 853901313 SQ-10 / DR-39.
Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
- S2-R8: “Clicking anywhere on a row, outside its own controls, opens the work order. A drag starts only after the pointer has moved a few pixels, so a click never turns into a drag.”
