# C368217 — The Part Sales list shows the right part request and return counts
Test in run 498: https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/tests/view/3792538

## Preconditions
Preconditions
- Needs: 1 user · 1 browser · about 30 minutes
- Build: the Work Orders QA build (sv10043, v26.40.8-7a95011), desktop browser, window at least 1024 px wide.
- Role: Owner/Admin. On the sign-in page click the Admin quick-login button (you appear as Admin ShopView). The Admin role has Work orders > View and Create & Edit (check: your initials (top right) > Settings > Roles & Permissions > Admin > edit).
- Location: Staging Heavy Duty - 9919 for the whole case (shown next to the bell; change it with your initials > Change Location).
- Customer "ZZAUTOTEST Part Sale Counts" (used by this case only) with one contact; part sale [PS-1] with two parts, one received with one return raised; a part sale with part requests at Staging Lethbridge - 4310.
Setup
- Customers > New Customer > Name "ZZAUTOTEST Part Sale Counts" > Save (the customer page opens). Contacts tab > New Contact > First Name "ZZAUTOTEST", Last Name "Contact" > Save. Assets tab > New Asset > Contact = ZZAUTOTEST Contact > Year 2022 > Make: type "Freight" and pick Freightliner > Model: type "M2" and pick it > Unit "TRK-118" > Save (Contact and Make are required).
- Click Parts in the top menu > Part Sales > New Part Sale > Customer: type and pick "ZZAUTOTEST Part Sale Counts" (add it first with Customers > New Customer if missing) > Save (if a Confirmation about the credit limit appears, click Create). The part sale page opens; record the number at the top left as [PS-1] (the expected results call it "PS-1001").
- On the Parts tab click Add Part > Description "Brake Pads" > Quantity 1 > Category Uncategorized > Vendor: pick any > Cost 100 > Sell Price 290.91 > Save & Close.
- Add a second part the same way (Category Uncategorized is required).
- Click Authorize (the parts read "Auth To Order" with an Order button). Click Order on one part (it then shows a Receive button) and click Receive: in the "Receive parts" window type a Vendor Invoice Number (e.g. "ZZINV-PS1"), type a part number in the part's row (e.g. "ZZPN-PS1"), set Qty Received to 1, tick the row and click Receive Parts (1); the message reads "Parts received." and the part reads "Received". Click that part's Return part button: in "Add new part return request" type a Return Reason (e.g. "ZZAUTOTEST wrong part"), keep Quantity 1 and click Save & Close. Then make the second location's part sale: your initials at the top right > Change Location > Staging Lethbridge - 4310; Parts > Part Sales > New Part Sale > Customer "ZZAUTOTEST Part Sale Counts" > Save; on its Parts tab Add Part twice the same way as above; then switch back with Change Location > Staging Heavy Duty - 9919.
- Check the setup worked: on [PS-1] one part reads "Auth To Order" and the returned part no longer shows under the Parts tab (it reads Parts (1)); the top bar shows Staging Heavy Duty - 9919. If not, finish the steps above before starting.

## Steps
- Click Parts in the top menu, then Part Sales.
- Type [PS-1] in the Search box at the top of the Part Sales list.
- Read its Parts and Returns columns.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- "PS-1001" shows 2 part requests and 1 part return, the ones you created.
- Counts from the second location's part sale are not added to it.
