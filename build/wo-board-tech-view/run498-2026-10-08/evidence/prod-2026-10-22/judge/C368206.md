# C368206 — Without See Financial Data the List has no Total price column or total
Test in run 498: https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/tests/view/3792527

## Preconditions
Preconditions
- Needs: 2 users · 2 browsers · about 15 minutes
- Build: the Work Orders QA build (sv10043, v26.40.8-7a95011), desktop browser, window at least 1024 px wide.
- Role: Owner/Admin. On the sign-in page click the Admin quick-login button (you appear as Admin ShopView). The Admin role has Work orders > View and Create & Edit (check: your initials (top right) > Settings > Roles & Permissions > Admin > edit). (for the setup).
- Location: Staging Heavy Duty - 9919 for the whole case (shown next to the bell; change it with your initials > Change Location).
- View: on Work Orders the List is shown (click List in the display switcher at the right of the toolbar; its tooltip reads "List").
- A second user [User-F] whose role has Work orders > View but See Financial Data off (custom role "ZZAUTOTEST No Financial"), at this location. Sign in as [User-F] in a private window for the steps.
Setup
- Settings > Roles & Permissions > Create Custom Role > in "Choose a template" pick Service Advisor > Apply > Role Name* "ZZAUTOTEST No Financial" > in Cross toggles turn See Financial Data off and click Disable in the "Disable See Financial Data?" question (it also turns off Part Sales, Invoicing & Payments and Order Parts) > Create (message "Role created successfully."). Then Settings > Staff > New Staff Member: First Name, Last Name, an Email you can open > Role = ZZAUTOTEST No Financial (listed under CUSTOM) > Departments = Service > Location = Staging Heavy Duty - 9919 > Save & Close. The new user accepts the invitation from that email and sets a password. Record the user as [User-F] (the case calls this user "that user").
- Check the setup worked: Settings > Roles & Permissions lists "ZZAUTOTEST No Financial"; Settings > Staff lists [User-F] with that role.

## Steps
- As [User-F] open Work Orders, click List in the display switcher and click the All tab.
- Read the column headings.
- Click Column Selection and read the options.
- Scroll to the bottom of the List.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- No Total price column is shown, Column Selection does not offer it, and no total row or total figure is shown at the bottom.
Source — where this behaviour comes from
Regression for epic SV-10043 (PR #3548); developer's regression list, received 8 Oct 2026; QA handoff test plan (generated 2026-10-08), §2; PRD Confluence 845185030 (edited 7 Oct 2026) where quoted (§1 Business Case). Source-verified 8 October 2026.
Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
- QA handoff §2: “Without financial data: no Total price column and no total.”
- PRD §1 Business Case: “List remains the default and stays exactly as it is in production today; the only addition to the Work Orders page is the switcher that offers the two new displays.”
