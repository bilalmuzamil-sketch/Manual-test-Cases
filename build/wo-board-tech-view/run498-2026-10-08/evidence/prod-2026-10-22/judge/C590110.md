# C590110 — Design: The view buttons, filter and search icons look as in the design
Test in run 498: https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/tests/view/3995719

## Preconditions
Setup file (for Claude sessions; a tester does not need it)
Preconditions
- You are signed in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role, which has Work orders > View and Create & Edit) at {Location-A}.
For example: {Location-A} is Staging Heavy Duty - 9919.
- The browser window is 1440 px wide with the zoom at 100%, and the design pictures "DC-01-toolbar-tech-view-light-1440.png" and "DC-01-toolbar-board-view-light-1440.png" attached to this case are open beside it at 100% (actual size). Names, numbers and rank numbers in the pictures are sample data.
- Customer {Customer-A} has one Approved work order, {Work-order-1}, led by technician {Technician-A}.
For example: {Customer-A} is ZZAUTOTEST C590110 Toolbar Icons, {Work-order-1} is S10043-17801 and {Technician-A} is ZZAUTOTEST C590110 Ana.
- Starting screen: "Work Orders" > "All" tab, "List" shown, "Search" empty, no filter chosen, "Assigned to me" off, density "Regular".
- If any record above is missing, mark this test Blocked: "setup not done".

## Steps
- On the "Work Orders" page, look at the three buttons of the display switcher.
- Look at the icon in "Search" and the icons on "Status", "Assigned to me" and "Asset on Site".
- Click "Tech View" in the display switcher.
- Look at the density button and the "Column Selection" button.
- Look at the icon in "Search" and the filter icons.
- Click the density button.
- Click "Board View" in the display switcher.
- Look at the icon in "Search" and the filter icons.
- Click "List" in the display switcher.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- Step 1: three thin outline icons side by side in a light-grey rounded track (a bulleted list, the head and shoulders of a person, a square board with three bars), the same drawings and size as in the design pictures, drawn in the same slate grey as in the pictures.
- Step 2: a magnifier in Search; on the filters a circle of short strokes (Status), a clipboard with lines (Assigned to me) and a truck (Asset on Site), thin outlines as in the design picture.
- Step 3: the Tech View button sits on a white raised segment, the other two look flat, and the switcher icons keep the same slate grey as in List.
- Step 4: the density button shows three lines with an up-and-down arrow and the Column Selection button a rectangle split into three columns, thin slate outlines as in the Tech View picture.
- Step 5: the same Search and filter icons as in List at step 2.
- Step 6: the density menu offers "Compact", "Regular" and "Comfortable", with "Regular" marked as in use.
- Step 7: the Board View button is now the white raised segment and the switcher icons keep the same slate grey.
- Step 8: the same Search and filter icons as in List at step 2.
- Step 9: apart from the Search and filter icons and the display switcher, List's toolbar icons look as they do in production today.
Until 15 Oct 2026 the PRD (version 52) left every List icon unchanged, so this case did not judge the filter icons. Chris Ward answered on 15 Oct 2026 (answer 12) that the filter chips and Search use the design file's icons in every display option, List included, and (answer 21) that today's tabs, toolbar and Create Work Order button stay. PRD versions 54 to 71 say so, and this case follows them. The design names the three options Table, By Lead Tech and Board; PRD version 71 still keeps List, Tech View and Board View (answer 24). The Technician filter's wrench icon is checked in the Technician filter design case.
PRD versions 55 and 58 (S1-R9a, S2-R23; Chris Ward rulings on Slavcho Mitrov's questions and PR notes, 15 Oct 2026) add that the display switcher's icons and the toolbar icons are the design's slate colour in every view, List included; steps 1, 3, 4 and 7 check it.
PRD version 71 removes Board View's density button (S3-R11), so this case no longer looks at a density button in Board View; the Board View picture is still used for the switcher and the Search and filter icons.
