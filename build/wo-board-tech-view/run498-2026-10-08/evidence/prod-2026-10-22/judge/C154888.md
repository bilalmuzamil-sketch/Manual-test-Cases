## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- Yesterday's 8:00–12:00 shift is unchanged.
- Tomorrow's 8:00–12:00 shift is gone.
- Today's shift now ends at the time of the change: e.g. 9:00–12:00 now reads 9:00–10:00 (3 hours before, 1 hour after). The part already under way stays on the Schedule and the rest is removed, just as Jeremy's 8:00–12:00 shift becomes 8:00–10:00 when the lead changes to Dana at 10:00.
What you should see today (build v26.40.8-7a95011, 10/8/2026): changing the lead (by dragging the card or with Reassign lead technician) shows no question about scheduled shifts, even when the outgoing lead has a shift booked for the entire work order. The lead changes at once with the "Lead technician updated" (or "Lead technician removed") message, and the outgoing lead's shift stays on the Schedule. (1) If you see exactly that, mark the case Failed and raise nothing new. (2) If it fails in a different way, that is a new problem: report it. (3) If it behaves as the expected results above say, the change has shipped: mark it Passed and tell the QA lead.

Review Decisions DR-38. Tech plan §12.2 item 12 still says a running shift is deleted whole; the PRD (trim) is followed. Source-verified 19 October 2026.
Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
- S4-R25: “When the user chooses to clear shifts: a shift that has already ended stays as it is; a shift that has not started yet is removed; a shift that is under way at the moment of the lead change is ended at that moment, so the part already under way stays on the schedule and the rest is removed.
