# C368215 — A part sale's status card shows in full
Test in run 498: https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/tests/view/3792536

## Preconditions
Preconditions
- Needs: 1 user · 1 browser · about 10 minutes
- Build: staging (app.staging.shopview.com) or the Work Orders QA build, desktop browser, window at least 1024 px wide.
- Role: Owner/Admin. Sign in as Admin ShopView (on a QA site, the sign-in page's Admin quick-login button signs you in as Admin ShopView). The Admin role has Work orders > View and Create & Edit (check: your initials (top right) > Settings > Roles & Permissions > Admin > edit).
- Location: Staging Heavy Duty - 9919 for the whole case (shown next to the bell; change it with your initials > Change Location).
- Customer "ZZAUTOTEST Part Sale Card" (used by this case only) with one contact.
- One part sale [PS-1] for that customer with one part.
Setup
- Customers > New Customer > Name "ZZAUTOTEST Part Sale Card" > Save (the customer page opens). Contacts tab > New Contact > First Name "ZZAUTOTEST", Last Name "Contact" > Save. Assets tab > New Asset > Contact = ZZAUTOTEST Contact > Year 2022 > Make: type "Freight" and pick Freightliner > Model: type "M2" and pick it > Unit "TRK-118" > Save (Contact and Make are required).
- Click Parts in the top menu > Part Sales > New Part Sale > Customer: type and pick "ZZAUTOTEST Part Sale Card" (add it first with Customers > New Customer if missing) > Save (if a Confirmation about the credit limit appears, click Create). The part sale page opens; record the number at the top left as [PS-1] (the expected results call it "PS-1001").
- On the Parts tab click Add Part > Description "Brake Pads" > Quantity 1 > Category Uncategorized > Vendor: pick any > Cost 100 > Sell Price 290.91 > Save & Close.
- Check the setup worked: Parts > Part Sales > Search [PS-1] lists it as Estimate with Parts 1.

## Steps
- Click Parts in the top menu, then Part Sales.
- Click the [PS-1] row in the Part Sales list to open it.
- Read the status card at the left of the page.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- The status card shows [PS-1]'s number at the top, the status Estimate, the Started date, and a Sales Representative field showing Unassigned or a person's name.
- As Admin on an Estimate, Sales Representative is a dropdown you can open to pick a person (on an invoiced or paid sale it is plain text).
- It shows no Lead technician, Service advisor or progress bar: those belong to work orders only.
- No empty box, missing field or error message.
