# C590113 — Design: Tech View opens with 13 columns and small grey column headings
Test in run 498: https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/tests/view/3995722

## Preconditions
Setup file (for Claude sessions; a tester does not need it)
Preconditions
- You are signed in with the "Admin" role at {Location-A}, and you have never changed Tech View's columns.
For example: {Location-A} is Staging Heavy Duty - 9919.
- The browser window is 1440 px wide with the zoom at 100%, and the design pictures "DC-04-tech-view-header-row-1440.png" and "DC-04-tech-view-all-columns-1440.png" attached to this case are open beside it at 100% (actual size). Names, numbers and rank numbers in the pictures are sample data. Your screen lets you widen the window to 1536 px.
- Customer {Customer-A} has an Approved work order {Work-order-1}, led by technician {Technician-A}.
For example: {Customer-A} is ZZAUTOTEST C590113 Columns, {Work-order-1} is S10043-17840 and {Technician-A} is ZZAUTOTEST C590113 Ana.
- Starting screen: "Work Orders" > "All" tab, "Tech View" shown, the name of {Customer-A} in "Search", no filter chosen, "Assigned to me" off, density "Regular".
- If any record above is missing, mark this test Blocked: "setup not done".

## Steps
- Read the column header labels from left to right, scrolling the table sideways to see them all.
- Compare the size, weight and colour of the header labels with the row text under them and with the design picture.
- Look at the space between each pair of neighbouring header labels.
- Click the "Column Selection" button.
- Turn on "On Site", "VIN/Serial #", "Lead Technician" and every other column the list offers that is off.
- Press Escape.
- Scroll the table sideways from left to right and look at every header label.
- Click the density button and choose "Compact".
- Click the density button and choose "Comfortable".
- Widen the window to 1536 px.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- Step 1: 13 columns, in this order: "Status", "Waiting On Parts", "Number", "Customer", "Unit", "Asset", "Progress", "Service Advisor", "Clocked In", "Lines", "Assigned Techs", "Created on", "Total price". "On Site", "VIN/Serial #" and "Lead Technician" are not shown.
- Step 2: the header labels are small (12 px in the design, smaller than the 14 px row text), medium weight (not bold) and grey (lighter than the row text), as in the picture.
- Step 3: no header label touches or overlaps its neighbour.
- Step 4: the column list opens and offers "On Site", "VIN/Serial #" and "Lead Technician".
- Step 5: each one turned on shows as a column.
- Step 6: the column list closes.
- Step 7: with every column on, no header label touches, overlaps or is cut off by its neighbour, as in the all-columns picture.
- Step 8: the header labels keep the same size, weight and grey colour at Compact.
- Step 9: the header labels keep the same size, weight and grey colour at Comfortable.
- Step 10: at 1536 px no header label touches or overlaps its neighbour.
Source — where this behaviour comes from
Epic SV-10043; stories SV-10048 (Story 5, Choose what is shown), SV-10050 (Story 7, Line technicians), SV-10045 (Story 2, Tech View); PRD "Work Orders — Board View & Tech View Display Options", Confluence 845185030, version 61 (16 Oct 2026, Chris Ward), S5-R4, S7-R7, S2-R21 and Out of Scope, read 16 Oct 2026. Claude Design project "Work Orders" (Branko Cicovic), export of 14 Oct 2026, board "Work Orders.dc.html" (the design pictures named in the preconditions were rendered from it), read 14 Oct 2026. Related tickets: SV-11317, SV-11321. Product owner answers: Chris Ward, WO Board and Tech View question sheet (https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1gBGmNB4B1bRrD2ZJvBn1tuvbfxFPjcsKddz_I5H3CvU), answers 14 and 25, read 15 Oct 2026. Source-verified 16 October 2026. Last checked against build v26.40.17-a5c4210 (staging) on 10/15/2026.
The design's header row reads "Unit #" and "Assigned Tech"; Chris Ward answered on 15 Oct 2026 (answer 14) that the headings are Unit and Assigned Techs, and PRD version 61 still says so. The design's Tech View column chooser does not offer On Site, VIN/Serial # or Lead Technician; PRD version 61 still keeps them as columns that can be added, and this case follows the PRD. Which columns cannot be turned off (answer 25) is checked in its own case.
PRD version 56 (Chris Ward and Slavcho Mitrov, Slack, 15 Oct 2026) took Collapse all / Expand all out of the Out of Scope list (now S2-R13a, checked in C614712); that Out of Scope quote is dropped here. What this case checks is unchanged.
Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
- S5-R4: “Until the user changes Tech View’s columns, Tech View shows these columns, in this order, as in the design: Status, Waiting On Parts, Number, Customer, Unit, Asset, Progress, Service Advisor, Clocked In, Lines, Assigned Techs, Created on, Total price (Total price subject to financial permission). On Site, VIN/Serial # and Lead Technician are off by default; each can be added with the column chooser (S2-R4). Column headings use the List’s labels for List columns (for example Unit, not Unit #) and Assigned Techs for the new column. List’s columns are unchanged.”
- S7-R7: “In Tech View the Assigned Techs column sits immediately after Lines, matching the design. It is on by default (S5-R4) and can be removed through the column chooser (S2-R4).”
- S2-R21: “Tech View’s column header labels are 12px, medium weight (500), in the secondary text color, as in the design. Labels never overlap a neighboring label.”
- Out of Scope: “Rank numbers (1, 2, 3, and so on) beside each Tech View row, as the design shows.”
- Design — Work Orders.dc.html, Tech View column header row: “font-size: 12px; font-weight: 500; color: var(--sv-text-secondary); white-space: nowrap;”
