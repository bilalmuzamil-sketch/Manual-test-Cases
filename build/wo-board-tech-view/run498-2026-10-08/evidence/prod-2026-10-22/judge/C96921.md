## Steps
- Open Work Orders in browser tab A.
- Open Work Orders in browser tab B (same user).
- In tab A, in the display switcher at the right of the toolbar, click Tech View (tooltip "Tech View").
- A few seconds later, in tab B, in the display switcher at the right of the toolbar, click Board View (tooltip "Board View").
- Refresh tab A.
- Refresh tab B.
- Open Work Orders in a new tab C.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- After refreshing, tab A, tab B and the new tab C all open in Board View, the last choice made.
- No warning or conflict message appears in either tab.
