# C96927 — Tech View's column menu offers List's columns and Assigned Techs, three stay on
Test in run 498: https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/tests/view/3481871

## Preconditions
Setup (manual QA tester and Claude session)
Preconditions
- You are signed in as an Owner or Admin user whose role has Work orders > View and Work orders > Create & Edit, at {Location-A} (for example Staging Heavy Duty - 9919).
- Your browser window is at least 1024 px wide, so the display switcher (List, Tech View, Board View) shows at the right of the Work Orders toolbar.
- Technician {Technician-A} (for example Esther Howard) is an active staff member at {Location-A} with Time Clock on and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User.
- Approved work order {Work-order-1} (for example S10043-17601) for customer {Customer-A} (for example "ZZAUTOTEST F2 Tech Columns") is led by {Technician-A} and has one approved line, {Line-1} (for example "Service - Full grease service").
- In Tech View's Column Selection, Assigned Techs is on, and On Site, VIN/Serial # and Lead Technician are off, as they start for a new user.

## Steps
- Open Work Orders and click the All tab.
- Type the name of {Customer-A} in Search.
- Click List in the display switcher at the right of the toolbar.
- Click the Column Selection button (tooltip "Column Selection") and write down every column name in the menu.
- Close the menu.
- Click Tech View in the display switcher (tooltip "Tech View").
- Click the Column Selection button and write down every column name in the menu, and which ones are on.
- Turn On Site on.
- Turn VIN/Serial # on.
- Turn Lead Technician on.
- Close the menu and read the Tech View column headers.
- Click the Column Selection button.
- Turn Assigned Techs off.
- Close the menu and read the Tech View column headers.
- Click the Column Selection button, turn Assigned Techs on and close the menu.
- Read the Tech View column headers.
- Click the Column Selection button and try to turn off Status, then Number, then Customer.
- Turn Unit off.
- Close the menu and read the Tech View column headers.
- Click the Column Selection button, turn Unit on and close the menu.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- Step 4: List's menu does not offer Assigned Techs.
- Steps 6 and 7: Tech View has its own Column Selection button, which opens Tech View's own column menu.
- Step 7: every column offered in List's menu is also offered in Tech View's menu, and Tech View's menu also offers Assigned Techs, which is on.
- Step 11: On Site, VIN/Serial # and Lead Technician each show as a column in the Tech View table.
- Step 14: the Assigned Techs column no longer shows in Tech View.
- Step 16: the Assigned Techs column shows in Tech View again.
- Step 17: Status, Number and Customer cannot be turned off: each is either not in the menu or shown switched on and locked, and all three stay in the Tech View table.
- Step 19: the Unit column no longer shows in Tech View, while Status, Number and Customer still show.
Source — where this behaviour comes from
Epic SV-10043; stories SV-10045 (Story 2, Tech View — table grouped by lead technician), SV-10048 (Story 5, Fields to display and columns) and SV-10050 (Story 7, Show line technicians on cards and rows); PRD "Work Orders — Board View & Tech View Display Options", Confluence 845185030, version 54 (15 Oct 2026, Chris Ward), S2-R4, S5-R4 and S7-R7 (S5-R4 and S7-R7 rewritten in version 51 on 14 Oct 2026 (Chris Ward ruling after Sasha Grosman's staging review: Tech View's default columns are the design's 13, and Assigned Techs is on by default)), read 15 Oct 2026. The earlier note that the design's Tech View menu leaves out some List columns is removed: version 51 keeps every List column available in Tech View, with On Site, VIN/Serial # and Lead Technician off by default. Product owner answers: Chris Ward, WO Board and Tech View question sheet (https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1gBGmNB4B1bRrD2ZJvBn1tuvbfxFPjcsKddz_I5H3CvU), answers 14 and 25, read 15 Oct 2026. S5-R4a was added in version 54 on 15 Oct 2026 from answer 25; S5-R4 gained the sentence on heading words (answer 14). Source-verified 15 October 2026. Last checked against build v26.40.19-1493c63 (staging) on 10/16/2026.
Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
- S2-R4: “Tech View offers the same columns as List, plus the Assigned Techs column (Story 7), through its own column chooser (Story 5).”
- S5-R4: “Until the user changes Tech View’s columns, Tech View shows these columns, in this order, as in the design: Status, Waiting On Parts, Number, Customer, Unit, Asset, Progress, Service Advisor, Clocked In, Lines, Assigned Techs, Created on, Total price (Total price subject to financial permission). On Site, VIN/Serial # and Lead Technician are off by default; each can be added with the column chooser (S2-R4). Column headings use the List’s labels for List columns (for example Unit, not Unit #) and Assigned Techs for the new column. List’s columns are unchanged.”
- S5-R4a: “As in the design, Status, Number and Customer are always shown in Tech View and cannot be turned off. Every other Tech View column, Unit included, can be turned off with the column chooser.”
- S7-R7: “In Tech View the Assigned Techs column sits immediately after Lines, matching the design. It is on by default (S5-R4) and can be removed through the column chooser (S2-R4).”

## Newest PASSED result in run 498 (staging judgement)
Tested on staging, build v26.40.19-1493c63, on 17 October 2026.
- Result: Passed on staging, following the test's steps.
- Step 4: List's column menu does not offer Assigned Techs. Steps 6 and 7: Tech View has its own Column Selection button; its menu offers every column List offers, plus On Site, Unit and Assigned Techs; Assigned Techs is on.
- Step 11: after turning them on, On Site, VIN/Serial # and Lead Technician each show as a column in Tech View.
- Step 14: Assigned Techs turned off no longer shows; step 16: turned on again, it shows again.
