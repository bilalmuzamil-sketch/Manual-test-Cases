# C581915 — Tech View and Board View offer a Technician filter that List does not
Test in run 498: https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/tests/view/3982130

## Preconditions
Setup file (for Claude sessions; a tester does not need it)
Preconditions
- You are signed in with the "Admin" role at {Location-A}, in a browser window at least 1024 px wide.
For example: {Location-A} is Staging Heavy Duty - 9919.
- Three technicians are active at {Location-A} with the "Technician" role and Time Clock on, added in this order: {Technician-A}, {Technician-B}, {Technician-C}.
For example: {Technician-A} is Ana Carter, {Technician-B} is Ana Brooks and {Technician-C} is Ben Adams.
- Technician {Technician-D} is deactivated at {Location-A} and still leads at least one work order there, so Tech View shows their group marked "Inactive".
For example: {Technician-D} is Aimee Francis.
- Starting screen: "Work Orders" > "All" tab, "Tech View" shown, "Search" empty, no filter chosen, "Assigned to me" off.
- If any record above is missing, mark this test Blocked: "setup not done".

## Steps
- Look at the filter bar.
- Click "Technician".
- Type the name of {Technician-D} in "Search technician".
- Clear the "Search technician" box.
- Tick {Technician-A}, then tick {Technician-B}.
- Click "Clear selection".
- Press Escape.
- Click "Board View" in the display switcher.
- Click "Technician".
- Press Escape.
- Click "List" in the display switcher.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- Step 1: a "Technician" filter sits right after "Assigned to me". There is no Department, Customer or Service Advisor filter.
- Step 2: the list starts with "Unassigned". The active technicians follow, by first name then last name: {Technician-B}, then {Technician-A}, then {Technician-C}, whatever order they were added in. Deactivated technicians come after every active one, each with the grey "Inactive" label.
- Step 3: {Technician-D} is found, with the grey "Inactive" label.
- Step 4: the whole list shows again.
- Step 5: {Technician-A} and {Technician-B} are both ticked at the same time, and Tech View shows only their two groups.
- Step 6: nothing is ticked.
- Step 7: the list closes and every technician group shows again.
- Step 8: Board View shows the same columns as Tech View had groups.
- Step 9: the "Technician" filter sits in the same place, right after "Assigned to me", with the same options in the same order as in step 2, {Technician-D} included with "Inactive".
- Step 10: the list closes.
- Step 11: List shows no "Technician" filter.
Known problem (SV-11575, In Progress; Bilal Muzamil, staging build v26.40.20-0509710, 20 Oct 2026): technicians who have their own group or column but are deactivated or no longer enrolled are missing from the Technician list (for example Aimee Francis: her column is marked Inactive, but typing her name in "Search technician" gives No results). (1) If you see exactly that at steps 2, 3 or 9, mark this test Failed and raise nothing new. (2) If it fails in a different way, report it. (3) If {Technician-D} is listed with "Inactive", mark it Passed and tell the QA lead: the fix has shipped.
Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
From: PRD "Work Orders — Board View & Tech View Display Options", Confluence 845185030, version 71 (20 Oct 2026, Chris Ward), read 20 Oct 2026 · Epic SV-10043, story SV-10044 (Story 1, Switch between display options); tickets SV-11326 and SV-11575
- S1-R15: “In Tech View and Board View the filter bar also offers a Technician filter, after Assigned to me. It is a multi-select listing Unassigned first, then everyone who gets a group or column at this location: the location’s technicians (S9-R4), and any lead technician who keeps a column while still leading work orders here (deactivated, S2-N2 and S3-N2; not enrolled here, S3-R20b; Time Clock turned off, S3-R20d). Active technicians come first, by first name then last name, then deactivated technicians, by first name then last name, each with the same grey “Inactive” label as their column. Someone who no longer gets a group or column is not listed. Choosing one or more shows only those technicians’ groups or columns; every other group or column, pinned ones included, is hidden. It filters by lead technician only.”
- S1-R15b: “List does not offer the Technician filter and stays as in production. Switching to List while technicians are chosen shows List as if none were chosen; switching back to Tech View or Board View brings the choice back. The choice is remembered the same way as the other filters.”
- Out of Scope: “A Department filter. The design shows one, but a work order’s department is not shown or editable on any work order screen today, so users could not see why a work order matches. Revisit once the work order shows its department.”
- Out of Scope: “The Customer and Service Advisor filter chips production retired in the redesign (SV-8785) are not brought back.”
