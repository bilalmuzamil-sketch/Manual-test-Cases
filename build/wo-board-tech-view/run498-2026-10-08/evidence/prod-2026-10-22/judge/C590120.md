# C590120 — Design: Board View cards use List's status label and the design's text sizes
Test in run 498: https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/tests/view/3995729

## Preconditions
- Owner/Admin with Work Orders View, Create & Edit and See financial data permissions. Use light mode, a 1440 px browser and 100% zoom.
- Keep the attached Detailed and Compact design pictures open at actual size: DC-11-board-card-detailed-light-1440.png and DC-11-board-card-compact-light-1440.png. Compare layout and typography; sample values and omitted rank numbers are not assertions. The PRD controls the status pill, default fields and density behavior where the pictures differ.
- For one unique customer, prepare WO1 = Approved, with a unit and asset (e.g. TRK-118 · 2022 Freightliner M2), lead technician A, line technician B, nonzero progress and total; WO2 = Invoiced, led by A.
- Use work orders whose List pills show nonzero red service-action and blue parts-action counts, across one or more work orders, so both counts can be compared. A and B must be active at the location.
- Board fields: defaults plus Line technicians. Density = Regular; clear filters and search; Assigned to me off.
Execution dependency: The latest execution is blocked on SV-11405 (In Progress, checked 16 Oct 2026): Board status-pill parity with List. Layout, typography, lock and density checks can be executed separately; automation remains on hold pending a full rerun.

## Steps
Open Work Orders → All → List. Search for the test customer and note the status wording, colors and red/blue counts for the prepared work orders.
- Switch to Board View and inspect WO1’s card from top to bottom against the Detailed design.
- Compare the number, customer, unit/asset line and total typography.
- Compare the same work orders’ Board status pills with List.
- Inspect WO2’s number.
- Look at the Board View toolbar between the filters and the display switcher.
- Click Tech View in the display switcher, choose Compact and then Comfortable from its density button, click Board View again and inspect WO1.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Step 1: The prepared work orders appear. Their List pills provide the baseline for each status and both action-count types.
Step 2: The blue work-order number is at top left and status at top right in a fixed position. Below are the dark customer name, a smaller grey unit · asset line, a thin blue progress bar and divider, then overlapping round technician avatars on the left and total on the right. The white card has rounded corners and a thin grey border. Avatars include lead A and distinct line technician B.
Step 3: Number, customer and total are 14 px at medium weight (500); none is larger or bolder than the others. The unit/asset line is 12 px and grey, matching the design.
Step 4: Each pill matches List’s wording, color, rounded shape and red service-action/blue parts-action counts. The Board pill is 22 px tall.
Step 5: An outline padlock appears immediately after the Invoiced work-order number.
Step 6: “Board View shows no Density control in this release (SV-11580).” (PRD v73, S3-R11)
Step 7: “Density applies to Tech View rows on Work Orders; Board View cards keep their current spacing in this release.” (PRD v73, S6-E1) In plain words (our restatement): WO1’s card looks the same as at step 2.
