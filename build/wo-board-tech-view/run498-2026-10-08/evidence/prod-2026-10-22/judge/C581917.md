# C581917 — The Technician filter combines with other filters and Clear all filters
Test in run 498: https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/tests/view/3982132

## Preconditions
- Owner/Admin with Work Orders View and Create & Edit permissions; browser at least 1024 px wide. Active, clockable technicians A and B are eligible at this location.
- Three Approved work orders: WO1 = lead A/customer A; WO2 = lead B/customer A; WO3 = lead A/customer B. Use distinct, searchable customer names.
- None of these work orders is assigned to the signed-in user as lead technician, line-item technician or service advisor. Customer A has no Paid work orders.
- Clear filters and search; Assigned to me off.
Source: PRD v60, S1-R15a, S1-R13 and S1-N1a.
Execution dependency: The Technician filter must be available. The latest execution is blocked by SV-11326 (Open, checked 16 Oct 2026). Rerun when available; automation remains on hold pending execution.

## Steps
Open Work Orders → All → Tech View. Search for customer A and select technician A.
- Turn Assigned to me on.
- Turn Assigned to me off.
- Apply Status = Paid while keeping the search and Technician selection.
- Click Clear all filters.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Step 1: Only WO1 appears: customer search and Technician filter are combined. WO2 and WO3 are excluded.
Step 2: No work orders appear. Show “No work orders match these filters”, “Try removing a filter to widen your results.” and Clear all filters. Assigned to me combines with the existing filters using AND.
Step 3: WO1 returns; search and Technician selection are retained.
Step 4: No work orders appear, with the same no-match message, supporting text and Clear all filters action. Status combines with the other conditions using AND.
Step 5: Technician and Status selections are cleared. WO2 is visible again.
AUTOMATION: Not available on Build to test Yet - Last checked 10/16/2026
