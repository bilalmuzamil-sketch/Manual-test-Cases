# C581895 — Technician names and avatar hover names match the staff record in both views
Test in run 498: https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/tests/view/3974851

## Preconditions
Setup (manual QA tester and Claude session)
Preconditions
- You are signed in as an Owner or Admin user whose role has Work orders > View and Work orders > Create & Edit, at {Location-A} (for example Staging Heavy Duty - 9919).
- Your browser window is at least 1024 px wide, so the display switcher (List, Tech View, Board View) shows at the right of the Work Orders toolbar.
- Technicians {Technician-A} (for example ZZAUTOTEST Staffname Ana) and {Technician-B} (for example ZZAUTOTEST Staffname Ben) are active staff members at {Location-A} with Time Clock on and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User.
- Each technician's staff record name is the same as the name on their sign-in account, because Settings > Staff shows the sign-in account name and step 2 must give the staff record name.
For example: on staging, Esther Howard and Ralph Edwards. Do not use a technician whose two names differ (on staging, the sign-in account Carol Neal, whose staff record reads David Sparks).
- Customer {Customer-A} (for example "ZZAUTOTEST S5R11b Staff Names") is used by this case only.
- Approved work order {Work-order-1} (for example S10043-17791) for {Customer-A} is led by {Technician-A} and has two approved lines, {Line-1} and {Line-2} (for example "Service - Full grease service" twice).
- {Line-2}'s technician is {Technician-B}, set through Edit Line.
- List shows the Lead Technician column, Board View's Fields to display has Lead technician and Line technicians on, and Tech View shows the Assigned Techs column (it is on by default).
- On the Work Orders page nothing is ticked in Status, Asset on Site shows no choice (if its chip shows Yes or No, click the x on the chip), Assigned to me is off and Search is empty.

## Steps
- Click your initials (top right) and open Settings > Staff.
- Write down the names shown for {Technician-A} and {Technician-B}.
- Open Work Orders, click the All tab and click List in the display switcher.
- Type the name of {Customer-A} in Search.
- Read the Lead Technician of {Work-order-1}.
- Click Board View in the display switcher.
- Read the lead technician name on {Work-order-1}'s card.
- Read the header of the column that holds {Work-order-1}.
- Hover each avatar in the card's line technicians, and the +N if one shows, and read each name.
- Click Tech View in the display switcher.
- Read the header of the group that holds {Work-order-1}.
- Hover each avatar in {Work-order-1}'s Assigned Techs cell, and the +N if one shows, and read each name.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- Step 5: List's Lead Technician shows {Technician-A}'s staff record name, as written down at step 2.
- Step 7: the card's lead technician name is the same staff record name.
- Step 8: the column header shows {Technician-A}'s staff record name.
- Step 9: the names shown on hover are the staff record names of {Technician-A} and {Technician-B}, as written down at step 2.
- Step 11: the group header shows {Technician-A}'s staff record name.
- Step 12: the names shown on hover are the staff record names of {Technician-A} and {Technician-B}.
- Steps 5 to 12: no other name for either technician appears anywhere (for example not ZZAUTOTEST Account Ana).
Source — where this behaviour comes from
Epic SV-10043; story SV-10048 (Story 5, Choose what is shown); SV-10050 (Story 7, Line technicians); PRD "Work Orders — Board View & Tech View Display Options", Confluence 845185030, version 54 (15 Oct 2026, Chris Ward), S5-R11b, S2-R10, S7-R1, S7-R6, S2-R12 and S3-R12, read 15 Oct 2026. S5-R11b was added in version 49 from Chris Ward's answer 4 (14 Oct 2026) and widened in version 54 from answer 23 to every name in both views, avatar hover names included. Developer note (Slack #fs-board-tech-view, 12 Oct 2026): staff record and account names can differ on the QA data. Staging keeps one name per technician (found by the test-run session on 15 Oct 2026). Product owner answers: Chris Ward, WO Board and Tech View question sheet (https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1gBGmNB4B1bRrD2ZJvBn1tuvbfxFPjcsKddz_I5H3CvU), answers 4 and 23, read 15 Oct 2026. Source-verified 15 October 2026. Last checked against build v26.40.19-72f4f3b (staging) on 10/16/2026.
Until 15 Oct 2026 the PRD named only cards and headers, and this case only checked that each technician could be found by name in a row's avatars. Chris Ward answered on 15 Oct 2026 (answer 23) that every technician name in both views, avatar hover names included, is the staff record name; PRD version 54 says so. On staging a technician's staff record and sign-in account always carry the same name, so there the case shows that every name matches Settings > Staff but cannot show which of the two the screen reads; run it where the names can differ to prove that.
Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
- S5-R11b: “Every technician name in Tech View and Board View, on cards, rows, group headers, column headers and the names shown on hover over any technician avatar (S2-R10, S7-R1), is the technician’s staff record name, the same name the List’s Lead Technician column shows.”
- S2-R10: “Hovering a technician header or avatar reveals the technician’s name.”
- S7-R1: “Board View cards (when the line technicians field is on) and a new optional Assigned Techs column in Tech View show a compact avatar group: the lead technician plus the distinct technicians assigned to work order lines. List does not get this column.”
- S7-R6: “Keep every involved technician discoverable by name.”
- S2-R12: “Each group header shows the technician’s name, avatar, and a count of the work orders currently shown in that group, after the filter view and filters are applied, so the count always matches the rows listed.”
- S3-R12: “Each column header shows the technician’s name, avatar, and a count of the work orders currently shown in that column, after the filter view and filters are applied, so the count always matches the cards listed.”

## Newest PASSED result in run 498 (staging judgement)
Tested on staging, build v26.40.20-c627fc7, on 21 October 2026.
- Result: Passed on staging - every name matches the staff record name; SV-11336's fix holds.
- Why re-run: Settings > Staff shows the sign-in account name, so the earlier run's technician (staff record David Sparks, sign-in Carol Neal) could never match step 2. On the QA lead's choice the test now uses technicians whose two names are the same, and its set-up sentence says so.
- Steps 1-2: Settings > Staff reads Esther Howard (Technician-A) and Ralph Edwards (Technician-B).
- Step 5: List's Lead Technician of S2-37951 reads Esther Howard.
- Steps 7-8: Board View's card lead and its column header read Esther Howard.
- Step 9: hovering the card's line technicians shows Esther Howard and Ralph Edwards.
- Step 11: Tech View's group header reads Esther Howard.
- Step 12: hovering the Assigned Techs avatars shows Esther Howard and Ralph Edwards; no other name appears.
