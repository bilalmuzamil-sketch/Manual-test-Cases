## Preconditions
Preconditions
- Needs: 1 user · 1 browser · about 15 minutes
- Build: the Work Orders QA build (sv10043, v26.40.8-7a95011). A desktop browser with the window at least 1024 pixels wide (full screen on an ordinary laptop or monitor). Below that width the page shows only List.
- [User-A]: an Owner/Admin user (on a QA branch: the Admin quick-login button on the sign-in page; you appear as "Admin ShopView"). The Admin role holds Work orders > View and Work orders > Create & Edit (to check: your initials at the top right > Settings > Roles & Permissions > Admin > Work orders).
- One location for the whole case, shown in the top bar next to the bell (e.g. "Staging Heavy Duty - 9919"); everything below is created in this location.
- Two technicians at this location with no profile photo: "Theresa Webb" and "Jenny Wilson" (the expected results name them, so keep these names; a newly added staff member has no photo). A person only counts as a technician here when the staff member is active (not deactivated), Time Clock is on, the Role is neither Office User nor Time Clock User, and this location is picked in their Location field.
- Customer "ZZAUTOTEST F2 Initials Avatar" (used by this case only), with one contact and one asset, so this case's work orders are kept apart from every other work order at the location.
- One Approved work order for that customer, [WO-1]: Lead Technician Theresa Webb, with one line given to Jenny Wilson so the work order has a line technician besides the lead.
- The Assigned Techs column (Tech View) and the line technicians field (Board View) may start on or off; the steps turn them on.

## Steps
- Open Work Orders.
- Click the All tab.
- Type ZZAUTOTEST F2 Initials Avatar in Search.

- Read the avatar beside Theresa Webb's name in her group header.
- Click the Column Selection button in the toolbar (tooltip "Column Selection").
- Tick Assigned Techs.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- Steps 5 and 10: Theresa Webb's header shows a round avatar holding her initials "TW" (first letter of the first name, then first letter of the last name), next to her name and the count.
- Steps 8 and 13: the avatar group shows "TW" first and "JW" for Jenny Wilson, each in its own round avatar.
- Nowhere is an empty circle, a blank space or a broken-image icon shown in place of an avatar.
Source — where this behaviour comes from
Epic SV-10043; story SV-10045 (Story 2, Tech View) and SV-10046 (Story 3, Board View) and SV-10050 (Story 7, line technicians); PRD Confluence 845185030, edited 7 Oct 2026 (status line "PRD v33"), S2-R12, S3-R12, S7-R1, read 8 Oct 2026. The PRD requires an avatar but does not say what it shows without a photo; the design does: design export of 8 Oct 2026 (Work Orders.dc.html) renders header avatars through SVAvatarFix and avatar groups through SVTechStack from the design system bundle (_ds_bundle.js L9963-L10013 StaffAvatar: photo, otherwise initials, also when the photo fails to load; L10015-L10096 TechStack). Design crawl 8 Oct shows initials avatars such as "TW", "JW", "KW" beside photo avatars. Design-only detail, cited to the design. Source-verified 8 October 2026. Design: the Work Orders design export uploaded by the QA lead on 8 Oct 2026 (on-screen labels and the design-only detail in this case).
Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
- S2-R12: “Each group header shows the technician’s name, avatar, and a count of the work orders currently shown in that group, after the filter view and filters are applied, so the count always matches the rows listed.”
- S3-R12: “Each column header shows the technician’s name, avatar, and a count of the work orders currently shown in that column, after the filter view and filters are applied, so the count always matches the cards listed.”
- S7-R1: “Board View cards (when the line technicians field is on) and a new optional Assigned Techs column in Tech View show a compact avatar group: the lead technician plus the distinct technicians assigned to work order lines. List does not get this column.”
