## Steps
- Open Work Orders, click the All tab and pick Board View in the display switcher (right side of the toolbar; tooltip "Board View").
- Type "ZZAUTOTEST F1 Remove Lead Lines" in Search.
- Hover [WO-1]'s card in [Tech-A]'s column, click More actions and choose Reassign lead technician.
- Pick Unassigned and confirm ([Tech-A] is still clocked in on Line 6).
- If a "Clear …'s scheduled shifts?" prompt opens, click Keep shifts.
- Open the work order and go to the Lines tab.
- Read each line's Labor technician and whether Line 6's clock is still running.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- The lead is removed even though Esther Howard is clocked in: the card is in the Unassigned column and the work order has no lead technician.
- Line 2 Implicit now reads Unassigned (it was following the outgoing lead). Line 1 still reads Unassigned.
- Line 3 Explicit still shows Dana Ortiz, Line 4 Complete and Line 5 Logged still show Esther Howard.
- Line 6 Clocked still shows Esther Howard and her clock is still running, although she was assigned only by following the lead and had no earlier labor on it.
Source — where this behaviour comes from
Epic SV-10043; story SV-10047 (Story 4, Reassign or unassign the lead technician); PRD Confluence 845185030, edited 7 Oct 2026 (status line "PRD v33"), S4-R8 (removal), S4-E1, S4-E6, read 8 Oct 2026. Review Decisions DR-22 (active clock-in kept on change or removal). Source-verified 8 October 2026.
Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
- S4-R8 removal: “Removing the lead technician follows the same table: lines that were following the outgoing lead return to unassigned, and every other line is left as it is.”
- S4-E1: “Allow lead changes while the outgoing lead is clocked into a work order line.”
- S4-E6: “An actively clocked-in line retains its assigned technician, even if assignment is implicit or no earlier labor exists.”
- S4-R8 table: active clock-in: “Has an active clock-in on the line, including an implicit assignment with no previously logged labor | No — preserve the clock-in and that technician’s line assignment”
