# C97013 — Reordering while filtered keeps the order right when the filter is cleared
Test in run 498: https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/tests/view/3481957

## Preconditions
Preconditions
- Needs: 1 user · 1 browser · about 20 minutes
- Build: the Work Orders QA build (sv10043). Desktop browser window at least 1024 px wide (narrower windows show only the List, without the display switcher).
- Role: Owner/Admin. On a QA branch sign in with the Admin quick-login button on the sign-in page; you appear as "Admin ShopView". The Admin role holds Work orders > View and Work orders > Create & Edit (to check: your initials at the top right > Settings > Roles & Permissions > Admin > Work orders). This user is the dispatcher.
- Location: one location for the whole case, shown in the top bar next to the bell (e.g. "Staging Heavy Duty - 9919"); to change it click your initials at the top right > Change Location. Every technician and work order below belongs to this location.
- Technicians (examples, the expected results call them by first name or full name): "ZZAUTOTEST Ana Alpha". A staff member counts as a technician when, in Settings > Staff > the staff member's edit icon > Edit Staff Member, they are Active, Time Clock is on, the Role is neither Office User nor Time Clock User, and this location is picked in their Location field.
- Customers (used by this case only, each with one contact and one asset): "ZZAUTOTEST F3 Filtered Reorder Alpha Co", "ZZAUTOTEST F3 Filtered Reorder Bravo Co", "ZZAUTOTEST F3 Filtered Reorder Charlie Co", "ZZAUTOTEST F3 Filtered Reorder Delta Co". The steps and expected results call them by the end of the name ("Alpha Co", "Bravo Co", "Charlie Co", "Delta Co").
- Four work orders with lead Ana: Alpha Co (In Progress), Bravo Co (Approved), Charlie Co (In Progress), Delta Co (Approved). Nobody has reordered Ana's work, so the full order is A, B, C, D (Alpha, Bravo, Charlie, Delta).
- The Status filter starts with nothing ticked.
- None of these technicians has a shift on the Schedule for these work orders (check the Schedule page). If a window titled "Clear … scheduled shifts?" still appears during a move, click "Keep shifts".

- Settings (your initials at the top right > Settings) > Staff: confirm "ZZAUTOTEST Ana Alpha" exist and count as technicians at this location (edit icon > Edit Staff Member). To add one: New Staff Member with those settings (at least one Department is required) (e.g. first name "ZZAUTOTEST Ana", last name "Alpha") > Save & Close.

For each: top menu Customers > New Customer, Name > Save; on the customer page click the Contacts tab > New Contact > First Name and Last Name > Save (a new asset needs a contact); then the Assets tab > New Asset > pick the Contact, then Year, Make, Model (e.g. 2022, Freightliner, M2) > Save. Make is required; Unit can be left empty.

## Steps
- Open Work Orders from the top menu and click the All tab.
- Type "ZZAUTOTEST F3 Filtered Reorder" in Search so only this case's work orders show.
- Click Tech View in the display switcher at the right of the toolbar (tooltip "Tech View").
- Read Ana's group: Alpha Co, Bravo Co, Charlie Co, Delta Co.
- Click Status and tick "In progress" only. Ana's group now shows Alpha Co and Charlie Co.
- Drag Charlie Co above Alpha Co and release.
- Click Status > "Clear selection" and read Ana's group.
- Drag the rows back into the order Alpha Co, Bravo Co, Charlie Co, Delta Co and read the group to confirm it.
- Click Status, tick "In progress" only, and drag Alpha Co below Charlie Co.
- Click Status > "Clear selection" and read Ana's group.
- Click Board View in the display switcher (tooltip "Board View").

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- Step 7: the order is Charlie Co, Alpha Co, Bravo Co, Delta Co (C, A, B, D): Charlie Co was placed right above Alpha Co and the hidden Bravo Co and Delta Co kept their relative order.
- Step 10: the order is Bravo Co, Charlie Co, Alpha Co, Delta Co (B, C, A, D): Alpha Co was placed right below Charlie Co.
- Step 11: Board View shows Ana's column in the same order, B, C, A, D.
Source — where this behaviour comes from
Epic SV-10043; story SV-10052 (Story 9, Drag to reorder work and technician groups/columns); PRD Confluence 845185030, edited 7 Oct 2026 (status line "PRD v33"), S9-E2, S9-E3, Story 9 filtered-order examples, read 8 Oct 2026.
Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
- S9-E2: “When reordering filtered results, place the moved item immediately before or after an unmoved visible anchor.”
- S9-E3: “Retain that relative position when filters clear.”
- Story 9 filtered-order examples: “Filtered-order examples — confirmed: Full order A, B, C, D; the filter shows A, C. Move C immediately above A: clearing the filter yields C, A, B, D. Starting again from A, B, C, D, move A immediately below C: clearing the filter yields B, C, A, D. Hidden items keep their relative order. Apply to work orders within a technician and technician groups/columns, subject to fixed Unassigned and pinned-area boundaries.”
