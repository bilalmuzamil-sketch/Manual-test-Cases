# C96930 — Only staff who can lead work orders get a technician group
Test in run 498: https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/tests/view/3481874

## Preconditions
Preconditions
- Needs: 1 user · 1 browser · about 20 minutes
- Build: the Work Orders QA build (sv10043, v26.40.8-7a95011). A desktop browser with the window at least 1024 pixels wide (full screen on an ordinary laptop or monitor). Below that width the page shows only List.
- [User-A]: an Owner/Admin user (on a QA branch: the Admin quick-login button on the sign-in page; you appear as "Admin ShopView"). The Admin role holds Work orders > View and Work orders > Create & Edit (to check: your initials at the top right > Settings > Roles & Permissions > Admin > Work orders).
- One location for the whole case, shown in the top bar next to the bell (e.g. "Staging Heavy Duty - 9919"); everything below is created in this location.
- Seven staff at this location, none of them leading any work order (the names are the ones the expected results use):
- Staff and how each differs:- "Esther Howard": role Technician, Time Clock on, Active, with this location picked in Location (should show).
- "Billy Nobill": like Esther but Billable off (should show).
- "Ina Active": like Esther, then set the staff record inactive (should not show).
- "Nick Noclock": like Esther but Time Clock off (should not show).
- "Olive Office": like Esther but role Office User (should not show).
- "Tim Clockuser": like Esther but role Time Clock User (should not show).
- "Ella Elsewhere": like Esther but enrolled only at another location (should not show).
- No customer or work order is needed: the groups and columns are checked with no work in them.
Setup
- Sign in with the Admin quick-login button on the sign-in page (you appear as "Admin ShopView"). Check the location in the top bar next to the bell; to change it click your initials at the top right > Change Location.
- Settings (your initials at the top right > Settings) > Staff > New Staff Member for each of the seven staff above: First Name and Last Name, an Email, the Role shown, at least one Department (required), the Location shown, Billable and Time Clock as shown > Save & Close. For "Ella Elsewhere" pick only the other location (e.g. "Staging Lethbridge - 4310").
- For "Ina Active", open the staff record again and set it inactive.
- Check the setup worked: Settings > Staff lists all seven with the roles, Billable, Time Clock and Location above, and "Ina Active" shows as inactive. If an "Esther Howard" already existed and leads work orders, that is fine for this case: only whether a group exists is checked.

## Steps
- Open Work Orders.
- Click the All tab on the left of the toolbar.
- In the display switcher at the right of the toolbar, click Tech View (tooltip "Tech View").
- Read every technician group header.
- In the display switcher at the right of the toolbar, click Board View (tooltip "Board View").
- Read every column header.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- Esther Howard and Billy Nobill each have a group in Tech View and a column in Board View, even with no work orders.
- Ina Active, Nick Noclock, Olive Office, Tim Clockuser and Ella Elsewhere have no group and no column.
Source — where this behaviour comes from
Epic SV-10043; story SV-10045 (Story 2, Tech View — table grouped by lead technician); PRD Confluence 845185030, edited 7 Oct 2026 (status line "PRD v33"), S2-R7, Terminology, read 8 Oct 2026. Review Decisions 853901313 FF-1 / DR-4.
Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
- S2-R7: “Include staff who are Clockable, Active, enrolled in the current location, and whose role is neither Office nor Time Clock User.”
- Terminology: “A staff member who meets all four criteria: Clockable is enabled; the staff record is Active; their role is neither Office nor Time Clock User; and they are enrolled in the current location. Billable is not a criterion.”
