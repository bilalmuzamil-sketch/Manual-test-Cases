## Preconditions
Preconditions
- Needs: 1 user · 1 phone or narrow browser · about 5 minutes
- Build: the Work Orders QA build (sv10043, v26.40.8-7a95011) on a phone, or a desktop browser narrowed to about 390 px wide.
- Role: Owner/Admin. On the sign-in page click the Admin quick-login button (you appear as Admin ShopView). The Admin role has Work orders > View and Create & Edit (check: your initials (top right) > Settings > Roles & Permissions > Admin > edit).

- Needs no test data of its own.
Setup
- At 390 px open Work Orders > tap the sort button (two arrows, "Sort work orders" next to the search) > Newest First.

## Steps
- Open Work Orders.
- Tap the sort button (two arrows, "Sort work orders").
- Pick Customer A-Z.
- Reload the page.
- Read the sort button (tap it: the tick is on the chosen sort) and the order of the cards.

## Newest PASSED result in run 498 (staging judgement)
Tested on staging, build v26.40.19-1493c63, on 17 October 2026.
- Result: Passed on staging, judged from this morning's reading (17 Oct, same build) now that the first card's place is explained.
- Phone width (390 px): the sort button offers Newest First, Oldest First, Customer A-Z and Customer Z-A; Customer A-Z was chosen and is still ticked after the reload.
- After the reload the cards are in customer name order A to Z: ZZBREAK (see below), then 4 Star Truck Repair, A & J Truck & Trailer Repair, Aberdeen Diesel Services LLC, Alicia's Truck & Trailer Repair, All American Truck & Trailer Repair, Andreasen Truck & Equipment Repair, Andrews' Truck & Trailer Repair LLC, Bravo Mechanical Services and on.
- The first row is another tester's deliberate edge-case customer, "ZZBREAK <b>bold</b> ...", whose saved name starts with two ordinary spaces (read back: two U+0020 characters, nothing hidden). A space comes before any letter or digit, so A to Z correctly puts it first. Neither the test nor its source says leading spaces are ignored; that customer belongs to another tester and was not renamed.
