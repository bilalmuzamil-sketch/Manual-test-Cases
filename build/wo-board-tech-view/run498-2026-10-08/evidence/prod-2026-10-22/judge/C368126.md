## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- Read the order of your four test technicians only (others at the location may sit between them). At the start: Unassigned, Esther Howard, Jenny Wilson, Kristin Watson, Ralph Edwards.
- After pinning Kristin Watson she is right after Unassigned, before every unpinned technician: Unassigned, Kristin Watson, Esther Howard, Jenny Wilson, Ralph Edwards.
- After pinning Esther Howard: Unassigned, Kristin Watson, Esther Howard, Jenny Wilson, Ralph Edwards (the new pin goes after the existing pin, not by name).
- Board View shows the same order.
Source — where this behaviour comes from
Epic SV-10043; story SV-10045 (Story 2, Tech View — table grouped by lead technician); PRD Confluence 845185030, edited 7 Oct 2026 (status line "PRD v33"), S2-R17, S3-R15, S3-R16, read 8 Oct 2026. PRD footer comment Slavcho Mitrov 2 Oct 2026 item 2, folded into the 7 Oct PRD.
Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
- S2-R17: “Tech View orders pinned technicians the same way as Board View: after the fixed Unassigned group and before unpinned technicians, each newly pinned technician after the existing pins (S3-R15, S3-R16).”
- S3-R15: “Place pinned technicians after fixed Unassigned and before unpinned technicians.”
- S3-R16: “Append each newly pinned technician after the existing pins.”
