# C368171 — A lead change on the work order page adds one Lead tech changed entry
Test in run 498: https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/tests/view/3792492

## Preconditions
Setup file (for Claude sessions; a tester does not need it)
Preconditions
- You are signed in with the "Admin" role at {Location-A}, in a browser window at least 1024 px wide.
For example: {Location-A} is Staging Heavy Duty - 9919.
- Technicians {Technician-A} and {Technician-B} are active at {Location-A}, and both are offered in the Lead Technician list on the work order page.
For example: AA ZZAUTOTEST C368171 Ana and AA ZZAUTOTEST C368171 Ben (names starting "AA" sit near the top of the work order page's technician lists, which offer only about the first 100 people in A to Z order (SV-11429)).
- Customer {Customer-A} has an Approved work order {Work-order-1} led by {Technician-A}, with two approved lines, {Line-1} and {Line-2}, that have no technician of their own (they follow the lead).
For example: {Customer-A} is ZZAUTOTEST Edit WO Audit, {Work-order-1} is S1-716, {Line-1} is "Service - Full grease service" and {Line-2} is "Service - Adjust clutch".
- If any record above is missing, mark this test Blocked: "setup not done".

## Steps
- Open "Work Orders" > "All".
- Type the name of {Customer-A} in "Search".
- Click {Work-order-1}.
- Click the three dots at the top right of the tabs and choose "Audit Log".
- Count the "Lead tech changed" entries and note the time.
- Close the window (X).
- On the status card at the left, click "Lead Technician" and pick {Technician-B}.
- Click the three dots at the top right of the tabs and choose "Audit Log".
- Read the newest entries.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- Step 1: Work Orders opens on the "All" tab.
- Step 2: {Work-order-1} shows in the List.
- Step 3: the work order page opens. {Line-1} and {Line-2} show {Technician-A} in their Labor rows.
- Step 4: the "Work Order Log" window opens.
- Step 5: you have the number of "Lead tech changed" entries (for example 1) and the time.
- Step 6: the window closes.
- Step 7: "Lead Technician" reads {Technician-B}; it saves at once.
- Step 8: the "Work Order Log" window opens.
- Step 9: exactly one new "Lead tech changed" entry appears (the count from step 5 plus 1). It names {Technician-A} as the previous lead, {Technician-B} as the new lead, your user, and the time you noted. No separate entries appear for {Line-1} and {Line-2}, which followed the lead.
