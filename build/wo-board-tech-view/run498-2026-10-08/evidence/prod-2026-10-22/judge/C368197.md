# C368197 — Paging the List shows no repeated or missing work orders
Test in run 498: https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/tests/view/3792518

## Preconditions
Preconditions
- Needs: 1 user · 1 browser · about 30 minutes
- Build: the Work Orders QA build (sv10043, v26.40.8-7a95011), desktop browser, window at least 1024 px wide.
- Role: Owner/Admin. On the sign-in page click the Admin quick-login button (you appear as Admin ShopView). The Admin role has Work orders > View and Create & Edit (check: your initials (top right) > Settings > Roles & Permissions > Admin > edit).
- Location: Staging Heavy Duty - 9919 for the whole case (shown next to the bell; change it with your initials > Change Location).
- View: on Work Orders the List is shown (click List in the display switcher at the right of the toolbar; its tooltip reads "List").
- Customer "ZZAUTOTEST List Paging" (used by this case only) with one contact, one asset and enough work orders to fill more than one page of the List.
Setup
- Customers > New Customer > Name "ZZAUTOTEST List Paging" > Save (the customer page opens). Contacts tab > New Contact > First Name "ZZAUTOTEST", Last Name "Contact" > Save. Assets tab > New Asset > Contact = ZZAUTOTEST Contact > Year 2022 > Make: type "Freight" and pick Freightliner > Model: type "M2" and pick it > Unit "TRK-118" > Save (Contact and Make are required).
- Work Orders > Create Work Order > New Work Order: Customer = "ZZAUTOTEST List Paging" > Asset = its asset > Save. If a Confirmation says the customer is over their credit limit, click Create. The work order opens on its Lines tab with the New Line form open. Record the number shown at the top left as [WO-1] (the expected results call it "S1-702").
- Create further work orders the same way until there are more than one page of them (the List loads about 30 at a time as you scroll). Write down every number.
- Check the setup worked: All tab > Search "ZZAUTOTEST List Paging" lists them.

## Steps
- Open Work Orders, click the All tab, click Search and type "ZZAUTOTEST List Paging".
- At the bottom of the List pick the smallest rows-per-page choice (e.g. 10).
- Write down every work order number on page 1.
- Go to page 2.
- Write down every number on page 2.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- Pages 1 and 2 together list every work order you created exactly once (e.g. 10 + 2 = 12).
- No number appears on both pages and none is missing.
