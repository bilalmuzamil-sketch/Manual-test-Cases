# C368127 — Unassigned opens expanded, keeps its header in view and loads as you scroll
Test in run 498: https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/tests/view/3761648

## Preconditions
Setup (manual QA tester and Claude session)
Preconditions
- You are signed in as an Owner or Admin user whose role has Work orders > View and Work orders > Create & Edit, at {Location-A} (for example Staging Heavy Duty - 9919).
- Your browser window is at least 1024 px wide, so the display switcher (List, Tech View, Board View) shows at the right of the Work Orders toolbar.
- On the All tab at {Location-A} several hundred work orders have no lead technician (long-running test locations usually do), so the Unassigned group holds more work orders than fit on the screen. The view loads every row at once when about 300 or fewer match, so loading in batches shows only above that.
- Technician {Technician-A} (for example Esther Howard) is an active staff member at {Location-A} with Time Clock on and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User.
- You have not collapsed the Unassigned group in Tech View.
- On the Work Orders page nothing is ticked in Status, Asset on Site shows no choice (if its chip shows Yes or No, click the x on the chip), Assigned to me is off and Search is empty.

## Steps
- Open Work Orders and click the All tab.
- Click Tech View in the display switcher.
- Look at the Unassigned group.
- Scroll down through the Unassigned rows for a while.
- Look at the top of the table while you scroll.
- Keep scrolling towards the end of Unassigned and watch what happens when you reach the last loaded rows.
- While you are deep inside the group, click the arrow on the Unassigned header.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- Step 3: Unassigned opens expanded, and its header count matches the number of work orders with no lead technician that List shows on the All tab.
- Step 5: the Unassigned header stays on screen just under the column header row while its rows scroll past.
- Step 6: more rows appear in batches as you scroll (a short loading pause, or more rows appearing at the end), not all at once when the view opens. If no loading pause is visible, write "loading in batches not seen by hand" in the result comment and pass or fail on the rest.
- Step 7: Unassigned collapses without scrolling back up.
Source — where this behaviour comes from
Epic SV-10043; story SV-10045 (Story 2, Tech View — table grouped by lead technician); PRD "Work Orders — Board View & Tech View Display Options", Confluence 845185030, version 54 (15 Oct 2026, Chris Ward), S2-R18 and S2-E1, read 15 Oct 2026. Tech plan revised 24 Sep 2026 (uploaded 8 Oct 2026), section 3.1. Product owner answers: Chris Ward, WO Board and Tech View question sheet (https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1gBGmNB4B1bRrD2ZJvBn1tuvbfxFPjcsKddz_I5H3CvU), answer 20, read 15 Oct 2026. Source-verified 15 October 2026. Last checked against build v26.40.19-72f4f3b (staging) on 10/16/2026.
Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
- S2-R18: “Unassigned opens expanded unless the user collapsed it (S2-R13). While the user scrolls through Unassigned, its header stays on screen as in S2-E1, so it can be collapsed from anywhere in the group. When it holds more work orders than fit on screen, which on the All tab can be thousands, it loads them in pages as the user scrolls, as List does, never all at once.”
- S2-E1: “While the user scrolls through an expanded group, that group’s header stays on screen just under the column headings. Only one group header is held at a time: when the next group’s header reaches it, the next header pushes it off and takes its place. Unassigned’s header works the same way (S2-R18).”
- Tech plan 3.1: “One request returns every group with counts; it also returns all cards when the filtered total ≤ ~300 (configurable; true for the Work Orders view at every location). Above that, cards come for the pinned + on-screen groups, then batches of ≤ 6 groups horizontally and 25 cards per group vertically.”

## Newest PASSED result in run 498 (staging judgement)
Tested on staging, build v26.40.19-1493c63, on 17 October 2026.
- Result: Passed on staging, following the test's steps (round 3 re-run with a brand-new Admin user, 17 Oct).

- Step 5: deep inside the group the Unassigned header, with its count, stays just under the column headings while the rows scroll past.
- Step 6: a loading bar showed at each scroll stop: rows load in batches as you scroll.
- Step 7: clicking the arrow on the held Unassigned header, from deep inside the group, collapsed Unassigned straight away (no scrolling up first); the next group, Aaron Baker, follows.
