# C614719 — First Tab skips a collapsed or empty Unassigned to the first technician
Test in run 498: https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/tests/view/4007027

## Preconditions
Signed in as Admin; browser pinned to the fixture workplace; window 1280 px or wider for steps 1-7, 1024 px wide for steps 8-10, and for steps 11-13 also short enough that the Tech View table's body shows about two and a half group headers
Two technicians eligible to lead work here, A and B
Customer-A has two Approved work orders, WO-1 led by A and WO-2 led by B; Customer-B has one Approved work order with no lead, WO-3; both names start with Search-A
Saved Work Orders view reset over the API, then A pinned first and B second
All tab, Tech View shown, Search-A in Search, every group expanded, nothing in the table focused yet
At least two more technicians eligible to lead work here, four in all; the last of them, C, not pinned, so C's Board column comes after all the others and C's Tech View group last
Customer-C, whose name does not contain Search-A, has three Approved work orders led by C (WO-4 to WO-6): enough rows under C's group header for the table to scroll that header to the top of its rows

## Steps
- Click the arrow on the "Unassigned" group header to collapse the group.
- Click once in the "Search" box.
- Press Tab until something in the table shows a focus ring.
- Click "Board View" in the display switcher.
- Replace the text in "Search" with the name of {Customer-A}.
- Click once in the "Search" box.
- Press Tab until something on the board shows a focus ring.
- Make the browser window narrower, about the width of a small laptop screen (1024 px). Board View is still shown.
- Replace the text in "Search" with the name of {Customer-C}, then click once in the "Search" box.
- Press Tab until something on the board shows a focus ring.
- Click "Tech View" in the display switcher. Then make the browser window shorter, so that only about two and a half technician headings show in the table.
- Click once in the "Search" box (it still shows the name of {Customer-C}).
- Press Tab until something in the table shows a focus ring.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- Step 1: the "Unassigned" group collapses; its header stays and {Work-order-3}'s row is hidden. {Technician-A}'s group follows, then {Technician-B}'s.
- Step 2: the cursor is in the "Search" box and nothing in the table has a focus ring.
- Step 3: the focus ring lands on {Work-order-1}'s row, the first row of {Technician-A}'s group. It does not land on the collapsed "Unassigned" header or on any other group header.
- Step 4: Board View shows the "Unassigned" column first, then {Technician-A}'s and {Technician-B}'s columns.
- Step 5: only {Customer-A}'s work orders show: {Work-order-1} in {Technician-A}'s column and {Work-order-2} in {Technician-B}'s column. The "Unassigned" column has no cards.
- Step 6: the cursor is in the "Search" box and nothing on the board has a focus ring.
- Step 7: the focus ring lands on {Work-order-1}'s card, the first card in {Technician-A}'s column, not on a column header.
- Step 9: only {Work-order-4} shows, as a card in {Technician-C}’s column, which is off to the right, out of sight. The "Unassigned" column has no cards.
- Step 10: the board scrolls sideways to {Technician-C}’s column and the focus ring lands on {Work-order-4}’s card, which is fully on the screen. It does not land on the "Unassigned" column header.
- Step 11: Tech View shows "Unassigned" (no work orders) and the first technicians’ groups; {Technician-C}’s group, with {Work-order-4}, is further down, out of sight.
- Step 13: the list moves down to {Technician-C}’s group. {Work-order-4}’s row sits just below {Technician-C}’s heading and has the blue focus ring; the whole row and its ring can be seen and the heading does not cover them. The ring is not on the "Unassigned" heading or any other heading.
Source — where this behaviour comes from
Epic SV-10043; story SV-10054 (Story 11, Keyboard access and focus); PRD "Work Orders — Board View & Tech View Display Options", Confluence 845185030, version 62 (16 Oct 2026, Chris Ward), S11-R1, S2-R2 and S2-R17, read 16 Oct 2026; S11-R1 re-read at version 68 on 20 Oct 2026 (unchanged). Steps 8 to 13 (added 20 Oct 2026): Chris Ward in SV-11422, comment 79047 (19 Oct 2026), on a row hidden under its heading: “not acceptable: the user has to be able to see which row they’re on”, asking for the whole row to sit just below the sticky heading with its blue focus ring visible, as Board View does with its card. Product owner answers: Chris Ward, WO Board and Tech View question sheet (https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1gBGmNB4B1bRrD2ZJvBn1tuvbfxFPjcsKddz_I5H3CvU), answer 33, read 16 Oct 2026. Source-verified 16 October 2026. Last checked against build v26.40.19-1493c63 (staging) on 10/16/2026.
Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
- S11-R1: “Each view is a single Tab stop. Tab lands on one work order row (Tech View) or card (Board View), the one last focused, or, when none has been, the first work order shown in on-screen order: the first row or card in Unassigned, or the first technician’s first row or card when Unassigned is collapsed or empty (never a group or column header while a work order is shown; when every group is collapsed, Tab lands on the first group header; after the user arrows onto a header and tabs out and back in, Tab returns to a work order row while any row is shown; on Board View, if the last-focused card has scrolled out of view in its column, Tab lands on the first card in view); the focused row or card shows a visible focus ring.”
- S2-R2: “Keep Unassigned, containing work orders without a lead technician, fixed before all technician groups.”
- S2-R17: “Tech View orders pinned technicians the same way as Board View: after the fixed Unassigned group and before unpinned technicians, each newly pinned technician after the existing pins (S3-R15, S3-R16).”
