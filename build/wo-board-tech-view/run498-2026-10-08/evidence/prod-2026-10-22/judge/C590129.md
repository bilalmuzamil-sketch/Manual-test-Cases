# C590129 — Design: The Reassign lead technician window looks as designed, Unassigned first
Test in run 498: https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/tests/view/3995738

## Preconditions
Setup (manual QA tester and Claude session)
Preconditions
- You are signed in as {User-A} (for example Admin ShopView), an Owner or Admin user whose role has Work orders > View, Work orders > Create & Edit and See financial data, at {Location-A} (for example Staging Heavy Duty - 9919).
- Your browser window is 1440 px wide, with the browser zoom at 100%.
- The design picture DC-20-reassign-dialog-light-1440.png is open beside the app at 100% zoom (actual size); it is attached to this case.
- In the design pictures the names, numbers, dates and amounts are sample data, and the rank numbers (1, 2, 3 …) beside rows and cards are left out of this release, so do not compare those.
- The design's dialog title reads "Reassign Lead Tech" and it has no Unassigned row; the PRD names the action Reassign lead technician and offers Unassigned, so compare the layout and look only.
- Technician {Technician-A} (for example ZZAUTOTEST DC20 Bea Dialog) is an active technician at {Location-A} who has a profile photo.
- Technician {Technician-B} (for example ZZAUTOTEST DC20 Cal Dialog) is an active technician at {Location-A} who has no profile photo.
- Customer {Customer-A} (for example "ZZAUTOTEST DC20 Reassign Dialog") is used by this case only.
- Approved work order {Work-order-1} (for example S10043-17854) for {Customer-A} is led by {Technician-A}.
- On the Work Orders page nothing is chosen in any filter (if the Asset on Site chip shows Yes or No, click the x on the chip), Assigned to me is off, Search is empty and density is Regular (the default).

## Steps
- Open Work Orders, click the All tab and click Tech View in the display switcher.
- Hover {Work-order-1}'s row.
- Click the three-dot button at the right end of the row.
- Click Reassign lead technician.
- Look at the dialog from top to bottom.
- Type the first three letters of {Technician-B}'s first name in the dialog's search box.
- Click Cancel.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- Step 5: a white dialog with rounded corners sits over a dimmed page: a title, and under it the work order number and the customer in grey; an X at the top right; a search box with a magnifier icon; a list where each row has a round avatar, the name and a grey "N open" line under it ({Technician-A}'s row shows the photo, {Technician-B}'s a light-grey circle with initials); {Technician-A}'s row, the current lead, is tinted light blue with a blue check mark; Unassigned is the first row of the list, before every technician; a Cancel button at the bottom right. There is no grouping of the technicians. Apart from the Unassigned row, the layout matches the design picture.
- Step 6: the list narrows to the technicians whose names match.
- Step 7: the dialog closes and {Work-order-1} still has {Technician-A} as its lead.
Source — where this behaviour comes from
Epic SV-10043; story SV-10047 (Story 4, Reassign the lead technician); PRD "Work Orders — Board View & Tech View Display Options", Confluence 845185030, version 54 (15 Oct 2026, Chris Ward), S4-R12, S4-R15 and S4-R4, read 15 Oct 2026; Claude Design project "Work Orders" (Branko Cicovic), export of 14 Oct 2026, board "Work Orders.dc.html", state states__work-orders__tech-reassign-dialog__light__1440 (design pictures DC-20-reassign-dialog-light-1440.png, rendered from that export at the width in the name), read 14 Oct 2026. Where the PRD says nothing about the look, the design is the source. Product owner answers: Chris Ward, WO Board and Tech View question sheet (https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1gBGmNB4B1bRrD2ZJvBn1tuvbfxFPjcsKddz_I5H3CvU), answer 19, read 15 Oct 2026. Source-verified 15 October 2026. Last checked against build v26.40.19-1493c63 (staging) on 10/16/2026.
The PRD (version 52) listed the dialog's content, search, grouping, avatars and N open count under design updates still required, and the design has no Unassigned row. Chris Ward answered on 15 Oct 2026 (answer 19) that the dialog is tested as drawn (search, avatars, N open per technician, no grouping), plus Unassigned as its first row, and that the design is final for this release. PRD version 54 says so, and this case follows it.
