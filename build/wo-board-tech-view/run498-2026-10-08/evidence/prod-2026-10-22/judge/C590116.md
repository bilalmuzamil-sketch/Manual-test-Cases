# C590116 — Design: Drag handles on Tech View rows and technician headings, not Unassigned
Test in run 498: https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/tests/view/3995725

## Preconditions
Setup (manual QA tester and Claude session)
Preconditions
- You are signed in as {User-A} (for example Admin ShopView), an Owner or Admin user whose role has Work orders > View, Work orders > Create & Edit and See financial data, at {Location-A} (for example Staging Heavy Duty - 9919).
- Your browser window is 1440 px wide, with the browser zoom at 100%.
- The design pictures DC-07-tech-view-row-hover-light-1440.png and DC-07-tech-view-view-only-light-1440.png are open beside the app at 100% zoom (actual size); they are attached to this case.
- In the design pictures the names, numbers, dates and amounts are sample data, and the rank numbers (1, 2, 3 …) beside rows and cards are left out of this release, so do not compare those.
- The view-only design picture also shows grips on the rows; the PRD shows grips only to users who can drag, so use that picture only to compare the grip's drawing.
- Technician {Technician-A} (for example ZZAUTOTEST DC07 Hana Grip) is an active technician at {Location-A}.
- Customer {Customer-A} (for example "ZZAUTOTEST DC07 Grip") is used by this case only.
- Approved work orders {Work-order-1} (for example S10043-17817) and {Work-order-2} (for example S10043-17818) for {Customer-A} are both led by {Technician-A}.
- Estimate {Work-order-3} (for example S10043-17819) for {Customer-A} has no lead technician, so it sits in the Unassigned group.
- User {User-B} (for example ZZAUTOTEST DC07 Viewer) has a role with Work orders > View but not Work orders > Create & Edit, at {Location-A}, and can sign in.
- On the Work Orders page nothing is chosen in any filter (if the Asset on Site chip shows Yes or No, click the x on the chip), Assigned to me is off, Search is empty and density is Regular (the default).

## Steps
- Open Work Orders, click the All tab and click Tech View in the display switcher.
- Look at the left edge of every group header, the Unassigned header included.
- Look at the left edge of every work order row, {Work-order-3}'s row in Unassigned included.
- Hover {Work-order-1}'s row.
- Press on {Work-order-1}'s Customer cell, move the mouse down past {Work-order-2}'s row and release it there.
- Click {Work-order-1}'s Customer cell without moving the mouse.
- Click your initials (top right) and click Logout, then sign in as {User-B}.
- Open Work Orders, click the All tab and click Tech View in the display switcher.
- Look at the left edge of the work order rows and of the group headers.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- Step 2: every technician group header shows a six-dot grip (two columns of three small grey dots) at its left edge, as in the design pictures. The Unassigned header shows no grip.
- Step 3: every row shows the same six-dot grip at its left edge.
- Step 4: the row's background turns light grey and the grip stays visible, as in the row-hover picture.
- Step 5: the row moves although you grabbed it outside the grip, and {Work-order-1} now sits below {Work-order-2}.
- Step 6: the work order opens; the click did not start a drag.
- Step 9: no grip shows on the work order rows for {User-B}, who cannot drag work orders. Every technician group header still shows the grip, because {User-B} can reorder technicians. The Unassigned header shows no grip.
Source — where this behaviour comes from
Epic SV-10043; stories SV-10052 (Story 9, Drag to reorder), SV-10045 (Story 2, Tech View), SV-10047 (Story 4, Reassign the lead technician); PRD "Work Orders — Board View & Tech View Display Options", Confluence 845185030, version 68 (19 Oct 2026, Chris Ward), S9-R1a, S2-R8 and S4-N1, read 19 Oct 2026; Claude Design project "Work Orders" (Branko Cicovic), export of 14 Oct 2026, board "Work Orders.dc.html", states states__work-orders__tech-row-hover__light__1440 and states__work-orders__tech-view-only__light__1440 (design pictures DC-07-tech-view-row-hover-light-1440.png, DC-07-tech-view-view-only-light-1440.png, rendered from that export at the width in the name), read 14 Oct 2026. Covers the design defects SV-11320 (reported 14 Oct 2026 by Chris Ward). Where the PRD says nothing about the look, the design is the source. Product owner answers: Chris Ward, WO Board and Tech View question sheet (https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1gBGmNB4B1bRrD2ZJvBn1tuvbfxFPjcsKddz_I5H3CvU), answer 16, read 15 Oct 2026. Source-verified 19 October 2026. Last checked against build v26.40.19-72f4f3b (staging) on 10/16/2026.
The PRD (version 52) put a grip on every group header, Unassigned included, for users who can drag. Chris Ward answered on 15 Oct 2026 (answer 16): technician group-header grips show to everyone who can reorder technicians, view-only users included; row grips show only to users with Work Orders create and edit; the Unassigned header has no grip. PRD version 54 says so, and this case follows it. The design shows row grips to a view-only user too; this case follows the PRD.
Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
- S9-R1a: “In Tech View every work order row, Unassigned’s rows included, shows the design’s six-dot drag grip at its left edge to users who can drag work orders (S4-N1). Every technician group header shows the grip to every user who can reorder technicians (S9-R2), view-only users included, because the technician order is personal. The Unassigned group header shows no grip, because Unassigned cannot move (S9-R8). The whole row still drags (S2-R8); the grip shows that it can.”
- S9-R2: “Users with Work Orders view permission can reorder technician groups and columns. The order is saved per user.”
- S2-R8: “Clicking anywhere on a row, outside its own controls, opens the work order. A drag starts only after the pointer has moved a few pixels, so a click never turns into a drag.”
- S4-N1: “Require Work Orders create and edit permission to drag work orders or use Assign lead technician, Reassign lead technician or Unassign. A user without it who drags a card or row over another technician’s column or group sees the design’s note “You don’t have permission to assign technicians” at the top of that column or group, and the drop is refused.”
- Design — Work Orders.dc.html, Tech View group header grip: “title="Drag to reorder technicians" style="width: 28px; flex: none; display: grid; place-items: center; color: var(--sv-text-muted);"”
- Design — Work Orders.dc.html, Tech View row hover: “style-hover="background: var(--sv-surface-hover);"”
