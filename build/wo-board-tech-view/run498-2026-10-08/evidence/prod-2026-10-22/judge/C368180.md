# C368180 — An asset's Work Orders tab lists exactly its own work orders
Test in run 498: https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/tests/view/3792501

## Preconditions
Preconditions
- Needs: 1 user · 1 browser · about 20 minutes
- Build: the Work Orders QA build (sv10043, v26.40.8-7a95011), desktop browser, window at least 1024 px wide.
- Role: Owner/Admin. On the sign-in page click the Admin quick-login button (you appear as Admin ShopView). The Admin role has Work orders > View and Create & Edit (check: your initials (top right) > Settings > Roles & Permissions > Admin > edit).
- Location: Staging Heavy Duty - 9919 for the whole case (shown next to the bell; change it with your initials > Change Location).
- Customer "ZZAUTOTEST Asset Tab List" (used by this case only) with one contact and two assets: unit TRK-201 and unit TRK-202.
- For TRK-201 three work orders: [WO-A] Estimate with one line, [WO-B] Approved with two lines of which only Line 1 is completed, [WO-C] Complete. For TRK-202 one Approved work order [WO-D].
- Numbers are examples; all work orders made at this location (the tabs list only the chosen location's work orders).
Setup
- Customers > New Customer > Name "ZZAUTOTEST Asset Tab List" > Save (the customer page opens). Contacts tab > New Contact > First Name "ZZAUTOTEST", Last Name "Contact" > Save. Assets tab > New Asset > Contact = ZZAUTOTEST Contact > Year 2022 > Make: type "Freight" and pick Freightliner > Model: type "M2" and pick it > Unit "TRK-201" > Save (Contact and Make are required).
- On the customer page click Assets > New Asset > Contact = ZZAUTOTEST Contact > Year 1999 > Make: type "Ford", pick Ford > Model: type "Explorer", pick it > Unit "TRK-202" > Save.
- Work Orders > Create Work Order > New Work Order: Customer = "ZZAUTOTEST Asset Tab List" > Asset = TRK-201 > Save. If a Confirmation says the customer is over their credit limit, click Create. The work order opens on its Lines tab with the New Line form open. Record the number shown at the top left as [WO-A] (the expected results call it "S1-750"). In New Line > What Are You Doing? type "grease" and pick "Service - Full grease service" > leave Line Approved OFF > Save & Close. [WO-A] status reads Estimate.
- Work Orders > Create Work Order > New Work Order: Customer = "ZZAUTOTEST Asset Tab List" > Asset = TRK-201 > Save. If a Confirmation says the customer is over their credit limit, click Create. The work order opens on its Lines tab with the New Line form open. Record the number shown at the top left as [WO-B] (the expected results call it "S1-751"). In New Line > What Are You Doing? type "grease" and pick the ready-made line "Service - Full grease service" (it has no parts) > turn Line Approved on > Save & Add Line. In New Line > What Are You Doing? type "clutch" and pick the ready-made line "Service - Adjust clutch" (it has no parts) > turn Line Approved on > Save & Close. On line 1 click Complete > type any Tech Story > Complete Line (message "1 line completed."). Leave line 2 open. Status reads Approved.
- Work Orders > Create Work Order > New Work Order: Customer = "ZZAUTOTEST Asset Tab List" > Asset = TRK-201 > Save

## Steps
- Click Customers in the top menu, open "ZZAUTOTEST Asset Tab List", click its Assets tab, click the TRK-201 row (the asset page opens) and click its Work Orders tab.
- Read every work order number in the table.
