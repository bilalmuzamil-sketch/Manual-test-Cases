# C368173 — Changing a line's technician in Edit Line saves the new technician
Test in run 498: https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/tests/view/3792494

## Preconditions
Setup file (for Claude sessions; a tester does not need it)
Preconditions
- You are signed in with the "Admin" role at {Location-A}, in a browser window at least 1024 px wide.
For example: {Location-A} is Staging Heavy Duty - 9919.
- Technicians {Technician-A}, {Technician-B} and {Technician-D} are active at {Location-A}, and all three are offered in Edit Line's "Add Technician" list.
For example: AA ZZAUTOTEST C368173 Ana, AA ZZAUTOTEST C368173 Ben and AA ZZAUTOTEST C368173 Dee (names starting "AA" sit near the top of the work order page's technician lists, which offer only about the first 100 people in A to Z order (SV-11429)).
- Customer {Customer-A} has an Approved work order {Work-order-1} led by {Technician-A}, with one approved line, {Line-1}, whose technician is {Technician-D}.
For example: {Customer-A} is ZZAUTOTEST Edit Line Tech, {Work-order-1} is S1-722 and {Line-1} is "Service - Full grease service".
- If any record above is missing, mark this test Blocked: "setup not done".

## Steps
- Open "Work Orders" > "All".
- Type the name of {Customer-A} in "Search".
- Click {Work-order-1}.
- Click the "Lines" tab.
- Click {Line-1}'s name to open Edit Line.
- In "Technicians", click the X next to {Technician-D}.
- Click "Add Technician" and pick {Technician-B}.
- Click "Save & Close".
- Reload the page.
- Click the "Lines" tab.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- Step 1: Work Orders opens on the "All" tab.
- Step 2: {Work-order-1} shows in the List.
- Step 3: the work order page opens.
- Step 4: {Line-1}'s Labor row shows {Technician-D}.
- Step 5: Edit Line opens with {Technician-D} under "Technicians".
- Step 6: {Technician-D} is removed from the list.
- Step 7: {Technician-B} is added under "Technicians".
- Step 8: the line saves, with no error message.
- Step 9: the page reloads.
- Step 10: {Line-1}'s Labor row shows {Technician-B} as its technician.
