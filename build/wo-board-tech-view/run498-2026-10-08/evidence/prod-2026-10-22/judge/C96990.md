# C96990 — Tech View row text stays the same size at every density
Test in run 498: https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/tests/view/3481934

## Preconditions
Setup file (for Claude sessions; a tester does not need it)
Preconditions
- You are signed in with the "Admin" role at {Location-A}, in a browser window at least 1024 px wide.
For example: {Location-A} is Staging Heavy Duty - 9919.
- Technician {Technician-A} is active at {Location-A}, with Time Clock on and a Role other than "Office User" or "Time Clock User".
For example: {Technician-A} is Esther Howard.
- Customer {Customer-A} has one Approved work order, {Work-order-1}, led by {Technician-A}.
For example: {Customer-A} is "ZZAUTOTEST F1 Compact Text" and {Work-order-1} is S10043-17651.
- Tech View shows the "Progress" column (it is on by default).
- Starting screen: "Work Orders" > "All" tab, "Tech View" shown, the name of {Customer-A} in "Search", nothing ticked in "Status", no choice in "Asset on Site", "Assigned to me" off.
- If any record above is missing, mark this test Blocked: "setup not done".

## Steps
- Click the density button and choose "Regular".
- Look at the size of the text in {Work-order-1}'s row, of the column header labels and of the percentage beside the progress bar.
- Click the density button and choose "Compact".
- Compare the same texts with what you saw at step 2.
- Click the density button and choose "Comfortable".
- Compare the same texts with what you saw at step 2.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- Step 1: "Regular" is in use.
- Step 2: the row text is the normal size (14 px). The column header labels and the percentage are smaller (12 px).
- Step 3: "Compact" is in use and the rows are tighter.
- Step 4: at Compact the row text is the same size as at Regular (14 px), not smaller. The column header labels and the percentage keep their own smaller size (12 px), the same as at Regular.
- Step 5: "Comfortable" is in use and the rows are roomier.
- Step 6: at Comfortable every text is the same size as at Regular.
The PRD (version 52) said only that Compact must not go below the minimum body text size, while the design's Compact rows use 13 px. Chris Ward answered on 15 Oct 2026 (answer 27) that row and card text is 14 px at every density and text the design sets at 12 px stays 12 px; PRD versions 54 to 71 say so, and this case follows it.
Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
From: PRD "Work Orders — Board View & Tech View Display Options", Confluence 845185030, version 71 (20 Oct 2026, Chris Ward), read 20 Oct 2026 · Epic SV-10043, story SV-10049 (Story 6, Density in Tech View and Board View)
- S6-R4: “Row and card text is 14px at every density; Compact never shrinks it. Text the design sets at 12px at every density, such as column header labels (S2-R21), the progress percentage and secondary lines, stays 12px.”
- Terminology, Density: “Design must define the three variants without reducing text below the application’s minimum body size.”
From: Chris Ward's answer 27, "WO Board and Tech View – questions for Chris Ward (14 Oct 2026)" (Google Sheet 1gBGmNB4B1bRrD2ZJvBn1tuvbfxFPjcsKddz_I5H3CvU, tab "Questions for Chris"), answered 15 Oct 2026, read 15 Oct 2026
- Chris Ward's answer 27: “27. (a). Row and card text is 14px at every density, and Compact never shrinks it. Text the design sets at 12px at every density (column header labels, the percentage, secondary lines) stays 12px. S6-R4.”
Not yet checked against a build in this wording.
