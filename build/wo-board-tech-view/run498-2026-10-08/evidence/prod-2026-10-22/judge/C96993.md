# C96993 — Cards and Tech View's Assigned Techs show the lead and line technicians
Test in run 498: https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/tests/view/3481937

## Preconditions
Setup (manual QA tester and Claude session)
Preconditions
- You are signed in as an Owner or Admin user whose role has Work orders > View and Work orders > Create & Edit and Schedule access, at {Location-A} (for example Staging Heavy Duty - 9919).
- Your browser window is at least 1024 px wide, so the display switcher (List, Tech View, Board View) shows at the right of the Work Orders toolbar.
- Technician {Technician-A} (for example Esther Howard) is an active staff member at {Location-A} with Time Clock on and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User.
- Technician {Technician-B} (for example Ralph Edwards) is an active staff member at {Location-A} with Time Clock on and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User.
- Technician {Technician-C} (for example Dana Ortiz) is an active staff member at {Location-A} with Time Clock on and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User.
- Technician {Technician-D} (for example Jenny Wilson) is an active staff member at {Location-A} with Time Clock on, the Service department and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User, so they have a row on the Schedule.
- Approved work order {Work-order-1} (for example S10043-17605) for customer {Customer-A} (for example "ZZAUTOTEST F3 Avatar Group") is led by {Technician-A} and has three approved lines: {Line-1} (for example "Service - Full grease service") given to {Technician-B}, {Line-2} (for example "Service - Adjust clutch") given to {Technician-C}, and {Line-3} (for example "Replace - Brake pot") with no labor technician.
- {Technician-D} has a Schedule shift on {Line-3} of {Work-order-1} tomorrow 8:00–10:00, so four different people are on {Work-order-1}: one lead, two line technicians and one scheduled technician.

## Steps
- Open Work Orders, click the All tab and click Board View in the display switcher (tooltip "Board View").
- Type the name of {Customer-A} in Search.
- Click Fields to display, turn line technicians on and close the menu.
- Read the avatar group on the {Work-order-1} card.
- Hover each avatar and read the name shown.
- Click Tech View in the display switcher.
- Click the Column Selection button and make sure Assigned Techs is on (it is on by default; turn it on if it was turned off), then close the menu.
- Read the avatar group in {Work-order-1}'s Assigned Techs cell.
- Click List in the display switcher.
- Click the Column Selection button and read the column names.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- Steps 4 and 5: the card shows a compact avatar group of 4: {Technician-A} (the lead) plus {Technician-B}, {Technician-C} and {Technician-D}. {Technician-D} counts as {Line-3}'s technician because of the Schedule shift.
- Step 8: Tech View's Assigned Techs column shows the same 4-avatar group.
- Step 10: List's menu does not offer an Assigned Techs column.
Source — where this behaviour comes from
Epic SV-10043; story SV-10050 (Story 7, Show line technicians on cards and rows); PRD "Work Orders — Board View & Tech View Display Options", Confluence 845185030, version 52 (14 Oct 2026, Chris Ward), S7-R1 and the Story 7 context note (scheduled technicians count as the line's technicians) and S7-R7 (S7-R7 rewritten in version 51 on 14 Oct 2026 (Chris Ward ruling after Sasha Grosman's staging review: Tech View's default columns are the design's 13, and Assigned Techs is on by default); the Tech View step now only makes sure Assigned Techs is on, the expected results are unchanged), read 14 Oct 2026. Review Decisions DR-33. Design export of 8 Oct 2026 (Claude Design project 787fef1a, Work Orders.dc.html): List's Columns shows an "Assigned Tech" column; the PRD (no column in List) is followed. Source-verified 14 October 2026. Last checked against build v26.40.8-7a95011 on 10/8/2026.
Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
- S7-R1: “Board View cards (when the line technicians field is on) and a new optional Assigned Techs column in Tech View show a compact avatar group: the lead technician plus the distinct technicians assigned to work order lines. List does not get this column.”
- Story 7 context note: “this feature counts technicians scheduled on a line as that line’s technicians (S7-R1).”
- S7-R7: “In Tech View the Assigned Techs column sits immediately after Lines, matching the design. It is on by default (S5-R4) and can be removed through the column chooser (S2-R4).”
