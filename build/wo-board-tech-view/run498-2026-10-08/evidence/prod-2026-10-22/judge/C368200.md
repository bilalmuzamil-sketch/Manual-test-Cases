# C368200 — Clicking a row's Asset on Site toggle does not open the work order
Test in run 498: https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/tests/view/3792521

## Preconditions
Preconditions
- Needs: 1 user · 1 browser · about 5 minutes
- Build: the Work Orders QA build (sv10043, v26.40.8-7a95011), desktop browser, window at least 1024 px wide.
- Role: Owner/Admin. On the sign-in page click the Admin quick-login button (you appear as Admin ShopView). The Admin role has Work orders > View and Create & Edit (check: your initials (top right) > Settings > Roles & Permissions > Admin > edit).
- Location: Staging Heavy Duty - 9919 for the whole case (shown next to the bell; change it with your initials > Change Location).
- View: on Work Orders the List is shown (click List in the display switcher at the right of the toolbar; its tooltip reads "List").
- Customer "ZZAUTOTEST Row Toggle" (used by this case only) with one contact, one asset and one Approved work order [WO-1].
Setup
- Customers > New Customer > Name "ZZAUTOTEST Row Toggle" > Save (the customer page opens). Contacts tab > New Contact > First Name "ZZAUTOTEST", Last Name "Contact" > Save. Assets tab > New Asset > Contact = ZZAUTOTEST Contact > Year 2022 > Make: type "Freight" and pick Freightliner > Model: type "M2" and pick it > Unit "TRK-118" > Save (Contact and Make are required).
- Work Orders > Create Work Order > New Work Order: Customer = "ZZAUTOTEST Row Toggle" > Asset = its asset > Save. If a Confirmation says the customer is over their credit limit, click Create. The work order opens on its Lines tab with the New Line form open. Record the number shown at the top left as [WO-1] (the expected results call it "S1-792").
- In New Line > What Are You Doing? type "grease" and pick the ready-made line "Service - Full grease service" (it has no parts) > turn Line Approved on > Save & Close.
- Check the setup worked: All tab > Search "ZZAUTOTEST Row Toggle" lists [WO-1] with a location pin icon in its On Site column.

## Steps
- Open Work Orders, click the All tab, click Search and type "ZZAUTOTEST Row Toggle".
- In the [WO-1] row click the Asset on Site toggle (the location pin icon in the On Site column).
- Read the page you are on and the toggle.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- You are still on the Work Orders List (the work order did not open) and the toggle's icon has flipped.
