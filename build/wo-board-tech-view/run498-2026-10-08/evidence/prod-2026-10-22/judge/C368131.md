# C368131 — Pinned technician columns after deactivation or removal from this location
Test in run 498: https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/tests/view/3761652

## Preconditions
Setup file (for Claude sessions; a tester does not need it)
Preconditions
- You are signed in as an Admin user (Work orders > View and Create & Edit) at {Location-A}, in a desktop browser window at least 1024 px wide. The company has a second location, {Location-B}.
For example: {Location-A} is Staging Heavy Duty - 9919 and {Location-B} is Staging Lethbridge - 4310.
- A second user, {User-B}, whose role has Work orders > View only, is signed in at {Location-A} in a second browser or a private window.
For example: {User-B} is ZZAUTOTEST C368131 Viewer.
- Three active technicians work at {Location-A}: {Technician-A}, and {Technician-B} and {Technician-C}, who are made for this case only. {Technician-B} leads no work order.
For example: {Technician-A} is Esther Howard, {Technician-B} is ZZAUTOTEST Jenny Pinned and {Technician-C} is ZZAUTOTEST Theresa Pinned.
- Customer {Customer-A} has two Approved work orders, with no shift booked for them on the Schedule: {Work-order-1} led by {Technician-A}, and {Work-order-2} led by {Technician-C}.
For example: {Customer-A} is ZZAUTOTEST C368131 Pinned Gone, {Work-order-1} is S10043-17691 and {Work-order-2} is S10043-17692.
- You and {User-B} both have {Technician-B} and {Technician-C} pinned in Board View.
- Starting screen: the Work Orders page with no filter set (nothing ticked in "Status", no choice in "Asset on Site", "Assigned to me" off, "Search" empty).
- If any record above is missing, mark this test Blocked: "setup not done".

## Steps
- Go to your initials (top right) > "Settings" > "Staff".
- Deactivate {Technician-B}: click the edit icon on {Technician-B}, click "Deactivate Account" and confirm.
- Move {Technician-C} to {Location-B}: click the edit icon on {Technician-C}, change "Location" to {Location-B} and click "Save & Close"; then click the "Department(s)" button on her row, click the bin icon next to {Location-A} and close the window (the Location field alone leaves her at {Location-A}).
- Click "Work Orders" in the top menu.
- Click the "All" tab and type the name of {Customer-A} in "Search".
- Click "Board View" in the display switcher.
- Look at the pinned columns right after "Unassigned".
- Find {Technician-C}'s column further along and look at its header.
- Drag {Work-order-1} from {Technician-A}'s column onto {Technician-B}'s column and let go.
- Drag {Work-order-1} from {Technician-A}'s column onto {Technician-C}'s column and let go.
- Read the message that appears and check where {Work-order-1} is.
- Drag {Work-order-2} from {Technician-C}'s column onto the "Unassigned" column and let go.
- Check which column {Work-order-2} is in and whether {Technician-C} still has a column.
- In the second browser, as {User-B}, go to "Work Orders" > "All" > "Board View".
- Look at the pinned columns right after "Unassigned".

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- Step 7: {Technician-B}'s column is still shown right after Unassigned, so the pin is kept. It is marked inactive and empty, and it reads "No work orders", not "Drag a work order here to assign it".
- Step 7: {Technician-C} is not among the pinned columns.
- Step 8: {Technician-C}'s column appears further along as a normal column holding {Work-order-2}, with no pin icon and no inactive marker.
- Step 9: the card cannot be dropped on {Technician-B}'s column. {Work-order-1} stays in {Technician-A}'s column.
- Steps 10 and 11: the drop is refused with the message "This technician can't be assigned work orders." and {Work-order-1} stays in {Technician-A}'s column.
- Step 13: {Work-order-2} is in Unassigned and {Technician-C} no longer has a column at all.
- Step 15: for the view-only {User-B}, {Technician-B}'s column also reads "No work orders", and there is no {Technician-C} column.
Source — where this behaviour comes from
Epic SV-10043; stories SV-10046 (Story 3, Board View — board by lead technician) and SV-10047 (Story 4, Reassign or unassign the lead technician); PRD "Work Orders — Board View & Tech View Display Options", Confluence 845185030, version 49 (14 Oct 2026, Chris Ward), S3-R20a, S3-E3, S3-N4, S3-R20b and its context note (Sasha Grosman's answer to SQ-11, 24 Sep 2026), S3-R20c (added in version 49) and S4-N5 (widened in version 49), both from Chris Ward's answer 2 of 14 Oct 2026 to our question sheet; read 14 Oct 2026. Sasha Grosman's answer to engineering of 24 Sep 2026 treated a technician who is no longer at this location like an inactive one; PRD version 49 (14 Oct 2026) says that column shows no inactive marker and refuses drops with its own message, and we take the later PRD as prevailing. Source-verified 14 October 2026. This version of the case has not yet been checked against a build (the previous version, updated 9 Oct 2026 for PRD v44, was not re-run on the QA build).
Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
- S3-R20a: “The empty column of a pinned technician who has been deactivated shows “No work orders” to every user, because nothing can be dropped there (S4-N5).”
- S3-E3: “Retain a technician’s pin after deactivation.”
- S3-N4: “Show an inactive indicator on deactivated technician columns.”
- S3-R20b: “A pinned technician who is not enrolled at this location gets no column here. If they still lead work orders at this location, they appear as a normal, unpinned column holding that work.”
- S3-R20c: “The column of S3-R20b takes no drops. Dropping a work order on it is refused with "This technician can't be assigned work orders." and the work order stays where it was. The column shows no inactive marker, because the technician is not deactivated (S3-N4). Once it holds no work orders, the column is no longer shown.”
- S4-N5: “Nothing can be dropped onto the column/group of an inactive technician (S2-N2, S3-N2) or of a technician who is not enrolled at this location (S3-R20c).”
- Context note (S3-R20a, S3-R20b): “A pin only counts at locations where the technician is enrolled.”
