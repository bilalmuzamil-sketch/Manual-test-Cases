## Steps
- Open [WO-1] and click the Lines tab.
- Click New Line.
- In What Are You Doing? type "ZZAUTOTEST Oil change", wait until the list under it reads "No results", then press Enter (if a ready-made line is offered instead, change a word until it reads "No results"). In Estimated Time type 1.
- In Technicians click Add Technician and pick [Tech-D].
- Click Save & Close.
- Reload the page and read the new line's Labor row.
