# C368193 — Changing tab in the List clears the search box
Test in run 498: https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/tests/view/3792514

## Preconditions
Preconditions
- Needs: 1 user · 1 browser · about 5 minutes
- Build: the Work Orders QA build (sv10043, v26.40.8-7a95011), desktop browser, window at least 1024 px wide.
- Role: Owner/Admin. On the sign-in page click the Admin quick-login button (you appear as Admin ShopView). The Admin role has Work orders > View and Create & Edit (check: your initials (top right) > Settings > Roles & Permissions > Admin > edit).
- Location: Staging Heavy Duty - 9919 for the whole case (shown next to the bell; change it with your initials > Change Location).
- View: on Work Orders the List is shown (click List in the display switcher at the right of the toolbar; its tooltip reads "List").
- Customer "ZZAUTOTEST Tab Clears Search" (used by this case only) with one contact, one asset and one Approved work order [WO-1].
Setup
- Customers > New Customer > Name "ZZAUTOTEST Tab Clears Search" > Save (the customer page opens). Contacts tab > New Contact > First Name "ZZAUTOTEST", Last Name "Contact" > Save. Assets tab > New Asset > Contact = ZZAUTOTEST Contact > Year 2022 > Make: type "Freight" and pick Freightliner > Model: type "M2" and pick it > Unit "TRK-118" > Save (Contact and Make are required).
- Work Orders > Create Work Order > New Work Order: Customer = "ZZAUTOTEST Tab Clears Search" > Asset = its asset > Save. If a Confirmation says the customer is over their credit limit, click Create. The work order opens on its Lines tab with the New Line form open. Record the number shown at the top left as [WO-1] (the expected results call it "S1-780").
- In New Line > What Are You Doing? type "grease" and pick the ready-made line "Service - Full grease service" (it has no parts) > turn Line Approved on > Save & Close.
- Check the setup worked: Work Orders > List > All tab > Search "ZZAUTOTEST Tab Clears Search" lists [WO-1].

## Steps
- Open Work Orders and click the All tab.
- Click Search and type "ZZAUTOTEST Tab Clears Search".
- Click the Estimates tab.
- Read the search box and the rows.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- The search box is empty and the Estimates tab shows its work orders without the search applied.
