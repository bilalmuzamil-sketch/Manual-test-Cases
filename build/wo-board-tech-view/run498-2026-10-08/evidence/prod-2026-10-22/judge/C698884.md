## Steps
- Look at the toolbar between the filters and the display switcher.
- Click "Column Selection".
- Press Escape.
- Click "Tech View" in the display switcher.
- Click the density button in the toolbar.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- Step 1: there is no density button. "Column Selection" sits centred between the toolbar's two dividers, with no empty gap or double divider where the density button was.
- Step 2: the picker of card fields opens, the same picker "Fields to display" opened before.
- Step 3: the picker closes and the cards keep their usual spacing.
- Step 4: Tech View shows, and its toolbar still has the density button.
- Step 5: the menu offers "Compact", "Regular" and "Comfortable".
Known problem (SV-11580, Open): until it ships, Board View still shows the density button and the field picker is named "Fields to display". (1) If you see exactly that, mark this test Failed and raise nothing new. (2) If it fails in a different way, report it. (3) If it behaves as the expected results say, mark it Passed and tell the QA lead: the change has shipped.

## Newest PASSED result in run 498 (staging judgement)
Tested on staging, build v26.40.20-4c6c9e0, on 21 October 2026.
- Result: Passed on staging - outcome (3) of this test: the change for SV-11580 has shipped (staging build v26.40.20-4c6c9e0, ticket in Testing Stage).
