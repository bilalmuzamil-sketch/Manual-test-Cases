# C682446 — Moving a timesheet entry to another line takes you off the old line
Test in run 498: https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/tests/view/4062447

## Preconditions
Setup file (for Claude sessions; a tester does not need it)
Preconditions
- You are signed in with the "Admin" role at {Location-A}, in a browser window at least 1024 px wide.
For example: {Location-A} is Staging Heavy Duty - 9919.
- Technician {Technician-A} is active at {Location-A}.
For example: ZZAUTOTEST C682446 Ana.
- Work order {Work-order-1} at {Location-A} has two Approved lines, {Line-1} and {Line-2}, each with {Technician-A} under Labor, and you are not under Labor on either line.
For example: {Work-order-1} is S10043-18489, {Line-1} is "Service - Cabin air filter" and {Line-2} is "Replace - Suspension air bag".
- You are not clocked in to any line.
- Starting screen: the work order page of {Work-order-1}, "Lines" tab.
- If any record above is missing, mark this test Blocked: "setup not done".

## Steps
- On {Line-1}, click "Start" under Labor.
- After about a minute, click "Stop".
- Leave "Line Completed?" off and click "Clock Out".
- Click ⋮ (top right of the lines table) > "Timesheets" and open the new entry.
- Change "Line" to {Line-2} and click "Save".
- Reload the page and open the "Lines" tab.
- Click ⋮ > "Timesheets" and open the same entry.
- Change "Line" to {Line-1} and click "Save".
- Reload the page and open the "Lines" tab.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- Step 1: the clock starts and you appear under {Line-1}'s Labor.
- Step 2: the clock stops.
- Step 3: you are clocked out.
- Step 4: your timesheet entry for {Line-1} opens.
- Step 5: the entry is saved with {Line-2} as its line.
- Step 6: {Line-1}'s Labor lists only {Technician-A}; you are no longer on it. {Line-2}'s Labor lists {Technician-A} and you, with your hours.
- Step 7: the entry opens, showing {Line-2}.
- Step 8: the entry is saved with {Line-1} as its line.
- Step 9: {Line-2}'s Labor lists only {Technician-A}. {Line-1}'s Labor lists {Technician-A} and you once, with your hours. Neither line has an extra labor row.
This fix ships in the bugfix release (PR #3619), separately from the Board View and Tech View work. On a site without it you will see what the ticket reported: at step 6 you stay under {Line-1}'s Labor with 0.00 hours, and every move back and forth adds another labor row. (1) If you see exactly that, mark this test Failed and raise nothing new. (2) If it fails in a different way, report it. (3) If the lines read as above, mark it Passed.
