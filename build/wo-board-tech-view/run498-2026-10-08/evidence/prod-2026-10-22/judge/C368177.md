- Customers > New Customer > Name "ZZAUTOTEST Move Labor" > Save (the customer page opens). Contacts tab > New Contact > First Name "ZZAUTOTEST", Last Name "Contact" > Save. - Work Orders > Create Work Order > New Work Order: Customer = "ZZAUTOTEST Move Labor" > Asset = its asset > Save. If a Confirmation says the customer is over their credit limit, click Create. The work order opens on its Lines tab with the New Line form open. Record the number shown at the top left as [WO-1] (the expected results call it "S1-732").
- In New Line > What Are You Doing? type "grease" and pick the ready-made line "Service - Full grease service" (it has no parts) > turn Line Approved on > Save & Add Line. In New Line > What Are You Doing? type "clutch" and pick the ready-made line "Service - Adjust clutch" (it has no parts) > turn Line Approved on > Save & Close. Leave Lead Technician empty.
- In browser 2 sign in as [Tech-A], open [WO-1] > Lines tab > click Start on Line 1's Labor row, wait at least 1 minute, click Stop > type any text in What Have You Been Doing On This Line? > Clock Out.
