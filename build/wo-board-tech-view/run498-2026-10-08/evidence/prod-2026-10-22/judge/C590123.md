# C590123 — Design: Dragging shows a blue line where it will drop, a dark message if refused
Test in run 498: https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/tests/view/3995732

## Preconditions
Setup (manual QA tester and Claude session)
Preconditions
- You are signed in as {User-A} (for example Admin ShopView), an Owner or Admin user whose role has Work orders > View, Work orders > Create & Edit and See financial data, at {Location-A} (for example Staging Heavy Duty - 9919).
- Your browser window is 1440 px wide, with the browser zoom at 100%.
- The design pictures DC-14-board-drag-allowed-light-1440.png, DC-14-board-drag-refused-light-1440.png and DC-14-tech-drag-reorder-light-1440.png are open beside the app at 100% zoom (actual size); they are attached to this case.
- In the design pictures the names, numbers, dates and amounts are sample data, and the rank numbers (1, 2, 3 …) beside rows and cards are left out of this release, so do not compare those.
- The design's refusal message reads "Invoiced · can't be reassigned"; the PRD sets no words for it, so compare only its look and place.
- Technician {Technician-A} (for example ZZAUTOTEST DC14 Tia Drag) is an active technician at {Location-A}.
- Technician {Technician-B} (for example ZZAUTOTEST DC14 Uma Drag) is an active technician at {Location-A}.
- Customer {Customer-A} (for example "ZZAUTOTEST DC14 Drag Looks") is used by this case only.
- Approved work orders {Work-order-1} (for example S10043-17839) and {Work-order-2} (for example S10043-17840) for {Customer-A} are both led by {Technician-A}.
- Estimate {Work-order-3} (for example S10043-17841) for {Customer-A} has no lead technician and no approved line, so it sits in Unassigned.
- Work order {Work-order-4} (for example S10043-17842) for {Customer-A} is Invoiced and led by {Technician-B}.
- On the Work Orders page nothing is chosen in any filter (if the Asset on Site chip shows Yes or No, click the x on the chip), Assigned to me is off, Search is empty and density is Regular (the default).

## Steps
- Open Work Orders, click the All tab and click Board View in the display switcher.
- Press on {Work-order-3}'s card in Unassigned and drag it over {Technician-A}'s column, keeping the mouse button down.
- Release the mouse button.
- Press on {Work-order-4}'s card and drag it over {Technician-A}'s column, keeping the mouse button down.
- Release the mouse button.
- Click Tech View in the display switcher.
- Press on {Work-order-1}'s row and drag it to just below {Work-order-2}'s row, keeping the mouse button down.
- Release the mouse button.
- Press on the grip of {Technician-A}'s group header and drag it below {Technician-B}'s group, keeping the mouse button down.
- Release the mouse button.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- Step 2: the dragged card looks faded and a thin blue line shows where it will land in {Technician-A}'s column, as in the allowed-drop design picture.
- Step 3: the card lands in {Technician-A}'s column where the blue line was.
- Step 4: no blue line shows over {Technician-A}'s column; instead a small dark message with a no-entry icon (a circle with a slash) appears at the top of the column, as in the refused-drop design picture.
- Step 5: {Work-order-4} stays in {Technician-B}'s column.
- Step 7: a thin blue line shows between the rows where the row will land, as in the reorder design picture.
- Step 8: {Work-order-1} now sits below {Work-order-2}.
- Step 9: a thin blue line shows between the groups where {Technician-A}'s group will land.
- Step 10: {Technician-A}'s group now sits below {Technician-B}'s group.
Source — where this behaviour comes from
Epic SV-10043; stories SV-10052 (Story 9, Drag to reorder), SV-10047 (Story 4, Reassign the lead technician); PRD "Work Orders — Board View & Tech View Display Options", Confluence 845185030, version 52 (14 Oct 2026, Chris Ward), S9-R1, S9-R2, S4-N2 and S4-N7, read 14 Oct 2026; Claude Design project "Work Orders" (Branko Cicovic), export of 14 Oct 2026, board "Work Orders.dc.html", states states__work-orders__board-drag-allowed__light__1440, states__work-orders__board-drag-refused-locked__light__1440 and states__work-orders__tech-drag-reorder__light__1440 (design pictures DC-14-board-drag-allowed-light-1440.png, DC-14-board-drag-refused-light-1440.png, DC-14-tech-drag-reorder-light-1440.png, rendered from that export at the width in the name), read 14 Oct 2026. Where the PRD says nothing about the look, the design is the source. Source-verified 14 October 2026. Last checked against build v26.40.17-a5c4210 (staging) on 10/15/2026.
The design's refusal words differ from the PRD, which sets none for this message; only the message's look is judged.
Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
- S9-R1: “Allow dragging work orders within or between technician groups/columns in Tech View and Board View.”
- S9-R2: “Users with Work Orders view permission can reorder technician groups and columns. The order is saved per user.”
- S4-N2: “Do not allow the lead technician to change in Invoiced or Paid status. An Invoiced or Paid work order can still be dragged to reorder it within its current technician (or within Unassigned), but it cannot be moved to another technician, to Unassigned, or out of Unassigned. Declined and Complete work orders can be moved freely, as today.”
- S4-N7: “Visually indicate that an Invoiced or Paid work order can only be reordered within its current technician, not moved to another one.”
- Design — Work Orders.dc.html, drop line: “border-radius: 2px; background: var(--sv-accent); pointer-events: none; z-index: 350;”
- Design — Work Orders.dc.html, refused drop message: “border-radius: 6px; background: var(--sv-surface-inverse); color: var(--sv-text-inverse); font-size: 12px; font-weight: 500;”
- Design — Work Orders.dc.html, dragged card: “style.opacity = "0.4";”

## Newest PASSED result in run 498 (staging judgement)
Tested on staging, build v26.40.19-1493c63, on 17 October 2026.
