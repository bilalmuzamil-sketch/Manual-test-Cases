# C96948 — Pins are shared with Tech View and follow the user to another device
Test in run 498: https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/tests/view/3481892

## Preconditions
Preconditions
- Needs: 2 users · 2 browsers · about 20 minutes
- Build: the Work Orders QA build (sv10043, v26.40.8-7a95011). A desktop browser with the window at least 1024 pixels wide (full screen on an ordinary laptop or monitor). Below that width the page shows only List.
- [User-A]: an Owner/Admin user (on a QA branch: the Admin quick-login button on the sign-in page; you appear as "Admin ShopView"). The Admin role holds Work orders > View and Work orders > Create & Edit (to check: your initials at the top right > Settings > Roles & Permissions > Admin > Work orders).
- One location for the whole case, shown in the top bar next to the bell (e.g. "Staging Heavy Duty - 9919"); everything below is created in this location.
- [User-B]: a second user whose role has Work orders > View (e.g. a new staff member with Role "Admin" at this location, Time Clock off), with an email you can open.
- Technicians "Ralph Edwards" and "Jenny Wilson" (the expected results name them, so keep these names) and [Tech-A] (e.g. "Esther Howard") at this location. A person only counts as a technician here when the staff member is active (not deactivated), Time Clock is on, the Role is neither Office User nor Time Clock User, and this location is picked in their Location field.
- Customer "ZZAUTOTEST F2 Pins Follow User" (used by this case only), with one contact and one asset, so this case's work orders are kept apart from every other work order at the location.
- Three Approved work orders for that customer, one led by each technician.
- A second browser (or computer) is available. Neither user has any pin at the start.
Setup
- Sign in with the Admin quick-login button on the sign-in page (you appear as "Admin ShopView"). Check the location in the top bar next to the bell; to change it click your initials at the top right > Change Location.
- Settings (your initials at the top right > Settings) > Staff: confirm each technician below is active at this location with Time Clock on, or add it: New Staff Member > First Name and Last Name, an Email, Role "Technician", at least one Department (required), Location = this location, Time Clock on > Save & Close. Record them as "Ralph Edwards" (keep this name, the expected results use it); "Jenny Wilson" (keep this name, the expected results use it); [Tech-A] (e.g. Esther Howard).
- Settings > Staff > New Staff Member > First Name and Last Name, an email you can open, Role "Admin", at least one Department (required), Location = this location, Time Clock off > Save & Close. Accept the invitation from that email and set a password. Record this user as [User-B].
- Top menu Customers > New Customer > Name "ZZAUTOTEST F2 Pins Follow User" > Save (if it already exists, open it instead). On the customer page click the Contacts tab > New Contact > First Name and Last Name > Save (a new asset needs a contact). Then click the Assets tab > New Asset > pick the Contact, then Year, Make, Model and Unit (e.g. 2022, Freightliner, M2, TRK-118) 

## Steps
- Open Work Orders, click the All tab, click Search and type ZZAUTOTEST F2 Pins Follow User.
- In the display switcher at the right of the toolbar, click Board View (tooltip "Board View").
- Pin Ralph Edwards and Jenny Wilson.
- In the display switcher at the right of the toolbar, click Tech View (tooltip "Tech View").
- Look at the group order and pin controls.
- Sign out, sign in again and open Work Orders in Board View.
- In the second browser sign in as the same user and open Board View.
- Sign in as [User-B] and open Board View.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- Tech View shows Ralph Edwards and Jenny Wilson pinned, right after Unassigned, without pinning them again.
- After signing in again, and in the second browser, the same two technicians are pinned.
- The second user sees none of your pins.
