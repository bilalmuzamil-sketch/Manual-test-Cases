# C614714 — A lead whose Time Clock is turned off keeps a named column that takes no drops
Test in run 498: https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/tests/view/4006366

## Preconditions
Setup file (for Claude sessions; a tester does not need it)
Preconditions
- You are signed in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role, which has Work orders > View and Create & Edit) at {Location-A}.
For example: {Location-A} is Staging Heavy Duty - 9919.
- Technician {Technician-A} is active at {Location-A} with the "Technician" role and Time Clock on. It was made for this case only, because this test turns that Time Clock off.
For example: {Technician-A} is ZZAUTOTEST N1 Tim.
- Customer {Customer-A} has two Approved work orders: {Work-order-1} led by {Technician-A}, and {Work-order-2} with no lead technician.
For example: {Customer-A} is ZZAUTOTEST N1 Time Clock Off, and {Work-order-1} and {Work-order-2} are S10043-17911 and S10043-17912.
- Starting screen: your initials (top right) > "Settings" > "Staff", in a browser window at least 1024 px wide.
- If any record above is missing, mark this test Blocked: "setup not done".

## Steps
- Click the pencil icon on {Technician-A}.
- Turn "Time Clock" off and click "Save & Close".
- Go to "Work Orders" > "All" > "Board View".
- Type the name of {Customer-A} in "Search".
- Drag the card for {Work-order-2} from "Unassigned" onto {Technician-A}'s column and let go.
- Click "Tech View" in the display switcher.
- Click the row for {Work-order-1} to open its work order.
- Set "Lead Technician" to "Unassigned".
- Go to "Work Orders" > "All" > "Board View".
- Click "Tech View" in the display switcher.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- Step 1: the staff record of {Technician-A} opens.
- Step 2: the record saves with "Time Clock" off.
- Step 3: Board View shows.
- Step 4: {Work-order-1} is still in a column of its own headed {Technician-A}.
- Step 5: the drop is refused: {Work-order-2} stays in "Unassigned" and the column still holds only {Work-order-1}.
- Step 6: {Work-order-1} is in a group headed {Technician-A}.
- Step 7: the work order page for {Work-order-1} opens.
- Step 8: "Lead Technician" reads "Unassigned".
- Step 9: Board View shows no column for {Technician-A}.
- Step 10: Tech View shows no group for {Technician-A}.
Source — where this behaviour comes from
Epic SV-10043; stories SV-10046 (Story 3, Board View), SV-10045 (Story 2, Tech View); PRD "Work Orders — Board View & Tech View Display Options", Confluence 845185030, version 61 (16 Oct 2026, Chris Ward), S3-R20d, S3-R20c and S4-N5, read 16 Oct 2026. S3-R20d was added in version 59 on 16 Oct 2026 (Chris Ward ruling on Slavcho Mitrov's question on SV-11416). Source-verified 16 October 2026. Last checked against build v26.40.19-1493c63 (staging) on 10/16/2026.
Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
- S3-R20d: “A lead technician whose Time Clock is turned off (not deleted) keeps their own column on Board View and group in Tech View, under their name, while they still lead work orders. Nothing can be dropped onto it, the same as S3-R20c. Once they lead no work orders, the column or group is no longer shown.”
- S3-R20c: “The column of S3-R20b takes no drops. Dropping a work order on it is refused with "This technician can't be assigned work orders." and the work order stays where it was. The column shows no inactive marker, because the technician is not deactivated (S3-N4). Once it holds no work orders, the column is no longer shown.”
- S4-N5: “Nothing can be dropped onto the column/group of an inactive technician (S2-N2, S3-N2) or of a technician who is not enrolled at this location (S3-R20c).”
