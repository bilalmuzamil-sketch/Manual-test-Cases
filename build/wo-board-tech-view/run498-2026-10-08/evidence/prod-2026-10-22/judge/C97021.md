# C97021 — Tab enters each view once and the arrow keys move between cards and rows
Test in run 498: https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/tests/view/3481959

## Preconditions
1. Sign in
Sign in as an Admin and select your test location.
Example: Staging Heavy Duty - 9919.
2. Have two technicians ready
Choose two technicians at this location and call them Technician A and Technician B throughout this test.
Both must:
- Be Active.
- Have the Technician role.
- Have Time Clock turned on.
Example names: ZZAUTOTEST C97021 Ana and ZZAUTOTEST C97021 Ben.

## Steps
- Click once in the "Search" box.
- Press Tab until something on the board shows a focus ring.
- Press the Right arrow key.
- Press the Down arrow key.
- Press Tab.
- Press Tab again.
- Click "Tech View" in the display switcher.
- Click once in the "Search" box.
- Press Tab until something in the table shows a focus ring.
- Press the Down arrow key.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- Step 1: the cursor is in the "Search" box and nothing on the board has a focus ring.
- Step 2: the focus ring lands on a card, the first one on the board ({Work-order-4}'s card in "Unassigned"). No column header gets the focus ring on the way.
- Step 3: focus moves one column to the right, into {Technician-A}'s column.
- Step 4: focus moves down to the next item in the same column, a card of {Technician-A}.
- Step 5: focus moves to the focused card's own "More actions" (…) button, not to another card.
- Step 6: focus leaves the board; no other card or column header gets the focus ring.
- Step 7: Tech View shows the "Unassigned" group first, then {Technician-A}'s and {Technician-B}'s groups.
- Step 8: the cursor is in the "Search" box and nothing in the table has a focus ring.
- Step 9: the focus ring lands on a work order row, the first one ({Work-order-4}'s row in "Unassigned"), not on a group header.
- Step 10: focus moves to the next item below, {Technician-A}'s group header.
Source — where this behaviour comes from
Epic SV-10043; story SV-10054 (Story 11, Keyboard access and focus); PRD "Work Orders — Board View & Tech View Display Options", Confluence 845185030, version 61 (16 Oct 2026, Chris Ward), S11-R1, S11-R3, S2-R17, S2-R2 and the Story 11 user story, read 16 Oct 2026. Product owner answers: Chris Ward, WO Board and Tech View question sheet (https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1gBGmNB4B1bRrD2ZJvBn1tuvbfxFPjcsKddz_I5H3CvU), answer 28, read 15 Oct 2026. Source-verified 16 October 2026. Last checked against build v26.40.19-1493c63 (staging) on 10/16/2026.
Chris Ward's answer 28 (15 Oct 2026) and PRD version 54 said Tab reaches every row, card and header in on-screen order. PRD versions 55, 60 and 61 (15 and 16 Oct 2026, Chris Ward rulings on Slavcho Mitrov's questions and the QA triage of SV-11395, SV-11397, SV-11422 and SV-11421) changed this: each view is one Tab stop that lands on a work order, and the arrow keys move between cards, rows, headers and columns. This case follows version 61, the latest.
Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
- S11-R1: “Each view is a single Tab stop. Tab lands on one work order row (Tech View) or card (Board View), the one last focused, or the first one when none has been (never a group or column header while a work order is shown; when every group is collapsed, Tab lands on the first group header; after the user arrows onto a header and tabs out and back in, Tab returns to a work order row while any row is shown; on Board View, if the last-focused card has scrolled out of view in its column, Tab lands on the first card in view); the focused row or card shows a visible focus ring.”
- S11-R3: “Inside a view the arrow keys move focus: Up and Down move between rows and group headers in Tech View, and between cards and the column header within a Board View column; Left and Right move between Board View columns. Tab from a focused row, card or header reaches its own controls (more actions, pin, collapse) in order, then leaves the view. Tab never stops on every row.”
- S2-R17: “Tech View orders pinned technicians the same way as Board View: after the fixed Unassigned group and before unpinned technicians, each newly pinned technician after the existing pins (S3-R15, S3-R16).”
- S2-R2: “Keep Unassigned, containing work orders without a lead technician, fixed before all technician groups.”
- Story 11 user story: “As a keyboard user, I want to navigate work orders and their actions so that these controls are usable without a mouse.”
