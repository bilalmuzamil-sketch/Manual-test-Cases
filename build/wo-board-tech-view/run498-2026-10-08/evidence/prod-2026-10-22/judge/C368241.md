# C368241 — A new photo shows in the top bar after a reload
Test in run 498: https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/tests/view/3792562

## Preconditions
Preconditions
- Needs: 1 user · 1 browser · about 5 minutes
- Build: the Work Orders QA build (sv10043, v26.40.8-7a95011), desktop browser, window at least 1024 px wide.
- Role: Owner/Admin. On the sign-in page click the Admin quick-login button (you appear as Admin ShopView). The Admin role has Work orders > View and Create & Edit (check: your initials (top right) > Settings > Roles & Permissions > Admin > edit).
- Location: Staging Heavy Duty - 9919 for the whole case (shown next to the bell; change it with your initials > Change Location).
- Two clearly different photos on your computer (e.g. a red square and a blue square).
- Your own profile photo is the red one before step 1.
Setup
- Your initials (top right) > Edit Profile > click "Click to upload a profile photo" (or the current photo) and pick the red photo. The photo shows on the page and in the top bar.
- Check the setup worked: the round picture at the top right shows the red photo.

## Steps
- Look at your avatar in the top bar (top right, next to the location).
- Upload the blue photo as your own photo: your initials > Edit Profile > click the photo > pick the blue photo.
- Go back to the same place (the Edit Profile page) and reload the page.
- Look at the avatar again.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- Before the upload the avatar shows the red photo.
- After the reload it shows the blue photo, not the old red one.
Source — where this behaviour comes from
Regression for epic SV-10043 (PR #3548); developer's regression list, received 8 Oct 2026, Medium 9; QA handoff test plan (generated 2026-10-08), §9, hotspot 5; PRD Confluence 845185030 (edited 7 Oct 2026) where quoted. Source-verified 8 October 2026.
Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
- Dev reply 9: “Avatars everywhere (header, Staff, Schedule, cards). Avatars are now cached. After someone uploads a new photo, a reload must show the new one.”
- QA handoff §9: “After a new photo is uploaded, a reload shows the new image.”
