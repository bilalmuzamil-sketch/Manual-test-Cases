# C96965 — Clear shifts removes only the old lead's whole-work-order shifts
Test in run 498: https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/tests/view/3481909

Setup (manual QA tester and Claude session)

- Your browser window is at least 1024 px wide, so the display switcher (List, Tech View, Board View) shows at the right of the Work Orders toolbar.

- Approved work order {Work-order-2} (for example S10043-17582) for {Customer-A} is led by {Technician-A} and has two approved lines, {Line-3} (for example "Service - Full grease service") and {Line-4} (for example "Service - Adjust clutch").
- Approved work order {Work-order-3} (for example S10043-17583) for {Customer-A} is led by {Technician-A} and has two approved lines (for example "Service - Full grease service" and "Service - Adjust clutch").
- {Work-order-1} has three shifts tomorrow: {Technician-A} 8:00–12:00 covering the entire work order, {Technician-A} 13:00–15:00 on {Line-1} only, and {Technician-C} 8:00–10:00 covering the entire work order.
- {Work-order-2} has two shifts tomorrow: {Technician-A} 8:00–12:00 covering the entire work order, and {Technician-A} 13:00–15:00 on {Line-3} only.
- {Work-order-3} has one shift the day after tomorrow: {Technician-A} 8:00–12:00 covering the entire work order.
- On the Work Orders page, Asset on Site shows no choice (if its chip shows Yes or No, click the x on the chip).

## Steps
- Open Work Orders, click the All tab and click Board View in the display switcher.
- Type the name of {Customer-A} in Search.
- Drag {Work-order-1} from {Technician-A}'s column into {Technician-B}'s column.
- Read the question that opens.
- Click Clear shifts.
- Drag {Work-order-2} from {Technician-A}'s column into {Technician-B}'s column.
- In the question that opens, click Keep shifts.
- Click Schedule in the top menu.
- Use the arrow next to Today to go to tomorrow.
- Read every shift on {Work-order-1} and {Work-order-2}.
- Use the arrow again to go to the day after tomorrow.
- Read the shift on {Work-order-3}.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- Steps 4 and 7: both drags open the question "Clear {Technician-A}'s scheduled shifts?" (with the example name: "Clear Esther Howard's scheduled shifts?").
- Step 5: {Work-order-1} moves to {Technician-B}'s column.
- Step 10: on {Work-order-1}, {Technician-A}'s 8:00–12:00 shift covering the entire work order is gone, and {Technician-C}'s 8:00–10:00 shift is still there.
- Step 12: {Technician-A}'s 8:00–12:00 shift on {Work-order-3} is still there, unchanged.
- Steps 7 and 10: {Work-order-2} is now in {Technician-B}'s column, and both of {Technician-A}'s shifts on it (8:00–12:00 and 13:00–15:00) are still there.
- Step 10: {Technician-A}'s 13:00–15:00 shifts on {Line-1} of {Work-order-1} and on {Line-3} of {Work-order-2} are still there: the question never clears a shift on only some lines.
Source — where this behaviour comes from
Epic SV-10043; story SV-10047 (Story 4, Reassign or unassign the lead technician); PRD "Work Orders — Board View & Tech View Display Options", Confluence 845185030, version 49 (14 Oct 2026, Chris Ward), S4-R11, S4-R22, S4-R23, S4-R24, S4-R28 (reworded in version 49 on 14 Oct 2026 from Chris Ward's answer 1 of 14 Oct 2026: a work-order-level shift is one that covers every approved line, judged at the moment the lead changes; first defined in version 47 after SV-11126) and S4-R29, read 14 Oct 2026. Review Decisions DR-31 (optional whole-work-order shift clearing). Schedule shift window labels "Entire work order" / "Choose lines" from the Schedule suite. Each work order has two lines so that the 13:00–15:00 shift covers only some of them. On a one-line work order a shift on that line counts (version 49, S4-R28). Source-verified 14 October 2026.
Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
- S4-R11: “When changing the lead technician in Board View or Tech View, ask whether to clear the outgoing lead’s work-order-level scheduled shifts.”
- S4-R29: “The prompt reads: title “Clear {Name}'s scheduled shifts?” (with no name: “Clear the lead technician's scheduled shifts?”); text “{Name} has shifts scheduled for this whole work order. Clearing removes shifts that haven't started and ends one that's under way now. Shifts on individual lines and recorded time don't change.”; buttons “Keep shifts”, “Clear shifts” and “Cancel”.”
- S4-R22: “If the user chooses to clear shifts, remove only the outgoing lead’s shifts scheduled against that whole work order.”
- S4-R23: “If the user chooses to keep shifts, change the lead technician and retain the existing shifts.”
- S4-R24: “Never clear shifts scheduled against individual work order lines through this prompt.”
- S4-R28: “The shift-clearing prompt (S4-R11, S4-R26) shows only when the outgoing lead has a work-order-level shift on this work order that has not ended. A work-order-level shift is one that covers every approved line of the work order, judged at the moment the lead changes; this is what the Schedule's Entire work order drop creates. Lines not yet approved, and declined lines, are ignored. On a work order with one line, a shift on that line counts, and so does a Choose lines shift that ticks every approved line. A shift on only some approved lines does not show the prompt, and that includes an Entire work order shift once a line has been added and approved after it was made.”
