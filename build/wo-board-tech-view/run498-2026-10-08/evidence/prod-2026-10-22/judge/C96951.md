## Steps
- Open Work Orders.
- Click the All tab on the left of the toolbar, click Search and type ZZAUTOTEST F2 Board Assigned.
- In the display switcher at the right of the toolbar, click Board View (tooltip "Board View").
- Turn on Assigned to me.
- Read every column shown.
- Look at the column headers for a pin control.
- Turn Assigned to me off.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)

- With Assigned to me on, Board View shows only the Unassigned column (holding S1-352) and your own column (holding S1-702).
