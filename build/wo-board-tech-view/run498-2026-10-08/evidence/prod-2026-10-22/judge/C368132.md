# C368132 — Empty technician columns stay on the board with no way to hide them
Test in run 498: https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/tests/view/3761653

## Preconditions
Preconditions
- Needs: 1 user · 1 browser · Schedule access · about 15 minutes
- Build: the Work Orders QA build (sv10043, v26.40.8-7a95011). A desktop browser with the window at least 1024 pixels wide (full screen on an ordinary laptop or monitor). Below that width the page shows only List.
- [User-A]: an Owner/Admin user (on a QA branch: the Admin quick-login button on the sign-in page; you appear as "Admin ShopView"). The Admin role holds Work orders > View and Work orders > Create & Edit (to check: your initials at the top right > Settings > Roles & Permissions > Admin > Work orders).
- One location for the whole case, shown in the top bar next to the bell (e.g. "Staging Heavy Duty - 9919"); everything below is created in this location.
- Two technicians at this location: "Esther Howard" and "Jenny Wilson" (the expected results name them, so keep these names). A person only counts as a technician here when the staff member is active (not deactivated), Time Clock is on, the Role is neither Office User nor Time Clock User, and this location is picked in their Location field.
- Customer "ZZAUTOTEST F2 Empty Tech Column" (used by this case only), with one contact and one asset, so this case's work orders are kept apart from every other work order at the location.
- Two Approved work orders for that customer, [WO-1] and [WO-2], both led by Esther Howard; nothing for this customer led by Jenny Wilson.
- The work orders you will drag have no shift on the Schedule for their lead technician. Assigned to me is off.
Setup
- Sign in with the Admin quick-login button on the sign-in page (you appear as "Admin ShopView"). Check the location in the top bar next to the bell; to change it click your initials at the top right > Change Location.
- Settings (your initials at the top right > Settings) > Staff: confirm each technician below is active at this location with Time Clock on, or add it: New Staff Member > First Name and Last Name, an Email, Role "Technician", at least one Department (required), Location = this location, Time Clock on > Save & Close. Record them as "Esther Howard" (keep this name, the expected results use it); "Jenny Wilson" (keep this name, the expected results use it).
- Top menu Customers > New Customer > Name "ZZAUTOTEST F2 Empty Tech Column" > Save (if it already exists, open it instead). On the customer page click the Contacts tab > New Contact > First Name and Last Name > Save (a new asset needs a contact). Then click the Assets tab > New Asset > pick the Contact, then Year, Make, Model and Unit (e.g. 2022, Freightliner, M2, TRK-118) > Save. Make is required; Unit can be left empty.
- Work Orders > Create Work Order > in the New Work Order window pick Customer "ZZAUTOTEST F2 Empty Tech Column" and its asset > Save. The work order opens on its Lines tab with the New Line form open: in "What Are You Doing?" pick a ready-made line (e.g. "Replace - Brake pot"; the build offers only ready-made lines), turn Line Approved on > Save & Clos

## Steps
- Open Work Orders.
- Click the All tab on the left of the toolbar.
- Click Search and type ZZAUTOTEST F2 Empty Tech Column so only this test's work orders show.
- Make sure Assigned to me is off.
- In the display switcher at the right of the toolbar, click Board View (tooltip "Board View").
- Find Jenny Wilson's column.
- Look through the toolbar, the column header and any menus for a control to hide empty columns.
- Drag the [WO-2] card onto Jenny Wilson's column and let go.
- Read both column counts.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- Jenny Wilson's column is on the board even though she leads no work orders.
- There is no control anywhere to hide empty technician columns.
- The card can be dropped on her column: Jenny Wilson now shows 1 and Esther Howard drops from 2 to 1.
- While Assigned to me is on or when nothing matches the filters, other rules apply (checked in other cases).
Source — where this behaviour comes from
Epic SV-10043; story SV-10046 (Story 3, Board View — board by lead technician); PRD Confluence 845185030, edited 7 Oct 2026 (status line "PRD v33"), S3-R9, S3-R21, read 8 Oct 2026. Review Decisions 853901313 SQ-7 / DR-26.
Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
- S3-R9: “Keep empty eligible technician columns visible as drop targets, except while Assigned to me is enabled (S3-N1), subject to S1-N1.”
- S3-R21: “Do not offer a control to hide empty eligible technician columns.”
