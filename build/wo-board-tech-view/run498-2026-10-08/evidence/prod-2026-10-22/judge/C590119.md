# C590119 — Design: Board View column headings show handle, avatar, name, pin and count
Test in run 498: https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/tests/view/3995728

## Preconditions
Setup file (for Claude sessions; a tester does not need it)
Preconditions
- You are signed in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role, which has Work orders > View and Create & Edit) at {Location-A}.
For example: {Location-A} is Staging Heavy Duty - 9919.
- The browser window is 1440 px wide with the zoom at 100%, and the design pictures "DC-10-board-column-headers-light-1440.png" and "DC-10-board-pinned-light-1440.png" attached to this case are open beside it at 100% (actual size). Names, numbers and rank numbers in the pictures are sample data.
- Four technicians are active at {Location-A} with the "Technician" role: {Technician-A}, who has a profile photo, {Technician-B}, who has none, {Technician-C} and {Technician-D}. No technician is pinned in your Board View.
For example: {Technician-A} to {Technician-D} are ZZAUTOTEST C590119 Lea, Max, Nia and Oli.
- Customer {Customer-A} has Approved work orders {Work-order-1} led by {Technician-A} and {Work-order-2} led by {Technician-B}, and an Estimate {Work-order-3} with no lead technician.
For example: {Customer-A} is ZZAUTOTEST C590119 Column Header, and {Work-order-1} to {Work-order-3} are S10043-17827 to S10043-17829.
- Starting screen: "Work Orders" > "All" tab, "Board View" shown, the name of {Customer-A} in "Search", no filter chosen, "Assigned to me" off, density "Regular".
- If any record above is missing, mark this test Blocked: "setup not done".

## Steps
- Look at the "Unassigned" column header from left to right.
- Look at {Technician-B}'s column header from left to right.
- Look at the avatar in {Technician-A}'s column header.
- Click the pin in {Technician-B}'s column header.
- Look at the pin in {Technician-B}'s column header.
- Click the pin in {Technician-A}'s column header.
- Click the pin in {Technician-C}'s column header.
- Hover the pin in {Technician-D}'s column header.
- Click the pins of {Technician-B}, {Technician-A} and {Technician-C} to unpin them.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- Step 1: a round light-grey avatar with a grey outline person icon, the word "Unassigned" in dark semibold text, a short grey divider, a clipboard outline icon and the count. No grip and no pin, as in the picture.
- Step 2: the six-dot grip, a light-grey circle with the initials in dark grey (a little darker than in the design picture, not a solid blue circle with white initials), the name in dark slate semibold text, a grey outline pin, a short divider, the clipboard icon and the count.
- Step 3: the avatar is the technician's round photo.
- Step 4: {Technician-B}'s column moves to just after "Unassigned".
- Step 5: the pin is a pin with a slash through it, in blue, as in the pinned picture.
- Step 6: {Technician-A} is pinned too.
- Step 7: {Technician-C} is pinned too; three technicians are pinned.
- Step 8: the pins on the unpinned columns look faded and the hover text reads "You can pin up to 3 technicians."
- Step 9: every technician column header shows the grey outline pin again.
Source — where this behaviour comes from
Epic SV-10043; stories SV-10046 (Story 3, Board View), SV-10044 (Story 1, Switch between display options); PRD "Work Orders — Board View & Tech View Display Options", Confluence 845185030, version 61 (16 Oct 2026, Chris Ward), S3-R12, S2-R23, S3-R7, S3-R8 and S1-R9a, read 16 Oct 2026. Claude Design project "Work Orders" (Branko Cicovic), export of 14 Oct 2026, board "Work Orders.dc.html" (the design pictures named in the preconditions were rendered from it), read 14 Oct 2026. Related tickets: SV-11319, SV-11338. Source-verified 16 October 2026. Last checked against build v26.40.19-1493c63 (staging) on 10/16/2026.
SV-11338 estimates the design's header name at about 16 px; the ticket's estimated design sizes differ from the design file, which draws the name at 14 px semibold; this case follows the design file. The design allows five pins; this case follows the PRD's limit of three.
PRD versions 55 and 58 (S2-R23) set no-photo initials to a darker grey than the design file (#4B5565 instead of #697586, for contrast) and header names slate semibold; step 2 checks it.
