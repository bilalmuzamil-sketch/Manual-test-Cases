# C368158 — The On Site toggle still saves on an Invoiced work order
Test in run 498: https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/tests/view/3761679

## Preconditions
Setup (manual QA tester and Claude session)
Preconditions
- You are signed in as an Owner or Admin user whose role has Work orders > View and Work orders > Create & Edit, at {Location-A} (for example Staging Heavy Duty - 9919).
- Your browser window is at least 1024 px wide, so the display switcher (List, Tech View, Board View) shows at the right of the Work Orders toolbar.
- Technician {Technician-A} (for example ZZAUTOTEST Ana Alpha) is an active staff member at {Location-A} with Time Clock on and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User.
- Customer {Customer-A} (for example "ZZAUTOTEST F3 Invoiced On Site") is used by this case only and has one work order, {Work-order-1} (for example S10043-17821), led by {Technician-A} and then Invoiced.
- List shows the On Site column with a toggle in each row.
- On the Work Orders page nothing is ticked in Status, Asset on Site shows no choice (if its chip shows Yes or No, click the x on the chip), Assigned to me is off and Search is empty.

## Steps
- Open Work Orders from the top menu and click the All tab.
- Type the name of {Customer-A} in Search.
- Click List in the display switcher.
- In {Work-order-1}'s row, click the On Site toggle to change it.
- Refresh the page and read the On Site value.
- Open {Work-order-1} and read Lead Technician and Asset on site.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- Step 4: the change is accepted with no error.
- Step 5: after the refresh the new On Site value is kept.
- Step 6: Lead Technician is still {Technician-A}, and Asset on site shows the new value. The lock on Invoiced work orders does not block the On Site toggle.
Source — where this behaviour comes from
Epic SV-10043; story SV-10047 (Story 4, Reassign or unassign the lead technician); PRD "Work Orders — Board View & Tech View Display Options", Confluence 845185030, version 54 (15 Oct 2026, Chris Ward), S4-N2, read 15 Oct 2026. In version 48 (12 Oct 2026) S4-N2 dropped Imported from the locked statuses (Imported work orders now open in List only); the Invoiced and Paid rules this case checks are unchanged, so the expected results are unchanged. Work Orders Board View & Tech View Display Options Technical Implementation Plan (revised 24 Sep 2026, uploaded 8 Oct 2026), section 5 (the change endpoint) and the Phase 3 browser walk. Source-verified 15 October 2026. Last checked against build v26.40.19-72f4f3b (staging) on 10/16/2026.
Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
- S4-N2: “Do not allow the lead technician to change in Invoiced or Paid status. An Invoiced or Paid work order can still be dragged to reorder it within its current technician (or within Unassigned), but it cannot be moved to another technician, to Unassigned, or out of Unassigned. Declined and Complete work orders can be moved freely, as today.”
- Tech plan 5 change endpoint: “Re-sending the *current* lead stays a no-op so the asset-on-site toggle keeps working on invoiced work orders.”
