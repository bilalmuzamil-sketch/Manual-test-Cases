# C682420 — Design- Tech View count bubbles sit on the status label as in List, any density
Test in run 498: https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/tests/view/4054407

## Preconditions
Preconditions
- Needs: 1 user · 1 browser · about 5 minutes · no setup
- Sign in as an Owner/Admin user (on staging or a QA branch: the Admin quick-login button on the sign-in page; you appear as "Admin ShopView"). Browser zoom 100%. Window about 1600 px wide.
- Location "Staging Heavy Duty - 9919" (shown in the top bar next to the bell). To change it: click your initials at the top right > Change Location.
- Work order S2-33351 has the status "Ready For Review" with a red number and a blue number on its status pill. (On another environment use any work order whose status pill shows both a red and a blue number.)

## Steps
- Open this link: https://app.staging.shopview.com/workorders?tab=all&search=S2-33351 (it opens Work Orders on the All tab with S2-33351 in the Search box).
- At the right of the bar above the list, click List (the left of the three view buttons). Look at the status pill of S2-33351 and where the red and blue numbers sit on it.
- Click Tech View (the middle view button, a person icon). If the "Unassigned" group is closed, click the small arrow to the left of "Unassigned" to open it.
- Click the density button (the icon just right of "Asset on Site") and choose Compact. Look at the pill of S2-33351.
- Click the density button and choose Regular. Look at the pill again.
- Click the density button and choose Comfortable. Look at the pill again.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- Step 2: the pill reads "Ready For Review". The red 2 and the blue 3 sit on its top-right corner and only touch the top of the letters, so the word is easy to read.
- Steps 4, 5 and 6: at Compact, Regular and Comfortable the red and blue numbers sit on the pill exactly as in List at step 2: on the top-right corner, only touching the top of the letters. They never cover the word.
- The pill is lower at Compact and Regular than at Comfortable. That is correct.
Source — where this behaviour comes from
Epic SV-10043; story SV-10045 (Story 2, Tech View); ticket SV-11467; PRD "Work Orders — Board View & Tech View Display Options", Confluence 845185030, version 66, S2-R19 and S6-R2a, read 19 Oct 2026.
Exact quote from the source
- S2-R19: “Tech View’s Status column is the same status pill List shows in production, with the same red service-actions count and blue parts-actions count on it. Its size follows density (S6-R2a).”
- S6-R2a: “In Tech View the status pill is 16px tall at Compact and Regular and 22px tall at Comfortable, the design’s small and regular badges. List’s status pill is unchanged: for the same work orders on the same page, every List pill is the same width and height as in production, including production’s matching widths down the column (for example, Approved as wide as Ready For Review when both are on the page). Tech View’s pill takes the same widths as List’s; only its height follows density.”
This is the expected behaviour as per epic SV-10043, story SV-10045, ticket SV-11467 and the Work Orders — Board View & Tech View Display Options specification version 66 (S2-R19, S6-R2a). Last checked against build v26.40.19-c850386 on 10/19/2026.

## Newest PASSED result in run 498 (staging judgement)
Tested on staging, build v26.40.19-c850386, on 19 October 2026, signed in as Admin, window 1440 x 900, zoom 100%. Result: Passed.
- Step 1: the link opens Work Orders on the All tab with S2-33351 in the Search box.
- Step 2 (List): the pill reads "Ready For Review" (21 px tall); the red 2 and blue 3 sit on its top-right corner and only touch the top of the letters.
- Steps 4 to 6 (Tech View): at Compact (pill 16 px), Regular (16 px) and Comfortable (22 px) the red 2 and blue 3 sit at the same height on the pill as in List and cover the same small top part of the letters. The word stays readable at every density.
