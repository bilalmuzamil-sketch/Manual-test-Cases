# C682419 — Board View's sideways scroll bar is thick and its handle is never tiny
Test in run 498: https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/tests/view/4053764

## Preconditions
Preconditions
- Needs: 1 user · 1 browser · about 10 minutes · no setup
- Sign in as an Owner/Admin user (on staging or a QA branch: the Admin quick-login button on the sign-in page; you appear as "Admin ShopView"). Browser zoom 100%.
- Use a location where Board View is wider than the window, so a sideways scroll bar shows under the board. On staging, "Staging Heavy Duty - 9919" has many technicians. To change location: click your initials at the top right > Change Location.
- For step 7 you also need a location with only a few technicians (on staging, "DVI Test", 8 technicians).

## Steps
- Click Work Orders in the top menu, then the All tab on the left of the toolbar.
- At the right of the toolbar, click Board View (the right-most of the three view buttons, tooltip "Board View").
- Look at the scroll bar at the very bottom of the board, under the technician columns.
- Scroll down inside the Unassigned column and look at the thin scroll bar that shows inside that column.
- Compare the grey handle on the bottom bar with the width of one technician column.
- Drag the grey handle all the way to the right, then all the way back to the left.
- Make the browser window narrower (about the width of a small laptop) and look at the handle again. Then change to a location with only a few technicians and look at the handle again.
- Click your initials at the top right > Dark, look at the bottom bar again, then switch back to Light.
- Click Tech View (middle view button), then List (left view button), and look at their scroll bars.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- The bottom bar is clearly thicker than the thin scroll bar inside a column.
- The grey handle is at least about half as wide as one technician column, at every window size.
- On a location with only a few technicians the handle is longer than on a big board. How much longer depends on the window width: on a wide window it is much longer, on a narrow one only a little. That is correct; it is never shorter than about half a column.
- Dragging right reaches the last technician; dragging left comes back to the first one. Unassigned stays in place on the left the whole time.
- In Dark the bar and its handle are still easy to see.
- Tech View and List keep their usual thin scroll bars; nothing changed there.
- In Firefox the bottom bar looks like Firefox's normal scroll bar. That is correct.
Source — where this behaviour comes from
Epic SV-10043; story SV-10046 (Story 3, Board View); ticket SV-11466; PRD Confluence 845185030 version 66, S3-E2, read 19 Oct 2026.
Exact quote from the source
- S3-E2: “Allow horizontal scrolling when technician columns exceed the available viewport; never hide technicians automatically. The board’s sideways scrollbar is 16px thick, and its grab handle is never shorter than 150px at any screen width or number of technician columns. When more of the board fits on screen, the handle is longer, as the browser draws it. The scrollbars inside each column are unchanged. Firefox shows its own standard scrollbar.”
A technician column is about 290 px wide, so half a column is about 150 px. The bar inside a column is about 10 px; the bottom bar is 16 px.
This is the expected behaviour as per epic SV-10043, story SV-10046 and the Work Orders — Board View & Tech View Display Options specification version 66 (S3-E2). Last checked against build v26.40.19-c850386 on 10/19/2026.

## Newest PASSED result in run 498 (staging judgement)
Tested on staging, build v26.40.19-c850386, on 19 October 2026, signed in as Admin. Result: Passed.
This result corrects one line of the previous result: on DVI Test the handle is not "most of the bar" at every window size. It is always longer than on a big board and never shorter than 150 px, as the spec asks:
- DVI Test (8 technicians): 1053 px at a 1920 px window, 516 px at 1440 px, 204 px at 1024 px.
- Staging Heavy Duty - 9919 (many technicians): 150 px at 1920, 1440, 1280 and 1024 px.
Everything else in the previous result stands (bar 16 px against about 10 px inside a column; drag end to end; Dark; Tech View and List unchanged; Firefox's own bar). The test's expected result was reworded today to match: "how much longer depends on the window width".
