# C368226 — The Work In Progress report keeps a chosen filter after a reload
Test in run 498: https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/tests/view/3792547

## Preconditions
Preconditions
- Needs: 1 user · 1 browser · about 5 minutes
- Build: the Work Orders QA build (sv10043, v26.40.8-7a95011), desktop browser, window at least 1024 px wide.
- Role: Owner/Admin. On the sign-in page click the Admin quick-login button (you appear as Admin ShopView). The Admin role has Work orders > View and Create & Edit (check: your initials (top right) > Settings > Roles & Permissions > Admin > edit).
- Location: Staging Heavy Duty - 9919 for the whole case (shown next to the bell; change it with your initials > Change Location).
- Your role can see reports (Admin: Page Access > Reports on) and the organization has two locations (Staging Heavy Duty - 9919, Staging Lethbridge - 4310).
- Needs no test data of its own; restore the filter at the end.
Setup
- Click Reports in the top menu > Work In Progress (left menu, PERFORMANCE group). If Location is not at its default (Staging Heavy Duty - 9919 only), set it back.
- Check the setup worked: the Location button shows its default value.

## Steps
- Click Reports in the top menu.
- In the PERFORMANCE group click "Work In Progress".
- Open the Location filter and tick Staging Lethbridge - 4310 as well (Staging Heavy Duty - 9919 is the only one ticked by default; unticking it would leave none). Write down which.
- Close the filter (Escape or click outside it) and write down the rows or totals shown.
- Reload the page.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- After the reload the filter still shows your choice and the report shows the same rows or totals as before the reload.
- No error message appears.
