## Steps
- Open Work Orders from the top menu and click the All tab.
- Type "ZZAUTOTEST F3 Hidden Tech Order" in Search so only this case's work orders show.
- Click Board View in the display switcher (tooltip "Board View").
- Click "Assigned to me". Only Ana's and Cal's columns show.
- Drag Cal's column header before Ana's and release.
- Click "Assigned to me" again to turn it off and read the column order.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- Step 6: the technician order is Cal, Ana, Ben, Dan. Cal was placed right before Ana and the hidden Ben and Dan kept their relative order (the same pattern as the work order examples: C, A, B, D).
Source — where this behaviour comes from
Epic SV-10043; story SV-10052 (Story 9, Drag to reorder work and technician groups/columns); PRD Confluence 845185030, edited 7 Oct 2026 (status line "PRD v33"), S9-E2, S9-E3, Story 9 filtered-order examples, read 8 Oct 2026.
Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
- S9-E2: “When reordering filtered results, place the moved item immediately before or after an unmoved visible anchor.”
- S9-E3: “Retain that relative position when filters clear.”
- Story 9 filtered-order examples: “Filtered-order examples — confirmed: Full order A, B, C, D; the filter shows A, C. Move C immediately above A: clearing the filter yields C, A, B, D. Starting again from A, B, C, D, move A immediately below C: clearing the filter yields B, C, A, D. Hidden items keep their relative order. Apply to work orders within a technician and technician groups/columns, subject to fixed Unassigned and pinned-area boundaries.”
