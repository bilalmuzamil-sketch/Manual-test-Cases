Preconditions
- Needs: 1 user · 1 browser · about 15 minutes
- Build: the Work Orders QA build (sv10043, v26.40.8-7a95011). A desktop browser with the window at least 1024 pixels wide (full screen on an ordinary laptop or monitor). Below that width the page shows only List.
- [User-A]: an Owner/Admin user (on a QA branch: the Admin quick-login button on the sign-in page; you appear as "Admin ShopView"). - Five technicians at this location: "Esther Howard", "Ralph Edwards", "Jenny Wilson", "Theresa Webb", "Kristin Watson" (the expected results name them, so keep these names). A person only counts as a technician here when the staff member is active (not deactivated), Time Clock is on, the Role is neither Office User nor Time Clock User, and this location is picked in their Location field.
- Customer "ZZAUTOTEST F2 Board Pin Limit" (used by this case only), with one contact and one asset, so this case's work orders are kept apart from every other work order at the location.
- Five Approved work orders for that customer, one led by each technician.

## Steps
- Open Work Orders, click the All tab, click Search and type ZZAUTOTEST F2 Board Pin Limit.
- In the display switcher at the right of the toolbar, click Board View (tooltip "Board View").
- Pin Ralph Edwards, Jenny Wilson and Theresa Webb.
- Hover the pin control on Esther Howard's header and read any text shown.
- Try to click it.
- Unpin Jenny Wilson.
- Pin Esther Howard.
- In the display switcher at the right of the toolbar, click Tech View (tooltip "Tech View").
- Look at the pinned groups and Kristin Watson's pin control.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- With three pinned, every other pin control is disabled and explains: "You can pin up to 3 technicians."
- Clicking the disabled control pins nobody.
- After unpinning Jenny Wilson, Esther Howard can be pinned.
- Tech View shows the same three pins (Ralph Edwards, Theresa Webb, Esther Howard) and Kristin Watson's pin control is disabled there too.
- The design allows 5 pins and says "Up to 5 pinned columns, unpin one first": a limit other than 3 or other wording is a fail.
Source — where this behaviour comes from
Epic SV-10043; story SV-10046 (Story 3, Board View — board by lead technician); PRD Confluence 845185030, edited 7 Oct 2026 (status line "PRD v33"), S3-R8, S3-R19, read 8 Oct 2026. PRD section 9 User Feedback Summary. Design: Claude Design export of 8 Oct 2026 (Work Orders.dc.html), Board pin limit; on-screen labels only.
Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
- S3-R8: “At three pinned technicians, disable other pin controls with: “You can pin up to 3 technicians.””
- S3-R19: “Unpinning frees a slot in both Tech View and Board View.”
