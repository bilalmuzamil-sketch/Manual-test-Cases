# C368156 — Pins for technicians at another location are hidden and free the slots
Test in run 498: https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/tests/view/3761677

## Preconditions
Preconditions
- Needs: 1 user · 1 browser · two locations · about 20 minutes
- Build: the Work Orders QA build (sv10043). Desktop browser window at least 1024 px wide (narrower windows show only the List, without the display switcher).
- Role: Owner/Admin. On a QA branch sign in with the Admin quick-login button on the sign-in page; you appear as "Admin ShopView". The Admin role holds Work orders > View and Work orders > Create & Edit (to check: your initials at the top right > Settings > Roles & Permissions > Admin > Work orders). This user is the dispatcher.
- Two locations in your organization (Settings > Locations), e.g. "Heavy Duty" ("Staging Heavy Duty - 9919") and "ZZAUTOTEST Loc2".
- Three eligible technicians enrolled only at Heavy Duty ("ZZAUTOTEST Ana Alpha", "ZZAUTOTEST Ben Bravo", "ZZAUTOTEST Cal Charlie") and three enrolled only at Loc2 ("ZZAUTOTEST Xia X-ray", "ZZAUTOTEST Yan Yankee", "ZZAUTOTEST Zoe Zulu") (Settings > Staff). A staff member counts as a technician when, in Settings > Staff > the staff member's edit icon > Edit Staff Member, they are Active, Time Clock is on, the Role is neither Office User nor Time Clock User, and this location is picked in their Location field.
- Your pins: nobody is pinned for you at either location (you can pin up to 3 per location).
Setup
- Settings > Locations: confirm the second location "ZZAUTOTEST Loc2" exists, or add it.
- Settings > Staff: confirm Ana, Ben and Cal have only Heavy Duty picked in Location, and Xia, Yan and Zoe only "ZZAUTOTEST Loc2"; add any that are missing with New Staff Member (at least one Department is required, Time Clock on) > Save & Close.
- At each location open Work Orders > Board View and unpin every pinned technician (pin icon in the column header). Write down who was pinned so you can pin them back after the case.
- Check the setup worked: at each location Board View shows no pinned columns; Heavy Duty shows Ana, Ben, Cal and Loc2 shows Xia, Yan, Zoe. If a technician shows at the wrong location, fix their Location field.

## Steps
- Click your initials at the top right > Change Location > "Staging Heavy Duty - 9919", open Work Orders > Board View and pin Ana, Ben and Cal (the pin icon in each header).
- Try to pin a fourth technician at Heavy Duty and read the message.
- Click your initials at the top right > Change Location > "ZZAUTOTEST Loc2" and open Board View.
- Pin Xia, Yan and Zoe.
- Change Location back to "Staging Heavy Duty - 9919" and open Board View.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- Step 2: the pin control is disabled with "You can pin up to 3 technicians."
- Step 3: at Loc2 none of the Heavy Duty pins show and no pinned columns appear.
- Step 4: all three pins at Loc2 succeed. The Heavy Duty pins did not use up the three slots there.
- Step 5: Ana, Ben and Cal are still pinned at Heavy Duty.
Source — where this behaviour comes from
Epic SV-10043; Work Orders Board View & Tech View Display Options Technical Implementation Plan (revised 24 Sep 2026, uploaded 8 Oct 2026), section 3.13 and section 1.4 (SQ-11); agreed by Sasha Grosman in the PRD footer comment of 24 Sep 2026. Epic SV-10043; story SV-10046 (Story 3, Board View); PRD Confluence 845185030, edited 7 Oct 2026 (status line "PRD v33"), S3-R8, S3-R18, read 8 Oct 2026. Review Decisions SQ-11 (29 Sep 2026) still lists cross-location pin behaviour as open (PO question raised).
Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
- S3-R8: “At three pinned technicians, disable other pin controls with: “You can pin up to 3 technicians.””
- S3-R18: “Persist pins per user across sessions, logout/login, and devices.”
- Tech plan 3.13 pins: “pins for technicians of other locations are hidden and do not consume the 3-pin cap there.”
