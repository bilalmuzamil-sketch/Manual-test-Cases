# C590128 — Design: Density changes Tech View row, avatar and bar sizes, not card avatars
Test in run 498: https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/tests/view/3995737

## Preconditions
Setup file (for Claude sessions; a tester does not need it)
Preconditions
- You are signed in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role, which has Work orders > View and Create & Edit) at {Location-A}.
For example: {Location-A} is Staging Heavy Duty - 9919.
- The browser window is 1440 px wide with the zoom at 100%, and the design pictures "DC-06-tech-view-compact-light-1440.png" and "DC-06-tech-view-regular-light-1440.png" and "DC-06-tech-view-comfortable-light-1440.png" attached to this case are open beside it at 100% (actual size). Names, numbers and rank numbers in the pictures are sample data.
- Customer {Customer-A} has an Approved work order {Work-order-1}, led by technician {Technician-A}, with a service advisor and line technician {Technician-B} on its approved line. On Board View cards the "Line technicians" field is turned on.
For example: {Customer-A} is ZZAUTOTEST C590128 Density, {Work-order-1} is S10043-17830, {Technician-A} is ZZAUTOTEST C590128 Ana and {Technician-B} is ZZAUTOTEST C590128 Ben.
- Starting screen: "Work Orders" > "All" tab, "Tech View" shown, the name of {Customer-A} in "Search", no filter chosen, "Assigned to me" off, density "Regular".
- If any record above is missing, mark this test Blocked: "setup not done".

## Steps
- Click the density button and choose "Compact".
- Look at the rows, the group headers, the avatars and the progress bars, and compare them with the Compact picture.
- Click the density button and choose "Regular".
- Look at them again and compare them with the Regular picture.
- Click the density button and choose "Comfortable".
- Look at them again and compare them with the Comfortable picture.
- Click "Board View" in the display switcher.
- Look at the avatars on the card for {Work-order-1}.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- Step 1: "Compact" is the density in use.
- Step 2: as in the Compact picture: the rows are at their lowest (36 px in the design), row avatars and the Service Advisor avatar 20 px, group header avatars 24 px, progress bars 48 × 4 px; the same columns and values show, the row text keeps its size and no hours line shows under the bar.
- Step 3: "Regular" is the density in use.
- Step 4: as in the Regular picture: rows 48 px, row and Service Advisor avatars 24 px, group header avatars 28 px, progress bars 64 × 6 px; the same columns and values show and no hours line shows.
- Step 5: "Comfortable" is the density in use.
- Step 6: as in the Comfortable picture: rows 64 px, row and Service Advisor avatars 32 px, group header avatars 36 px; the same columns and values show, no hours line shows, and the header labels and the percentage keep their small size.
- Step 7: Board View shows the card for {Work-order-1}.
- Step 8: the card's avatars are 24 px: the same size as the row avatars at Regular (step 4), and smaller than the 32 px row avatars at Comfortable (step 6).
The design's Compact row text is 13 px and the design adds the hours under the progress bar only at Comfortable. Chris Ward answered on 15 Oct 2026 (answer 27) that row and card text is 14 px at every density and Compact never shrinks it, while text the design sets at 12 px stays 12 px, and (answer 18) that no hours line shows at any density. PRD version 71 still says so, and this case follows it.
PRD versions 60 and 61 (S2-R23; Chris Ward rulings on the QA triage of SV-11395, SV-11397, SV-11422 and SV-11421, 16 Oct 2026) add the avatar sizes by density (rows 20, 24 and 32 px; group headers 24, 28 and 36 px; the Service Advisor avatar too), the Compact progress bar of 48 × 4 px, and Board card avatars that stay 24 px; steps 2, 4, 6 and 8 check them. PRD version 71 removes Board View's density button (S3-R11), so the card avatars are checked after Tech View's density has changed.
Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
From: PRD "Work Orders — Board View & Tech View Display Options", Confluence 845185030, version 71 (20 Oct 2026, Chris Ward), read 20 Oct 2026 · Epic SV-10043, story SV-10049 (Story 6, Density in Tech View and Board View); ticket SV-11318
- S2-R23: “In Tech View and Board View, as in the design: a technician avatar without a photo is a light-grey circle (--sv-grey-100, #EEF2F6) with initials in --sv-grey-600 (#4B5565) in light mode, which meets accessible contrast (6.7:1; the design file’s #697586 is 4.2:1); dark mode uses the design’s dark colors; group and column header names are 14px, weight 600, in the design’s slate text color (#364152), with the design’s smaller initials, in the same --sv-grey-600 (#4B5565) as every other avatar’s initials; Board card customer and total price are weight 500; toolbar and switcher icons are the design’s slate color (the switcher in List too, S1-R9a). Technician avatars, and the Service Advisor avatar, follow density in Tech View as in the design: 20px, 24px and 32px on rows and 24px, 28px and 36px on group headers at Compact, Regular and Comfortable; Board View card avatars stay 24px. The Progress bar is 48 by 4px at Compact, as in the design. The name bubble on hover over a technician avatar on a row or card (the +N included) opens above the avatar, in both views; group and column header avatars keep the browser’s plain name tip, because the name is printed beside them. In Tech View, Status, Waiting On Parts and Progress are left-aligned. The Density and Columns tooltips open above their button, like the switcher’s (S1-R9b), and are hidden while their menu is open. List is unchanged.”
- S6-R1: “Offer Compact, Regular, and Comfortable density in Tech View. Board View has no Density control in this release (S3-R11).”
- S6-R2: “Density adjusts spacing and the size of rows, cards, and their elements.”
- S6-R4: “Row and card text is 14px at every density; Compact never shrinks it. Text the design sets at 12px at every density, such as column header labels (S2-R21), the progress percentage and secondary lines, stays 12px.”
- S6-R6: “Changing density never changes which fields, columns, or values are displayed.”
- S6-E1: “Density applies to Tech View rows on Work Orders; Board View cards keep their current spacing in this release. List keeps today’s row size, and other application tables are unchanged.”
- S2-R20: “Tech View’s cells follow the design: the work order number shows as a link in the design system’s link color (#175CD3), which meets accessible contrast on white; Progress shows as a thin bar with the percentage beside it, with no hours line under it at any density (S6-R6); Service Advisor shows the advisor’s avatar before the name. Clocked In keeps List’s display (S5-R11).”
