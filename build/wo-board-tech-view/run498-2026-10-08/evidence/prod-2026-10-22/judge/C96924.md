Preconditions
- Needs: 1 user · 1 browser · about 20 minutes

## Steps
- Open Work Orders.
- Click the All tab on the left of the toolbar.
- Click Search and type ZZAUTOTEST F2 Tech Grouping so only this test's work orders show.
- In the display switcher at the right of the toolbar, click Tech View (tooltip "Tech View").
- Look at how the table is divided.
- Read the header of [Tech-A]'s group: name, picture or initials, and number.
- Count the rows under [Tech-A].
- Open the Status filter, tick Approved only and close it.
- Read [Tech-A]'s header number and count her rows again.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- The table is split into groups, one per lead technician, with a visible separator line between groups.
- Each group header shows the technician's name, their avatar (photo or initials) and a number.
- The number equals the rows listed under it. Example: Esther Howard shows 3 with 3 rows (S1-702, S1-644, S1-455), Ralph Edwards shows 2 with 2 rows.
- After filtering to Approved, Esther Howard's number becomes 2 and 2 rows are listed (S1-702, S1-644): 3 - 1 Estimate = 2.
Source — where this behaviour comes from
Epic SV-10043; story SV-10045 (Story 2, Tech View — table grouped by lead technician); PRD Confluence 845185030, edited 7 Oct 2026 (status line "PRD v33"), S2-R1, S2-R12, read 8 Oct 2026. Design: Claude Design export of 8 Oct 2026 (Work Orders.dc.html), By Lead Tech group header (count tooltip "N work orders"); on-screen labels only.
Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
- S2-R1: “Group work orders by lead technician with visible separators.”
- S2-R12: “Each group header shows the technician’s name, avatar, and a count of the work orders currently shown in that group, after the filter view and filters are applied, so the count always matches the rows listed.”
