# C368169 — The work order page saves a new lead and a new mileage together
Test in run 498: https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/tests/view/3792490

## Preconditions
Setup file (for Claude sessions; a tester does not need it)
Preconditions
- You are signed in with the "Admin" role at {Location-A}, in a browser window at least 1024 px wide.
For example: {Location-A} is Staging Heavy Duty - 9919.
- Technicians {Technician-A} and {Technician-B} are active at {Location-A}, and both are offered in the Lead Technician list on the work order page.
For example: AA ZZAUTOTEST C368169 Ana and AA ZZAUTOTEST C368169 Ben (names starting "AA" sit near the top of the work order page's technician lists, which offer only about the first 100 people in A to Z order (SV-11429)).
- Customer {Customer-A} has an Approved work order {Work-order-1} led by {Technician-A}, with Mileage 120,000.
For example: {Customer-A} is ZZAUTOTEST Edit WO Lead Mileage and {Work-order-1} is S1-712.
- If any record above is missing, mark this test Blocked: "setup not done".

## Steps
- Open "Work Orders" > "All".
- Type the name of {Customer-A} in "Search".
- Click {Work-order-1}.
- On the status card at the left, click "Lead Technician" and pick {Technician-B}.
- On the status card, click "Mileage" and change it to 120500.
- Click outside the Mileage field.
- Reload the page.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- Step 1: Work Orders opens on the "All" tab.
- Step 2: {Work-order-1} shows in the List.
- Step 3: the work order page opens. On the status card "Lead Technician" reads {Technician-A} and "Mileage" reads 120,000.
- Step 4: "Lead Technician" reads {Technician-B}. It saves at once, with no error message.
- Step 5: the field shows 120500.
- Step 6: the mileage saves, with no error message.
- Step 7: "Lead Technician" reads {Technician-B} and "Mileage" reads 120,500. Both changes were saved.
