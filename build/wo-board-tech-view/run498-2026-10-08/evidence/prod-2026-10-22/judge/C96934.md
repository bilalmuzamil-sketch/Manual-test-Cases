Test in run 498: https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/tests/view/3481878

## Preconditions
Preconditions
- Needs: 1 user · 1 browser · about 15 minutes
- Build: the Work Orders QA build (sv10043, v26.40.8-7a95011). A desktop browser with the window at least 1024 pixels wide (full screen on an ordinary laptop or monitor). Below that width the page shows only List.
- [User-A]: an Owner/Admin user (on a QA branch: the Admin quick-login button on the sign-in page; you appear as "Admin ShopView"). - One location for the whole case, shown in the top bar next to the bell (e.g. "Staging Heavy Duty - 9919"); everything below is created in this location.
- Technician "Ralph Edwards" at this location, active (the expected results name him, so keep this name). A person only counts as a technician here when the staff member is active (not deactivated), Time Clock is on, the Role is neither Office User nor Time Clock User, and this location is picked in their Location field.
- Customer "ZZAUTOTEST F2 Inactive Lead" (used by this case only), with one contact and one asset, so this case's work orders are kept apart from every other work order at the location.
- Two Approved work orders for that customer, [WO-1] and [WO-2], both led by Ralph Edwards.
- What must hold: Ralph Edwards is active at the start; the case deactivates him in step 1 and he is set active again afterwards.

## Steps
- Deactivate Ralph Edwards: Settings > Staff > Ralph Edwards' edit icon > click Deactivate Account and confirm.
- Click Work Orders in the top menu.
- Click the All tab on the left of the toolbar, click Search and type ZZAUTOTEST F2 Inactive Lead.
- In the display switcher at the right of the toolbar, click Tech View (tooltip "Tech View").
- Find Ralph Edwards' group and read its header and rows.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- Ralph Edwards' group is still shown with his work orders (S1-691 and S1-578, count 2).
- His group header is marked as inactive (an inactive label or indicator). Write down how it is shown.
- Afterwards, set Ralph Edwards active again to restore the data.
Source — where this behaviour comes from
Epic SV-10043; story SV-10045 (Story 2, Tech View — table grouped by lead technician); PRD Confluence 845185030, edited 7 Oct 2026 (status line "PRD v33"), S2-N2, S2-N3, Key Decisions, read 8 Oct 2026.
Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
- S2-N2: “Keep matching work visible under its deactivated lead technician.”
- S2-N3: “Indicate deactivated technicians as inactive in group headers.”
- Key Decisions: “Deactivating a technician does not change work orders they already lead. They remain the lead technician on those work orders so the work stays visible and attributable; they simply receive no new assignments”
