# C368189 — The asset tab's toggle on an old page does not undo a new lead
Test in run 498: https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/tests/view/3792510

## Preconditions
Preconditions
- Needs: 1 user · 2 tabs · about 15 minutes
- Build: the Work Orders QA build (sv10043, v26.40.8-7a95011), desktop browser, window at least 1024 px wide.
- Role: Owner/Admin. On the sign-in page click the Admin quick-login button (you appear as Admin ShopView). The Admin role has Work orders > View and Create & Edit (check: your initials (top right) > Settings > Roles & Permissions > Admin > edit).
- Location: Staging Heavy Duty - 9919 for the whole case (shown next to the bell; change it with your initials > Change Location).
- Two technicians named exactly "Ana Alpha" and "Ben Bravo" (the expected results name them) at this location: Role Technician, a Department, Time Clock on.
- Customer "ZZAUTOTEST Asset Tab Old Page" (used by this case only) with one contact and the asset TRK-201.
- One Approved work order [WO-1] for TRK-201 with Lead Technician "Ana Alpha".
- Two tabs of the same browser window, both signed in as Admin ShopView.
Setup
- Your initials > Settings > Staff: make sure technicians named exactly "Ana Alpha" and "Ben Bravo" exist (New Staff Member > First Name Ana, Last Name Alpha / First Name Ben, Last Name Bravo > Role Technician > Departments any one > Location Staging Heavy Duty - 9919 > Time Clock on > Save & Close).
- Customers > New Customer > Name "ZZAUTOTEST Asset Tab Old Page" > Save (the customer page opens). Contacts tab > New Contact > First Name "ZZAUTOTEST", Last Name "Contact" > Save. Assets tab > New Asset > Contact = ZZAUTOTEST Contact > Year 2022 > Make: type "Freight" and pick Freightliner > Model: type "M2" and pick it > Unit "TRK-201" > Save (Contact and Make are required).
- Work Orders > Create Work Order > New Work Order: Customer = "ZZAUTOTEST Asset Tab Old Page" > Asset = TRK-201 > Save. If a Confirmation says the customer is over their credit limit, click Create. The work order opens on its Lines tab with the New Line form open. Record the number shown at the top left as [WO-1] (the expected results call it "S1-762").
- In New Line > What Are You Doing? type "grease" and pick the ready-made line "Service - Full grease service" (it has no parts) > turn Line Approved on > Save & Close.
- On [WO-1], on the status card at the left, click Lead Technician and pick "Ana Alpha". (Lead Technician is a dropdown only once the work order is Approved; while it is an Estimate it reads "Unassigned" and cannot be changed.) The choice saves at once.
- Check the setup worked: the asset page of TRK-201 > Work Orders tab lists [WO-1]; [WO-1] shows Lead Technician Ana Alpha.

## Steps
- In tab 1, open Customers > "ZZAUTOTEST Asset Tab Old Page" > Assets > TRK-201 > Work Orders tab and leave it open.
- In tab 2, open [WO-1] and change Lead Technician to "Ben Bravo".
- In tab 1, without reloading, click the [WO-1] row's Asset on Site toggle (location pin).
- In tab 1, open [WO-1] and read Lead Technician and Asset on Site (on the asset page row: green pin = on site).

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- Lead Technician reads "Ben Bravo". The older page in browser 1 did not put Ana Alpha back.
- The Asset on Site change from step 3 is kept.
