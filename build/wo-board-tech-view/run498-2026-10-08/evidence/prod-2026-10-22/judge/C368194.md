# C368194 — A column turned on in List Column Selection stays after a reload
Test in run 498: https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/tests/view/3792515

## Preconditions
Preconditions
- Needs: 1 user · 1 browser · about 5 minutes
- Build: the Work Orders QA build (sv10043, v26.40.8-7a95011), desktop browser, window at least 1024 px wide.
- Role: Owner/Admin. On the sign-in page click the Admin quick-login button (you appear as Admin ShopView). The Admin role has Work orders > View and Create & Edit (check: your initials (top right) > Settings > Roles & Permissions > Admin > edit).
- Location: Staging Heavy Duty - 9919 for the whole case (shown next to the bell; change it with your initials > Change Location).
- View: on Work Orders the List is shown (click List in the display switcher at the right of the toolbar; its tooltip reads "List").
- Starting value: Column Selection > Days open is off (the List has no Days Open column).
- Needs no test data of its own.
Setup
- Work Orders > List > Column Selection: if Days open is on, click its switch to turn it off, then close the menu (Escape or click outside).
- Check the setup worked: the List headings do not include Days Open.

## Steps
- Open Work Orders and click the All tab.
- Click Column Selection in the toolbar.
- Turn on Days open (click the switch next to it).
- Close the menu (Escape or click outside it).
- Reload the page.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)

- Step 4: the List shows a Days open column.
- Step 5: after the reload Days open is still shown.
