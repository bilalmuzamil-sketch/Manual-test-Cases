- Click "Tech View" in the display switcher.
- Click the density button.

Expected results
- Step 1: List shows no density button.
- Step 2: Tech View shows, with a density button in its toolbar.
- Step 3: the menu offers three choices, "Compact", "Regular" and "Comfortable", and "Regular" is marked as in use.
Developers also check this with unit tests: DensityMenu.spec.ts (lines 36 and 57) and WorkOrdersToolbar.spec.ts (lines 207 and 219).
