# C682421 — First Tab goes to the work order even when it is far down or off to the side
Test in run 498: https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/tests/view/4054408

## Preconditions
Preconditions
- Needs: 1 user · 1 browser · about 5 minutes · no setup
- Sign in as an Owner/Admin user (on staging or a QA branch: the Admin quick-login button on the sign-in page; you appear as "Admin ShopView"). Browser zoom 100%. Window about 1440 x 900 px (an ordinary laptop screen).
- Location "Staging Heavy Duty - 9919" (shown in the top bar next to the bell). To change it: click your initials at the top right > Change Location.
- Work order S2-16688 (status Paid) has Brandi Smith as its lead technician, and no other work order matches the search "S2-16688". (On another environment use any work order whose lead technician’s group is further down the page than the screen shows.)

## Steps
- Open this link: https://app.staging.shopview.com/workorders?tab=all&search=S2-16688 (it opens Work Orders on the All tab with S2-16688 in the Search box).
- At the right of the bar above the list, click Tech View (the middle of the three view buttons, a person icon).
- Make sure every group is open: point at the small arrow at the far left of the row of column names. If its tooltip says "Expand all", click it once.
- Look at the table without scrolling.
- Click once in the Search box, then press Tab until the keyboard focus moves into the table.
- Click Board View (the right-most view button).
- Click once in the Search box, then press Tab until a blue focus ring shows on the board.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- Step 4: "Unassigned" shows 0 work orders ("No work orders"). S2-16688 is not on the screen: it is further down, under Brandi Smith.
- Step 5: the list moves down to Brandi Smith’s group. The S2-16688 row sits just below the "Brandi Smith" heading and has the blue focus ring. The whole row and its ring can be seen; the heading does not cover them. The ring is not on the "Unassigned" heading.
- Step 7: the board scrolls sideways to Brandi Smith’s column and the S2-16688 card has the blue focus ring and is fully on the screen. The ring is not on the "Unassigned" column heading.
Source — where this behaviour comes from
Epic SV-10043; story SV-10054 (Story 11, Keyboard access and focus); ticket SV-11422; PRD "Work Orders — Board View & Tech View Display Options", Confluence 845185030, version 68, S11-R1, read 20 Oct 2026. Chris Ward in SV-11422 (16 Oct 2026): "The Tab stop must land on a work order row, never the Unassigned header while a row is shown." Chris Ward in SV-11422 (comment 79047, 19 Oct 2026), on the row that was hidden under its heading: "not acceptable: the user has to be able to see which row they’re on", asking for the whole row to sit just below the sticky heading with its blue focus ring visible.
Exact quote from the source
- S11-R1: “Each view is a single Tab stop. Tab lands on one work order row (Tech View) or card (Board View), the one last focused, or, when none has been, the first work order shown in on-screen order: the first row or card in Unassigned, or the first technician’s first row or card when Unassigned is collapsed or empty (never a group or column header while a work order is shown; when every group is collapsed, Tab lands on the first group header; after the user arrows onto a header and tabs out and back in, Tab returns to a work order row while any row is shown; on Board View, if the last-focused card has scrolled out of view in its column, Tab lands on the first card in view); the focused row or card shows a visible focus ring.”
This is the expected behaviour as per epic SV-10043, story SV-10054, ticket SV-11422 and the Work Orders — Board View & Tech View Display Options specification version 68 (S11-R1), and Chris Ward’s ruling in SV-11422 (comment 79047, 19 October 2026), which matches S11-R1’s “visible focus ring”.
