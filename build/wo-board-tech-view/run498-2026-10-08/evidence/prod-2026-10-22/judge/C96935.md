# C96935 — A tall group's header stays on screen while you scroll its rows
Test in run 498: https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/tests/view/3481879

## Preconditions
Setup (manual QA tester and Claude session)
Preconditions
- You are signed in as an Owner or Admin user whose role has Work orders > View and Work orders > Create & Edit, at {Location-A} (for example Staging Heavy Duty - 9919).
- Your browser window is at least 1024 px wide, so the display switcher (List, Tech View, Board View) shows at the right of the Work Orders toolbar.
- Technician {Technician-A} (for example Esther Howard) is an active staff member at {Location-A} with Time Clock on and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User.
- Technician {Technician-B} (for example Ralph Edwards) is an active staff member at {Location-A} with Time Clock on and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User.
- Customer {Customer-A} (for example "ZZAUTOTEST F2 Sticky Header") has about 25 Approved work orders led by {Technician-A}, more rows than fit on the screen, and 3 Approved work orders led by {Technician-B}.
- In Tech View {Technician-B}'s group comes after {Technician-A}'s group (no pins, no reordering).
- On the Work Orders page nothing is ticked in Status, Asset on Site shows no choice (if its chip shows Yes or No, click the x on the chip), Assigned to me is off and Search is empty.

## Steps
- Open Work Orders and click the All tab.
- Type the name of {Customer-A} in Search.
- Click Tech View in the display switcher.
- Scroll down slowly through {Technician-A}'s rows until the group's last row.
- Look at the top of the table.
- Keep scrolling slowly into {Technician-B}'s group.
- Look at the top of the table.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- Step 5: while you scroll through {Technician-A}'s rows, {Technician-A}'s group header (name and count) stays on screen just under the column header row.
- Step 7: {Technician-B}'s header pushes {Technician-A}'s header up and off and takes its place just under the column header row. Only one group header is held there at a time.
Source — where this behaviour comes from
Epic SV-10043; story SV-10045 (Story 2, Tech View — table grouped by lead technician); PRD "Work Orders — Board View & Tech View Display Options", Confluence 845185030, version 54 (15 Oct 2026, Chris Ward), S2-E1, read 15 Oct 2026. Product owner answers: Chris Ward, WO Board and Tech View question sheet (https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1gBGmNB4B1bRrD2ZJvBn1tuvbfxFPjcsKddz_I5H3CvU), answer 20, read 15 Oct 2026. Source-verified 15 October 2026. Last checked against build v26.40.19-72f4f3b (staging) on 10/16/2026.
The PRD (version 52) only said the header stays visible. Chris Ward answered on 15 Oct 2026 (answer 20) that it stays just under the column headings, only one header is held at a time and the next group's header pushes it off; PRD version 54 says so, and this case follows it.
Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
- S2-E1: “While the user scrolls through an expanded group, that group’s header stays on screen just under the column headings. Only one group header is held at a time: when the next group’s header reaches it, the next header pushes it off and takes its place. Unassigned’s header works the same way (S2-R18).”

## Newest PASSED result in run 498 (staging judgement)
Tested on staging, build v26.40.19-1493c63, on 16 October 2026.
- Result: Passed on staging, following the test's steps.
- Customer "ZZAUTOTEST F2 Sticky Header": 25 Approved work orders led by Ayesha Khan and 3 led by Bilal Muzamil.
- Step 5: while scrolling through Ayesha Khan's rows (readings every 125 to 250 px), her header (name and count 25) stayed on screen just under the column header row.
- Step 7: Bilal Muzamil's header pushed hers off and took its place just under the column header row; further down the next group's header did the same to his.
- Only one group header was held under the column headings at any time.
