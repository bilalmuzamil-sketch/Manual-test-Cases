# C96980 — Board View's default fields, with total price only for financial access
Test in run 498: https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/tests/view/3481924

## Preconditions
Preconditions
- Needs: 3 users · 2 browsers · about 20 minutes
- Build: the Work Orders QA build (sv10043, v26.40.8-7a95011). A desktop browser with the window at least 1024 pixels wide (full screen on an ordinary laptop or monitor). Below that width the page shows only List.
- [User-A]: an Owner/Admin user (on a QA branch: the Admin quick-login button on the sign-in page; you appear as "Admin ShopView"). The Admin role holds Work orders > View and Work orders > Create & Edit (to check: your initials at the top right > Settings > Roles & Permissions > Admin > Work orders).
- One location for the whole case, shown in the top bar next to the bell (e.g. "Staging Heavy Duty - 9919"); everything below is created in this location.
- Two brand-new users who have never opened Board View, each signed in in their own browser (the expected results name them, so keep these names): "Fay Financial" (role with Work Orders view and See Financial Data) and "Nate Nofinance" (role with Work Orders view but See Financial Data off).
- One technician at this location: [Tech-A] (e.g. "Esther Howard"). A person only counts as a technician here when the staff member is active (not deactivated), Time Clock is on, the Role is neither Office User nor Time Clock User, and this location is picked in their Location field.
- Customer "ZZAUTOTEST F2 Board Default Fields" (used by this case only), with one contact and one asset, so this case's work orders are kept apart from every other work order at the location.
- One Approved work order for that customer, [WO-1], led by [Tech-A], with a price.
Setup
- Sign in with the Admin quick-login button on the sign-in page (you appear as "Admin ShopView"). Check the location in the top bar next to the bell; to change it click your initials at the top right > Change Location.
- Settings (your initials at the top right > Settings) > Staff: confirm each technician below is active at this location with Time Clock on, or add it: New Staff Member > First Name and Last Name, an Email, Role "Technician", at least one Department (required), Location = this location, Time Clock on > Save & Close. Record them as [Tech-A] (e.g. Esther Howard).
- Settings > Roles & Permissions > Create Custom Role (e.g. "ZZAUTOTEST F2 No Financial") > turn on Work Orders view, leave See Financial Data off > Save.
- Settings (your initials at the top right > Settings) > Staff > New Staff Member with an email you can open for "Fay Financial", role = a role with Work Orders view and See Financial Data (e.g. Admin), at least one Department (required), with this location picked in Location > Save & Close, then accept the invitation and set a password.
- Settings (your initials at the top right > Settings) > Staff > New Staff Member with an email you can open for "Nate Nofinance", role = the new custom role, at least one Department (required), with this location picked in Location > Save & Close, then accept the invitation and set a password.
- Top menu Customers > New Cus

## Steps
- As Fay Financial open Work Orders, pick Board View and search for ZZAUTOTEST F2 Board Default Fields.
- Read the [WO-1] card and open Fields to display; write down which fields are on.
- Repeat steps 1 and 2 as Nate Nofinance.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- Fay Financial's card shows 8 things by default: lead technician name, work order number, customer, unit number, asset, progress, total price and status. VIN/serial, service advisor, clocked in, line count, line technicians, estimated hours, on-site indicator and created date are off.
- Nate Nofinance's defaults are the same 7 without total price, and total price is not offered in his Fields to display.
Source — where this behaviour comes from
Epic SV-10043; story SV-10048 (Story 5, Fields to display (Board View) and columns (Tech View)); PRD Confluence 845185030, edited 7 Oct 2026 (status line "PRD v33"), S5-R13, read 8 Oct 2026. Review Decisions FF-7 / DR-21 / UX-13. Source-verified 8 October 2026.
Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
- S5-R13: “Board View’s default selection is lead technician name, work order number, customer, unit number, asset, progress, total price, and mandatory status, subject to financial permission.”
