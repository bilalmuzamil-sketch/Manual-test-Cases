# C97011 — A cancelled drag leaves the order and the lead unchanged
Test in run 498: https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/tests/view/3481955

## Preconditions
Setup (manual QA tester and Claude session)
Preconditions
- You are signed in as an Owner or Admin user whose role has Work orders > View and Work orders > Create & Edit, at {Location-A} (for example Staging Heavy Duty - 9919).
- Your browser window is at least 1024 px wide, so the display switcher (List, Tech View, Board View) shows at the right of the Work Orders toolbar.
- Technician {Technician-A} (for example ZZAUTOTEST Ana Alpha) is an active staff member at {Location-A} with Time Clock on and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User.
- Technician {Technician-B} (for example ZZAUTOTEST Ben Bravo) is an active staff member at {Location-A} with Time Clock on and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User.
- Customer {Customer-A} (for example "ZZAUTOTEST F3 Cancelled Drag") has three Approved work orders: {Work-order-1} and {Work-order-2} (for example S10043-17761 and S10043-17762) led by {Technician-A}, and {Work-order-3} (for example S10043-17763) led by {Technician-B}.
- {Technician-A}'s column reads {Work-order-1}, {Work-order-2}, and the column counts are {Technician-A} 2 and {Technician-B} 1.
- No technician has a shift on the Schedule for these work orders.
- On the Work Orders page nothing is ticked in Status, Asset on Site shows no choice (if its chip shows Yes or No, click the x on the chip), Assigned to me is off and Search is empty.

## Steps
- Open Work Orders from the top menu and click the All tab.
- Type the name of {Customer-A} in Search.
- Click Board View in the display switcher.
- Write down {Technician-A}'s column order and both column counts.
- Press on {Work-order-2}'s card, drag it over {Technician-B}'s column and, still holding the mouse button, press Escape. Then release the mouse button.
- Press on {Work-order-2}'s card, drag it above {Work-order-1} and press Escape before releasing.
- Press on {Work-order-2}'s card, drag it onto the toolbar (outside every column) and release it there.
- Click Tech View in the display switcher.
- Press on {Work-order-2}'s row, drag it over {Technician-B}'s group and, still holding the mouse button, press Escape. Then release the mouse button.
- Refresh the page, open {Work-order-2} and read Lead Technician.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- Steps 5 to 9: each drag is cancelled. {Work-order-2} returns to its place, {Technician-A}'s column (or group) still reads {Work-order-1}, {Work-order-2}, the counts stay {Technician-A} 2 and {Technician-B} 1, and no message appears.
- Step 10: after the refresh nothing has changed and {Work-order-2}'s Lead Technician is still {Technician-A}.
- Steps 5, 6 and 9: Escape cancelling a drag comes from the engineering plan only. If Escape does not cancel, write that down and judge the drop-outside cancel (step 7) on its own.
Source — where this behaviour comes from
Epic SV-10043; story SV-10052 (Story 9, Drag to reorder work and technician groups/columns); PRD "Work Orders — Board View & Tech View Display Options", Confluence 845185030, version 54 (15 Oct 2026, Chris Ward), S9-N3, read 15 Oct 2026. Tech plan (revised 24 Sep 2026) Phase 11. Until version 54 the PRD's S11-R5 asked the design to define how Escape cancels an interaction; version 54 rewrote it to "Escape closes an open menu, dialog or tooltip", so Escape cancelling a drag now rests on the tech plan alone and is no longer quoted from the PRD. The expected results are unchanged. Source-verified 15 October 2026. Last checked against build v26.40.19-1493c63 (staging) on 10/16/2026.
