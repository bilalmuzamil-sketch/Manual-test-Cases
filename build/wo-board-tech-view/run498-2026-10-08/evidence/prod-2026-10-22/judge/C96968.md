## Steps
- Open Work Orders, click the All tab and pick Board View in the display switcher (right side of the toolbar; tooltip "Board View").
- Type "ZZAUTOTEST F1 Reassign Offline" in Search.
- Turn off this computer's network connection (e.g. switch Wi-Fi off) while the board stays open.
- Drag the card from [Tech-A]'s column into [Tech-B]'s column.
- Read where the card ends up and the alert that appears.
- Wait 10 seconds, then click the alert's close (X) button.
- Turn the network back on and reload the page.
- Repeat steps 3 to 7 in Tech View.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- The card (or row) goes back to Esther Howard's column (group).
- An alert reads exactly "Failed to update lead technician, please try again.", stays on screen until you close it, and closes when you click X. After the reload the lead is still Esther Howard. If the app shows a different offline message instead, write down its words: that is a deviation to report.

## Newest PASSED result in run 498 (staging judgement)
Tested on staging, build v26.40.19-1493c63, on 17 October 2026.
- Result: Passed on staging, following the test's steps.
- Customer ZZAUTOTEST F1 Reassign Offline: a work order led by Esther Howard. In Board View the card was dragged to Ralph Edwards' column while saving was failing (the save was made to fail by the script).
- The card went back to Esther Howard's column.
- The alert read exactly "Failed to update lead technician, please try again." It was still on screen after 10 seconds and went away when its Close button was clicked.
- After a reload the lead is still Esther Howard.
- Tech View behaved the same way: the row went back to Esther Howard's group with the same alert, which stayed until closed.
