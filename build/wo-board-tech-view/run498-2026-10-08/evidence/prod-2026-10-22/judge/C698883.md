## Steps
- Click "Technician".
- Look at the bottom of the list that opens, above "Clear selection".
- Tick "Unassigned".
- Press Escape.
- Click "Board View" in the display switcher.
- Click "Technician".
- Untick "Unassigned" and look at the bottom of the list again.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- Step 1: the list opens, starting with "Unassigned".
- Step 2: the message "This change would exceed the 50-value limit across all filters" does not show.
- Step 3: "Unassigned" is ticked and the message does not show.
- Step 4: the list closes.
- Step 5: Board View shows.
- Step 6: the Board View list opens with "Unassigned" still ticked, and the message does not show.
- Step 7: nothing is ticked and the message does not show.
