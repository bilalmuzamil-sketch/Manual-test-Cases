# C368152 — Reassign and Unassign work from the keyboard through the More actions menu
Test in run 498: https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/tests/view/3761673

## Preconditions
Setup file (for Claude sessions; a tester does not need it)
Preconditions
- You are signed in with the "Admin" role at {Location-A}.
For example: {Location-A} is Staging Heavy Duty - 9919.
- Two technicians are active at {Location-A} with the "Technician" role and Time Clock on: {Technician-A} and {Technician-B}.
For example: {Technician-A} is ZZAUTOTEST C368152 Ana and {Technician-B} is ZZAUTOTEST C368152 Ben.
- Customer {Customer-A} has three Approved work orders: {Work-order-1} led by {Technician-A}, {Work-order-2} led by {Technician-B}, and {Work-order-3} with no lead technician. None has a shift on the Schedule. {Technician-B} is not the technician on any line of {Work-order-1}.
For example: {Customer-A} is ZZAUTOTEST C368152 Reassign, and {Work-order-1} to {Work-order-3} are S10043-17721 to S10043-17723.
- {Technician-A} and {Technician-B} are pinned in your Board View, and no other technician is pinned.
- Starting screen: "Work Orders" > "All" tab, "Board View" shown, the name of {Customer-A} in "Search", no filter chosen, "Assigned to me" off, in a browser window at least 1024 px wide.
- If any record above is missing, mark this test Blocked: "setup not done".

## Steps
- Click once in the "Search" box.
- Press Tab until a card shows a focus ring.
- Use the arrow keys to move the focus ring to the card for {Work-order-1}.
- Press Tab.
- Press Enter.
- Choose "Reassign lead technician" with the arrow keys and press Enter.
- Type the first letters of {Technician-B}'s name in the dialog's search box.
- Choose {Technician-B} with the arrow keys and press Enter.
- Use the arrow keys to move the focus ring to the card for {Work-order-2}.
- Press Tab.
- Press Enter.
- Choose "Unassign" with the arrow keys and press Enter.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- Step 1: the cursor is in the "Search" box.
- Step 2: a card shows the focus ring: the card you focused last or, if no card has been focused yet, the first card in "Unassigned" ({Work-order-3}).
- Step 3: the card for {Work-order-1} shows the focus ring.
- Step 4: the card's "More actions" (…) button shows and has the focus.
- Step 5: the menu opens and offers "Reassign lead technician" and "Unassign".
- Step 6: the "Reassign lead technician" dialog opens.
- Step 7: the dialog's list narrows to {Technician-B}.
- Step 8: the dialog closes, {Work-order-1} moves to {Technician-B}'s column and the message "{Work-order-1} assigned to {Technician-B}" shows, with an "Undo" button.
- Step 9: the card for {Work-order-2} shows the focus ring.
- Step 10: the card's "More actions" (…) button has the focus.
- Step 11: the menu opens.
- Step 12: {Work-order-2} moves to the "Unassigned" column and the message "{Work-order-2} moved to Unassigned" shows, with an "Undo" button.
Source — where this behaviour comes from
Epic SV-10043; stories SV-10054 (Story 11, Keyboard access and focus), SV-10047 (Story 4, Reassign or unassign the lead technician); PRD "Work Orders — Board View & Tech View Display Options", Confluence 845185030, version 68 (19 Oct 2026, Chris Ward), S11-R1, S11-R3, S11-R9 and S4-R5, read 19 Oct 2026. Tech plan (revised 24 Sep 2026) Phase 11. Product owner answers: Chris Ward, WO Board and Tech View question sheet (https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1gBGmNB4B1bRrD2ZJvBn1tuvbfxFPjcsKddz_I5H3CvU), answer 28, read 15 Oct 2026. Source-verified 19 October 2026. Not yet checked against a build in this wording.
Until 15 Oct 2026 Story 11 only listed keyboard behaviour the design still had to define, so this case asked the tester to try keys and write down what each did. Chris Ward answered on 15 Oct 2026 (answer 28): Tab reaches every control, Enter opens, Escape closes, focus returns to the control that opened a menu or dialog, reassigning by keyboard goes through the ⋯ menu, and there is no keyboard drag. PRD version 54 rewrites Story 11 into these requirements, and this case now checks them.
Chris Ward's answer 28 (15 Oct 2026) and PRD version 54 said Tab reaches every row, card and header in on-screen order. PRD versions 55, 60 and 61 (15 and 16 Oct 2026, Chris Ward rulings on Slavcho Mitrov's questions and the QA triage of SV-11395, SV-11397, SV-11422 and SV-11421) changed this: each view is one Tab stop that lands on a work order, and the arrow keys move between cards, rows, headers and columns. Version 62 (16 Oct 2026, Chris Ward ruling on Bilal's QA questions 32 and 33) added where the first Tab lands when nothing has been focused yet, and version 67 (19 Oct 2026) reworded S11-R9 for the Assign or Reassign lead technician dialog. In versions 66 to 68 the messages after a lead change became "{work order number} assigned to {technician name}" and "{work order number} moved to Unassigned", with Undo. This case follows version 68, the latest.
Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
- S11-R1: “Each view is a single Tab stop. Tab lands on one work order row (Tech View) or card (Board View), the one last focused, or, when none has been, the first work order shown in on-screen order: the first row or card in Unassigned, or the first technician’s first row or card when Unassigned is collapsed or empty (never a group or column header while a work order is shown; when every group is collapsed, Tab lands on the first group header; after the user arrows onto a header and tabs out and back in, Tab returns to a work order row while any row is shown; on Board View, if the last-focused card has scrolled out of view in its column, Tab lands on the first card in view); the focused row or card shows a visible focus ring.”
- S11-R3: “Inside a view the arrow keys move focus: Up and Down move between rows and group headers in Tech View, and between cards and the column header within a Board View column; Left and Right move between Board View columns. Tab from a focused row, card or header reaches its own controls (more actions, pin, collapse) in order, then leaves the view. Tab never stops on every row.”
- S11-R9: “Assigning, reassigning and unassigning by keyboard go through the more-actions menu: Assign lead technician or Reassign lead technician (S3-R14) opens the lead technician dialog, and Unassign is a menu item. There is no keyboard drag in this release, so reordering work orders and technicians is by mouse or touch only.”
- S4-R5: “After a successful lead change on Board View or Tech View, whether by drag, the dialog or Unassign, show the design’s toast (white, with the check icon) with an Undo button. It reads “{work order number} assigned to {technician name}”; “{work order number} lead technician set to {technician name}” when the new lead was already the technician on at least one of the work order’s lines; or “{work order number} moved to Unassigned” after removing the lead. Examples: “S1-357 assigned to Esther Howard”, “S1-702 lead technician set to Ralph Edwards”, “S1-352 moved to Unassigned”. This replaces the standard success toast for these messages only.”
- Tech plan Phase 11 dialog: “The dialog is the keyboard path for moving work between technicians; it runs the same permission and status checks as the pointer path (S11-N1)”

## Newest PASSED result in run 498 (staging judgement)
Tested on staging, build v26.40.19-1493c63, on 17 October 2026.
- Result: Passed on staging, following the test's steps.
- Set-up: Work-order-1 (S2-37021) led by Technician-A, Work-order-2 (S2-37022) by Technician-B.
- Steps 1 to 5: Tab and the arrow keys reach S2-37021's card; Tab reaches its More actions; Enter opens the menu with Reassign lead technician and Unassign.
- Steps 6 and 7: the dialog opens; typing "ZZAUTOTEST Be" narrows it to Unassigned and Ben Bravo.
- Step 8: picking Ben Bravo and Reassign by keyboard moves S2-37021 to his column; "Lead technician updated" shows.
- Steps 9 to 12: the arrow keys reach S2-37022; Tab reaches its More actions; the menu opens; Unassign moves it to Unassigned and "Lead technician removed" shows.
- The earlier failure (no Unassign item, SV-11419, Testing Stage) is gone.
