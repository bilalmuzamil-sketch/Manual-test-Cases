## Preconditions
Preconditions
- Needs: 1 user · 1 browser · about 5 minutes
- Build: the Work Orders QA build (sv10043, v26.40.8-7a95011), desktop browser, window at least 1024 px wide.
- Role: Owner/Admin. On the sign-in page click the Admin quick-login button (you appear as Admin ShopView). The Admin role has Work orders > View and Create & Edit (check: your initials (top right) > Settings > Roles & Permissions > Admin > edit).

Setup
- Customers > New Customer > Name "ZZAUTOTEST New WO Listed" > Save (the customer page opens). Contacts tab > New Contact > First Name "ZZAUTOTEST", Last Name "Contact" > Save. - Check the setup worked: Customers > "ZZAUTOTEST New WO Listed" > Assets tab shows the asset.

## Steps
- Open Work Orders and click the All tab.
- Click Create Work Order.
- Pick "ZZAUTOTEST New WO Listed" as Customer and its asset as Asset > Save (if a Confirmation about the credit limit appears, click Create). Write down the number shown at the top left; close the New Line window that opens (X).
- Go back to Work Orders (top menu) without reloading.
- Click the All tab, click Search and type "ZZAUTOTEST New WO Listed".
