# C96916 — Browser Back restores scroll, tab and filters in Tech View and Board View
Test in run 498: https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/tests/view/3481861

## Preconditions
Setup file (for Claude sessions; a tester does not need it)
Preconditions
- You are signed in with the "Admin" role at {Location-A}.
For example: {Location-A} is Staging Heavy Duty - 9919.
- Eight technicians are active at {Location-A} with the "Technician" role and Time Clock on: {Technician-A}, {Technician-B}, {Technician-C}, {Technician-D}, {Technician-E}, {Technician-F}, {Technician-G} and {Technician-H}.
For example: {Technician-A} to {Technician-H} are ZZAUTOTEST C96916 Ana, Ben, Cal, Dee, Eve, Fay, Gus and Hal.
- Customer {Customer-A} has about 20 Approved work orders led by {Technician-H} and 2 led by each of the other seven technicians. With a search on its name, Board View is wider than the window (you scroll right to reach {Technician-H}'s column), and that column and the Tech View table are taller than the window.
For example: {Customer-A} is ZZAUTOTEST C96916 Scroll Back.
- Starting screen: "Work Orders" > "All" tab (the "Status" filter is on this tab), "Board View" shown, the name of {Customer-A} in "Search", no filter chosen, "Assigned to me" off, "Status" filter set to "Approved" only, in a browser window at least 1024 px wide.
- If any record above is missing, mark this test Blocked: "setup not done".

## Steps
- Scroll the board right until {Technician-H}'s column is fully in view.
- Scroll down inside that column until a card well below the first screen is at the top of the column.
- Write down the columns in view and the card at the top of the column.
- Click a card that is in view to open its work order.
- Press the browser Back button.
- Click "Tech View" in the display switcher.
- Scroll down until a row well below the first screen is at the top, and scroll right if the table is wider than the window.
- Write down the row at the top and the columns in view.
- Click a row to open its work order.
- Press the browser Back button.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- Step 1: {Technician-H}'s column is fully in view.
- Step 2: a card well below the first screen sits at the top of the column.
- Step 3: you have the columns and the top card written down.
- Step 4: the work order page opens.
- Step 5: Board View comes back on the "All" tab with "Status" "Approved" still chosen, the same columns in view at exactly the same sideways position, and the card that was at the top back at the top, fully shown (at most one card away).
- Step 6: Tech View shows the same work orders, still on the "All" tab with "Status" "Approved".
- Step 7: a row well below the first screen sits at the top.
- Step 8: you have the row and the columns written down.
- Step 9: the work order page opens.
- Step 10: Tech View comes back on the "All" tab with "Status" "Approved" still chosen, the same columns in view at exactly the same sideways position, and the row that was at the top back at the top, fully shown (at most one row away).
Source — where this behaviour comes from
Epic SV-10043; story SV-10044 (Story 1, Switch between display options); PRD "Work Orders — Board View & Tech View Display Options", Confluence 845185030, version 61 (16 Oct 2026, Chris Ward), S1-R8, read 16 Oct 2026. Review Decisions 853901313 FF-3 / DR-6. Product owner answers: Chris Ward, WO Board and Tech View question sheet (https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1gBGmNB4B1bRrD2ZJvBn1tuvbfxFPjcsKddz_I5H3CvU), answer 30, read 15 Oct 2026. Source-verified 16 October 2026. Last checked against build v26.40.19-e435950 (staging) on 10/19/2026.
QA lead decision, 19 Oct 2026: the "Status" filter is offered on the "All" tab only (production does the same), so this case uses the "All" tab.
On staging the up-and-down position came back about one row off. Chris Ward answered on 15 Oct 2026 (answer 30) that sideways comes back exactly and vertically the row or card that was at the top comes back at the top, fully shown, within one row or card; PRD version 54 says so, and this case follows it.
PRD version 61 (16 Oct 2026, Chris Ward ruling on Slavcho Mitrov's questions on SV-11395, SV-11397, SV-11422 and SV-11421) adds that Back also restores the filter view (tab) and filters in Tech View and Board View; this case starts on the All tab with Status Approved and steps 5 and 10 check that both come back.
Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
- S1-R8: “In Tech View and Board View, browser Back restores both vertical and horizontal scroll position. Horizontal position comes back exactly, and so do the filter view (tab) and filters that were on screen, in Tech View and Board View. List’s Back behavior is unchanged in this release. Vertically, the row or card that was at the top of the view comes back at the top, fully shown; it may sit up to one row or card from the exact pixel position. List behaves as it does today.”
