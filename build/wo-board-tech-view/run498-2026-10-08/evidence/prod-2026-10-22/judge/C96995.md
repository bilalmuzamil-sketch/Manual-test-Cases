# C96995 — Extra technicians show as +N and every name can be found by hovering
Test in run 498: https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/tests/view/3481939

## Preconditions
Setup (manual QA tester and Claude session)
Preconditions
- You are signed in as an Owner or Admin user whose role has Work orders > View and Work orders > Create & Edit, at {Location-A} (for example Staging Heavy Duty - 9919).
- Your browser window is at least 1024 px wide, so the display switcher (List, Tech View, Board View) shows at the right of the Work Orders toolbar.
- Technician {Technician-A} (for example Esther Howard) is an active staff member at {Location-A} with Time Clock on and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User.
- Technician {Technician-B} (for example Ralph Edwards) is an active staff member at {Location-A} with Time Clock on and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User.
- Technician {Technician-C} (for example Dana Ortiz) is an active staff member at {Location-A} with Time Clock on and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User.
- Technician {Technician-D} (for example Jenny Wilson) is an active staff member at {Location-A} with Time Clock on and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User.
- Technician {Technician-E} (for example Kristin Watson) is an active staff member at {Location-A} with Time Clock on and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User.
- Technician {Technician-F} (for example Theresa Webb) is an active staff member at {Location-A} with Time Clock on and a Role other than Office User or Time Clock User.
- Approved work order {Work-order-1} (for example S10043-17607) for customer {Customer-A} (for example "ZZAUTOTEST F3 Plus N Avatars") is led by {Technician-A} and has five approved lines, each given to a different technician: {Line-1} (for example "Service - Full grease service") to {Technician-B}, {Line-2} (for example "Service - Adjust clutch") to {Technician-C}, {Line-3} (for example "Replace - Brake pot") to {Technician-D}, {Line-4} (for example a second "Service - Full grease service") to {Technician-E} and {Line-5} (for example a second "Service - Adjust clutch") to {Technician-F}.
- So six different people are on {Work-order-1}, more than the avatar group can show.

## Steps
- Open Work Orders, click the All tab and click Board View in the display switcher (tooltip "Board View").
- Type the name of {Customer-A} in Search.
- Click Fields to display, turn line technicians on and close the menu.
- Count the avatars shown on the {Work-order-1} card and read the +N indicator.
- Hover each visible avatar, one at a time, and read the name shown.
- Hover the +N indicator and read what it shows.
- Click Tech View in the display switcher.
- Click the Column Selection button and make sure Assigned Techs is on (it is on by default; turn it on if it was turned off), then close the menu.
- Count the avatars shown in {Work-order-1}'s Assigned Techs cell and read the +N indicator.
- Hover each visible avatar, one at a time, and read the name shown.
- Hover the +N indicator and read what it shows.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- Steps 4 and 9: if not all 6 fit, the first avatars are shown followed by +N, and the avatars shown plus N equal 6 (for example 4 avatars and "+2").
- Steps 5 and 10: hovering an avatar shows that avatar's own name, only while the pointer is on that avatar.
- Steps 6 and 11: every one of the 6 technicians can be found by name, including those behind +N (for example hovering "+2" lists {Technician-E} and {Technician-F}).
Source — where this behaviour comes from
Epic SV-10043; story SV-10050 (Story 7, Show line technicians on cards and rows); PRD "Work Orders — Board View & Tech View Display Options", Confluence 845185030, version 52 (14 Oct 2026, Chris Ward), S7-R4, S7-R6, S7-R2 and S7-R7 (S7-R7 rewritten in version 51 on 14 Oct 2026 (Chris Ward ruling after Sasha Grosman's staging review: Tech View's default columns are the design's 13, and Assigned Techs is on by default); the Tech View step now only makes sure Assigned Techs is on, the expected results are unchanged), read 14 Oct 2026. Design export of 8 Oct 2026 (Claude Design project 787fef1a, Work Orders.dc.html) / design system TechStack: up to 5 avatars, else 4 avatars + "+N"; hovering +N lists the hidden names. Source-verified 14 October 2026. Last checked against build v26.40.8-7a95011 on 10/8/2026.
Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
- S7-R4: “When avatars exceed available space, show the first avatars followed by a +N overflow indicator.”
- S7-R2: “Use each visible avatar’s own visible area as its hover target.”
- S7-R6: “Keep every involved technician discoverable by name.”
- S7-R7: “In Tech View the Assigned Techs column sits immediately after Lines, matching the design. It is on by default (S5-R4) and can be removed through the column chooser (S2-R4).”
