# C590115 — Design: Tech View status label matches List, 16 px tall, 22 px at Comfortable
Test in run 498: https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/tests/view/3995724

## Preconditions
Setup (manual QA tester and Claude session)
Preconditions
- You are signed in as {User-A} (for example Admin ShopView), an Owner or Admin user whose role has Work orders > View, Work orders > Create & Edit and See financial data, at {Location-A} (for example Staging Heavy Duty - 9919).
- Your browser window is 1440 px wide, with the browser zoom at 100%.
- The design pictures DC-06-tech-view-compact-light-1440.png, DC-06-tech-view-regular-light-1440.png and DC-06-tech-view-comfortable-light-1440.png are open beside the app at 100% zoom (actual size); they are attached to this case.
- The design pictures show the status pill without the red and blue numbers; the PRD keeps List's pill with its numbers, so compare only the pill's shape and height with the pictures.
- Technician {Technician-A} (for example ZZAUTOTEST DC06 Gia Pills) is an active technician at {Location-A}.
- Customer {Customer-A} (for example "ZZAUTOTEST DC06 Status Pill") is used by this case only.
- Approved work order {Work-order-1} (for example S10043-17815) for {Customer-A} is led by {Technician-A} and has one approved line, {Line-1} (for example "Service - Full grease service"), that is not complete and has a part {Part-A} (for example ZZAUTOTEST-PN-DC06) requested and not yet ordered, so List shows a red and a blue number on its status pill.
- On the Work Orders page nothing is chosen in any filter (if the Asset on Site chip shows Yes or No, click the x on the chip), Assigned to me is off, Search is empty and density is Regular (the default).

## Steps
- Open Work Orders, click the All tab and click List in the display switcher.
- Look at {Work-order-1}'s status pill and the red and blue numbers on it.
- Hover the red number, then the blue number, and read each tooltip.
- Click Tech View in the display switcher.
- Look at {Work-order-1}'s status pill.
- Hover the red number, then the blue number, and read each tooltip.
- Click the density button and choose Compact.
- Look at the status pills and compare them with the Compact design picture.
- Click the density button and choose Regular.
- Look at the status pills and compare them with the Regular design picture.
- Click the density button and choose Comfortable.
- Look at the status pills and compare them with the Comfortable design picture.
- Click List in the display switcher and look at {Work-order-1}'s status pill.

## Expected (source quote — judge against this, never change it)
Expected results
- Step 5: the pill is the same pill as in List at step 2: the same colours, the same word, the same rounded shape and the same width, with the same red and blue numbers on it.
- Step 6: each number shows the same tooltip as in List at step 3.
- Steps 8 and 10: the pills are the same height at Compact and at Regular, as low as the small pills in the Compact and Regular design pictures (16 px).
- Step 12: at Comfortable the pills are taller than at Regular, as tall as the pills in the Comfortable picture (22 px), and keep the same width.
- Step 13: List's pill looks exactly as at step 2; Tech View's density did not change it.
Source — where this behaviour comes from
Epic SV-10043; stories SV-10045 (Story 2, Tech View), SV-10049 (Story 6, Density); PRD "Work Orders — Board View & Tech View Display Options", Confluence 845185030, version 54 (15 Oct 2026, Chris Ward), S2-R19, S6-R2a and S6-E1, read 15 Oct 2026; Claude Design project "Work Orders" (Branko Cicovic), export of 14 Oct 2026, board "Work Orders.dc.html", states core__work-orders__tech-compact__light__1440, core__work-orders__tech-regular__light__1440 and core__work-orders__tech-comfortable__light__1440 (design pictures DC-06-tech-view-compact-light-1440.png, DC-06-tech-view-regular-light-1440.png, DC-06-tech-view-comfortable-light-1440.png, rendered from that export at the width in the name), read 14 Oct 2026. Covers the design defects SV-11318 (reported 14 Oct 2026 by Chris Ward). Where the PRD says nothing about the look, the design is the source. Product owner answers: Chris Ward, WO Board and Tech View question sheet (https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1gBGmNB4B1bRrD2ZJvBn1tuvbfxFPjcsKddz_I5H3CvU), answer 15, read 15 Oct 2026. Source-verified 15 October 2026. Last checked against build v26.40.19-72f4f3b (staging) on 10/16/2026.
The PRD (version 52) said 18 px at Compact and Regular, while the design draws the small pill 16 px tall. Chris Ward answered on 15 Oct 2026 (answer 15) that the pill is 16 px at Compact and Regular and 22 px at Comfortable, as the design draws it; PRD version 54 corrects the size and adds that Tech View's pill takes List's widths. This case follows version 54. List's own pill widths are checked in their own case.
Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
- S2-R19: “Tech View’s Status column is the same status pill List shows in production, with the same red service-actions count and blue parts-actions count on it. Its size follows density (S6-R2a).”
- S6-R2a: “In Tech View the status pill is 16px tall at Compact and Regular and 22px tall at Comfortable, the design’s small and regular badges. List’s status pill is unchanged: for the same work orders on the same page, every List pill is the same width and height as in production, including production’s matching widths down the column (for example, Approved as wide as Ready For Review when both are on the page). Tech View’s pill takes the same widths as List’s; only its height follows density.”
- S6-E1: “Density applies to Tech View rows and Board View cards on Work Orders. List keeps today’s row size, and other application tables are unchanged.”
- Design — Work Orders.dc.html, Tech View status badge size: “badgeSize: rowDensity === "large" ? "md" : "sm"”
