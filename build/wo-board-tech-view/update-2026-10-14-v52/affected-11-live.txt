Preconditions

The Admin role holds Work orders > View and Work orders > Create & Edit (to check: your initials at the top right > Settings > Roles & Permissions > Admin > Work orders).

• One technician at this location: [Tech-A] (e.g. "Esther Howard"). A person only counts as a technician here when the staff member is active (not deactivated), Time Clock is on, the Role is neither Office User nor Time Clock User, and this location is picked in their Location field.

Setup
• Sign in with the Admin quick-login button on the sign-in page (you appear as "Admin ShopView"). Check the location in the top bar next to the bell; to change it click your initials at the top right > Change Location.

• Work Orders > Create Work Order > in the New Work Order window pick Customer "ZZAUTOTEST F2 Tech Columns" and its asset > Save. • Set the leads: on an Approved work order pick the technician in the Lead Technician dropdown on the work order page; it saves at once. (While a work order is an Estimate its Lead technician is read-only on the work order page; give an Estimate its lead from Board View instead: the card's More actions (…) > Reassign lead technician > pick the technician > Reassign.) Leave Lead Technician empty on the ones that should sit in Unassigned. Here: [WO-1] = [Tech-A].
• Check the setup worked: Work Orders > All tab > Search ZZAUTOTEST F2 Tech Columns — [WO-1] shows with Lead Technician [Tech-A]. If not, set the lead before starting.
--STEPS
• Open Work Orders.
• Click the All tab, click Search and type ZZAUTOTEST F2 Tech Columns.
• In List, click the Column Selection button (tooltip "Column Selection") and write down every column name in the menu.
• Close the menu.
• In the display switcher at the right of the toolbar, click Tech View (tooltip "Tech View").
• Click the Column Selection button and write down every column name.
• Turn on Assigned Techs if it is off and close the menu.
--EXP
Expected results
• Tech View has its own Columns button that opens a column menu.
• Every column offered in List's menu is also offered in Tech View's menu, and Tech View's menu also offers Assigned Techs.
• List's menu does not offer Assigned Techs.
• After turning it on, an Assigned Techs column shows in the Tech View table.
• The design's Tech View menu offers 10 columns and leaves out some List columns (e.g. VIN/Serial #, On Site, Lead Technician): if the build matches the design rather than List, it is a fail, write down the missing columns.
Source — where this behaviour comes from
