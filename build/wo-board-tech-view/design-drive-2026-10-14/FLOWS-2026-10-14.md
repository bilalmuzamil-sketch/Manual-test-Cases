# WO Board & Tech View — design export of 14 Oct 2026: multi-step flows driven (Rule 124 gate (c))

**How:** every multi-step flow the files define was listed from the files (dialogs with a search, drags, menus inside a view, confirm /
undo, empty and error states behind an action, the new preview panel) and clicked through two to three levels deep with real mouse and
keyboard input in Chromium (Playwright) on a copy of the package served over http://127.0.0.1. Script:
`scripts/flow_drive.py` (16 flows, 158 recorded steps and 57 checks); step-by-step evidence with the text that appeared and disappeared at
each step: `evidence/flows-steps-2026-10-14.md`; screenshots kept in the session scratchpad (161 PNGs, not in the repo). Four short
probes (`scripts/probe_*.py`: filter / search empty states per display, work-order and technician drag order) are recorded in the table below. Expected behaviour still comes only from
the spec; this file records what the design shows.

Machine time: Details board ≈ 5 min, Work Orders board ≈ 4 min, Add Part ≈ 1 min, plus probes; review time within the ~30 min per board.
