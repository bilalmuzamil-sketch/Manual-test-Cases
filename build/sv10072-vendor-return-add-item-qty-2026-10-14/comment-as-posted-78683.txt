h3. How to see it
No screen in the app adds an item to an existing vendor return, so this needs one line pasted into the browser console.
# Open https://app.staging.shopview.com and log in as *Admin*. The location at the top right should read *Staging Heavy Duty - 9919*.
# In the top menu click {{Parts}}. The Inventory page opens ({{Inventory}} is highlighted in the left-hand menu under PARTS). If a {{Bin Location}} filter chip shows above the list, click the small ⊗ on it to clear it. Above the list, on the right, click {{Search}} (the magnifying glass) and type {{201.6215}}. The *WHEEL STUD* row shows *16 Available* in the {{Total Qty}} column.
# Press F12, open the *Console* tab, paste this and press Enter:
{code:javascript}
fetch('https://api.staging.shopview.com/api/inventory/returns/add-item',{method:'POST',credentials:'include',headers:{'Content-Type':'application/json'},body:JSON.stringify({return_id:'1b285dfe-0259-427f-a3b3-527850aea3b9',inventory_part_id:'18658006-86a7-4ef0-934c-4668995722bf',quantity:'2',credits:10})}).then(r=>console.log(r.status))
{code}
It prints *201*.

# Click the clock icon at the left of the row: the top line reads *Returned qty 2 … Qty: 16 → 14 (-2)*.
