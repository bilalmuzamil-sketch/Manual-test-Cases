### How to read the `NOT-TESTABLE` 46 — this is not 46 requirements waved away

**This spec expresses many requirements as two-cell table rows**: a **label cell** (*"Today button"*,
*"Double-booked"*, *"Priority"*) and a **description cell** (*"Jumps the grid to the current date."*,
*"Two different work orders overlap on the same technician at the same time."*). The extractor sees
two lines; the requirement is the row. **The label cell is verdicted `NOT-TESTABLE` and the assertion
is verdicted on the description cell**, which appears in the `COVERED` count. No assertion is lost,
and every label cell says in its row which case carries its pair.

The 46 break down as: **§6** 8 toolbar control names · **§8.1** 12 data-model entity names and field
lists · **§9** 8 View-Options / Filter-and-Display control names · **§5.1** 5 filter names and option
lists · **§4.11** 4 conflict-type names · **§4.1** 3 drag-source names · **§1.2** 4 goal statements ·
**§4.9** 1 (*"Technician."*, a modal field label) · plus **§8.1-L153** (`sid, woId, rowKey (tech),
date, startHour, blockDuration, lines[], seriesId`) and **§8.1-L166** (*"Belongs to Department; has
many Shifts and Events"*), both data-model definitions that the automatic classifier missed on
punctuation and which are **hand-classified here** rather than left in the weak bucket.

**The one place a value cell IS load-bearing, and is verdicted as covered rather than as a label:**
§9's **default-state** cells. Those are real requirements and they are covered verbatim —
*"All on / Off / Off"* by **SCH-VIEW-01 = [C30042](https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/cases/view/30042)**
