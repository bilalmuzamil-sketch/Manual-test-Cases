## 5. VIN §4.13-vs-§9 inconsistency — RESOLVED by the design

- **Was:** §4.13 says the hover tooltip always shows "unit, vehicle, and VIN"; §9 says the
  VIN toggle gates VIN "in hover tooltips" (toggle off by default) → contradiction. Spec
  `_1` body is unchanged, so the **textual** inconsistency persists in the spec.
- **Design ruling (latest-wins):** the tooltip shows VIN **unconditionally when present**
  (code: `unitAsset = unit · asset (+ ' · VIN '+vin)`); the **"VIN Number"** toggle drives
  only the **shift-BLOCK** VIN line + lane height; the modal always shows VIN.
- **→ RESOLVED in favour of §4.13** (tooltip always shows VIN). §9's tooltip-gating claim is
  superseded. Actionable as **SCH-VIEW-04 (C30045)** edit in §3A. **Still recommend flagging
  to Branko** that the spec §9 prose should be corrected to match the design (doc hygiene).
