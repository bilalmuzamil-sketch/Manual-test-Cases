## Honesty notes (Rule 12)

1. **Nothing here is live-build-verified.** Schedule has no QA branch (OQ-3). All 24 cases remain
   **VIU-Pending**. Spec-, design-, tech-plan- and **PO-pinned still ≠ VIU-Verified** — a PO
   answer tells us what the build *should* do, never what it *does*.
2. **Three assertions were deliberately NOT made**, because the spec is silent and Branko did not
   address them (Rule 15 — never pick a side silently): whether event hours feed the per-technician
   **OT tag / hover breakdown** (A1), whether a **department-assigned** event consumes each
   technician's time (A2), and whether an **all-day** event consumes a full day (A3). The stale
   "events do not count" claims were **withdrawn without being replaced by their opposite.**
3. **One new open point was created by the ruling itself** and is recorded, not assumed: does
   turning the **'Events' display toggle OFF** also remove event hours from the capacity bars, or
   only hide the blocks? Spec silent → question **A7**, noted on SCH-VIEW-05 (C30046).
4. **`notes` and `viu_status` never reach TestRail** — the executor pushes only title /
   preconds / steps / expected / refs. 9 of the 24 edits are therefore local-only and are labelled
   as such in `../backup/MANIFEST.md`.
5. **Retired bodies were left alone.** SCH-REAS-04 / SCH-REAS-05 still contain "right-click" in
   their steps; both were merged away and deleted from TestRail on 2026-07-31 and are kept locally
   only as history. They are excluded from the import, the push and this audit's scope.
