*What is happening:* on a work order, a part that was first added from the *Catalog* (to be ordered from a vendor) cannot be switched to the *Inventory* version of the same part number. Clicking *Save & Close* shows "Missing required parameter. Please try to resolve this." and nothing is saved.

h3. Steps to reproduce
# Log in to production (app.shopview.com) and make sure the location at the top right reads *Trucks Hill 2*.
# Open work order *S2-968* ([direct link|https://app.shopview.com/workorders/4b2e1143-122d-471f-a045-7fdf89e6a404/lines]) and stay on the *Lines* tab.
# Under *Parts*, click *+ Add Part*. In *Part number* type *ZZ11243-920183* and choose the option that says *Catalog* under the name (not the one that says "Inventory Qty"). In *Qty* type *25*, then click the blue *Save* button at the right end of that row.
# The new part row reads *Auth To Order* with a blue *Order* button and $0.00. Click the part number *ZZ11243-920183* on that row.
!sv11243-01-lines-hd.png|width=1280,height=360!
# A pop-up titled *Edit Part Request* opens. Click the *Part Number* field. The list shows the same part twice. Choose the one that says *Inventory Qty*.
!sv11243-02-pick-inventory-hd.png|width=608,height=691!
# The title changes to *Edit Part* and an *Inventory* section appears at the bottom. Type *1* under *Amount*, then click *Save & Close*.
!sv11243-03-amount-1-hd.png|width=608,height=842!
# A red message appears at the bottom right: "Missing required parameter. Please try to resolve this." The pop-up stays open. Close it and reload the page: the part is unchanged (still Auth To Order, $0.00).
!sv11243-04-error-hd.png|width=1008,height=976!

*Fastest way:* open the [S2-968 link|https://app.shopview.com/workorders/4b2e1143-122d-471f-a045-7fdf89e6a404/lines] (location Trucks Hill 2) and start at step 4. Any of the four *Auth To Order* rows for ZZ11243-920183 on that work order will do.

h3. Current and expected behaviour
* *Current:* the change is not saved, and the message does not say which field is the problem.
* *Expected* (from this ticket): "The system should successfully save the updated part when all required information is present. If a required parameter is missing or invalid, the error message should clearly identify the affected field so the user can correct it."

h3. What makes it happen
* It happens only when the part was first added from the *Catalog* option and is then switched to the *Inventory* option of the *same* part number. That matches the customer's note that part #920183 "appears in both Inventory and Catalog".
* The *Amount* doesn't matter: 1, 25 and leaving it empty all give the same error.
* If the part was first added under a *different* part number (a generic one) and then replaced with an Inventory part, it saves without an error. That may be why it could not be reproduced before.

h3. Environment
Production app.shopview.com, build v26.40.17-f5bf8f4, 13 October 2026, location Trucks Hill 2, work order S2-968, part ZZ11243-920183, logged in with our production test account (bilal.muzamil@shopview.com). Reproduced 4 times; the last time was just before posting this comment.
