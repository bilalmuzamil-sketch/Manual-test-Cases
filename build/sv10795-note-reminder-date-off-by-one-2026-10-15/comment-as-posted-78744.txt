h3. How to see it
The difference only shows on a computer whose time zone is behind UTC. Set yours to US Eastern Time first (Windows: *Settings → Time & language → Date & time*, turn off *Set time zone automatically*, choose *(UTC-05:00) Eastern Time (US & Canada)*), then restart Chrome.
# Open https://sv10795.qa.shopview.com and click *Admin* on the login page.
# In the top menu click {{Work Orders}}, click {{Search}} (the magnifying glass above the list, on the right) and type {{S10795-17580}}, then click the work order number *S10795-17580* in the list.
# Click the {{Notes}} tab (next to {{Parts}}), then the blue {{New Note}} button at the top right.
# Type any text. Click the calendar icon in the {{Reminder Date}} box and click tomorrow's date. The box shows tomorrow.
# Click {{Save}}. At the top right of the new note, next to the bell, the date is tomorrow's, in grey.
# Do the same on production (https://app.shopview.com): the note shows the day before the one you picked, in red.
