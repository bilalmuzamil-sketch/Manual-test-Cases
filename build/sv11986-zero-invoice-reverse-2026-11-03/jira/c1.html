The steps on the branch

How to see it on the branch

  1. Open sv11986 as Admin, location Staging Heavy Duty - 9919.

  2. Create a work order for any customer, add a line in the New Line dialog with Labor Rate Fixed Line Total and Fixed Line Total 0.00, tick Line Approved, Save & Close. Complete the line and the work order, then invoice it. The badge reads Paid.

  3. In the top menu click Customers, open the customer, open the Payments tab. Click the red bin on the $0.00 "Payment for invoice .." row, then Reverse in the dialog.

  4. Open the work order again. The badge now reads Invoiced.

  5. On the Finance tab click the three dots beside the settings cog, then Reverse, then Reverse in the dialog. The badge reads Complete and Create Invoice is available again.
