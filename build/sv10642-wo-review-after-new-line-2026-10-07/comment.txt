h3. How to see it yourself

_(Updated on 7 October 2026, TIMEHERE CDT: steps rewritten to say exactly where to click.)_
# Open [sv10642.qa.shopview.com|https://sv10642.qa.shopview.com] and click the *Admin* quick-login button.
# In the top menu, click {{Work Orders}}.
# Above the list, on the right, click {{Search}} (the magnifying glass) and type *S10642-17582*.
# Click *S10642-17582* in the {{Number}} column. The work order opens on its {{Lines}} tab, and the small label at the top left, next to the work order number, reads {{Review}}.
# At the top right, click the blue {{New Line}} button.
# In the {{New Line}} window, click the {{What Are You Doing?}} box, type *Battery* and click *Service - Battery service*.
# Leave the {{Line Approved}} box (below {{Labor Rate}}) unticked, and click the blue {{Save & Close}} button at the bottom right of the window.

*Expected:*
* The work order changes from {{Review}} to {{Approved}}.
* {{Mark Reviewed}} is no longer shown.
* On the new line, in the {{Action}} column, click the red {{Decline}} button: the work order goes back to {{Review}}.
