h3. How to see it yourself

# Open [sv10642.qa.shopview.com|https://sv10642.qa.shopview.com] with the *Admin* quick-login.
# Open any work order whose lines are all {{Complete}}, so it shows {{Review}} (for example *S10642-17582*).
# Click {{New Line}}, pick *Service - Battery service* in {{What Are You Doing?}}, leave {{Line Approved}} unticked and click {{Save & Close}}.

*Expected:*
* The work order changes from {{Review}} to {{Approved}}.
* {{Mark Reviewed}} is no longer shown.
* Click {{Decline}} on the new line, and the work order goes back to {{Review}}.
