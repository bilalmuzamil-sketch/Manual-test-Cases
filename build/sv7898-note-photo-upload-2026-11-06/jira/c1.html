Tested on the QA branch sv7898 (build v26.45.1-a5c66b3) on 6 Nov 2026, in Chrome's iPhone 12 Pro phone view, against the steps in this ticket and the test plan in the pull request (PR #4134). Production (build v26.45.1-753355c) was used for the "before".

How the bad connections were made

Comparison only: the dropped connection, offline, slow connection, stall and server errors were made in the test browser (Chrome's developer tools network controls and request blocking). They are not ShopView features. On QA branches a dropped connection also starts the branch's own "QA env is sleeping" redirect (the developer mentions this in the pull request); I switched that redirect off in the test browser so the screens could be read. It does not exist on production or staging, and you will see its extra "QA env is sleeping" message if you repeat this on the branch.

What I could not test

A real iPhone camera: I have no physical phone. On an iPhone, "Take Photo" is offered by iOS itself and hands the app an ordinary JPEG, so the test used a camera-size photo (4032 × 3024, 1.45 MB, named image.jpg as an iPhone capture is) in Chrome's iPhone 12 Pro view.
