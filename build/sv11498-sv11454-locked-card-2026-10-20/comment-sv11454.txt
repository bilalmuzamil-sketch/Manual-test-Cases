Tested on Staging (build v26.40.20-9c75418) on 20 October 2026, signed in as Admin, window 1440 x 900 px, zoom 100%.
* When a move is refused because the work order was invoiced in another tab, the card now updates: it reads *Invoiced* with a lock icon, matching the alert. Before, it kept reading *Approved* until the page was reloaded.
* The same works when the work order was paid instead, and in Tech View.

Spec "Work Orders — Board View & Tech View Display Options", version 68:

*How to see it yourself*
# Open Staging: https://app.staging.shopview.com and sign in as Admin.
# Tab A: click {{Work Orders}}, the {{All}} tab, then the right view button {{Board View}}. Find an Approved work order that has a lead technician. Leave this tab as it is.
# Tab B: open the same work order and take it to Invoiced (finish its line, then on the {{Finance}} tab click {{Create Invoice}}).
# Back in tab A, without reloading, drag that card into another technician's column.
# The alert appears and the card goes back to its column reading {{Invoiced}}, with a small lock icon after its number.
