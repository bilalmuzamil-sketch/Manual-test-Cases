*About the words of the note*
The note reads "The lead technician can't be changed once a work order is Invoiced or Paid." Since version 68 (19 October) the spec asks for shorter words, "Invoiced · can't be reassigned" or "Paid · can't be reassigned". That change is already raised as [SV-11545|https://shopview.atlassian.net/browse/SV-11545] (Open), so I have not counted it against this ticket. This ticket was about the note not showing at all with the keyboard, and it now shows.

*Where the expected result comes from*
Spec "Work Orders — Board View & Tech View Display Options", version 68:
{quote}S4-N6: On an Invoiced or Paid work order the Assign lead technician or Reassign lead technician action (S3-R14) and the Unassign action are disabled, and their tooltip reads "Invoiced · can't be reassigned" or "Paid · can't be reassigned", as in the design.{quote}
{quote}S11-N1: Keyboard access must not bypass action permissions or work order status restrictions.{quote}

*How to see it yourself*
# Open Staging: https://app.staging.shopview.com and sign in as Admin. Check the top bar shows {{Staging Heavy Duty - 9919}}.
# In the top menu click {{Work Orders}}, then the {{All}} tab (top left), type {{S2-37313}} in {{Search}} and click the right view button, {{Board View}}.
# Click once in the {{Search}} box. From now on use only the keyboard.
# Press *Tab* until the card {{S2-37313}} has the blue focus ring, then *Tab* once more to its {{...}} (More actions) button and press *Enter*.
# Press the *Down* arrow: as each greyed-out item ({{Reassign lead technician}}, {{Unassign}}) gets the focus, a dark note appears just below it.
