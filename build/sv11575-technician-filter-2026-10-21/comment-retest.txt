h3. How to see it

# Open staging: [https://app.staging.shopview.com/workorders] and sign in as Admin. At the top right, choose the location {{Staging Heavy Duty - 9919}}.
# Above the list, on the right, click the last of the three view icons ({{Board View}}).
# Above the board, click {{Technician}} (the wrench icon) and tick names from the top, one at a time. No message shows.
# At the 50th tick the red line "50 selected (max 50 across all filters)" appears at the bottom of the list and the other names go grey.
# Untick any name. The red line goes and the grey names can be ticked again.
# Click {{Clear selection}} at the bottom of the list when you're done.
