# Report Suite — DELIBERATE DECISIONS / ANTICIPATED-CHALLENGE REGISTER (Standing Rule 46)

**Why this exists.** So the QA lead is never blindsided in a public channel by a decision we made on
purpose — and, the sharper half, because **an undocumented deliberate omission is indistinguishable
from a miss**. On 2026-07-31 entry "N2" of a deltas document was written in exactly the register of a
considered decision and was an error; nothing let a reader tell the two apart.

**Honesty clause, applied.** This register records what I **decided**, not what I wish I had decided.
Where something was **missed** rather than chosen, it says so and is dated. Nothing here is a
mistake re-labelled as a choice.

**Risk column, read honestly:** HIGH does not mean we are wrong. It means **if this is raised
publicly, we have a concession to make, not just an explanation.**
