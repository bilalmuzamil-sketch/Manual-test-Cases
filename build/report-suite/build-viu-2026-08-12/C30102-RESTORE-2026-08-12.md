nine periods in the specified order, no All Time"* — on **Sales By Customer, one of the three reports

## What was put back, and why it is a restore rather than authoring

The sentence is **verbatim from [C30201](https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/cases/view/30201)**,
this case's exact structural twin — same three items, same family, same shared chooser. Its trailing
*"There is no 'All Time' option."* was left off **only because C30102 already asserts that as an item
of its own**; nothing else was changed and no scope was added.

The nine periods it names were **observed live yesterday on build `v3.6-8c28eed`** — the build running
now, confirmed by etag and sha256 at the start of this session — and **on two reports**, Work In
Progress and Sales By Customer, so it is the shared component rather than a per-report list:

`Last 12 Months` · `This Year` · `Last Year` · `This Quarter` · `Last Quarter` · `This Month` ·
`Last Month` · `This Week` · `Last Week`, and **no `All Time`**.

That is word for word Chris Ward's 8 August decision, which the case's own provenance line already
cites as the authority.
