# Report Suite — TITLE TRIM to <= 80 characters (2026-07-31)

> **One line: 288 titles were over 80 characters; 0 are now — 286 shortened by the trim pass and 2 by
> the de-duplication step. 6 further titles that were already inside the cap were re-worded to kill
> pre-existing duplicates (294 titles changed in all). Every single piece of dropped detail was
> checked and found ALREADY present in the case's Preconditions / Steps / Expected Results, so
> nothing a tester needs was lost.**

- **Standing instruction applied:** a TestRail case title must display in full on the case page (<= ~80 chars) and must still read sensibly on its own; the full detail belongs in Steps / Expected, never in a long title.
- **Before → after:** titles over 80 chars **288 → 0**. Longest title now **80**. Shortest trimmed title **36**. No title is under 30 characters (asserted).
- **Script:** `trim_titles.py` (local only). Per-case before/after: `title-trim-log.json`.
- **Live-build check:** not applicable (Rule 22) — no wording claim here depends on observing the build; the labels inside the titles are unchanged spec labels.

## 1. How each title was shortened

| Method | Cases | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Hand-written | 206 | The mechanical cut would have read badly (a dangling word, a broken quote, or the subject lost), so the title was re-written by hand to keep the subject + the key assertion. |
| Cut at a strong clause boundary | 77 | Cut only at a `;` / `:` / ` — ` / `. ` boundary — never at a weak comma or an opening bracket, because those leave a dangling fragment. |
| Compressed only | 3 | Meaning-preserving substitutions were enough (e.g. `, and ` → `; `). |
| De-duplicated | 8 | See section 3. |
| **Total titles changed** | **294** | 288 of these were the over-cap titles; 6 more were already inside the cap and changed only for uniqueness. |

## 2. No distinguishing detail was lost — proven, not asserted
