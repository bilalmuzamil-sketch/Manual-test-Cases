h3. What's happening

* Customer and asset notes: Tech View roles still see no {{For Customer}} checkbox, whatever their Customers permission.
* Part sale notes: a Tech View role with Part Sales permission cannot open a part sale, so it never reaches the notes.

h3. Steps to reproduce

*Customer and asset notes (rows 3 to 6)*
# Open [sv9667.qa.shopview.com|https://sv9667.qa.shopview.com] with the *Admin* quick-login.
# Go to {{Administration}} > {{Staff}}.
# Search for *tech@shopview.com*.
# Click the edit icon on *Tech ShopView*.
# In {{Edit Staff Member}}, set {{Role}} to *ZZ10323 TechView edit* (Tech View, with Customers → Edit and Part Sales → Edit).
# Click {{Save & Close}}.
# Sign out, then sign in with the *Tech* quick-login.
# Open {{Customers}} > *West Mifflin Diesel Repair* > {{Notes}}.
# Look at the file *zz10323_customer.png*.

*Actual Result*
No {{For Customer}} checkbox (picture 1). Signed in as Sales Representative, the same file shows the checkbox and it can be ticked (picture 2).

For view-only, use the *Technician* role in step 5 and open {{Customers}} > *Lamkin Diesel Services Inc* > {{Assets}} > *Unit 24 / A1305B* > {{Notes}}. The file *zz10323_vehicle.png* shows no checkbox (picture 3).

*Part sale notes (rows 7 and 8)*
# Sign in as Tech with the role *ZZ10323 TechView edit* (steps 1 to 7 above).
# Open {{Customers}} > *Bloomingdale Diesel Repair* > {{Part Sales}}.
# Click *P9667-368*.

*Actual Result*
The part sale does not open; the list stays on screen (picture 4). Clicking {{Parts}} in the top menu does nothing either. The same happens with the role *ZZ10323 TechView view-only* (Part Sales → View).
