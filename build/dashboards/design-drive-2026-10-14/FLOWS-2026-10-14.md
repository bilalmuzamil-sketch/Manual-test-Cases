# Dashboard design: multi-step flows, 14 Oct 2026 (Rule 124 gate, targeted flow drive)

**What kind of design this is.** Every board on the canvas is a screenshot of the SV-8311 build, captured 2026-09-28, except
row 15, which is drawn (Main board: "Every board is a screenshot, not a drawing, except row 15"). The flows therefore cannot be
clicked inside the images. Each flow is a sequence of boards. I drove each one by loading every board in its row in order on the
canvas (the canvas driver and the sweep's "Click to load" on every board). I then read the screens in order and checked the
labels against the transcription. The one-click sweep exercised every interactive element the canvas itself has (see
DESIGN-COVERAGE).

## Flows NOT driven, because the design does not draw them (named here, not dropped)

The design has no screen for any of these, so there was nothing to click. All are spec-only behaviour, and existing cases
cover them from the PRD:

1. The tile **after** Apply on a new preset. F3 shows the open menu only; S7-R3..R6 are covered by C88617, C88618.
2. The **Advisor** and **Technician** filter lists opened, with a selection made or every technician unticked. Covered by S8 cases C88619, C88620, C351717 and C88649. S8-R5a and S8-N1a are new in v44 and still need cases (triage B).
3. The detail-table pager's next page. The pager is drawn only as "1 - 10 of n" with arrows. Covered by C88627, C88628.
4. Collapsing a tile, and opening a second tile while one is open (S6-R9/R10). Each board shows one tile open; there is no switching sequence. Covered by C88614.
5. The report-link tooltip (S10-R4) and the report page as opened from a tile (S10-R3/R5).
6. The At Risk panel's own "Inactive for" select opened (D-3).
7. The phone "View Report" tap and the menu-button tap (S6-E2, S1-N8). Covered by C88616, C88597.
8. Loading and failed tile states (S6-R14/R15), and the product in dark theme (S12-R3/R5). Only the canvas viewer was captured in dark.

Drags: none. The design defines no drag and drop, so the drag gate does not apply.
