# Dashboard design: multi-step flows, 14 Oct 2026 evening (Rule 124 gate, targeted flow drive)

**What kind of design this is.** The Main board now says: "Every board is a screenshot, not a drawing." Row 15 was the
last drawn row, and it is now a build screenshot too. Boards 1 to 9, the Revenue hover, 14 and 15 were reshot on
14 Oct 2026 from SV-490 at 39fb049 (PRD v45). The other boards are the 28 Sep 2026 shots. Nothing inside a screenshot
can be clicked, so each flow is a **sequence of boards**. Each flow was driven on the canvas in order: every board was
played, its Export menu opened, and the canvas returned to (see the play/export record in `evidence/`). The screens
were then read in order and their labels checked against tonight's transcription. The one-click sweep exercised every
interactive element the canvas page itself has (see DESIGN-COVERAGE).

**Drags:** none. The design defines no drag and drop, so the drag gate does not apply.
