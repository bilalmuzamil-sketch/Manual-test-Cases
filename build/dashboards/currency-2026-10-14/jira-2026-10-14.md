## SV-490 — Epic — Ready for Development
**Summary:** Dashboard  
**Link:** https://shopview.atlassian.net/browse/SV-490  
**Parent:** — · **Created:** 2022-09-20T02:32 · **Updated:** 2026-10-13T11:26 · **Reporter:** Sasa Stojkovic · **Assignee:** Chris Ward · **Resolution:** — · **Priority:** Medium
**Links:** relates to SV-8417; relates to SV-10931; relates to SV-10932

**Description (current, verbatim):**

### The problem

The dashboard is the first screen a shop owner opens and it cannot be trusted. Its figures disagree with the reports they are supposed to mirror, because the same measure is calculated twice in two places. The previous attempt made this worse by letting every user assemble their own dashboard from a card catalog: nobody could say what any given person was looking at, and the numbers were wrong on top of it.

### What we're building

One fixed manager dashboard, the same for every user who can see it. Each widget reads the calculation that already belongs to its report, so a card and the report behind it cannot disagree. Clicking a widget opens that report rather than an expanded view, and the widget's chart is promoted onto the report page above the rows it summarises, where it follows the report's own date filter. Access is the existing reports permission, all or nothing, scoped by location; login lands on the dashboard. Per-user dashboards, starter templates, the card catalog and edit mode are all out of scope - they were the previous attempt.

**Product specification:** [Dashboard](https://shopview.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/~712020aa00b8d6a71f4259891982a304227c20/pages/788430850/Dashboard)
