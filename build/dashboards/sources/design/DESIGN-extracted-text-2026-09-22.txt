Nesting the dashboard — details tucked under their hero , one click away.
The same real dashboard, reorganised to kill scrolling. The four hero KPIs stay compact, and each one opens to reveal its own chart and detail table — on click. Sales by Customer and At Risk Customers read as heroes too — a headline count rather than a revenue figure (revenue already belongs to the Revenue hero) — sitting just below the four KPIs and opening the same way. Each hero's date filter is a blue filter pill, and its full report opens from the arrow-in-a-square icon right beside the hero name. Current is the baseline. Flip Light / Dark on either.
