Build **v26.39.1-3ef6ade** (the branch moved from `v26.39.0-07c719b` during this session).

**version 26**, **SV-9253 Story 7, The bulk action bar**.

## The source, verbatim — both cases quote it faithfully, so there is no case-vs-source divergence

> The bar sits at the top of the list and replaces the column headers while a selection is active. Nothing on the page shifts when it appears
> Selecting a row highlights the whole line, including its story, labour and parts
> Layout is n selected, then Deselect all, then the line actions, then the parts actions, then More, then a close control, with a divider between each group
> Dividers sit after n selected, after the line group and after the parts group. They are always in those places, so the bar reads the same whatever it is holding
> Deselect all clears the selection and leaves the bar in place. The close control clears the selection and dismisses the bar. Both exist because a user who over-selected wants to start again, not to lose the bar and the column headers with it
> A group holding nothing renders no divider and takes no space
> **The bar never covers the work order's own tabs. It belongs to the line list and appears inside it**

## Positive control, because three of these are negatives (Rule 104)

- **"Nothing is highlighted"** — the bar was present and read *"1 selected"* at the moment of
  measurement, so the tick had registered. The instrument worked; the product does nothing.
- **"No Deselect all"** — the same scan that found nothing named *Deselect* listed every other control
  in the bar by name and position, so it can see controls.
- **"Headers not replaced"** — measured as boxes and computed visibility, and the tabs' disappearance
  proves the bar does replace *something*.

| *"the bar is added above the column headings"* | **Right in effect, wrong in mechanism** — it is not appended, it replaces the tab strip. That matters, because the requirement names the tabs explicitly |

## A neighbouring check re-tested rather than assumed — and it survives

**C44572 is marked Passed** and asserts *"More contains, in order: Authorization required, Split to new
work order, Decline."* Two selections gave a **one-item** More, which looked like a false pass. Tested
under the selection the case actually describes — **every open line** — More holds
`Authorization required (3) · Split work order · Decline (4)`, in that order. **C44572 stands.**
The narrower selections hid items because nothing in them qualified, which is the bar's own rule.
(The product's word is *Split work order*, not the source's *Split to new work order* — the product's
word governs, Rule 110, and the field is not edited, Rule 114.)
