Story 14**The receive page and PO bulk receiveAs an accountant, I want the purchase-order pages grouped by vendor with the missing ones first, so that I can see what is blocking the shop before I open anything. | **Design**[Purchase Order Details](https://claude.ai/design/p/d2b4d45e-f8f2-4ae6-b69f-24637c6a0d7d?file=Purchase+Order+Details.dc.html)Both surfaces stay pages, because they are purchase-order-scoped and can span work orders, which the modal cannot. This is a light functional change and a redesign, not a rebuild.**Prerequisite** - **Vendor & Order Mgmt: Create & Edit** to receive, plus **See Financial Data** to edit cost and see sell - A role with Vendor & Order Mgmt: View only, such as Office, can open the page but not receive **Requirements** - Purchase orders group by vendor, with **Missing vendors first**, then named vendors alphabetically - Group headers carry the vendor name, a rollup such as *3 POs, 6 parts*, and Expand all. The page header carries the vendor count - Every group renders collapsed, including Missing vendors, so the page opens as a list of vendors rather than a wall of parts - Each purchase order row shows its number, the work order number and the part count - A vendor can be assigned two ways: hovering a collapsed row reveals **Assign vendor**, and expanding makes it the required first field. **Both link an existing vendor or create a new one**, on the same rules as the receive modal — a name that matches nothing offers Create, which opens the existing new-vendor modal with the name prefilled and name and tax rate required. See *Receiving from the work order* - Missing-vendor groups carry an amber treatment and a *Vendor missing* label in both states. Once resolved they show the vendor name. **The vendor stays changeable only while the purchase order has no received parts**; once anything on it is received the name is shown as text, with no edit affordance - **A panel is expanded per purchase order, not per vendor**, so every figure entered in it belongs to exactly one purchase order and one vendor bill - The expanded panel carries vendor invoice number, invoice date, delivery note, then the parts, with per-part tick boxes and Deselect all, and a subtotal, editable tax and total beneath - The primary action reads **Receive parts (n)** and is disabled while anything required is unfilled - The tax typed here is vendor-side. It is stored on the vendor bill with the invoice number, invoice date and due date, and feeds what the shop owes its suppliers. It is not the sales tax charged to the customer - It is typed, not calculated. Changing a cost or quantity afterwards does not recompute it - **Sell price is shown here and only here**, read-only, on every purchase order - Everything that makes a receive valid is identical to the receive modal: **vendor, invoice number and invoice date required**, **cost and tax required but able to be zero and always arriving prefilled**, quantity rules the same, part number required, cost locked once invoiced or paid - **Selecting purchase orders on the list page raises the same bulk action bar as the work order**, not a bar of its own design. Layout is *n selected*, then Deselect all, then **Receive selected** as the primary action, then More, then a close control, with the same dividers - **Assign vendor is not the primary action on that bar.** It belongs to a row, because a selection may span several vendors and a missing one. It lives in More and acts only on the rows in the selection that have no vendor - The bar carries no amber treatment. Amber marks a missing vendor on a row; on the bar it would say something the bar does not mean **Negative cases** - The same invoice number may be reused across several of a vendor's purchase orders. It is typed per purchase order and is not shared from the group header - Sell price is not shown in the receive modal or on the receive page reached from Parts - Without **See Financial Data** the cost, tax, subtotal, total and sell columns are absent, not masked. Receiving still works, because those fields arrive with a value and are never asked of that user - **Select all selects what is loaded, which is one page.** The list pages at thirty rows and loads more on scroll, so a user who selects all and then scrolls finds unselected rows below. Every paged list in the product behaves this way and it is not changed here. It is written down so it is raised as a question rather than as a defect, and it resolves when pagination is replaced, which is outside this release |
