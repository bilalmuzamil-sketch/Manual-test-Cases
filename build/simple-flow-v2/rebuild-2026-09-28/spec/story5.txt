Story 5**Parts no longer block completing a lineAs a technician, I want to close my line when the work is done, so that the parts clerk's paperwork is not my blocker. | **Design**[Shopview App](https://claude.ai/design/p/0c2ed95b-ce68-4b7f-a86d-0e3de1af5a28?file=Shopview+App.dc.html)This is the central change in the release. Everything else makes work faster; this one removes a blocker that has one department waiting on another.**Prerequisite** - **Work Orders: Create & Edit** to change the work order's status - **WO Lines: Create & Edit** plus **Full View** to approve lines. Tech View hides Approve - The line is Approved. **A line in Needs Approval cannot be completed by any path** — no transition from Needs Approval to Complete exists, so Approve comes first, on the line or in the bulk action bar **Requirements** - **A line completes whatever the state of its parts.** Unordered, unpicked and unreceived parts do not prevent it, in any combination - Each of the three parts settings behaves the same way, and each is a requirement in its own right rather than an edge case     - **Ordering required, part never ordered**: the line completes. The part stays waiting to be ordered and is ordered later, or is swept up when the parts are received     - **Picking required, inventory part not picked**: the line completes. Invoicing still requires the pick, so the part is picked before the work order closes     - **Receiving required, part ordered but not received**: the line completes. The work order cannot be invoiced until that part is received or deferred with *Received later*     - **Any of the three switched off**: the setting already resolved those parts, so nothing is outstanding and the line completes with nothing to chase - This holds at every entry point: the line itself, the bulk action bar, the completion wizard and clock out - Every parts requirement stays at work order level, so review and invoicing still require parts to be resolved. The requirement is not removed, it is moved to where the money is - The other line-level requirements stay: tech story, mileage, engine hours, each only when its setting is on, and core resolution     - **Where receiving is not required, an unresolved core is asked before the line completes**, because such a part may never pass through a receive and the invoice still has to be right. This is V1 behaviour and is unchanged - Completing a line changes nothing about its parts. They keep whatever status the settings gave them - Complete stays on the work order line and does not move onto labour lines - A line reaches Complete only through these paths, and nothing else completes a line     - Complete, on the line     - Complete lines, in the bulk action bar, for the selection — *Bulk complete lines*     - Complete all lines, in the bulk action bar, when the selection covers every open line — *Bulk complete lines*     - Create invoice, which closes the work order's Approved lines as a stated consequence of invoicing and is refused while any line is still in Needs Approval — *Create invoice as the finish action*     - Clock out and complete, in the clock out modal - **Mark as reviewed is not on that list.** It only becomes available once every line is already complete, so there is never an open line for it to close - Satisfying the last outstanding requirement makes a line completable. It does not complete it - At clock out we will open current prod modal , when technician needs to toggle the Line completed .  - **The *****line completed***** tick box is hidden.** Until now that tick box was how a line was completed from this modal, and the two new buttons take that job over. Hiding it leaves one way to complete from clock out instead of two, so a user cannot tick the box and press the wrong button - The clock out modal is otherwise unchanged - If a tech story is required and empty, it is required at clock out. Entering it needs **WO Lines: Create & Edit** - **Nothing completes a line that is not approved, and nothing approves a line on the way to completing it.** Complete lines counts only Approved lines, and Create invoice is refused while any line is still in Needs Approval — see *Bulk complete lines* and *Create invoice as the finish action* **Negative cases** - Complete is never disabled for a parts reason, anywhere - No timer and no background job completes a line - A line reopened after completion returns to Approved with its parts unchanged - A Technician in Tech View cannot complete, because they cannot approve. They can still pick parts |
