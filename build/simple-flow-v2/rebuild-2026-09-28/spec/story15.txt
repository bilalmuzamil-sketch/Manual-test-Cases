Story 15**Receive laterAs a parts clerk, I want to record that parts arrived even though the vendor bill has not, so that missing paperwork does not hold the whole work order. | **Design**[Shopview App](https://claude.ai/design/p/0c2ed95b-ce68-4b7f-a86d-0e3de1af5a28?file=Shopview+App.dc.html)**This story exists because the vendor invoice number is required to receive.** The parts are on the shelf, the job is done, and the only thing missing is a piece of paper from the supplier. Without a way out, that piece of paper holds the whole work order.**Prerequisite** - **Received later**, the one new permission in this release. See *Permissions on the new surfaces* - **Require receiving parts before completion is on.** With it off there is nothing to defer and no split button appears **Requirements** - Receive becomes a split button: Receive, a divider, and a caret offering **Received later** - It is available on the part row, in the bulk action bar and on the wizard's receive step - **It is chosen per part, never for a whole card at once.** Inside the receive modal and inside the wizard's receive step, each part carries its own choice, so a delivery where three parts arrived with a bill and one without is recorded truthfully in a single pass - Choosing it sets the part to **Received later**, removes its Receive affordance from the row and confirms with a toast - A part in that state satisfies the completion requirement, so the work order is no longer blocked by it - It stays receivable later from its … menu, and receiving it then asks for the invoice number as normal - Deferring several parts at once applies straight away, with a toast stating how many were deferred. The permission is the gate, and the action is undone by simply receiving the part - A deferred part keeps counting in the Waiting on Parts column, because a vendor bill is still to come - **A part that carries a core charge can be deferred, and its core follows the parent.** Choosing Received later on the parent puts the core sibling in the same state, so the pair stays consistent and neither blocks completion on its own - **The core is never offered a Received later choice of its own**, because it has no separate vendor bill — it belongs to the parent's. It carries no caret and no row action while its parent is deferred - Receiving the parent later resolves the core with it, in the same pass and under the same invoice number - **An unresolved core is still asked for before the line completes**, exactly as it is when receiving is not required. Deferring a receive defers the paperwork, not the core decision — see *Parts no longer block completing a line* **Negative cases** - Without the permission, Receive is a plain button with no caret anywhere, and a missing vendor bill has no workaround - Received later is never duplicated in the part's … menu while the split button is showing - Deferring does not create a vendor bill, move stock or touch the accounting system. It records that the paperwork is outstanding and nothing else - A core sibling never appears twice — once inside the parent's deferral and again as a row of its own - Deferring a part with a core moves no stock and creates no core credit. Nothing about the core's value is decided until the parent is received - A part whose core is already resolved defers like any other part |
