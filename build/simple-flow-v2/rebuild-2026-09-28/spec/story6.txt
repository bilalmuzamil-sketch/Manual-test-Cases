Story 6**Which actions appear on a line and on a partAs a user, I want to be offered only the actions that will actually do something, so that I stop pressing buttons that change nothing. | **Design**[Shopview App](https://claude.ai/design/p/0c2ed95b-ce68-4b7f-a86d-0e3de1af5a28?file=Shopview+App.dc.html)**Prerequisite** - **WO Lines: Create & Edit** plus **Full View** **Requirements** - Line actions by status. Where an action is not listed it is not visible, on the line or in the bulk action bar     - **Needs Approval**: Approve, Decline, Request part, Delete line     - **Approved**: Decline, Authorization required, Complete, Request part, Delete line     - **Declined**: Approve, Authorization required, Request part, Delete line     - **Complete**: Decline, Authorization required, Uncomplete - **A declined line can still be approved from its own menu.** That is deliberate and stays: somebody who declined a line by mistake, or a customer who changed their mind, needs a way back without deleting and rebuilding the line. What changes is that **the bulk actions leave declined lines alone**, which is set out under *Bulk approve and decline* - Complete is not offered on a Needs Approval line and Approve is not offered on a line that is already Approved. Both are offered today and neither does anything - Decline stays visible but disabled while the line holds parts that were received or picked, with the reason *Return this line's received parts before declining it* - Part actions by state. The product has seven part states and all of them appear here, so nothing is left without a rule     - **Requested** — where a part lands when it is typed in without pricing. It counts as unfinished work. **Nothing on the row**, because the part is not ready to order until somebody gives it a price and an Order button there would offer a press that solves nothing. **It is still counted in the bulk bar's Order (n)**, so the work stays visible and whoever wants to push it through can     - **Quoted**: Order     - **Auth to order**: Order     - **In Stock and not picked**: Pick     - **Awaiting**: Receive, with *Received later* behind its caret when receiving is required     - **Received later**: nothing on the row, Receive in the … menu. This is the state a part lands in when somebody defers a receive because the vendor bill has not arrived, and it is defined in full under *Receive later*     - **Received** or **picked**: nothing. The part is finished     - **Returned**: nothing. The part has been sent back and stays visible on the line and in returns - **Requested is the one place where a count includes something with no button beside it.** That asymmetry is deliberate, not an oversight, and it is the reason it is written down here: the row stays clean and the work stays countable. It should not be "fixed" by adding an Order button to the row - Ordering a Requested part only does something when the part is **vendor-sourced**, which includes a vendorless part marked *Vendor missing*. An inventory or found part, or one with no source at all, needs its details completed first, and that flow belongs to SV-9315 - **Quoted** is where a part sits before anything has been ordered, and a part arrives there by exactly three routes     - It was just created on the line with pricing, whether typed in by hand or picked from the catalogue     - Its line was **declined**     - Its line was sent back with **Authorization required** - A part leaves Quoted only by being ordered, and approving a declined line again restores its parts to where they were - Ordering comes before receiving. With ordering required, Receive does not appear until the part has been ordered, and the two are never offered at once for the same part - With receiving not required, Receive moves from the part row into the part's … menu. It is never removed outright - Pick follows the part, not the setting. Auto-picking can fail through insufficient stock or a missing bin, and those parts must stay pickable **Negative cases** - **Declining a line, or sending it back, returns only the parts that have not arrived yet.** A **Requested** part is left where it is, and so are **Received** and **Returned** parts. Only In Stock, Quoted, Auth to order and Awaiting parts are moved back to Quoted - Parts that were received or picked are not touched by a line status change, and no stock moves because of one - Declining a line does not remove its parts from a purchase order. Only deleting a part does that - A part action the user's role does not carry is absent even when the part's state qualifies. Pick follows **Pick Parts**, Order follows **Order Parts** |
