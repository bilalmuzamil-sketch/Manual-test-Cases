Story 8**Bulk approve and declineAs an advisor, I want to approve or decline a whole estimate at once, so that a customer's answer takes one press rather than ten. | **Design**[Shopview App](https://claude.ai/design/p/0c2ed95b-ce68-4b7f-a86d-0e3de1af5a28?file=Shopview+App.dc.html)**Prerequisite** - **WO Lines: Create & Edit** plus **Full View**, the same gate as approving one line - Lines are selected in the bulk action bar **Requirements** - Each selected line is judged on its own, by the rule that already applies to one line - Every line that qualifies goes through. One line failing does not stop the rest - **A declined line is never swept up by a bulk action.** Declining is a decision somebody made deliberately, line by line, so a bulk press must not quietly undo it. A declined line is therefore left alone by Approve and by Authorization required, and it is not in either count - Approve counts lines in **Needs Approval** - Decline counts lines in **Needs Approval and Approved** - Authorization required counts lines in **Approved** - Completed lines are excluded from all three counts - **Approving a declined line stays possible from the line itself**, where the person doing it is looking at that one line and means it. Only the bulk path leaves declined lines out, so the two are not in conflict - The result names each failure with its line number and reason, in one message, for example *3 lines approved. 1 line couldn't be declined: Line 3, parts must be returned first* - Undo restores only the lines that changed, to the status they held **Negative cases** - A selection of four lines where one holds received parts declines three and reports the fourth. It does not refuse all four - Lines already in the target status are skipped silently and were never in the count - A selection made up entirely of declined lines shows no Approve button at all, because the count would be zero |
