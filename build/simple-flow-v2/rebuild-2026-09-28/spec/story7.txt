Story 7**The bulk action barAs an advisor, I want to act on several lines at once, so that finishing a work order does not mean repeating the same press down the page. | **Design**[Shopview App](https://claude.ai/design/p/0c2ed95b-ce68-4b7f-a86d-0e3de1af5a28?file=Shopview+App.dc.html)**Prerequisite** - **WO Lines: Create & Edit** plus **Full View** for the bar to appear at all - One or more lines are selected **Requirements** - The bar sits at the top of the list and replaces the column headers while a selection is active. Nothing on the page shifts when it appears - Selecting a row highlights the whole line, including its story, labour and parts - Layout is *n selected*, then **Deselect all**, then the line actions, then the parts actions, then More, then a close control, with a divider between each group - Each action is shown by one rule: nothing in the selection qualifies, hidden. Something qualifies but is blocked, shown disabled with the reason. It qualifies and is not blocked, shown with its count - **Line actions and parts actions sit in separate groups and never compete for the same slot.** The line group takes up to three — the finish action when the selection covers every open line, then Complete lines, then Approve. The parts group takes up to three — Order, Receive, Pick, in that order. **A parts action is never pushed into More by a line action.** The two groups are sized independently, because a parts clerk and an advisor read different halves of the same bar - Dividers sit after *n selected*, after the line group and after the parts group. They are always in those places, so the bar reads the same whatever it is holding - Line actions count lines and parts actions count parts. *Receive 5 parts* may come from two lines and still says parts - A count never includes something the action would skip - **A count may include a part that has no button of its own.** *Requested* is the one case: the row offers nothing because the part has no price yet, but the part is still counted in Order (n) so the work does not go invisible. See *Which actions appear on a line and on a part* - Decline is always in More, never in a primary slot - More contains, in order: Authorization required, Split to new work order, Decline - **Create invoice** acts on the whole work order whatever is selected, so it carries no count, and it disappears once an invoice exists - Approve, decline, authorization required, complete and uncomplete apply immediately and offer an undo in the result toast - Ordering parts and creating an invoice ask for confirmation first and cannot be undone - Receiving and picking open their own screens, so they neither confirm nor offer undo - One toast per bulk action, summarising the result - **Deselect all clears the selection and leaves the bar in place. The close control clears the selection and dismisses the bar.** Both exist because a user who over-selected wants to start again, not to lose the bar and the column headers with it **Negative cases** - A user without **WO Lines: Create & Edit** sees no checkboxes and no bar - A count of zero means the button is absent, not greyed out - If More would be empty, the More button is not rendered - A selection where nothing applies shows *n selected* and *No actions available for this selection* - **Delete lines is not in the bar in this release.** Bulk deletion is a follow-up, so the bar carries no delete action and More does not list one - **Mark as reviewed is not in the bulk bar at all.** It acts on the whole work order whatever is ticked, so it does not belong to a selection of lines. It lives in the header and the … menu - A group holding nothing renders no divider and takes no space - Clearing the selection through Deselect all returns the column headers, exactly as closing the bar does - The bar never covers the work order's own tabs. It belongs to the line list and appears inside it |
