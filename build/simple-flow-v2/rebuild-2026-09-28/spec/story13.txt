Story 13**Receiving from the work orderAs a parts clerk, I want to receive parts without leaving the work order, so that I do not lose my place every time a delivery arrives. | **Design**[Shopview App](https://claude.ai/design/p/0c2ed95b-ce68-4b7f-a86d-0e3de1af5a28?file=Shopview+App.dc.html)Receive on a work order currently navigates to another page. It opens a modal instead, and the user stays where they are.**Prerequisite** - **Order Parts** (`woOrderParts`) on the front end. The back end accepts any of Delivery: Create & Edit, Work Order Part: Create, or Work Orders: Create & Edit, exactly as today - Assigning a vendor from inside the modal additionally needs **Vendor & Order Mgmt: Create & Edit** - At least one part on the work order is awaiting receipt **Requirements** - Receive from a part row, a line menu, the bulk action bar or the completion wizard opens a modal. There is no navigation, and it is the same modal in all four cases - What the modal contains depends on where it was opened from     - A part row where the part has a vendor: every part on this work order from that same vendor still awaiting receipt, all ticked     - A part row where the part has no vendor: every part on this work order with no vendor, none ticked     - A line menu: that line's outstanding parts     - The bulk action bar or the completion wizard: every outstanding part on the work order - **The settings never change what the modal contains.** *Require receiving parts before completion* decides where the Receive control lives and whether outstanding parts block invoicing, and nothing more. Once the modal is open it holds the same parts either way - Same-vendor parts are pre-ticked because they normally arrive together in one delivery. Vendorless parts are not, because they may each be from a different supplier - One card per vendor, showing vendor name, part count and total. **Vendor missing sorts first** - An expanded card shows vendor invoice number, invoice date and delivery note on one row, then the parts. The invoice date is a visible field defaulting to today, because it is changed often - **Vendor, vendor invoice number and invoice date are all required to receive.** This is how the app already works and it does not change. Without them the receive is refused with *Invoice number is missing* or *Vendor ID is missing*, so the modal marks all three as required and disables its own receive button until they are filled, rather than letting the user submit into an error - The delivery note is optional - Each card header carries select all and deselect all - Editable per part: part number, quantity received and cost. Editing the part number into a catalogue part needs **Catalog & Inventory: Create & Edit** - **Cost is required and may be zero.** It is never left empty, because the field arrives prefilled with the cost already on the purchase order. It is editable here because the vendor bill is the moment the real figure is known - **Tax is required and may be zero.** It arrives prefilled from the vendor's own tax rate, and falls back to zero only when there is nothing to derive it from, such as a part typed in by hand with no vendor - Quantity received must be greater than zero, may include decimals, and may exceed what was ordered. The excess is shown but not blocked and the purchase order absorbs it - Changing cost shows a note that the sell price will update - Where no vendor is set, **Assign vendor is the first field in the form**, marked required, as a search field where the user types and results appear below. Everything below it is disabled until a vendor is chosen - Assigning a vendor applies to the ticked parts only. The rest stay in the vendor-missing card - **A vendor that does not exist yet can be created from that field.** Typing a name that matches nothing offers **Create** with the typed name, below the results. Choosing it opens the existing new-vendor modal with the name prefilled, and the user finishes it there. This reuses the vendor form the product already has rather than inventing an inline one, because a vendor is more than a name - **Name and tax rate are both required**, exactly as they are on the vendor page today. Everything else on that form stays optional. The tax rate matters here because it is what prefills tax on every receive for that vendor, and because a vendor without one does not reach the accounting system - On save the modal closes, the new vendor is selected in the field, and the form continues where it left off. Nothing already entered is lost - Creating a vendor here needs the same vendor-management permission as creating one from the vendor page. A user without it sees results only and no Create option - **A vendor can be corrected while the parts are unreceived.** Where the purchase order was created without one, the assigned vendor can be replaced until the part is received. **Once a part is received its vendor is fixed** — it belongs to that delivery and that vendor's bill, and changing it would rewrite a record the supplier and the accounting system already agree on - The cursor starts in the vendor invoice number field when the vendor is known, and in the vendor field when it is not. Tab order is identical in both states, and new fields are added after the existing path rather than inserted into it - A vendor bill always belongs to one purchase order. Where one vendor's parts on this work order sit on **two** purchase orders, the card still shows one invoice number field, and receiving produces **two vendor bills carrying that same invoice number**. The card says so before the user confirms - Receiving uses the existing pipeline, so the vendor bill reaches the accounting system as it does today **Negative cases** - **A user who cannot see money can still receive.** Cost and tax are removed from their screen and the prefilled values stand. No money field is ever both hidden and required, which is what keeps **See Financial Data** and the receive permission from deadlocking each other - A part with no part number is outlined red and its card cannot be received until it is filled - Quantity of zero or a negative number is rejected - Where every part in a card is unticked, that card's receive is disabled - Parts not yet ordered, and parts on lines that are not approved, are never included - One invoice number never spans two vendors - **There is no way to receive without a vendor bill.** A user who has the parts but not the paperwork uses **Received later**, which is why that feature exists - If the work order is invoiced mid-session, receiving is refused with the reason - Empty state reads *Nothing to receive* - A vendor created here is a full vendor record, not a placeholder. It appears on the vendor page like any other - Cancelling the new-vendor modal returns to the receive form with the typed name still in the field and no vendor selected - **A purchase order that already carries a named vendor offers no change of vendor.** Where parts genuinely belong to another supplier they are removed from the purchase order and ordered again - The guard is on the back end as well as in the interface. A receive call must not accept a vendor that differs from the one already recorded against a received part |
