# Business Case

Finishing a work order still takes more presses than the job deserves, and the app asks for them in the wrong order. A **Complete Work Order** button sits in the top right from the moment a work order is created, before any work exists, so the most prominent action on the screen is one nobody wants yet. A technician who has finished the job cannot close their line because the parts clerk has not entered the vendor bill, which turns one department's paperwork into another department's blocker. And the steps a user must take are not always visible: in review, the least ShopView-trained person in the room missed the _Assign vendor_ control entirely and went straight for the invoice number.

Simple Flow V1 made the ceremony optional. V2 changes where the ceremony sits: the header offers only the next real action, parts stop blocking the person who did the work, and everything that used to be done one row at a time can be done to a selection.

_Stories are cross-referenced by title rather than by number, so a reference stays correct when the numbering shifts._

# Feature Overview

**Work order**

* The header shows the next real action only. Completing, reviewing and invoicing stay in the … menu until every line is complete, then the right one is promoted
* The finish action reads **Create invoice**. _Complete Work Order_ is retired
* Parts never block completing a line. The requirement moves to the work order, where review and invoicing still need parts resolved
* A **bulk action bar** covering approve, decline, send back for approval, complete, order, pick and receive
* Every action offered is one that will do something, with a count that excludes what it would skip
* Parts can be dragged to reorder within their line, and that order is what the invoice prints

**Receiving**

* Receive on a work order opens a **modal** instead of navigating to another page
* The two purchase-order pages stay and are redesigned
* One rule set across all three surfaces: **vendor, vendor invoice number and invoice date are required**, **cost and tax are required but may be zero**, and one vendor bill belongs to one purchase order
* **Receive later** is the way out when the parts are in the shop but the vendor bill is not, which is exactly the case the required invoice number would otherwise stall

**Settings**

* **One setting renamed and one added**, both reading _Require…_ so that on always means more work. Every default reproduces today's behaviour
* A settings change now applies to **every open work order**, not only to new ones, and asks for confirmation first

**Out of scope:**

* **Bulk deletion of lines.** It is written up under _Bulk delete lines_ so the thinking is not lost, but it is a follow-up and not part of this release
* Making the vendor invoice number optional. It is required today and stays required. **Receive later** is the answer to a missing bill, not a relaxed field
* Moving Complete onto individual labour lines (rejected 5 Aug; parts no longer blocking is the substitute fix)
* A ready tick instead of a blank status cell (deferred to the wider work order redesign)
* A core-charges setting, and any change to how cores are resolved. The wizard's **Resolve cores** step stays exactly as it is
* New permission atoms, apart from **Received later**. Everything else reuses what SV-8183 already mapped
* Lines completing by themselves (ruled out 11 Aug)
* Part status derived from sell price (ruled out 11 Aug)
* Bulk selection on the work order list (everything here is work-order-level)
* Notifications
