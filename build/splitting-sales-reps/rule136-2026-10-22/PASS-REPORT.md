## 3. How each rule was applied
**Split (Rule 136 §1-§4).** A case was split only where every part checks a different requirement (its own quotes), can start from
its own preconditions, and has no result that depends on another part's steps. The original key keeps its C-id as the first part,
always the part its old title named (title changed only where it no longer fitted the part); the other parts are new keys `<key>b`,
`<key>c` in the same section (the build, the setup renderer and `apply4.py` accept the format: a key with no C-id is added with
`add_case` to its section). Each part is complete: own title (≤ 80, no semicolons, "Design: " on the design parts whose title was
written or rewritten), preconditions with {placeholders} and "For example" lines, steps, one "Step N:" result per step, only the quotes
and From lines that back its results, design references and pictures of its own boards, notes that concern it, marker, refs (from its
stories) and its own setup recipe. Where a part changes data the other part needs, it has its own records (every case of this suite
owns its records); where the parts only read (S8-06, S8-09, REL-19, D-15), they share the same kind of seeded records.

**Never split (§2)**, and kept whole: save-then-reload cases (S1-03, S1-04, S2-01, S2-04, S2-07 part a), before/after with a captured
value (S5-09, S7-03), two tabs or windows on one record (S1-09, S2-09, S2-14, S4-23), a change and the log entry it writes (S2-11, S4-09,
each S7-01 part, D-09), ordered sequences on one filter or one flow (S2-10, S4-07, S8-01, S8-03, S8-07, S8-08, REL-08, REL-11, REL-17).
Also kept whole when the parts would be one requirement sentence checked twice (S1-05 Escape or click outside, S1-11 and S7-05 gone or
other-organisation staff, S4-08, S4-11, S5-05, S6-03, REL-01) and when a hover is only the lead-in to clicking the same control (S2-01,
S2-05, S4-03).

**Shorter but runnable (§5).** (a) 56 steps that only read what the step before produced were folded into that step's "Step N:"
result; a reading step that needs its own action stayed (open the downloaded file in S4-21, open the export in REL-03, hover, expand).
(b) State a case does not test moved into the preconditions: the report already open for today with Show Unassigned and the Sales Type
column as the case needs; an invoice already made where the invoice step's only result was "the invoice is created" (invoicing stays a
step where its own total is checked: S5-03, S5-04, S5-16, S5-20, and where the order matters: S2-10, S5-09, REL-02, REL-08, REL-11);
a switch already turned off, an account already deactivated or a name already changed where that change is the setup and its effect
is what is checked (S1-07, S1-08, S4-26, S5-14, S6-01, S6-02, S4-20b); Edit Customer already open. (c) Rule 135 still holds: one action
per step, and no step a tester needs was removed.

**Runnability fixed on the way (Rule 134).** Sales By Representative shows a representative's invoice rows only once that row is
expanded (SBR spec v28 Story 6). The old report cases read invoice rows without expanding; every case that reads an invoice row now
