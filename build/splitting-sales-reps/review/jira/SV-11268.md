## Summary

Let the shop manager set a customer's default representatives (one, or split into Service Labor, Service Parts and Part Sales) once, so the customer's new work orders and part sales start with the right people and are credited correctly when nobody is on them.

## Context

The user needs the 'Customers — Create & Edit' permission, or 'Work Orders — Create & Edit' when the New Customer window is opened from the work order window, as today. The user is in the Edit Customer or the New Customer window.

Context note (S4-R27, S4-N2): a person whose Sales Representative switch is off but whose account is active can stay in a customer field (S4-N2). A new work order does not copy them, so its card reads "Unassigned", but when it is invoiced with nobody on it, S4-R17 still credits them from the customer. QA should expect both.

Context note (S4-R24 to S4-R29): before this change a new work order started with nobody in Sales Representative, and the customer's representative was used only when the work order was invoiced with nobody on it. Every shop sees this change: new work orders and part sales now open with the customer's representatives on the card. S4-R17 and S4-R18 stay as the safety net for a field left "Unassigned".

## Requirements

* S4-R1: The Edit Customer window and the New Customer window both show a Sales Representative field, in the place Edit Customer shows it today, with the outlined box every other field in the window has.
* S4-R2: The field is optional. A customer can be saved with nobody in it.
* S4-R3: Its dropdown starts with "Unassigned", followed by every staff member whose Sales Representative switch is on and whose account is active, sorted by first name, then last name, A to Z, as on the work order (S1-R8). Every one of them is listed, however many there are. It has no search box (S1-R12).
* S4-R4: No field in the window has an x inside it; picking "Unassigned" clears it (S1-R10). Each field's dropdown arrow is vertically centered (S1-R11). In the window the + and the remove x are always shown; they do not wait for hover.
* S4-R5: The + button of S2-R1 to S2-R3 sits to the right of the first field, with the tooltip "Add representative".
* S4-R6: Its menu lists, in this order, each field not yet added: "Sales Representative - Service Parts" and "Sales Representative - Part Sales". When both are added the + is not shown.
* S4-R7: Adding Service Parts renames the first field "Sales Representative - Service Labor" and shows "Sales Representative - Service Parts" under it. Both start with the person who was in the first field, or "Unassigned".
* S4-R8: Adding Part Sales shows "Sales Representative - Part Sales" last. It starts with the person new part sales get at that moment (S4-R26), or "Unassigned".
* S4-R9: The fields always show in this order: the first field ("Sales Representative", or "Sales Representative - Service Labor" once Service Parts is added), then Service Parts, then Part Sales.
* S4-R10: Service Parts and Part Sales each have the remove x of S2-R8 to their right, with the tooltip "Remove Sales Representative - Service Parts" or "Remove Sales Representative - Part Sales". The first field has the + instead, while there is something left to add, and otherwise no button.
* S4-R11: Removing Service Parts renames the first field "Sales Representative" again. It keeps the Service Labor person, who becomes the customer's one representative for labor and parts. A Part Sales field, if added, keeps its choice.
* S4-R12: Removing Part Sales makes new part sales follow again (S4-R26).
* S4-R13: While the Part Sales field is not added, a change to Service Parts, or to the one representative, changes what new part sales get at once.
* S4-R14: Picking a person, or "Unassigned", in the Part Sales field keeps that choice until someone changes the field or removes it. Changing Service Parts never changes it, even when both hold the same person.
* S4-R15: Neither dropdown opens on its own after a field is added.
* S4-R16: Nothing in the window is saved, including adding or removing a field, until the user saves the customer.
* S4-R17: A work order invoiced with nobody on either side is credited with the customer's Service Parts person on the parts side and the Service Labor person on the labor side. A customer that is not split gives its one person both sides.
* S4-R18: A part sale invoiced with no representative is credited, for the whole sale, to the person new part sales get from the customer (S4-R26), or to nobody when the Part Sales field reads "Unassigned".
* S4-R24: A new work order for a customer that is not split starts with the customer's representative in its one Sales Representative field. The work order is not split.
* S4-R25: A new work order for a split customer starts with the Labor and Parts fields: "Sales Representative - Labor" holds the customer's Service Labor person and "Sales Representative - Parts" holds the customer's Service Parts person. Removing its Parts field works as S2-R16.
* S4-R26: A new part sale starts with the customer's Part Sales field when it is added: the person picked, or "Unassigned" when "Unassigned" was picked. Without a Part Sales field it starts with the Service Parts person, or the one representative when the customer is not split.
* S4-R31: The Counter Sale customer works like any other customer here. Each location's Counter Sale starts with no representative, so walk-in part sales start "Unassigned" unless the shop sets one on Counter Sale.
* S4-R32: Picking a person, or "Unassigned", on a work order or part sale changes only that work order or part sale, never its customer.
* S4-R27: Only a staff member whose account is active and whose Sales Representative switch is on is copied (S1-R8). Anyone else leaves that field "Unassigned", and S4-R17 and S4-R18 still apply at invoicing.
* S4-R28: The customer's representatives are copied only when the work order or part sale is created. Changing the customer on an existing work order or part sale never changes its representatives.
* S4-R30: Changing a customer's representatives never changes its existing work orders or part sales. Only work orders and part sales created afterwards start with the new choice.
* S4-R29: A work order or part sale made from another one, by Split Work Order, Split Part Sale or reopening a paid work order, starts with the representatives of the one it came from: one field, or the Labor and Parts fields with the same people. It does not take the customer's.
* S4-R19: The customer page shows the representatives with the window's labels, in the window's order: "Sales Representative" or "Sales Representative - Service Labor", then "Sales Representative - Service Parts" when added, then "Sales Representative - Part Sales" when added. An empty one reads "Unassigned".
* S4-R20: Deactivating a staff member's account removes them from every customer field they hold. The customer's other fields keep their people. A Part Sales field where they were picked is removed, so new part sales follow again (S4-R26).
* S4-R21: The warning shown before deactivating a sales representative counts every customer where they are in the first field, Service Parts or Part Sales, and counts each customer once.
* S4-R22: Renaming a staff member updates their name in every customer field they hold.
* S4-R23: The Sales Representative Assignments export lists one row per customer per person, with a column, Side, after "Representative is active?". The one representative of a customer that is not split reads "Parts and Service". A split customer lists "Service Labor" and "Service Parts". "Part Sales" is listed for the person in an added Part Sales field, split or not. A person in more than one field of one customer gets one row listing them in window order, separated by ", ", for example "Parts and Service, Part Sales".
* S4-N1: A work order with a representative on either side does not use the customer's representatives, even for the empty side (S2-N1).
* S4-N2: Turning off a staff member's Sales Representative switch, without deactivating their account, leaves them in the customer fields they hold, as today.
* S4-N3: Re-importing an existing customer through the contacts import never changes its representatives. Every field, including Service Parts and Part Sales, stays as it was. Before this change a re-import cleared the customer's representative.
* S4-E1: On release day every existing customer has the one "Sales Representative" field, with no Service Parts or Part Sales field, and keeps its one representative, so the customer page and every invoice read exactly as before, except that an empty field reads "Unassigned" instead of "N/A".
* S4-E2: A Part Sales field that holds the same person as Service Parts stays added. It does not change when Service Parts changes (S4-R14).
