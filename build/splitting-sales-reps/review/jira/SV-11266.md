# SV-11266 — Splitting Sales Representatives - Story 2 - Give a work order a separate Labor and Parts representative

## Summary

Let the service writer credit a work order's labor to one representative and its parts to another, through a + button that splits the one field into Labor and Parts fields, so each person is credited with what they sold.

## Context

Same as Story 1: the user needs the 'Work Orders — Create & Edit' permission, and the fields can be changed only while the work order is not invoiced or paid, and not when the work order is opened from its history. Builds on the field from Story 1.

_Context note (S2-N2): the part sale's Sales Representative field belongs to Part Sales Update v1 (SV-10266), which is not on staging yet. It is in this PRD only so the two features line up. Visually nothing changes there: the part sale card keeps the field exactly as Part Sales Update v1 builds it, with no + button, no remove x and no change to how it looks._

## Requirements

* S2-R1: A + button sits to the right of the Sales Representative field: the design system's Tertiary button in its small icon-only size, 32px square with 8px corners, showing the Lucide plus icon at 20px.
* S2-R2: At rest the icon is grey on no fill. Hovering the button shows a light fill. The light fill stays while its menu is open.
* S2-R3: Hovering the + shows the tooltip "Add representative". The tooltip does not show while the menu is open.
* S2-R4: Clicking the + opens a menu below it with one option, "Sales Representative - Parts". Clicking outside the menu or pressing Escape closes it without a change.
* S2-R5: Picking "Sales Representative - Parts" closes the menu and replaces the one field with two fields, in this order: "Sales Representative - Labor" and "Sales Representative - Parts".
* S2-R6: Both fields start with the person who was in the one field, or "Unassigned" if it was empty. Adding the Parts field on its own changes nobody's credit.
* S2-R7: Neither dropdown opens on its own after the Parts field is added.
* S2-R8: A remove x sits to the right of "Sales Representative - Parts", in the column the + uses: the same Tertiary button, 32px square, showing the Lucide x icon at 20px, grey at rest with a light fill on hover.
* S2-R9: Hovering the remove x shows the tooltip "Remove Sales Representative - Parts".
* S2-R10: While the two fields show, the + is not shown, because there is nothing left to add. "Sales Representative - Labor" has no button beside it.
* S2-R11: Each of the two fields works as S1-R2 to S1-R15.
* S2-R12: Picking in one field never changes the other.
* S2-R13: "Sales Representative - Labor" is credited with the labor side of the invoice and "Sales Representative - Parts" with the parts side (Story 5).
* S2-R14: Both labels show in full on the card, never cut off.
* S2-R15: The work order keeps both fields when the page is reloaded or reopened, including when both hold the same person.
* S2-R16: Clicking the remove x replaces the two fields with the one "Sales Representative" field. It holds the person who was in "Sales Representative - Labor", or "Unassigned", and that person is credited with the whole work order, labor and parts.
* S2-R17: Once the work order is invoiced or paid, or when it is opened from its history, each field is plain text with the same label and wording, with no + or remove x. A work order with both fields shows both labels with their names.
* S2-N1: A side is never refilled on its own. Setting one side to "Unassigned" never hands that side to the other person and never fills it from the customer after the work order is created (S4-R24, S4-R28).
* S2-N2: A part sale's card has no + button. A part sale keeps one Sales Representative field.
* S2-E1: A person who is inactive (S1-E2) stays on the side they hold and reads "(Inactive)" in that field. Changing the other side is not refused because of them.
* S2-E2: Removing the Parts field while "Sales Representative - Labor" holds an inactive person keeps that person in the one field, reading "(Inactive)", until someone changes it.
* S2-E3: Removing the Parts field while "Sales Representative - Labor" reads "Unassigned" leaves the work order with nobody, so S4-R17 applies when it is invoiced.
* S2-E4: Changing a representative after the work order is invoiced or paid is not offered. A change sent by any other route is refused with "The sales representative cannot be changed on an invoiced or paid work order." The invoice keeps the names captured when it was created (Story 5). Reversing the invoice unlocks the field again, and the next invoice captures the names afresh.
* S2-E5: A work order split off another keeps the representatives of the one it came from: one field, or the Labor and Parts fields with the same people (S4-R29).

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Given a work order with one Sales Representative field, when the user hovers the field, then a + button shows to its right: the design system's Tertiary button in its small icon-only size, 32px square with 8px corners, showing the Lucide plus icon at 20px, grey on no fill at rest, with a light fill on hover that stays while its menu is open.
- [ ] Given the + shows, when the user hovers it, then the tooltip "Add representative" shows; it does not show while the menu is open.
- [ ] Given the user clicks the +, then a menu opens below it with one option, "Sales Representative - Parts"; clicking outside it or pressing Escape closes it with no change.
- [ ] Given the menu is open, when the user picks "Sales Representative - Parts", then the menu closes, the one field is replaced by "Sales Representative - Labor" and "Sales Representative - Parts", in that order, both holding the person who was in the one field (or "Unassigned"), nobody's credit changes, and neither dropdown opens on its own.
- [ ] Given the two fields show, then a remove x sits to the right of "Sales Representative - Parts" in the column the + uses (same Tertiary button, 32px square, Lucide x icon at 20px, grey at rest, light fill on hover) with the tooltip "Remove Sales Representative - Parts", the + is not shown, and "Sales Representative - Labor" has no button beside it.
- [ ] Given the two fields show, when the user uses either one, then it works as the Story 1 field (S1-R2 to S1-R15), and picking in one never changes the other.
- [ ] Given a split work order, when it is invoiced, then the Labor person is credited with the labor side and the Parts person with the parts side (Story 5).
- [ ] Given the two fields show, then both labels show in full on the card, never cut off.
- [ ] Given a split work order, including one where both fields hold the same person, when the page is reloaded or the work order reopened, then both fields are still there.
- [ ] Given the two fields show, when the user clicks the remove x, then the one "Sales Representative" field returns holding the person who was in "Sales Representative - Labor" (or "Unassigned"), and that person is credited with the whole work order, labor and parts.
- [ ] Given a work order that is invoiced, paid or opened from its history, then each field is plain text with the same label and wording and no + or remove x; a split work order shows both labels with their names.
- [ ] Given one side is set to "Unassigned", then that side is never handed to the other person and never filled from the customer after the work order is created.
- [ ] Given a part sale, then its card has no + button and keeps one Sales Representative field.
- [ ] Given one side holds an inactive person, then that field reads "(Inactive)" after their name, and changing the other side is not refused because of them.
- [ ] Given "Sales Representative - Labor" holds an inactive person, when the user removes the Parts field, then the one field keeps that person, reading "(Inactive)", until someone changes it.
- [ ] Given "Sales Representative - Labor" reads "Unassigned", when the user removes the Parts field, then the work order has nobody, and S4-R17 applies when it is invoiced.
- [ ] Given an invoiced or paid work order, then changing a representative is not offered, a change sent by any other route is refused with "The sales representative cannot be changed on an invoiced or paid work order.", and the invoice keeps the names captured when it was created; when the invoice is reversed, the field unlocks and the next invoice captures the names afresh.
- [ ] Given a work order split off another, then it keeps the representatives of the one it came from: one field, or the Labor and Parts fields with the same people.
