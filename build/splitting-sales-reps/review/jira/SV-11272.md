## Summary

Add a Sales Type filter (Labor, Parts, Part Sales) to Sales By Representative, and rename the Sales By Customer "Product Type" filter to "Sales Type", so managers do not mistake one kind of sale for another.

## Context

The user needs the 'Reports — Page Access' permission. It governs both reports on screen and their exports.

Context note: Sales By Representative used to filter by kind of invoice, like Sales By Customer. Now that one work order can credit two people, it filters by side instead: Labor, Parts or Part Sales.

## Requirements

Sales By Representative

* S8-R1: Sales By Representative has a "Sales Type" filter with three options, in this order: "Labor", "Parts", "Part Sales". All three are selected by default.
* S8-R2: Any combination can be selected. Rows, summary rows, totals and invoice counts follow the selection (S5-R10 to S5-R12).
* S8-R3: With all three selected the filter reads "Sales Type: All". With none it reads "Sales Type: None". Otherwise it reads the selected names in option order, for example "Sales Type: Labor, Parts".
* S8-R4: The filter line at the top of every PDF and CSV export of Sales By Representative reads the same: "Sales Type: All", or the selected names. With no option selected there is nothing to export: Export shows "Empty export" and makes no file.
* S8-R5: With no option selected the report includes no invoices and shows its empty state.

Sales By Customer

* S8-R6: On Sales By Customer, the filter labeled "Product Type" today is labeled "Sales Type".
* S8-R7: Its two options read "Part Sales" and "Service Sales".
* S8-R8: With both options selected, the filter reads "Sales Type: All Sales". With one, it reads that option's name, for example "Sales Type: Part Sales". With none, it reads "Sales Type: No sales".
* S8-R9: The filter line at the top of every PDF and CSV export of Sales By Customer reads "Sales Type: All Sales", "Sales Type: Part Sales only", or "Sales Type: Service Sales only". With no option selected, Export shows "Empty export" and makes no file.
* S8-R10: What each option includes does not change. "Part Sales" are invoices from part sales. "Service Sales" are invoices from service work orders, including the parts sold on them.
* S8-N1: No Sales By Customer figure, row or count changes because of the new wording.
* S8-E1: A Sales By Customer filter choice remembered from before the release opens with the same options selected, under the new names.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Given Sales By Representative, then it has a "Sales Type" filter with "Labor", "Parts" and "Part Sales", in that order, all selected by default.
- [ ] Given any combination is selected, then rows, summary rows, totals and invoice counts follow the selection.
- [ ] Given all three are selected, then the filter reads "Sales Type: All"; with none, "Sales Type: None"; otherwise the selected names in option order, for example "Sales Type: Labor, Parts".
- [ ] Given a PDF or CSV export of Sales By Representative, then its filter line reads "Sales Type: All" or the selected names; with no option selected, Export shows "Empty export" and makes no file.
- [ ] Given no option is selected on Sales By Representative, then the report includes no invoices and shows its empty state.
- [ ] Given Sales By Customer, then the filter labeled "Product Type" is now labeled "Sales Type" and its two options read "Part Sales" and "Service Sales".
- [ ] Given Sales By Customer with both options selected, then the filter reads "Sales Type: All Sales"; with one, that option's name, for example "Sales Type: Part Sales"; with none, "Sales Type: No sales".
- [ ] Given a PDF or CSV export of Sales By Customer, then its filter line reads "Sales Type: All Sales", "Sales Type: Part Sales only" or "Sales Type: Service Sales only"; with no option selected, Export shows "Empty export" and makes no file.
- [ ] Given Sales By Customer before and after the release, then each option includes the same invoices ("Part Sales" from part sales, "Service Sales" from service work orders including the parts sold on them), and no figure, row or count changes.
- [ ] Given a Sales By Customer filter choice remembered from before the release, when the report opens, then the same options are selected under the new names.
