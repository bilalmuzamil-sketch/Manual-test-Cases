## Summary

The part sale header card carries a Sales Representative field, so counter sales reach Sales By Representative.

## Context

Counter sales cannot be attributed to the person who made them today, so they never appear in the reporting a shop pays people from.

## Requirements

* S5-R1: The header card carries a Sales Representative field.
* S5-R2: It offers the shop’s active sales representatives and can be cleared.
* S5-R3: The choice is captured on the invoice, and the sale appears in Sales By Representative under that person.
* S5-R4: The change is recorded in the part sale log, with the previous and new name.
* S5-N1: With no rep on the sale, the invoice is attributed to the customer’s assigned representative, exactly as a work order is.
* S5-N2: With neither set, the sale appears as Unassigned.
* S5-N3: Once invoiced or paid the field is read-only and the captured attribution does not change. Reversing the invoice unlocks the field again, as on a work order, and the next invoice captures the representative afresh.
* S5-N4: A staff member not marked as a sales representative cannot be selected.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Given an open part sale, when a rep is chosen and the sale invoiced, then it appears under that rep in Sales By Representative.
- [ ] Given no rep on the sale, when it is invoiced, then it is attributed to the customer’s rep.
- [ ] Given a rep change, when the log is opened, then the previous and new names are in it.
- [ ] Given an invoiced part sale, when the header is read, then the field is read-only.
- [ ] Given an invoiced part sale, when the invoice is reversed, then the Sales Representative field can be changed again; when invoiced again, the new choice is captured.
