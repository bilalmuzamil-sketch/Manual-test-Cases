{quote}S3-E2: Allow horizontal scrolling when technician columns exceed the available viewport; never hide technicians automatically. When more of the board fits on screen, the handle is longer, as the browser draws it. The scrollbars inside each column are unchanged. Firefox shows its own standard scrollbar.{quote}

*How to see it*
# Sign in to Staging (https://app.staging.shopview.com) as Admin and check the top bar shows *Staging Heavy Duty - 9919*.
# In the top menu click *Work Orders*, then the *All* tab (top left).
# At the right of the bar above the list, click *Board View* (the right-most of the three view buttons).
# Look at the grey bar at the very bottom of the board, under the technician columns: it is about twice as thick as the thin bar inside a column, and its grey handle is about half as wide as one technician column.
# Drag the handle to the far right: the last column, "Deleted technician", comes into view and Unassigned stays on the left.

There is no "before" picture: Production does not have Board View yet, and Staging already has this fix, so the old 8 px bar cannot be shown on the same screen. The ticket's own numbers for the old bar were 8 px thick with a handle of 67 px at 1440 px wide.
