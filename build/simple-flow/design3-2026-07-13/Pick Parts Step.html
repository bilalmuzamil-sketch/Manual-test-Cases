PICKING THE RIGHT ONE

A · Optimistic pre-pick — least friction; advisor confirms rather than initiates. Good when auto-pick is off for cost/bin review, not physical sign-off.

B · Fold into Continue — fewest controls on screen; the step vanishes into the flow when everything is clean.

C · Confirm (attestation) — keeps the deliberate tap. Use when a human must vouch that the parts were actually pulled from the shelf.
