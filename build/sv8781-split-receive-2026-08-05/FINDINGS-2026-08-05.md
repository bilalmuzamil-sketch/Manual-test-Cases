## What the fix is, in plain words

From Dipesh's 2026-08-03 explanation and his 2026-08-05 *Ready for QA* note:

**The old behaviour:** when a work-order line was **split** onto a new work order, any parts already
ordered for that line **stayed attached to the original work order's purchase order**. The parts
looked right on the new work order, but at receive time they were not available there — because the
purchase order they belonged to still sat on the old work order. So they appeared to "split away" from
the rest of the vendor invoice and could not be received together.

**What changed:** splitting a line now **moves its already-ordered, unreceived parts onto the new work
order's own purchase order**, and the receive screen **merges a vendor's purchase orders into one
block** for a work-order-scoped receive — so everything for that work order can be received on one
vendor invoice.

**Note:** moving a part between lines already worked correctly; only **Split** was affected.
**Out of QA scope** (Dipesh's own words): the CLI repair command for already-affected work orders is a
dev/ops action.
