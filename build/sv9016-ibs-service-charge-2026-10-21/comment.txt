h3. How to see it

# Open the QA branch: [https://sv9016.qa.shopview.com/] and sign in as Admin.
# Click the avatar at the top right, then *Settings*. In the left menu, click *IBS* (just below *QuickBooks*).
# The *IBS Service Charge* section is under *Credentials*. Click its pencil on the right, type {{3.5}} and click *Save*.
# In the top menu, click *Reports*, then *IBS Batches* in the left menu. Stay on the *Ready To Send* tab.
# Tick one invoice, click the ⋮ button above the list on the right, and choose *Create Batch*.
# Open the downloaded *batch_export.csv*. The *S/C* line is 3.5% of the *Invoices* amount.

*Production before the fix:* I made one invoice for the IBS customer Ahsan (S-988, $299.90) and created a batch. The cover sheet shows S/C $5.70, which is 1.90% (Picture 2, top).
