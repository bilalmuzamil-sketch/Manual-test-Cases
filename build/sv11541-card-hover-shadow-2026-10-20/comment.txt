*How to see it yourself*
# Open Staging: https://app.staging.shopview.com and sign in as Admin.
# In the top menu click {{Work Orders}}, then the {{All}} tab (top left of the page).
# On the right of the bar above the list there are three view buttons. Click the right one, {{Board View}}.
# Move the mouse pointer over any card (don't click): a soft shadow appears on both sides and below it, and its border gets darker.
# Move the pointer away: the shadow goes.
