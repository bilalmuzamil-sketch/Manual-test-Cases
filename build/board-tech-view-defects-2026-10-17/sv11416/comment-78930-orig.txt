*How to see it yourself*
# Open Staging: https://app.staging.shopview.com/workorders and click the *All* tab (top left).
# Click *Search* in the bar above the list and type {{ZZAUTOTEST F1 Assigned To Me}}.
# On the right of that bar, click the *Board View* button (the right of the three view buttons). Scroll the board all the way to the right: the last column is *Deleted technician* with a grey *Inactive* label and two cards, S2-35709 and S2-36641. It has no six-dot drag grip at the left of its header.
# Click the *Tech View* button (the middle view button) and scroll to the bottom: the last group is *Deleted technician*, with the same *Inactive* label.
# Back on Board View, drag a card from another column onto the Deleted technician column: it goes back where it was. Drag S2-35709 out to the *Unassigned* column: the message "Lead technician removed" appears.
# Click the *List* button (the left view button) and search {{S2-36641}}: the *Lead Technician* column shows "Unassigned".
