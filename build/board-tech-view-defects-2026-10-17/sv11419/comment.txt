*How to see it yourself*
# Open Staging: https://app.staging.shopview.com/workorders and click the *All* tab (top left).
# Click *Search* in the bar above the list and type {{ZZAUTOTEST F1 Prompt Where}}.
# On the right of that bar, click the *Board View* button (the right of the three view buttons). Find the S2-35490 card in the *Esther Howard* column.
# Click the three dots (⋯) at the top right of the card: the menu shows *Reassign lead technician* and a red *Unassign*. (With the keyboard: press the Down arrow to reach Unassign and Enter.)
# Choose *Unassign*: the question "Clear Esther Howard's scheduled shifts?" appears. Click *Keep Shifts*. The message "Lead technician removed" appears and the card moves to the *Unassigned* column.
# To put it back, open the card's ⋯ menu, choose *Reassign lead technician*, pick Esther Howard and confirm.
# Search {{S2-15322}} (a Paid work order) and open its ⋯ menu: both items are greyed out.
