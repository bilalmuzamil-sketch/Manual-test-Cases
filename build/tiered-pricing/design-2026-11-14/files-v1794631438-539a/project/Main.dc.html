Tiered Pricing: design

Approved by the design authority on Nov 9, 2026. The canvas follows the PRD: page N is PRD Story N, each row on a page is one flow, and screens are numbered N.1, N.2 … in click order. A screen titled “Click …” shows where the click happens, with the target outlined in orange; the next screen shows the result. On other screens the outline marks what was clicked, pointed at or typed in. Gray notes mark today's production screens and screens that don't change. Every screen is drawn from the interactive prototype (v4.24) in the ShopView design system, except the “Today” screenshots of production and the phone screens (9.35–9.38), which are drawn to match the prototype's Edit Customer.
