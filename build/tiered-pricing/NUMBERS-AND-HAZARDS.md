# Tiered Pricing — every number the feature shows, and the hazards each needs (Rule 116)

Intake, 14 Nov 2026. Sources: Tiered Pricing PRD 938213378 page v37, Sales by Tier and Sales by Category PRD 968687617 page v21, both tech plans, the story tickets' acceptance criteria (worked numbers). Expected values below are the sources' own examples or arithmetic that follows from a quoted rule; cases will seed their own data and compute independently (Rules 14, 12). Read only.

Hazard codes (Rule 116 §3): **EX** exact value from seeded inputs · **PAR** parity with another surface · **POOL** ratio pooled, never averaged · **ZERO** zero/empty denominator shows the empty token · **NEG** negatives, credits, voids, reversals · **DIST** distinct counting · **SPLIT** proportional split sums back (and a zero-share member) · **RND** rounding/precision · **TIME** date buckets, boundaries both sides, time zone · **SCOPE** filter/location/client isolation · **FRESH** figure equals source right after a change · **CONS** headline = rows = levels.

- The 0.3-second fill at the largest client's size (TP S10-R22) and "no slower than Sales by Customer" (reports §6, NFR-R2): engineering measurements; a manual case can only observe the price filling in and the report opening (AUTOMATION: HOLD for the timing).
- Tie-out exceptions: tech plan 2 Appendix C (the RPT-S1-R7 list of invoices whose part lines don't add up) is still empty; historical-data parity checks must exclude exactly the signed-off invoices once that list exists (outstanding).
- Read-replica freshness (~150 ms accepted lag, tech plan 2) — a tester sees it only as a figure catching up on reload.
