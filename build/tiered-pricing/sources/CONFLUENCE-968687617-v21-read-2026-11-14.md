# Sales by Tier and Sales by Category

Confluence: [Sales by Tier](https://shopview.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/PM/pages/968687617/Sales+by+Tier). Related: [Tiered Pricing PRD](https://shopview.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/PM/pages/938213378/Tiered+Pricing). These reports read what Tiered Pricing records on each part line and ship in the same release.

## 1. Business Case

Tiered Pricing lets a client put customers on numbered pricing tiers and price every tier category by category. An owner then needs to know whether each tier earns the parts margin it was set up to earn, which categories make the margin, and how much is priced by hand. No report today groups sales by anything at the customer level, so owners export to Excel and sort by hand, or keep parts in customer-named categories, which breaks category reporting. This PRD adds two reports, Sales by Tier and Sales by Category, built from what each line recorded, and changes no existing report.

### Workarounds

- **Export Sales by Customer to Excel and group by hand** (about 6 companies in calls and chats): the owner tags each customer's group in a spreadsheet every time they want the split.

- **Print Sales by Customer and mark it up**: one client prints it and highlights its own equipment to separate internal from outside work.

- **Customer-named part categories**: CS has taught this since 2024. Sales show by category, so the category stands in for the customer group, but category reporting stops describing the parts (1,449 part lines were switched into a customer or internal category on work orders), and it depends on someone switching each part by hand.


## 2. Feature Overview

**Core ShopView**

- Two new reports, Sales by Tier and Sales by Category, under Reports › Parts, above Parts Velocity and Inventory Value.

- Sales by Tier: one row per pricing source: each tier (Default included), Fixed price, Fixed line total, Manual and Before tiers.

- Sales by Category: one row per category with sales, opening to how its sales were priced, then customers, then invoices.

- Columns: Parts Cost, Parts Invoiced, Parts Credits, Parts Net, Parts Margin, Parts Margin % and % of Total, with a Totals row whose Parts Invoiced matches Sales by Customer to the cent.

- Each row opens to its customers, and each customer opens to their invoices and credits, which link to the work order, Part Sale or credit.

- Filters for date range, Priced By, Category, Customer, Sales Type and Location. Every column sorts; the first click on a number column puts the biggest number on top.

- Downloads: a summary and an expanded version, each as PDF and CSV, for both reports.

- Layout, filters, sorting, Totals, loading and downloads follow the ShopView report standard. The closest reference report is Sales by Customer.


**Out of Scope**

- **Contracts.** Parked with Tiered Pricing's contracts for a later release; there is no Contracts row or grouping.

- **Grouping by matrix.** Sales by Category groups by category only.

- **Labor, shop supplies and whole work order or Part Sale fees.** Tiered Pricing prices parts only, so only parts are reported. Labor columns come when labor reaches tiers (Labor in Contracts).

- **Changes to Sales by Customer or Sales.** This PRD changes neither (Tiered Pricing S17-R3). The credits rule these reports follow applies to every sales report through Report Modernization, not through this PRD.

- **Credits with no part lines.** Credits created on the customer page for goodwill, overpayments, fees, write-offs or moved balances name no part, so these reports leave them out. Sales and Sales by Customer still show them.

- **A separate credits report.**

- **Dashboard widgets and charts.** A table only.

- **Period comparison** (this quarter against last). Not in today's reports either.

- **A permission for these reports alone.** Reports have one permission today.

- **Internal and no-charge work.** These work orders close without an invoice, so they are not in these reports, Sales or Sales by Customer; how they report and post belongs to the Internal Work Orders PRD.


## 3. Jobs to be Done / Goals

**When** I have put customers on tiers, **I want to** see the parts sales and margin each tier produced, **so I can** tell whether each agreement earns what I set it up to earn.

**When** a tier's margin looks wrong, **I want to** open it to the customers and invoices behind it, **so I can** find the work orders that pulled it down without opening every one.

**When** I work on a category's pricing, **I want to** see its parts sales and margin split by how they were priced, **so I can** see where margins are made and lost.

**When** I review a period with my bookkeeper, **I want to** export the split, **so I can** file it or work with it outside ShopView.

Goals:

- An owner sees parts sales, credits and margin by tier and by category for any period, without exporting.

- Each report's Parts Invoiced and Parts Cost match Sales by Customer to the cent for the same filters; Sales by Category's Totals equal Sales by Tier's.

- At 90 days, report how many clients with a tier pricing parts have opened either report. There is no target; this is the first read of demand.


## 4. Key Decisions

- **New reports, not a filter on Sales by Customer.** **Rationale:** a new report is simpler and lower risk than changing Sales by Customer, a report in production that 13% of active clients filter in a month; a new report can't break it. **Rejected:** a Priced By filter on Sales by Customer.

- **Their own PRD, released with Tiered Pricing.** In Jira it is a story under the Tiered Pricing epic, so the two can't ship apart. **Rationale:** each report can be found and built on its own, and it must ship with the release. **Rejected:** folding the reports into the Tiered Pricing PRD.

- **Rows are pricing sources, and every row sorts together.** Typed prices and fixed prices are each a row of their own, beside the tiers. **Rationale:** an owner sees everything that priced parts in one list. **Rejected:** tiers only, with the rest hidden.

- **Customers are listed under what priced their parts.** A customer moved from Fleet to Wholesale shows under both, each with only the lines it priced, so every level adds up to the one above. Within a row, a line is listed under its invoice's customer. **Rationale:** reports attribute sales by what each line recorded. **Rejected:** listing customers under their current tier.

- **The part lines are added up, and they tie out to Sales by Customer.** Parts Invoiced and Parts Margin mean what they mean on Sales by Customer. Parts Cost is the parts cost Sales by Customer stores with each invoice, which it doesn't show today. The Totals tie to Sales by Customer to the cent (S1-R7). The Sales report counts parts differently (part-line discounts and fees, every credit on the day it was made, returned parts' cost, and a work order's whole parts cost on its newest invoice), so these reports don't try to match it. Wherever a rule here could differ from Sales by Customer (discounts, fixed line totals, customers, dates), it follows Sales by Customer. **Rationale:** two reports must never disagree. **Rejected:** reading Sales by Customer's invoice totals and spreading them across the lines; letting the lines win without a tie-out. A fix to how Sales by Customer counts a number (for example [SV-11334](https://shopview.atlassian.net/browse/SV-11334)) applies here too.

- **These two reports show Invoiced, Credits and Net.** Credits follow the sales-report credits rule (S1-E8). **Rationale:** Sales and Sales by Customer differ by exactly their credits today, so showing both ties these reports to each. **Rejected:** leaving credits out; a separate credits row not traced to tiers.

- **Margin and % of Total use Parts Invoiced.** **Rationale:** the same as Sales by Customer; a credit carries no cost, so taking it off the margin would show every return as a loss. **Rejected:** margin on Parts Net.

- **Sales by Category groups by the line's category.** **Rationale:** the line's category is what priced it, and QuickBooks and AccountingHub book part revenue by the line's category too, so an invoice books under the same category the report shows. **Rejected:** the part's catalog category; a matrix level.

- **"Manual", not "Typed by hand".** **Rationale:** the same word as the line's pricing source. **Rejected:** "Typed by hand".

- **"Parts Margin" stays as the column name, and "Parts Margin %" replaces "Margin %".** **Rationale:** on the report standard "Margin %" is the whole job's margin; these reports show parts only. **Rejected:** "Profit"; keeping "Margin %".

- **Sorting has three states.** The first click on a number column sorts biggest first, the second reverses, the third returns to the first-load order. **Rationale:** an owner looks for the biggest number and needs a way back to tier order. **Rejected:** two states only (the report standard; this is a deviation for the standard's owner).

- **No pinned column.** Parts Invoiced is third and bold. **Rationale:** the owner reads cost, then sales, then margin; the columns fit without sideways scrolling. **Rejected:** pinning Parts Invoiced at the far right (the report standard; a deviation for the standard's owner).

- **A renamed tier is one row under its current name.** Lines are grouped by the tier itself; a line's price details still show the name recorded when it was priced. **Rationale:** one tier, one row. **Rejected:** a row per name.

- **Feature flag: none**, as Tiered Pricing. **Loading and errors:** the report loading and error states of Sales by Customer. **Design wording is binding:** the labels and messages here ship as written. **Downstream surfaces:** the downloads (PDF and CSV) are the only output; nothing reaches invoices or the portal. **Phone:** desktop only by rule (detailed reports).

- **Permissions.** Viewing needs 'Reports — Page Access', like every report; cost and margin show to anyone who has it, as on Sales by Customer. Opening an invoice from the report follows Sales by Customer's rules for that link. No new permission is introduced. This is the one exception to Tiered Pricing S16-R5 (Tiered Pricing S16-R7). **Rationale:** reports have one permission today. **Rejected:** a permission for these reports alone.


## 5. Terminology

- **Priced By** → what priced a part line, recorded on the line when it was priced: a tier, Fixed price, Fixed line total, Manual, or Before tiers. In Sales by Tier it is the row a line belongs to.

- **Manual** → a sell price or margin typed on the line that differs from the calculated one.

- **Before tiers** → part lines priced before Tiered Pricing's release, which recorded no pricing source, whatever their invoice date.

- **Parts Credits** → customer credit memos with part lines: Part Sale returns and other credits for parts, counted per part line, at the amount credited after any restocking fee, before tax. Vendor credits are never included.

- **Parts Net** → Parts Invoiced plus Parts Credits (credits are negative).


## 6. Assumptions

- A client has a handful of tiers plus archived ones; there is no limit. Verify against the first 90 days of tiers.

- A customer group holds a median of 5 customers today (p90 14); 11% of clients invoice parts to more than 100 customers in 90 days.

- A typical client has 4 part categories (median); the largest about 270.

- The busiest client invoiced about 12,400 part lines in 90 days (FoxyDB, 2026-11-03), about 50,000 a year.

- Performance: for the same date range and client, each report loads, opens a row and downloads no slower than Sales by Customer. Engineering records Sales by Customer's times for one year at the largest client in the tech plan as the bar.


## 7. Requirements

### Story 1: See parts sales and margin by what priced them

As an owner, I want one row per tier with its parts sales and margin so that I can see which agreements earn what.

Design: [Tiered Pricing design canvas](https://claude.ai/artifact/A3NtAX9CHJCD87SXDTn9AE?sk=z3YP2vy3EJhhhP_Dr9mV0Q#page-9641025c0626), Page 20, screens 20.1–20.24 Jira: [SV-11718](https://shopview.atlassian.net/browse/SV-11718) Form factor: Desktop only by rule (reports).

Prerequisites:

- The user has 'Reports — Page Access'.

- Tiered Pricing has shipped; these reports are released with it.


Requirements:

- S1-R1: Reports › Parts lists Sales by Tier, Sales by Category, Parts Velocity and Inventory Value, in that order. Neither report appears under Performance.

- S1-R2: Sales by Tier reports invoiced part lines from work orders and Part Sales. Lines are dated by the date their invoice was created, as on Sales by Customer. Only invoiced lines count; an open work order appears once it is invoiced. Void invoices never count. Each line belongs to the row of what priced it, as recorded on the line (Tiered Pricing S12-R4).

- S1-R3: Every invoiced part line is in exactly one row: each tier (Default included), Fixed price, Fixed line total, Manual or Before tiers. A part's core and environmental charges, and any fee or discount attached to that single part, are in the same row and category as the part. A row shows only when it has invoiced lines or credits in the range and filters, so a month in which no Wholesale customer was invoiced has no Wholesale row.

- S1-R6: The columns, in this order:

  - **Priced By** (or **Category** on Sales by Category).

  - **Parts Cost:** the cost of the row's parts, as Sales by Customer stores it with each invoice.

  - **Parts Invoiced** (bold, the headline column): the parts sales of the row's lines, calculated as Parts Invoiced on Sales by Customer.

  - **Parts Credits:** the row's credits (S1-E8), shown as a negative.

  - **Parts Net:** Parts Invoiced plus Parts Credits.

  - **Parts Margin:** Parts Invoiced minus Parts Cost, as Parts Margin on Sales by Customer.

  - **Parts Margin %:** Parts Margin ÷ Parts Invoiced, two decimals; "—" when Parts Invoiced is $0.00 or less.

  - **% of Total:** the row's Parts Invoiced ÷ the Totals' Parts Invoiced, at every level, so opened rows add up to their parent's share. "—" when the Totals' Parts Invoiced is $0.00 or less; a negative row shows a negative share. The header's tooltip says "Share of Parts Invoiced for everything that matches your filters."

  - Column names follow the report standard. No column is pinned.


- S1-R7: A bold "Totals" row stays at the bottom while scrolling and sums the whole filtered result, not only the rows loaded. For the same date range, locations and Sales Type, its Parts Invoiced equals Sales by Customer's Parts Invoiced, to the cent. Its Parts Cost equals the parts cost Sales by Customer stores with those invoices, and its Parts Net equals Parts Invoiced plus these reports' credits. These reports don't tie to the Sales report. Engineering lists in the tech plan any invoice whose part lines don't add up to its invoice totals; the owning PM signs off each, with the report standard's owner.

- S1-R8: A renamed tier is one row under its current name; a renamed Default shows its current name (Tiered Pricing S4-R15). Archived tiers stay in the report while they have sales in the range.

- S1-R9: Labor, shop supplies and whole work order or Part Sale fees are not in the report. A fee attached to a single part follows that part (S1-R3).

- S1-R10: Internal and no-charge work orders close without an invoice, so their part lines are not in the report, as they are not in Sales or Sales by Customer.

- S1-R11: Active tier rows show the tier's number and name ("2 · Fleet"). An archived tier has no number (Tiered Pricing S2-R15), so its row shows its name with "(archived)". Other rows show their name. No icon beside any row name.

- S1-R12: 30 rows a page at every level (rows, customers, invoices), with more loading as the user scrolls.

- S1-R13: A row whose lines made no margin shows $0.00 Parts Margin; negative amounts show as negatives ("−$12.40") on screen, in the Totals and in every download.

- S1-R14: A column chooser, remembered per browser, can hide Parts Cost, Parts Credits, Parts Net, Parts Margin, Parts Margin % and % of Total. Only the column choice is remembered; the sort isn't, and the report always opens in its first-load order (S3-R7). The first column and Parts Invoiced always show. Downloads follow the columns shown.


Negative Cases:

- S1-N1: With default filters and nothing invoiced or credited in the date range, the report shows "There are no results for selected date range" and no Totals row.

- S1-N2: Without 'Reports — Page Access', the report isn't listed and its link shows "Access restricted", as every report does.


Edge Cases:

- S1-E1: A part priced for one customer on a work order whose customer was later changed stays in the row of what priced it, under the invoice's customer; the expanded download names the customer it was priced for (Tiered Pricing S12-R4, S9-R17, S11-R2).

- S1-E2: A line typed over a fixed price is in Manual.

- S1-E4: A fixed line total's parts are in Fixed line total, whatever the customer's tier: the fixed total priced them (Tiered Pricing S10-R13). The row reports the parts amount Sales by Customer counts for that line, and Parts Cost is the sum of its parts' costs. Engineering confirms that amount in the tech plan; any difference from the line's stored parts amount goes on the S1-R7 list. Fixed line totals invoiced before release recorded no pricing source, so they are in Before tiers.

- S1-E5: There is no canned line row. A part from a canned line is priced by the customer's tier when added and is in that tier's row.

- S1-E6: A discount on a single part line, on a work order or a Part Sale, lowers Parts Invoiced in the row of what priced that part, as on Sales by Customer. Whole work order and whole Part Sale fees and discounts are not in these reports. Older invoices whose line discounts Sales by Customer counts as whole-invoice adjustments follow Sales by Customer; any difference goes on the S1-R7 list.

- S1-E7: A line priced by a tier as Same as previous tier is in that tier's row (the customer's tier), not the tier it followed (Tiered Pricing S12-R19).

- S1-E8: Credits follow the sales-report credits rule. Only customer credit memos with part lines count: Part Sale returns and other credits for parts. Overpayments (money the customer already paid) and whole-invoice discounts carried over to another invoice are the same kind of record, but they are not in these reports. A credit line that names a part line counts at the amount credited after any restocking fee, before tax, on the date the credit was created, whether or not it has been applied. Void credits and vendor credits never count. A credit line is in the row and category of the part line it credits, at the original invoiced price (Tiered Pricing S11-N1), under the credit's customer and location; a credit on a line priced before release is in Before tiers. A credit with no part lines, such as one created on the customer page for goodwill, an overpayment, a fee or a write-off, is not in these reports: nothing says which part it was for. It still shows in Sales and Sales by Customer. A credit line that names no part line on a credit that has part lines is not in these reports either. A credit never changes the original invoice or a past month.

- S1-E9: Warranty parts are typed to $0 and are in Manual. A part with no cost is in the customer's tier at $0.


Error Handling:

- A failed load shows the report error state of Sales by Customer, keeping the filters.


### Story 2: Open a row to its customers and invoices

As an owner, I want to open a tier or a category to the customers and invoices behind it so that I can find what pulled its margin down.

### Story 3: Filter and sort

As an owner, I want to narrow the reports to a period, a location, a few tiers or categories so that I can answer one question at a time.

Tiers apply to the whole client, so this shows a location's sales by tier.

- S3-R12: A filter chip shows as selected (blue) only when it holds a specific selection. Date range and Location always do, so they are always blue. Every other filter is plain when it is All or nothing is picked: the shared filter chip treats a filter whose default is all selected (Priced By, Category, Sales Type) as nothing picked, so it is plain when all are selected.

- S3-R6: Filters are kept in the page address, so back, reload, a link or a bookmark reopens the same view. A new visit starts from the defaults.

- S3-R11: Switching between the two reports carries the shared filters (date range, Location, Sales Type, Customer, Category, Priced By) in the page address, closes open rows and resets the sort.

- S3-R7: On first load, Sales by Tier's rows are in tier number order (Default first, then active tiers, then archived tiers in the order they were archived), then Fixed price, Fixed line total, Manual and Before tiers.

- S3-R8: Sorting is done on the server, over the whole filtered result. Every column sorts. The first click on text sorts A to Z and on a number column biggest first; the second click reverses; the third returns to the first-load order (S3-R7). Parts Credits are negative, so the first click puts the largest credit on top. Ties break by the S3-R7 order and then by name for rows, by name for customers, and by number, smallest first, for invoices and credits. Customers and invoices sort the same way within their row. Downloads use the same order.


Negative Cases:

- S3-N1: Filters that match nothing, including no Sales Type selected, show "No record matches these filters", "Try changing filters to widen your results." and a "Clear All Filters" button, and no Totals row.


Edge Cases:

- S3-E1: A filter search with no match shows no options and keeps the current picks.


Error Handling:

- Not applicable beyond the report's own load error (S1 Error Handling).

### Story 5: Sales by Category

As an owner, I want parts sales and margin by category, split by how they were priced, so that I can see where margins are made.

Design: [Tiered Pricing design canvas](https://claude.ai/artifact/A3NtAX9CHJCD87SXDTn9AE?sk=z3YP2vy3EJhhhP_Dr9mV0Q#page-9641025c0626), Page 20, screens 20.1–20.24 Jira: [SV-11718](https://shopview.atlassian.net/browse/SV-11718) Form factor: Desktop only by rule (reports).

Prerequisites:

- The user has 'Reports — Page Access'.


Requirements:

- S5-R1: Sales by Category has Sales by Tier's columns, filters, sorting, Totals, credits and downloads (Stories 1, 3 and 4).

- S5-R2: Rows are categories, by the category on each part line, under its current name; a renamed Default tier shows its current name (Tiered Pricing S4-R15). A category opens to its Priced By rows (Default, each tier, Fixed price, Fixed line total, Manual, Before tiers), each to customers, each to invoices and credits.

- S5-R3: % of Total is as S1-R6.

- S5-R4: Only categories with sales or credits in the range show; a category with neither has no row. Uncategorized sorts first on first load, then categories A to Z.

- S5-R5: A category with sales but no margin shows $0.00 Parts Margin; negative totals show as negatives (S1-R13).

- S5-R6: Inside a category, Priced By rows follow Sales by Tier's order (S3-R7).


Negative Cases:

- S5-N1: Empty results show the same two empty states as Sales by Tier (S1-N1, S3-N1).


Edge Cases:

- S5-E1: A line whose category was deleted reports under Uncategorized (Tiered Pricing S8-E1).

- S5-E2: Sales by Category's Totals equal Sales by Tier's for the same filters.

- S5-E3: A fixed line total that spans categories is split across them by the split the line recorded (Tiered Pricing S10-R13): in proportion to each part's cost at invoicing, in cents, with any leftover cent placed as Tiered Pricing S10-R13 sets out, so the parts always add up to the line's parts amount. Parts that all cost $0 split by quantity. Fixed line totals invoiced before release recorded no split, so the report splits them the same way when it runs. The Category filter on Sales by Tier uses the same split.

- S5-E4: The report groups by category only; there is no matrix grouping.

- S5-E5: Lines invoiced before release stay under the category they were invoiced with, including customer-named categories. As clients stop switching categories after release, sales move to the parts' own categories. This is expected.


Error Handling:

- A failed load shows the report error state of Sales by Customer, keeping the filters.
