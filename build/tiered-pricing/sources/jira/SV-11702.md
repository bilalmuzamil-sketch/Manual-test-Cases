# SV-11702 Tiered Pricing - Story 13 - Part Sales

## Description

## Summary

Parts on a Part Sale are priced by the customer's Part Sales tier, from the matrix's Part Sale side when it is on, and show what priced them. The parts counter quotes a wholesale or fleet customer quickly without typing prices.

## Context

* Role: parts counter person with 'Part Sales — Create & Edit'; prices and details need 'See Financial Data'. Part Sales follow today's form factor.
* What must be true first: a Part Sale with a customer; the pricing order (Story 10) and Priced by (Story 12). A customer's Part Sales tier is independent of their work order tier (Story 9); walk-in sales use the Counter Sale customer's Part Sales tier.

## Requirements

* S13-R1: Parts on a Part Sale are priced by the customer's Part Sales tier when the Part Sale was made, from the Part Sale Matrix side when on and the Work Order Matrix side when off. A customer whose Part Sales tier was never changed is priced by Default. Priced by and price details work as on work orders.
* S13-E1: Change Customer on a Part Sale (SV-8105) works as on a work order: parts keep their prices and the same notice shows (Story 14).
* A part with no cost works as on work orders (S10-N1, S12-N2).

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Given a customer on Wholesale for Part Sales and Fleet for work orders, when a part is added to a Part Sale, then Wholesale prices it and Priced by reads Wholesale.
- [ ] Given the part's matrix has its Part Sale side on, then that side prices it; with the side off, the Work Order Matrix side does.
- [ ] Given a customer whose Part Sales tier was never changed, then Default prices the part.
- [ ] Given a Part Sale made while the customer was on Wholesale, when the customer moves to Fleet for Part Sales and a part is added, then Wholesale still prices it.
- [ ] Given a walk-in Part Sale and the Counter Sale customer on Counter for Part Sales, then Counter prices its parts.
- [ ] Given a Part Sale line, then the price opens its details and the Priced by column shows, as on work orders.
- [ ] Given Change Customer on a Part Sale, then parts keep their prices and the notice shows the count of lines priced for the previous customer.
- [ ] Given a part with no cost on a Part Sale, then it sells at $0.00 and its details name the Part Sale's tier, with no Manual icon.
