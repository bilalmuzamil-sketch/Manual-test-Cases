# SV-11705 Tiered Pricing - Story 16 - Who can do what

## Description

## Summary

Every new pricing screen is protected by permissions clients already manage, with no new permission, and every rule is enforced, not only hidden. Only the right people see and change prices.

## Context

* Applies to every new screen and control in this feature; not a screen of its own.
* Permissions, as named in settings: Settings › Pricing (Tiers, Matrices, tier and matrix pages, the General Matrix, the simulator, creating, archiving, reactivating and deleting a tier) and every Audit Log: 'Settings — Parts' and 'See Financial Data'. Fixed Rules tab: 'Settings — Parts'. Categories page and creating, renaming and deleting a category: 'Settings — Parts'; its pricing columns, matrix and grid also 'See Financial Data'. A tier's Customers tab: those two plus 'Customers — View'. Manage Customers: those two plus 'Customers — Create & Edit'. A customer's tiers in Edit Customer and New Customer: 'Customers — Create & Edit'. The customer page: 'Customers — View'; its Pricing section also 'View and Manage AP/AR Data'. Priced by, price details and the Manual icon: 'See Financial Data'. Typing a price or cost on a line: the existing line permissions plus 'See Financial Data'. Sales by Tier and Sales by Category: 'Reports — Page Access'.

## Requirements

* S16-R6: The section 4 permissions govern every new screen; no new permission appears in settings. Any internal check needs both 'Settings — Parts' and 'See Financial Data' wherever S16-R5 does.
* S16-R2: Controls a user may not use are hidden, not greyed, as today; a user who can see a page but not change it sees it read-only, with no Edit, Save or New.
* S16-R4: A customer's tiers never need 'Settings — Parts': 'Customers — Create & Edit' views and changes them in Edit Customer and New Customer; 'Customers — View' with 'View and Manage AP/AR Data' shows them read-only on the customer card.
* S16-R8: The Categories page's pricing columns, its grid and every matrix edit need 'Settings — Parts' and 'See Financial Data'; without 'Settings — Parts' there is no Categories page.
* S16-R5: Tiers, matrices, the simulator, category pricing and the Audit Log need both 'Settings — Parts' and 'See Financial Data'. Every rule is enforced however an action is attempted. Without 'See Financial Data', price details and the pricing source are withheld like line prices.
* S16-R11: With 'Settings — Parts' but not 'See Financial Data': Settings › Pricing shows only Fixed Rules; Categories shows only the Category column; New Category and a category's page have no Pricing Matrix field or grid, and a new category uses the General Matrix.
* S16-R7: Sales by Tier and Sales by Category are the one exception: they show to 'Reports — Page Access', costs and margins included, as Sales by Customer does.
* S16-N1: Without 'Settings — Parts', the Settings Parts group is hidden; a link to Pricing shows "Access restricted" with "Looks like you don't have access to this page. Reach out to your administrator to request access." and Go to home.
* S16-N4: A technician without 'See Financial Data' can add parts, priced by the customer's tier without seeing the price; a user without it can split or change a part's category, and the parts are priced again by the rules.
* S16-N5: With 'Settings — Parts' but not 'Customers — Create & Edit', a tier's Customers tab is read-only and Manage Customers is hidden.
* S16-E1: With 'Settings — Parts' but not 'Customers — View', a tier's Customers tab doesn't show, and archiving or deleting a tier with customers gives the count only: "12 customers are on Fleet. Someone who can manage customers moves them first."
* Error: an action refused by the server for a missing permission shows "Access restricted", as today.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Given Settings › Permissions after release, then no new permission appears.
- [ ] Given a user with 'Settings — Parts' and 'See Financial Data', then they see and edit Tiers, Matrices, tier and matrix pages, the simulator, category pricing and every Audit Log.
- [ ] Given a user with 'Settings — Parts' but not 'See Financial Data', then Settings › Pricing shows only Fixed Rules, Categories shows only the Category column, New Category and category pages have no Pricing Matrix or grid, and a category they create uses the General Matrix.
- [ ] Given that user calls a tier, matrix, simulator or Audit Log action directly, then the server refuses it and "Access restricted" shows.
- [ ] Given a user without 'Settings — Parts', then the Settings Parts group is hidden, there is no Categories page, and a link to Pricing shows "Access restricted" with Go to home.
- [ ] Given a user with 'Customers — Create & Edit' but not 'Settings — Parts', then they can view and change a customer's tiers in Edit Customer and New Customer.
- [ ] Given 'Customers — View' with 'View and Manage AP/AR Data', then the customer card shows the tiers read-only; without 'Customers — Create & Edit', there is no edit pencil.
- [ ] Given 'Settings — Parts' and 'See Financial Data' but not 'Customers — Create & Edit', then a tier's Customers tab is read-only and Manage Customers is hidden.
- [ ] Given 'Settings — Parts' and 'See Financial Data' but not 'Customers — View', then the tier's Customers tab doesn't show, and archiving Fleet with 12 customers says "12 customers are on Fleet. Someone who can manage customers moves them first."
- [ ] Given a user who can view a page but not change it, then it is read-only with no Edit, Save or New buttons, and no greyed controls.
- [ ] Given a technician without 'See Financial Data', when they add a part to a Fleet work order, then Fleet prices it and they see no price, Priced by or details; when they split the work order or change a part's category, then the moved or recategorized parts are priced again.
- [ ] Given a user with 'Reports — Page Access' but not 'See Financial Data', then Sales by Tier and Sales by Category show with costs and margins.
