# SV-4027 Customer-specific parts pricing matrices

## Description

**Customer Name:** Schnell Equipment Services

**Number of Users:** 2

**Date Requested:** July 3, 2025

**Description:**
Schnell Equipment Services has requested the ability to apply **different parts pricing matrices by customer**, not just by part type or category.

They would like to set **unique markups** depending on the **type of customer** (e.g., equipment customers vs trucking customers), even when the same part category is involved.

For example, a part under the category "Filter" may need to be treated as "Truck Parts" for trucking customers to apply a different pricing matrix. Currently, this is not possible without manually adjusting categories or prices.

They also mentioned that this type of functionality would support broader use cases like:

* Offering **“preferred customer” pricing**
* Handling **contract-specific pricing** for key accounts
* Reducing manual overrides or custom part duplication

**Impact:**

* Improves flexibility and accuracy in pricing
* Supports customer loyalty strategies
* Reduces errors and time spent managing pricing
