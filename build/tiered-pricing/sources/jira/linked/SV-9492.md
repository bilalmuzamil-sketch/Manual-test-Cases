## Summary

Add a COST + pricing model to Price Matrix with tiered adders and optional correlation with margin pricing.

## Problem Statement

Not stated in the conversation.

## Proposed Solution

Customer asked for Price Matrix to support a COST + model where items costing from $0.01 to $100 add $30 to cost to determine the sell price, and where items from 100.01 and up add 10% margin. They also asked for this to be used in correlation with margin pricing: “0-100 cost will add $30.00 to the cost to come up with a sell price” and then “anything from 100.01 and up add 10% margin.” They noted this would allow more flexibility on low-scale items in a bulk pricing zone and that tires are an example of an industry where they use a cost + pricing scheme.
