# SV-4409 Matrix min and max values in the rules

## Description

The **part** price **doesn’t** fall within the **minimum and maximum cost** bracket of a price matrix, but the s**ell price is still being calculated** according to **that matrix.**

(Please follow the steps given below to reproduce)

* Average cost for the part is **$1**
* There is only **one** pricing matrix in the account that is specifically set for that part.
* The Minimum cost is set to $90000 and the Maximum cost for that pricing matrix is set to $100000
  (Note: The price of part is just $1)
* When we go to Inventory and look for that part, its Sell price appears to be calculating according to that irrelevant pricing matric because its minimum and maximum cost is far higher than the actual average price of the part.
