# SV-11718 Tiered Pricing - Sales by Tier and Sales by Category reports

## Description

## Summary

Two new reports, Sales by Tier and Sales by Category, under Reports › Parts. Sales by Tier shows parts sales, credits and margin by what priced each part line: each tier (Default included), Fixed price, Fixed line total, Manual and Before tiers. Sales by Category shows the same numbers by category, each opening to how its sales were priced. Every row opens to its customers and then to their invoices and credits, the Totals tie out to Sales by Customer and Sales to the cent, and both reports download as PDF and CSV. An owner can see whether each tier earns the parts margin it was set up to earn and where margins are made and lost. They have their own PRD and ship in the same release as Tiered Pricing.

## Context

* Role: user with 'Reports — Page Access'. Desktop only (reports).
* What must be true first: Tiered Pricing has shipped and records on each part line what priced it and the customer it was priced for (Tiered Pricing S12-R4). These reports are released with it.
* Layout, filters, sorting, Totals, loading and downloads follow the ShopView report standard; the closest reference report is Sales by Customer. Two deviations agreed with the standard's owner: sorting has three states, and no column is pinned.
* Sales and Sales by Customer are unchanged. Requirement ids below are from the Sales by Tier PRD.

## Requirements

**Story 1: See parts sales and margin by what priced them**

* S1-R1: Reports › Parts lists Sales by Tier, Sales by Category, Parts Velocity and Inventory Value, in that order. Neither report appears under Performance.
* S1-R2: Sales by Tier reports invoiced part lines from work orders and Part Sales, dated by the date their invoice was created, as on Sales by Customer. Only invoiced lines count; an open work order appears once it is invoiced; void invoices never count. Each line belongs to the row of what priced it, as recorded on the line (Tiered Pricing S12-R4).
* S1-R3: Every invoiced part line is in exactly one row: each tier (Default included), Fixed price, Fixed line total, Manual or Before tiers. A row shows only when it has invoiced lines or credits in the range and filters, so a month with no Wholesale invoices has no Wholesale row.
* S1-R6: Columns, in order: Priced By (Category on Sales by Category); Parts Cost (as Sales by Customer stores it with each invoice); Parts Invoiced (bold, the headline column, calculated as on Sales by Customer); Parts Credits (negative); Parts Net (Parts Invoiced plus Parts Credits); Parts Margin (Parts Invoiced minus Parts Cost, as on Sales by Customer); Parts Margin % (Parts Margin ÷ Parts Invoiced, two decimals, "—" when Parts Invoiced is $0.00 or less); % of Total (the row's Parts Invoiced ÷ the Totals' Parts Invoiced at every level, "—" when the Totals' Parts Invoiced is $0.00 or less, negative for a negative row; header tooltip "Share of Parts Invoiced for everything that matches your filters."). Column names follow the report standard. No column is pinned.
* S1-R7: A bold "Totals" row stays at the bottom while scrolling and sums the whole filtered result, not only the rows loaded. For the same date range, locations and Sales Type, its Parts Invoiced equals Sales by Customer's to the cent, its Parts Cost equals the parts cost Sales by Customer stores with those invoices, and its Parts Net equals Parts Invoiced plus these reports' credits. These reports don't tie to the Sales report. Engineering lists in the tech plan any invoice whose part lines don't add up to its invoice totals; the owning PM signs off each, with the report standard's owner.
* S1-R8: A renamed tier is one row under its current name; a renamed Default shows its current name (Tiered Pricing S4-R15). Archived tiers stay in the report while they have sales in the range.
* S1-R9: Labor, shop supplies and whole work order or Part Sale fees are not in the report. A part's core and environmental charges, and any fee or discount attached to that single part, are in the same row and category as the part.
* S1-R10: Internal and no-charge work orders close without an invoice, so their part lines are not in the report, as they are not in Sales or Sales by Customer.
* S1-R11: Active tier rows show the tier's number and name ("2 · Fleet"). An archived tier has no number (Tiered Pricing S2-R15), so its row shows its name with "(archived)". Other rows show their name. No icon beside any row name.
* S1-R12: 30 rows a page at every level (rows, customers, invoices), with more loading as the user scrolls.
* S1-R13: A row whose lines made no margin shows $0.00 Parts Margin; negative amounts show as negatives ("−$12.40") on screen, in the Totals and in every download.
* S1-R14: A column chooser, remembered per browser, can hide Parts Cost, Parts Credits, Parts Net, Parts Margin, Parts Margin % and % of Total. Only the column choice is remembered; the sort isn't, and the report always opens in its first-load order. The first column and Parts Invoiced always show. Downloads follow the columns shown.
* S1-N1: With default filters and nothing invoiced or credited in the date range, the report shows "There are no results for selected date range" and no Totals row.
* S1-N2: Without 'Reports — Page Access', the report isn't listed and its link shows "Access restricted", as every report does.
* S1-E1: A part priced for one customer on a work order whose customer was later changed stays in the row of what priced it, under the invoice's customer; the expanded download names the customer it was priced for (Tiered Pricing S12-R4, S9-R17, S11-R2).
* S1-E2: A line typed over a fixed price is in Manual.
* S1-E4: A fixed line total's parts are in Fixed line total, whatever the customer's tier (Tiered Pricing S10-R13). The row reports the parts amount Sales by Customer counts for that line, and Parts Cost is the sum of its parts' costs. Engineering confirms that amount in the tech plan; any difference from the line's stored parts amount goes on the S1-R7 list. Fixed line totals invoiced before release recorded no pricing source, so they are in Before tiers.
* S1-E5: There is no canned line row. A part from a canned line is priced by the customer's tier when added and is in that tier's row.
* S1-E6: A discount on a single part line, on a work order or a Part Sale, lowers Parts Invoiced in the row of what priced that part, as on Sales by Customer. Whole work order and whole Part Sale fees and discounts are not in these reports. Older invoices whose line discounts Sales by Customer counts as whole-invoice adjustments follow Sales by Customer; any difference goes on the S1-R7 list.
* S1-E7: A line priced by a tier set to Same as previous tier is in that tier's row (the customer's tier), not the tier it followed (Tiered Pricing S12-R19).
* S1-E8: Credits follow the sales-report credits rule. Only customer credit memos with part lines count: Part Sale returns and other credits for parts. Overpayments and whole-invoice discounts carried over to another invoice are not in these reports. A credit line that names a part line counts at the amount credited after any restocking fee, before tax, on the date the credit was created, applied or not. Void credits and vendor credits never count. A credit line is in the row and category of the part line it credits, at the original invoiced price (Tiered Pricing S11-N1), under the credit's customer and location; a credit on a line priced before release is in Before tiers. A credit with no part lines, such as one created on the customer page for goodwill, an overpayment, a fee or a write-off, is not in these reports: nothing says which part it was for. It still shows in Sales and Sales by Customer. A credit line that names no part line on a credit that has part lines is not in these reports either. A credit never changes the original invoice or a past month.
* S1-E9: Warranty parts are typed to $0 and are in Manual. A part with no cost is in the customer's tier at $0.
* Error: a failed load shows the report error state of Sales by Customer, keeping the filters.

**Story 2: Open a row to its customers and invoices**

* S2-R1: Each report opens with every row closed.
* S2-R2: Opening a row lists the invoices' and credits' customers for the lines in that row, with the same columns for that customer's lines in the row only. A customer priced by two tiers shows under both. Invoices with no customer are under "No Company", as on Sales by Customer.
* S2-R3: Opening a customer lists their invoices and credits with the row's lines on them, each with its date and the same columns for those lines only. An invoice or credit number links to it (the work order's Finance tab or the Part Sale; a credit opens its PDF in a new tab) only when it belongs to the signed-in location and the user may open it, as on Sales by Customer; otherwise the number is plain text.
* S2-R4: Each level adds up to the row above it.
* S2-R5: An invoice is always listed under its own customer, with its own number and date, as on Sales by Customer. The only other customer a line may name is the one it was priced for, in the expanded download (S1-E1).
* S2-N1: An invoice or credit from another location, or one the user may not open, shows its number as plain text (S2-R3).
* S2-E1: A customer deleted after their invoices were made still shows under their name as recorded on the invoice, as on Sales by Customer.
* Error: if a row's customers or invoices fail to load, the row shows "Ooooops! An error occurred" with Try again.

**Story 3: Filter and sort**

* S3-R1: Date range: the first chip; required, this month by default, one year at most, as on Sales by Customer.
* S3-R3: Priced By: lists every tier (archived ones marked "(archived)"), then Fixed price, Fixed line total, Manual and Before tiers. All selected by default.
* S3-R9: Category: lists every category with Uncategorized first, then A to Z. All selected by default. Both reports have it.
* S3-R4: Customer: search for one or more customers, as on Sales by Customer. Empty means all.
* S3-R5: Sales Type: two options, "Part Sales" (Part Sale invoices and their credits) and "Work Orders" (work order invoices, including their parts), both selected by default. The closed filter reads "Sales Type: All", the one option picked, or "Sales Type: None". A customer can be on different tiers for each (Tiered Pricing S9-R1). The same two names are used wherever these reports name an invoice's type.
* S3-R2: Location: the last chip, per the report standard. Multi-select, opens on the signed-in location (or all accessible locations if the user can't access that one), hidden for users with one location, and carried over when the user switches location. A Location column shows only when more than one location is in scope. A location the user can't access is refused with "Unknown location". Tiers apply to the whole client, so this shows a location's sales by tier.
* S3-R10: Opening a filter's dropdown puts the cursor in its search box.
* S3-R12: A filter chip shows as selected (blue) only when it holds a specific selection. Date range and Location always do, so they are always blue. Priced By, Category and Sales Type are plain when all are selected; every other filter is plain when nothing is picked.
* S3-R6: Filters are kept in the page address, so back, reload, a link or a bookmark reopens the same view. A new visit starts from the defaults.
* S3-R11: Switching between the two reports carries the shared filters (date range, Location, Sales Type, Customer, Category, Priced By) in the page address, closes open rows and resets the sort.
* S3-R7: On first load, Sales by Tier's rows are in tier number order (Default first, then active tiers, then archived tiers in the order they were archived), then Fixed price, Fixed line total, Manual and Before tiers.
* S3-R8: Sorting is done on the server over the whole filtered result, and every column sorts. The first click on text sorts A to Z and on a number column biggest first; the second click reverses; the third returns to the first-load order (S3-R7). Parts Credits are negative, so the first click puts the largest credit on top. Ties break by the S3-R7 order then by name for rows, by name for customers, and by number, smallest first, for invoices and credits. Customers and invoices sort the same way within their row. Downloads use the same order.
* S3-N1: Filters that match nothing, including no Sales Type selected, show "No record matches these filters", "Try changing filters to widen your results." and a "Clear All Filters" button, and no Totals row.
* S3-E1: A filter search with no match shows no options and keeps the current picks.

**Story 4: Download**

* S4-R1: The ⋯ menu offers four downloads for each report: "Download Summary (PDF)", "Download Expanded View (PDF)", "Download Summary (CSV)" and "Download Expanded View (CSV)". They are built on the server; CSVs go through the shared CSV-injection guard.
* S4-R2: Summary has the rows and the Totals. Expanded has every level (rows, then the levels under them, down to invoices and credits) whatever is open on screen.
* S4-R3: Both use the current filters, sort and shown columns, and show Priced By in plain text, with tier numbers and "(archived)" as on screen (S1-R11).
* S4-R4: The expanded download names the customer a line was priced for when it differs from the invoice's customer (S1-E1), in a "Priced For" column.
* S4-R5: A PDF covers up to 2,000 rows and a CSV up to 50,000. Above that, the download is refused with "This report is too large to export. Choose a shorter date range, then try again."
* S4-R6: Download headers match Sales by Customer's downloads word for word where the column is the same: "Customer", "Invoice #", "Date", "Location", "Parts Invoiced", "Parts Margin", "Parts Margin %", and "Totals" on the Totals row. The added columns read "Priced By", "Category", "Priced For", "Parts Cost", "Parts Credits", "Parts Net" and "% of Total". Before the reports are built, engineering checks the headers against Sales by Customer's downloads as they are then.
* S4-R7: Every download starts with the report's title; "Date Range: <from> – <to>"; "Locations: All locations" or the location names; then one line each for Sales Type, Category, Priced By and Customer, written out even when set to all. The PDF shows the client's logo.
* S4-N1: A download over its limit is refused with the message in S4-R5, and no file is made.
* S4-N2: A download with no rows shows the existing "Empty export" notice, and no file is made.
* S4-E1: Negative amounts download as negative numbers (S1-R13).
* Error: if a download fails, today's error toast shows "Ooooops! An error occurred" with "Contact support for more information".

**Story 5: Sales by Category**

* S5-R1: Sales by Category has Sales by Tier's columns, filters, sorting, Totals, credits and downloads (Stories 1, 3 and 4).
* S5-R2: Rows are categories, by the category on each part line, under its current name. A category opens to its Priced By rows (Default, each tier, Fixed price, Fixed line total, Manual, Before tiers), each to customers, each to invoices and credits.
* S5-R3: % of Total is as in S1-R6.
* S5-R4: Only categories with sales or credits in the range show. Uncategorized sorts first on first load, then categories A to Z.
* S5-R5: A category with sales but no margin shows $0.00 Parts Margin; negative totals show as negatives (S1-R13).
* S5-R6: Inside a category, Priced By rows follow Sales by Tier's order (S3-R7); a renamed Default shows its current name (Tiered Pricing S4-R15).
* S5-N1: Empty results show the same two empty states as Sales by Tier (S1-N1, S3-N1).
* S5-E1: A line whose category was deleted reports under Uncategorized (Tiered Pricing S8-E1).
* S5-E2: Sales by Category's Totals equal Sales by Tier's for the same filters.
* S5-E3: A fixed line total that spans categories is split across them by the split the line recorded (Tiered Pricing S10-R13): in proportion to each part's cost at invoicing, in cents, with any leftover cent placed as Tiered Pricing S10-R13 sets out, so the parts always add up to the line's parts amount. Parts that all cost $0 split by quantity. Fixed line totals invoiced before release are split the same way when the report runs. The Category filter on Sales by Tier uses the same split.
* S5-E4: The report groups by category only; there is no matrix grouping.
* S5-E5: Lines invoiced before release stay under the category they were invoiced with, including customer-named categories. As clients stop switching categories after release, sales move to the parts' own categories. This is expected.
* Error: a failed load shows the report error state of Sales by Customer, keeping the filters.

## Out of Scope

* Contracts: parked with Tiered Pricing's contracts for a later release.
* Grouping by matrix: Sales by Category groups by category only.
* Labor, shop supplies and whole work order or Part Sale fees: Tiered Pricing prices parts only.
* Changes to Sales by Customer or Sales (Tiered Pricing S17-R3).
* A separate credits report.
* Dashboard widgets and charts: a table only.
* Period comparison (this quarter against last).
* A permission for these reports alone: reports have one permission today.
* Internal and no-charge work: these work orders close without an invoice; how they report belongs to the Internal Work Orders PRD.
