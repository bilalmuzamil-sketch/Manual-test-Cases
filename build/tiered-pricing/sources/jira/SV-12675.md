# SV-12675 Verify: pricing engine, matrix page in view, Price Part Simulator and below-cost warning (Phases 2 and 12)

## Description

**Covers:** TP-S3-R5, R6, R8, R9, R12, R13, R16, R18, R20, R21, R23, R25–R28, R30–R32, N3, N4, E3, E4; TP-S4-R1, R3–R5, R7, R10, R12, R15, R16, R18–R20, R22, R26–R32, N1, E3; TP-S5-R1–R6, N1; TP-S10-R1–R7, R11–R22, N1, N2, E2–E8 (browser engine); TP-S15-R2, R3, R5, N3–N5; TP-S19-R1, R3, R5–R10, R12–R15, R18–R23, E2; NFR-006; decisions D21, D22, D23, D29, D30

**Checklist**

1. As `admin`, open a matrix from the Matrices list: `/administration/pricing/matrices/<id>` shows the breadcrumb Settings › Pricing › Matrices › <matrix> and a loading skeleton first. Opened from a category it reads Settings › Categories › <category> › <matrix>; from a tier, Settings › Pricing › Tiers › <tier> › <matrix>; from a link or bookmark, the Matrices path (TP-S3-R26, TP-S19-R7).
2. Header (TP-S3-R18, TP-S3-R28, TP-S3-R13, TP-S3-N4): the title renames in place and saves when it loses focus; a blank name, one under 3 or over 127 characters, or a taken one shows the inline error and keeps the old name. Inactive shows as plain gray text beside the name. The Categories line has the info tooltip "A category uses one matrix. Adding one here moves it from its current matrix."; its picker has search and Select all, shows "Now: <matrix>" beside categories on another matrix, never lists Uncategorized, can't be used on the General Matrix (whose line reads "All categories without a matrix (n)"), and saves when it closes.
3. Grid in view (TP-S4-R1, TP-S4-R3, TP-S4-R27, TP-S4-R28): Cost Range (info tooltip "A part's cost picks its row. The first range starts at $0.00; the last has no upper limit."), Default, then each active tier by number with its number and name and no tooltip. Cells read "10% off", "$5 off", "Cost + $5.50", "35% markup" or "30% margin"; a column with mixed types reads "Mixed rules". Columns share the width between 128px and 176px, the grid is never wider than 1760px and scrolls sideways with Cost Range pinned, a long tier name is cut short with "…" and shows in full on hover, and a 1512-wide screen shows at least Default and 3 tiers.
4. "Uses previous tier" hover (TP-S4-R3, D29): "Uses previous tier, 2 · Wholesale."; when that tier also uses its previous tier, "Uses previous tier, 3 · Fleet. Priced by 2 · Wholesale's rule."; a chain that ends at Default names Default by its number and current name ("Priced by 1 · <Default's name>'s rule.").
5. Price Part Simulator (TP-S5-R1–R6, TP-S5-N1): under the grid on both tabs, titled "Price Part Simulator" with the info tooltip "Type a part cost to see each tier's price and profit. Nothing is saved." Part Cost starts at $100.00; its columns line up with the grid's; each shows the sell price and its profit ("$12.50 profit"), updating as you type; an empty, zero or non-numeric cost shows a field error and "—" under every column; a negative profit has a minus sign; nothing typed is saved. Tier and category pages have no simulator.
6. Exact math in the browser (D23, TP-S10-R5, TP-S10-R15, TP-S3-E3): in the simulator, 15% markup on $1.70 gives $1.96; $10.005 prices as $10.01 and $10.0049 as $10.00; 10% off a $10.005 Default price gives $9.01; a cost on a range boundary uses the range that contains it. For the same cost, category and tier, the simulator's price equals the price a work order line gets.
7. Below-cost warning (TP-S15-R2, TP-S15-R3, TP-S15-R5, TP-S15-N3–N5): in a $0.00 to $100.00 range where Default is Cost + $10 and Fleet is 20% off, Fleet's cell shows the warning icon right of the value with the tooltip "Fleet sells a $40.03 part below cost.", and the simulator at $40.03 shows the icon with "Below cost.". A price exactly at cost (0% markup, + $0.00) never warns, a "Uses previous tier" cell has no icon of its own, the icon has a screen reader name, and it never blocks saving.
8. Part Sale tab (TP-S3-R21, TP-S3-R27, TP-S3-R31): the "Separate Part Sale pricing" switch is off by default, with an info tooltip and no grid or Edit. Turning it on saves at once and copies the Work Order side, every tier column included. Turning it off asks "Turn Off Part Sale Pricing?" with "Turning off the separate Part Sale matrix will revert the pricing to the work order matrix." and Cancel / Turn Off; turned on again, it starts as a fresh copy.
9. Archived view (TP-S4-R19, D30): with two archived tiers, `?view=archived` shows Default, then the archived tiers in the order they were archived, earliest first, without numbers. A Same as previous tier column follows the column to its left (Default for the first archived tier); the simulator under it prices Default and the archived tiers by that chain, and the hover names the same chain. Active grids never show archived tiers.
10. The shared pieces from Phase 2 changed nothing elsewhere: the work order lines bulk action bar and the inventory bulk status bar look and work as today, and existing lists keep their own load error text. Evidence: the engine's vectors spec passes, and `npx vue-tsc --noEmit` and the compile gate are clean.
