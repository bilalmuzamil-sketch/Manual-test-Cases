# SV-11706 Tiered Pricing - Story 17 - Reports and totals stay consistent

## Description

## Summary

Every total and tax keeps calculating from the prices saved on the lines, so tiers never create a second version of the numbers. Parts sales and profit by what priced each line and by category are in the new Sales by Tier and Sales by Category reports, which have their own PRD and ship in the same release.

## Context

* Role: owner or manager with 'Reports — Page Access'. Desktop only (reports).
* What must be true first: lines record what priced them (Story 12). The report screens are in the Sales by Tier and Sales by Category PRD and its own story (SV-11718).

## Requirements

* S17-R1: Work order and Part Sale Stats, invoice totals and taxes calculate from the saved line prices, as today.
* S17-R2: Sales by Tier and Sales by Category, under Reports › Parts, show parts sales and profit by what priced each line and by category, read only what each line recorded, and ship in the same release. Rows, columns, filters, permission and exports are in their own PRD.
* S17-R5: Both reports show Invoiced, Credits and Net. Credits are customer credit memos only (Part Sale returns and credits made on the customer page), before tax, after any restocking fee, dated by the credit, and counted in the credited line's tier or category at the original invoiced price.
* S17-R3: Sales and Sales by Customer are unchanged.
* S17-N1: None: totals only read saved prices, so there is nothing to refuse.
* S17-E1: A customer Fees & Discounts entry applies at the end of the work order, after tier pricing, as today; the price details explain the unit price before that discount.

## Out of Scope

* The Sales by Tier and Sales by Category screens themselves (their own PRD and story).

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Given a work order with Default-, Fleet-, Fixed-, fixed-line-total- and Manual-priced lines, then Stats, the invoice total and taxes are calculated from the saved line prices.
- [ ] Given Sales and Sales by Customer run after release, then their rows, columns and totals are the same as before release for the same data.
- [ ] Given Reports › Parts, then Sales by Tier and Sales by Category are listed and read each line's recorded source and category.
- [ ] Given a $100.00 Fleet line invoiced and then credited at $40.00 with a $5.00 restocking fee, then Sales by Tier shows the credit of $35.00 under Fleet, before tax, dated by the credit, and Net is Invoiced less Credits.
- [ ] Given a customer with a 5% military discount on a Fleet work order, then the discount applies after the Fleet prices and the price details show the unit price before it.
