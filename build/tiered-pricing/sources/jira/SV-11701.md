# SV-11701 Tiered Pricing - Story 12 - Seeing what priced a part

## Description

## Summary

Every priced part line names what priced it in a Priced by column, the price itself opens the price details, and a gray icon marks a Manual price. The line keeps that record for good, so an owner can answer "why was this invoice priced like this?"

## Context

* Permission: 'See Financial Data'; the work order Parts tab also needs 'Work Orders — Order Parts', as today.
* Form factor: desktop and tablet; neither the Priced by column nor the price details shows on a phone in this release.
* What must be true first: lines priced by the lookup in Story 10.
* Nothing about the pricing source reaches the customer.

## Requirements

* S12-R1: On the work order Parts and Lines tabs and the Part Sale parts table, every sell price with a pricing source (Default, tier, fixed price, fixed line total, Manual, before tiers) opens its price details; no hover icon. The price is reachable by keyboard.
* S12-R10: A gray Manual icon shows beside a Manual price, and only there, with the screen reader name "Price typed by hand: see details". Customer changes, tier changes, fixed prices and fixed line totals show no icon.
* S12-R2: Hovering the price on desktop, tapping on a tablet, or Alt+Down in the price box opens the details (typing still edits). They show the source; tier name; matrix, side and rule (range and calculation); cost used; sell price; when it was priced; for % Off or $ Off, the Default price used; and where the part came from ("Inventory, bin <bin>" or "<vendor> - <vendor bill #>"; no line for a part with neither, such as a quick-added part with no vendor). Focus returns to the price when they close.
* S12-R18: For Same as previous tier, the details name the tier whose rule was used: "Fleet is the same as the previous tier, Wholesale, in Brakes."
* S12-R12: The details add: for a fixed line total, "Part of a fixed line total of $450.00 (parts $210.00)"; for a customer since moved, "Priced by Fleet, Acme Freight's tier when this work order was made. Acme Freight is now on Wholesale."; for a canned line's saved price on a Default work order, "Saved price from canned line <canned line>."; before release, "Priced before tiers" with what the line recorded.
* S12-R3: The work order Parts tab and Part Sale parts table get a Priced by column after Sell Price, naming the source in words with the Manual icon for Manual; hover or focus opens the details. The Lines tab opens details from part prices but has no Priced by column.
* S12-R4: When a part is priced, the line records what the details show, its source and the customer it was priced for; for Manual, who typed it.
* S12-R15: The line records the tier itself as well as its name, so a renamed tier groups under its current name in Sales by Tier.
* S12-R19: A Same as previous tier price records the work order's tier as its source and the tier whose rule was used as a detail; Priced by, Sales by Tier and the tier delete check use the work order's tier.
* S12-R17: A line kept through Change Customer says so: "Priced by Fleet for Acme Freight".
* S12-R5: Later changes to a tier, matrix, rule or category never change what a line recorded. After a rename, Priced by shows the tier's current name; the price details show the name when priced, plus the current one if it changed ("Fleet (now Fleet Plus)"); Default always shows its current name.
* S12-R6: Lines priced before release never show the Manual icon; details read "Priced before tiers"; reports show "Before tiers".
* S12-R7: Priced by and the price details show no part status, such as "Received". The Parts and Lines tables keep today's status.
* S12-R8: While a part is being added (Quick Add, inline row), no source shows; details and the Manual icon appear once saved.
* S12-R9: The Priced by column is read-only.
* S12-N2: A part with no cost opens details for the customer's tier at $0.00, never the Manual icon; a found part's typed price is Manual.
* S12-N3: Nothing about what priced a part shows on the invoice, estimate, printout, PDFs or the customer portal.
* S12-E1: On a tablet, a tap on the price opens the details and a tap elsewhere closes them.
* Error: if the details fail to load, the popover shows "Ooooops! An error occurred" with Try again.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Given Default-, Fleet-, Fixed-, fixed-line-total-, Manual- and before-tiers-priced lines on the Parts tab, then each price opens its details, Priced by names the source in words after Sell Price, and only the Manual line shows the gray Manual icon.
- [ ] Given the Manual icon, then a screen reader reads "Price typed by hand: see details".
- [ ] Given a desktop, when the price is hovered or Alt+Down is pressed in the price box, then the details open with source, tier, matrix, side, rule, cost, sell price, when priced and where the part came from; when closed, focus returns to the price; typing still edits the price.
- [ ] Given a Fleet 10% off line, then the details show the Default price it was taken from.
- [ ] Given an inventory part in bin A3, then the details show "Inventory, bin A3"; given a special-order part, then "<vendor> - <vendor bill #>".
- [ ] Given Fleet on Same as previous tier after Wholesale in Brakes, then the details say "Fleet is the same as the previous tier, Wholesale, in Brakes.", Priced by reads Fleet, and Sales by Tier counts it under Fleet.
- [ ] Given a fixed line total of $450.00 with parts $210.00, then the details say "Part of a fixed line total of $450.00 (parts $210.00)".
- [ ] Given Acme Freight moved from Fleet to Wholesale after the work order was made, then the details say "Priced by Fleet, Acme Freight's tier when this work order was made. Acme Freight is now on Wholesale." with no Manual icon.
- [ ] Given a line kept through Change Customer, then the details say "Priced by Fleet for Acme Freight" with no Manual icon.
- [ ] Given a canned line's saved price on a Default work order, then the details say "Saved price from canned line <canned line>."
- [ ] Given a line priced before release, then the details read "Priced before tiers" with what it recorded, no Manual icon, and reports show "Before tiers".
- [ ] Given a Manual price, then the line records who typed it.
- [ ] Given Fleet renamed Fleet Plus, or its matrix edited, archived or deleted, then the line's details still show what it recorded, and Sales by Tier groups it under Fleet Plus.
- [ ] Given the Lines tab, then part prices open their details and there is no Priced by column.
- [ ] Given a part being added in Quick Add or the inline row, then no source shows until it is saved.
- [ ] Given a received part, then no "Received" status shows in Priced by or the price details, and the Parts and Lines tables keep today's status.
- [ ] Given the Priced by cell, then clicking it doesn't reprice the line.
- [ ] Given a part with no cost on a Fleet work order or Part Sale, then the details name Fleet at $0.00 and no Manual icon shows.
- [ ] Given the invoice, estimate, printout, PDF and customer portal, then nothing about the pricing source shows.
- [ ] Given a tablet, when the price is tapped, then the details open; a tap elsewhere closes them; given a phone, then no Priced by column or details show.
- [ ] Given the details fail to load, then the popover shows "Ooooops! An error occurred" with Try again.
