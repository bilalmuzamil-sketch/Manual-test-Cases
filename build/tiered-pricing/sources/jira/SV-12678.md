# SV-12678 Verify: a tier's Customers tab and Manage Customers add and remove (Phase 15)

## Description

**Covers:** TP-S2-R6; TP-S9-R1–R5, R7–R12, R14–R24, R27, R30, R31, N1, N3, N6, E2, E3, E5–E7; TP-S16-N5, E1; NFR-010; decision D21

**Checklist**

1. Customers tab (TP-S9-R4, TP-S9-R5, TP-S9-R9): lists the tier's customers with their channels, 50 a page, with a search by customer name, a channel filter ("Work orders", "Part Sales", "Both"), customer name A to Z by default and a sortable channel column (ties broken by name); rows have no actions menu; search, filters, sort and page are kept on return.
2. Manage Customers (TP-S9-R4, TP-S9-R10, TP-S9-R17): titled "Manage Customers on <tier>", with Add and Remove tabs, each explained in a tooltip on its title. Add lists every customer not on the tier for at least one channel and Remove those on it, each with the count, and each row shows the customer's current work order and Part Sales tiers. Search matches the customer's name and their current tier names ("Fleet" finds the customers on Fleet). It loads 50 at a time as you scroll, and the tab count and Select all cover every match, loaded or not.
3. Picking (TP-S9-R18–R21, TP-S9-R24): ticking a customer shows the dark bulk bar above the search, holding "3 selected", Select All with the number of customers in the list (while not every customer is ticked), a Work Orders and a Part Sales checkbox, and the X that clears the selection. A channel checkbox there is checked when every ticked customer has that channel, mixed when only some do, and sets it on every ticked customer. A customer ticked later starts with both channels they aren't on yet. Each row has Work Orders and Part Sales chips, and a locked chip is grayed and shows only its name. Switching tabs clears the picks.
4. Footer and button (TP-S9-R23, TP-S9-N1, D21): the footer reads like "Adding 3 customers: 2 for both, 1 for Work Orders only."; the button reads "Add 3 Customers", or a red "Remove 2 Customers", which asks "Remove 2 Customers?" first (design 9.17–9.18). A ticked customer with no channel shows "Pick Work Orders, Part Sales or both.", and with nobody ticked the footer says "No customers picked yet."; the button stays off in both cases.
5. Skips (TP-S9-E5, TP-S9-E7): on Add, Select All with a channel checked leaves out customers already on the tier for it, and the footer says how many, for example "12 skipped: already on Fleet Platinum for Work Orders."; Remove leaves out customers not on it. Skipped customers never block Save.
6. Saving (TP-S9-E2, TP-S9-E6, TP-S9-N3, TP-S9-R22, TP-S9-R7): Select All over more than 50 customers (for example 1,284) saves with the spinner, then the confirmation toast. A failure saves nothing and keeps the picks; closing without saving changes nothing. Adding a customer for a channel takes them off their old tier for it, and removing puts them back on Default. The dialog says "Existing work orders and Part Sales keep their prices and tier.", and their open work orders keep their tier.
7. Permissions (TP-S16-N5, TP-S16-E1): with 'Settings — Parts' and 'See Financial Data' but not 'Customers — Create & Edit', the Customers tab is read-only and Manage Customers is hidden; without 'Customers — View', there is no Customers tab.
8. Empty states (TP-S9-N6): a search with no match, on the Customers tab or in Manage Customers, reads 'No customer matches "…".'; the Add tab when every customer is already on the tier reads "Every customer is already on Fleet Platinum."; the Remove tab of an empty tier reads "No customer is on Fleet Platinum yet." (each with the tier's own name).
9. Archived tier (TP-S2-R6, D21): its Customers tab has no Manage Customers and shows Reactivate Tier, which applies at once with no confirmation (design 9.21–9.23).
