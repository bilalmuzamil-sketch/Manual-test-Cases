# SV-12673 Verify: customer tier API, Manage Customers bulk saves and the pricing Audit Log reads (Phase 10)

## Description

**Covers:** TP-S9-R1–R5, R7–R12, R14–R24, R27, R30, R31, N1, N3, N6, E2, E3, E5–E7; TP-S16-R2, R4–R6, R8, R11, N1, N4, N5, E1; TP-S19-R1, R3, R5–R10, R12–R15, R18–R23, E2; NFR-002, NFR-010; decisions D7, D16, D35

**Checklist**

1. Customer tiers through the customer API (TP-S9-R1, TP-S16-R4): `POST /api/customers/create` and `POST /api/customers/change` with `workOrderPricingTierId` or `partSalesPricingTierId` need only 'Customers — Create & Edit', not 'Settings — Parts'; a missing key leaves that tier unchanged; an archived tier or another client's tier gives 422; changing one channel never changes the other.
2. Pricing summary (TP-S9-R12, TP-S9-R15): `GET /api/customers/{companyId}/pricing-summary` needs 'Customers — View' and 'View and Manage AP/AR Data'. `tax` is `{taxId, name}` with the tax's name exactly as stored, or both null when no tax is picked or the picked tax no longer exists; another client's tax is never returned. The labor rate is the rate at the user's current location, with `variesByLocation`; the shop supplies `kind` is `none`, `own` or `locationDefault`.
3. Tier customers (TP-S9-R9, TP-S9-R10, TP-S16-N5, TP-S16-E1): a tier's customer list needs 'Customers — View'; the candidates and assignment endpoints also need 'Customers — Create & Edit' on top of 'Settings — Parts' and 'See Financial Data' (403 otherwise). Add candidates are every customer not on the tier for at least one channel and Remove candidates those on it, with the count, each row with the customer's current work order and Part Sales tiers. Candidate search matches the customer's name and their current tier names ("Fleet" finds the customers on Fleet), and `meta` gives the counts the skip messages need. The tier's customer list searches by customer name, filters by channel, sorts by name A to Z by default with a sortable channel column (ties broken by name), and gives 50 a page. The usage endpoint names customers only for users with 'Customers — View'.
4. Bulk assignments (TP-S9-R22, TP-S9-E5–E7, NFR-010): Select All over more than one page (for example 1,284 customers) is applied on the server; customers already on the tier (add) or not on it (remove) for the picked channels are skipped and counted; remove puts them back on Default; one foreign customer id, or any other failure, saves nothing; an archived tier is refused.
5. Customer audit (TP-S19-R20, TP-S19-R23, D35): from Edit Customer and from Manage Customers, "Customer Added" when a customer joins the tier on a first channel, "Customer Changed" when they stay on it but their channels change, and "Customer Removed" when they leave its last channel. A bulk save makes one entry on the tier's log ("3 customers added, 1 removed"), and the tier they left gets its own entry. Each entry records what changed, who made it and when (TP-S19-R8).
6. Audit Log reads (TP-S19-R19, TP-S19-R21): `GET /api/pricing/{matrices|tiers|categories}/audit-log` and `…/{id}/audit-log` need 'Settings — Parts' and 'See Financial Data'. They return 50 entries a page, newest first, searchable, with deleted entities included in the list logs and each change as label, old and new value. Tier events name the customers ("Deleted customer" for one that no longer exists). Another client's entries never appear.
7. `bin/smoke-test.sh` shows no 500s, and the backend log is clean.
