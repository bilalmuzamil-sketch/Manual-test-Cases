# SV-11707 Tiered Pricing - Story 18 - Accounting and QuickBooks receive the right figures

## Description

## Summary

Invoices reach QuickBooks and AccountingHub with each part's sell price exactly as charged, whatever priced it, with no change to what is sent. The books keep matching the invoices.

## Context

* Applies when the QuickBooks integration or ShopView accounting is set up for the client. No new screens.
* Trigger: when an invoice is created or synced from a work order or Part Sale, as today. Source: the line prices from Story 10 and what each line records (Story 12).
* Matched pair: AccountingHub's side lives in shopview-accounting; check both sides.

## Requirements

* S18-R1: Each part line is sent with its sell price exactly as on the invoice, whatever priced it. The shape is unchanged: no new fields, no pricing source or details. An invoice re-synced after a tier or matrix change sends the prices on its lines.
* S18-R3: QuickBooks and AccountingHub book part revenue by the category on the work order or Part Sale line, as today. Credits and refunds use the inventory part's current category, as today.
* S18-R5: An invoice already synced isn't sent again because a deleted category's lines moved to Uncategorized; re-synced later, its part revenue books to Uncategorized's QuickBooks item.
* S18-R6: Deleting a category is sent to AccountingHub the way category changes are today; an invoice already sent isn't sent again because its lines moved.
* S18-R4: The Open API is unchanged: part lines return their sell price as on the invoice; tiers, matrices and pricing source are neither exposed nor settable.
* S18-N1: With no integration connected, nothing is sent, as today.
* S18-N2: Internal and no-charge work orders are sent as the Internal Work Orders PRD specifies; tiers add nothing.
* Error: sync failures behave and read exactly as today.

## Out of Scope

* Tiers, matrices and the pricing source in the Open API, QuickBooks or AccountingHub.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Given an invoice with Fleet-, Default-, Fixed-, fixed-line-total- and Manual-priced part lines, when it syncs to QuickBooks and AccountingHub, then each part line's sell price matches the invoice and the payload has no new fields.
- [ ] Given a synced invoice, when a matrix or tier changes and the invoice is re-synced, then the same prices are sent.
- [ ] Given a part whose catalog category is Brakes on a line moved to Tires, then QuickBooks and AccountingHub book its revenue to Brakes.
- [ ] Given a category deleted after its invoice synced, then the invoice isn't sent again; when re-synced later, then its part revenue books to Uncategorized's QuickBooks item.
- [ ] Given a category deleted, then AccountingHub receives the delete as it receives category changes today.
- [ ] Given the Open API, then part lines return the invoice sell price and no tier, matrix or source field, and none can be set.
- [ ] Given no integration connected, then nothing is sent.
- [ ] Given an internal work order, then it is sent as the Internal Work Orders PRD specifies, with nothing added by tiers.
- [ ] Given a sync failure, then it behaves and reads as today.
