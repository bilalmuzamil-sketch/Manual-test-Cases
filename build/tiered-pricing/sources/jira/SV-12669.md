# SV-12669 Verify: Priced by column, price details, Manual icon, tier-aware add paths and the receive notice (Phase 6)

## Description

**Covers:** TP-S9-R27; TP-S10-R1, R14, R17, R19, R20, N1; TP-S11-R1–R10, R12, R14–R16, N1, N2, E2, E5–E8; TP-S12-R1–R10, R12, R15, R17–R19, N2, N3, E1; TP-S13-R1; TP-S16-N4; TP-S19-R5; NFR-003, NFR-011; decisions D19, D21, D32, D34

**Checklist**

1. As `admin` on `/work-orders/<id>` (Parts tab, in both of its display modes) and on a Part Sale's parts table, a Priced by column sits right after Sell Price and names the source in words: the tier's current name, Default's current name, "Manual" with the gray icon, "Fixed price", "Fixed line total" or "Before tiers". Header and body cells line up, the column is hidden at phone width, and the tables keep today's status column. The Lines tab has no Priced by column, but its part prices open the details (TP-S12-R3, TP-S12-R7).
2. Price details (TP-S12-R1, TP-S12-R2, TP-S12-E1): hovering a price on desktop, or tapping it on a tablet, opens the details; with the cursor in the price box, Alt+Down opens them while typing still edits the price; focus returns to the price when they close, and a tap elsewhere closes them. They list the pricing source; the tier name; the matrix, its side (Work Order Matrix or Part Sale Matrix) and the rule (cost range and calculation); the cost used; the sell price; when it was priced; for % Off or $ Off, the Default price; and where the part came from, "Inventory, bin <bin>" or "<vendor> - <vendor bill #>", with no line when neither applies. They show the popover spinner while loading, and a failed load shows "Ooooops! An error occurred" with Try again.
3. Wording (TP-S12-R5, TP-S12-R6, TP-S12-R12, TP-S12-R17, TP-S12-R18): a renamed tier reads "Fleet (now Fleet Plus)" and Default only its current name; Same as previous tier reads "Fleet is the same as the previous tier, Wholesale, in Brakes."; a moved customer reads "Priced by Fleet, Acme Freight's tier when this work order was made. Acme Freight is now on Wholesale."; a fixed total reads "Part of a fixed line total of $450.00 (parts $210.00)"; a canned line's saved price reads "Saved price from canned line <canned line>."; a pre-release line reads "Priced before tiers" and never shows the Manual icon.
4. Manual icon (TP-S12-R8, TP-S12-R10, TP-S11-R5, TP-S19-R5): typing a different price shows the gray Manual icon beside it (screen reader name "Price typed by hand: see details") and "Manual" in Priced by; typing the same price doesn't; clearing the price restores the calculated one. While a part is being added (inline row, Quick Add) no source shows; it appears once the part is saved. A customer change, a tier change, a fixed price and a fixed line total show no icon.
5. Add paths (NFR-011, D32): for a customer on a tier, the inline row's prefilled price is the tier price, and saving without touching it records the tier, not Manual. With no category picked, a typed part number that matches a catalog part suggests the same price the save records.
6. Cost edits from the screens (D19, TP-S11-R12): in the part dialog, change only the quantity or description of a Manual line: the unit price and Manual stay. Retype the same cost on a special-order part not yet received, in the part dialog, the inline row, a Parts tab cost cell and a Part Sale parts table cost cell: each reprices; tabbing through the cost field does nothing. A category change on a staged or received part shows the new price and Priced by from the response.
7. A cost emptied or set to $0.00 (TP-S10-N1): in the part dialog and the inline row the sell price shows $0.00 straight away, matching what the save records; a fixed-price part keeps its fixed price.
8. Without 'See Financial Data' (TP-S10-N2, TP-S16-N4, D34): as a role without it (and as `tech`, if that role lacks it), the Parts tab has no Priced by column, no price details and no Manual icon, and a technician can still add parts, which are priced by the tier. In the inventory part dialog, changing a part's category sends no `calculate-sell-price` request and never lands on the Access Denied page.
9. Receiving (TP-S11-R15, TP-S11-R9): the work order receive modal, the purchase order receive page and the multi-vendor receive page all read "Updating the cost also updates the sell price."; on a purchase order receive, a tier-priced line at a new cost previews the tier's new price, and a Default line previews the kept margin.
10. Nothing about what priced a part shows on the customer's invoice, the estimate, the work order printout, a PDF or the customer portal (TP-S12-N3). The browser console shows no red errors during this walk.
