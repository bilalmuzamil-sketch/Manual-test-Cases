# SV-12667 Verify: every add, edit, receive, split and Change Customer path prices by the work order's tier (Phase 4)

## Description

**Covers:** TP-S1-R3; TP-S10-R14, R17, N1; TP-S11-R1–R10, R12, R14–R16, N1, N2, E2, E5–E8; TP-S13-R1, E1; TP-S14-R2–R5, N1, E1; NFR-011; decisions D19, D20, D32

**Checklist**

1. Kept tier (TP-S10-R14, TP-S13-R1): as `admin`, put a customer on Fleet for work orders and on another tier for Part Sales. A new work order prices added parts by Fleet; a work order opened before the move keeps its tier, and one open on release day keeps Default, for parts added later too. A Part Sale prices by the Part Sales tier, from the matrix's Part Sale side when it is on, else its Work Order side. A reopened paid work order takes the customer's current tier. A work order that keeps a tier archived later still prices new parts by it.
2. Every add path (TP-S11-R7, NFR-011): the inline row, catalog search, Quick Add, the part request modal, Tech View, the phone part request, inspections and ShopCoach all price by the work order's tier once the part has a cost. The add row's prefill is already the tier price (the picker takes `workOrderId`; another client's `workOrderId` gives 404), and an untouched prefill is never saved as Manual (`sellPriceTyped: false`; an absent flag keeps today's meaning). A typed part number that matches a catalog part is priced by that part's own category, and `calculate-sell-price?partNumber=` gives the same price (TP-S10-R17, D32).
3. Canned lines, including those added by Maintenance Reminders (TP-S11-R7, TP-S11-E8): on a tier work order every part, inventory, catalog or special-order, is priced by the tier. On a Default work order a catalog or special-order part comes in at the canned line's saved price and records Default with the canned line's name. A part with no cost keeps the saved price, records the work order's tier, and is priced by it once its cost comes in or it is received. The canned line's labor isn't repriced.
4. Typed prices (TP-S11-R5, TP-S11-E2, TP-S11-E5, TP-S11-E7): a typed price or margin that differs from the calculated one makes the line Manual, the same price doesn't, and clearing the price restores the fixed or calculated one. A typed $0.00 (warranty) or negative price is Manual; Quick Add still refuses a negative one with "Sell price cannot be negative."; a found part's typed price is Manual.
5. Cost edits (TP-S11-R2, TP-S11-R4, TP-S11-R12, TP-S11-R16, TP-S10-N1, D19): `change-request` with `costTyped: false` and the stored cost changes nothing, on a Manual line and on a tier line whose rule changed since; `costTyped: true` with the same cost on a special-order part not yet received reprices it; a quantity-only save keeps the unit price and a Manual price; a different cost reprices and replaces a Manual price; a fixed price is kept. A received work order part's cost can't be re-entered and doesn't reprice. A cost edited to $0.00 or emptied sets the sell price to $0.00 with no + $, recorded under the work order's tier (Default included).
6. Category change (TP-S11-R3, TP-S11-N1, TP-S11-N2): change the category of a received, tier-priced part on an open work order. It is repriced at the cost on the line by the new category's matrix at the work order's tier, its record is updated, and the work order parts minted from it carry the new price. A Manual price is replaced; a fixed price, a fixed line total and a no-cost canned part's saved price are kept. As a user without 'See Financial Data' the reprice still happens and the response is masked. On an invoiced work order the price doesn't change, and a credit on that invoice uses the invoiced price.
7. Receiving at a new cost (TP-S11-R9, TP-S11-R10): a tier-priced special-order part is repriced by the tier and customer recorded on the line, even after the customer moved to another tier and even if that tier is archived; a Default, before-tiers or Manual line keeps its margin ($2.00 → $3.00 on a $4.00 part becomes $6.00); a fixed price or fixed line total doesn't change. A cost changed from a vendor invoice or an order item updates the cost but not the price. On PO receive, a sell price the browser sends counts only when it is flagged as typed.
8. Split (TP-S11-E6): move a customer to another tier, then split their work order and a Part Sale. The new one takes the customer's current tier; every moved part, received or not, is repriced at its line's cost; Manual, fixed price, fixed line total and canned saved prices are kept; lines left behind keep their prices. Moving parts between Part Sales reprices nothing.
9. Change Customer API (TP-S14-R2, TP-S14-R3, TP-S14-N1, D20): changing the customer reprices nothing. The response has `linesPricedForPreviousCustomer {count, tierName}`, counting only tier and Default lines recorded for the previous customer (Manual, Fixed price, Fixed line total and Before tiers lines don't count), with `tierName` only when one tier priced them all; the key is absent for a user without 'See Financial Data'. Parts added afterwards price by the work order's tier. Deposits still block the change.
10. Nothing else reprices (TP-S11-R6, TP-S11-N1, TP-S11-R14): parts already on a work order or Part Sale keep their prices through changes to tiers, matrices, rules or categories' matrices, Set Rule on a tier's page, Change All, the Part Sale switch and deleting a matrix, moving the customer to another tier, and changing the customer. An invoiced work order or Part Sale never reprices, reopening it keeps its prices, and approving an estimate reprices nothing. Walk the plan's regression list: Tech View add, part dialog create and edit, inspection and ShopCoach builds, the three receive surfaces (first-cost derive, kept margin), PO receive, canned lines including Maintenance, split work order and Part Sale, and Change Customer on both. `bin/smoke-test.sh` shows no 500s.
