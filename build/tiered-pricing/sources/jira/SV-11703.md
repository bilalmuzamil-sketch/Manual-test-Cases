# SV-11703 Tiered Pricing - Story 14 - Changing the customer on a work order

## Description

## Summary

Changing the customer on a work order or Part Sale keeps its parts' prices and its tier, and tells the advisor how many lines were priced for the previous customer. Advisors fix those lines on purpose, or split the work order to use the new customer's tier.

## Context

* Role: service advisor allowed to change the customer, as today. Follows today's Change Customer form factor.
* What must be true first: the work order isn't invoiced and has no deposits. Lines record the customer they were priced for (Story 12).

## Requirements

* S14-R2: Changing the customer never reprices parts. Afterwards a notice says how many lines were priced for the previous customer ("3 lines priced at Default for the previous customer"), counting tier- and Default-priced lines whose recorded customer is the previous one (not Manual, Fixed price, Fixed line total or Before tiers). With more than one tier among them: "5 lines priced for the previous customer". Users without 'See Financial Data' don't see the notice. Those lines show no Manual icon; their details name the customer they were priced for.
* S14-R3: Changing the customer changes who is invoiced, not the work order's tier; new parts are priced by the work order's tier. To use the new customer's tier, the advisor splits the lines onto a new work order.
* S14-R4: The Change Customer dialog keeps today's grey caption and adds "Parts already on the work order keep their prices, and new parts keep this work order's pricing." (on a Part Sale: "Parts already on the Part Sale keep their prices, and new parts keep this Part Sale's pricing.").
* S14-R5: When the new customer's tier differs from the work order's, an amber note names both: "This work order keeps Default pricing. Fleet Plus applies to new work orders, or split this one to use it." (Part Sale: "This Part Sale keeps Default pricing. Fleet Plus applies to new Part Sales, or split this one to use it."). Red is not used.
* S14-N1: Changing the customer is blocked while deposits exist, as today.
* S14-E1: Changing to a customer on the same tier shows no amber note; lines still record the customer they were priced for.
* Error: Change Customer's failures read exactly as today.

## Out of Scope

* Showing the work order's tier on the work order, and moving its parts to the customer's current tier in bulk (a fast follow).

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Given a work order with 3 Default-priced lines, 1 Manual line and 1 Fixed price line for the previous customer, when the customer is changed, then no price changes and the notice reads "3 lines priced at Default for the previous customer".
- [ ] Given the counted lines were priced by Default and Fleet, then the notice reads "5 lines priced for the previous customer" with no tier name.
- [ ] Given those lines, then they show no Manual icon and their details name the customer they were priced for.
- [ ] Given a Default work order changed to a Fleet Plus customer, when a part is added, then Default prices it; when the lines are split onto a new work order, then Fleet Plus prices them.
- [ ] Given the Change Customer dialog on a work order, then today's grey caption shows with "Parts already on the work order keep their prices, and new parts keep this work order's pricing."; on a Part Sale, the Part Sale wording.
- [ ] Given a new customer on Fleet Plus and a Default work order, then the amber note reads "This work order keeps Default pricing. Fleet Plus applies to new work orders, or split this one to use it."; on a Part Sale, the Part Sale wording; no red is used.
- [ ] Given a new customer on the same tier as the work order, then no amber note shows.
- [ ] Given a work order with deposits, then Change Customer is blocked, as today.
- [ ] Given Change Customer fails, then the error reads as today.
