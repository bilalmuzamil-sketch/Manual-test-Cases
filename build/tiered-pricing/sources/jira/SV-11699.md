# SV-11699 Tiered Pricing - Story 10 - How a part is priced

## Description

## Summary

Every part is priced by one lookup: a typed price, then a fixed price on the part, then the work order's tier in the matrix of the part's category. The same part costs the same customer the same thing every time, and the line records what priced it. This is the price precedence engine the sell-price clean-up (SV-11165) also uses.

## Context

* Applies to any part on a work order or Part Sale with a customer, once the part has a cost above $0.00. Every user who adds parts; prices and details show only with 'See Financial Data'.
* Form factor: the phone part request prices parts by the same order, with no Priced by column or price details.
* What must be true first: matrices with tier columns (Stories 3 and 4) and customers on tiers (Story 9).
* Covers SV-11165, SV-4387, SV-7160, SV-5744 and SV-9414.

## Requirements

* S10-R1: Unless the line has a fixed line total, a part is priced by the first that applies: (1) a sell price or margin typed on the line by a user allowed to change pricing — Manual; (2) a fixed sell price on the part — Fixed price; (3) the work order's tier in the matrix of the part's category — Tier, or Default. Every way a part reaches a line uses this order, and the line records what priced it.
* S10-R17: The part's category gives the matrix: its own, or the General Matrix when it has none. A part number typed on the add row that matches a catalog part is priced by that part's own category, the same as picking it.
* S10-R18: On a work order the Work Order Matrix side prices; on a Part Sale the Part Sale side when on, otherwise the work order side. The General Matrix too.
* S10-R19: A customer on Default pays that side's Default column.
* S10-R20: A customer on another tier pays that tier's column: From cost by its own rule; % Off or $ Off off Default's price on that side for the part's cost range ($ Off never below $0.00); Same as previous tier exactly what the tier before it pays, chained down to Default.
* S10-R21: A tier never borrows from another matrix: a Same as previous tier column in a category's matrix is priced from that matrix, even if the General Matrix differs.
* S10-R2: Default always prices a part that has a cost, so every tier always has a price. Fees & Discounts apply after, as today.
* S10-R3: The range containing the cost applies; a cost above the last range's start uses the last rule (SV-4409). Every cost falls in exactly one range.
* S10-R4: Markup and margin use today's math (cost × (1 + markup); cost ÷ (1 − margin)); + $ is cost plus the amount per unit; % Off is the Default price at that moment on the same side less the percent; $ Off that Default price less the amount, floored at $0.00.
* S10-R5: Sell prices are rounded to the cent, half away from zero, exactly, so the simulator, grid, line prices and reports always agree; the rare half-cent ties today's math rounds down are one cent higher from release day. Every price shown is rounded.
* S10-R15: % Off rounds the Default price to the cent first, then discounts and rounds again ($10.005 shows $10.01; 10% off gives $9.01).
* S10-R6: + $ applies per unit, in plain amounts, with no currency conversion; line total is unit price × quantity.
* S10-R7: The cost used is the line's cost per unit.
* S10-R11: A customer's tiers price the same at every location.
* S10-R16: Per-location settings are unchanged: a fixed sell price belongs to the location's inventory part, so one customer can get a fixed price at one location and their tier price at another. Fees & Discounts and labor rates follow the location.
* S10-R12: Special-order and catalog parts follow the same order; they can't carry a fixed price. With no cost, they are priced when the cost is entered or the part is received.
* S10-R13: A part on a fixed-line-total line keeps its own sell price; the customer pays the fixed total. The line stores the parts amount (total less labor), its source is Fixed line total, and it records each part's share split by cost at invoicing, in cents (by quantity when every part costs $0.00). Any leftover cent goes on a part with a quantity of 1, or on the line's labor when there is none, as today.
* S10-R14: A work order or Part Sale keeps the tier its customer had for that channel when it was made, even if that tier is archived later; every part added is priced by that tier's current rules. One open on release day keeps Default. Moving the customer affects only work orders and Part Sales made afterwards or by splitting. Changing the customer doesn't change its tier.
* S10-R22: Typing a cost on a work order part line fills the sell price as quickly as today (about 0.3 seconds) at about 270 categories, 10 tiers and 20 cost ranges. Settings actions have no speed target.
* S10-N1: A part with no cost (empty, $0.00 or below) sells at $0.00 with no + $ added, and is logged under the work order's tier at $0.00. This also applies when the cost of a part already on a line is edited to $0.00 (today that keeps the old sell price). It is priced once its cost is entered (canned lines are the exception, Story 11).
* S10-N2: Without 'See Financial Data', the price, margin, Manual icon, Priced by column and details are hidden, as prices are today.
* S10-E2: A rule that gives a price below cost stands; the margin shows negative and no approval is added.
* S10-E3: If a matrix is saved while a part is being priced, the part uses the configuration saved at that moment.
* S10-E4: Labor fee and diagnostic lines are not priced by tiers.
* S10-E7: A fee entered as a part is a part like any other: a fixed sell price keeps it fixed, otherwise the tier prices it by its category. No outreach is planned for fee parts without a fixed price.
* S10-E5: An internal customer is priced like any other; a cost + 0% or + $0 tier sells at cost and shows that tier as the source. No separate internal price path (SV-9414).
* S10-E6: On a fixed-total line the part price can't be typed; cost changes and tiers never change the fixed total or its parts amount.
* S10-E8: Same as previous tier skips archived tiers; tier numbers never repeat, so a chain always ends at Default.

## Out of Scope

* Changing the cost a matrix prices from (SV-6607), contracts and per-customer part prices, and a cost floor (see PRD Out of Scope).

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Given a part with a fixed sell price on a Fleet work order, when added, then the fixed price applies and the source is Fixed price; when a sell price is typed that differs, then the typed price applies and the source is Manual.
- [ ] Given a part with no fixed price in Brakes (on Brake Plus) on a Fleet work order, then Brake Plus's Fleet column on the Work Order Matrix side prices it and the source names Fleet.
- [ ] Given a category with no matrix of its own, then the General Matrix prices its parts.
- [ ] Given a Part Sale and a matrix whose Part Sale side is on, then the Part Sale side prices it; with the side off, the Work Order Matrix side does.
- [ ] Given a Default customer, then the Default column prices the part and the source is Default.
- [ ] Given Default is 35% markup and Fleet 10% off, when a $100.00 part is priced for Fleet, then it sells at $121.50; given Fleet $5 off, then $130.00; given Fleet $200 off, then $0.00.
- [ ] Given Fleet + $5 on a $20.00 part, then $25.00 per unit, and the line total for 3 is $75.00.
- [ ] Given Fleet 30% margin on a $10.00 part, then $14.29; given 30% markup, then $13.00.
- [ ] Given Wholesale on Same as previous tier after Fleet, then Wholesale pays exactly Fleet's price in the same matrix and side; given both on Same as previous tier, then Default's price.
- [ ] Given Fleet on Same as previous tier in Brake Plus while the General Matrix gives Fleet 10% off, then a Brakes part for Fleet is priced from Brake Plus only.
- [ ] Given a Default price that computes to $10.005 and Fleet 10% off, then Default shows $10.01 and Fleet $9.01.
- [ ] Given a cost on a range boundary, then the range containing it prices it; a cost in the last open range uses the last rule.
- [ ] Given a customer on Fleet, when parts are added at two locations, then Fleet prices both, except a part with a fixed sell price at one location.
- [ ] Given a special-order part with no cost, when its cost is entered, then the tier prices it.
- [ ] Given a fixed-line-total line of $450.00 with $240.00 labor and two parts costing $30.00 and $10.00, then the parts amount is $210.00, the source is Fixed line total, and the shares recorded are $157.50 and $52.50; tier changes and cost changes never change the total.
- [ ] Given a work order made while the customer was on Fleet, when the customer moves to Wholesale and a part is added, then Fleet prices it; given Fleet archived afterwards, then Fleet still prices parts added to it.
- [ ] Given a work order open on release day for a customer later put on Fleet, then parts added to it are priced by Default.
- [ ] Given the customer is changed on a Fleet work order to a Wholesale customer, then new parts are still priced by Fleet.
- [ ] Given the largest client's size (270 categories, 10 tiers, 20 ranges), when a cost is typed on a work order part line, then the sell price fills in about 0.3 seconds, as today.
- [ ] Given a part with no cost on a Fleet work order, then it sells at $0.00, no + $ is added, and Priced by names Fleet; once its cost is entered, Fleet prices it.
- [ ] Given a user without 'See Financial Data', then the price, margin, Manual icon, Priced by and details are hidden.
- [ ] Given Fleet 60% off on a $10.00 part whose Default price is $15.00, then it sells at $6.00 with a negative margin and no approval step.
- [ ] Given a labor service-call line, then no tier prices it; given a fuel surcharge part with no fixed price, then the customer's tier prices it by its category.
- [ ] Given an internal customer on a tier of cost + 0% markup, then parts sell at cost and Priced by names that tier.
- [ ] Given Fleet archived between Default and Wholesale on Same as previous tier, then Wholesale follows Default.
