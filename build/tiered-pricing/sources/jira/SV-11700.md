# SV-11700 Tiered Pricing - Story 11 - When a part is priced again

## Description

## Summary

Only a cost or category change, receiving at a new cost, replacing the part, or splitting it onto a new work order or Part Sale prices a part again. Changes to tiers, matrices or the customer never move a price that is already on a line, so service advisors can trust that prices on a job don't change behind their back.

## Context

* Applies to parts on a work order or Part Sale that isn't invoiced. Role: service advisor, parts counter or technician; typing a price or cost needs the existing line permissions ('Work Order Lines — Create & Edit'; 'Part Sales — Create & Edit') plus 'See Financial Data'.
* Form factor: the phone part request follows the same rules.
* What must be true first: the pricing order (Story 10).

## Requirements

* S11-R1: A part is priced when it is added with a cost or when its cost is first entered.
* S11-R2: Changing the cost of a priced part recalculates its price and margin with the rule that applies then, as today, and records what priced it again. Only a Fixed price is kept. A cost changed from a vendor invoice or an order item doesn't reprice.
* S11-R16: Entering or changing the cost replaces a typed sell price with the calculated one, as today, including a Manual price and a price typed before the part had a cost.
* S11-R12: The cost can be re-entered only on a special-order or catalog part not yet received, or a Part Sale part until invoiced; not on inventory, found or received work order parts or invoiced work orders. Re-entering the same cost still reprices. Engineering confirms this for received parts.
* S11-R3: Changing the part's category recalculates it with the new category's matrix, including on a received part (new behavior; today only parts not yet received reprice on a category change).
* S11-R4: Changing the quantity keeps the unit price.
* S11-R5: Typing a sell price or margin that differs from the calculated one makes the line Manual; typing the same price doesn't. Manual stays when the quantity changes but not when cost or category changes. Clearing the price restores the fixed or calculated price.
* S11-R6: Parts already on a work order or Part Sale keep their prices through changes to tiers, matrices, rules, categories' matrices, Set Rule, Change All, the Part Sale switch, deleting a matrix, moving the customer to another tier and changing the customer. A user reprices one line on purpose by removing and re-adding it, re-entering its cost where allowed, or typing a price. Splitting is the only whole-work-order reprice.
* S11-R7: Every way a part line is created is priced by S10-R1 once it has a cost: adding a part (inline row, catalog search, Quick Add); the part request modal; a technician (Tech View, phone part request); canned lines, including those added by Maintenance Reminders (SV-10561); inspections; ShopCoach. Canned line parts are priced by the work order's tier when added, except: on a Default work order a special-order or catalog part comes in at the canned line's saved price and records Default, its details naming the canned line; and a part with no cost. The canned line's labor isn't repriced. Engineering confirms each path; any exception is listed, never left out silently. Imported invoices and the Open API create no part lines.
* S11-R14: Approving an estimate creates no part lines and reprices nothing.
* S11-R8: Replacing the part on a line prices it fresh from the new part.
* S11-R9: Receiving at a cost: a first cost prices the part; a Default, before-tiers or Default-work-order canned line price received at a different cost keeps its margin (SV-9965); any other tier's price is priced again by that tier's current rules (archived or not), using the customer and tier recorded on the line, with the details showing the rule and cost used; a Manual price keeps its margin ($2.00 → $3.00 on a $4.00 part becomes $6.00); a Fixed price or fixed line total doesn't change. Received parts keep their prices unless split.
* S11-R15: The receive window's 6-second notice reads "Updating the cost also updates the sell price." and says nothing about tiers.
* S11-R10: A cost changed from a vendor invoice after receiving, or from an order item before receiving, updates the cost only; the line keeps its price and source, so its margin changes. Invoiced and paid work orders aren't updated.
* S11-N1: No rule or tier reprices a part on an invoiced work order or Part Sale; reopening keeps prices; a credit or return uses the original invoiced price.
* S11-N2: A part with a fixed sell price keeps it through cost and category changes (SV-7160, SV-5744).
* S11-E2: Nothing sets a warranty part to $0 by itself; the advisor types $0, so the line is Manual with the Manual icon, and the part keeps its cost.
* S11-E8: A catalog or special-order part with no cost on a canned line keeps the canned line's saved price until its cost is entered or it is received, then the work order's tier prices it. It records the work order's tier, and its details say "Saved price from canned line <canned line>. Priced by <tier> when its cost comes in."
* S11-E7: A found part can't take a cost, so its price is typed and shows as Manual.
* S11-E5: A negative sell price can still be typed on the Parts tab and in the part dialog (Manual); Quick Add blocks it ("Sell price cannot be negative."). Tiers never produce a negative price.
* S11-E6: Splitting moves the chosen lines to a new work order (or Part Sale) for the same customer and asset, which takes the customer's current tier; its parts are priced again by S10-R1 at the cost on their line, received or not, except Manual, Fixed, fixed line total and canned line saved prices, which are kept. Lines left behind keep their prices. The split screen is unchanged.

## Out of Scope

* Repricing saved lines in bulk, or moving a work order's parts to the customer's current tier in one step (a fast follow). Keeping a sell price typed before the part has a cost (SV-10594).

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Given a special-order part added with no cost, when its cost is entered, then it is priced by the work order's tier.
- [ ] Given a priced special-order line not yet received, when its cost changes from $20.00 to $30.00, then the price and margin are recalculated and Priced by is recorded again; given the same cost re-entered, then it is repriced too.
- [ ] Given a Manual price on that line, when the cost is changed, then the calculated price replaces it; given a Fixed price, then it is kept.
- [ ] Given an inventory part, a found part or a received work order part, then its cost can't be re-entered; given a Part Sale part, then it can until the Part Sale is invoiced.
- [ ] Given a part whose category changes from Brakes to Tires, then it is repriced by Tires' matrix.
- [ ] Given a quantity change from 2 to 5, then the unit price is unchanged, Manual included.
- [ ] Given a typed price equal to the calculated one, then the line isn't Manual; different, then it is Manual; cleared, then the fixed or calculated price returns.
- [ ] Given parts on an open work order, when the matrix, Set Rule, Change All, the Part Sale switch, a matrix delete, the customer's tier or the customer changes, then no price on the work order changes.
- [ ] Given a canned line with an inventory part added to a Fleet work order, then Fleet prices the part and the canned line's labor is unchanged.
- [ ] Given a canned line with a special-order part with a cost added to a Default work order, then it comes in at the canned line's saved price, Priced by reads Default, and the details say "Saved price from canned line <canned line>."
- [ ] Given a canned line with a catalog part with no cost on a Fleet work order, then it keeps the saved price, records Fleet, and its details say "Saved price from canned line <canned line>. Priced by Fleet when its cost comes in."; when the cost comes in, then Fleet prices it.
- [ ] Given parts added from the inline row, catalog search, Quick Add, the part request modal, Tech View, the phone part request, a Maintenance Reminder canned line, an inspection and ShopCoach for a Fleet customer, then each is priced by Fleet.
- [ ] Given an estimate is approved, then no part line is created and no price changes.
- [ ] Given a line's part is replaced, then it is priced fresh from the new part.
- [ ] Given a Fleet-priced line ordered at $0.01 and received at $40.00, then it is priced again by Fleet's current rules for the customer and tier recorded on the line, and the details show the rule and cost used at receipt; given Fleet is archived, then the same.
- [ ] Given a Default-priced or before-tiers line received at a new cost, then it keeps its margin.
- [ ] Given a Manual $4.00 price on a $2.00 cost received at $3.00, then it becomes $6.00.
- [ ] Given a Fixed price or a fixed line total received at a new cost, then the price doesn't change.
- [ ] Given the receive window, then its notice reads "Updating the cost also updates the sell price."
- [ ] Given a received line's cost changed from a vendor invoice, then the cost updates, the price and source stay, and the margin changes; given an invoiced work order, then it isn't updated.
- [ ] Given an invoiced work order, when it is reopened, then its prices don't change; a credit or return uses the invoiced price.
- [ ] Given a part with a fixed sell price, when its cost or category changes, then the fixed price stays.
- [ ] Given a warranty part, then its price doesn't change by itself; typed $0, it is Manual with the Manual icon and keeps its cost.
- [ ] Given a found part with a typed price, then it shows as Manual.
- [ ] Given a negative price on the Parts tab, then it saves as Manual; in Quick Add, then "Sell price cannot be negative." blocks it.
- [ ] Given a Default work order whose customer is now on Fleet, when lines are split, then the new work order takes Fleet and its moved parts, received or not, are priced by Fleet at their line cost, except Manual, Fixed, fixed line total and canned line saved prices; lines left behind keep their prices.
