# SV-11698 Tiered Pricing - Story 6 - Give a customer their own contract for a date range

## Description

## Summary

Owners record one customer's own pricing as a contract with a start and end date; while it runs it prices every part for that customer, on work orders and Part Sales. Clients can hold one-customer deals that a shared tier can't, and see which contracts are expiring.

## Context

* Permission: 'Customers — Create & Edit' and 'Settings — Parts' to set up or change a contract; 'Customers — View' to see one, plus 'See Financial Data' for its prices.
* Set up from the customer's page. Setting up and editing contracts is desktop only.
* The Counter Sale customer can have a contract, like any customer (design review 2026-10-22).
* Key Decisions relied on: an active contract prices every part (a default matrix covering all categories plus category matrices; the tier applies only when no contract is active); contract dates (end on or after start, end date inclusive, the time zone of the location where the part is priced; past start dates allowed; scheduled contracts can be deleted, active ones ended early by editing the end date, expired ones read-only); contract matrices live on the contract, not in the Pricing matrices list; unsaved changes ask first.
* Out of scope: per-part contract prices; contract expiry reminders; several billing profiles per national account; shop supplies rules per customer.

## Requirements

* S6-R1: A contract has a name, a start date and an optional end date, and belongs to one customer. It is set up from the customer's page.
* S6-R2: Every contract has a default matrix covering all categories, and can have category matrices, with the same editor and rules as a tier (Story 3).
* S6-R3: While a contract is active it prices every part for that customer, on work orders and Part Sales, except parts priced by a typed price, a fixed price or a fixed line total (S7-R1). The customer's tier applies only when no contract is active. (owning PM, PRD review 2026-10-26)
* S6-R4: The end date is optional. A contract with no end date runs until the client gives it one, never expires and has no Expiry warning (design authority, 2026-10-27). When set, the end date is on or after the start date and is inclusive. Dates are checked in the time zone of the location where the part is priced (ShopView keeps a time zone per location, not per client): a contract ending Oct 31 still prices a part added at 11 p.m. on Oct 31 in Regina, though it is already Nov 1 in Toronto. Contracts have no location of their own, so the location pricing the part is the simplest rule; a location on a contract can be revisited in a later release. (owning PM, 2026-10-20 and 2026-10-23; replaces "the client's time zone" from D12; confirmed by the design authority, 2026-10-27)
* S6-R5: A customer's contracts can't overlap; a contract with no end date overlaps any contract after it, so the client gives it an end date before scheduling the next one. Duplicate as next contract (on a contract with an end date) copies a contract to start after it ends; a following contract takes over from its start date.
* S6-R6: A start date in the past is allowed; the contract prices parts added from the moment it's saved, and parts added earlier keep their prices. (owning PM, PRD review 2026-10-26)
* S6-R7: A scheduled contract can be edited in full (name, dates, Expiry warning, matrices) and deleted (owning PM, tech review 2026-10-27). On an active contract, the name, the Expiry warning (S6-R11) and the matrices can be edited (matrix changes price parts from then on; saved lines keep their prices), and an end date can be added, extended, brought forward to end it early, or removed to make the contract open-ended when no contract is scheduled after it (owning PM, R27 review walk, 2026-10-27), but not set to before today in the time zone of the user's current location (owning PM, PRD review 2026-10-26). Its start date can be edited until the contract has priced a line, then it is locked. An expired contract is read-only. (owning PM, grill 2026-10-22)
* S6-R8: When a contract ends with nothing after it, the customer's tier prices new parts. A line priced after a contract expired says so in its price details.
* S6-R9: Clients can see which contracts are expiring and on what date, on a Contracts tab on the Customers list. The tab's layout (owning PM, 2026-10-23): count cards across the top, a filter bar, then one table of contracts with each one's customer, status, start and end dates and next contract, and an actions column. If the Maintenance reminders work on the Customers list (on its own branch, not released) ships first, its tab is the pattern to follow (owning PM, PRD review 2026-10-26). The count cards group contracts by the calendar time left before they end, in bands that don't overlap: 0–30 days, 31–60 days and 61–90 days, plus Expired with nothing next and Scheduled to start. A contract counts in one card at most: a scheduled contract counts only under Scheduled to start, whatever its end date (owning PM, tech review 2026-10-27), and an active contract with no end date is in no card and never Expired (owning PM, R27 review walk, 2026-10-27). Clicking a card clears the filters and shows only that card's contracts; clicking it again, or changing a filter, clears the card (owning PM, 2026-10-23; canvas 7.4 to 7.9). The cards are a planning view and differ from the amber warning: a contract with a 30-day Expiry warning that ends in 45 days counts under 31–60 days but isn't amber yet. The amber ending warning is in S6-R11. The tab shows with 'Customers — View' and needs no 'See Financial Data', as it shows no prices; its actions show only to users who can change contracts (section 4). (owning PM, 2026-10-23) The actions column offers, by contract status: Edit (active, scheduled), Duplicate as next contract (any contract with an end date and nothing scheduled after it, expired ones included, so a lapsed deal can be renewed in one click) and Delete (scheduled); expired contracts also offer View (owning PM, 2026-10-27). The Expired card counts the same contracts as the Expired filter: those that ended in the last 12 months with nothing next (S6-R19). (owning PM, tech review 2026-10-27)
* S6-R10: Matrices in a new contract are drafts until the contract is saved. Closing a contract with unsaved changes asks "Discard these changes?" (Story 17).
* S6-R11: Each contract with an end date has an Expiry warning, chosen with the end date: 30, 60 or 90 days before its end date, 60 by default. Inside that window, with no contract scheduled after it, its status badge, the contract card, the customer card on the customer page, and the customer card on work orders, Part Sales and imported work orders (as a Contract row, only where that card already shows customer details) show an amber "Ends in N days" warning, and a banner offers Duplicate as next contract. Red is not used. Once it has ended, it shows Expired in grey and the customer's tier prices new parts (S6-R8). A contract with no end date has no Expiry warning and shows no ending warning (design authority, 2026-10-27). Duplicate as next contract copies the Expiry warning. The same setting is the lead time a future expiry reminder or notification will use (Out of scope). (owning PM, 2026-10-22; research 47) (the two customer cards: owning PM, PRD review 2026-10-26)
* S6-R12: The customer's Contracts tab shows a count of all their contracts, active, scheduled and expired, for example "Contracts (3)"; with none, no number shows. The list shows every contract, with the active one told apart from scheduled and expired ones. (owning PM, 2026-10-19; confirmed 2026-10-22)
* S6-R13: New Contract offers Start with, one of three: a copy of contract "<name>" (the customer's running or most recent contract; its matrices are copied), a copy of tier <tier>, picked in a dropdown beside it (that tier's matrices are copied, so tiers serve as templates; archived tiers aren't offered), or a brand new contract (no matrices until the client adds its default matrix, S6-R16). Copied matrices belong to the new contract; later changes to the tier or contract they came from don't change it. The new contract's dates are always set by the client. (design review with the design authority, 2026-10-22; wording, the copied matrices staying fixed and the archived-tier rule confirmed by the owning PM, 2026-10-22) Every customer gets the copy of a tier and brand new choices, a new customer with no tier or contract included; the copy of a contract choice shows only when the customer has a contract. (owning PM, click-through 2026-10-26) Design: 6.9–6.11.
* S6-R14: A new contract's first matrix is named "<customer name> default matrix" (for example "Acme Freight default matrix"); the name can be edited before the contract is saved, and an info icon in the name field says so on hover. A matrix copied from a contract or a tier (S6-R13) keeps its name. (owning PM, 2026-10-19)
* S6-R15: On the customer page, Contracts is its own tab, marked New, beside Fees & Discounts; each keeps its own permission (Fees & Discounts as today, Contracts as in section 4). When the tabs don't fit the screen, the page shows as many as fit and a More tab lists the rest in a dropdown. This applies to the customer page only, and its look follows the design system. (design review with the design authority, 2026-10-22) It uses the design system's Tabs overflow: the row never wraps or scrolls; tabs drop into More from the right; the More menu lists them with their counts; the selected tab always stays in the row, so picking one from More brings it into the row in its own place and the tab before it moves into More if there is no room; an alert on a tab inside More also shows on More (owning PM, design system Tabs overflow, 2026-10-29).
* S6-R16: New Contract's Save stays off until the contract has at least one matrix; the empty matrices area says so. (design review with Milos, 2026-10-21)
* S6-R17: The Contracts tab on the Customers list has a search by customer or contract name, and a Status filter for Active, Ending soon, Scheduled and Expired, with Active and Ending soon selected by default; the search and the filter use the shared filter bar used on the customer page's Work Orders and Part Sales tabs (owning PM, from a design review of the walkthrough, 2026-10-27). It is sorted by end date, soonest first; contracts with no end date come last, by customer name. Ending soon means inside the contract's own Expiry warning with nothing after it: the same set that shows the amber "Ends in N days" (S6-R11). It shows 50 contracts a page, breaks ties by customer name, shows "No contracts match these filters" with Clear all filters when nothing matches, and keeps its search, filters, sort and page when the user comes back. (owning PM, grill 2026-10-22; 2026-10-23) The customer name in each row opens that customer's page. (owning PM, click-through 2026-10-26) (paging: owning PM, PRD review 2026-10-26)
* S6-R18: The contract's edit icon matches the other edit icons and opens Edit Contract for the whole contract (canvas 7.1); the edit icon beside each matrix in the contract edits only that matrix. (owning PM, 2026-10-23)
* S6-R19: On the Customers list's Contracts tab, Expired covers contracts that ended in the last 12 months; older ones stay on the customer's own Contracts tab. (owning PM, PRD review 2026-10-26)
* S6-R20: A customer's Contracts tab shows the active contract first, then scheduled ones by start date, then expired ones by end date, newest first, 25 a page. (owning PM, PRD review 2026-10-26)
* S6-N1: No name, or an end date before the start date: an inline error, nothing saved.
* S6-N2: Dates that overlap another contract for the customer: Save is blocked and the message names the other contract. (design review with the design authority, 2026-10-22) When that contract has no end date, the message says to give it an end date first, for example "Acme 2026 has no end date. Give it an end date before this contract starts." (owning PM, R27 review walk, 2026-10-27)
* S6-E1: A work order started under a contract: parts added after the contract ended are priced by the tier (or the next contract); parts already on it keep their prices.
* S6-E2: A contract that starts and ends on the same day is active for that day.
* S6-E3: A line priced before the customer's contract started (for example quoted on Dec 20 under Fleet, approved on Jan 5 after Acme 2027 started on Jan 1) keeps its price; nothing reprices saved lines (S8-R6). To apply the contract, the advisor removes the part and adds it again, or types the price (Manual); re-entering the cost reprices it only where the cost can still be edited (S8-R2) (owning PM, tech review 2026-10-27). The price details say so (S9-R2). (owning PM, 2026-10-21)

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Given a user with 'Customers — Create & Edit' and 'Settings — Parts' on a customer page, when the user creates a contract with a name, start date and end date, then it saves against that customer with a default matrix covering all categories.
- [ ] Given a user with 'Customers — Create & Edit' but not 'Settings — Parts', when they open a customer page, then they can see the customer's contracts but New Contract, Edit, Duplicate and Delete are hidden.
- [ ] Given a contract, when the user adds a Tires category matrix, then it uses the same editor, calculations and validation as a tier matrix (Story 3), and Tires parts are priced by it while the contract is active.
- [ ] Given an active contract, when a part is added on a work order or a Part Sale for that customer, then the contract prices it (its category matrix, otherwise its default matrix), whatever the customer's tiers are.
- [ ] Given no active contract, when a part is added, then the customer's tier for that channel prices it.
- [ ] Given a contract with no name, when the user saves, then an inline error shows and nothing is saved.
- [ ] Given an end date before the start date, when the user saves, then an inline error shows and nothing is saved.
- [ ] Given a contract that starts and ends on the same day, when a part is added that day, then the contract prices it; the next day, the tier does.
- [ ] Given a contract ending 31 March, when a part is added on 31 March in the location's time zone, then the contract prices it; on 1 April it doesn't. Given two locations in different time zones, when a part is added at each just after midnight on 1 April local time, then neither is priced by the contract.
- [ ] Given an existing contract for 1 Jan–30 Jun, when the user saves another contract for the same customer from 1 Jun, then Save is blocked and the message names the existing contract.
- [ ] Given a contract, when the user uses Duplicate as next contract, then a copy is created starting the day after it ends, and from that start date the new contract prices new parts.
- [ ] Given a contract created today with a start date last month, when the contract is saved, then parts already on work orders keep their prices and only parts added from now on are priced by it.
- [ ] Given a scheduled contract (starts in the future), when the user deletes it, then it is removed.
- [ ] Given an active contract, when the user edits it, then the name and matrices can be changed (matrix changes price parts from then on; saved lines keep their prices), the end date can be extended or brought forward but not to before today, and the start date can be changed only until the contract has priced a line; given an expired contract, then nothing can be edited.
- [ ] Given an active contract, when the user looks for delete, then it isn't offered; editing the end date to today ends it early.
- [ ] Given an expired contract, when the user opens it, then it is read-only.
- [ ] Given a contract that has ended with nothing after it, when a new part is added, then the customer's tier prices it and its price details say "Contract expired".
- [ ] Given a work order opened under a contract that has since ended, when a part is added, then the tier (or the next contract, if one has started) prices it, and parts already on the work order keep their prices.
- [ ] Given the Customers list, when the user opens the Contracts tab, then it shows count cards, the shared filter bar (search and a Status filter) and a table of contracts with customer, status, start date, end date, next contract and actions, and the user can tell which contracts are expiring and on what date.
- [ ] Given a new contract with matrices added but not saved, when the user closes it, then "Discard these changes?" asks Keep editing or Discard changes; discarding saves neither the contract nor its matrices.
- [ ] Given a new contract, when its matrices are open, then they don't appear in the Pricing matrices list, before or after the contract is saved.
- [ ] Given the Counter Sale customer, when its customer page opens, then it has a Contracts tab like any customer.
- [ ] Given a tablet, when the user taps the icon on a contract-priced line, then the price details name the contract.
- [ ] Given a contract ending in 30 days or fewer with nothing scheduled after it, when the customer page or the Contracts tab opens, then it shows an amber "Ends in N days" warning and a banner offers Duplicate as next contract; given one with a next contract scheduled, then no warning shows; given an ended contract, then it shows Expired in grey.
- [ ] Given a customer with one active, one scheduled and one expired contract, when their customer page opens, then the tab reads "Contracts (3)" and lists all three, with the active one told apart; given a customer with no contracts, then it shows no number.
- [ ] Given New Contract for a customer with a running contract, when it opens, then Start with offers: a copy of contract "Acme 2026", a copy of tier with a tier dropdown beside it, and a brand new contract; picking the contract or a tier copies its matrices with their names and rules; picking a brand new contract leaves no matrices and Save off until the default matrix is added; archived tiers are not in the dropdown; the dates are always set by the user.
- [ ] Given a contract started as a copy of Fleet, when Fleet's matrices are later changed, then the contract's matrices don't change.
- [ ] Given a customer page whose tabs don't fit the screen, when it opens, then the tabs that fit show and a More tab lists the rest; Contracts is its own tab beside Fees & Discounts.
- [ ] Given a new contract for Acme Freight, when its first matrix is created, then it is named "Acme Freight default matrix" and the name can be edited before saving; given matrices copied from a contract or a tier, then each keeps its name.
- [ ] Given a user with 'Customers — Create & Edit' and 'View and Manage AP/AR Data' but not 'Settings — Parts', when they open a customer, then the Pricing tab shows with Fees & Discounts only; given 'Settings — Parts' and 'Customers — Create & Edit' without AP/AR, then it shows and opens on Contracts; given neither pair, then there is no Pricing tab.
- [ ] Given a part quoted for Acme on Dec 20 under Fleet and a contract that starts Jan 1, when the quote is approved on Jan 5, then the part keeps its Fleet price and its price details read "Priced by Fleet on Dec 20; contract Acme 2027 has applied since Jan 1."; when the advisor re-enters the cost, then the contract prices it.

- [ ] Given the Contracts tab on the Customers list, when it opens, then Active and Ending soon are selected, contracts are sorted by end date with the soonest first, and it can be searched by customer or contract name.
- [ ] Given a user with 'Customers — View' but not 'See Financial Data', when they open the Contracts tab on the Customers list, then it shows; given a user who can't change contracts, then its actions are hidden.
- [ ] Given New Contract, when it opens, then Expiry warning offers 30, 60 or 90 days and is set to 60; given a contract with a 90-day warning, when it is 90 days from its end with nothing after it, then the amber "Ends in 90 days" warning shows and the contract is under Ending soon on the Contracts tab.
- [ ] Given an active contract, when the user changes its Expiry warning, then the warning follows the new window; given Duplicate as next contract, then the new contract keeps the same Expiry warning.
- [ ] Given contracts ending in 16, 46 and 77 days, when the Contracts tab opens, then the 0–30, 31–60 and 61–90 days cards each count 1; clicking 31–60 days shows only the contract ending in 46 days, and clicking it again clears the filter.
- [ ] Given a new customer with no tier change and no contract, when New Contract opens, then Start with offers Copy of a tier and Brand new; Copy of a contract shows only for a customer who has a contract.
- [ ] Given the Contracts tab on the Customers list, when the user clicks a customer's name, then that customer's page opens.
- [ ] Given New Contract with no end date, when it is saved, then the contract is active with no end date, has no Expiry warning and no ending warning, is in no count card, and sorts last on the Customers list's Contracts tab.
- [ ] Given a customer with an active contract that has no end date, when the user saves a contract that starts later, then Save is blocked and the message says "Acme 2026 has no end date. Give it an end date before this contract starts."
- [ ] Given an active contract with an end date and nothing scheduled after it, when the user removes the end date and saves, then the contract becomes open-ended; with a contract scheduled after it, removing the end date is refused with "Acme 2027 is scheduled after this contract. Delete or move it before removing the end date."
- [ ] Given a customer page on a laptop where the tabs don't all fit, when it opens, then the tab row doesn't wrap or scroll: the tabs that don't fit move into a More tab from the right, More lists them with their counts, and the selected tab stays in the row; picking Contracts from More brings it into the row in its own place.
