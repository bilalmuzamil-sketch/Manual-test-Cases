# SV-12749 Verify: Sales by Tier endpoints, filter validation, drill keys, permissions and paging (Phases 5 and 7)

## Description

**Covers:** RPT-S1-R2, R3, R6, R8–R13, N1, N2, E1, E2, E4–E9; RPT-S2-R2–R5, N1, E1; RPT-S3-R1–R5, R7–R9, N1; RPT-UF3; NFR-R6; decisions R-D4, R-D5, R-D6, R-D7, R-D8, R-D13, R-D24, R-D28

**Checklist**

1. Permission (RPT-S1-N2): every `/api/reporting/reports/sales-by-tier…` endpoint refuses a user without 'Reports — Page Access' with 403, and answers a reports-only role, which also sees Parts Cost and Parts Margin (Tiered Pricing S16-R7).
2. Contract (R-D7, R-D28): the L1 rows with `totals`, `/drill/customers?rowKey=`, `/drill/documents?rowKey=&customerKey=`, `filter-options` and `filter-options/customers` (with `/resolve`) are exactly as the plan's Section 5 names them; responses use `collection`, `pagination.rowsNumber` and `totals`; rows carry `rowKey`, `customerKey` or `documentKey` in the Section 5 formats: a tier UUID (Default included) or one of the kind slugs `fixedPrice`, `fixedLineTotal`, `manual` and `beforeTiers`; a company UUID, or the nil UUID for No Company; `invoice_…` or `credit_…`.
3. Validation (RPT-S3-R2, RPT-UF3, R-D24, NFR-R6): a tier or category id from another client, or an unknown key (`unlinkedCredits` is no longer one), gives 422 ('Unknown priced by "x".' for Priced By); more than 500 ids in one list gives 422; a range over 366 days gives 422; a location the user can't access is refused with "Unknown location".
4. Filters (RPT-S3-R1–R5, RPT-S3-N1, R-D4): `customers`, `pricedBy` and `categories` are tri-state (absent = all, empty = none, a list = these); `salesType` is one value, `all`, `parts`, `service` or `none`, split by invoice number as on Sales by Customer. Filters that match nothing, `salesType=none` included, return no rows and no `totals` without running the report query.
5. Paging and sorting (RPT-S1-R12, RPT-S3-R7, RPT-S3-R8): 30 rows a page at every level. With no `sortBy`, rows come in first-load order: Default first, then active tiers, then archived tiers in the order they were archived, then Fixed price, Fixed line total, Manual and Before tiers. Every column sorts on the server over the whole result, with RPT-S3-R8's tie-breaks, and `partsCredits` descending puts the largest credit on top.
6. Levels (RPT-S2-R2, RPT-S2-R4, RPT-S2-R5, R-D6): each level adds up to the row above it; a customer priced by two tiers shows under both; an invoice is always listed under its own customer, number and date. % of Total comes back at L1 only.
7. Labels (RPT-S1-R11, R-D8): active tier rows read "2 · Fleet", with the number matching the Tiered Pricing tier list; an archived tier reads its name with "(archived)"; other rows read their name.
8. Customer search (RPT-S3-R4, R-D13): `filter-options/customers` also finds a customer whose only activity in the range is a credit with part lines, such as a Part Sale return; `/resolve` resolves up to 500 ids.
9. Empty and gates (RPT-S1-N1): a range with nothing invoiced or credited returns no rows and no `totals`. `bin/smoke-test.sh` (with the new probes) shows no 500s, the backend log is clean, and the endpoints are in `ReportFetchersReadReplicaTest`.
