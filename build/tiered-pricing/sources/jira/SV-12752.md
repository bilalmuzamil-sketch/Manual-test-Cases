# SV-12752 Verify: drilling rows to customers and documents, paging, document links and in-row errors (Phase 10)

## Description

**Covers:** RPT-S1-R12, E8; RPT-S2-R1–R5, N1, E1, EH1; RPT-S5-R2, R6; RPT-UF6; decisions R-D15, R-D27, R-D29, R-D36

**Checklist**

1. Levels (RPT-S2-R1, RPT-S2-R2, RPT-S2-R3, RPT-S2-R4, RPT-S5-R2): as `admin` on `/reports/sales-by-tier`, every row is closed on load. Expanding a row lists its customers with the same columns for that customer's lines in the row; expanding a customer lists their invoices and credits with the row's lines, oldest first, then smallest number first. Each level adds up to the row above it. Sales by Category opens four levels: category, Priced By, customer, documents.
2. Paging (RPT-S1-R12): every level shows 30 rows, then loads the next 30 as you scroll, nested levels included; a loading row shows while a level loads.
3. Document links (RPT-S2-R3, RPT-S2-N1, R-D27): an invoice number opens the work order's Finance tab or the Part Sale; a credit number opens the credit's PDF in a new tab. Each is a link only when the document belongs to the signed-in location and the user may open it (a credit also needs invoice view); otherwise the number is plain text, as for another location's invoice.
4. Credit rows (RPT-S1-E8, R-D29, R-D36): a credit shows as its own document row with its date beside the number, for example "Credit CM-5003 · Oct 21, 2026", carrying only its lines that name a part line in that row. There are no credit-application or reversal rows, and a credit with no part lines never shows.
5. Customers (RPT-S2-R2, RPT-S2-R5, RPT-S2-E1): a customer priced by two tiers shows under both; invoices with no customer are under "No Company"; a customer deleted later shows under the name recorded on the invoice; an invoice is always under its own customer, number and date.
6. In-row error (RPT-S2-EH1, RPT-UF6): block `*/drill/*` in DevTools and expand a row: the row shows "Ooooops! An error occurred" with Try again and no toast, and Try again loads it. A failure on page 2 keeps page 1's rows. The error still reaches Sentry.
