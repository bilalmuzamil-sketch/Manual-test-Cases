## Summary

Add a floor-price (minimum margin) rule per Pricing Matrix bracket to prevent price inversion.

## Problem Statement

The current Pricing Matrix can produce price inversion where a part with slightly higher cost is sold with a lower selling price/higher or lower margin than a part in the previous bracket (e.g., due to decreasing markup as cost increases).

## Proposed Solution

Add an area/setting to define a floor price for each Pricing Matrix bracket so the floor ensures at least as much margin as the highest margin in the previous bracket. The customer gave an example where the second bracket’s floor would be “12.01 sell price.”

## User Impact

Shops using Pricing Matrix rules would benefit by avoiding price inversion while still allowing reduced markup as part costs increase.

## Customer's Own Words

\> I think adding a feature to your price matrix to help with price inversion would be extremely beneficial (like a floor price rule).

\> If you had a area for setting each brackets floor price (atleast as much margin as the highest margin in the previous bracket) it would solve this issue while still allowing you to reduce your markup as price goes up without incentivizing the sale of higher priced items for less margin.

\> The floor would be 12.01 sell price for the second bracket
