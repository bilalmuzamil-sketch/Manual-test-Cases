# SV-11704 Tiered Pricing - Story 8 - Categories over time

## Description

## Summary

Adding, renaming or deleting a category never leaves parts unpriced: a new category is on the General Matrix unless another is picked, and deleting a category asks first and moves its parts and lines to Uncategorized instead of being blocked. Owners can tidy categories, including customer-named ones, without breaking pricing.

## Context

* Role: user with 'Settings — Parts', which the Categories page already requires; a category's pricing also needs 'See Financial Data'. Desktop only (settings).
* What must be true first: the Categories page and category page (Story 7).

## Requirements

* S8-R1: Renaming a category keeps it on the same matrix.
* S8-R10: New Category opens a New Category page: name, Pricing Matrix (the General Matrix unless another is picked), QuickBooks Products and Services (only when QuickBooks is connected) and Tax Exempt, with the matrix's grid read-only. A new category is priced for every tier from the start. Save and Cancel; nothing is created until Save.
* S8-R3: Every new part starts in Uncategorized; changing its category reprices it.
* S8-R4: Uncategorized is a system category: it can't be renamed, deleted or edited and always uses the General Matrix. Its page is read-only with no rename, Delete or matrix picker; Edit is grayed with a link to the General Matrix.
* S8-R8: Delete on a category's page asks first instead of being blocked by parts that use it; nothing blocks deleting any category but Uncategorized. "Delete <category>?" with "Its parts and lines, invoiced ones included, move to Uncategorized, and its QuickBooks mapping is removed. Invoices already sent aren't sent again; one synced again later books these parts to Uncategorized's QuickBooks item. This can't be undone." and Cancel / Delete (red), with no counts. On confirm, parts and every line move to Uncategorized, the QuickBooks mapping is removed, the delete is sent to AccountingHub, and its matrix stays in the library.
* S8-N3: Category names are unique, ignoring capitals ("A category called <name> already exists"), and need at least 3 characters.
* S8-E1: A deleted category's catalog parts, work order and Part Sale lines (invoiced included), canned line parts, and order and delivery items move to Uncategorized. Past lines report as Uncategorized. Invoices already sent to QuickBooks or AccountingHub aren't sent again. A matrix left with no category prices nothing.
* S8-E2: Deleting a category changes no price and never reprices lines in bulk; a moved part is priced by the General Matrix only at its next reprice (cost or category change).
* Error: a failed save shows today's error toast and keeps what was typed.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Given Brakes on Brake Plus, when it is renamed Brakes HD, then it stays on Brake Plus.
- [ ] Given New Category, when it opens, then it is a page with name, Pricing Matrix set to the General Matrix, Tax Exempt, QuickBooks Products and Services only when QuickBooks is connected, and the General Matrix's grid read-only.
- [ ] Given a new category saved on Brake Plus, when a part in it is priced for a Fleet customer, then Brake Plus's Fleet column prices it; given New Category cancelled, then nothing is created.
- [ ] Given a new name "brakes" when Brakes exists, or "Ab", then the inline error shows and nothing saves.
- [ ] Given a new inventory part, then its category is Uncategorized; when the category is changed on a line, then the line is repriced by the new category's matrix.
- [ ] Given Uncategorized's page, then it has no rename, Delete or matrix picker, and Edit is grayed with a link to the General Matrix.
- [ ] Given a category used by parts and invoiced lines, when Delete is clicked, then "Delete <category>?" shows the PRD's text with no counts and a red Delete.
- [ ] Given Delete confirmed, then the category is gone, its catalog parts, lines (invoiced included), canned line parts and order and delivery items are in Uncategorized, its QuickBooks mapping is removed, the delete reaches AccountingHub, and its matrix stays in the library.
- [ ] Given a deleted category's open line, then its price is unchanged; when its cost is re-entered, then the General Matrix prices it.
- [ ] Given an invoice already synced to QuickBooks or AccountingHub with lines in the deleted category, then it isn't sent again.
- [ ] Given a category report for a past period, then the moved lines report as Uncategorized.
- [ ] Given the deleted category's matrix had no other category, then it shows Inactive and prices nothing.
- [ ] Given a category save that fails, then today's error toast shows and the page keeps what was typed.
