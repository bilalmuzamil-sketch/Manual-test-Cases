# SV-12748 Verify: the numbers: row membership, credit rules and Totals tying to Sales by Customer to the cent (Phase 6)

## Description

**Covers:** RPT-S1-R2, R3, R6–R13, N1, E1, E2, E4–E9; RPT-S2-R2–R5, N1, E1; RPT-S3-R1–R5, R7–R9, N1; RPT-S5-R1–R6, N1, E1–E5; NFR-R1, NFR-R3, NFR-R6; decisions R-D1, R-D12, R-D19, R-D20, R-D21, R-D22, R-D26, R-D30, R-D36

**Checklist**

1. Tie-out (RPT-S1-R7, RPT-S5-E2, R-D20, NFR-R3): for the same date range, locations and Sales Type, Sales by Tier's Totals Parts Invoiced equals Sales by Customer's Parts Invoiced to the cent, and its Parts Margin equals Sales by Customer's (so Parts Cost equals the parts cost Sales by Customer stores with those invoices); Parts Net equals Parts Invoiced plus Parts Credits. Check with default filters, each Sales Type, a location subset and one customer. Sales by Category's Totals equal Sales by Tier's. Nothing is compared with the Sales report. Evidence: the tie-out functional tests pass.
2. Row membership (RPT-S1-R2, RPT-S1-R3, RPT-S1-R10, RPT-S1-E2, RPT-S1-E5, RPT-S1-E7, RPT-S1-E9): only invoiced lines count, dated by the invoice's created date, and void invoices never count. Each line is in exactly one row: each tier (Default included), Fixed price, Fixed line total, Manual or Before tiers. A line typed over a fixed price is in Manual; a canned line's part is in its tier's row; a Same as previous tier line is in the customer's tier, not the tier it followed; a warranty part typed to $0 is in Manual; a part with no cost is in the customer's tier at $0. Internal and no-charge work orders aren't there. A row shows only when it has invoiced lines or credits in the range.
3. Part attribution (RPT-S1-R3, RPT-S1-R9, RPT-S1-E6, R-D26): a part's core and environmental charges, and a fee or discount on that single part, are in the part's row and category; a discount on a single part line lowers Parts Invoiced in that part's row, as on Sales by Customer. Labor, shop supplies and whole work order or Part Sale fees and discounts are in no row.
4. Fixed line totals (RPT-S1-E4, RPT-S5-E3, R-D30): a fixed line total's parts are in Fixed line total whatever the customer's tier; the row reports the parts amount Sales by Customer counts for that line, and Parts Cost is the sum of its parts' costs. On Sales by Category the line is split across its categories by the split it recorded, and Sales by Tier's Category filter uses the same split.
5. Credits with part lines (RPT-S1-E8, R-D21, R-D19, R-D12): only customer credit memos with part lines count, such as Part Sale returns and other credits for parts. Each credit line that names a part line counts on the date the credit was created, whether or not it has been applied, at the amount credited after any restocking fee and before tax, in the row and category of the part line it credits, at the original invoiced price, under the credit's customer and location. A credit on a line priced before release is in Before tiers; a credit on a line whose invoice was later reversed reads that line's own recorded pricing; when a part line is on several invoices (voided, then reissued), its credit goes to the latest non-void invoice's row.
6. Credits left out (RPT-S1-E8, R-D21, R-D36): a credit with no part lines, such as a goodwill credit created on the customer page and applied to an invoice, an overpayment, a carried-over discount, a fee or a write-off, appears in neither report, and neither does a credit line that names no part line on a credit that has part lines. The Totals' Parts Credits exclude them all, while Sales and Sales by Customer still show them. Void credits and vendor credits never count. Evidence: the functional tests showing that a goodwill credit, an overpayment credit and an unnamed line on a part credit never appear pass.
7. Dates (RPT-S1-E8): a credit never changes the original invoice or a past month. A return created this month for a part invoiced last month counts in this month's Parts Credits, and last month's figures don't move.
8. No credits row (R-D36): neither report has a "Credits not tied to a part" row, and the Priced By and Category filters have no option or special case for one.
9. Names (RPT-S1-R8, RPT-S1-R11, RPT-S5-R2, RPT-S5-E1, RPT-S5-E5, RPT-S2-R2, RPT-S2-E1): a renamed tier is one row under its current name, and a renamed Default shows its current name; an archived tier with sales in the range stays, shown by its name with "(archived)"; a line whose category was deleted reports under Uncategorized; pre-release lines stay under the category they were invoiced with; invoices with no customer are under "No Company"; a customer deleted later shows under the name recorded on the invoice.
10. Scope (NFR-R1, NFR-R6): every query runs on the read replica, scoped by client and accessible locations, with a tenant-isolation and an out-of-scope-location functional test per query repository.
