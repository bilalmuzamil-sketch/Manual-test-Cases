# **Setup: Revenue tile shows the exact amount to the cent and equals the Sales report**

Test case: [C88633](https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/cases/view/88633), Dashboard. Part 1 is for the manual QA tester. Part 2 is for a Claude session that prepares the case on its own. Both create the starting state listed in the test case's Preconditions; the numbers below match that list.

## **What must be true before you start**

1.  User: you are signed in as an Admin (the Admin role has every permission, including Reports).
2.  Location: you are in a quiet test location: nobody else invoices, issues credits or clocks time there this week; on the Dashboard, the Revenue tile on This Week reads $0.00 with 0 Invoices.
3.  Timing: you run the whole case on one day, and not on the last evening of the week, so every record you create stays inside This Week.
4.  Data: a test customer with one contact and one asset that uses that contact (for example ZZAUTOTEST Dash Customer 1, contact Dana Test, unit ZZ-101).
5.  Data: an in-stock inventory part with a Sell price (for example $200.00).
6.  Data: Invoice 1 for that customer (not sent, not paid): one approved labor line (for example 10.00 hours at a rate from the list) and a flat Discount (for example $100.00). You have written down its Subtotal (before any discount and before tax).
7.  Data: Invoice 2 for the same customer (not sent, not paid): one approved labor line (for example 2.00 hours) with the part from 5 added (quantity 1, In stock). You have written down its Subtotal.
8.  Data: a credit on invoice 2 that returns the part, Outcome Store Credit. You have written down its amount before tax.

# **Part 1. Setup for manual QA tester**

Each line says which precondition it sets up. Where a precondition is not listed, it needs no setup.

  - **For precondition 1:**

<!-- end list -->

1.  On the QA site's sign-in page, in the DEV MODE — QUICK LOGIN panel, click Admin (this signs you in as Admin ShopView).
2.  To use another role, check it has Reports: profile icon \> Settings \> Roles & Permissions \> edit icon on the role \> Reports is switched on.

<!-- end list -->

  - **For precondition 2:**

<!-- end list -->

1.  To change location, click your profile icon (your initials, top right, beside the current location name) \> under Change Location: click the orange button showing the current location \> pick the location from the list
2.  Pick ZZAUTOTEST Dashboard Quiet (it already exists and charges 0 % shop supplies).
3.  Check: Dashboard \> Revenue tile \> range pill \> This Week: it reads $0.00 with 0 Invoices.
4.  If it does not, try another location in the list.
5.  Locations cannot be deleted, so do not create another one: if no location is quiet this week, mark the case Blocked and write in the result comment that no quiet location was free this week.

<!-- end list -->

  - **For precondition 4:**

<!-- end list -->

1.  Customers \> New Customer \> Name \> Save
2.  On its Contacts tab: New Contact
3.  Add an asset (Add beside Asset in the New Work Order window, or the customer's Assets tab) and pick that contact in the asset's Contact field.

<!-- end list -->

  - **For precondition 5:** Parts \> Inventory (pick a part with stock on hand, or use New Inventory Part).
  - **For precondition 6 and 7:**

<!-- end list -->

1.  Work Orders \> Create Work Order \> in the New Work Order window pick the Customer and an Asset (Add beside either if needed) \> Save (if a Confirmation says the customer is over their credit limit, click Create)
2.  Set Service Advisor to any name in the list in the left panel
3.  In the New Line window (\> New Line on the Lines tab for each further line): What Are You Doing? (any text), Add Technician (any), Labor Rate (any rate in the list, for example EVR Travel to EVO ($100 an hour)), Estimated Time and Tech Time, tick Line Approved \> Save & Close.
4.  Discount (invoice 1): three dots at the top right of the Lines tab \> Add Work Order Fee / Discount \> Type Discount, the flat amount \> Add Discount.
5.  Part (invoice 2): on the line click Add Part and choose the inventory part (it must show In stock).
6.  Then on the line click Story (Add tech story for this line) \> type a short story \> Update
7.  Click Complete (if a Missing Details window asks for Mileage, enter one \> Complete All Lines)
8.  Click Mark Reviewed
9.  Finance tab \> Create Invoice
10. Close the New Customer Payment window without paying (do not send or pay the invoice).
11. The Subtotal is on the Finance tab.

<!-- end list -->

  - **For precondition 8:**

<!-- end list -->

1.  Invoice 2's Finance tab \> the invoice's three dots \> Issue Credit \> choose the part, Outcome Store Credit \> save.
2.  The credit shows on Customers \> the customer \> Invoices tab (turn Open only off if you cannot see it).

# **Part 2. Setup for Claude session**

Prepare the same starting state by carrying out Part 1 in a browser with Playwright. Record the real values you used, the build marker and screenshots in the Manual-test-Cases repository, and judge pass or fail against the test case's Expected results only.

## **Environment**

  - Dashboard QA site: app https://sv490.qa.shopview.com, API https://sv490api.qa.shopview.com.
  - Record the build marker (app-version on the app page) before you start.
  - Browser: Chromium at /opt/pw-browsers/chromium through Playwright. Do not run "playwright install".

## **Access**

  - Sign in with the QA site's quick login: node build/testing-tools/qa-branch-boot.mjs (see build/APP-ACTIONS-PLAYBOOK.md section A). Carry the sv\_sso\_session cookie only, scoped to the host.
  - Keep cookies in /tmp only, with file mode 600. Never write a cookie into a document, a commit or a log.
  - Change location through the profile icon (your initials, top right) \> Change Location.

## **Test data**

Name anything you create with ZZAUTOTEST at the start. Leave all test data in place; nothing is deleted afterwards.
