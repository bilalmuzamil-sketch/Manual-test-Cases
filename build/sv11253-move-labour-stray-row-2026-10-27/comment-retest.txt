Tested on the QA branch sv11253 (build v26.43.1-baa44e3) on 27 October 2026, after Move labor was set up on the branch. 6 of 6 checks passed.

When a technician claims an empty labor row with *Start* and that row is then moved to another line with *Move labor*, moving the technician's time away from that line no longer leaves an empty row behind. On production the same steps still leave an extra empty row, shown as "Deleted user".

What the ticket expects:
{quote}Moving a row with Move labour to line counts as on purpose, like picking a technician in Edit Line, so nothing is handed back on the new line.{quote}

h3. Steps to see it on sv11253
# Open [https://sv11253.qa.shopview.com|https://sv11253.qa.shopview.com] and sign in as Admin. Create a work order with three lines that have no technician (on each line, {{Labor}} reads {{Unassigned}}).
# Sign in as a technician (for example Tech ShopView) and open the work order. On line 1, click the green {{Start}} button at the right of the {{Labor}} row. Wait a minute, click the button again, type a story and click {{Clock Out}}. Line 1's {{Labor}} now shows the technician.
# Sign in as Admin. Right-click line 2's title, click {{Edit labor}}, type "David" in {{Technician}}, pick {{David Combs}} and click {{Save & Close}}.
# Right-click line 1's title and click {{Move labor}}. In {{Technician}} pick {{Tech - ShopView}}; in {{Target Line}} pick {{Service - PDI}}; click {{Move To Line}}. Line 2's {{Labor}} now shows Tech ShopView and David Combs.
# At the top right of the work order, click the three-dot button just left of the blue {{New Line}} button and choose {{Timesheets (1)}}.
# Move the mouse over the Tech ShopView row and click the small edit icon at the right end of the row. The {{Edit Timesheet}} window opens. In {{Line}} pick {{3 Service - Battery Service}} and click {{Save}}.
# Click the {{Lines}} tab. Line 2's {{Labor}} shows {{Tech ShopView}} and {{David Combs}} only, with no empty row and no "Deleted user" row.
