I tested this on staging ([app.staging.shopview.com|https://app.staging.shopview.com/workorders], build v26.40.20-c627fc7) on 21 October 2026, as Admin at Staging Heavy Duty - 9919.

* The Lines tab technician list and the Edit Line dialog now name a technician by their staff record name.
* It is the same name that List, Board View and Tech View show, so one person no longer appears under two names.

For the test I used the technician whose staff record reads *Debra Garcia* while her login account reads *Lindsay Perez*. She is the only active technician on staging with two different names.

h3. How to see it

# Open staging: [https://app.staging.shopview.com/] and sign in as Admin.
# At the top right, click the location and choose {{Staging Heavy Duty - 9919}}.
# In the top menu, click {{Work Orders}}. In the search box above the list, type {{S2-37763}} and click the work order number.
# On the {{Lines}} tab, look at line 1 ({{Service - Transmission service (Automatic)}}). Next to {{Labor}} it reads {{Debra Garcia}} and, under it, {{David Sparks}}.
# Click the line name. In the {{Edit Line}} window, under {{Technicians}}, it reads {{Debra Garcia}} and {{David Sparks}}. Click the X at the top right to close it.
# Go back to {{Work Orders}}. Above the list, click {{Technician}}, type {{Debra}} and tick {{Debra Garcia}}. Click the last of the three view icons ({{Board View}}). The column header and the card for S2-37763 both read {{Debra Garcia}}.
