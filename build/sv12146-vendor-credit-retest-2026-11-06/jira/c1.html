How to repeat it

  1. Open the QA branch sv11839. In the top menu click Parts, then Vendors, and open the vendor ZZAUTOTEST SV12146 A (or any vendor with an unpaid bill).

  2. On the Unpaid Invoices tab click New Credit, enter 100 in the amount, add a memo and save.

  3. Tick the checkbox on a bill row and on the new Credit row, then click New Payment. The credit row in the window reads "Applies $10.00 · $90.00 remaining" for a $10.00 bill. Click Make Payment.

  4. The Unpaid Invoices tab now shows the credit with the rest still open. On the Payments tab click the small arrow at the left of the payment row to open it, then click the receipt icon in the Remittance column to download the slip.
