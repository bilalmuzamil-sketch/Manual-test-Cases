Updated on 9 November 2026, 08:26 CST: the example, steps and picture now use production only.

Summary

In a work order's Add Part box, a word that matches more parts than fit on one page shows only that page. The parts after it can't be found with that word at all; you have to type more of the name, and nothing on the screen says that more parts match. This happens on production.

Steps to reproduce

Production has 210 test parts named ZZ12410 Seal Ring 001 to ZZ12410 Seal Ring 210 (part numbers ZZ12410-001 to ZZ12410-210) in stock at location Trucks Hill 2. They are left in place for this ticket.

  1. Sign in at https://app.shopview.com. At the top right, the location is Trucks Hill 2.

  2. Open work order S2-1943.

  3. On the Lines tab, under line 1, next to Parts, click + Add Part.

  4. Click the Part number box and type ZZ12410.

  5. Scroll the list that opens all the way to the end. It stops at ZZ12410 Seal Ring 200. Seal Ring 201 to 210 never appear (picture, top).

  6. Clear the box and type ZZ12410-210. ZZ12410 Seal Ring 210 appears (picture, bottom), so the part is there.

Fastest way to see it: open S2-1943 on production, click + Add Part under line 1, type ZZ12410 and scroll to the end of the list.

Picture: production. Top: typed ZZ12410 and scrolled to the end; the list stops at Seal Ring 200 of 210. Bottom: typed ZZ12410-210; the part is there.

What should happen

This needs a decision on how a broad search should behave (for example: every matching part can be reached, or the list says more parts match and asks you to narrow the search). Dusan proposed this ticket in his comment on SV-11413:

"The Add Part list shows one page of results. When a word matches more parts than fit, some are always left out."

"Each tag on a part takes up its own row in that page. So parts with several tags mean fewer distinct parts are shown, sometimes well under the page size."

"I suggest we handle both, the page limit on broad searches and the tag rows, together in a separate ticket."

Related: SV-12286 (making search behave the same across the app).
