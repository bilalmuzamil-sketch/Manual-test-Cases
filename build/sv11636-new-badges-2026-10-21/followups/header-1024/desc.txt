Found while testing [SV-11636|https://shopview.atlassian.net/browse/SV-11636]

h3. Summary
When the browser window is 1024 pixels wide (a tablet width), the top menu is wider than the screen. The round profile button with your initials, at the far right, is pushed off the edge: only a thin sliver of it is left, and the page can't be scrolled sideways to bring it back. From about 1100 pixels wide it fits again.

h3. Steps to reproduce
Open staging: [https://app.staging.shopview.com/workorders] (Admin, location *Staging Heavy Duty - 9919*).
# Sign in as Admin. At the top right, click the round button with your initials and check that the location reads *Staging Heavy Duty - 9919*. Close the menu.
# Make the browser window exactly 1024 pixels wide. In Chrome: right-click the page, choose *Inspect*, click the small phone-and-tablet icon at the top left of the panel that opens, then type *1024* in the width box at the top of the page.
# Look at the right end of the top menu, after *Clock In* and the bell. The round profile button is cut off: only a sliver of its left edge shows at the very edge of the screen:
!01-profile-button-cut-off-at-1024-hd.png|width=360,height=685!
# Change the width to *1100*. The whole profile button shows again.

*Fastest way to see it:* open [https://app.staging.shopview.com/workorders] with the window 1024 pixels wide and look at the top right corner.

h3. Expected behaviour
At 1024 pixels wide, everything in the top menu fits on the screen, including the profile button. Nothing is cut off.
*Where this comes from:*
* [SV-11636|https://shopview.atlassian.net/browse/SV-11636], Chris Ward's comment (21 October): at tablet widths everything in the top menu must fit, with nothing cut off — _"at tablet widths around 1024px the nav and the view switcher still fit with the badges on, with nothing overlapping or clipped."_
* Work Orders — Board View & Tech View Display Options, version 73, S1-R14: 1024 pixels is a supported width on any device — _"At 1024px and wider, Tech View and Board View are offered, whatever the device"_

h3. Current behaviour
At 1024 pixels wide, 37 of the profile button's 40 pixels are past the right edge of the screen and can't be scrolled into view. The menu behind it still opens if you manage to click the sliver, but you can't see the button.

It happened on every try:
||Window width||Profile button||
|1024 px (two separate tries)|Cut off, only a 3 px sliver on screen|
|1100 px|Fully visible|
|1180 px|Fully visible|
|1280 px|Fully visible|

Production (build 26.43.0) shows the same at 1024 pixels: there the profile button is entirely past the edge.

Tested on staging, build v26.43.0-e48011d, 22 October 2026.
