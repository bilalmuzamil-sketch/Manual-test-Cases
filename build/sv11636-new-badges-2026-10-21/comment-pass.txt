h3. Where the expected behaviour comes from

* This ticket, "How to check": "Dashboard shows "New" on its top-left corner; nav items are in the same place as on production." and "The view switcher shows one "New" pill centered above it; all three buttons are fully visible and clickable." and "After Oct 31, 2026 07:00 UTC both badges are gone, including in a tab left open across that time."
* Chris Ward's comment on this ticket: the phone menu must not break and, at about 1024 px, nothing may overlap or be cut off.
* Chris Ward in Slack, 22 October: "Dashboard is not visible to anyone that doesnt have reports access, so that pill shouldnt appear if they cannot have reports access" and, for phone, the "new" shouldn't show where the Board / Tech options aren't visible. This matches epic SV-490 (Dashboard): "Access is the existing reports permission, all or nothing, scoped by location".

h3. How to see it

# Open [https://app.staging.shopview.com/workorders] and sign in as Admin.
# At the top right, make sure the location reads {{Staging Heavy Duty - 9919}}.
# Make the browser window at least 1440 pixels wide. In the top menu, a small blue {{New}} pill sits on the top-left corner of {{Dashboard}}, to the right of {{Reports}}.
# Under the top menu, on the right, look at the three view buttons (list, person and board icons). One {{New}} pill sits just above them.
# Make the window narrower than 1024 pixels (or open the page on a phone). The view buttons and their pill are gone. Click the three-line menu icon at the top left: the {{Dashboard}} row has a {{New}} pill.
