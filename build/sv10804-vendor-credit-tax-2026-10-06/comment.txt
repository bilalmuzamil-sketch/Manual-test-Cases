h3. How to see it yourself

Fastest way: open [credit C-13665|https://sv10360.qa.shopview.com/accounting/purchases/credits/01a1119a-0056-714e-aaee-3d1b823fd858] and click {{Journal entry}} *#13732*.

To make a new one:
# Open [sv10360.qa.shopview.com|https://sv10360.qa.shopview.com] as Admin, location {{Staging Heavy Duty - 9919}}.
# Open work order *S2-17435*.
# Under any line's {{Parts}}, click {{+ Add Part}}.
# Click {{More options}}.
# Pick vendor *5 Star Truck Repair*, quantity 2, cost 85.50.
# Click {{Save Part}}.
# Click {{Order}} on the part's row.
# Click {{Receive}}, enter a {{Vendor Invoice Number}}, tick the part and click {{Receive Parts (1)}}.
# Click the part's {{⋮}} > {{Return}}, enter a {{Return Reason}} and {{Quantity}} 2, then click {{Save & Close}}.
# Go to {{Parts}} > {{Returns}}, tick the part's row and click {{Receive Credit}}.
# Enter a {{Credit Memo Number}} and click {{Post Credit}}.
# Click {{ShopHub}} > {{AccountingHub}} > {{Vendor credits}}, open the new credit and click its {{Journal entry}}.

Expected:
* {{1300 Parts Inventory}} is credited the part only ($171.00).
* {{6110 Sales Tax Expense}} is credited the tax ($8.55).

Note: AccountingHub had not been started on this branch, so I started it as of 6 October first ({{Settings}} > {{Onboarding sync}} > {{Go live}}).
