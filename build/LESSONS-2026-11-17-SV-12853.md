## Root causes
1. **The "before" baseline was captured with the app's own time-zone setting not recorded (it was on UTC).** The 12 Nov
   baseline README records only "browser time zone Asia/Karachi". Rule 99's fourth check (the app's own
   `localStorage['timezone']`, which falls back to UTC — amendment 29 Oct) was not done or recorded. The "after" side was
   captured with all four zones matched. The two sides were measured on different clocks, so the "difference" was the test,
   not the product.
2. **"Different from production" was claimed without looking at production.** The production session had expired (401) and
   no new production login was made; the ticket still stated what production shows. That is an inference presented as an
   observation (Rule 12).
3. **No picture of the "before" side.** The ticket says so itself. The one piece of evidence that could have exposed the
   UTC setting was never captured.
4. **A contradicting signal was not resolved before filing.** The developer had written (SV-12663, 13 Nov) that the reports
   keep the time zone they used before. Our own note said "the data shows UTC" and concluded the developer was wrong,
   instead of treating the conflict as a reason to check production on screen first.
5. **The title stated a defect as fact** ("... Different From Production") although the body was a question.
6. **This session's share:** it listed SV-12853 twice as "waiting for Chris" (16 Nov) and its own A/R-A/P worker found the
   new reports consistently use the location's calendar day — the very thing Nemanja later showed production also does —
   but it did not connect the two or challenge the ticket.

## Prevention — RECORDED as Rule 105 (QA lead confirmed 18 Nov: "1. Yes.")
See the chat proposal "Rule 105". Summary:
- A before/after or staging/production comparison is only valid when BOTH sides were observed in the same run conditions:
  same location, all four zones (session, browser, process, app setting) recorded on BOTH sides, screenshots on BOTH sides.
- "Production shows X" may only be written if production was observed that day, on screen, in the location's zone.
  If production cannot be reached: no ticket; tell the QA lead what is missing.
- Every date / day / time-zone finding passes a time-zone challenge before filing: the stored value, the day it falls on in
  the location's zone, and the same record on the reference side (production or the old build) in the same zone.
- A developer statement that contradicts our finding is resolved first (check production / ask), never overruled by our data.
- A finding that compares environments gets an independent "try to disprove it" check by a fresh worker before filing.
- Titles state what the screen shows, never "different from production" unless production was observed.
