*OVERALL QA STATUS: PASSED*

I tested this on the QA branch *sv10841* (build *v26.40.17-c177759*) on *14 October 2026*, and compared it with production (build *v26.40.17-f5bf8f4*).

* When a cored part arrives in two or more deliveries, the {{Resolve cores}} step now lists one core per delivery, and each delivery's core is billed or returned. On production the same work order lists only one.
* Each row shows the amount that will be billed and its own vendor invoice number. The {{+$X to invoice}} total matched what was billed on the line every time.
