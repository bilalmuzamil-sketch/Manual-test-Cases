h3. How to see it

# Open the QA branch: [https://sv11414.qa.shopview.com/] and sign in as Admin. The location at the top right is {{Staging Heavy Duty - 9919}} (Edmonton time).
# In the top menu, click {{Work Orders}} and open a work order in {{Complete}} status for a Net 30 customer. I used *S2-16673* (Monroe Truck Service & Repair, Net 60) for the Net 60 check; *S2-17513* (Orchard City Truck & Trailer Repair) and *S2-17362* (Phillipsburg Truck & Trailer Repair) are Net 30 and still Complete.
# Click the {{Finance}} tab (the last tab, after {{Stats}}).
# Click in the {{Invoice Date}} box next to {{Customer PO}}, select all, type {{10/09/2026}} and press Tab.
# On the invoice below, the {{Due date}} under the invoice number reads {{Nov 8, 2026}} for a Net 30 customer.
