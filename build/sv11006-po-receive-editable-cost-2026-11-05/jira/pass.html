OVERALL QA STATUS: PASSED

Tested on the QA branch sv11006 (build v26.45.2-b7be383) and compared with production (build v26.45.1-753355c) on 5 Nov 2026. All 8 checks in the handoff and all 4 regression areas passed. Every check was run on the current branch build.

A part's cost can now be changed on the purchase order receive screen, which is what the customer asked for. It stays locked once the part's work order is invoiced or paid.

Before and after
