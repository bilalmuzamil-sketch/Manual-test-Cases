⚙ Start every "user can't see / can't do X" ticket with these 6 checks
  1. What role does the user have? (Administration → Staff)
  2. What does that role actually allow? Use "View Permissions" next to the role for a read-only summary.
  3. Did they log out and back in after a role change? Role changes only take effect at the next login.
  4. Is a master switch off? "Parts Department" off hides all parts pages; "Settings" off hides all admin pages — no matter the detailed settings.
  5. Is a money switch off? "See Financial Data" off hides every price and total app-wide; "Manage Accounts Payable and Receivable" off hides payment tabs and credit fields.
  6. Is the user in Tech View? Tech View intentionally hides approve buttons, labor rates, tech time and Send to Portal — that's the simplified technician screen, not a bug.
