# SV-8978 — Sales By Representative on a phone has no separate totals bar under the table

1. What happens now
On a narrow screen the report should show a simple totals bar underneath the table, outside the part that scrolls sideways, with the word Totals on the left and the grand Subtotal on the right.
There is no such bar. The full wide Totals row stays inside the table, so to read the Subtotal total you have to scroll the table all the way to the right.
2. What should happen instead
Below the table, and outside its sideways scroll, a simple bar reading Totals on the left and the grand Subtotal on the right, with no other figures.

4. How bad is it
Low. The figure is reachable, just not where the description puts it and not without scrolling.
5. What we ruled out
Measured at 390 by 844 pixels with touch on. We checked the rest of the mobile layout first and it is right: the table scrolls sideways on its own (1335 pixels of content in a 370 pixel window) while the PAGE does not scroll sideways at all, which is what the description asks for.
6. Where this expected behaviour comes from
Where this expected behaviour comes from: the Sales By Representative report specification, version 17, requirement S10-R5, which says: "Mobile (< 1024px): a simplified external totals bar directly below the table and outside its horizontal scroll container, showing "Totals" left and the grand Subtotal right (no other metrics).". That is source type 2: the specification (PRD) in Confluence. The version number given is the Confluence page version, not the version written inside the page.
