# SV-8944 — Technician Utilization total hours do not match Timesheet Activities for the same technician, range and location

1. What happens now
A technician's Total Hours on Technician Utilization does not agree with the same technician's total on the Timesheet Activities report, even when both cover the same dates, the same single location and only finished clock records. The gap is small but real - up to a quarter of an hour on the technicians checked.
2. What should happen instead
Technician Utilization specification, version 6, requirement S1-R9: "For the same date range and the same single location, and counting closed clock records, this report's Total Hours for a technician equals the Timesheet Activities report's total for that technician to the cent."
The same specification, section 3: "The Timesheet Activities report rounds the same way this report does (round-half-up, two decimals) from the same clock data; the to-the-cent reconciliation (S1-R9) depends on that shared rounding."
3. How to see it
Open Reports > Technician Utilization, set the date range to This Year and choose the single location Staging Heavy Duty - 9919.
Find the row for Andrew Wade and write down his Total Hours.
Click that Total Hours figure. Timesheet Activities opens for the same technician and the same dates. Wait for the page to finish loading - the staff name settles to Andrew Wade - and read the Totals line at the bottom.
Exact test data this was seen with: organisation Staging Foothills Group Inc; signed-in user Admin ShopView; active location Staging Heavy Duty - 9919; the other accessible location Staging Lethbridge - 4310; date range This Year (1 January 2026 to 6 August 2026), which returns 29 technicians. The technicians compared were Andrew Wade, Brittany Anderson, Colleen Guerrero, Fernando Mercer, Kellie Ayers, Angela Roman, Christian Pitts and Alyssa Randall.
4. What was actually shown
Technician Utilization showed Andrew Wade 1080.44 hours. Timesheet Activities showed 1080.64 for the same technician and dates - a difference of 0.20 hours.
Six of the eight technicians compared disagreed: Andrew Wade +0.20, Fernando Mercer +0.25, Kellie Ayers +0.21, Brittany Anderson +0.16, Colleen Guerrero +0.05, Angela Roman -0.01 and Christian Pitts -0.03. Alyssa Randall matched exactly at 744.51.
The likely cause, from the underlying data: Timesheet Activities adds up each clock record after that record has already been rounded to two decimals, while Technician Utilization adds up the raw times and rounds once at the end. Andrew Wade's 604 individual records add up to 1080.62 once each has been rounded, against 1080.4422 raw.
5. What was ruled out
It is not an open clock. Andrew Wade has no clock records at all in August 2026, so nothing was still running when either report was read, and the two readings were taken two seconds apart.
It is not the multi-location drill-through difference the specification allows. Technician Utilization was scoped to the single location Staging Heavy Duty - 9919, which is also the shop the user is signed in to, and this technician has no hours at the other location - his figure is identical whether one location or both are selected.
It is not a date-range difference. Both reports covered 1 January 2026 to 6 August 2026.
Note for triage: two older tickets described a similar disagreement between the previous reports and Timesheet Activities - SV-6431 (Done) and SV-5334 (obsolete). This one is about the new Technician Utilization report.
6. How often
Every time, and on most technicians - six of the eight checked.
7. Where it was seen
Reports > Technician Utilization, on the QA branch, build v3.5-16cf83f, on 6 August 2026.
 
Where this expected behaviour comes from: the Technician Utilization report specification, version 6, requirement S1-R9, which says: "Reconciliation guarantee (scope). For the same date range and the same single location, and counting closed clock records, this report's Total Hours for a technician equals the Timesheet Activities report's total for that technician to the cent."
