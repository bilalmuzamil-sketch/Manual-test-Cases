# SV-8959 — Schedule: hover tooltip puts the conflict warning at the bottom, not beside the customer name

Snapshot taken before the 2026-08-06 reformat. Live `updated` at snapshot time: 2026-08-06T02:50:31.224-0500

---

### What happens now

When you hover a shift that has a clash, the tooltip does warn you - but the warning sits at the very bottom of the tooltip, several rows below the customer name. The customer name row itself carries no warning mark.

### Why this matters

The customer name is the first thing anyone reads. Putting the warning at the bottom means a planner scanning quickly can miss that the shift has a clash at all.

### How to see it

- Open the Schedule page in Week view.
- Find a shift that shows an amber warning triangle on the block itself.
- Hover over the middle of that block and wait for the tooltip.
- Read the tooltip from the top down.

### What you see

- The first row is just the customer name - for example "Brabay Maintenance" - with no warning mark next to it.
- The warning appears only in the last row, as "Starts before working hours - Extends past working hours - Double-booked" with a triangle beside it.
- The warning text is amber, which is correct.

### What should happen

From the Schedule specification, section 4.13, the shift tooltip's first line is "customer name (plus the conflict icon if conflicted)". The warning mark belongs next to the customer name.

### Exact test data used

- Location: Staging Heavy Duty - 9919. Build v3.5-7ec992f. Signed in as admin@shopview.com.
- The shift hovered: Brabay Maintenance, VIN C44ECN300699, Tuesday 4 August 2026, 27 lines, technician Ayesha Khan AK, three clash reasons at once.
- Week of 3 August 2026, Week view, at a 1680 by 1050 window.

### Things checked and ruled out first

- Not a missing tooltip - the whole tooltip renders correctly, with customer, VIN, date and time, technician, line list and progress bar.
- Not the amber colour - the reason text is amber as the specification asks, so only the position of the mark is wrong.
- Not the wrong tooltip - the shift's own tooltip was read, not the small tooltip that the warning triangle on the block shows on its own.
- Not the same as SV-8877, which is about the conflict LIST not naming the technician or day. This is the hover tooltip's layout.

### Where this came from

Found while re-checking Schedule test case SCH-TIP-02 (TestRail C30035) on 6 August 2026.

 

Where this expected behaviour comes from: the Schedule specification, version 23, section 4.13 (Hover tooltips), which sets out the order of the tooltip's contents beginning: "Shift tooltip: customer name (plus the conflict icon if conflicted); unit, vehicle, and VIN; date and time range; technician; scope summary..." and ending "...and the conflict reason in amber when conflicted." So the reason at the bottom is correct; what is missing is the icon beside the customer name on the first line.
