# SV-8851 — Turning on the Tech Hours option in View Options changes nothing on the screen

Snapshot taken before the 2026-08-06 reformat. Live `updated` at snapshot time: 2026-08-06T03:43:46.902-0500

---

### Description

The View Options menu offers a 'Tech Hours' switch. Turning it on is supposed to show each technician's working hours next to their name, so a planner can see at a glance who starts at 6 and who starts at 8. Switching it on and off makes no visible difference anywhere on the page - not even for the one technician in this shop who actually has custom hours saved.

### Steps to reproduce

- Open the Schedule and pick the Week view.
- Note that technician Ayesha Khan has custom hours saved in Edit Staff Member: Monday 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM and Tuesday to Friday 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM, with Saturday and Sunday set to Not working.
- Click the View Options control in the toolbar (the sliders icon on the right).
- Note that 'Tech Hours' starts switched off, and switch it on.
- Close the menu and look at the technician names down the left-hand side, including Ayesha Khan's row.
- Switch 'Tech Hours' back off and compare - the screen is identical.

### Expected behaviour

With Tech Hours on, each technician's working hours appear beside their name (for example '7:00 AM - 7:00 PM' next to Ayesha Khan), and disappear again when it is switched off.

### Current behaviour

Nothing changes. The rows show only the initials, the name and the job title, both before and after. The number of shaded cells on the grid is also identical before, during and after (2 in every case), so no shading changes either.

toggle_schedule_technician_hours flips its aria-checked correctly, so the control itself works and the preference is stored - nothing consumes it. A/B measured: the .fc-datagrid-cell-cushion text for the Ayesha Khan row was byte-identical ('AK Ayesha Khan') before, with the toggle on, and after; .fc-cell-shaded count 2 / 2 / 2. Spec section 9: 'Tech Hours | Off | Displays each technician's working hours next to their name.'

 

Where this expected behaviour comes from: the Schedule specification, version 23, section 9 (View options and customization). Its View Options table gives the Tech Hours option a default of Off and this effect: "Displays each technician's working hours next to their name."
