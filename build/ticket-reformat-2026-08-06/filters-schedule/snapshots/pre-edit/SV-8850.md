# SV-8850 — The '+N more' link on a crowded day opens an empty box - the hidden shifts are never listed

Snapshot taken before the 2026-08-06 reformat. Live `updated` at snapshot time: 2026-08-06T03:35:21.057-0500

---

### Description

When a technician has more than three overlapping jobs on the same day, the extra ones are folded away behind a '+1 more' link. Clicking that link opens a small box that shows only the date and the capacity bar - the hidden jobs are not listed. There is no other way to reach them from that view, so work that is genuinely on the schedule is invisible to the planner.

### Steps to reproduce

- Open the Schedule and pick the Week view for the week of Aug 2 to 8, 2026.
- Look at technician Colleen Guerrero's row, Sunday Aug 2 - she has four overlapping jobs there (Ceview Builders unit 10, Pamill Paving unit 713, Xiriver Apparel unit 16604 and one more).
- Note that only three blocks are drawn and a '+1 more' link sits underneath them.
- Click '+1 more'.
- Read what is inside the box that opens.

### Expected behaviour

The box lists the hidden job or jobs, so the planner can see them and click through to them.

### Current behaviour

The box opens with the heading 'August 2, 2026' and a close X, and its body contains only the day cell and its capacity bar. No job is listed at all.

### Technical details for developers

The popover element is .fc-popover.fc-more-popover; its .fc-popover-body contains only .fc-more-popover-misc (the schedule-day-cell and its capacity_bar) and zero .schedule-block children. Confirmed by reading the popover's innerHTML directly.

 

Where this expected behaviour comes from: the Schedule specification, version 23, section 4.7, which says: "Visible lanes are capped at 3. Additional overlapping shifts collapse into a “+N more” affordance that opens a popover listing the hidden shifts. This applies in day, week, and month views (week and month reach the overflow much sooner because cells are narrower)." Listing the hidden shifts is the whole purpose the specification gives that control.
