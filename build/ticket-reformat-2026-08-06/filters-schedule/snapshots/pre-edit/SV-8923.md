# SV-8923 — Schedule: the Business Hours switch shades nothing - out-of-hours time looks identical to the working day

Snapshot taken before the 2026-08-06 reformat. Live `updated` at snapshot time: 2026-08-06T01:07:57.442-0500

---

### Description

The Schedule has a display switch called Business Hours. It is meant to grey out the parts of the day the shop is not open, so that a planner can see at a glance which part of the row is real working time and which part is the middle of the night.

The switch does nothing you can see. With it turned on, the whole timeline from midnight through to eleven at night is the same flat white as the working day, so a shift placed at three in the morning looks exactly as normal as one placed at ten in the morning.

It matters because this is the only cue on the board that tells a planner where the working day begins and ends. Without it, out-of-hours work is invisible until somebody notices the time and questions it, and the switch itself looks broken because turning it on and off changes nothing on screen.

### Steps to reproduce

- Sign in as a user who can view the schedule (an Administrator is fine) and pick the location Staging Heavy Duty - 9919.
- Open Schedule from the top navigation.
- Switch to Day view using the Day / Week / Month control, and go to Thursday, 6 August 2026. Any day behaves the same way; the fault does not depend on the date or on any particular work order, technician or shift, and no test data is needed to see it.
- Open the FILTER & DISPLAY dropdown in the toolbar and find the Business Hours switch. It is already on, which is its default.
- Look along the timeline from 12 AM through to 11 PM.
- Turn the Business Hours switch off, look again, then turn it back on and look a third time.

### Expected behaviour

With the switch on, the hours outside the working day should be covered by a grey overlay so they are visibly different from the working part of the day.

The Schedule specification asks for exactly that, in two places:

"With the toggle on, the hours OUTSIDE the working day are shaded with a grey overlay."

Governing requirement: Schedule specification, Confluence page 713031682, version 23, section 9 (Business Hours) and section 4.8 (Business-hours shading).

### Current behaviour

- With the switch ON, the timeline from 12 AM to 11 PM renders uniformly white. Nothing is shaded, tinted or bordered.
- With the switch OFF, the timeline looks identical.
- With the switch turned back ON, the timeline still looks identical.
- The only shaded element anywhere in the calendar in all three states is the divider between the department groups, which is unrelated to the time of day.

### Technical details for developers

Measured with the toggle ON, OFF and restored to ON. In each of the three states the count of elements carrying the shaded cell class was the same:

- Elements matching fc-cell-shaded: 2 in all three states. Both are the resource-timeline divider (class fc-resource-timeline-divider fc-cell-shaded), which separates the department groups; neither is a time-of-day overlay.
- No element in the Day-view time grid carries any non-working-hours class, background tint or overlay in any of the three states.
- Confirmed visually on the captured screenshot, not only by class name.
