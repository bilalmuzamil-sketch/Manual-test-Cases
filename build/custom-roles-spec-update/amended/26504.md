# TestRail Case 26504 — Amendment Audit Log

**Case URL:** https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/cases/view/26504
**Section:** Custom Roles - (Revised) > Per-Role Verification (section 3548)
**Amended:** 2026-07-05
**Spec change:** #6 — MANAGE AP/AR (Removed gate). The 6 AP/AR aging reports (AR Aging Summary,
AR Aging Detail, AR Aging Collection, AP Aging Summary, AP Aging Detail, AP Unpaid Invoices) are
no longer gated by Manage Accounts Payable and Receivable. They now follow the Reports permission
(all-or-nothing): Reports ON => all 6 aging reports visible/openable regardless of Manage AP/AR;
Reports OFF => none available. Manage AP/AR still gates the customer/vendor AP/AR TABS
(Unpaid Invoices/Payments/Credits) and sensitive customer fields (unchanged).

Authorized write: POST /api/v2/update_case/26504, HTTP 200. Only fields title, custom_preconds,
custom_steps, custom_expected were updated. No other case was touched.

**What changed:** Clarified that AP/AR aging-report access comes from the Reports permission being ON (all-or-nothing), NOT from See AP/AR. See AP/AR only controls the customer/vendor AP/AR tabs and sensitive customer fields.

```
5. Verify Cross-Cutting: See Financial ON, See AP/AR ON, View History Logs OFF.

```

```
3. The user can open the Reports area and all 6 AP/AR aging reports, because the Reports permission is ON.
4. Access to the AP/AR aging reports comes from the Reports permission (all-or-nothing), NOT from See AP/AR. See AP/AR only controls the customer/vendor AP/AR tabs and sensitive customer fields, not the aging reports.
```
