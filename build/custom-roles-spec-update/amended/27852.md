## AFTER (as stored)

**Title:**
```
Verify Office can open AP/AR reports and customer AP/AR tabs
```

**Preconditions (custom_preconds):**
```
1. Log in to ShopView as a user assigned the Office role.
2. The role has the Reports permission ON (this grants access to the AP/AR aging reports).
3. The role has Manage Accounts Payable and Receivable ON (this controls the customer/vendor AP/AR tabs: Unpaid Invoices, Payments, Credits).
4. Invoice/payment data exists so the reports and tabs have content.
```

**Steps to Reproduce (custom_steps):**
```
1. Click Reports in the top navigation.
2. Open the 6 AP/AR aging reports one at a time: AR Aging Summary, AR Aging Detail, AR Aging Collection, AP Aging Summary, AP Aging Detail, AP Unpaid Invoices.
3. Click Customers and open a customer record.
4. Open the Unpaid Invoices, Payments, and Credits tabs on the customer.
```

**Expected Results (custom_expected):**
```
1. All 6 AP/AR aging reports open with data and no permission error. They are available because the Reports permission is ON (aging reports follow the Reports permission, not Manage AP/AR).
2. The customer AP/AR tabs (Unpaid Invoices, Payments, Credits) are visible and open successfully. These tabs are controlled by Manage Accounts Payable and Receivable (unchanged by this spec).
3. The two are gated independently: the aging reports depend on the Reports permission, while the customer/vendor AP/AR tabs depend on Manage AP/AR. Turning Manage AP/AR OFF would hide the tabs but would NOT hide the aging reports.
```
