**Amended:** 2026-07-05
**Spec change:** #6 — MANAGE AP/AR (Removed gate). The 6 AP/AR aging reports (AR Aging Summary,
AR Aging Detail, AR Aging Collection, AP Aging Summary, AP Aging Detail, AP Unpaid Invoices) are
no longer gated by Manage Accounts Payable and Receivable. They now follow the Reports permission
(all-or-nothing): Reports ON => all 6 aging reports visible/openable regardless of Manage AP/AR;
Reports OFF => none available. Manage AP/AR still gates the customer/vendor AP/AR TABS
(Unpaid Invoices/Payments/Credits) and sensitive customer fields (unchanged).

Authorized write: POST /api/v2/update_case/27757, HTTP 200. Only fields title, custom_preconds,
custom_steps, custom_expected were updated. No other case was touched.

**What changed:** Re-attributed aging-report access from Manage AP/AR to the Reports permission. Expanded to the 6 canonical aging-report names. Added that Manage AP/AR ON/OFF does not change aging-report visibility.

## AFTER (as stored)

**Title:**
```
Verify Administrator can open all AP/AR reports
```

**Preconditions (custom_preconds):**
```
1. Log in to ShopView as a user assigned the Administrator role.
2. The role has the Reports permission ON (this is what grants access to the AP/AR aging reports).
3. The role also has Manage Accounts Payable and Receivable ON (this controls the customer/vendor AP/AR tabs, not the aging reports).
4. Invoice/payment data exists so the reports have content.
```

**Steps to Reproduce (custom_steps):**
```
1. Click Reports in the main navigation.
2. Open the AR Aging Summary report.
3. Open the AR Aging Detail report.
4. Open the AR Aging Collection report.
5. Open the AP Aging Summary report.
6. Open the AP Aging Detail report.
7. Open the AP Unpaid Invoices report.
```

**Expected Results (custom_expected):**
```
1. Each of the 6 AP/AR aging reports opens and displays data. No report is hidden and none returns a permission (403) error.
2. The aging reports are available because the Reports permission is ON; access follows the Reports permission (all-or-nothing), not Manage AP/AR.
3. Manage Accounts Payable and Receivable being ON or OFF does not change the visibility of the aging reports - they would still open with Reports ON.
```
