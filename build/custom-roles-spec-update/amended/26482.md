## AFTER (as stored)

**Title:**
```
AP/AR OFF + Reports ON: all 6 AP/AR aging reports are STILL visible (aging follows the Reports permission)
```

**Preconditions (custom_preconds):**
```
1. Log in as a user assigned a custom role.
2. The role has the Reports permission turned ON.
3. The role has Manage Accounts Payable and Receivable turned OFF.
4. There is at least some AP/AR data in the account (a few invoices/payments) so the reports have rows to show.
```

**Steps to Reproduce (custom_steps):**
```
1. Click Reports in the top navigation.
2. Look at the list of available reports on the Reports page.
3. Find each of the 6 AP/AR aging reports in the list: AR Aging Summary, AR Aging Detail, AR Aging Collection, AP Aging Summary, AP Aging Detail, AP Unpaid Invoices.
4. Click AR Aging Summary to open it.
5. Go back to Reports and open each of the other 5 aging reports one at a time (AR Aging Detail, AR Aging Collection, AP Aging Summary, AP Aging Detail, AP Unpaid Invoices).
```

**Expected Results (custom_expected):**
```
1. The Reports page opens.
2. All 6 AP/AR aging reports are listed, even though Manage Accounts Payable and Receivable is OFF (aging reports now follow the Reports permission, not Manage AP/AR).
3. All 6 reports are present in the list: AR Aging Summary, AR Aging Detail, AR Aging Collection, AP Aging Summary, AP Aging Detail, AP Unpaid Invoices.
4. AR Aging Summary opens normally and shows its data with no permission error.
5. Each of the other 5 aging reports also opens normally with no permission error. No aging report is hidden, greyed out, or blocked because Manage AP/AR is OFF.
```
