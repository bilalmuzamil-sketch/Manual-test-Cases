# CORRECTION — 2026-07-06 (product-owner scope fix)

**Reason:** The View History Logs permission covers work-order-level history AND line-level (line story/history) — but for **WORK ORDERS ONLY**. There is NO history log for Part Sales or Purchase Orders. The prior amendment above wrongly asserted "part sales and parts orders (PO)" history behavior — removed. ON scenario preserved.
