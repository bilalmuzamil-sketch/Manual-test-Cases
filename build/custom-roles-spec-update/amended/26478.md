# TestRail Case 26478 — Amendment Audit Log

**Case URL:** https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/cases/view/26478
**Section:** Custom Roles - (Revised) > Manage Accounts Payable and Receivable (section 3545)
**Amended:** 2026-07-05
**Spec change:** #6 — MANAGE AP/AR (Removed gate). The 6 AP/AR aging reports (AR Aging Summary,
AR Aging Detail, AR Aging Collection, AP Aging Summary, AP Aging Detail, AP Unpaid Invoices) are
no longer gated by Manage Accounts Payable and Receivable. They now follow the Reports permission
(all-or-nothing): Reports ON => all 6 aging reports visible/openable regardless of Manage AP/AR;
Reports OFF => none available. Manage AP/AR still gates the customer/vendor AP/AR TABS
(Unpaid Invoices/Payments/Credits) and sensitive customer fields (unchanged).

Authorized write: POST /api/v2/update_case/26478, HTTP 200. Only fields title, custom_preconds,
custom_steps, custom_expected were updated. No other case was touched.

**What changed:** Re-attributed the reason: with Reports ON the 6 aging reports are listed BECAUSE of the Reports permission (all-or-nothing), not because Manage AP/AR is ON. Case stays PASS. Title updated to make the Reports-permission attribution explicit.

## AFTER (as stored)

**Title:**
```
Reports ON: all 6 AP/AR aging reports listed (regardless of Manage AP/AR)
```

**Preconditions (custom_preconds):**
```
1. Log in as a user assigned a custom role.
2. The role has the Reports permission turned ON.
3. The role has Manage Accounts Payable and Receivable turned ON.
4. There is at least some AP/AR data in the account (a few invoices/payments) so the reports have rows to show.
```

**Steps to Reproduce (custom_steps):**
```
1. Click Reports in the top navigation.
2. Look at the list of available reports on the Reports page.
3. Find each of the 6 AP/AR aging reports in the list: AR Aging Summary, AR Aging Detail, AR Aging Collection, AP Aging Summary, AP Aging Detail, AP Unpaid Invoices.
4. Open each of the 6 aging reports one at a time.
```

**Expected Results (custom_expected):**
```
1. The Reports page opens.
2. All 6 AP/AR aging reports are listed. They are shown because the Reports permission is ON (aging reports follow the Reports permission, not Manage AP/AR).
3. All 6 reports are present: AR Aging Summary, AR Aging Detail, AR Aging Collection, AP Aging Summary, AP Aging Detail, AP Unpaid Invoices.
4. Each of the 6 reports opens normally and shows its data with no permission error.
5. Manage Accounts Payable and Receivable being ON or OFF does not change this - the same 6 aging reports would be listed either way, because their visibility depends only on the Reports permission.
```
