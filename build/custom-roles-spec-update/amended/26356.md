# Case 26356 — Amendment

Case URL: https://shopview.testrail.io/index.php?/cases/view/26356
Section: 3532
Updated: 2026-07-06 (authorized single-case write)

Reason: Case was previously blocked/uncertain. Product owner confirmed (with
screenshot) that the read-only Permission Summary is opened via an EYE icon
positioned next to the Role selection dropdown on the Edit Staff Member modal.

## BEFORE

**Title:** Permission Summary is accessible from the Staff page next to the role selector

**Preconditions:**
On Settings sidebar > Staff page, editing or assigning a user.

**Steps:**
1. Open the role selector for any user.
2. Select 'Foreman' from the dropdown.
3. Locate the 'View Permissions' button.
4. Click 'View Permissions'.

**Expected:**
Step 4: Same read-only Permission Summary opens, showing Foreman's full permission set matching the Foreman matrix column..

## AFTER

**Title:** View a role's permission summary from the Edit Staff Member modal via the eye icon next to the Role dropdown

**Preconditions:**
1. Log in as a user who can edit staff (e.g. Admin).
2. At least one staff member exists.

**Steps:**
1. Go to the Staff page (Administration > Staff).
2. Open a staff member to bring up the Edit Staff Member modal.
3. Find the Role dropdown and the eye icon next to it.
4. Click the eye icon next to the Role dropdown.

**Expected:**
1. An eye icon appears next to the Role selection dropdown on the Edit Staff Member modal.
2. Clicking the eye icon opens a read-only Permission Summary for the role currently selected in the dropdown.
3. The summary lists that role's permissions in read-only form (no editing); it reflects whatever role is chosen in the dropdown.
