Work order made with its lead:
# Click *Schedule* in the top menu and find a technician's row (we used *Owen Created QA1019*).
# Click an empty cell in that row. A small menu opens: click *New Work Order*.
# Choose a *Customer* and an *Asset*, then click *Save*. Do this a few times for customers whose names sort differently.
# Go back to *Work Orders* > *All* > *Tech View* and find that technician's group: the work orders are in the List's order (customer A to Z), not the order you made them. The four we made are S2-37443, S2-37446, S2-37444, S2-37442 under *Owen Created QA1019*.
