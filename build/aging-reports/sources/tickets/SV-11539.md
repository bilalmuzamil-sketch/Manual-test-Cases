# SV-11539 — A/R and A/P Report Sorting - Story 9 - A/R Collection call list
Story · TESTING QA · assignee None · parent SV-11315 · QA branch https://sv11315.qa.shopview.com

## Description
h2. Summary

Show each customer's phone and, for users who manage A/R and A/P data, their credit term on A/R Collection's customer rows and in its exports, so it works as a call list.

h2. Context

As a user chasing overdue accounts, I want each customer's phone and credit term on A/R Collection so that I can call without opening every customer.

Prerequisites: User must have 'Reports → Page Access' setting enabled.

Form factor: Desktop only by rule: detailed reports.

h2. Requirements

* S9-R1: On A/R Collection, each customer's group row shows, after the customer's name, "Phone: " and the customer's phone number as the customer card shows it.
* S9-R2: For users with the 'View and Manage AP/AR Data' switch, the group row also shows "Credit term: " and the customer's credit term as the customer card shows it ("COD" when none is set).
* S9-R3: The PDF prints the same phone and credit term on each customer's line; the CSV carries them in columns (S3-R8a).
* S9-N1: A customer with no phone shows no "Phone:" label, on screen or in the PDF.
* S9-N2: A user without 'View and Manage AP/AR Data' sees no credit term on screen, none in the PDF, and no Credit term column in the CSV.

h2. Acceptance Criteria

* Given a customer with a phone, then the customer's group row shows "Phone: <number>" after the name, as the customer card shows it (S9-R1).
* Given a user with 'View and Manage AP/AR Data', then the row also shows "Credit term: <term>", "COD" when none is set (S9-R2).
* Given the PDF and CSV, then the PDF prints the same on each customer's line and the CSV carries "Phone" and "Credit term" columns after "Customer" (S9-R3, S3-R8a).
* Given a customer with no phone, then no "Phone:" label shows on screen or in the PDF (S9-N1).
* Given a user without 'View and Manage AP/AR Data', then no credit term shows on screen or in the PDF, and the CSV has no Credit term column (S9-N2).

h2. QA Checklist (do these)

h3. Screen [S9-R1, S9-R2, S9-N1]

* Each customer group row reads "Phone: …" after the bold name and count. The pinned row repeats it.
* With View and Manage AP/AR Data: "Credit term: …" (small t) too; "COD" when the customer has no term.
* A customer without a phone shows no "Phone:" label.
* Scroll the table sideways (narrower than 1440 px): the customer name and count stay at the left; Phone and Credit term slide under them.

h3. Permission [S9-N2]

* Custom role with Reports → Page Access only (no View and Manage AP/AR Data): no Credit term on screen, in the pinned row, in the PDF or in the CSV (no column at all).

h3. Exports [S3-R8a, S9-R3]

* [ ] Download (CSV): headings "Date, …, ID, Customer, Phone, *Credit Term*, Due Date, …" — "Credit Term" with a capital T, only with the permission. Each row carries its customer's phone and term (COD when none).
* [ ] Download (PDF): each customer line reads "Phone: … Credit term: …" (small t; it is a label, not a heading).

h3. The rest of the list

* Only overdue invoices (Past Due 1 or more, in calendar days at the location) plus unused credit memos and deposits. An invoice due today is not listed.
* Totals follow the Customer filter: tick one customer → the Totals row equals that customer's "Total" [S5-R18].

h2. Known mismatches (not bugs)

* Jira SV-11539's acceptance criteria say "Credit term" for the CSV column. The PRD (v10) and the build say "Credit Term". The screen and PDF keep "Credit term:".

h2. What is NOT impacted

* The rest of A/R Collection (groups, sort, links, 100-document windows) — see SV-11531 to SV-11537.
* Customer records themselves (phone and term are read only).
