# SV-11536 — A/R and A/P Report Sorting - Story 6 - Location column
Story · TESTING QA · assignee None · parent SV-11315 · QA branch https://sv11315.qa.shopview.com

## Description
h2. Summary

Give all six reports a column labelled "Location", shown when two or more locations are in scope, so every row says where its document lives.

h2. Context

As a user of a multi-location shop, I want to see each row's location so that the Location filter has something to match.

Prerequisites: User must have 'Reports → Page Access' setting enabled.

Form factor: Desktop only by rule: detailed reports.

h2. Requirements

* S6-R1: All six reports have a column labelled exactly "Location". This renames A/R Detail's and A/R Collection's "Location full name" and A/P Detail's and A/P Unpaid Invoices' "Location Name", and adds the column to A/R Summary and A/P Summary.
* S6-R2: The column is shown when two or more locations are ticked (All locations counts when the report's list has two or more) and hidden when one or none is ticked.
* S6-R3: It sits right after the Date column, before the money columns; on A/R Summary and A/P Summary, which have no Date column, right after the customer or vendor name.
* S6-R4: On A/R Summary and A/P Summary a row shows the location's name when all of that customer's or vendor's open documents at the chosen locations are at one location, otherwise "Multiple".
* S6-R5: The column sorts A to Z on its first click; "Multiple" sorts as text.
* S6-N1: With one location chosen, the column is hidden on screen and in the export.

h2. Acceptance Criteria

* Given any of the six reports, then the column is labelled exactly "Location" (replacing "Location full name" and "Location Name"; new on the Summaries) (S6-R1).
* Given All locations with two or more locations in the report's list, or two or more chosen, then the column shows; given one location chosen, then it is hidden on screen and in the export (S6-R2, S6-N1, S3-R6).
* Given a detail report, then the column sits right after Date; on the Summaries right after the name (S6-R3).
* Given a Summary row whose open documents at the chosen locations are at one location, then it shows that location; otherwise "Multiple"; the column sorts A to Z on its first click, "Multiple" as text (S6-R4, S6-R5).
