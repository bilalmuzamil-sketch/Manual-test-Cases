C715539 Design: the pinned customer row matches the design picture
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role).
- Make sure customer A and customer B are next to each other in name order, and each has more overdue invoices than fit on the screen.
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide, in light theme at 100% zoom.
- Open A/R Collection (Reports, then "A/R Aging Collection" under ACCOUNTS RECEIVABLE in the left menu).
- Leave the report with every group expanded.
- Open the design picture "The customer row stays pinned" (ar-ap-p3-the-customer-row-stays-pinned.png) (attached to this case) beside the browser at 100% zoom; compare the layout, labels and styles, not the names and amounts, which are only examples.
STEPS: - Scroll down slowly into customer A's rows.
- Keep scrolling until customer B's group row reaches the top.
- Widen the browser window to 1536 px and look across the page.
EXP: Expected Result
Step 1:
- Customer A's group row stays just under the column headings while its rows slide beneath it.
- As in the picture, the pinned row shows the blue chevron, the bold name and count and the grey Phone and Credit term, over the rows sliding beneath it.Step 2:
- Customer B's row pushes customer A's up and off and takes its place.
- Only one group row is pinned at a time.Step 3:
- Nothing overlaps, is cut off or clipped.
- The layout is the same as at 1440 px.
