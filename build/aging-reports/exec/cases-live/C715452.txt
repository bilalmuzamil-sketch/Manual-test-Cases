C715452 A/R Collection: 30 customers at a time, none repeated or skipped
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role).
- Make sure that between 31 and 90 customers have overdue invoices on today's date.
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide.
- Open A/R Collection (Reports, then "A/R Aging Collection" under ACCOUNTS RECEIVABLE in the left menu).
STEPS: - Choose "Download (CSV)" from the ⋯ menu.
- Open the CSV and count the customers in it (each name once).
- Count the customers shown before any more load (or compare them with the first 30 names in the CSV) and note the last one.
- Scroll down until the next customers load.
- Keep scrolling to the end of the list, writing down each customer's name.
- Click the "Open Balance" header.
- Scroll from the top to the end of the list again, writing down each name.
- In the Past due filter, tick "1-30 days".
- Tick "91+ days".
- Scroll from the top to the end of the list again, writing down each name.
EXP: Expected Result
Step 1:
- The CSV downloads.Step 2:
- Note the number of customers (for example 46).Step 3:
- Exactly 30 customers.Step 4:
- The next customers load below as you scroll (there are no page buttons), starting with the customer that comes right after the last one noted in step 3, not a repeat.Step 5:
- Every customer appears exactly once and the count equals the CSV's.Step 6:
- The rows sort by Open Balance, largest first.Step 7:
- In this sort too, every customer appears exactly once and none is missing.Step 8:
- The chip reads "Past due: 1-30 days" and the report reloads with that range only.Step 9:
- The chip reads "Past due: 2 ranges" and the report reloads with both ranges.Step 10:
- Every customer left by the filter appears exactly once.
- None repeats as more load.
Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
From: PRD "A/R and A/P Report Sorting, Export and Location Filter", Confluence 947650561, version 11 (5 Nov 2026, Chris Ward), read 10 Nov 2026 · Epic SV-11315, story SV-11531 (Story 1, Reports open in QuickBooks name order)
- S1-R8: “A/R Summary, A/P Summary, A/R Collection and A/P Unpaid Invoices show 30 customers or vendors at a time, and the next 30 load as the user scrolls down, as today. There are no page buttons. A/R Detail and A/P Detail show each bucket's first 100 lines, then load more as the user scrolls (S4-R4). Paging never repeats or skips a customer, vendor or row, in any sort. On A/R Collection and A/P Unpaid Invoices, an open customer or vendor shows its first 100 documents, then the next 100 as the user scrolls; its document count, its subtotals, the Totals row and the exports always cover all of its documents.”
- What changes for existing users: “A/R reports no longer come out in an unpredictable order; A/R Summary and A/R Collection no longer repeat or skip customers between pages.”Last checked against build v26.45.2-6a8b89c (QA site sv11315) on 11/10/2026.
