C715542 Design: the description tooltip matches the pictures in both themes
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role).
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide, in light theme at 100% zoom.
- Open A/R Collection (Reports, then "A/R Aging Collection" under ACCOUNTS RECEIVABLE in the left menu).
- Open the design pictures "Info icon open, light" (ar-ap-p6-info-icon-open-light.png) and "Info icon open, dark" (ar-ap-p6-info-icon-open-dark.png) (attached to this case) beside the browser at 100% zoom; compare the layout, labels and styles, not the names and amounts, which are only examples.
STEPS: - Hover over the info icon right of the title.
- Move the pointer away.
- Switch ShopView to dark theme.
- Hover over the info icon again.
EXP: Expected Result
Step 1:
- A tooltip opens.
- As in the light picture, the icon turns blue.
- The tooltip is a dark-grey box with white text on four lines, opening below the icon and starting near it, over the left of the filter row.
- It reads, letter for letter, "Overdue invoices, unused credit memos and deposits for each customer to help you follow up on payments. Excludes invoices that aren’t due yet."Step 2:
- The tooltip closes.Step 3:
- The page switches to dark theme.Step 4:
- The tooltip opens in dark theme, as in the dark picture: a mid-grey box with white text, in the same place and with the same words, readable.
