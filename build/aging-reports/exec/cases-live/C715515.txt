C715515 Design: A/R Export menu matches Sales By Customer's ⋯ menu
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role).
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide, in light theme at 100% zoom.
- Open A/R Collection (Reports, then "A/R Aging Collection" under ACCOUNTS RECEIVABLE in the left menu).
- Open Sales By Customer (Reports, then "Sales By Customer" under PERFORMANCE in the left menu) in a second tab of the same window, for comparison.
STEPS: - Hover over the ⋯ button at the right of the title row.
- Click it and read the menu, letter for letter.
- Compare with Sales By Customer's ⋯ menu.
EXP: Expected Result
Step 1:
- The ⋯ button is at the right of the title row and is labelled "Export report".Step 2:
- "Download (PDF)", then "Download (CSV)".Step 3:
- The same button, menu, order and position.
