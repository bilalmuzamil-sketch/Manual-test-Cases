C715617 Sales By Customer link, PDF and CSV work as before the release
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role).
- Make sure the organization has two or more locations, so one location can be ticked in Sales By Customer's Location filter.
- Make sure that before the release was installed, you saved Sales By Customer's PDF and CSV for an as-of date before the release with one location ticked, and copied a link to Sales By Customer with that filter.
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide.
- After the release is installed, open Reports (top menu; its left menu lists the A/R reports under ACCOUNTS RECEIVABLE and the A/P reports under ACCOUNTS PAYABLE).
STEPS: - Open the Sales By Customer link saved before the release.
- Choose "Download (PDF)" from the ⋯ menu.
- Open the PDF and compare it with the saved PDF.
- Choose "Download (CSV)" from the ⋯ menu.
- Open the CSV and compare it with the saved CSV.
EXP: Expected Result
Step 1:
- Sales By Customer opens with the same filter ticked as before.Step 2:
- The PDF downloads.Step 3:
- The same rows, amounts and layout as the saved PDF.Step 4:
- The CSV downloads.Step 5:
- The same rows, amounts and layout as the saved CSV.
Why: the six aging reports reuse the filter chips, totals row and export of Inventory Value, Sales By Customer and Work In Progress, so a change made for the aging reports could reach those three.
