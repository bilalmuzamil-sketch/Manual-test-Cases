C715556 Design: two customers ticked look as in the design picture
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role).
- Have a customer, customer A, with open invoices on today's date.
- Have a customer, customer B, with nothing open.
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide, in light theme at 100% zoom.
- Open A/R Summary (Reports, then "A/R Aging Summary" under ACCOUNTS RECEIVABLE in the left menu).
- Open the design picture "Two customers ticked" (ar-ap-p7-two-customers-ticked.png) (attached to this case) beside the browser at 100% zoom; compare the layout, labels and styles, not the names and amounts, which are only examples.
STEPS: - Click the Customer chip.
- Tick customer A and customer B.
- Close the menu.
EXP: Expected Result
Step 1:
- The menu opens.Step 2:
- Both are ticked.
- As in the picture: both names show as blue tags, each with an "×", inside the search box, and a circled "×" sits at the box's top right.
- "All customers" is unticked with "2 selected" at its right.
- The helper line is gone.
- The ticked customers keep their place in the A to Z list.
- The chip reads "Customer: 2 customers" in a blue pill.Step 3:
- The menu closes.
- As in the picture: one row, customer A (customer B has nothing open, so it is not listed).
- The Totals row equals customer A's row, an empty bucket reading $0.00 in the Totals row.
