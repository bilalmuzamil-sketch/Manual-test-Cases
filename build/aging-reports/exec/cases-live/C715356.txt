C715356 A/P Detail: the top-left chevron collapses and expands every group
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role).
- Make sure the report has open bills and vendor credits in at least three aging buckets on today's date.
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide.
- Open A/P Detail (Reports, then "A/P Aging Detail" under ACCOUNTS PAYABLE in the left menu).
- Leave the report with every group collapsed.Setup
- Make vendor A: Click Parts in the top bar, then Vendors in the left menu, then New Vendor. Type a new Name for vendor A, pick Credit Terms "Net 30" and Taxes "Exempt (0%)", then click Save & Close.
- Make bill 1: Click Parts in the top bar, then Purchase Orders in the left menu, then New PO. Pick vendor A in Vendor. In Part Number type a new part number and click "Add new special order part: …"; type a Description, 1 in Quantity and 100.00 in Cost; click Add, then Save & Close. In the Purchase Orders list click Receive on that order (Vendor column: vendor A); type a Vendor Invoice # (for example bill 1), type today's date in Invoice Date, type 0 in Tax, then click Receive Parts (1).
- Make bill 2: Same way as bill 1, with vendor A in Vendor: Cost 200.00; type the date 45 days before today in Invoice Date.
- Make bill 3: Same way as bill 1, with vendor A in Vendor: Cost 300.00; type the date 75 days before today in Invoice Date.
- Make vendor credit 1: Click Parts in the top bar, then Returns in the left menu, then Create Return. Pick vendor A in Vendor, type a Packaging Slip, pick the part received on bill 1 in Part Number, type 50.00 in Price Per Unit, then click Save Return. In the Returns list tick that return and click Receive Credit; type a Credit Memo Number, type the date 20 days before today in Credit Date, type 0 in Tax, then click Post Credit.
STEPS: - Click the chevron on one bucket row.
- Hover over the chevron button in the table's top-left header cell.
- Click that chevron button.
- Hover over the chevron button again.
- Click it.
EXP: Expected Result
Step 1:
- That bucket expands.
- Its own chevron turns from pointing right to pointing down.Step 2:
- The button's chevron points down (a group is expanded).
- A tooltip above the button reads "Collapse all".Step 3:
- Every group collapses.
- The column under the button was not sorted: the sort mark is still on the column the report opened on.Step 4:
- The chevron now points right.
- The tooltip reads "Expand all".Step 5:
- Every group expands.
- The column under the button is still not sorted.
- Every bucket row's own chevron points down (open).
This release does not change how the groups open, so they open as today; the starting screen asks for every group collapsed, collapsing any that are open.
Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
From: PRD "A/R and A/P Report Sorting, Export and Location Filter", Confluence 947650561, version 11 (5 Nov 2026, Chris Ward), read 10 Nov 2026 · Epic SV-11315, story SV-11534 (Story 4, Collapse all, Expand all and pinned group rows)
- S4-R1: “The table's top-left header cell holds one chevron button. While any group is expanded it points down, its tooltip (above the button) reads "Collapse all", and a click collapses every group. When every group is collapsed it points right, its tooltip reads "Expand all", and a click expands every group.”
- S4-R3: “Clicking the button never sorts the column under it.”
- S4-R5: “Each group's own expand toggle uses the same chevron: pointing down when open, right when closed. Behavior is unchanged.”
- User Feedback Summary: “Collapse all / Expand all button | "Collapse all" / "Expand all" | Tooltip above the button”Last checked against build v26.45.2-6a8b89c (QA site sv11315) on 11/10/2026.
