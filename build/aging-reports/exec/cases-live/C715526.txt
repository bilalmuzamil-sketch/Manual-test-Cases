C715526 Design: A/R Collection PDF prints the call list on each customer's line
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role).
- Make sure your role has the "View and Manage AP/AR Data" switch on, so the credit term shows.
- Have a customer, customer A, with an overdue invoice, a phone number and a credit term.
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide.
- Open A/R Collection (Reports, then "A/R Aging Collection" under ACCOUNTS RECEIVABLE in the left menu).
- Leave the report with only customer A ticked.
STEPS: - Choose "Download (PDF)" from the ⋯ menu.
- Open the PDF at 100% zoom.
EXP: Expected Result
Step 1:
- The PDF downloads.Step 2:
- The PDF opens.
- The lines under the title read "As of " and the date, then "Locations: All locations", then "Customer: " and the name.
- Customer A's line reads the name, "Phone: " and the number, "Credit term: " and the term, nothing overlaps.
