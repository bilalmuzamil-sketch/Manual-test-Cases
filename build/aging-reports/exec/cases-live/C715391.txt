C715391 A/P Unpaid Invoices: a vendor credit belongs to its return or vendor
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role).
- Have open documents in these reports at two locations, location A and location B, with location A's name first in A to Z order.
- Have a vendor, vendor A, with two unused vendor credits: vendor credit A, from a vendor return made at location B, and vendor credit B, entered directly (not from a vendor return) while signed in at location A.
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide.
- Open A/P Unpaid Invoices (Reports, then "A/P Unpaid Invoices" under ACCOUNTS PAYABLE in the left menu; the page title reads "A/P Unpaid Invoices Report").
- Leave the report with the Vendor filter showing vendor A ticked and its group expanded.Setup
- Locations to use: Use "Staging Heavy Duty - 9919" as location A (it comes first in A to Z order) and "Staging Lethbridge - 4310" as location B. To work at location B, click the location name and your initials at the top right of the top bar, then Change Location, then "Staging Lethbridge - 4310"; switch back to "Staging Heavy Duty - 9919" the same way when you are done.
- Make vendor A: Click Parts in the top bar, then Vendors in the left menu, then New Vendor. Type a new Name for vendor A, pick Credit Terms "Net 30" and Taxes "Exempt (0%)", then click Save & Close.
- Make bill 1: While switched to location B (see the Locations to use item): Click Parts in the top bar, then Purchase Orders in the left menu, then New PO. Pick vendor A in Vendor. In Part Number type a new part number and click "Add new special order part: …"; type a Description, 1 in Quantity and 300.00 in Cost; click Add, then Save & Close. In the Purchase Orders list click Receive on that order (Vendor column: vendor A); type a Vendor Invoice # (for example bill 1), type today's date in Invoice Date, type 0 in Tax, then click Receive Parts (1).
- Make vendor credit A: While switched to location B (see the Locations to use item): Click Parts in the top bar, then Returns in the left menu, then Create Return. Pick vendor A in Vendor, type a Packaging Slip, pick the part received on bill 1 in Part Number, type 40.00 in Price Per Unit, then click Save Return. In the Returns list tick that return and click Receive Credit; type a Credit Memo Number, type today's date in Credit Date, type 0 in Tax, then click Post Credit.
- Make vendor credit B: Make vendor credit B for vendor A without a vendor return.
STEPS: - Read vendor credit A's and vendor credit B's cells in the Location column.
- In the Location menu, untick location A.
- Expand vendor A's group.
EXP: Expected Result
Step 1:
- Vendor credit A shows location B (the return's location).
- Vendor credit B shows location A (the vendor's location for that credit, where it was entered, as today).Step 2:
- The report narrows to location B.Step 3:
- Only vendor credit A is listed.
A vendor has no single location of its own: each bill or credit is written to the vendor's account at one location, and that is the location it shows (tech plan, 21 Oct 2026).
Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
From: PRD "A/R and A/P Report Sorting, Export and Location Filter", Confluence 947650561, version 11 (5 Nov 2026, Chris Ward), read 10 Nov 2026 · Epic SV-11315, story SV-11535 (Story 5, Filters: customer or vendor, past due and location)
- S5-R7: “Choosing locations narrows every row, group, subtotal, total, page count, sort and export to the open documents at those locations. A row belongs to the location of its own document. On A/R: the invoice, credit memo or deposit. On A/P: a bill belongs to the location of the delivery it was received on; a vendor credit from a vendor return belongs to that return's location; any other vendor credit keeps its vendor's location, as today. That is the location the Location column shows.”From: Tech plan "A/R and A/P Report Sorting, Export and Location Filter — Technical Implementation Plan" (2026-10-21, uploaded 22 Oct 2026), read 22 Oct 2026
- Tech plan §1 Clarifications, Q2 A/P row location: “A vendor account is per (vendor, location) on every write path (delivery, manual credit, return credit), so vendor_account.workplace_id already is the document's location.”
- Tech plan §3 D13: “Equal to S5-R7's per-document rule by construction (every write path finds or creates the (vendor, location) account); prod 0 mismatches.”Partly checked against build v26.45.2-6a8b89c (QA site sv11315) on 11/10/2026; not yet seen on that build: a vendor credit entered directly, not from a vendor return.
