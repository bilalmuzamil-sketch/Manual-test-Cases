C715454 A/R Detail: a long bucket loads in 100s with no line repeated or skipped
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role).
- Make sure one aging bucket, bucket A, has between 101 and 400 open lines on today's date.
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide.
- Open A/R Detail (Reports, then "A/R Aging Detail" under ACCOUNTS RECEIVABLE in the left menu).
- Leave the report with every bucket collapsed.
STEPS: - Choose "Download (CSV)" from the ⋯ menu.
- Open the CSV and count the rows whose bucket is bucket A.
- Expand bucket A.
- Scroll to the end of bucket A, letting every batch load, writing down each document number.
- Click the "Amount" header.
- Look at bucket A and count its lines before any more load.
- Scroll to the end of bucket A again, letting every batch load, writing down each number.
EXP: Expected Result
Step 1:
- The CSV downloads.Step 2:
- Note the count (for example 250).Step 3:
- Bucket A opens and its first 100 lines show.Step 4:
- More lines load as you scroll.
- Every document appears exactly once.Step 5:
- The rows sort by Amount, largest first, inside each bucket.Step 6:
- Bucket A is still expanded and starts again from its first 100 lines, now in Amount order (largest first).Step 7:
- More lines load as you scroll.
- In this sort too, every document appears exactly once.
- Both lists (this one and the one from step 4) hold exactly the CSV's documents for bucket A: none repeated, none missing.
