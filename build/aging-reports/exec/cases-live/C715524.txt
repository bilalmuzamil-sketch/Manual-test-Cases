C715524 Design: A/R Summary PDF heading 91 And Over stays on one line
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role).
- Have a customer with open invoices on today's date in every bucket: not yet due, 1-30, 31-60, 61-90 and more than 90 days past due.
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide.
- Open A/R Summary (Reports, then "A/R Aging Summary" under ACCOUNTS RECEIVABLE in the left menu).
STEPS: - Choose "Download (PDF)" from the ⋯ menu.
- Open the PDF at 100% zoom and read the bucket headings, letter for letter.
EXP: Expected Result
Step 1:
- The PDF downloads.Step 2:
- The PDF opens.
- The bucket headings read "Current", "1-30 Days", "31-60 Days", "61-90 Days", "91 And Over", as on screen.
- The last one, "91 And Over", is on one line, not wrapped.
