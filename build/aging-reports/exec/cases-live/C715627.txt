C715627 Design: A/P Summary Total and Totals row match Sales By Customer
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role).
- Have a vendor with an open bill on today's date, so the report has rows.
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide, in light theme at 100% zoom.
- Open A/P Summary (Reports, then "A/P Aging Summary" under ACCOUNTS PAYABLE in the left menu).
- Open Sales By Customer (Reports, then "Sales By Customer" under PERFORMANCE in the left menu) in a second tab of the same window, for comparison.
STEPS: - Look at the "Total" column on the header, the rows and the Totals row, and compare with Sales By Customer.
- Compare the Totals row with Sales By Customer's Totals row.
EXP: Expected Result
Step 1:
- "Total" is pinned to the right edge and bold on the header, every row and the Totals row, as on Sales By Customer.Step 2:
- Side by side, the Totals row looks the same as Sales By Customer's Totals row: the same background colour, text size and weight.
