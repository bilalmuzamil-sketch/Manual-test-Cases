C715477 A work order link from another location still asks to switch
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role).
- Have open documents in these reports at two locations, your current location and location B.
- Make sure you are enrolled at both locations.
- Have a work order, work order 1, made at location B, and a link to it copied from the address bar.
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide.
- After the release is installed, click Work Orders in the top menu at your current location.
STEPS: - Paste the link to work order 1 into the address bar.
- Press Enter.
- Click "Cancel".
EXP: Expected Result
Step 1:
- The link shows in the address bar.Step 2:
- The existing switch-location prompt asks to switch to location B, as before the release.Step 3:
- The prompt closes and you are on Work Orders, with its message, as before the release.
