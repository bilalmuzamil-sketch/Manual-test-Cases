C715553 Design: the Past due menu matches the design picture
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role).
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide, in light theme at 100% zoom.
- Open A/R Summary (Reports, then "A/R Aging Summary" under ACCOUNTS RECEIVABLE in the left menu).
- Open the design picture "Past due menu" (ar-ap-p7-past-due-menu.png) (attached to this case) beside the browser at 100% zoom; compare the layout, labels and styles, not the names and amounts, which are only examples.
STEPS: - Click the Past due chip.
- Widen the browser window to 1536 px and look across the page.
EXP: Expected Result
Step 1:
- The menu opens, as in the picture: while the menu is open the chip has a light-grey background.
- The white menu opens under it.
- Its rows read, top to bottom, "Any" (ticked), a dividing line, the grey line "All ranges are included. Pick one or more to narrow.", then "1-30 days", "31-60 days", "61-90 days", "91+ days".
- Each range box shows a grey dash (neither empty nor ticked) while "Any" is ticked.Step 2:
- Nothing overlaps, is cut off or clipped.
- The layout is the same as at 1440 px.
