C715549 Design: one location ticked hides the Location column as pictured
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role).
- Have open documents in these reports at two locations, location A and location B, with location A's name first in A to Z order.
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide, in light theme at 100% zoom.
- Open A/R Detail (Reports, then "A/R Aging Detail" under ACCOUNTS RECEIVABLE in the left menu).
- Leave the report with every bucket collapsed.
- Open the design picture "One location ticked" (ar-ap-p4-one-location-ticked.png) (attached to this case) beside the browser at 100% zoom; compare the layout, labels and styles, not the names and amounts, which are only examples.
STEPS: - Click the Location chip.
- Untick location A.
- Close the menu.
EXP: Expected Result
Step 1:
- The menu opens.Step 2:
- The count reads "1 selected" and "Select all" is no longer ticked.
- As in the picture, while the menu stays open the rows do not move: location A, now unticked, stays where it was.
- The chip reads "Location: " and location B's name, shortened with "..." when long ("Location: Staging Lethbri..." in the picture), still in a blue pill.Step 3:
- The menu closes, and as in the picture the Location column is gone: the header row reads the chevron, then "Date", "Transaction Type", "ID", "Customer Full Name", "Due Date", "Amount", "Open Balance".
