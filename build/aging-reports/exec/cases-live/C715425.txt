C715425 With Invoicing and Work Orders View, a work order invoice is a link
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as a user whose custom role has "Reports" in the Page Access card, "Invoicing & payments → View", "Work orders → View" but not "Part sales → View" (under the Parts Department switch).
- Have a customer with an open work order invoice A and an open part sale invoice B, both made at your current location.
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide.
- Open A/R Detail (Reports, then "A/R Aging Detail" under ACCOUNTS RECEIVABLE in the left menu).
- Leave the report with that customer ticked and every bucket expanded.Setup
- Make customer A: Click Customers in the top bar, then New Customer. Type a new Name for customer A, type a Phone, then click Save. On the customer's page click the edit icon beside the name; in Edit Customer pick Credit Terms "Net 30", pick Taxes "Exempt (0%)" and type 0 in Default Shop Supplies (so totals stay exact), then click Save. Click Add Contact, type a First name and Last name, then click Save. On the Assets tab click New Asset, pick the Contact, fill Make and the other boxes the window marks as required, then click Save.
- Make work order invoice A: On customer A's page click New Work Order, pick the Asset, then click Save (click Ok if a Customer Notes window opens). In the New Line window type a Title, pick Labor Rate "Fixed Line Total" (type "Fixed" in the box to find it), type 300.00 in Fixed Line Total, tick Line Approved, then click Save & Close. On the line click Complete; in Tech stories type a story and click Complete All Lines (if Missing Details asks for Mileage, type it and click Complete All Lines again). Open the Finance tab and click Mark Reviewed (if a window asks for the VIN, type one and click Confirm Review). Type today's date in Invoice Date, then click Create Invoice. When the New Customer Payment window opens, reload the page (F5) so the invoice stays unpaid. Do not close that window with its ×: that takes the new invoice away again.
- Make part sale invoice B: Click Parts in the top bar, then Part Sales in the left menu, then New Part Sale; pick customer A in Customer and click Save. Click Add Part: in Part Number type a new part number and click "Add new special order part: …", type a Description, 1 in Quantity and a Cost, then click Save & Close; on the part's row type 300.00 in the Sell Price box. Click Authorize. On the part's row pick any Vendor and click Order (the status reads Awaiting), then click Receive on the row; tick the part, type a Vendor Invoice Number and click Receive Parts (1) (the status reads Received). Open the Finance tab, type today's date in Invoice Date, then click Create Invoice; reload the page when the New Customer Payment window opens.
- Make the role for this test: Click the location name and your initials at the top right of the top bar, then Settings, then Roles & Permissions in the left menu, then Create Custom Role. In "Choose a template" click Skip (every switch then starts off). Type a Role Name, then switch on "Reports" in the Page Access card, tick View in the "Invoicing & payments" card and View in the "Work orders" card, and leave the Parts Department switch off, then click Create.
- Sign in with that role: Click the location name and your initials at the top right of the top bar, then Settings, then Staff in the left menu. On the row "Tech ShopView" click the edit icon; in Edit Staff Member pick the new role in Role and click Save & Close. Then click the location name and your initials at the top right of the top bar, then Logout, and on the QA site's sign-in page click Tech in the quick login panel. After the test, sign in as Admin again and set Tech ShopView's Role back to Technician.
STEPS: - Look at invoice B's number in the ID column.
- Click invoice A's number.
EXP: Expected Result
Step 1:
- Invoice B's number is plain text: the role lacks "Part Sales → View".Step 2:
- The work order's finance page opens.
