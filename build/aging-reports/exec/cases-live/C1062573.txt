C1062573 A/P Detail: Date and Due Date read as dates, never Today or Yesterday
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role).
- Have a vendor with three open bills, each due 30 days after its own date: bill A dated today, bill B dated yesterday and bill C dated 2 days ago.
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide.
- Open A/P Detail (Reports, then "A/P Aging Detail" under ACCOUNTS PAYABLE in the left menu).
- Leave the report with only that vendor ticked in the Vendor filter.Setup
- Make vendor A: Click Parts in the top bar, then Vendors in the left menu, then New Vendor. Type a new Name for vendor A, pick Credit Terms "Net 30" and Taxes "Exempt (0%)", then click Save & Close.
- Make bill A: Click Parts in the top bar, then Purchase Orders in the left menu, then New PO. Pick vendor A in Vendor. In Part Number type a new part number and click "Add new special order part: …"; type a Description, 1 in Quantity and 100.00 in Cost; click Add, then Save & Close. In the Purchase Orders list click Receive on that order (Vendor column: vendor A); type a Vendor Invoice # (for example bill A), type today's date in Invoice Date, type 0 in Tax, then click Receive Parts (1).
- Make bill B: Same way as bill A, with vendor A in Vendor: Cost 200.00; type the date 1 days before today in Invoice Date.
- Make bill C: Same way as bill A, with vendor A in Vendor: Cost 300.00; type the date 2 days before today in Invoice Date.
STEPS: - Expand the "Current" bucket.
EXP: Expected Result
Step 1:
- Bill A, bill B and bill C are listed.
- Their Date cells read today's date, yesterday's date and the date of 2 days ago as month, day and year (for example "Nov 10, 2026").
- Their Due Date cells read dates in the same form.
- No Date or Due Date cell reads "Today", "Yesterday" or "2 days ago".
