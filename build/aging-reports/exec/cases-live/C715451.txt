C715451 A/P Summary: 30 vendors at a time, none repeated or skipped
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role).
- Make sure that between 31 and 90 vendors have open documents on today's date.
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide.
- Open A/P Summary (Reports, then "A/P Aging Summary" under ACCOUNTS PAYABLE in the left menu).
STEPS: - Choose "Download (CSV)" from the ⋯ menu.
- Open the CSV and count the vendors in it (each name once).
- Count the vendors shown before any more load (or compare them with the first 30 names in the CSV) and note the last one.
- Scroll down until the next vendors load.
- Keep scrolling to the end of the list, writing down each vendor's name.
- Click the "Total" header.
- Scroll from the top to the end of the list again, writing down each name.
- In the Past due filter, tick "1-30 days".
- Tick "91+ days".
- Scroll from the top to the end of the list again, writing down each name.
EXP: Expected Result
Step 1:
- The CSV downloads.Step 2:
- Note the number of vendors (for example 46).Step 3:
- Exactly 30 vendors.Step 4:
- The next vendors load below as you scroll (there are no page buttons), starting with the vendor that comes right after the last one noted in step 3, not a repeat.Step 5:
- Every vendor appears exactly once and the count equals the CSV's.Step 6:
- The rows sort by Total, largest first.Step 7:
- In this sort too, every vendor appears exactly once and none is missing.Step 8:
- The chip reads "Past due: 1-30 days" and the report reloads with that range only.Step 9:
- The chip reads "Past due: 2 ranges" and the report reloads with both ranges.Step 10:
- Every vendor left by the filter appears exactly once.
- None repeats as more load.
