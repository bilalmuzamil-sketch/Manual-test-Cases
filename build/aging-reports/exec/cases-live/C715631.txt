C715631 Design: A/P Unpaid Invoices Open Balance and Totals row match Sales By Customer
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role).
- Have a vendor with an unpaid bill on today's date, so the report has rows.
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide, in light theme at 100% zoom.
- Open A/P Unpaid Invoices (Reports, then "A/P Unpaid Invoices" under ACCOUNTS PAYABLE in the left menu; the page title reads "A/P Unpaid Invoices Report").
- Leave the report with every group expanded (Expand all).
- Open Sales By Customer (Reports, then "Sales By Customer" under PERFORMANCE in the left menu) in a second tab of the same window, for comparison.
STEPS: - Look at the "Open Balance" column on the header, the rows and the Totals row, and compare with Sales By Customer.
- Compare the Totals row with Sales By Customer's Totals row.
EXP: Expected Result
Step 1:
- "Open Balance" is pinned to the right edge and bold on the header, every row, every expanded row and the Totals row, as on Sales By Customer.Step 2:
- Side by side, the Totals row looks the same as Sales By Customer's Totals row: the same background colour, text size and weight.
