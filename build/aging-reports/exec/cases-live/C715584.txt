C715584 Sales By Customer's filter menus behave as before the release
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role).
- Have open documents in these reports at two locations, so the Location filter shows and can be cleared.
- Make sure that before the release was installed, you did the steps below on Sales By Customer and noted the Location chip and the page address after "Select all", whether the cursor stayed in the Customer menu's search box while you typed, and how the filter row looked at 900 px.
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide.
- After the release is installed, open Sales By Customer (Reports, then "Sales By Customer" under PERFORMANCE in the left menu).
STEPS: - In the Location menu, click "Clear selection".
- Click "Select all".
- Open the Customer menu.
- Type a few letters of a customer's name into its search box.
- Keep typing without clicking the box again.
- Make the browser window 900 px wide and look at the filter row.
EXP: Expected Result
Step 1:
- No location is ticked.Step 2:
- Every location is ticked.
- The Location chip and the page address read exactly as you noted before the release.Step 3:
- The Customer menu opens with its search box.Step 4:
- The list narrows as you type.Step 5:
- The cursor stays in the search box and every letter appears, as before the release.Step 6:
- The mobile filter row shows, as before the release.
Why: the release changes the shared filter parts (an opt-in setting for the aging reports' Past due menu); Sales By Customer, which the aging reports copy, must not change.
