C715415 A/R Collection: a part sale invoice at your location opens its finance page
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role).
- Have a customer with an overdue part sale invoice A made at your current location.
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide.
- Open A/R Collection (Reports, then "A/R Aging Collection" under ACCOUNTS RECEIVABLE in the left menu).
- Leave the report with that customer's group expanded.Setup
- Make customer A: Click Customers in the top bar, then New Customer. Type a new Name for customer A, type a Phone, then click Save. On the customer's page click the edit icon beside the name; in Edit Customer pick Credit Terms "Net 30", pick Taxes "Exempt (0%)" and type 0 in Default Shop Supplies (so totals stay exact), then click Save. Click Add Contact, type a First name and Last name, then click Save. On the Assets tab click New Asset, pick the Contact, fill Make and the other boxes the window marks as required, then click Save.
- Make part sale invoice A: Click Parts in the top bar, then Part Sales in the left menu, then New Part Sale; pick customer A in Customer and click Save. Click Add Part: in Part Number type a new part number and click "Add new special order part: …", type a Description, 1 in Quantity and a Cost, then click Save & Close; on the part's row type 300.00 in the Sell Price box. Click Authorize. On the part's row pick any Vendor and click Order (the status reads Awaiting), then click Receive on the row; tick the part, type a Vendor Invoice Number and click Receive Parts (1) (the status reads Received). Open the Finance tab, type the date 45 days before today in Invoice Date, then click Create Invoice; reload the page when the New Customer Payment window opens.
STEPS: - Hover over invoice A's Open Balance cell.
- Click invoice A's Open Balance cell.
- Click invoice A's number.
EXP: Expected Result
Step 1:
- The pointer does not turn into a hand.
- The row's text is not blue: only the number is a link.Step 2:
- Nothing opens.Step 3:
- The part sale's finance page opens, as today.
