C715552 Design: clicking a customer on A/R Summary matches the drill-down picture
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role).
- Make sure your role has the "Customers → View" permission and the "View and Manage AP/AR Data" switch on, so customer A's name is a link and its credit term shows.
- Have a customer, customer A, with open invoices on an earlier date, date A.
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide, in light theme at 100% zoom.
- Open A/R Summary (Reports, then "A/R Aging Summary" under ACCOUNTS RECEIVABLE in the left menu).
- Leave the report with the as-of date set to date A.
- Open the design picture "Drill-down keeps the as-of date" (ar-ap-p4-drill-down-keeps-the-as-of-date.png) (attached to this case) beside the browser at 100% zoom; compare the layout, labels and styles, not the names and amounts, which are only examples.Setup
- Make customer A: Click Customers in the top bar, then New Customer. Type a new Name for customer A, type a Phone, then click Save. On the customer's page click the edit icon beside the name; in Edit Customer pick Credit Terms "Net 30", pick Taxes "Exempt (0%)" and type 0 in Default Shop Supplies (so totals stay exact), then click Save. Click Add Contact, type a First name and Last name, then click Save. On the Assets tab click New Asset, pick the Contact, fill Make and the other boxes the window marks as required, then click Save.
- Make invoice 1: On customer A's page click New Work Order, pick the Asset, then click Save (click Ok if a Customer Notes window opens). In the New Line window type a Title, pick Labor Rate "Fixed Line Total" (type "Fixed" in the box to find it), type 100.00 in Fixed Line Total, tick Line Approved, then click Save & Close. On the line click Complete; in Tech stories type a story and click Complete All Lines (if Missing Details asks for Mileage, type it and click Complete All Lines again). Open the Finance tab and click Mark Reviewed (if a window asks for the VIN, type one and click Confirm Review). Type the date 20 days before today in Invoice Date, then click Create Invoice. When the New Customer Payment window opens, reload the page (F5) so the invoice stays unpaid. Do not close that window with its ×: that takes the new invoice away again.
- Date A: Use the date 10 days before today as date A (invoice 1 was already open then).
STEPS: - Click customer A's name.
EXP: Expected Result
Step 1:
- A/R Collection opens (title "A/R Aging Collection").
- As in the picture: the filter row reads "As of: " and date A in a blue pill.
- "Customer: " and customer A's name in a blue pill (one customer ticked).
- "Past due: Any" resting.
- "Location: All locations".
- One closed group row for customer A with its count, Phone and Credit term, "Total" and the sums.
- The Totals row equals that group's sums.
