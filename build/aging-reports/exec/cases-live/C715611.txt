C715611 A/P Unpaid Invoices: the description is not in the PDF or CSV
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role).
- Have a vendor, vendor A, with an unpaid bill on today's date.
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide.
- Open A/P Unpaid Invoices (Reports, then "A/P Unpaid Invoices" under ACCOUNTS PAYABLE in the left menu; the page title reads "A/P Unpaid Invoices Report").
STEPS: - Choose "Download (PDF)" from the ⋯ menu.
- Choose "Download (CSV)" from the ⋯ menu.
- Search both files for vendor A's name.
- Search both files for the words "grouped by vendor".
EXP: Expected Result
Step 1:
- The PDF downloads.Step 2:
- The CSV downloads.Step 3:
- Vendor A's name is found in both files: they hold the report's rows.Step 4:
- The words are not found: the description is not in the PDF or CSV.
