C715523 Design: A/R Collection customer row shows Phone and Credit term
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role).
- Have a customer, customer A, with an overdue invoice, a phone number and a credit term.
- Have a customer, customer B, with an overdue invoice and no phone.
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide, in light theme at 100% zoom.
- Open A/R Collection (Reports, then "A/R Aging Collection" under ACCOUNTS RECEIVABLE in the left menu).Setup
- Make customer A: Click Customers in the top bar, then New Customer. Type a new Name for customer A, type a Phone, then click Save. On the customer's page click the edit icon beside the name; in Edit Customer pick Credit Terms "Net 30", pick Taxes "Exempt (0%)" and type 0 in Default Shop Supplies (so totals stay exact), then click Save. Click Add Contact, type a First name and Last name, then click Save. On the Assets tab click New Asset, pick the Contact, fill Make and the other boxes the window marks as required, then click Save.
- Make invoice 1: On customer A's page click New Work Order, pick the Asset, then click Save (click Ok if a Customer Notes window opens). In the New Line window type a Title, pick Labor Rate "Fixed Line Total" (type "Fixed" in the box to find it), type 100.00 in Fixed Line Total, tick Line Approved, then click Save & Close. On the line click Complete; in Tech stories type a story and click Complete All Lines (if Missing Details asks for Mileage, type it and click Complete All Lines again). Open the Finance tab and click Mark Reviewed (if a window asks for the VIN, type one and click Confirm Review). Type the date 45 days before today in Invoice Date, then click Create Invoice. When the New Customer Payment window opens, reload the page (F5) so the invoice stays unpaid. Do not close that window with its ×: that takes the new invoice away again.
- Make customer B: Click Customers in the top bar, then New Customer. Type a new Name for customer B (leave Phone empty), then click Save. On the customer's page click the edit icon beside the name; in Edit Customer pick Credit Terms "Net 30", pick Taxes "Exempt (0%)" and type 0 in Default Shop Supplies (so totals stay exact), then click Save. Click Add Contact, type a First name and Last name, then click Save. On the Assets tab click New Asset, pick the Contact, fill Make and the other boxes the window marks as required, then click Save.
- Make invoice for customer B: Same way as invoice 1, on customer B's page: Fixed Line Total 200.00; type the date 45 days before today in Invoice Date; then in the New Customer Payment window reload the page (F5) so the invoice stays unpaid. Do not close that window with its ×: that takes the new invoice away again.
STEPS: - Read customer A's group row, letter for letter.
- Read customer B's group row, and look for overlap or clipping on both rows.
- Switch ShopView to dark theme.
EXP: Expected Result
Step 1:
- The name, then "Phone: " and the number, then "Credit term: " and the term, on the group row after the name.Step 2:
- The name and "Credit term: COD" (if none set).
- No "Phone:" label.
- On both rows nothing overlaps or is clipped.Step 3:
- The page switches to dark theme.
- On both rows nothing overlaps or is clipped.
