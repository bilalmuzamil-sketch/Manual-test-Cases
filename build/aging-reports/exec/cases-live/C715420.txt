C715420 A/P Detail: a vendor credit links to the return it came from
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role).
- Have open documents in these reports at two locations: your current location and your other location.
- Have a vendor with three unused vendor credits: vendor credit A from a vendor return made at your current location, vendor credit B from a vendor return made at your other location, and vendor credit C not from a vendor return.
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide.
- Open A/P Detail (Reports, then "A/P Aging Detail" under ACCOUNTS PAYABLE in the left menu).
- Leave the report with that vendor ticked and its rows showing.Setup
- Locations to use: Use "Staging Heavy Duty - 9919" as location A (it comes first in A to Z order) and "Staging Lethbridge - 4310" as location B. To work at location B, click the location name and your initials at the top right of the top bar, then Change Location, then "Staging Lethbridge - 4310"; switch back to "Staging Heavy Duty - 9919" the same way when you are done.
- Make vendor A: Click Parts in the top bar, then Vendors in the left menu, then New Vendor. Type a new Name for vendor A, pick Credit Terms "Net 30" and Taxes "Exempt (0%)", then click Save & Close.
- Make bill 1: Click Parts in the top bar, then Purchase Orders in the left menu, then New PO. Pick vendor A in Vendor. In Part Number type a new part number and click "Add new special order part: …"; type a Description, 1 in Quantity and 300.00 in Cost; click Add, then Save & Close. In the Purchase Orders list click Receive on that order (Vendor column: vendor A); type a Vendor Invoice # (for example bill 1), type today's date in Invoice Date, type 0 in Tax, then click Receive Parts (1).
- Make vendor credit A: Click Parts in the top bar, then Returns in the left menu, then Create Return. Pick vendor A in Vendor, type a Packaging Slip, pick the part received on bill 1 in Part Number, type 40.00 in Price Per Unit, then click Save Return. In the Returns list tick that return and click Receive Credit; type a Credit Memo Number, type today's date in Credit Date, type 0 in Tax, then click Post Credit.
- Make bill 2: While switched to location B (see the Locations to use item): Same way as bill 1, with vendor A in Vendor: Cost 300.00; type today's date in Invoice Date.
- Make vendor credit B: While switched to location B (see the Locations to use item): Click Parts in the top bar, then Returns in the left menu, then Create Return. Pick vendor A in Vendor, type a Packaging Slip, pick the part received on bill 2 in Part Number, type 50.00 in Price Per Unit, then click Save Return. In the Returns list tick that return and click Receive Credit; type a Credit Memo Number, type today's date in Credit Date, type 0 in Tax, then click Post Credit.
- Make vendor credit C: Make vendor credit C for vendor A without a vendor return.
STEPS: - Look at the three vendor credits' numbers in the No. column.
- Click vendor credit A's number.
EXP: Expected Result
Step 1:
- Vendor credit A's number is a link.
- Vendor credit B's (another location) and vendor credit C's (no vendor return) are plain text.Step 2:
- The vendor return vendor credit A came from opens.
