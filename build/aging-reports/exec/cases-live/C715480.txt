C715480 The location switcher in the top right is unchanged
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role).
- Have open documents in these reports at two locations, your current location and location B.
- Make sure you are enrolled at both locations, so you can switch to location B in the location switcher.
- Make sure that before the release was installed, you took a screenshot of the open location switcher.
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide.
- Open A/R Summary (Reports, then "A/R Aging Summary" under ACCOUNTS RECEIVABLE in the left menu).
STEPS: - Open the location switcher in the top right of ShopView.
- Switch to location B.
EXP: Expected Result
Step 1:
- It opens.
- It looks and lists the locations exactly as in the screenshot taken before the release.Step 2:
- It switches as it always has.
- A/R Summary reloads with the same filters and rows, in the opening order, at the top.
