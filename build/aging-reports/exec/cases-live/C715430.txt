C715430 Without Vendor Order Management, bills and vendor credits are plain text
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as a user whose custom role has "Reports" in the Page Access card but not "Vendor and order management → View" (under the Parts Department switch) or "Vendor and order management → Create & Edit" (under the Parts Department switch).
- Have a vendor with an unpaid bill A received at your current location and an unused vendor credit A from a vendor return made at your current location.
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide.
- Open A/P Detail (Reports, then "A/P Aging Detail" under ACCOUNTS PAYABLE in the left menu).
- Leave the report with that vendor ticked and every bucket expanded.Setup
- Make vendor A: Click Parts in the top bar, then Vendors in the left menu, then New Vendor. Type a new Name for vendor A, pick Credit Terms "Net 30" and Taxes "Exempt (0%)", then click Save & Close.
- Make bill A: Click Parts in the top bar, then Purchase Orders in the left menu, then New PO. Pick vendor A in Vendor. In Part Number type a new part number and click "Add new special order part: …"; type a Description, 1 in Quantity and 300.00 in Cost; click Add, then Save & Close. In the Purchase Orders list click Receive on that order (Vendor column: vendor A); type a Vendor Invoice # (for example bill A), type today's date in Invoice Date, type 0 in Tax, then click Receive Parts (1).
- Make vendor credit A: Click Parts in the top bar, then Returns in the left menu, then Create Return. Pick vendor A in Vendor, type a Packaging Slip, pick the part received on bill A in Part Number, type 50.00 in Price Per Unit, then click Save Return. In the Returns list tick that return and click Receive Credit; type a Credit Memo Number, type today's date in Credit Date, type 0 in Tax, then click Post Credit.
- Make the role for this test: Click the location name and your initials at the top right of the top bar, then Settings, then Roles & Permissions in the left menu, then Create Custom Role. In "Choose a template" click Skip (every switch then starts off). Type a Role Name, then switch on "Reports" in the Page Access card (leave everything else off), then click Create.
- Sign in with that role: Click the location name and your initials at the top right of the top bar, then Settings, then Staff in the left menu. On the row "Tech ShopView" click the edit icon; in Edit Staff Member pick the new role in Role and click Save & Close. Then click the location name and your initials at the top right of the top bar, then Logout, and on the QA site's sign-in page click Tech in the quick login panel. After the test, sign in as Admin again and set Tech ShopView's Role back to Technician.
STEPS: - Look at bill A's and vendor credit A's numbers in the No. column.
- Click bill A's number.
EXP: Expected Result
Step 1:
- Both are plain text.Step 2:
- Nothing opens.
