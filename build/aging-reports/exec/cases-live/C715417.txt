C715417 A/R Collection: a part sale invoice at another location asks to switch
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role).
- Have open documents in these reports at two locations, your current location and location B.
- Make sure you are enrolled at both locations.
- Have a customer with an open part sale invoice A made at location B.
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide.
- Open A/R Collection (Reports, then "A/R Aging Collection" under ACCOUNTS RECEIVABLE in the left menu).
- Leave the report with that customer ticked and its rows showing.Setup
- Locations to use: Use "Staging Heavy Duty - 9919" as location A (it comes first in A to Z order) and "Staging Lethbridge - 4310" as location B. To work at location B, click the location name and your initials at the top right of the top bar, then Change Location, then "Staging Lethbridge - 4310"; switch back to "Staging Heavy Duty - 9919" the same way when you are done.
- Make customer A: Click Customers in the top bar, then New Customer. Type a new Name for customer A, type a Phone, then click Save. On the customer's page click the edit icon beside the name; in Edit Customer pick Credit Terms "Net 30", pick Taxes "Exempt (0%)" and type 0 in Default Shop Supplies (so totals stay exact), then click Save. Click Add Contact, type a First name and Last name, then click Save. On the Assets tab click New Asset, pick the Contact, fill Make and the other boxes the window marks as required, then click Save.
- Make part sale invoice A: While switched to location B (see the Locations to use item): Click Parts in the top bar, then Part Sales in the left menu, then New Part Sale; pick customer A in Customer and click Save. Click Add Part: in Part Number type a new part number and click "Add new special order part: …", type a Description, 1 in Quantity and a Cost, then click Save & Close; on the part's row type 300.00 in the Sell Price box. Click Authorize. On the part's row pick any Vendor and click Order (the status reads Awaiting), then click Receive on the row; tick the part, type a Vendor Invoice Number and click Receive Parts (1) (the status reads Received). Open the Finance tab, type today's date in Invoice Date, then click Create Invoice; reload the page when the New Customer Payment window opens.
STEPS: - Click invoice A's number.
- Click "Switch location".
EXP: Expected Result
Step 1:
- A dialog opens on the report, before leaving it.
- Its title reads "Switch location?".
- Its message reads "This part sale is in " followed by location B's name and "Switch your active location to view it?".
- The button "Cancel" is on the left and the button "Switch location" (small l) is on the right.
- A close "×" is at the top right.Step 2:
- The part sale's finance page opens.
- The location switcher in the top right shows that your active location is now location B.
What you should see today: in step 1, the right-hand button of the "Switch location?" dialog reads "Switch Location" with a capital L instead of "Switch location". This is a known problem and it is already reported — see https://shopview.atlassian.net/browse/SV-12139 (filed by Chris Ward on 3 Nov 2026).
· If you see exactly that, mark this test FAILED and do not raise anything new.
· If it fails in a DIFFERENT way from what is described above, that is a NEW problem — please report it.
· If it PASSES, the fix has shipped: report it so the ticket can be closed and this note removed.
