C715424 A/R Detail: a document at a location you are not enrolled in is plain text
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as an "Admin" user enrolled at one location only.
- Have a customer with an open invoice A made at another location, one this user is not enrolled in.
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide.
- Open A/R Detail (Reports, then "A/R Aging Detail" under ACCOUNTS RECEIVABLE in the left menu).
- Leave the report with that customer ticked and every bucket expanded.Setup
- Locations to use: Use "Staging Heavy Duty - 9919" as location A (it comes first in A to Z order) and "Staging Lethbridge - 4310" as location B. To work at location B, click the location name and your initials at the top right of the top bar, then Change Location, then "Staging Lethbridge - 4310"; switch back to "Staging Heavy Duty - 9919" the same way when you are done.
- Make customer A: Click Customers in the top bar, then New Customer. Type a new Name for customer A, type a Phone, then click Save. On the customer's page click the edit icon beside the name; in Edit Customer pick Credit Terms "Net 30", pick Taxes "Exempt (0%)" and type 0 in Default Shop Supplies (so totals stay exact), then click Save. Click Add Contact, type a First name and Last name, then click Save. On the Assets tab click New Asset, pick the Contact, fill Make and the other boxes the window marks as required, then click Save.
- Make invoice A: While switched to location B (see the Locations to use item): On customer A's page click New Work Order, pick the Asset, then click Save (click Ok if a Customer Notes window opens). In the New Line window type a Title, pick Labor Rate "Fixed Line Total" (type "Fixed" in the box to find it), type 100.00 in Fixed Line Total, tick Line Approved, then click Save & Close. On the line click Complete; in Tech stories type a story and click Complete All Lines (if Missing Details asks for Mileage, type it and click Complete All Lines again). Open the Finance tab and click Mark Reviewed (if a window asks for the VIN, type one and click Confirm Review). Type today's date in Invoice Date, then click Create Invoice. When the New Customer Payment window opens, reload the page (F5) so the invoice stays unpaid. Do not close that window with its ×: that takes the new invoice away again.
- Sign in as an Admin at one location: Click the location name and your initials at the top right of the top bar, then Settings, then Staff in the left menu. On the row "Tech ShopView" (it is enrolled at "Staging Heavy Duty - 9919" only) click the edit icon; in Edit Staff Member pick Admin in Role and click Save & Close. Then Logout, and on the QA site's sign-in page click Tech in the quick login panel. After the test, sign in as Admin again and set Tech ShopView's Role back to Technician.
STEPS: - Look at invoice A's row.
- Click invoice A's number.
EXP: Expected Result
Step 1:
- The row is listed (the report stays organization-wide).
- Its number is plain text.Step 2:
- Nothing opens and no dialog shows.
