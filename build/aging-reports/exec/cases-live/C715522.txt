C715522 Design: A/P info icon and tooltip match Work In Progress
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role).
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide, in light theme at 100% zoom.
- Open A/P Summary (Reports, then "A/P Aging Summary" under ACCOUNTS PAYABLE in the left menu).
- Open Work In Progress (Reports, then "Work In Progress" under PERFORMANCE in the left menu) in a second tab of the same window, for comparison.
STEPS: - Look at the info icon and compare with Work In Progress's.
- Hover over it and look at the tooltip's position and style.
- Compare with Work In Progress's tooltip.
- Switch ShopView to dark theme.
- Hover over the info icon again.
EXP: Expected Result
Step 1:
- The same info icon as Work In Progress's, directly right of the title.Step 2:
- The description shows in a tooltip.Step 3:
- The tooltip looks the same as Work In Progress's tooltip: the same colours, text size and position next to the icon.Step 4:
- The page switches to dark theme.Step 5:
- The tooltip shows, readable in dark theme.
