C1062574 A/R Detail: Transaction Type names Credit Memo and Deposit and sorts them
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role).
- Have a customer with an open invoice A, an unused credit memo A and an unused deposit A, all dated 10 days ago, with invoice A due the same day, so all three sit in the "1-30 days past due" bucket.
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide.
- Open A/R Detail (Reports, then "A/R Aging Detail" under ACCOUNTS RECEIVABLE in the left menu).
- Leave the report with only that customer ticked in the Customer filter.Setup
- Make customer A: Click Customers in the top bar, then New Customer. Type a new Name for customer A, type a Phone, then click Save. On the customer's page click the edit icon beside the name; in Edit Customer pick Credit Terms "Net 30", pick Taxes "Exempt (0%)" and type 0 in Default Shop Supplies (so totals stay exact), then click Save. Click Add Contact, type a First name and Last name, then click Save. On the Assets tab click New Asset, pick the Contact, fill Make and the other boxes the window marks as required, then click Save.
- Make invoice A: First set customer A's Credit Terms to "Due on Receipt" (edit icon beside the name, Edit Customer, Save), so this invoice falls due on its own date; set them back to "Net 30" right after. On customer A's page click New Work Order, pick the Asset, then click Save (click Ok if a Customer Notes window opens). In the New Line window type a Title, pick Labor Rate "Fixed Line Total" (type "Fixed" in the box to find it), type 300.00 in Fixed Line Total, tick Line Approved, then click Save & Close. On the line click Complete; in Tech stories type a story and click Complete All Lines (if Missing Details asks for Mileage, type it and click Complete All Lines again). Open the Finance tab and click Mark Reviewed (if a window asks for the VIN, type one and click Confirm Review). Type the date 10 days before today in Invoice Date, then click Create Invoice. When the New Customer Payment window opens, reload the page (F5) so the invoice stays unpaid. Do not close that window with its ×: that takes the new invoice away again.
- Make credit memo A: On customer A's page open the Invoices tab and click Issue Credit. Type the date 10 days before today in Credit Date, type 50.00 in Amount, click Issue Store Credit (not Issue Refund), type a Reason, then click Issue Credit.
- Make deposit A: On customer A's page click New Work Order, pick the Asset, click Save, and close the New Line window. Open the Finance tab and click Add Deposit; in Create Deposit pick a Payment Method and type 41.00 in Deposit Amount, then click Record Deposit. Open the Lines tab and click New Line; in the New Line window type a Title, pick Labor Rate "Fixed Line Total" (type "Fixed" in the box to find it), type 1.00 in Fixed Line Total, tick Line Approved, then click Save & Close. On the line click Complete; in Tech stories type a story and click Complete All Lines (if Missing Details asks for Mileage, type it and click Complete All Lines again). Open the Finance tab and click Mark Reviewed (if a window asks for the VIN, type one and click Confirm Review). Leave Invoice Date as today, click Create Invoice, and reload the page (F5) when the New Customer Payment window opens. The invoice is paid from the deposit, and the rest, 40.00, stays as an unused deposit dated today.
STEPS: - Expand the "1-30 days past due" bucket.
- Click the "Transaction Type" header.
- Click the "Transaction Type" header again.
EXP: Expected Result
Step 1:
- Invoice A reads "Invoice", credit memo A reads "Credit Memo" and deposit A reads "Deposit" under Transaction Type.
- No row reads "Credit".Step 2:
- The rows sort A to Z by these names: credit memo A, then deposit A, then invoice A.Step 3:
- The order reverses: invoice A, then deposit A, then credit memo A.
Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
From: PRD "A/R and A/P Report Sorting, Export and Location Filter", Confluence 947650561, version 11 (5 Nov 2026, Chris Ward), read 10 Nov 2026 · Epic SV-11315, story SV-11532 (Story 2, Sort by clicking any column header)
- S2-R1b: “Transaction Type reads "Invoice" for invoices and bills, as today, and names each kind of credit: "Credit Memo" and "Deposit" on A/R, "Vendor Credit" on A/P. The same names appear in the PDF and the CSV, and Transaction Type sorts A to Z by these names (S2-R2).”
- S2-R2: “The first click gives the useful order for the kind of data: text (names, Location, Transaction Type) A to Z; money columns largest first; dates oldest first; Past Due largest first; ID and No. smallest number first (S2-R4). The second click reverses it. The third click repeats the first; there is no unsorted state.”
- What changes for existing users: “Transaction Type names each kind of credit: "Credit Memo", "Deposit" or "Vendor Credit"; today all three read "Credit" (S2-R1b).”Partly checked against build v26.45.2-6a8b89c (QA site sv11315) on 11/10/2026; not yet seen on that build: an unused deposit dated an earlier day (the part of a deposit left over is dated the day its invoice is made).
