C715528 Design: A/P Detail narrower than 1440 px scrolls under the pinned column
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role).
- Have open documents in these reports at two locations, so the Location column shows.
- Use a desktop browser window 1280 px wide.
- Open A/P Detail (Reports, then "A/P Aging Detail" under ACCOUNTS PAYABLE in the left menu).
- Leave the report with every bucket expanded.
STEPS: - Look at the right edge of the table.
- Scroll the table sideways to the right.
EXP: Expected Result
Step 1:
- "Open Balance" is pinned to the right edge and the table has a sideways scroll.Step 2:
- The other columns slide under the pinned "Open Balance" column, nothing overlaps.
- The bucket labels stay visible at the left.
- The title row stays on one line.
