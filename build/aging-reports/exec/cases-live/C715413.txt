C715413 A/P Summary: a row shows one location's name or Multiple
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role).
- Make sure the organization has open documents at three locations, location A, location B and location C.
- Have a vendor, vendor A, with open bills only at location A.
- Have a vendor, vendor B, with open bills at location A and location B.
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide.
- Open A/P Summary (Reports, then "A/P Aging Summary" under ACCOUNTS PAYABLE in the left menu).Setup
- Make location C: Click the location name and your initials at the top right of the top bar, then Settings, then Locations in the left menu, then New Location. Type a Name, fill the boxes the window marks as required, then click Save & Close. To make documents there, switch to it: click the location name and your initials at the top right of the top bar, then Change Location, then the new location.
- Locations to use: Use "Staging Heavy Duty - 9919" as location A (it comes first in A to Z order) and "Staging Lethbridge - 4310" as location B. To work at location B, click the location name and your initials at the top right of the top bar, then Change Location, then "Staging Lethbridge - 4310"; switch back to "Staging Heavy Duty - 9919" the same way when you are done.
- Make vendor A: Click Parts in the top bar, then Vendors in the left menu, then New Vendor. Type a new Name for vendor A, pick Credit Terms "Net 30" and Taxes "Exempt (0%)", then click Save & Close.
- Make bill 1: Click Parts in the top bar, then Purchase Orders in the left menu, then New PO. Pick vendor A in Vendor. In Part Number type a new part number and click "Add new special order part: …"; type a Description, 1 in Quantity and 100.00 in Cost; click Add, then Save & Close. In the Purchase Orders list click Receive on that order (Vendor column: vendor A); type a Vendor Invoice # (for example bill 1), type today's date in Invoice Date, type 0 in Tax, then click Receive Parts (1).
- Make bill 2: While switched to location B (see the Locations to use item): Same way as bill 1, with vendor A in Vendor: Cost 200.00; type today's date in Invoice Date.
- Make bill 3: While switched to location C (see the Make location C item): Same way as bill 1, with vendor A in Vendor: Cost 300.00; type today's date in Invoice Date.
- Make bill 1 for vendor A: Same way as bill 1, with vendor A in Vendor: Cost 100.00; type today's date in Invoice Date.
- Make vendor B: Click Parts in the top bar, then Vendors in the left menu, then New Vendor. Type a new Name for vendor B, pick Credit Terms "Net 30" and Taxes "Exempt (0%)", then click Save & Close.
- Make bill 1 for vendor B: Same way as bill 1, with vendor B in Vendor: Cost 100.00; type today's date in Invoice Date.
- Make bill 2 for vendor B: While switched to location B (see the Locations to use item): Same way as bill 1, with vendor B in Vendor: Cost 200.00; type today's date in Invoice Date.
STEPS: - Read vendor A's and vendor B's cells in the Location column.
- Untick location B in the Location menu (leaving location A and location C).
EXP: Expected Result
Step 1:
- Vendor A shows location A's name.
- Vendor B shows "Multiple".Step 2:
- The Location column stays (two locations ticked), and vendor B's Location cell now shows location A's name: only its documents at the chosen locations count.
- They are all at location A.
