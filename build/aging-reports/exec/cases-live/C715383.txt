C715383 A/R Summary: the Location menu has a search box above 8 locations
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role).
- Make sure the organization has open A/R documents at nine or more locations on today's date, among them location A.
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide.
- Open A/R Summary (Reports, then "A/R Aging Summary" under ACCOUNTS RECEIVABLE in the left menu).
STEPS: - Click the Location chip.
- Type the first word of location A's name into the search box.
EXP: Expected Result
Step 1:
- The menu opens with every location ticked, and a search box shows at the top, with the placeholder "Search locations".Step 2:
- The list narrows to the locations whose names match, location A among them.
