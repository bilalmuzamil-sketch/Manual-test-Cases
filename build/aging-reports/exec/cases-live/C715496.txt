C715496 Design: A/P Unpaid Invoices at 1440 px in light theme shows every column
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role).
- Have open documents in these reports at two locations, so the Location column shows.
- Have a vendor, vendor A, with a long name (more than 40 characters) and open documents at both locations on today's date, including a vendor credit (its Transaction Type is the longest).
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide, in light theme at 100% zoom.
- Open A/P Unpaid Invoices (Reports, then "A/P Unpaid Invoices" under ACCOUNTS PAYABLE in the left menu; the page title reads "A/P Unpaid Invoices Report").
- Leave the report with every group expanded (Expand all).
- Open Sales By Customer (Reports, then "Sales By Customer" under PERFORMANCE in the left menu) in a second tab of the same window, for comparison.Setup
- Locations to use: Use "Staging Heavy Duty - 9919" as location A (it comes first in A to Z order) and "Staging Lethbridge - 4310" as location B. To work at location B, click the location name and your initials at the top right of the top bar, then Change Location, then "Staging Lethbridge - 4310"; switch back to "Staging Heavy Duty - 9919" the same way when you are done.
- Make vendor A: Click Parts in the top bar, then Vendors in the left menu, then New Vendor. Type the Name ZZAUTOTEST Northern Alberta Heavy Haul and Towing Ltd (more than 40 characters), pick Credit Terms "Net 30" and Taxes "Exempt (0%)", then click Save & Close.
- Make bill 1: Click Parts in the top bar, then Purchase Orders in the left menu, then New PO. Pick vendor A in Vendor. In Part Number type a new part number and click "Add new special order part: …"; type a Description, 1 in Quantity and 300.00 in Cost; click Add, then Save & Close. In the Purchase Orders list click Receive on that order (Vendor column: vendor A); type a Vendor Invoice # (for example bill 1), type today's date in Invoice Date, type 0 in Tax, then click Receive Parts (1).
- Make bill 2: While switched to location B (see the Locations to use item): Same way as bill 1, with vendor A in Vendor: Cost 200.00; type today's date in Invoice Date.
- Make vendor credit 1: Click Parts in the top bar, then Returns in the left menu, then Create Return. Pick vendor A in Vendor, type a Packaging Slip, pick the part received on bill 1 in Part Number, type 50.00 in Price Per Unit, then click Save Return. In the Returns list tick that return and click Receive Credit; type a Credit Memo Number, type today's date in Credit Date, type 0 in Tax, then click Post Credit.
STEPS: - Look at the title row.
- Look at where the filter row sits, and compare with Sales By Customer.
- Read the column headings left to right, letter for letter, capitals included.
- Look for a sideways scroll bar below the last row of the report page.
- Look at vendor A's rows: the name and Transaction Type cells, and the money, date and number cells.
- Look across the whole page for any text that overlaps, is cut off or is clipped.
EXP: Expected Result
Step 1:
- The title, its info icon directly right of it, and the ⋯ Export button at the right end, all on one line.Step 2:
- The filter row sits below the title row, in the same place as on Sales By Customer.Step 3:
- "Date", "Location", "Transaction Type", "No.", "Vendor Name", "Due Date", "Past Due", "Amount", "Open Balance" ("Location" shown because two locations are in view; it sits right after "Date").
- No heading wraps.Step 4:
- There is none: every column is visible at 1440 px without scrolling sideways.Step 5:
- The long name and a long Transaction Type wrap onto a second line.
- Money, date and number cells stay on one line, and a long location name wraps onto a second line (Location wraps only here and on A/P Detail).Step 6:
- Nothing overlaps, is cut off or clipped.
Where the tech plan differs: the tech plan (21 Oct 2026) says Location wraps only on A/P Detail; PRD version 11 (its layout rules, 5 Nov 2026) lets it wrap on A/P Unpaid Invoices too. This case follows the PRD.
