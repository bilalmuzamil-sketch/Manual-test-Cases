C715535 Design: the Export menu matches the design picture
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role).
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide, in light theme at 100% zoom.
- Open A/R Summary (Reports, then "A/R Aging Summary" under ACCOUNTS RECEIVABLE in the left menu).
- Open the design picture "Export menu: PDF and CSV" (ar-ap-p2-export-menu-pdf-and-csv.png) (attached to this case) beside the browser at 100% zoom; compare the layout, labels and styles, not the names and amounts, which are only examples.
STEPS: - Click the ⋯ button at the right of the title row.
EXP: Expected Result
Step 1:
- The menu opens, as in the picture: while the menu is open the ⋯ button shows a light-grey square.
- The white menu with a shadow opens under it, its right edge lined up with the button, over the right end of the column headings.
- Its rows read, letter for letter, "Download (PDF)" then "Download (CSV)", nothing else.
