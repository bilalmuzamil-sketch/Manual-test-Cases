C715514 Design: A/P Past due chip and its tick-box menu
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role).
- Have open documents in this report at two locations, so the Location chip shows.
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide, in light theme at 100% zoom.
- Open A/P Detail (Reports, then "A/P Aging Detail" under ACCOUNTS PAYABLE in the left menu).
STEPS: - Look at the Past due chip.
- Click it and read the menu, row by row, letter for letter.
- Tick "91+ days".
- Click the Location chip next to it and compare its menu with the Past due menu.
EXP: Expected Result
Step 1:
- "Past due: Any" with an hourglass icon, in the resting (grey) style.Step 2:
- In this order: "Any", the line "All ranges are included. Pick one or more to narrow.", "1-30 days", "31-60 days", "61-90 days", "91+ days".Step 3:
- The chip reads "Past due: 91+ days", no longer in the resting style.Step 4:
- The Location menu opens.
- The Past due menu is the same kind of tick-box menu as the Location menu: the same tick boxes, spacing and position under its chip.
