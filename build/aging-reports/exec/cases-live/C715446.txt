C715446 A/R Collection: a customer with no phone shows no Phone label
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role).
- Have a customer, customer A, with an overdue invoice and no phone number on its customer card.
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide.
- Open A/R Collection (Reports, then "A/R Aging Collection" under ACCOUNTS RECEIVABLE in the left menu).
- Leave the report with only customer A ticked.Setup
- Make customer A: Click Customers in the top bar, then New Customer. Type a new Name for customer A (leave Phone empty), then click Save. On the customer's page click the edit icon beside the name; in Edit Customer pick Credit Terms "Net 30", pick Taxes "Exempt (0%)" and type 0 in Default Shop Supplies (so totals stay exact), then click Save. Click Add Contact, type a First name and Last name, then click Save. On the Assets tab click New Asset, pick the Contact, fill Make and the other boxes the window marks as required, then click Save.
- Make invoice for customer A: On customer A's page click New Work Order, pick the Asset, then click Save (click Ok if a Customer Notes window opens). In the New Line window type a Title, pick Labor Rate "Fixed Line Total" (type "Fixed" in the box to find it), type 100.00 in Fixed Line Total, tick Line Approved, then click Save & Close. On the line click Complete; in Tech stories type a story and click Complete All Lines (if Missing Details asks for Mileage, type it and click Complete All Lines again). Open the Finance tab and click Mark Reviewed (if a window asks for the VIN, type one and click Confirm Review). Type the date 45 days before today in Invoice Date, then click Create Invoice. When the New Customer Payment window opens, reload the page (F5) so the invoice stays unpaid. Do not close that window with its ×: that takes the new invoice away again.
STEPS: - Read customer A's group row.
- Choose "Download (PDF)" from the ⋯ menu.
- Read customer A's line in the PDF.
EXP: Expected Result
Step 1:
- No "Phone:" label at all.Step 2:
- The PDF downloads.Step 3:
- No "Phone:" label on the line.
