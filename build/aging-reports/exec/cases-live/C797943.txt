C797943 A/P Summary: the Vendor menu has no search box with 8 vendors or fewer
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role).
- Make sure your organization has 8 vendors or fewer in total.
- Make sure that at least one of those vendors, vendor A, has an unpaid bill on today's date.
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide.
- Open A/P Summary (Reports, then "A/P Aging Summary" under ACCOUNTS PAYABLE in the left menu).
STEPS: - Click the Vendor chip and read the menu from top to bottom.
EXP: Expected Result
Step 1:
- The menu opens with no search box: it starts with the "All vendors" row, then the line "All vendors are included. Pick one or more to narrow.", then the vendors, vendor A among them.
