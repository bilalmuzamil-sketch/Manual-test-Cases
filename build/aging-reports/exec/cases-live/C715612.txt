C715612 A/R Summary: the Totals row adds up every customer, not only those loaded
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role).
- Make sure that between 31 and 90 customers have open documents on today's date.
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide.
- Open A/R Summary (Reports, then "A/R Aging Summary" under ACCOUNTS RECEIVABLE in the left menu).
STEPS: - Choose "Download (CSV)" from the ⋯ menu.
- Open the CSV and read its Totals row.
- Switch back to the report and read its Totals row.
- Scroll down until the next customers load.
EXP: Expected Result
Step 1:
- The CSV downloads.Step 2:
- Note the Totals row's Total (it adds up every row of the report).Step 3:
- The screen's Totals row reads the same Total as the CSV's: it adds up every customer, not only the 30 loaded so far.Step 4:
- The Totals row reads the same after more load.
Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
From: PRD "A/R and A/P Report Sorting, Export and Location Filter", Confluence 947650561, version 11 (5 Nov 2026, Chris Ward), read 10 Nov 2026 · Epic SV-11315, story SV-11531 (Story 1, Reports open in QuickBooks name order), story SV-11533 (Story 3, Export to PDF and CSV), story SV-11535 (Story 5, Filters: customer or vendor, past due and location)
- S5-R18: “The Totals row always adds up exactly the rows the filters leave (customer or vendor, Past due, Location), on screen on every page, in the PDF and in the CSV. On A/R Collection and A/P Unpaid Invoices this fixes today's Totals row, which ignores the customer or vendor filter.”
- S3-R3: “The PDF and the CSV contain exactly the rows on screen for the chosen as-of date, customer or vendor and locations, in the on-screen sort, including a sort changed after an earlier export, without refreshing the page.”
- S1-R8: “A/R Summary, A/P Summary, A/R Collection and A/P Unpaid Invoices show 30 customers or vendors at a time, and the next 30 load as the user scrolls down, as today. There are no page buttons. A/R Detail and A/P Detail show each bucket's first 100 lines, then load more as the user scrolls (S4-R4). Paging never repeats or skips a customer, vendor or row, in any sort. On A/R Collection and A/P Unpaid Invoices, an open customer or vendor shows its first 100 documents, then the next 100 as the user scrolls; its document count, its subtotals, the Totals row and the exports always cover all of its documents.”Last checked against build v26.45.2-6a8b89c (QA site sv11315) on 11/10/2026.
