C715472 On release day an untouched A/P report shows the same rows and amounts
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role).
- Have open documents in these reports at two locations, so the Location chip shows.
- Make sure that before the release was installed, you took screenshots (and, for A/P, any print or export there was) of the three A/P reports for one as-of date before the release, untouched.
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide.
- After the release is installed, open Reports (top menu; its left menu lists the A/R reports under ACCOUNTS RECEIVABLE and the A/P reports under ACCOUNTS PAYABLE).
- On each report, as you open it, choose the as-of date the screenshots show.
STEPS: - Open A/P Summary from the Reports menu without touching any filter, and compare its rows, amounts and Totals with the screenshot of A/P Summary taken before the release.
- Open A/P Detail from the Reports menu without touching any filter, and compare its rows, amounts and Totals with the screenshot of A/P Detail taken before the release.
- Open A/P Unpaid Invoices from the Reports menu without touching any filter, and compare its rows, amounts and Totals with the screenshot of A/P Unpaid Invoices taken before the release.
EXP: Expected Result
Step 1:
- The same vendors and the same amounts, shown the same way (same $ sign, commas, two decimals and credit format).
- Only the order may differ, and, as PRD section 2 lists for this release, a vendor credit dated exactly on the as-of date now counts in Current, where it was in 1-30 Days.
- The Location chip reads "Location: All locations": an untouched report is the organization-wide report it was before.Step 2:
- The same documents and the same amounts, shown the same way (same $ sign, commas, two decimals and credit format).
- Only the order may differ, and these three changes PRD section 2 lists for this release: Date and Due Date are plain dates (for example "Nov 4, 2026") where the screenshot shows "Today", "Yesterday" or "2 days ago"; a vendor credit shows "Vendor Credit" under Transaction Type where it shows "Credit"; and a vendor credit dated exactly on the as-of date is in "Current" with Past Due 0, where it was in "1-30 days past due" with Past Due 1.
- The Location chip reads "Location: All locations": an untouched report is the organization-wide report it was before.Step 3:
- The same documents and the same amounts, shown the same way (same $ sign, commas, two decimals and credit format).
- Only the order may differ, and these two changes PRD section 2 lists for this release: Date and Due Date are plain dates (for example "Nov 4, 2026") where the screenshot shows "Today", "Yesterday" or "2 days ago"; and a vendor credit shows "Vendor Credit" under Transaction Type where it shows "Credit".
- The Location chip reads "Location: All locations": an untouched report is the organization-wide report it was before.
Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
From: PRD "A/R and A/P Report Sorting, Export and Location Filter", Confluence 947650561, version 11 (5 Nov 2026, Chris Ward), read 10 Nov 2026 · Epic SV-11315, story SV-11531 (Story 1, Reports open in QuickBooks name order), story SV-11532 (Story 2, Sort by clicking any column header), story SV-11535 (Story 5, Filters: customer or vendor, past due and location)
- What changes for existing users: “Which rows and amounts each user can see is unchanged: an untouched report shows exactly today's organization-wide numbers (apart from the Totals rows noted above), and the reports show money exactly as they do today.”
- S5-R5: “The filter opens with every location in the list ticked (All locations), so an untouched report shows exactly today's organization-wide rows and totals. "Select all" ticks every location again.”
- Key Decisions, organization-wide: “The reports stay organization-wide, by decision. Everyone with access sees every location's data, as today; the Location filter only narrows. Rationale: no permission changes in this release (decided 2026-10-15). Rejected: restricting the reports to the locations a user is enrolled in, which would be its own announced permissions change later.”
- What changes for existing users: “A credit memo, deposit or vendor credit dated exactly on the as-of date now shows in Current with Past Due 0, as in QuickBooks; today it shows in 1-30 (S1-R5a).”
- What changes for existing users: “Date and Due Date show plain dates, for example "Nov 4, 2026", instead of "Today", "Yesterday" or "2 days ago" (S2-R1a).”
- What changes for existing users: “Transaction Type names each kind of credit: "Credit Memo", "Deposit" or "Vendor Credit"; today all three read "Credit" (S2-R1b).”Last checked against build v26.45.2-6a8b89c (QA site sv11315) on 11/10/2026.
