C715548 Design: the Location menu with two locations matches the design picture
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role).
- Have open documents in these reports at two locations, location A and location B, with location A's name first in A to Z order.
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide, in light theme at 100% zoom.
- Open A/R Detail (Reports, then "A/R Aging Detail" under ACCOUNTS RECEIVABLE in the left menu).
- Open the design picture "The native Location menu" (ar-ap-p4-the-native-location-menu.png) (attached to this case) beside the browser at 100% zoom; compare the layout, labels and styles, not the names and amounts, which are only examples.
STEPS: - Click the Location chip.
- Widen the browser window to 1536 px and look across the page.
EXP: Expected Result
Step 1:
- The menu opens under the chip.
- As in the picture, while the menu is open the chip shows a circled "×" in place of its down arrow.
- The menu rows read, top to bottom, "Select all" with "2 selected" at its right, location A, location B, a dividing line, then "Clear selection".
- No search box.
- The ticked boxes have a light-blue fill with a blue tick.Step 2:
- Nothing overlaps, is cut off or clipped.
- The layout is the same as at 1440 px.
