C715510 Design: A/P Location chip and menu match Sales By Customer's
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role).
- Have open documents in these reports at two locations, so the Location menu lists two locations ("2 selected").
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide, in light theme at 100% zoom.
- Open A/P Unpaid Invoices (Reports, then "A/P Unpaid Invoices" under ACCOUNTS PAYABLE in the left menu; the page title reads "A/P Unpaid Invoices Report").
- Open Sales By Customer (Reports, then "Sales By Customer" under PERFORMANCE in the left menu) in a second tab of the same window, for comparison.
STEPS: - Look at the Location chip and compare with Sales By Customer's.
- Click it and read the menu, row by row, letter for letter.
- Compare the open menu with Sales By Customer's open Location menu.
- Switch ShopView to dark theme.
- Click the Location chip again.
EXP: Expected Result
Step 1:
- Label "Location" with the location pin icon, the same chip as Sales By Customer's.Step 2:
- In this order: "Select all", the count ("2 selected"), the locations with the ticked ones first, and "Clear selection".
- No search box (fewer than 9 locations).Step 3:
- The same menu: same rows, order, tick boxes, spacing and position under the chip.Step 4:
- The page switches to dark theme.Step 5:
- The menu opens: the same, readable in dark theme, nothing clipped.
