C715632 Design: A/P Unpaid Invoices links are blue, semi-bold and not underlined
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role).
- Have a vendor with an unpaid bill received on a delivery at your current location on today's date, so its number shows as a link.
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide, in light theme at 100% zoom.
- Open A/P Unpaid Invoices (Reports, then "A/P Unpaid Invoices" under ACCOUNTS PAYABLE in the left menu; the page title reads "A/P Unpaid Invoices Report").
- Leave the report with every group expanded (Expand all).
- Open Work In Progress (Reports, then "Work In Progress" under PERFORMANCE in the left menu) in a second tab of the same window, so its links can be compared.
STEPS: - On the report page, look at the document numbers that are links and compare them with the links on Work In Progress.
- Press Tab until a link has keyboard focus.
EXP: Expected Result
Step 1:
- They are blue, the same blue as the links on Work In Progress, semi-bold and not underlined.Step 2:
- The focused link is underlined.
- Links are underlined only on keyboard focus.
