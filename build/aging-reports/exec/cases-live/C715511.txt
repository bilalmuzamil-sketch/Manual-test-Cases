C715511 Design: A/R Customer chip and menu match Sales By Customer's
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role).
- Have a customer, customer A, with open invoices on today's date and a long name (more than 40 characters).
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide, in light theme at 100% zoom.
- Open A/R Collection (Reports, then "A/R Aging Collection" under ACCOUNTS RECEIVABLE in the left menu).
- Open Sales By Customer (Reports, then "Sales By Customer" under PERFORMANCE in the left menu) in a second tab of the same window, for comparison.Setup
- Make customer A: Click Customers in the top bar, then New Customer. Type the Name ZZAUTOTEST Northern Alberta Heavy Haul and Towing Ltd (more than 40 characters), type a Phone, then click Save. On the customer's page click the edit icon beside the name; in Edit Customer pick Credit Terms "Net 30", pick Taxes "Exempt (0%)" and type 0 in Default Shop Supplies (so totals stay exact), then click Save. Click Add Contact, type a First name and Last name, then click Save. On the Assets tab click New Asset, pick the Contact, fill Make and the other boxes the window marks as required, then click Save.
- Make invoice 1: On customer A's page click New Work Order, pick the Asset, then click Save (click Ok if a Customer Notes window opens). In the New Line window type a Title, pick Labor Rate "Fixed Line Total" (type "Fixed" in the box to find it), type 100.00 in Fixed Line Total, tick Line Approved, then click Save & Close. On the line click Complete; in Tech stories type a story and click Complete All Lines (if Missing Details asks for Mileage, type it and click Complete All Lines again). Open the Finance tab and click Mark Reviewed (if a window asks for the VIN, type one and click Confirm Review). Type today's date in Invoice Date, then click Create Invoice. When the New Customer Payment window opens, reload the page (F5) so the invoice stays unpaid. Do not close that window with its ×: that takes the new invoice away again.
STEPS: - Look at the Customer chip and compare with Sales By Customer's.
- Click it and read the menu, row by row, letter for letter.
- Tick customer A and look at the chip.
- Compare the open menu with Sales By Customer's open Customer menu.
EXP: Expected Result
Step 1:
- "Customer: All customers" with the person icon, in the resting (grey) style, as on Sales By Customer.Step 2:
- In this order: a search box, "All customers", the line "All customers are included. Pick one or more to narrow.", then the customers.Step 3:
- The chip shows the name shortened with "..." and leaves the resting style.Step 4:
- The same menu: rows, order, tick boxes, spacing and position.
