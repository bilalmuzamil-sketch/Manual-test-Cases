C715316 A/R Summary: the sort stays while changing date, scrolling and filters
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role).
- Have open documents in these reports at two locations, location A and location B, with location A's name first in A to Z order.
- Make sure the report has more than 30 customers with open documents on today's date and on yesterday's date, at both locations.
- Have a customer, customer A, with open documents at both locations.
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide.
- Open A/R Summary (Reports, then "A/R Aging Summary" under ACCOUNTS RECEIVABLE in the left menu).Setup
- Locations to use: Use "Staging Heavy Duty - 9919" as location A (it comes first in A to Z order) and "Staging Lethbridge - 4310" as location B. To work at location B, click the location name and your initials at the top right of the top bar, then Change Location, then "Staging Lethbridge - 4310"; switch back to "Staging Heavy Duty - 9919" the same way when you are done.
- Make customer A: Click Customers in the top bar, then New Customer. Type a new Name for customer A, type a Phone, then click Save. On the customer's page click the edit icon beside the name; in Edit Customer pick Credit Terms "Net 30", pick Taxes "Exempt (0%)" and type 0 in Default Shop Supplies (so totals stay exact), then click Save. Click Add Contact, type a First name and Last name, then click Save. On the Assets tab click New Asset, pick the Contact, fill Make and the other boxes the window marks as required, then click Save.
- Make invoice 1: On customer A's page click New Work Order, pick the Asset, then click Save (click Ok if a Customer Notes window opens). In the New Line window type a Title, pick Labor Rate "Fixed Line Total" (type "Fixed" in the box to find it), type 100.00 in Fixed Line Total, tick Line Approved, then click Save & Close. On the line click Complete; in Tech stories type a story and click Complete All Lines (if Missing Details asks for Mileage, type it and click Complete All Lines again). Open the Finance tab and click Mark Reviewed (if a window asks for the VIN, type one and click Confirm Review). Type today's date in Invoice Date, then click Create Invoice. When the New Customer Payment window opens, reload the page (F5) so the invoice stays unpaid. Do not close that window with its ×: that takes the new invoice away again.
- Make invoice 2: While switched to location B (see the Locations to use item): Same way as invoice 1, on customer A's page: Fixed Line Total 200.00; type today's date in Invoice Date; then in the New Customer Payment window reload the page (F5) so the invoice stays unpaid. Do not close that window with its ×: that takes the new invoice away again.
STEPS: - Click the "Total" header once.
- Change the as-of date to yesterday.
- Scroll down until the next 30 customers load.
- Open the Customer filter.
- Tick customer A.
- In the Location filter, untick location B.
EXP: Expected Result
Step 1:
- The rows sort by Total, largest first.
- "Total" is marked as the sorted column (a small arrow beside its heading).Step 2:
- The report reloads for yesterday and is still sorted by Total, largest first.Step 3:
- The new rows are still sorted by Total, largest first.Step 4:
- The Customer menu opens.Step 5:
- Only customer A's rows remain, still sorted by Total, largest first.Step 6:
- Only location A rows remain, still sorted by Total, largest first.
- "Total" is still marked as the sorted column (a small arrow beside its heading).
Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
From: PRD "A/R and A/P Report Sorting, Export and Location Filter", Confluence 947650561, version 11 (5 Nov 2026, Chris Ward), read 10 Nov 2026 · Epic SV-11315, story SV-11531 (Story 1, Reports open in QuickBooks name order), story SV-11532 (Story 2, Sort by clicking any column header)
- S2-R7: “The sort is kept while the user stays on the report: changing the as-of date, loading more rows by scrolling, and changing the customer, vendor, Past due or Location filter keep it, except as in S2-R7a.”
- S2-R2: “The first click gives the useful order for the kind of data: text (names, Location, Transaction Type) A to Z; money columns largest first; dates oldest first; Past Due largest first; ID and No. smallest number first (S2-R4). The second click reverses it. The third click repeats the first; there is no unsorted state.”
- S1-R8: “A/R Summary, A/P Summary, A/R Collection and A/P Unpaid Invoices show 30 customers or vendors at a time, and the next 30 load as the user scrolls down, as today. There are no page buttons. A/R Detail and A/P Detail show each bucket's first 100 lines, then load more as the user scrolls (S4-R4). Paging never repeats or skips a customer, vendor or row, in any sort. On A/R Collection and A/P Unpaid Invoices, an open customer or vendor shows its first 100 documents, then the next 100 as the user scrolls; its document count, its subtotals, the Totals row and the exports always cover all of its documents.”Partly checked against build v26.45.2-6a8b89c (QA site sv11315) on 11/10/2026; not yet seen on that build: more than 30 customers with open documents at both locations on today's and yesterday's date (not counted on this build).
