C715545 Design: A/R Detail invoice numbers are blue links, credit memos plain text
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role).
- Have open documents in these reports at two locations: your current location and your other location.
- Make sure you are enrolled at both locations, so an invoice at your other location is a link.
- Have a customer, customer A, with an open invoice made at your other location, more than 90 days past due.
- Have a customer, customer B, with an unused credit memo at your current location, more than 90 days past due.
- Have a customer, customer C, with an open invoice at your current location, more than 90 days past due.
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide, in light theme at 100% zoom.
- Open A/R Detail (Reports, then "A/R Aging Detail" under ACCOUNTS RECEIVABLE in the left menu).
- Leave the report with the "91 or more days past due" bucket expanded.
- Open the design picture "Links only where they can open" (ar-ap-p5-links-only-where-they-can-open-reshot-2026-11-05.png) (attached to this case) beside the browser at 100% zoom; compare the layout, labels and styles, not the names and amounts, which are only examples. Do not use the older attachment of the same picture, "Links only where they can open" (ar-ap-p5-links-only-where-they-can-open.png): it shows the design before 23 Oct 2026.Setup
- Locations to use: Use "Staging Heavy Duty - 9919" as location A (it comes first in A to Z order) and "Staging Lethbridge - 4310" as location B. To work at location B, click the location name and your initials at the top right of the top bar, then Change Location, then "Staging Lethbridge - 4310"; switch back to "Staging Heavy Duty - 9919" the same way when you are done.
- Make customer A: Click Customers in the top bar, then New Customer. Type a new Name for customer A, type a Phone, then click Save. On the customer's page click the edit icon beside the name; in Edit Customer pick Credit Terms "Net 30", pick Taxes "Exempt (0%)" and type 0 in Default Shop Supplies (so totals stay exact), then click Save. Click Add Contact, type a First name and Last name, then click Save. On the Assets tab click New Asset, pick the Contact, fill Make and the other boxes the window marks as required, then click Save.
- Make invoice for customer A: While switched to location B (see the Locations to use item): On customer A's page click New Work Order, pick the Asset, then click Save (click Ok if a Customer Notes window opens). In the New Line window type a Title, pick Labor Rate "Fixed Line Total" (type "Fixed" in the box to find it), type 100.00 in Fixed Line Total, tick Line Approved, then click Save & Close. On the line click Complete; in Tech stories type a story and click Complete All Lines (if Missing Details asks for Mileage, type it and click Complete All Lines again). Open the Finance tab and click Mark Reviewed (if a window asks for the VIN, type one and click Confirm Review). Type the date 130 days before today in Invoice Date, then click Create Invoice. When the New Customer Payment window opens, reload the page (F5) so the invoice stays unpaid. Do not close that window with its ×: that takes the new invoice away again.
- Make customer B: Click Customers in the top bar, then New Customer. Type a new Name for customer B, type a Phone, then click Save. On the customer's page click the edit icon beside the name; in Edit Customer pick Credit Terms "Net 30", pick Taxes "Exempt (0%)" and type 0 in Default Shop Supplies (so totals stay exact), then click Save. Click Add Contact, type a First name and Last name, then click Save. On the Assets tab click New Asset, pick the Contact, fill Make and the other boxes the window marks as required, then click Save.
- Make credit memo for customer B: On customer B's page open the Invoices tab and click Issue Credit. Type the date 100 days before today in Credit Date, type 50.00 in Amount, click Issue Store Credit (not Issue Refund), type a Reason, then click Issue Credit.
- Make customer C: Click Customers in the top bar, then New Customer. Type a new Name for customer C, type a Phone, then click Save. On the customer's page click the edit icon beside the name; in Edit Customer pick Credit Terms "Net 30", pick Taxes "Exempt (0%)" and type 0 in Default Shop Supplies (so totals stay exact), then click Save. Click Add Contact, type a First name and Last name, then click Save. On the Assets tab click New Asset, pick the Contact, fill Make and the other boxes the window marks as required, then click Save.
- Make invoice for customer C: Same way as invoice for customer A, on customer C's page: Fixed Line Total 300.00; type the date 130 days before today in Invoice Date; then in the New Customer Payment window reload the page (F5) so the invoice stays unpaid. Do not close that window with its ×: that takes the new invoice away again.
