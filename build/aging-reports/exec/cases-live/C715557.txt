C715557 Design: the call list for one customer matches the design picture
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role).
- Make sure your role has the "View and Manage AP/AR Data" switch on, so the credit term shows.
- Have a customer, customer A, with a long name, an overdue invoice, a phone number and a credit term.
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide, in light theme at 100% zoom.
- Open A/R Collection (Reports, then "A/R Aging Collection" under ACCOUNTS RECEIVABLE in the left menu).
- Open the design picture "Call list on A/R Collection" (ar-ap-p7-call-list-on-a-r-collection.png) (attached to this case) beside the browser at 100% zoom; compare the layout, labels and styles, not the names and amounts, which are only examples.Setup
- Make customer A: Click Customers in the top bar, then New Customer. Type the Name ZZAUTOTEST Northern Alberta Heavy Haul and Towing Ltd (more than 40 characters), type a Phone, then click Save. On the customer's page click the edit icon beside the name; in Edit Customer pick Credit Terms "Net 30", pick Taxes "Exempt (0%)" and type 0 in Default Shop Supplies (so totals stay exact), then click Save. Click Add Contact, type a First name and Last name, then click Save. On the Assets tab click New Asset, pick the Contact, fill Make and the other boxes the window marks as required, then click Save.
- Make invoice 1: On customer A's page click New Work Order, pick the Asset, then click Save (click Ok if a Customer Notes window opens). In the New Line window type a Title, pick Labor Rate "Fixed Line Total" (type "Fixed" in the box to find it), type 100.00 in Fixed Line Total, tick Line Approved, then click Save & Close. On the line click Complete; in Tech stories type a story and click Complete All Lines (if Missing Details asks for Mileage, type it and click Complete All Lines again). Open the Finance tab and click Mark Reviewed (if a window asks for the VIN, type one and click Confirm Review). Type the date 45 days before today in Invoice Date, then click Create Invoice. When the New Customer Payment window opens, reload the page (F5) so the invoice stays unpaid. Do not close that window with its ×: that takes the new invoice away again.
STEPS: - In the Customer filter, tick customer A.
EXP: Expected Result
Step 1:
- Only customer A's group is listed.
- As in the picture: the chip reads "Customer: " and customer A's name, shortened with "...", in a blue pill.
- A closed group row: a chevron pointing right, the bold name and count, grey "Phone: " and the number, grey "Credit term: " and the term, "Total" and the sums.
- The Totals row equals the group's sums.
