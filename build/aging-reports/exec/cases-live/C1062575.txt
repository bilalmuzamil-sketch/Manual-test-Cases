C1062575 A/P Detail: Transaction Type names Vendor Credit and sorts it after Invoice
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role).
- Have a vendor with an unpaid bill A and an unused vendor credit A, both dated 10 days ago, with bill A due the same day, so both sit in the "1-30 days past due" bucket.
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide.
- Open A/P Detail (Reports, then "A/P Aging Detail" under ACCOUNTS PAYABLE in the left menu).
- Leave the report with only that vendor ticked in the Vendor filter.Setup
- Make vendor A: Click Parts in the top bar, then Vendors in the left menu, then New Vendor. Type a new Name for vendor A, pick Credit Terms "Net 30" and Taxes "Exempt (0%)", then click Save & Close.
- Make bill A: Click Parts in the top bar, then Purchase Orders in the left menu, then New PO. Pick vendor A in Vendor. In Part Number type a new part number and click "Add new special order part: …"; type a Description, 1 in Quantity and 300.00 in Cost; click Add, then Save & Close. In the Purchase Orders list click Receive on that order (Vendor column: vendor A); type a Vendor Invoice # (for example bill A), type the date 10 days before today in Invoice Date, type 0 in Tax, then click Receive Parts (1).
- Make vendor credit A: Click Parts in the top bar, then Returns in the left menu, then Create Return. Pick vendor A in Vendor, type a Packaging Slip, pick the part received on bill A in Part Number, type 50.00 in Price Per Unit, then click Save Return. In the Returns list tick that return and click Receive Credit; type a Credit Memo Number, type the date 10 days before today in Credit Date, type 0 in Tax, then click Post Credit.
STEPS: - Expand the "1-30 days past due" bucket.
- Click the "Transaction Type" header.
- Click the "Transaction Type" header again.
EXP: Expected Result
Step 1:
- Bill A reads "Invoice" and vendor credit A reads "Vendor Credit" under Transaction Type.
- No row reads "Credit".Step 2:
- The rows sort A to Z by these names: bill A (Invoice), then vendor credit A (Vendor Credit).Step 3:
- The order reverses: vendor credit A, then bill A.
Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
From: PRD "A/R and A/P Report Sorting, Export and Location Filter", Confluence 947650561, version 11 (5 Nov 2026, Chris Ward), read 10 Nov 2026 · Epic SV-11315, story SV-11532 (Story 2, Sort by clicking any column header)
- S2-R1b: “Transaction Type reads "Invoice" for invoices and bills, as today, and names each kind of credit: "Credit Memo" and "Deposit" on A/R, "Vendor Credit" on A/P. The same names appear in the PDF and the CSV, and Transaction Type sorts A to Z by these names (S2-R2).”
- S2-R2: “The first click gives the useful order for the kind of data: text (names, Location, Transaction Type) A to Z; money columns largest first; dates oldest first; Past Due largest first; ID and No. smallest number first (S2-R4). The second click reverses it. The third click repeats the first; there is no unsorted state.”
- What changes for existing users: “Transaction Type names each kind of credit: "Credit Memo", "Deposit" or "Vendor Credit"; today all three read "Credit" (S2-R1b).”Partly checked against build v26.45.2-6a8b89c (QA site sv11315) on 11/10/2026; not yet seen on that build: a bill that falls due on its own date (vendor Credit Terms other than Net 30 were not tried).
