C715512 Design: A/P Vendor chip and menu match Inventory Value's
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role).
- Have a vendor with an unpaid bill on today's date, so the Vendor menu lists vendors.
- Make sure the organization has more than 8 vendors (the Vendor menu shows its search box only when there are more than 8 vendors).
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide, in light theme at 100% zoom.
- Open A/P Detail (Reports, then "A/P Aging Detail" under ACCOUNTS PAYABLE in the left menu).
- Open Inventory Value (Reports, then "Inventory Value" under PARTS in the left menu) in a second tab of the same window, for comparison.
STEPS: - Look at the Vendor chip and compare with Inventory Value's Vendor chip.
- Click it and read the menu, row by row, letter for letter.
- Compare the open menu with Inventory Value's open Vendor menu.
EXP: Expected Result
Step 1:
- "Vendor: All vendors" with the truck icon, in the resting (grey) style, as on Inventory Value.Step 2:
- In this order: a search box with the placeholder "Search vendors", "All vendors", the line "All vendors are included. Pick one or more to narrow.", then the vendors.Step 3:
- The same menu: rows, order, tick boxes, spacing and position.
