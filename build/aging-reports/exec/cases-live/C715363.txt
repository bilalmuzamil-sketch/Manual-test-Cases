C715363 A/R Collection: the group row stays pinned under the headings
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role).
- Make sure your role has the "View and Manage AP/AR Data" switch on, so the credit term shows.
- Make sure that on today's date the report has two customers next to each other in name order, customer A then customer B, each with more overdue invoices than fit on the screen and with a phone number and a credit term on its customer card.
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide.
- Open A/R Collection (Reports, then "A/R Aging Collection" under ACCOUNTS RECEIVABLE in the left menu).
- Leave the report with the groups of customer A and customer B expanded.Setup
- Make customer A: Click Customers in the top bar, then New Customer. Type the Name ZZAUTOTEST Call List A, type a Phone, then click Save. On the customer's page click the edit icon beside the name; in Edit Customer pick Credit Terms "Net 30", pick Taxes "Exempt (0%)" and type 0 in Default Shop Supplies (so totals stay exact), then click Save. Click Add Contact, type a First name and Last name, then click Save. On the Assets tab click New Asset, pick the Contact, fill Make and the other boxes the window marks as required, then click Save.
- Make invoice 1 for customer A: On customer A's page click New Work Order, pick the Asset, then click Save (click Ok if a Customer Notes window opens). In the New Line window type a Title, pick Labor Rate "Fixed Line Total" (type "Fixed" in the box to find it), type 10.00 in Fixed Line Total, tick Line Approved, then click Save & Close. On the line click Complete; in Tech stories type a story and click Complete All Lines (if Missing Details asks for Mileage, type it and click Complete All Lines again). Open the Finance tab and click Mark Reviewed (if a window asks for the VIN, type one and click Confirm Review). Type the date 45 days before today in Invoice Date, then click Create Invoice. When the New Customer Payment window opens, reload the page (F5) so the invoice stays unpaid. Do not close that window with its ×: that takes the new invoice away again.
- Make 19 more invoices for customer A: Same way as invoice 1 for customer A, 19 more times (any amounts, Invoice Date 45 days before today), so customer A has more overdue invoices than fit on the screen.
- Make customer B: Click Customers in the top bar, then New Customer. Type the Name ZZAUTOTEST Call List B, type a Phone, then click Save. On the customer's page click the edit icon beside the name; in Edit Customer pick Credit Terms "Net 30", pick Taxes "Exempt (0%)" and type 0 in Default Shop Supplies (so totals stay exact), then click Save. Click Add Contact, type a First name and Last name, then click Save. On the Assets tab click New Asset, pick the Contact, fill Make and the other boxes the window marks as required, then click Save.
- Make invoice 1 for customer B: Same way as invoice 1 for customer A, on customer B's page: Fixed Line Total 10.00; type the date 45 days before today in Invoice Date; then in the New Customer Payment window reload the page (F5) so the invoice stays unpaid. Do not close that window with its ×: that takes the new invoice away again.
- Make 19 more invoices for customer B: Same way as invoice 1 for customer B, 19 more times (any amounts, Invoice Date 45 days before today), so customer B has more overdue invoices than fit on the screen.
STEPS: - Scroll down slowly into customer A's rows.
- Keep scrolling until customer B's row reaches the top.
- Keep scrolling into customer B's rows.
- Scroll back up slowly until you are inside customer A's rows again.
- Scroll down fast through customer A's and customer B's rows.
- Scroll fast back up through customer B's and customer A's rows.
EXP: Expected Result
Step 1:
- Customer A's row stays pinned just under the column headings while its rows scroll beneath it.
- It shows the customer group's chevron (pointing down) and its label: customer A's name and count, then "Phone: " and the phone number and "Credit term: " and the credit term.
- It shows no amounts (the customer group's sums stay in its own subtotal row).Step 2:
- Customer B's row pushes customer A's row up and off and takes its place.
- Only one group row is pinned at a time.Step 3:
- Customer B's row stays pinned.Step 4:
- The change reverses: customer A's row comes back down into the pinned place.Step 5:
- At speed too, each pinned row is pushed up and replaced cleanly: it never covers the column headings or the Totals row at the bottom.
- Two group rows never overlap.Step 6:
- The same going back up: each pinned row is replaced cleanly, never covers the column headings or the Totals row.
- Two group rows never overlap.
Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
From: PRD "A/R and A/P Report Sorting, Export and Location Filter", Confluence 947650561, version 11 (5 Nov 2026, Chris Ward), read 10 Nov 2026 · Epic SV-11315, story SV-11534 (Story 4, Collapse all, Expand all and pinned group rows), story SV-11539 (Story 9, A/R Collection call list)
- S4-R6: “While the user scrolls through an expanded group, that group's row (the bucket label, customer or vendor) stays pinned just under the column headings. Only one group row is held at a time: when the next group's row reaches it, the next row pushes it up and off and takes its place. Scrolling back reverses this.”
- S9-R1: “On A/R Collection, each customer's group row shows, after the customer's name, "Phone: " and the customer's phone number as the customer card shows it.”
- S9-R2: “For users with the 'View and Manage AP/AR Data' switch, the group row also shows "Credit term: " and the customer's credit term as the customer card shows it ("COD" when none is set).”From: Tech plan "A/R and A/P Report Sorting, Export and Location Filter — Technical Implementation Plan" (2026-10-21, uploaded 22 Oct 2026), read 22 Oct 2026
- Tech plan §3 D21: “Pinned group row = scroll-driven overlay (ReportPinnedGroupRow + useReportPinnedGroup) showing the group row's chevron and full label — on A/R Collection including "Phone:" and "Credit term:" (S9-R1/R2) — but no money cells (the group's subtotals stay in its own row)”
- Tech plan §7 Manual testing checklist: “3. Fling-scroll a long expanded group: pinned row pushes up without overlapping header or totals.”Last checked against build v26.45.2-6a8b89c (QA site sv11315) on 11/10/2026.
