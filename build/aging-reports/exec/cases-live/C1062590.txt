C1062590 A credit dated on the as-of date moves from 1-30 to Current
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role).
- Have a customer with an unused credit memo, credit memo A, dated a day before the release, date A.
- Make sure that before the release was installed, you noted that A/R Detail with date A as the as-of date showed credit memo A in "1-30 days past due".
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide.
- After the release is installed, open A/R Detail (Reports, then "A/R Aging Detail" under ACCOUNTS RECEIVABLE in the left menu).
- Leave the report with date A as the as-of date and only that customer ticked in the Customer filter.
STEPS: - Expand every bucket and find credit memo A.
EXP: Expected Result
Step 1:
- With date A as the as-of date, credit memo A is in "Current", not in "1-30 days past due" as before the release: from this release a credit dated exactly on the as-of date is Current.
Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
From: PRD "A/R and A/P Report Sorting, Export and Location Filter", Confluence 947650561, version 11 (5 Nov 2026, Chris Ward), read 10 Nov 2026 · Epic SV-11315, story SV-11531 (Story 1, Reports open in QuickBooks name order)
- What changes for existing users: “A credit memo, deposit or vendor credit dated exactly on the as-of date now shows in Current with Past Due 0, as in QuickBooks; today it shows in 1-30 (S1-R5a).”
- S1-R5a: “A document is Current when its due date is on or after the as-of date. Its Past Due, where the report shows one, is the number of days from the due date to the as-of date: negative while the document is not yet due (a bill due 9 days after the as-of date reads -9, as today) and 0 on its due date. A credit memo, deposit or vendor credit counts its own date as its due date, so one dated exactly on the as-of date is in Current with Past Due 0, not in "1-30 days past due". This is how QuickBooks ages them (SV-11441).”Last checked against build v26.45.2-6a8b89c (QA site sv11315) on 11/10/2026.
