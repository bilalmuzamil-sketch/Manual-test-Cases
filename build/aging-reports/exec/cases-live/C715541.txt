C715541 Design: A/R Collection with groups closed matches the pictures in both themes
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role).
- Have open documents in this report at two locations, so the Location chip shows.
- Make sure your role has the "View and Manage AP/AR Data" switch on, so the credit term shows.
- Make sure that at least ten customers have overdue invoices on today's date, each with a phone number and a credit term.
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide, in light theme at 100% zoom.
- Open A/R Collection (Reports, then "A/R Aging Collection" under ACCOUNTS RECEIVABLE in the left menu).
- Leave the report with every group collapsed.
- Open the design pictures "Info icon beside the title, light" (ar-ap-p6-info-icon-beside-the-title-light.png) and "Info icon beside the title, dark" (ar-ap-p6-info-icon-beside-the-title-dark.png) (attached to this case) beside the browser at 100% zoom; compare the layout, labels and styles, not the names and amounts, which are only examples.Setup
- Make customer 1: Click Customers in the top bar, then New Customer. Type a new Name for customer 1, type a Phone, then click Save. On the customer's page click the edit icon beside the name; in Edit Customer pick Credit Terms "Net 30", pick Taxes "Exempt (0%)" and type 0 in Default Shop Supplies (so totals stay exact), then click Save. Click Add Contact, type a First name and Last name, then click Save. On the Assets tab click New Asset, pick the Contact, fill Make and the other boxes the window marks as required, then click Save.
- Make invoice for customer 1: On customer 1's page click New Work Order, pick the Asset, then click Save (click Ok if a Customer Notes window opens). In the New Line window type a Title, pick Labor Rate "Fixed Line Total" (type "Fixed" in the box to find it), type 100.00 in Fixed Line Total, tick Line Approved, then click Save & Close. On the line click Complete; in Tech stories type a story and click Complete All Lines (if Missing Details asks for Mileage, type it and click Complete All Lines again). Open the Finance tab and click Mark Reviewed (if a window asks for the VIN, type one and click Confirm Review). Type the date 45 days before today in Invoice Date, then click Create Invoice. When the New Customer Payment window opens, reload the page (F5) so the invoice stays unpaid. Do not close that window with its ×: that takes the new invoice away again.
- Make customers 2 to 10: Same way as customer 1 and its invoice, nine more times (a new Name, a Phone and Credit Terms "Net 30" each), so ten customers have an overdue invoice.
