C715448 A/R Collection: without AP/AR Data access there is no credit term
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as a user whose custom role has "Reports" in the Page Access card and the "View and Manage AP/AR Data" switch off.
- Have a customer, customer A, with an overdue invoice, a phone number and a credit term.
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide.
- Open A/R Collection (Reports, then "A/R Aging Collection" under ACCOUNTS RECEIVABLE in the left menu).
- Leave the report with only customer A ticked.Setup
- Make customer A: Click Customers in the top bar, then New Customer. Type a new Name for customer A, type a Phone, then click Save. On the customer's page click the edit icon beside the name; in Edit Customer pick Credit Terms "Net 30", pick Taxes "Exempt (0%)" and type 0 in Default Shop Supplies (so totals stay exact), then click Save. Click Add Contact, type a First name and Last name, then click Save. On the Assets tab click New Asset, pick the Contact, fill Make and the other boxes the window marks as required, then click Save.
- Make invoice 1: On customer A's page click New Work Order, pick the Asset, then click Save (click Ok if a Customer Notes window opens). In the New Line window type a Title, pick Labor Rate "Fixed Line Total" (type "Fixed" in the box to find it), type 100.00 in Fixed Line Total, tick Line Approved, then click Save & Close. On the line click Complete; in Tech stories type a story and click Complete All Lines (if Missing Details asks for Mileage, type it and click Complete All Lines again). Open the Finance tab and click Mark Reviewed (if a window asks for the VIN, type one and click Confirm Review). Type the date 45 days before today in Invoice Date, then click Create Invoice. When the New Customer Payment window opens, reload the page (F5) so the invoice stays unpaid. Do not close that window with its ×: that takes the new invoice away again.
- Make the role for this test: Click the location name and your initials at the top right of the top bar, then Settings, then Roles & Permissions in the left menu, then Create Custom Role. In "Choose a template" click Skip (every switch then starts off). Type a Role Name, then switch on "Reports" in the Page Access card and leave "View and Manage AP/AR Data" off, then click Create.
- Sign in with that role: Click the location name and your initials at the top right of the top bar, then Settings, then Staff in the left menu. On the row "Tech ShopView" click the edit icon; in Edit Staff Member pick the new role in Role and click Save & Close. Then click the location name and your initials at the top right of the top bar, then Logout, and on the QA site's sign-in page click Tech in the quick login panel. After the test, sign in as Admin again and set Tech ShopView's Role back to Technician.
STEPS: - Read customer A's group row.
- Choose "Download (PDF)" from the ⋯ menu.
- Open the PDF and read customer A's line.
- Choose "Download (CSV)" from the ⋯ menu.
- Open the CSV and read its heading row.

Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
From: PRD "A/R and A/P Report Sorting, Export and Location Filter", Confluence 947650561, version 11 (5 Nov 2026, Chris Ward), read 10 Nov 2026 · Epic SV-11315, story SV-11533 (Story 3, Export to PDF and CSV), story SV-11539 (Story 9, A/R Collection call list)
- S9-N2: “A user without 'View and Manage AP/AR Data' sees no credit term on screen, none in the PDF, and no Credit Term column in the CSV.”
- S3-R8a: “On A/R Collection, every CSV row also carries the customer's "Phone" and, for users with 'View and Manage AP/AR Data', "Credit Term", in columns right after "Customer"; the PDF prints them on each customer's line (Story 9).”
- Key Decisions, Permissions: “Permissions. Viewing all six reports, and exporting them, is governed by 'Reports → Page Access', unchanged. No new permission is introduced and no existing permission changes. Opening a document from a report uses the permission that already opens that document (see Story 7). The credit term on the A/R Collection call list follows the customer card's rule: only users with the 'View and Manage AP/AR Data' switch see it, on screen, in the PDF and in the CSV (Story 9). Amounts show to everyone who can open the reports, as today and as on Sales By Customer and Inventory Value; 'See Financial Data' does not hide them.”Partly checked against build v26.45.2-6a8b89c (QA site sv11315) on 11/10/2026; not yet seen on that build: signing in as Tech ShopView after giving it another role.
