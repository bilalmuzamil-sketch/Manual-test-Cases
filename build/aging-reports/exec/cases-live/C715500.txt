C715500 Design: A/R Detail loading bar and empty state match Sales By Customer
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role).
- Have open documents in these reports at two locations, location A and location B, with location A's name first in A to Z order.
- Have a customer, customer A, with open invoices only at location B.
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide.
- Open A/R Detail (Reports, then "A/R Aging Detail" under ACCOUNTS RECEIVABLE in the left menu).
- Open Sales By Customer (Reports, then "Sales By Customer" under PERFORMANCE in the left menu) in a second tab of the same window, for comparison.
STEPS: - Change the as-of date to yesterday and watch the table straight away.
- Change the as-of date back to today.
- In the Location menu, untick location B.
- In the Customer filter, tick customer A.
- Read the empty state, letter for letter, and compare it with Sales By Customer's empty state.
- Click "Clear all filters".
EXP: Expected Result
Step 1:
- A progress bar shows on the table while the report loads, the same loading bar Sales By Customer shows.Step 2:
- The report shows today again.Step 3:
- The report narrows to location A.Step 4:
- The report has no rows: customer A has nothing open at location A.Step 5:
- "No record matches these filters" and "Try changing filters to widen your results.", with a "Clear all filters" button, laid out the same as Sales By Customer's empty state when the two tabs are compared.
- No Totals row.Step 6:
- The filters return to their defaults and rows show.
