C715507 Design: A/P Detail chevron button, tooltips and pinned group row
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role).
- Make sure the report has open documents in several buckets on today's date, one with more lines than fit on the screen.
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide, in light theme at 100% zoom.
- Open A/P Detail (Reports, then "A/P Aging Detail" under ACCOUNTS PAYABLE in the left menu).
- Leave the report with every group collapsed.
STEPS: - Look at the table's top-left header cell.
- Look at each bucket row's own chevron.
- Hover over the top-left chevron button.
- Click it.
- Hover over it again.
- Scroll down through the tallest bucket.
- Switch ShopView to dark theme.
- Hover over the top-left chevron button.
EXP: Expected Result
Step 1:
- One chevron button, the design system's chevron icon, in the top-left header cell, pointing right (every group collapsed).Step 2:
- Every bucket row shows the same chevron, pointing right when closed.Step 3:
- A tooltip above the button reads "Expand all", letter for letter.Step 4:
- Every bucket opens and its chevron points down.Step 5:
- The tooltip above the button reads "Collapse all".Step 6:
- The bucket's row stays pinned just under the column headings, with its label visible at the left.Step 7:
- The page switches to dark theme.
- The pinned row is readable.Step 8:
- The tooltip sits above the button and is readable in dark theme.
