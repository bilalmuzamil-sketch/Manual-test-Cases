C715445 A/R Collection: each customer row shows phone and credit term
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role).
- Make sure your role has the "View and Manage AP/AR Data" switch on, so the credit term shows.
- Have a customer, customer A, with an overdue invoice, a phone number and a credit term on its customer card.
- Have a customer, customer B, with an overdue invoice and no credit term.
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide.
- Open A/R Collection (Reports, then "A/R Aging Collection" under ACCOUNTS RECEIVABLE in the left menu).
STEPS: - Read customer A's group row.
- Open customer A's customer card in another tab and compare the phone and credit term.
- Switch back to the report's tab.
EXP: Expected Result
Step 1:
- After the name: "Phone: " and the phone number, then "Credit term: " and the credit term.Step 2:
- Both read exactly as the customer card shows them.Step 3:
- Customer B's row reads "Credit term: COD" (none is set).
- Neither row shows an email address or a primary contact.
