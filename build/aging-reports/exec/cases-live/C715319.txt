C715319 A/P Summary: the sort stays while changing date, scrolling and filters
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role).
- Have open documents in these reports at two locations, location A and location B, with location A's name first in A to Z order.
- Make sure the report has more than 30 vendors with open documents on today's date and on yesterday's date, at both locations.
- Have a vendor, vendor A, with open documents at both locations.
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide.
- Open A/P Summary (Reports, then "A/P Aging Summary" under ACCOUNTS PAYABLE in the left menu).Setup
- Locations to use: Use "Staging Heavy Duty - 9919" as location A (it comes first in A to Z order) and "Staging Lethbridge - 4310" as location B. To work at location B, click the location name and your initials at the top right of the top bar, then Change Location, then "Staging Lethbridge - 4310"; switch back to "Staging Heavy Duty - 9919" the same way when you are done.
- Make vendor A: Click Parts in the top bar, then Vendors in the left menu, then New Vendor. Type a new Name for vendor A, pick Credit Terms "Net 30" and Taxes "Exempt (0%)", then click Save & Close.
- Make bill 1: Click Parts in the top bar, then Purchase Orders in the left menu, then New PO. Pick vendor A in Vendor. In Part Number type a new part number and click "Add new special order part: …"; type a Description, 1 in Quantity and 100.00 in Cost; click Add, then Save & Close. In the Purchase Orders list click Receive on that order (Vendor column: vendor A); type a Vendor Invoice # (for example bill 1), type today's date in Invoice Date, type 0 in Tax, then click Receive Parts (1).
- Make bill 2: While switched to location B (see the Locations to use item): Same way as bill 1, with vendor A in Vendor: Cost 200.00; type today's date in Invoice Date.
STEPS: - Click the "Total" header once.
- Change the as-of date to yesterday.
- Scroll down until the next 30 vendors load.
- Open the Vendor filter.
- Tick vendor A.
- In the Location filter, untick location B.
EXP: Expected Result
Step 1:
- The rows sort by Total, largest first.
- "Total" is marked as the sorted column (a small arrow beside its heading).Step 2:
- The report reloads for yesterday and is still sorted by Total, largest first.Step 3:
- The new rows are still sorted by Total, largest first.Step 4:
- The Vendor menu opens.Step 5:
- Only vendor A's rows remain, still sorted by Total, largest first.Step 6:
- Only location A rows remain, still sorted by Total, largest first.
- "Total" is still marked as the sorted column (a small arrow beside its heading).
Exact quotes from the source (for reproducibility)
From: PRD "A/R and A/P Report Sorting, Export and Location Filter", Confluence 947650561, version 11 (5 Nov 2026, Chris Ward), read 10 Nov 2026 · Epic SV-11315, story SV-11531 (Story 1, Reports open in QuickBooks name order), story SV-11532 (Story 2, Sort by clicking any column header)
- S2-R7: “The sort is kept while the user stays on the report: changing the as-of date, loading more rows by scrolling, and changing the customer, vendor, Past due or Location filter keep it, except as in S2-R7a.”
- S2-R2: “The first click gives the useful order for the kind of data: text (names, Location, Transaction Type) A to Z; money columns largest first; dates oldest first; Past Due largest first; ID and No. smallest number first (S2-R4). The second click reverses it. The third click repeats the first; there is no unsorted state.”
- S1-R8: “A/R Summary, A/P Summary, A/R Collection and A/P Unpaid Invoices show 30 customers or vendors at a time, and the next 30 load as the user scrolls down, as today. There are no page buttons. A/R Detail and A/P Detail show each bucket's first 100 lines, then load more as the user scrolls (S4-R4). Paging never repeats or skips a customer, vendor or row, in any sort. On A/R Collection and A/P Unpaid Invoices, an open customer or vendor shows its first 100 documents, then the next 100 as the user scrolls; its document count, its subtotals, the Totals row and the exports always cover all of its documents.”Partly checked against build v26.45.2-6a8b89c (QA site sv11315) on 11/10/2026; not yet seen on that build: more than 30 vendors with open documents at both locations on today's and yesterday's date (not counted on this build).
