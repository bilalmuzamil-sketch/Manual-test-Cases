C715517 Design: export Success and Empty export messages match Sales By Customer
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role).
- Have open documents in these reports at two locations, so the Location filter shows and can be cleared.
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide, in light theme at 100% zoom.
- Open A/R Summary (Reports, then "A/R Aging Summary" under ACCOUNTS RECEIVABLE in the left menu).
- Open Sales By Customer (Reports, then "Sales By Customer" under PERFORMANCE in the left menu) in a second tab of the same window, for comparison.
STEPS: - Download the CSV from the ⋯ menu and read the message, letter for letter.
- Compare it with the message Sales By Customer shows after a CSV download.
- In the Location menu, click "Clear selection".
- Download the PDF from the ⋯ menu and read the message.
- Compare it with Sales By Customer's warning for an empty export.
EXP: Expected Result
Step 1:
- "Success", "Data exported successfully."Step 2:
- The same success message, look and position.Step 3:
- No location is ticked.Step 4:
- "Empty export", "Export didn't yield any results", nothing downloads.Step 5:
- The warning looks the same and appears in the same place as Sales By Customer's empty-export warning.
