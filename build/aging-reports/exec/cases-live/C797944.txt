C797944 A/R Detail: the × on the switch prompt leaves the report as it was
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role), with location A as your current location.
- Have open documents in these reports at two locations, location A and your other location.
- Make sure you are enrolled at both locations.
- Have a customer, customer A, with open invoices at location A and one open work order invoice, invoice A, made at your other location.
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide.
- Open A/R Detail (Reports, then "A/R Aging Detail" under ACCOUNTS RECEIVABLE in the left menu).
- Leave the report with customer A ticked, the "Amount" header clicked once, every bucket expanded and the page scrolled so invoice A is in the middle of the screen.
- Make sure you have noted which rows are at the top and bottom of the screen.
STEPS: - Copy the page address.
- Click invoice A's number.
- Click the "×" at the dialog's top right.
- Compare the page address with the copied one.
EXP: Expected Result
Step 1:
- The address is copied.Step 2:
- The "Switch location?" dialog opens, with a close "×" at its top right.Step 3:
- The dialog closes.
- No message pops up.
- You stay on the report exactly where you were: customer A still ticked, the Amount sort, every bucket still expanded and the same rows at the top and bottom of the screen (the same scroll position).
- The location switcher in the top right still shows location A.Step 4:
- The address is the same.
