C715473 A/R PDF prints the as-of date you picked, late in the day too
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role), with location A as your current location.
- Make sure the report has open invoices on today's date, so the PDF has rows.
- Run this test in the evening at location A's local time, when the date in UTC is already tomorrow.
- Set this computer's clock to location A's time zone.
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide.
- Open A/R Detail (Reports, then "A/R Aging Detail" under ACCOUNTS RECEIVABLE in the left menu).Setup
- Locations to use: Use "Staging Heavy Duty - 9919" as location A (it comes first in A to Z order) and "Staging Lethbridge - 4310" as location B. To work at location B, click the location name and your initials at the top right of the top bar, then Change Location, then "Staging Lethbridge - 4310"; switch back to "Staging Heavy Duty - 9919" the same way when you are done.
STEPS: - Check that the date chip shows today's date.
- Choose "Download (PDF)" from the ⋯ menu.
- Open the PDF and read its as-of line and its row order.
EXP: Expected Result
Step 1:
- "As of: " and today's date at location A.Step 2:
- The PDF downloads (A/R has an Export menu, no Print button).Step 3:
- The as-of line reads "As of " and that same date: three-letter month, day, four-digit year (for example "As of Oct 15, 2026"), not tomorrow's date. On a day before the 10th, judge only which date it is: whether the day carries a leading zero is waiting on the product owner.
- The PDF prints the on-screen order.
The date is the location's own date: the tech plan says the as-of line uses the location's time zone, which is what fixes today's next-day date.

- S3-R3: “The PDF and the CSV contain exactly the rows on screen for the chosen as-of date, customer or vendor and locations, in the on-screen sort, including a sort changed after an earlier export, without refreshing the page.”From: Tech plan "A/R and A/P Report Sorting, Export and Location Filter — Technical Implementation Plan" (2026-10-21, uploaded 22 Oct 2026), read 22 Oct 2026
- Tech plan §3 D9: “As-of label via the existing AsOfAnchorResolver (api/src/Reporting/Shared/Application/AsOf/), wrapped by a thin AgingAsOfResolver that supplies the workplace timezone and produces both formats: PDF "As of Oct 15, 2026", CSV "As of: Oct 15, 2026" (M j, Y)”
- Tech plan §3 D9, rationale: “Fixes the A/R PDF printing the next day's date (S3-R4).”Last checked against build v26.45.2-6a8b89c (QA site sv11315) on 11/10/2026.
