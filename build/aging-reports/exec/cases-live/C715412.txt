C715412 A/R Summary: a row shows one location's name or Multiple
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role).
- Make sure the organization has open documents at three locations, location A, location B and location C.
- Have a customer, customer A, with open invoices only at location A.
- Have a customer, customer B, with open invoices at location A and location B.
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide.
- Open A/R Summary (Reports, then "A/R Aging Summary" under ACCOUNTS RECEIVABLE in the left menu).Setup
- Make location C: Click the location name and your initials at the top right of the top bar, then Settings, then Locations in the left menu, then New Location. Type a Name, fill the boxes the window marks as required, then click Save & Close. To make documents there, switch to it: click the location name and your initials at the top right of the top bar, then Change Location, then the new location.
- Locations to use: Use "Staging Heavy Duty - 9919" as location A (it comes first in A to Z order) and "Staging Lethbridge - 4310" as location B. To work at location B, click the location name and your initials at the top right of the top bar, then Change Location, then "Staging Lethbridge - 4310"; switch back to "Staging Heavy Duty - 9919" the same way when you are done.
- Make customer A: Click Customers in the top bar, then New Customer. Type a new Name for customer A, type a Phone, then click Save. On the customer's page click the edit icon beside the name; in Edit Customer pick Credit Terms "Net 30", pick Taxes "Exempt (0%)" and type 0 in Default Shop Supplies (so totals stay exact), then click Save. Click Add Contact, type a First name and Last name, then click Save. On the Assets tab click New Asset, pick the Contact, fill Make and the other boxes the window marks as required, then click Save.
- Make invoice 1: On customer A's page click New Work Order, pick the Asset, then click Save (click Ok if a Customer Notes window opens). In the New Line window type a Title, pick Labor Rate "Fixed Line Total" (type "Fixed" in the box to find it), type 100.00 in Fixed Line Total, tick Line Approved, then click Save & Close. On the line click Complete; in Tech stories type a story and click Complete All Lines (if Missing Details asks for Mileage, type it and click Complete All Lines again). Open the Finance tab and click Mark Reviewed (if a window asks for the VIN, type one and click Confirm Review). Type today's date in Invoice Date, then click Create Invoice. When the New Customer Payment window opens, reload the page (F5) so the invoice stays unpaid. Do not close that window with its ×: that takes the new invoice away again.
- Make invoice 2: While switched to location B (see the Locations to use item): Same way as invoice 1, on customer A's page: Fixed Line Total 200.00; type today's date in Invoice Date; then in the New Customer Payment window reload the page (F5) so the invoice stays unpaid. Do not close that window with its ×: that takes the new invoice away again.
- Make invoice 3: While switched to location C (see the Make location C item): Same way as invoice 1, on customer A's page: Fixed Line Total 300.00; type today's date in Invoice Date; then in the New Customer Payment window reload the page (F5) so the invoice stays unpaid. Do not close that window with its ×: that takes the new invoice away again.
- Make invoice 1 for customer A: Same way as invoice 1, on customer A's page: Fixed Line Total 100.00; type today's date in Invoice Date; then in the New Customer Payment window reload the page (F5) so the invoice stays unpaid. Do not close that window with its ×: that takes the new invoice away again.
- Make customer B: Click Customers in the top bar, then New Customer. Type a new Name for customer B, type a Phone, then click Save. On the customer's page click the edit icon beside the name; in Edit Customer pick Credit Terms "Net 30", pick Taxes "Exempt (0%)" and type 0 in Default Shop Supplies (so totals stay exact), then click Save. Click Add Contact, type a First name and Last name, then click Save. On the Assets tab click New Asset, pick the Contact, fill Make and the other boxes the window marks as required, then click Save.
- Make invoice 1 for customer B: Same way as invoice 1, on customer B's page: Fixed Line Total 100.00; type today's date in Invoice Date; then in the New Customer Payment window reload the page (F5) so the invoice stays unpaid. Do not close that window with its ×: that takes the new invoice away again.
- Make invoice 2 for customer B: While switched to location B (see the Locations to use item): Same way as invoice 1, on customer B's page: Fixed Line Total 200.00; type today's date in Invoice Date; then in the New Customer Payment window reload the page (F5) so the invoice stays unpaid. Do not close that window with its ×: that takes the new invoice away again.
STEPS: - Read the Location cell of customer A and customer B.
- Untick location B in the Location menu (leaving location A and location C).
EXP: Expected Result
Step 1:
- Customer A shows location A's name.
- Customer B shows "Multiple".Step 2:
- The Location column stays (two locations ticked), and customer B's Location cell now shows location A's name: only its documents at the chosen locations count.
- They are all at location A.
