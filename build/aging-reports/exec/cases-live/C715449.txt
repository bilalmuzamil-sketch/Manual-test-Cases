C715449 A/R Collection: a tap on the info icon shows the description
PRE: Preconditions
- Sign in as an Admin user (the "Admin" role).
- Use a touch-screen device whose screen is at least 1024 px wide, with no mouse.
- On that device, use a desktop browser window 1440 px wide.
- Open A/R Collection (Reports, then "A/R Aging Collection" under ACCOUNTS RECEIVABLE in the left menu).
STEPS: - Tap the info icon right of the title.
- Tap an empty part of the page.
EXP: Expected Result
Step 1:
- The description tooltip shows: "Overdue invoices, unused credit memos and deposits for each customer to help you follow up on payments. Excludes invoices that aren’t due yet."Step 2:
- The tooltip closes.
