*OVERALL QA STATUS: PASSED (on production)*
{panel}
I tested this on production *app.shopview.com* (build *v26.40.13-013e543*) on *8 October 2026*, with the new invoice layout turned on.

Fees and discounts now print under their own names on estimates, invoices, part sale invoices and the customer portal. They print in the fixed order Chris asked for. The Legacy layout looks exactly as it did before.

h3. Before and after
!prod-01-before-vs-after-hd.png|width=512,height=1096!
_Same fees and discounts, estimate, new layout. Before (production, 7 Oct) every labor fee was lumped into "Labor $70.00" and the parts fee and discount were netted into "Parts $1.00". Now (production, 8 Oct) each one prints by name._
