Summary (if filed): Dashboard - the tile date buttons show blue text; the PRD asks for near-black

Epic: SV-11839 (Dashboard).

What we see

In the light theme, every tile's date button ("This Month", "Last 12 Months") and the At Risk "120 Days" button show blue text on a light-blue pill. The measured text colour is rgb(56, 116, 255). The tile labels next to them ("Revenue", "Billing Efficiency") are near-black, rgb(49, 55, 74). The supporting lines are dark grey.

What the document says

Dashboard PRD (Confluence "Dashboard", v53), S12-R2: "The descriptive text on the tiles — tile labels, supporting lines, and the date-range and inactivity-window controls — is shown in a near-black color in the light theme." Its context note: "the descriptive text was darkened from muted grey to a near-black (light) and near-white (dark) so the words read clearly on both themes."

The story SV-9584 (Dashboard v1 - Story 12, Done) says the same: "Tile labels, supporting lines, and the date-range and inactivity-window controls are near-black in light theme". Its design link ("Dashboard v1 — Design Directions", dated 28 Sep 2026) says: "Each card has its own date filter (the blue pill)", but not what colour the pill's text is.

Why this is a question and not a defect: the blue pill looks like deliberate styling, shared with the report filter buttons ("Date: Last quarter" is a blue pill on the reports too). The design calls the control a "blue pill" without giving the text colour, and SV-9584 passed QA with the blue text. The PRD wording covers the date controls, but the context note is about muted grey, not blue. We do not choose a side; Chris decides.
