Summary (if filed): Dashboard - at 1024 px a long custom date on a tile's date button sticks out of the tile

Epic: SV-11839 (Dashboard). Related: SV-11255 (the same problem on small screens, fixed).

What we see

At a screen 1024 pixels wide, the narrowest size the PRD calls desktop (S12-R1), a tile set to a custom range shows the full dates on its button, for example "Nov 3, 2026 - Nov 20, 2026". The button is then wider than the tile. Its right end and the arrow cross the tile's edge into the gap next to Billing Efficiency.

  • At 1280 px and wider it fits.
  • On a phone (390 px) it fits, and the page does not scroll sideways (S12-R13 PASS).
  • Light and dark themes are the same.

What the document says

S12-R13: "On a small screen the page never scrolls sideways, whatever date range a tile shows. A long date label fits within its tile (SV-11255)." That rule is written for small screens. The PRD has no rule for a long label on a desktop-width screen, so this is a question, not a defect.
