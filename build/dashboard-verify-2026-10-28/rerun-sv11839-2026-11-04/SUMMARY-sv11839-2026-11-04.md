## Observations (not tickets; for the QA lead to decide)
- **O1 — percentages of 1,000 % or more show no comma** ("10000.00%"), while money shows commas ("$1,234.56").
  - The PRD sets two decimals only.
  - Seen only where a tile is extreme: Test Location, 2.00 invoiced hours / 0.02 clocked.
  - A PO question at most.
- **O2 — a remembered technician filter can show a technician the user never chose.** On Technician Utilization (Heavy Duty, Last Quarter) we unticked every technician (chart and table hidden, S8-N1). After a reload the tile opens on This Month and reads "Technician: Tech ShopView", with one row.
  - **Why:** the app stores the technicians you UNticked (`useCardEntityFilter.ts`: *"a technician not explicitly deselected is selected, so one that first appears in a later window shows up"*, following the reports' S5-R9/R10). Tech ShopView was not in the Last Quarter list.
  - **PRD S8-R6:** *"A tile's chart filter selection is remembered for that tile between visits."* It is silent on technicians who were not in the list when the choice was made.
  - **Rule 58:** a PO question, not a defect. The same encoding explains staging's Technician Efficiency reload reading "All technicians" when the list is empty.
