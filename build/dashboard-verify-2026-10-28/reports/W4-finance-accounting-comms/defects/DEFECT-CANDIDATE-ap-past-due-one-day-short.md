## A9 — THE SIX REFUSALS, ARGUED AND ANSWERED
1. "This is unbuilt / not finished yet." → SV-5654 / SV-5655 / SV-5773 / SV-5774 are Done; the reports are in the live menu.
2. "The spec changed — this is obsolete." → "AP reports" v13 is the current version; the newer Sorting PRD (v9) does not redefine Past Due.
3. "Works as designed." → The PRD formula is explicit and the build follows it for past-due bills; only bills not yet due are off. A bill due tomorrow showing 0 — the same as a bill due today — makes the two indistinguishable, which the PRD's "negative or positive value will be shown" rules out. The muddled sign sentence in Detail §6 ("if due date is before selected date, that value is negative…") does not affect the count: whichever sign is used, the size is one day short.
4. "Cannot reproduce." → Reproduced through the screen at two locations in two time zones, plus 3,919 of 3,961 not-yet-due bill rows across five as-of dates in the report's own data. The data is permanent: picking a past as-of date reproduces it any time.
5. "Duplicate." → Two JQL searches (A4); the A/R equivalent (SV-5605) is a different report and is fixed.
6. "Environment / data / your account." → Two time zones, two locations, quick-login Admin, every vendor; balances agree with the vendor pages, so the data is sound.
