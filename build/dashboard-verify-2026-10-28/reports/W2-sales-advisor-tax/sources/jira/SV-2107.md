**If you need help on the calculations, here they are: (Keep in mind, the calculations for the total row that are PERCENTAGES, are NOT an average of all the rows above it. The calculation needs to be done again with the cells from the total row.)**

**Calculations for advanced totals:**

* **Labor Efficiency Row= (Total hours invoiced/Total hours clocked)\*100 (PERCENT)** 
* **Labor Efficiency TOTAL = SUM(Total hours invoiced for the entire row)/SUM(total hours clocked for all work order) \*100 (PERCENT)** 
* \*Labor Margin Row= (Labor sell price - labour cost)/labor sell price \* 100 (Percent)\*
* **Labor Margin TOTAL= (SUM(Total labor sell price)-SUM(Total labor cost))/SUM(Total labor sell price)\*100 (PERCENT)**
* \*Parts Margin=  (Parts sell price - parts cost)/parts sell price \* 100 (Percent)\*
* **Parts Margin TOTAL =  (SUM(Total parts sell price)-SUM(Total parts cost))/SUM(Total parts sell price)\*100 (PERCENT)**
* \*Total Margin Row=(Total sell price - Total cost)/Total sell price \* 100 (Percent)\*
* **Total Margin TOTAL = (SUM(Total sell price)-SUM(Total cost))/SUM(Total sell price)\*100 (PERCENT)**
