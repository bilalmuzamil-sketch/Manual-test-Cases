# SV-10457 — Fees & Discounts to AccountingHub: taxable fees and discounts on the sales tax report
Status: Done · created 2026-09-24 · updated 2026-10-06 · parent SV-10452

## Summary

Taxable sales on the sales tax report include taxable fees and reflect taxable discounts, so the figure a shop copies onto its sales tax return is right.

## Context

* Fees and discounts arrive itemized with whether each is taxable and its tax amount associated with the invoice's combined tax setup (SV-10453).
* Today the tax on a taxable fee is spread over the job's labor and parts lines: tax collected is right, but taxable sales leave out taxable fees and discounts.

## Requirements

1. A taxable fee's amount counts in taxable sales, under the tax it was charged under. — (PRD: Story 7, R1)
2. A taxable discount reduces taxable sales, and its negative tax reduces tax collected, under the tax it was charged under. — (PRD: Story 7, R2)
3. Fee and discount tax is represented explicitly in the detailed rows. The invoice's final tax is authoritative: saved fee or discount tax must not cause detailed rows to disagree with that total. The edge-case reconciliation approach requires the engineering investigation below. — (PRD: Story 7, R3)
4. When a whole-job fee is split across classes, its tax is split in the same proportions. — (PRD: Story 7, R4)
5. AccountingHub's total tax matches the tax actually charged on the ShopView invoice, and all detailed tax rows for that invoice add up exactly to the same total. — (PRD: Story 7, R5)
6. The report's drill-down to individual invoices shows fee and discount lines with their tax. Add a Description column showing each invoice line's name (for example, Hazmat fee, Brake labor or Oil filter), on screen and in exports. AccountingHub supplies the description and Core's accounting report screen displays it. — (PRD: Story 7, R6)
7. Fees and discounts use the same combined tax setup and reporting treatment as labor and parts. A setup containing several rates (for example, state and county) does not introduce separate per-rate fee rows or require a per-rate fee breakdown. — (PRD: Story 7, R7)

## Engineering investigation before implementation

Reproduce invoices combining fees with the old API-only percentage discount and invoices where a taxable discount exceeds the taxable amount. Confirm what existing reconciliation already handles and propose the smallest safe way for detailed tax rows to match final invoice tax. Owner: engineering, Core and AccountingHub. The required outcome is decided; the calculation approach is not. Do not assume an additional tax-adjustment row or a change to ShopView invoice calculations. — (PRD: Section 7; Story 7, E2)

## Out of Scope

* Changing sales tax periods already filed or closed.
* Credit memo tax (restocking fees, carry-forward credits).

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Given a $1,000 taxable job plus a taxable $50 hazmat fee at 8%, when the invoice is booked, then taxable sales show $1,050 and tax collected shows $84.
- [ ] Given a $1,000 taxable job with a taxable −$100 discount at 8%, when the invoice is booked, then taxable sales show $900 and tax collected shows $72.
- [ ] Given a non-taxable fee, when the invoice is booked, then it carries no tax and does not count in taxable sales.
- [ ] Given a taxable whole-job fee split across two classes, then its tax is split in the same proportions as the fee.
- [ ] Given any invoice with fees or discounts, then its total tax matches ShopView's invoice exactly.
- [ ] Given a taxable fee under a setup containing state and county rates, when it is booked and reported, then it follows the same combined-tax treatment as labor and parts, without separate per-rate fee rows.
- [ ] Given a taxable discount under a setup containing several rates, then it follows the same combined-tax reporting treatment as labor and parts, and the invoice's detailed tax rows reconcile exactly to its final tax.
- [ ] Given a drill-down from the report into an invoice, then its labor, part, fee and discount rows display their line names in a Description column, and the same descriptions appear in report exports.
- [ ] Given either the old API-only percentage discount or a taxable discount exceeding the taxable amount, then AccountingHub's final tax matches the ShopView invoice and its detailed tax rows add up to that total; the engineering investigation establishes and validates the reconciliation approach before implementation.
- [ ] Given a sales tax period filed before release, then its figures do not change.
