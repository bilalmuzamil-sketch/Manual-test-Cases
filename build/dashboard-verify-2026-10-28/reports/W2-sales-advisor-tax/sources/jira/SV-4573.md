# SV-4573 — BE/FE Sales Tax report view
Status: Done · created 2025-09-26 · updated 2026-03-12 · parent SV-4233

**As a user**, I want to be able to **see a Sales Tax report for all invoices** (Paid, Partially Paid, and Unpaid) so that I can quickly determine how much tax I need to pay based on the status of invoices.

---

### Requirements

1. On the **Reports page**:

    * Create a new section called **Finance**.
    * **Sales Tax report** is visible in the Finance section.
    * permission TBD?????
    
2. When the Sales Tax report is selected, display a **table** by default with the following columns:

    * **Invoice Status** (, Partially Paid, Paid)
    * **Invoice Created Date** (Today, Yesterday, or specific date)
    * **Invoice Paid Date** (Today, Yesterday, or specific date); for partially paid invoices, show the date of the **latest payment**
    * **Invoice Number** (linked to the Finance tab)
    * **Customer**
    * **Labor Total** (without tax)
    * **Parts Total** (without tax)
    * **Shop Supply Total** (without tax)
    * **Invoice Subtotal** (without tax)
    * **Tax column**
    
        * **Custom tax columns** for every tax rate the user has created; 
        * 
        
    * **Invoice Total** (with tax)
    
3. The **last row** of the table should show the **total** for all tax column or to show separate component with total tax rate amounts for invoices 

    * Totals are calculated **on the backend**.
    * Pagination **must not affect** the total row.
    

---

### Acceptance Criteria

* Reports page contains a new **Finance** section.
* Sales Tax report is listed under Finance.
* Selecting Sales Tax report displays a table with the correct default columns: Invoice Status, Invoice Created Date, Invoice Paid Date, Invoice Number (linked), Customer, Labor Total, Parts Total, Shop Supply Total, Invoice Subtotal, Tax columns (Tax Rate Name & Tax Amount), Invoice Total.
* Invoice Paid Date shows the latest payment date for partially paid invoices.
* Custom tax columns are hidden by default if they contain no data for selected invoices.
* Last row shows total for all tax columns (calculated on the backend) and is **always visible**, unaffected by pagination.
* Invoice Number links correctly to the Finance tab.
