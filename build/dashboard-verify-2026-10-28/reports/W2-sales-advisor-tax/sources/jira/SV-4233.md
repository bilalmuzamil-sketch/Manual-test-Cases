# SV-4233 — Feature Request: Sales Tax Reporting
Status: Open · created 2025-08-14 · updated 2026-04-17 · parent None

Client: LA Diesel Works

Users: 3 users

Date requested: Aug 7, 2025

**Summary:**  
Add a built-in reporting function in ShopView to track and summarize sales tax–related data for compliance and filing purposes.

**Details:**  
We would like a new report that provides the following information for any selected date range:

* **Gross Sales** – total sales amount before deductions.
* **Taxable Sales** – portion of gross sales subject to sales tax.
* **Tax Collected** – total sales tax collected from customers.
* **Exempt Sales** – sales not subject to sales tax (e.g., to resellers with valid exemption certificates).

**Additional Requirements:**

* Ability to filter by location, date range, and possibly tax jurisdiction.
* Option to export results to CSV, Excel, and PDF.
* Totals should match what is stored in transaction history for audit purposes.
* Ideally, include breakdowns by tax rate/jurisdiction for states that require multi-jurisdiction filing.

**Purpose/Benefit:**  
This will help streamline sales tax filing in the U.S. by giving users the exact figures they need to complete their returns, reducing manual calculation errors and saving time during compliance periods.
