**Calculations for advanced totals:**

* **Labor Efficiency Row= (Total hours invoiced/Total hours clocked)\*100 (PERCENT)** 
* **Labor Efficiency TOTAL = SUM(Total hours invoiced for the entire column)/SUM(total hours clocked for all work order) \*100 (PERCENT)** 
* \*Parts Margin=  (Parts sell price - parts cost)/parts sell price \* 100 (Percent)\*
* **Parts Margin TOTAL =  (SUM(Total parts sell price)-SUM(Total parts cost))/SUM(Total parts sell price)\*100 (PERCENT)**
* \*Total Margin Row=(Total sell price - Total cost)/Total sell price \* 100 (Percent)\*
* **Total Margin TOTAL = (SUM(Total sell price)-SUM(Total cost))/SUM(Total sell price)\*100 (PERCENT)**
