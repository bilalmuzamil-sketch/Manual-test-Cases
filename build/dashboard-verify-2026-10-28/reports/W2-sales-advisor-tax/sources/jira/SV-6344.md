# SV-6344 — BUG: Sales Report Showing Incorrect Parts Cost and Margin Compared to Work Order Stats
Status: Done · created 2026-03-26 · updated 2026-10-13 · parent None

### **Account-Specific Issue**

**Customer:** Kimberly Christiansen  
**Company:** Foothills Group Inc  
**Users:** 57

---

### **Summary**

The **Sales Report** is displaying incorrect Parts Cost and Parts Margin values when compared to the actual data in the corresponding Work Order Stats. This discrepancy is impacting financial accuracy and reporting reliability.

---

### **Steps to Reproduce**

1. Navigate to **Reports > Sales**.
2. Locate invoice **S5-13857** or **S5-13937**.
3. Note the **Parts Cost** and **Parts Margin** values shown in the Sales Report.
4. Open the corresponding Work Order.
5. Go to the **Stats** tab and review the Parts Cost and Parts Margin values.
6. Compare the values between the report and the work order.

---

### **Expected Result**

The **Parts Cost** and **Parts Margin** displayed in the Sales Report should match the values shown in the Work Order Stats.

---

### **Actual Result**

The Sales Report displays incorrect and inconsistent values:

**Example 1 – Invoice S5-13857**

## **Additional Customer Findings (New Information)**

The customer has identified a consistent pattern related to **returned parts and cores** affecting the Parts Cost and Margin shown in the Sales Report.

### **Newly Identified Examples**

**Invoice: S5-14032**

* **Sales Report:**

    * Cost: $233.69
    * Margin: 59.98%
    
* **Work Order Stats:**

    * Cost: $357.51
    * Margin: 38.78%
    

Breakdown:

* 4 parts billed = total cost **$357.51**
* 1 returned part (Part #24334) = **$123.82**

Report calculation:  
$357.51 − $123.82 = **$233.69** (matches Sales Report)

The Sales Report is subtracting the cost of the returned part from the total billed parts cost.

---

**Invoice: S5-13843**

* **Sales Report:**

    * Cost: $187.81
    * Margin: 47.83%
    
* **Work Order Stats:**

    * Cost: $258.31
    * Margin: 28.25%
    

Breakdown:

* Returned part cost = **$70.50**
* $258.31 − $70.50 = **$187.81** (matches Sales Report)

---

**Invoice: S5-13774**

* **Sales Report:**

    * Cost: $530.82
    * Margin: 56.81%
    
* **Work Order Stats:**

    * Cost: $676.82
    * Margin: 44.93%
    

Breakdown:

* 2 returned cores at $73 each = **$146**
* $676.82 − $146 = **$530.82** (matches Sales Report)

---

## **Observed Pattern**

* The Sales Report is deducting returned part/core cost from the total cost of billed parts.
* The **Parts Invoiced dollar amount is correct**.
* Only **Parts Cost and Parts Margin** are affected.
* This recalculation results in artificially inflated margin percentages.
