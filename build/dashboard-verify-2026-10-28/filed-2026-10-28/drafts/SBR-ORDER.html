Description

In Sales By Representative, when a representative (or the Unassigned row) is opened, invoices that share a date are listed highest invoice number first. The written rule is that invoices on the same day are listed by invoice number, lowest first. Across different days the order is right (newest day first).

Impact: on a busy day the invoices under a representative appear in reverse number order, so a reader looking for one invoice by its number has to scan the whole day.

What is NOT affected: every figure on the rows and totals is correct; only the order within one day is wrong. This was first reported as SV-8974 (closed on 9 Aug 2026); it still happens on the current build.

Steps to Reproduce

  1. Sign in to staging as an Admin. In the top bar, choose the location DVI Test.
  2. Open Reports. In the left menu, under Performance, click Sales By Representative.
  3. Click Date: and choose This month (from 1 November 2026 onward choose Last month, so the range is October 2026).
  4. Click Show Unassigned.
  5. Click the arrow at the left of the Unassigned row to open it.
  6. Read the invoice numbers on the rows dated Oct 28 2026. They run S1-38214, S1-38212, S1-38211, S1-38206, S1-38204, S1-38203, S1-38200, S1-38199, S1-38198, S1-38196, S1-38195: highest number first.
  7. Read the two rows dated Oct 13 2026. S1-35010 is listed above S1-35003.

The invoices above are test data at DVI Test and stay in place. Any day with more than one invoice shows the same order, at every location.

Actual Result

  • Invoices on the same day are listed highest number first (S1-38214 … S1-38195; S1-35010 above S1-35003).

Expected Result

  • Invoices on the same day are listed by invoice number, lowest first: S1-38195, S1-38196, S1-38198, S1-38199, S1-38200, S1-38203, S1-38204, S1-38206, S1-38211, S1-38212, S1-38214; then on Oct 13, S1-35003 above S1-35010. Days stay newest first.

Source: Confluence Sales By Representative report specification, version 30 (21 Oct 2026), requirement S6-R9: "Within an expanded rep (and within the Unassigned row), invoice detail rows are ordered by invoice date descending (newest first), tie-broken by invoice number ascending by the numeric portion after the P/S prefix (numeric, not lexical — so S999 precedes S1000); if both the date and the numeric portion tie (e.g., P100 and S100 on the same day), P (Parts) sorts before S (Service)."

Technical details for developers

  • Rows come from GET /api/reporting/reports/sales-by-representative/{repKey}/invoices and appear to be ordered by the full invoice time stamp, newest first, instead of by date and then invoice number.
