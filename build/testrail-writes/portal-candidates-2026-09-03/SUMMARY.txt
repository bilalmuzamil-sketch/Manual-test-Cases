C18621  Partial payment can be initiated on an unpaid invoice via ShopPay
  section 2486: Test Cases > Administration > Customer Portal > SV-5957 - Support Multiple / Partial Payments > Multiple Payments – Different Methods > Partial Payments – Basic Flow
  created_by 3 = Bilal Muzamil <bilal.muzamil@shopview.com>
  atmstatus 1 = Not automated   automation_type 0 = None
  refs None   suite 1   updated_by 1
  AUTOMATION markers found: NONE

--- PRECONDITIONS ---
A customer invoice exists in ShopView with a balance > $0.50. The customer portal (ShopPay) is accessible.

--- STEPS ---
- Log in to the customer portal.

  - Navigate to the Invoices section.

  - Open an unpaid invoice.

  - Click 'Pay Now'.

  - Enter a partial amount (less than the full balance but ≥ $0.50).

  - Complete the payment.

--- EXPECTED ---
Payment is accepted. Invoice status changes to 'Partially Paid'. Remaining balance is updated correctly.

C45245  Portal-collected deposit cannot be reversed in ShopView
  section 3018: Test Cases > Work Orders > Deposits - Scroll down on the left side for the tests > D. Delete, Reverse, Split Deposits
  created_by 1 = Vladimir Tomovic <vladimir.tomovic@shopview.com>
  atmstatus 3 = AUTOMATED   automation_type 1 = E2E
  refs None   suite 1   updated_by 1
  AUTOMATION markers found: NONE

--- PRECONDITIONS ---
Organization has the ShopPay feature enabled and an active location.
A customer account with an open (estimate/approved/in_progress) work order.
A deposit on that work order collected through the Customer Portal (created via POST /api/external/customer-portal/deposits with a gateway_reference), status held.
A second, ordinary in-app deposit on the same customer, status held, with no gateway reference.
Logged in as an owner/admin with Invoicing Create & Edit.

--- STEPS ---
(empty)

--- EXPECTED ---
(empty)

--- STEPS (separated) ---
1. Open the customer profile and select the Deposits tab
   => Both deposits are listed; the portal-collected row shows payment method "Online".
2. Inspect the Reverse control on the portal-collected deposit row
   => The control is present but disabled.
3. Inspect the Reverse control on the ordinary in-app deposit row
   => The control is present and enabled.
4. Select the Payments tab and locate the portal-collected deposit's standalone row
   => Its remove/reverse control is disabled.
5. Expand the payment that applied the portal-collected deposit
   => The nested applied-deposit sub-row renders and its remove control is disabled.
