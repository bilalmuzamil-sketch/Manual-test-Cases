Canned lines per location
A service’s canned lines are set up once, at the location the schedule was built at. A work order at any other location adds the same work at its own rates, without prices or parts; each copied line notes the parts used at the original location. A schedule can then serve the whole organization.

The rules
1
A service’s canned lines are set up once, at the location the schedule was built at (Calgary South).
2
A work order at that location adds those lines, as today.
3
A work order at any other location adds the same work: names, descriptions and hours, at its own rates. Prices and parts are not copied.
4
Each copied line carries an internal line note with the parts used at the original location, for reference.
5
Nothing about copying appears in the Add Service modal; the card’s (i) explains it.
6
Tracking and reset stay organization wide: one due date per service, whichever location does the work.
