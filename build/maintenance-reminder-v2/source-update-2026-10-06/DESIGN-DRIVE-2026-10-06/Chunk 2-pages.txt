One email, every state
A unit can be upcoming, due today and past due at the same moment. Three emails would have an owner opening three messages about one truck.
Sent by hand
Sent only when someone presses Send reminder on the contact card. The Send email dialog opens with this wording, and the sender can change it before sending. Nothing is sent automatically.
It holds for any mix
One service or several, all past due or all upcoming: the opening line never needs a singular and a plural version.
The state sits on the row
Not in the heading, not in the subject. The table carries it.
No unsubscribe
Honouring one properly is consent management, which is its own feature. Consent lives on the customer record instead.
Soon, where a date would be a guess
A unit with no basis to estimate from reads Soon rather than a date the shop would be held to.
