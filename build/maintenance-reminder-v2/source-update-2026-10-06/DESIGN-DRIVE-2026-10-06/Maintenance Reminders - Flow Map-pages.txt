======================== PAGE sec-12 — Reading the gaps / Most of the 26 with no way in are alternate states, not missing work: S1b, S1d, S1e, S1f and S1c are  ========================
Reading the gaps
Most of the 26 with no way in are alternate states, not missing work: S1b, S1d, S1e, S1f and S1c are the workbench in another condition, C2, C3, D2 and D3 are the same form further along, and E, B, C are the program editor mid-build. They are drawn to be reviewed side by side and would never be reached by clicking.
Four are worth a decision. S2m, the program row menu, is reached only from a ⋮ no card links to. B4, the snoozed list, has no filter wired to it. S5b holds the enrollment edge states. A5, the missing-certificate state, is reached from neither the asset nor the enrollment dialog.
Not drawn at all: Settings › Canned Lines, the schedule board, and the customer record. This feature links into them; they are existing product screens and were not redesigned.
