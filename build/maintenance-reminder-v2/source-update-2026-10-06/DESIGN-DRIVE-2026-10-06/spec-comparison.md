Method: every text the drive EXPOSED (tooltips, hover cards, native title tooltips, designer notes) was matched
sentence-by-sentence against both specs (scripted shortlist, then read by hand); in addition the screens' visible
labels were checked for ~90 UI strings the spec states (button names, headings, empty states, messages, toasts).
"Designer note" = a hover on the artboard caption (the board's annotation, not part of the product screen).
The design gives screen names, navigation and what a tester sees; where it disagrees with the spec, **the spec wins**.
