**Reading this page.** 🧑 marks an item the design authority raised himself; other rows say who raised it and whether he took it up. A tier marked *proposed* was assigned by the review tool from what happened in the room (a decision taken for this release → must fix before release; something cut for this release → deferred; follow-up language → fast follow) and stands until the design authority confirms or changes it — the tool approves nothing. Open questions carry a decider. Every row links the review it came from and every later review that returned to it; meeting notes are visible to the PM team. Each review declares what it covered and what it deliberately did not, in the Reviews index and again on its row in the Change log — so silence about a part of the feature means no review has reached it, not that it passed.
