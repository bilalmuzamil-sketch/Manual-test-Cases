One email, every stateA unit can be upcoming, due today and past due at the same moment. Three emails would have an owner opening three messages about one truck.
The wording is fixedIdentical for every shop. Nothing here is editable, which is why there is no settings screen behind it.
It holds for any mixOne row or forty, all past due or all upcoming: the opening line never needs a singular and a plural version.
The state sits on the rowNot in the heading, not in the subject. The table carries it.
No unsubscribeHonouring one properly is consent management, which is its own feature. Consent lives on the customer record instead.
Soon, where a date would be a guessA unit with no basis to estimate from reads Soon rather than a date the shop would be held to.
