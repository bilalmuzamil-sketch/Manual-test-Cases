Edited where customers are editedA toggle on the customer info card, below IBS, with a tooltip saying it covers every asset the customer owns. The edit dialog carries the same field as a checkbox in its trailing row — specified in the PRD, not drawn. No settings card, no settings tab, no preferences page.
One field, two placesThe enrolment modal shows the current value and offers the change in place. Card, dialog and modal all write the same boolean on the customer.
On the customer, not the assetA fleet is one relationship. Consent given once covers every unit the customer owns, including units enrolled later.
Off does not stop trackingEvery due date, every status and every worklist row stays exactly as it was. Only the email stops.
Default onDecided 16 September: every customer starts on. A shop switches individual customers off on the record. There is no bulk action and no turn-everyone-on button.
Permission follows the customerEditing this field follows editing a customer. It is not financial data, so it does not sit behind the AP/AR visibility gate the way PO is required does.
No unsubscribe in the emailConsent is managed here, by the shop, on the record. That is why the email carries no unsubscribe link.
