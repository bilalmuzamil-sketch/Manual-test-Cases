K01Compliance switched on · empty
OPENFold compliance inspections into a routine service with a Term trigger? A compliance inspection carries four rules a trigger alone would not: it can never be snoozed, it absorbs nothing and is absorbed by nothing in the routine ladder, its certification date is required, and it holds certificate records with type-labelled numbers and optional files. Folding it in means carrying all four on the Term trigger.
