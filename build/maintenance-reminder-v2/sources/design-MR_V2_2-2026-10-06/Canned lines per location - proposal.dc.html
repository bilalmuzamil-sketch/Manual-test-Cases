SV-3780 · MAINTENANCE REMINDERS · PROPOSAL

Canned lines per location

A service’s canned lines are set up once, at the location the schedule was built at. A work order at any other location adds the same work at its own rates, without prices or parts; each copied line notes the parts used at the original location. A schedule can then serve the whole organization.Calgary South · Lethbridge · Highway Tractor PM · PM-A

NotesRules
NThe rules
1A service’s canned lines are set up once, at the location the schedule was built at (Calgary South).
2A work order at that location adds those lines, as today.
3A work order at any other location adds the same work: names, descriptions and hours, at its own rates. Prices and parts are not copied.
4Each copied line carries an internal line note with the parts used at the original location, for reference.
5Nothing about copying appears in the Add Service modal; the card’s (i) explains it.
6Tracking and reset stay organization wide: one due date per service, whichever location does the work.
