Part History already records what happened to a part and who did it. Counts, bin transfers, price and field edits, receipts from a purchase order. Every event is written as one pipe-delimited line in the Event column, with the staff member, the date and the time beside it.
There is no entry for retiring a part, because retiring a part is not possible. The nearest thing a shop can do is delete it, and a deleted part takes its history with it.
