Catalog Part is required, and it is the first thing on the screen. An inventory part cannot exist unless a matching catalog part already does, so adding one part off the shelf means creating two records in two places, in the right order. It is drawn in the required blue, which is the only field on the dialog that is.

There is no status anywhere on this dialog. A part cannot be retired from here, and nothing on the screen suggests retiring is possible at all.

Min and Max sit loose between Core Charge and Tags, with nothing saying what they belong to or when they matter. Every part has them, because every part is tracked.

The destructive action available here is Delete, which removes the part outright. There is no middle option between keeping a part in everyone's way and deleting it.
