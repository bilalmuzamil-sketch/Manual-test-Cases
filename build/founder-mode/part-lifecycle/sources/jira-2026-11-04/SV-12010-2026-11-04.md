Requested by Chris in [SV-10647 (comment 79703)](https://shopview.atlassian.net/browse/SV-10647?focusedCommentId=79703).

**Today:** changing the part number on a received vendor bill line to an inactive part's number is accepted. On a stock line the received quantity then moves onto the retired part. The stock line's part picker already hides inactive parts (S9-R6), but the system has no check of its own, and a special-order line accepts any typed number.

**Expected:**

* The save is refused with "{part number} is inactive. Activate it from the Inactive tab to use it.", as S9-R17 does for purchase order lines.
* Same match as S9-R17: upper and lower case, hyphens, asterisks, periods and spaces are ignored; a number an active part at this location also has is not refused.
* A line that already holds an inactive part can still be saved with its own number (S9-E2).

**Where:** `POST /api/inventory/deliveries/change-item`
