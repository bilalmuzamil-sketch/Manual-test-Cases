Everything the shop has ever created is offered. Two superseded housings nobody has stocked in years sit in the same list, in exactly the same styling, as the filters in use. Nothing marks them out: the shading on this drawing is not in the product. A service writer under time pressure picks one, and the mistake is found at invoicing, or not at all.

Nothing on a row says the part is dead. The second line carries the source or the inventory quantity on the left and the vendor on the right. Neither answers “should I be using this”, and a part with no stock looks exactly like a part that was retired on purpose.

The rest of the dialog, the Description, Quantity, Source, Category, Vendor, Cost, Core Charge, Sell Price and Margin fields below this one, and all three Save buttons, are not touched by this work.
