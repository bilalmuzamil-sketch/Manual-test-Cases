Why the narrative carries no label
Because the two invoice views disagree about the label and agree about everything else. Legacy prints the narrative as a plain paragraph under the description with no label at all. Modern prints it under a “Scope of work” heading. Both print the title and the description with no labels, both put the narrative last, and both drop it entirely when it is empty or reads “Tech story missing.” Printing no label is the only choice that matches what a customer sees in either view.

Whether a line has a narrative at all is a shop setting
Administration, Work Order Settings, Require Tech Story: “Tech story will be a required field before a line can be completed on a work order.” With it on, the completion flow stops on a gate and refuses an empty value, so every invoiced line carries one and the two rows above are what a bookkeeper sees. With it off, a line is completed and invoiced with nothing there, and the line has to end cleanly after the description.
