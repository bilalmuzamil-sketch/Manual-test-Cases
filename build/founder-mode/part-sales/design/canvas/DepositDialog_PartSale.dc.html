8a. Create Deposit on a part sale

The work order's dialog: deposit date, payment method, amount, reference number, memo. The memo pre-fills "Deposit for Part Sale P-196".

Button row as it ships
Record Deposit
Collect in Portal On hover: the Customer Portal's own reason, shown verbatim

Two buttons, as on a work order. Record Deposit writes the deposit, and it auto-applies when the sale is invoiced, including the overflow-to-credit case. Collect in Portal is offered once the Customer Portal says it can collect for this sale. While the portal declines, the button stays visible and disabled and hovering it shows the portal's own reason, verbatim (S8-R8 to S8-R11). The screenshot above was taken before that change and shows Record Deposit alone; the button row beneath it is what ships.
