## SV-11665 — Internal Work Orders
Type: Epic · Status: Open · Parent: None · Created: 2026-10-22T09:56:01.376-0500 · Updated: 2026-11-09T05:33:55.303-0500

**What**

Let a shop run work it will not bill: an organization-wide "No-Charge Work Orders" setting, an Internal customer flag on the shop's own customers, a No charge checkbox with a reason on a regular customer's work order, Close Work Order instead of an invoice (a third invoice state, Closed, with nothing owed), and the parts cost and shop supplies posted to mapped expense accounts in QuickBooks Online and AccountingHub. Money reports leave these work orders out; a new No-Charge Work Orders report lists them; the Open API exposes the classification and the closed status.

**Why**

Shops today invoice themselves or leave work orders open forever to avoid revenue on work nobody pays for, which inflates sales and hides the real cost of internal, warranty, policy and goodwill work (PowerTools theme #514, 122 insights across 77 companies).
