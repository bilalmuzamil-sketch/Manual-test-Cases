Which customers are your own company?
Work orders for these customers are internal: no prices and no invoice; parts cost posts to your Internal Work Orders Expense account when you use QuickBooks or AccountingHub. Nothing is picked for you: your location is not always a customer, and the names can differ. Pick one or more, or do it later from the customer page.
You can change this any time: Customers > Edit Customer > Internal customer.
