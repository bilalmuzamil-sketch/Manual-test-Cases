# Design-case plan — Internal Work Orders (12 Nov 2026, drafts for the case worker)

For the TestRail section **"Design — how it looks"** under section 136701 "Internal Work order (11/12/2026)" (Rule 126). Written in the QA lead's pattern (Rule 117 tenth, eleventh, twelfth, fourteenth and fifteenth amendments, model C665915): title starting "Design: " (at most 80 characters, no semicolons), plain "Sign in as … / Use a … / Have a …" preconditions, numbered steps with one action each, "Step N:" results with bullets, a blank line, the exact PRD quotes under their "From:" line and the build sentence, then **References from Design** (page, board, picture, the design's own words, the difference with its register row), the AUTOMATION line last. No "mark this test Blocked" line. Expected behaviour and wording come from PRD Final v1.0 (its header: "Where a frame and this text disagree, the text wins"); the design gives the look where the PRD is silent (Rule 126). Generated by `tools/make_case_plan.py` from `tools/cases_data.py`: every PRD quote is copied from the saved PRD by code (whole requirement or whole sentence), every design quote is checked word for word against the rendered boards and their source, and titles, one-action steps and the step/result pairing are checked by script.

Boards are 1280 px wide (lists 1440, phone 390): the cases run at 1440 px and add 1536 px where a line could wrap (settings hints, categories table, work orders list, report). Names and numbers in the pictures (Staging Heavy Duty Fleet, Hilltop Logistics, S2-34918, $675.00 …) are the designer's data, never preconditions. Each case that changes a record has its own new record in its preconditions (Rule 137). Held cases (DV-xx) wait for Milos Vasic's answers (NEW-QUESTIONS.py) and are not added to the run until then (Rule 130). Where the design differs from the PRD and the PRD settles it, the case follows the PRD and names the register row (`../DESIGN-DIVERGENCE-REGISTER.json`).

## DC-01 · Design: Work Orders settings show the Internal Work Orders toggle, off

**Title:** Design: Work Orders settings show the Internal Work Orders toggle, off

**Preconditions**
- Sign in as an Admin (a user with every setting, See Financial Data included).
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 pixels wide at 100% zoom.
- Have an organization where Internal Work Orders has never been turned on.
- Open the attached pictures iwo-flow0-01-01-settings-work-orders-setting-off.png, iwo-happy-01-01-turn-on-internal-work-orders.png at 100% zoom beside the screen.

**Steps**
1. Open Settings > Work Orders.

**Expected Result**
Step 1:
- Under Workflow, after Require Review Before Completion, a toggle row "Internal Work Orders" (Title Case, bold like the other toggle names) is shown, switched off.
- Its grey description under the name reads "Lets you flag your own company as an internal customer and mark a customer's work order, or a single line of a billable work order, as No Charge (warranty, goodwill, comeback). Internal and no-charge work orders close without an invoice; their parts cost posts to the expense account of their category instead of Cost of Parts."
- No hint lines show under the description while the toggle is off.

**Expected Result**
Step 1:
- A window opens over the page with the title "Which customers are your own company?" and an X at its top right.
- Its body reads "Work orders for these customers are internal: no prices and no invoice; parts cost posts to the expense account of their category when you use QuickBooks or AccountingHub. - Below it is one customer search field with nothing picked, the hint "Pick up to 50 customers here. Flag more from Edit Customer." and the line "You can change this any time: Customers > Edit Customer > Internal customer."
- Two buttons sit at the bottom right in this order: "Do this later" (white) then "Save" (blue), and Save is greyed out (disabled) because no customer is picked.
