Seen on production, outside this spec
Fixed prices print with whatever decimals the value carries: $4.5, $16.1, $1.4 rather than $4.50, $16.10, $1.40. Sorting by value is unaffected, and this spec does not touch formatting, but a money column that is not to two decimals is worth its own ticket.
