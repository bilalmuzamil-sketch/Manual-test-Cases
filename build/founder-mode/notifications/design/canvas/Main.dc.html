Notifications gets a home
Every board on this canvas measures against staging. That is a caveat rather than a claim: production can sit behind staging, and nothing here was checked against production. On staging the Notifications page is an inbox and nothing else, and tag groups, quick notes and notification preferences do not exist anywhere in the product. The question was never where to move them. It was where they should first appear.
They arrive as three new tabs on the Notifications page the bell already opens, beside the inbox. Each tab has its own address, so a tab can be linked to directly.

Finding a notification
Search looks only at the notifications already on screen, and the inbox stops at the newest hundred. Anything older cannot be reached at all.
Search runs on the server across the note text, the sender, and the work order or part sale number on the pill. The inbox loads 50 at a time with a Load more.
Editing and deleting a note
Anyone holding the record's View permission can edit anyone's note, which on a shop floor is very nearly everybody.
Editing is the author's alone. Deleting is the author, or a user holding Delete for that kind of record. See board 11.
