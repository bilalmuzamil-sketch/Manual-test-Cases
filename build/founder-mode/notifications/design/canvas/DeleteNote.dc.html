Editing belongs to the author, and to nobody else. A note carries its author's name, so another person editing it puts words in that author's mouth while the attribution stays put. No permission and no role opens it.
Deleting is moderation, so it follows the Delete permission. Taking down something wrong, offensive, or written on the wrong job is a supervisor's task. Work Orders: Delete covers a work order, line or part sale note; Customers: Delete covers a customer or asset note. The author may always delete their own.
What this replaces. Today anyone holding the record's View permission can edit anyone's note, which on a real shop floor is very nearly everybody. That rule came from SV-8003 on 8 July 2026 and is overridden here.
