The prompt reads “You can @tag and notify another team member”, one person at a time, because a tag group did not exist yet. There is nothing on screen to reuse: no saved set of recipients and no saved message, so the same instruction gets retyped and the same four people get tagged by hand every time. The prompt now names both things a writer can reach for: “Use @ to tag team members or tag groups”. Two rows of pills arrive under the message. Tag groups inserts a whole saved set of recipients, and hovering one shows its member names. Quick notes drops a saved message body in, and hovering shows that body. Reminder Date stays exactly as it is today, under the pills: it still feeds the Reminders report, the dashboard's reminders panel and the urgent-reminder signal on the bell.
This is the screen that answers “why does a technician need tag groups at all”. Everything saved on the Tag Groups and Quick Notes tabs turns up here as a pill. That is why all of it sits on the Notifications page: the thing a user saves and the place they spend it are two clicks apart.
