The @ is part of the field, drawn outside the input in the muted label color. It cannot be selected, deleted, typed over or reached by the caret, and it is there before the user clicks. It is not part of the name: what saves, lists and duplicate-checks is the typed text alone. The cap is now 40 typed characters, and the @ does not count toward it.
