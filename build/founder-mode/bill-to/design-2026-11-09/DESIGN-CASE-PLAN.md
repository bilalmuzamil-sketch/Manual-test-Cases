**Title:** Design: New Customer shows Bill Invoices To between Credit Limit and Sales Rep

**Preconditions**
- Sign in as an Admin at the test location.
- Use a desktop browser window 1440 pixels wide, at 100% zoom.
- Start on the Customers page.
- Open the attached pictures bt-p1-02-after-bill-invoices-to-empty.png, bt-p1-01-before-new-customer.png at 100% zoom beside the screen.

**Steps**
1. Click New Customer.
2. Resize the browser window to 1536 pixels wide.

**Expected Result**
Step 1:
- A full-width field labelled "Bill Invoices To" sits directly under the Credit Terms | Credit Limit row and directly above Sales Representative, as in the picture.
- It reads "This customer" and has a down arrow at its right, like the other dropdown fields.
- No help line shows under it.
- Address 1 has a + at its right and there is no Address 2 field.
Step 2:
- The field order is unchanged and nothing in the window wraps, overlaps or is cut off.
