

# SV-12289 — Bill To

Type: Epic | Status: Design In Progress | Updated: 2026-11-06T14:25:04.923-0500

**PRD:** <custom data-type="smartlink" data-id="id-0">https://shopview.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/~712020aa00b8d6a71f4259891982a304227c20/pages/1003585538/Bill+To</custom>

**Design:** <custom data-type="smartlink" data-id="id-1">https://claude.ai/artifact/2prSHgwt2tWCz96QD2DWkd</custom>

### What

Let a work order, part sale and its invoice bill a different customer (the Bill To) while the customer the work is for keeps the truck, its history, pricing and revenue. A customer can have a saved default Bill To for new work orders and part sales.

### Why

When a warranty company, dealer, parent company or lessor pays, the only way to bill them today is to make them the work order's customer, which moves the truck's history and splits receivables. About 55 customer insights ask for this (theme #183, SV-5196, SV-5610).

### Scope

* Saved default Bill To in New and Edit Customer; Bill To on the work order and part sale Customer card.
* Receivable, terms, credit limit, Credit Hold, PO, deposits and payments follow the Bill To.
* "Service For" band on invoices and estimates when the Bill To differs.
* Authorizer list adds the Bill To's contacts; asset Work Orders tab shows the whole history with a Customer filter.
* QuickBooks invoice under the Bill To, customers linked by ID; customer portal; IBS.
* Release dependencies: AccountingHub change (Hub team) and the customer portal deposit check.
* Out of scope: Ship To (its own release after this one).
