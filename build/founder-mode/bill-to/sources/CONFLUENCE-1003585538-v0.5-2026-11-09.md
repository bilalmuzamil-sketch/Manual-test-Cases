## 1. Business Case

Today a work order, a part sale and its invoice name one customer, who both owns the truck and owes the bill. When someone else pays (a warranty company or dealer, a parent company, a lessor, a carrier), the only way to bill them is to make them the work order's customer, which moves the truck's history, pricing and authorizer to the wrong customer and splits receivables. Customers ask for this repeatedly: about 55 PowerTools insights (14 from 8 companies in theme #183), plus SV-5196 (warranty billed to the dealer) and SV-5610 (parent pays, child keeps the history). This PRD adds a Bill To to every work order, part sale and invoice: the customer who owes the invoice, while the customer the work is for keeps the truck, the history, the pricing and the revenue. Shops that never pick a different Bill To keep working exactly as today.

### Workarounds

- **Duplicate customer records.** Service writers create a second customer for the payer and invoice it. The shop's receivables split across two records and the truck's history no longer sits in one place (PowerTools #59694, #175072).

- **Changing the work order's customer to the payer.** Service writers swap the customer before invoicing. The truck links to the payer, its history moves with it and the authorizer is cleared.

- **Reversing and re-invoicing a paid invoice to fix the name.** Office staff reverse a paid invoice just to put the right customer on it (PowerTools #79559). It costs a payment reversal, a re-invoice and a second QuickBooks sync for one name change.


## 2. Feature Overview

### Core Bill To

- Every work order, part sale and invoice has two customers: the **customer** (the work is for them) and the **Bill To** (they owe the invoice). The Bill To starts as the customer itself.

- The customer keeps the truck, the service history, pricing, tax, discounts, fees, shop supplies and the revenue.

- The Bill To owns the receivable: payment terms and due date, credit limit, Credit Hold, Customer PO and "PO is required", deposits, payments, credits, the QuickBooks invoice customer, IBS, the portal Pay button and the invoice email.

- A customer can have a saved default Bill To. New work orders and part sales for that customer start billed to it.

- Staff pick or change the Bill To on the work order or part sale Customer card, until it is invoiced.

- Once invoiced, the Bill To is locked. The only correction is to reverse the invoice, change the Bill To and invoice again.

- When the Bill To differs from the customer, printed invoices and estimates carry a "Service For" band naming the customer and the unit.

- The Bill To shows only where it differs from the customer. A customer that pays for itself sees nothing new on its documents.


### Integrations

- **QuickBooks Online:** the invoice is sent under the Bill To.

- **AccountingHub:** the invoice is sent under the Bill To, as it is to QuickBooks; nothing changes in what AccountingHub receives or how it reads it (Story 18).

- **IBS:** the Bill To's IBS account applies; its number is saved on the invoice at creation.

- **Customer portal:** the Bill To's contacts see and pay the invoice; the customer's contacts see it read-only.

- **Public API:** Bill To is added alongside existing fields; nothing existing changes meaning. Creating an invoice for a Bill To on Credit Hold is refused (Story 19).


### Release day

Nothing is migrated. Every existing work order, part sale and invoice already has one customer in both roles, so every total, balance, report and document matches the day before. Credits on invoices created before release keep today's revenue attribution. Bill To has no feature flag; it is on for every shop at release. These are the only changes a shop can see on release day:

- The asset page's Work Orders tab lists the asset's whole history at the current location (every customer and payer), not only the current owner's work orders, and gains a Customer filter (Story 14).

- Users who can change a work order's customer see a "Bill To" field reading "This customer" on every work order and part sale Customer card (Story 3).

- Users with 'Customers → Create & Edit' and 'View and Manage AP/AR Data' see a "Bill Invoices To" field in New Customer and Edit Customer (Story 2).

- In New Customer and Edit Customer, an empty Address 2 is hidden behind a + button beside Address 1. A customer with an Address 2 opens with it showing (Story 2).

- The invoice CSV export gains a last column, "Service Customer", blank on every invoice billed to its own customer (Story 19).

- Credit Hold blocking Create Invoice is now also checked on the server. The same customers are blocked, with the same message (Story 7).

- Creating an invoice through the Public API for a customer on Credit Hold is now refused (Story 19).


### State by action

| Action | Open | Invoiced | Internal or No Charge |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pick or change the Bill To | Allowed. Refused while deposits are held (M1). | Read-only (M4) | No Bill To |
| Change Customer | Allowed. A default Bill To follows, a picked one stays. Deposits block only if the Bill To would change. | As today; the invoice keeps its Bill To. A self-billed document stays self-billed. | As the Internal Work Orders PRD |
| Take a deposit | Allowed; lands on the Bill To's account | As today | n/a |
| Create the invoice | Allowed, unless the Bill To is on Credit Hold (M2) or requires a PO and Customer PO is empty | n/a | No invoice |
| Take a payment | n/a | From the Bill To's account only (M3) | n/a |
| Reverse the invoice | n/a | As today: refused while any payment, deposit or credit is applied; reopens the work order to Complete | n/a |
| Invoice again after Reverse | Bill To unlocked; new invoice to the current Bill To, same invoice number | n/a | n/a |

### Out of Scope

- **Ship To.** Ships after Bill To as its own release. The Service For band leaves room for a Ship To block beside the Bill To.

- **A separate payer on each payment, split billing, parent and child accounts, tax by service location.** Not in this release.

- **Credit Terms (SV-11638) and Split Representatives (SV-10890).** Their own PRDs own their fields and behavior. This PRD only notes where a Bill To screen depends on them.

- **Bill To in New Work Order and New Part Sale.** Neither window changes. A saved default applies on create and the Bill To is changed on the Customer card afterwards.

- **A saved default Bill To in the Public API.** Set in ShopView only.

- **Setting a Bill To through the Public API.** Read and filter only.

- **Standalone credits** (goodwill, overpayment, with no invoice). Unchanged: one customer, as today.

- **Historical imports and AccountingHub manual invoices.** No Bill To; unchanged.

- **Work Orders list, Board View and Tech View.** No Bill To on these screens.

- **Report columns for Bill To** (Bill To on sales, margin and technician reports; service customer on A/R Aging, statements, Customer Transactions): follow-up with Report Modernization. Until then, sales reports name the customer the work was for.

- **A "Paid by" pointer on the service customer's side:** follow-up.

- **Estimate emails and approval links:** unchanged; they go to the customer's contacts, as today.

- **A history of changes to a customer's saved default Bill To:** not in this release.

- **Changing how Reverse works:** unchanged; Bill To uses it as the correction path.


## 3. Jobs to be Done / Goals

**When** a warranty company or dealer pays for work on a customer's truck, **I want to** invoice the payer while the work stays on the truck owner's record, **so I can** get paid without corrupting the truck's history.

**When** a parent company pays for every job on its branches' trucks, **I want to** set that once on the branch customer, **so I can** stop picking the payer by hand on every work order.

**When** I collect payment, **I want** the balance, terms, credit limit and Credit Hold to be the payer's, **so I can** chase the right customer for the right amount.

**When** I bill the wrong payer by mistake, **I want** one clear correction path, **so I can** fix it without support.

### Goals

- A shop can invoice a customer other than the one the work is for, without changing the work order's customer.

- The truck's history, pricing and revenue stay with the customer the work was for on every invoice billed to someone else.

- A saved default bills every new work order and part sale for that customer to the payer, with no step per job.

- A shop that never picks a different Bill To sees every total, balance, report and document unchanged.


## 4. Key Decisions

- **Two customers, split by job.** The customer owns pricing, tax, discounts, fees, shop supplies, history and revenue; the Bill To owns the receivable, terms, credit limit, Credit Hold, PO, deposits, payments, QuickBooks customer and IBS. **Rationale:** a Bill To change never changes the invoice total, and the payer's account carries what it owes. **Rejected:** billing by changing the work order's customer, which moves the truck. *Basis:* product owner rules of 2026-10-02 (QB-01); W1, W2, W4, W5, W7, A7.

- **Bill To shows only where it differs from the customer.** **Rationale:** about 99% of customers pay for themselves, so an always-on Bill To is noise. **Rejected:** always showing "Bill To: This customer". *Basis:* Bill To review, 2026-11-04.

- **Invoiced means locked; Reverse and invoice again is the only correction, and Reverse works as today.** The invoice is removed, AccountingHub keeps the reversal record, QuickBooks deletes its copy and receives the re-issued invoice, which keeps the same number. **Rationale:** an issued invoice is never edited, and the customer's PO still matches the number. **Rejected:** editing the Bill To on an issued invoice; a guided change wizard. *Basis:* A2 (2026-10-28); owning PM, 2026-11-04; `d-credit-separate-document` (decided 2026-01-15).

- **Deposits lock the Bill To.** A deposit belongs to the Bill To it was taken under and never moves between customers. The refusal names the amount and payer and opens the payer's Deposits tab. A payment may use only deposits and credits held by the paying customer. **Rejected:** moving a deposit to the new Bill To. *Basis:* A4; owning PM, 2026-11-04; tech review 2026-11-06.

- **A saved default Bill To, one hop, applied at creation only.** Set in New Customer and Edit Customer; applied silently when a work order or part sale is created and shown on its Customer card. **Rationale:** the parent-pays case needs no per-job step. **Rejected:** a Bill To field in New Work Order and New Part Sale. *Basis:* A8, N1 (2026-10-29), A1 approved 2026-11-05, owning PM 2026-11-05.

- **The authorizer list adds the Bill To's contacts when the Bill To differs.** **Rationale:** the payer can approve the work it pays for. *Basis:* owning PM, 2026-11-06.

- **"Service For" band on documents, only when the Bill To differs.** The document header stays Bill To led; the band names the customer and unit, satisfying the California and Florida customer-naming rules (Bus. & Prof. Code 9880.1(f); Fla. Stat. 559.903(1)). **Rejected:** Bill To and Service For blocks side by side (tight on a printed page). *Basis:* A6, D9; owning PM 2026-11-06 (option B2); `d-reversal-invoice-header-addresses` (decided 2026-08-14), distinguished: the band renders only when the roles differ.

- **Counter Sale is never the Bill To for another customer, and always bills itself by default.** *Basis:* D3.

- **The Bill To's Credit Hold blocks invoicing, checked on the server; its credit limit warns but never blocks.** *Basis:* D4, W2.

- **A payment settles only invoices its own customer owes.** Enforced for new payments only; existing applications are left as they are. *Basis:* E2 and its product-owner caveat.

- **A credit issued from an invoice reduces the revenue of that invoice's customer, not the Bill To's.** Applies to invoices created after release; older credits keep today's attribution so no existing figure moves. *Basis:* product owner rules of 2026-10-02 (revenue); tech review 2026-11-06.

- **The asset's Work Orders tab shows its whole history at the current location by default, with a Customer filter matching the customer or the Bill To.** **Rationale:** a customer wants an asset's history filtered to "work done by company x, y, and/or z". *Basis:* owning PM, 2026-11-06.

- **The Public API is additive, with one deliberate change: an invoice for a Bill To on Credit Hold is refused.** *Basis:* D4, D5, D10.

- **Change Customer never makes a self-billed document bill the old customer.** On an invoiced document the invoice keeps its Bill To; after Reverse, invoicing again bills the new customer, as today. **Rationale:** shops that never use Bill To keep today's correction path. *Basis:* tech review 2026-11-06.

- **AccountingHub receives the invoice under the Bill To, as QuickBooks does.** The invoice, its payments, deposits and credits sit on one customer, and no AccountingHub change is needed. **Rejected:** sending both customers so AccountingHub splits the receivable from revenue, which needs AccountingHub work and a timed release. ShopView's own reports still credit the work to the customer. *Basis:* owning PM, 2026-11-06.

- **Address 2 on demand ships with Bill To.** An empty Address 2 hides behind a + in New Customer and Edit Customer. **Rationale:** both changes touch the same customer windows, so they ship in one release. *Basis:* owning PM, 2026-11-05.

- **Known limitation: tax follows the customer.** A tax-exempt Bill To is still charged tax on a taxable customer's job. Tax-exempt settings are unused today, so nothing regresses. *Basis:* W4 caveat.

- **Permissions.** No new permission. Setting or removing a saved default Bill To needs 'Customers → Create & Edit' and 'View and Manage AP/AR Data' (the Credit Terms pattern). Changing a work order's Bill To needs 'Work orders → Create & Edit'; a part sale's needs 'Part sales → Create & Edit' or 'Work orders → Create & Edit' (the Change Customer permission). Anyone who can view the customer or work order sees the Bill To. *Basis:* `d-inherit-nearest-permission` (decided 2026-08-17); `d-permissions-page-scoped-simplicity` (decided 2026-09-01).


## 5. Terminology

- **Customer** → the customer the work is for: owns the truck, history, pricing and revenue. Called the "service customer" where both roles are named.

- **Bill To** → the customer who owes the invoice. Equals the customer unless someone else is picked.

- **Saved default Bill To** → a customer-level setting ("Bill Invoices To") that sets the Bill To of new work orders and part sales for that customer. Never changes existing ones.

- **Counter Sale** → the system customer walk-ins are invoiced to. A normal customer in this PRD, never a Bill To for others.

- **Open / Invoiced** → Open means Estimate, Approved, In Progress, Ready for Review, Complete or Declined. Invoiced means Invoiced or Paid.

- **Reverse** → ShopView's existing action that removes an invoice and puts the work order back to Complete.

- **Vocabulary.** This PRD uses the frontend vocabulary, *customer* and *location*, which map to *company* and *workplace* in the backend.


## 6. Assumptions

- About 1% of customers have someone else pay; every surface is sized so the other 99% see nothing new on their documents.

- Staff pick a Bill To at a desk, before invoicing, with the work order or part sale already open.
