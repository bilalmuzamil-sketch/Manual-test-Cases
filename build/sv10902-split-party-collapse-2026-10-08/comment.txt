Tested on the QA branch [sv10360|https://sv10360.qa.shopview.com] (build v26.40.8-3e5c1df) on 8 October 2026, signed in as Admin, using a test bank account with imported rows. The "before" was taken on production (build v26.40.12-106a0f1) the same day with the same steps.

In short:
* When a category-only rule collapses a split row whose lines disagree, the row now comes back with *no customer*. The customer it had before it was split no longer comes back.
* On production the same steps bring the old customer back. That is the problem in this ticket, and it is gone on the QA branch.
* Rows whose split lines all agree keep that customer. Rows whose lines the user picked and that disagree are left alone. Clearing the splits by hand still leaves the row blank.

h3. How to see it yourself
# Open [https://sv10360.qa.shopview.com|https://sv10360.qa.shopview.com] and sign in as Admin.
# At the top left, next to the logo, click {{ShopHub}} (with the small arrow) and choose {{AccountingHub}}.
# In the left menu under {{BANKING}}, click {{Transactions}}. In the row of bank cards near the top, click the card {{ZZAUTOTEST SV-10902 bank}}.
# The list under {{For review}} shows the test rows. Rows {{ZZ10902-A}} and {{ZZ10902-G}} were split with disagreeing lines before the rule ran: in the {{CUSTOMER / VENDOR}} column they show the grey placeholder {{Customer / Vendor}}, meaning no customer (pictures above).

To repeat it from scratch with a new row (use a letter not used yet, for example M):
# On {{Transactions}}, click the three dots ({{⋮}}) at the top right of the bank card {{ZZAUTOTEST SV-10902 bank}} and choose {{Import statement}}. Under {{Statement file (CSV)}} pick a CSV with one row, for example a file containing the two lines {{Date,Description,Amount}} and {{2026-10-10,ZZ10902-M supplies,-100.00}}. Leave the other settings as they are and click {{Import}}.
# In the left menu under {{BANKING}}, click {{Rules}}, then {{New rule}}. Fill {{Name}}, set {{Pattern}} to {{ZZ10902-M}}, under {{Customer / Vendor}} click {{Set customer/vendor}} and pick {{ZZ Acme}} in the box that appears, tick {{Also apply to For review now}} and click {{Save}}.
# Go back to {{Transactions}} and the {{ZZAUTOTEST SV-10902 bank}} card. On the ZZ10902-M row, click the three dots ({{⋮}}) at the right end and choose {{Split}}. The window shows two lines, both with ZZ Acme. Give each line a {{CATEGORY}} and an {{AMOUNT}} (for example 60.00 and 40.00), leave {{CUSTOMER / VENDOR}} as it is and click {{Save splits}}.
# Make another rule like step 2, but pick {{ZZ Bob}}. Both lines now show ZZ Bob.
# On the row, click {{Split (2)}} in the {{ACCOUNT}} column, click {{+ Add split line}}, change the amounts to 50.00, 30.00 and 20.00 with a category on the new line, leave the new line's {{CUSTOMER / VENDOR}} empty and click {{Save splits}}.
# Make a third rule for {{ZZ10902-M}}: pick {{6110 Sales Tax Expense}} under {{Set account}}, leave {{Customer / Vendor}} on {{No customer/vendor action}}, tick {{Also apply to For review now}} and click {{Save}}.
# Back on {{Transactions}}, the ZZ10902-M row is no longer split and its {{CUSTOMER / VENDOR}} shows the grey placeholder {{Customer / Vendor}}: no customer. On production the same steps show {{ZZ Acme}} there.
