Tested on the QA branch [sv10360|https://sv10360.qa.shopview.com] (build v26.40.8-3e5c1df) on 8 October 2026, signed in as Admin, on a test bank account with imported rows. The "before" was taken on production (build v26.40.12-106a0f1) the same day with the same steps.

In short:
* Saving from the *Edit* window no longer undoes a memo you had just typed on the row, and no longer silently wipes a change made in another tab.
* When the row was changed somewhere else, the window now stops with "This transaction changed. Reload it before saving." and a *Reload* button, and keeps what you typed while that message shows.
* A row locked by that kind of conflict has *Edit* and *Split* greyed out until it is reloaded.

h3. How to see it yourself
# Open [https://sv10360.qa.shopview.com|https://sv10360.qa.shopview.com] and sign in as Admin.
# At the top left, next to the logo, click *ShopHub* (with the small arrow) and choose *AccountingHub*.
# In the left menu under *BANKING*, click *Transactions*, then click the bank card *ZZAUTOTEST SV-10903 bank* near the top.
# Open the same page in a second browser tab and click the same bank card there.
# In the first tab, on a row (for example *ZZ10903-1 supplies*), click the three dots (⋮) at the right end and choose *Edit*.
# In the second tab, on the same row, change the *ACCOUNT* dropdown to another account.
# Back in the first tab, type a memo in *Memo (optional)* and click *Save*: the red message "This transaction changed. Reload it before saving." appears with *Reload*, and what you typed is still there. Nothing was saved over the second tab's account.
# For the locked row: in the second tab change the memo of a row (click the small arrow at the right end of the row to show *Memo*), then in the first tab change the memo of the same row and click somewhere else. The row shows the same red message; its ⋮ menu has *Edit* and *Split* greyed out until you click the row's *Reload*.

The memo-before-Edit case (check 1) is hard to do by hand on a fast connection, because the memo usually saves before the Edit window can be opened.
